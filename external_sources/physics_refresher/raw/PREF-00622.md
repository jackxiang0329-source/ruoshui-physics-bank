# PREF-00622 · challenge

Two strings of different linear mass densities, $\mu_1=0.002$ kg/m and $\mu_2=0.008$ kg/m, are under the same tension of 100 N. Find the wave speed on each, and find the ratio of their speeds, explaining whether this ratio matches what you'd expect from the square-root relationship in $v=\sqrt{T/\mu}$.

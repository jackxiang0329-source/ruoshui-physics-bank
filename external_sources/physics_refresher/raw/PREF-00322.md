# PREF-00322 · challenge

A car of mass 1300 kg must safely stop from 26 m/s within a total distance of 45 m, using a combination of a constant braking force for the first 35 m and a crumple zone for the final 10 m in the event of a collision (i.e., in a non-collision stop, the full 45 m of braking is available). Find the required constant braking force to stop within 45 m, and separately, in a collision scenario where only the last 10 m (crumple zone) is used to absorb the same total kinetic energy from 26 m/s, find the average crumple-zone force required.

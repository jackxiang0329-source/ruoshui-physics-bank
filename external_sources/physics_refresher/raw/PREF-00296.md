# PREF-00296 · challenge

A car manufacturer wants to redesign a crumple zone so that a 1600 kg car traveling at 32 m/s experiences no more than 150 m/s² of deceleration. Find the required crumple zone length, then determine what maximum speed the same crumple zone length could safely handle if the deceleration limit were relaxed to 250 m/s² (perhaps for a different vehicle class).

# PREF-00096 · challenge

Three cables pull on a ring with forces $\vec{F_1} = 20$ N at $0°$, $\vec{F_2} = 20$ N at $120°$, and $\vec{F_3} = 20$ N at $240°$. Calculate the resultant force, and explain physically what the result tells you about the ring's state (hint: consider what equal magnitudes at $120°$ apart imply for the sum of components).

# PREF-00496 · challenge

A charged particle is accelerated from rest through a potential difference of 3000 V, then enters a 0.3 T magnetic field perpendicular to its velocity, tracing a circular path of radius 0.05 m. Given the particle's charge is $1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, find its mass (hint: first express speed in terms of the radius using $r=mv/(qB)$, then substitute into the work-energy relationship $q\Delta V=\frac12mv^2$ and solve for $m$).

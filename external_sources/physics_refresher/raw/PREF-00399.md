# PREF-00399 · challenge

A circuit has a $10\ \Omega$ resistor in parallel with a series combination of two $5\ \Omega$ resistors (i.e., the series pair, totaling $10\ \Omega$, is itself in parallel with the single $10\ \Omega$ resistor). Find the overall equivalent resistance of this combination, and explain why the answer being exactly half of $10\ \Omega$ makes sense given the symmetry of the arrangement.

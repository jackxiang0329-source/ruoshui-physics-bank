# PREF-00697 · challenge

Two coupled oscillators (mass 0.5 kg each, $k=150$ N/m, $k_c=25$ N/m) begin with one mass displaced 0.05 m and the other at rest. Find both normal-mode frequencies, the beat period, and describe qualitatively what an observer watching both masses would see over a time span of several beat periods.

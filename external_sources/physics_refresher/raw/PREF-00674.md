# PREF-00674 · challenge

A pedestrian bridge is redesigned with a tuned mass damper to shift its effective natural frequency away from typical pedestrian driving frequencies (2.5–3.5 Hz range). If the bridge's original natural frequency was 2.0 Hz (effective mass $4\times10^4$ kg, effective stiffness $k$), find the original $k$, and then find the new effective stiffness $k_{\text{new}}$ required to shift the natural frequency to 3.2 Hz (safely outside the problematic range), assuming the effective mass stays the same.

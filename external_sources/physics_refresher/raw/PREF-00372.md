# PREF-00372 · challenge

Three charges are arranged at the corners of an equilateral triangle with side length 0.3 m: $q_1=+2\ \mu\text{C}$, $q_2=+3\ \mu\text{C}$, $q_3=-4\ \mu\text{C}$. Find the total electric potential energy of this three-charge system (hint: sum the pairwise potential energies for all three unique pairs).

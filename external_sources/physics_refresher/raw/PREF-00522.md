# PREF-00522 · challenge

A seismic P-wave (longitudinal, speed 6000 m/s) and S-wave (transverse, speed 3500 m/s) are generated simultaneously at an earthquake's origin, 84 km from a monitoring station. Find the arrival time of each wave at the station, and find the time delay between them (this delay is how seismologists estimate distance to an earthquake's origin).

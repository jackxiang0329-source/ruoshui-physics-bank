# PREF-00599 · challenge

A structural engineer models two adjacent building floors as coupled oscillators, each with effective mass $2\times10^5$ kg and stiffness $5\times10^7$ N/m, coupled by an effective stiffness of $1\times10^7$ N/m (representing the structural connection between floors). Find both normal-mode frequencies (in Hz, using $f=\omega/2\pi$) and the resulting beat period, and discuss briefly why a structural engineer would want to know both the individual mode frequencies and the beat period when designing for earthquake safety.

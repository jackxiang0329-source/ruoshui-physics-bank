# PREF-00446 · challenge

Two capacitors, $C_1=6\ \mu\text{F}$ and $C_2=3\ \mu\text{F}$, are first connected in series to a 12 V source, then disconnected and reconnected in parallel with each other (with no external source). Find (a) the charge on each capacitor while in the series configuration, and (b) the total energy stored in the series configuration, then compare briefly to the parallel configuration's maximum possible stored energy at the same total charge, without solving the full parallel redistribution problem.

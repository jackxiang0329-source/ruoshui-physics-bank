# PREF-00323 · challenge

A 4 kg mass swings as a conical pendulum (see Lesson 2.5's challenge problems) with string length 2 m at angle $30°$ from vertical, then the string is suddenly cut. Using kinematics and projectile-style reasoning (horizontal velocity at the moment of cutting, then free-fall under gravity), describe the qualitative shape of the resulting motion and calculate the mass's speed at the instant the string is cut.

# PREF-00275 · challenge

Estimate the minimum orbital speed of a satellite in a circular orbit close to the Earth's surface (radius approximately 6,370 km), assuming gravity alone provides the centripetal force, and that at this altitude $g\approx9.8 \text{ m/s}^2$ acts as the centripetal acceleration (i.e., $g = v^2/r$). Compare your answer to the speed of sound (~340 m/s) to comment on how fast this orbital speed actually is.

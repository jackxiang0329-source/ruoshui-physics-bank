# PREF-00624 · challenge

A seismic wave passes through two rock layers with different effective wave speeds: 4000 m/s in the upper layer and 6000 m/s in the lower layer. If the wave's frequency remains 2 Hz as it crosses from one layer to the other (frequency is preserved across a boundary, though wavelength is not — a fact you may take as given here), find the wavelength in each layer.

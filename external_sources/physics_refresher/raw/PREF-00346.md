# PREF-00346 · challenge

Three point charges are placed at the corners of a right triangle: $q_1=+5\ \mu\text{C}$ at the origin, $q_2=+3\ \mu\text{C}$ at $(0.2, 0)$ m, and $q_3=-4\ \mu\text{C}$ at $(0, 0.2)$ m. Find the net force on $q_1$ due to both $q_2$ and $q_3$ (magnitude and direction), using vector components as in Lesson 1.4.

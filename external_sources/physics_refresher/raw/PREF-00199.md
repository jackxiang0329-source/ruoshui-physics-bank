# PREF-00199 · challenge

A rope pulls a 15 kg sled up a $10°$ incline with $\mu_k = 0.2$, with tension 100 N applied parallel to the incline surface. Find the sled's acceleration up the slope, accounting for gravity's component along the slope, friction (opposing the upward motion), and the applied tension.

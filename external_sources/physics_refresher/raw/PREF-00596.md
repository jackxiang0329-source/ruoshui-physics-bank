# PREF-00596 · challenge

Two coupled oscillators, each of mass 0.6 kg, are connected to walls by springs of unknown stiffness $k$ and to each other by a coupling spring $k_c=40$ N/m. If the measured antisymmetric frequency is 18 rad/s and the measured symmetric frequency is 15 rad/s, verify that these two measurements are mutually consistent with the relationships $\omega_{\text{sym}}^2=k/m$ and $\omega_{\text{antisym}}^2=(k+2k_c)/m$ (i.e., solve for $k$ from the symmetric frequency, then check whether the antisymmetric frequency prediction matches the given value).

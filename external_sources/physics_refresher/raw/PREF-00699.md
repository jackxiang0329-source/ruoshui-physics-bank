# PREF-00699 · challenge

A spring-mass oscillator (SHM) with $m=0.8$ kg, $k=320$ N/m is subject to light damping such that its amplitude decreases by 10% every complete oscillation cycle. Find the angular frequency and period of oscillation (treating damping as light enough not to significantly affect the frequency, per Lesson 4.3's discussion), and find the amplitude remaining after 10 complete cycles if it starts at 1.1 m.

# PREF-00673 · challenge

Two seismic stations, 200 km apart, both recorded the same earthquake. Station A measured a P-S delay of 30 s, and Station B measured a P-S delay of 45 s. Using $v_P=6000$ m/s and $v_S=3500$ m/s, estimate each station's distance from the earthquake's origin, and explain (without full triangulation calculations) why data from two or more stations allows seismologists to pinpoint a specific location rather than just a distance.

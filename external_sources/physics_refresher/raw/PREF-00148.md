# PREF-00148 · challenge

An engineer measures a beam's deflection angle as $7° \pm 0.5°$ under load. Using the small-angle approximation, estimate the vertical deflection at the beam's tip (length 2.5 m) as (length × tan θ), and propagate the angle's uncertainty into the final deflection's uncertainty (hint: for small angles, treat tan θ ≈ θ in radians, and use the percentage-uncertainty rule for multiplication).

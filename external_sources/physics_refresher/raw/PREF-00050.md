# PREF-00050 · challenge

Using only dimensional analysis (not the full derivation), determine the exponents $a$, $b$, and $c$ in a proposed equation for the period $T$ of a pendulum, $T = k\, L^a g^b m^c$, where $L$ is length (m), $g$ is acceleration due to gravity (m/s²), $m$ is mass (kg), and $k$ is a dimensionless constant — given that $T$ must have units of seconds, and noting that mass cannot appear in the final expression if no other quantity in the equation can cancel its dimension.

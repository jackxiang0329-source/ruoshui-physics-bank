# PREF-00175 · challenge

A elevator of mass 800 kg (including passengers) accelerates upward from rest at $1.2 \text{ m/s}^2$ for 2 s, then travels at constant velocity, then decelerates at $1.2 \text{ m/s}^2$ to a stop. Using Newton's Second Law, find the net force during the acceleration phase, and explain why passengers feel "heavier" during this phase even though their actual weight ($mg$) hasn't changed (hint: consider the total force the floor must exert on a passenger during acceleration, not just gravity).

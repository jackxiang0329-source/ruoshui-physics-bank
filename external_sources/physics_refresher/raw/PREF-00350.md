# PREF-00350 · challenge

A dipole consists of charges $+q$ and $-q$ separated by a small distance $d$, placed along the x-axis with the midpoint at the origin. Using superposition, find an expression for the electric field at a point on the axis a distance $x$ (measured from the midpoint) much larger than $d$, and show that this field decreases proportionally to $1/x^3$ for large $x$ (a faster falloff than a single point charge's $1/x^2$) — you may use the approximation $(x-d/2)^{-2}-(x+d/2)^{-2}\approx 2d/x^3$ for $x\gg d$ without deriving it.

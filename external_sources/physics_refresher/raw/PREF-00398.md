# PREF-00398 · challenge

A voltage divider consists of two resistors, $R_1=1000\ \Omega$ and $R_2=3000\ \Omega$, in series across a 12 V supply. Find the voltage across $R_2$ (this is the "output" voltage of the divider), and explain, using the series-circuit voltage-drop relationship, why a larger $R_2$ relative to $R_1$ produces a larger fraction of the supply voltage at the output.

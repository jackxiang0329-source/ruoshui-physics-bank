# PREF-00074 · challenge

A GPS receiver reports a systematic position bias of about 2 m to the east due to atmospheric signal delay, plus a random scatter of about ±1 m from electronic noise. Explain what would happen to each type of error if you averaged the GPS position over 100 readings taken at the same fixed location, and explain why the averaged result would still not be perfectly accurate.

# PREF-00474 · challenge

A simplified DC motor's current loop carries 2 A through a 0.08 m side length, in a magnetic field of 1.5 T, oriented so the field is perpendicular to that side. Find the force on that side of the loop, and if the same-magnitude, oppositely-directed force acts on the opposite side (0.08 m away, parallel), estimate the torque as (force) × (distance between the two sides), even though a fully rigorous torque treatment is beyond this lesson's scope.

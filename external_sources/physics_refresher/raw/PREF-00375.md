# PREF-00375 · challenge

A charge of $+10\ \mu\text{C}$ is brought from very far away (where potential is taken as zero) to a point 0.15 m from a fixed charge of $+20\ \mu\text{C}$. Calculate the work required to do this (equal to the final potential energy of the pair, since the initial PE was zero at infinite separation), and explain why this process requires positive work done by an external agent.

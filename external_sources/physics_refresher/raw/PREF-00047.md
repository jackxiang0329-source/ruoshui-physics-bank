# PREF-00047 · challenge

A engineer proposes a new equation, $P = \dfrac{Fv^2}{t}$, for mechanical power, where $F$ is force (N), $v$ is speed (m/s), and $t$ is time (s). Given that the correct SI unit of power (the watt) is $\text{kg}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{s}^3$, use dimensional analysis to determine whether this proposed equation could be correct, and if not, suggest what might be wrong with it.

# PREF-00400 · challenge

A 100 W light bulb and a 60 W light bulb (both rated for use at 120 V) are connected in series, rather than their normal parallel household configuration, across a 120 V supply. Using $P=V^2/R$ to first find each bulb's resistance (at its rated voltage), then analyzing the series circuit, determine which bulb will actually glow brighter in this (unusual) series configuration, and explain the surprising result in terms of how resistance and power interact in series versus parallel.

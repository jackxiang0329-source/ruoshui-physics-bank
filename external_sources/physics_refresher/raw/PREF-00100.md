# PREF-00100 · challenge

An aircraft needs a ground velocity of exactly 300 km/h due north, while a crosswind blows at 60 km/h due east. Determine the airspeed and heading (angle from north) the pilot must fly to achieve the desired ground velocity, given that the aircraft's velocity relative to the ground is the vector sum of its velocity relative to the air and the wind's velocity.

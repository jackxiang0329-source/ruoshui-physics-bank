# PREF-00472 · challenge

A defibrillator capacitor of $100\ \mu\text{F}$ is charged to a voltage that must be safety-rated with a factor of 2.8 above its normal operating voltage of 2000 V. Find the rated voltage, and find the energy the capacitor must safely withstand at that rated voltage versus the energy actually delivered at the normal 2000 V operating voltage.

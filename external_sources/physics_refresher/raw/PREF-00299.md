# PREF-00299 · challenge

A satellite is to be placed in a circular orbit such that it completes exactly one orbit every 24 hours (a "geostationary" orbit, matching Earth's rotation). Given that at this orbit the required relationship is $g' = 4\pi^2 r/T^2$ (derived by combining $a_c=\omega^2r$ from Lesson 2.5 with $\omega=2\pi/T$), and using $T=86{,}400$ s, estimate the orbital radius $r$ if $g' \approx 0.224 \text{ m/s}^2$ at that altitude (you do not need to independently verify $g'$ — treat it as given).

# PREF-00223 · challenge

A 5 kg block is pulled 8 m across a horizontal floor by a rope at $30°$ above horizontal with tension 60 N, while kinetic friction ($\mu_k=0.2$) acts on the block. Find the normal force (accounting for the vertical component of the tension reducing the effective normal force — recall the free-body diagram method from Lesson 2.2), then find the work done by friction and by the tension.

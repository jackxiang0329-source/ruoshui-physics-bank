# PREF-00250 · challenge

A 70 kg cyclist and bicycle (combined) coast down a hill, dropping 12 m in height, and reach the bottom at 14 m/s. Using conservation of energy with losses, determine how much energy was lost to air resistance and rolling friction during the descent, then calculate the average power dissipated by these losses if the descent took 9 s.

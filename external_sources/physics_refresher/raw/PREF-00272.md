# PREF-00272 · challenge

A pilot performs a vertical circular loop of radius 300 m at constant speed 150 m/s. Find the centripetal acceleration, express it as a multiple of $g$, and explain (using Newton's Second Law and a free-body diagram at the top and bottom of the loop) why the seat's normal force on the pilot differs between the top and bottom of the loop even though the centripetal acceleration's magnitude is the same throughout.

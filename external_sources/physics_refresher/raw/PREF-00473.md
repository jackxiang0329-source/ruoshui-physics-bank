# PREF-00473 · challenge

Two different ions (masses $2.0\times10^{-26}$ kg and $4.0\times10^{-26}$ kg, both with charge $+1.6\times10^{-19}$ C) are accelerated through the same 3000 V potential difference and enter the same 0.35 T magnetic field. Find the radius of each ion's circular path, and explain how a mass spectrometer could use the difference between these two radii to separate the two ion types physically.

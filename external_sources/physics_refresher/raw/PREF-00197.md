# PREF-00197 · challenge

Two masses, 4 kg and 6 kg, are connected by a light string over a frictionless pulley (an Atwood machine), with the 6 kg mass hanging and descending. Using Newton's Second Law applied separately to each mass (with the same-magnitude acceleration and tension throughout the string), find the system's acceleration and the tension in the string.

# PREF-00521 · challenge

A sound wave and a light wave are both said to have a frequency of 500 Hz (treat this as a hypothetical for the sound wave; a truly low frequency for light is unrealistic, but proceed mathematically). Given the sound wave travels at 340 m/s and the light wave travels at $3\times10^8$ m/s, find both wavelengths, and explain why the same frequency produces such vastly different wavelengths.

# PREF-00571 · challenge

A building's natural sway frequency is 0.2 Hz. An earthquake produces ground motion with significant energy across a range of frequencies from 1.1 Hz to 2 Hz. Explain why this building would be at particular resonance risk during this earthquake, and describe (in general terms) how a tuned mass damper could be designed to mitigate this risk.

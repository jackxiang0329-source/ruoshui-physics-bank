# PREF-00423 · challenge

A rectangular current-carrying loop (10 cm × 5 cm, carrying 2 A) is placed in a uniform magnetic field of 1.3 T, oriented so that the field is parallel to the plane of the loop (i.e., perpendicular to two of the loop's sides and parallel to the other two). Find the force on each of the two sides where the field is perpendicular to the current direction (each of length 5 cm), and explain qualitatively (without full torque calculations) why these two forces tend to rotate the loop rather than simply pushing it sideways.

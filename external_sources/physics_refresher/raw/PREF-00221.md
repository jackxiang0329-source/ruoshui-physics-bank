# PREF-00221 · challenge

A 1000 kg car is pushed up a $10°$ incline a distance of 20 m at constant velocity by an applied force acting parallel to the incline, with a kinetic friction coefficient of $\mu_k=0.05$. Find the work done by (a) the applied force, (b) gravity, (c) friction, and (d) verify that the net work done is consistent with the car's constant velocity (zero change in kinetic energy — you may state this as a check even though kinetic energy is formally covered in Lesson 2.4).

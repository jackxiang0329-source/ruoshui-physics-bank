Two waves travel through the same medium: Wave A has twice the frequency of Wave B. Assuming both waves travel at the same speed (a property of the medium, not the wave itself), find the ratio of their wavelengths, and explain why wave speed being medium-dependent (rather than frequency-dependent) leads to this result.

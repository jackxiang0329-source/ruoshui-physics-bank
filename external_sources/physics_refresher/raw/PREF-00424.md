# PREF-00424 · challenge

A charged particle moves through a region containing both a uniform electric field (magnitude $E$, pointing in the $+y$ direction) and a uniform magnetic field (magnitude $B$, pointing in the $+z$ direction, i.e., perpendicular to $E$), with the particle's velocity along $+x$. This is called a "velocity selector." Using the fact that only particles with a specific speed will experience exactly canceling electric and magnetic forces (and thus travel in a straight line), derive an expression for that specific speed $v$ in terms of $E$ and $B$.

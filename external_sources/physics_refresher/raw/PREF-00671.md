# PREF-00671 · challenge

An acoustic engineer is designing a room and wants to avoid having any of the first three room-mode frequencies fall between 60 Hz and 90 Hz (a particularly problematic range for this application). Given the speed of sound is 340 m/s, find a room length $L$ (or a range of lengths) that avoids this problem for at least the fundamental mode, and check whether the second harmonic of your chosen length also avoids the problem range.

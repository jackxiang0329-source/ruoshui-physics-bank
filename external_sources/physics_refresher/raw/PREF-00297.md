# PREF-00297 · challenge

A roller coaster loop of radius 10 m is entered at the bottom of the loop (not from a separate hill) with the coaster having descended a total height of 25 m from the very start of the ride to the bottom of the loop (assume negligible friction throughout). Find the coaster's speed at the bottom of the loop, then find its speed and confirm whether it exceeds the minimum required speed at the top of the loop (accounting for the additional height gained going from the bottom to the top of the loop, $2r$).

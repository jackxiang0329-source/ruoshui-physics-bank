# PREF-00149 · challenge

A component's mass is reported as $(500 \pm 15)$ g, and it experiences an acceleration of $(2.0 \pm 0.1)$ m/s². Convert the mass to kilograms, then calculate the resulting force ($F = ma$) with its combined percentage uncertainty, and express the final answer in newtons with correct significant figures.

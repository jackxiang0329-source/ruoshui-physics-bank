# PREF-00321 · challenge

A 2.5 kg block starts at rest at the top of a frictionless ramp of height 6 m, which leads into a horizontal section with friction ($\mu_k=0.2$) for 3 m, followed by a circular loop of radius 1.2 m. Find the block's speed entering the loop (after crossing the frictional section) and determine whether it maintains contact at the top of the loop.

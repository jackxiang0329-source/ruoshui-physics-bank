# PREF-00471 · challenge

A city requires 5,000,000 W of delivered power through a transmission line of resistance $3\ \Omega$. Find the transmission voltage required to keep power losses below 0.1% of delivered power, and comment on whether this voltage is realistic compared to real-world high-voltage transmission lines (typically hundreds of thousands of volts).

# PREF-00046 · challenge

The equation for gravitational force is $F = \dfrac{Gm_1m_2}{r^2}$, where $m_1, m_2$ are masses (kg), $r$ is distance (m), and $G$ is the gravitational constant. Given that $F$ must have units of newtons ($\text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s}^2$), determine the SI base units that $G$ must have for the equation to be dimensionally consistent.

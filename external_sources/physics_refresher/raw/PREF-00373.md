# PREF-00373 · challenge

A parallel-plate capacitor-like arrangement has plates separated by 0.005 m with a potential difference of 600 V. An electron (charge $-1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, mass $9.11\times10^{-31}$ kg) starts at rest at the negative plate. Find its speed when it reaches the positive plate, using the work-energy theorem.

# PREF-00173 · challenge

A 1500 kg car traveling at 20 m/s brakes to a stop over a distance of 40 m. Find the braking force required (assume constant deceleration), and then find how much additional stopping distance would be needed if the car had instead been traveling at 30 m/s under the same braking force (hint: use $v^2=v_0^2+2a(x-x_0)$ with the same $a$).

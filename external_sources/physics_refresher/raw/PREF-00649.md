# PREF-00649 · challenge

A closed-end wind instrument tube (closed at one end, open at the other) has a different boundary condition than a string fixed at both ends: the closed end must be a node, but the open end must be an antinode, which restricts allowed wavelengths to $\lambda_n = 4L/n$ for only odd values of $n$ ($n=1,3,5,\ldots$) — you may take this boundary condition as given rather than re-deriving it. Using a tube length of 1.5 m and a sound speed of 340 m/s, find the frequencies of the first three allowed harmonics ($n=1,3,5$).

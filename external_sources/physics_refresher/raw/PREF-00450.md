# PREF-00450 · challenge

Derive, using the "area under the graph" method explicitly (rather than the integral shortcut), why the energy stored in a capacitor charged to final charge $Q$ and voltage $V$ is $\frac12QV$, by describing the shape of a graph of voltage $v=q/C$ versus charge $q$ as the capacitor charges from $0$ to $Q$, and computing the area of that shape geometrically (compare this to the identical geometric argument used for spring energy in Lesson 2.3).

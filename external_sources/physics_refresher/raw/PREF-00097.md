# PREF-00097 · challenge

A robot arm's end-effector must reach a point 30 cm to the right and 40 cm up from its base, using exactly two straight-line segments (two vectors) whose magnitudes are equal. Determine the magnitude of each segment and the angle each must make with the horizontal, assuming the two segments are placed head-to-tail and are mirror images of each other about the line to the target.

# PREF-00574 · challenge

A rotating machine shaft has a critical speed (natural frequency) of 3000 RPM. The machine is designed to operate continuously at 2850 RPM and to pass briefly through 3000 RPM only during startup and shutdown. Explain, using resonance concepts, why briefly passing through the critical speed during startup/shutdown is generally considered acceptable, while sustained operation at that speed would not be.

# PREF-00347 · challenge

Two charges, $+Q$ and $+4Q$, are fixed 1 m apart. Find the location(s) along the line joining them where the net electric field is zero (hint: set the two individual field magnitudes equal and solve for position, being careful about which region between/outside the charges could physically have canceling fields given both charges are positive).

# PREF-00271 · challenge

A 1000 kg car rounds a curve of radius 120 m banked at $10°$, with a coefficient of static friction $\mu_s=0.3$ between tires and road. Find the range of speeds (minimum and maximum) at which the car can round the curve without skidding, accounting for both the banking angle and friction (hint: at maximum speed, friction acts down the slope, assisting the normal force's horizontal component; at minimum speed, friction acts up the slope, since the banking alone would provide more centripetal force than needed).

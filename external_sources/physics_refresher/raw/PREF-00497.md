# PREF-00497 · challenge

A circuit consists of a 20 V battery, a $4\ \Omega$ resistor in series with a parallel combination of a $6\ \Omega$ resistor and a $12\ \mu\text{F}$ capacitor. In steady state (after the capacitor is fully charged and no current flows into it), find the current through the $4\ \Omega$ and $6\ \Omega$ resistors, and the voltage across (and hence charge stored in) the capacitor (hint: in steady state, no current flows through a fully charged capacitor, so it can be treated as an open branch for the current calculation, though it still shares the voltage of the parallel resistor).

# PREF-00550 · challenge

Two identical mass-spring systems, both with mass $m$ and spring constant $k$, are set into oscillation with amplitudes $A$ and $2A$ respectively. Find the ratio of their total energies, the ratio of their maximum speeds, and explain why the periods of both systems are still identical despite the different amplitudes and energies.

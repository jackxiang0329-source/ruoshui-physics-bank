# PREF-00650 · challenge

A laser cavity has mirrors separated by exactly 0.30 m, supporting a standing light wave with boundary conditions analogous to a string fixed at both ends (both mirror surfaces must be nodes of the electric field, in this simplified treatment). Given the speed of light $c=3\times10^8$ m/s, find the frequency of the 500,000th harmonic of this cavity (a physically realistic harmonic number for visible-light standing waves in a cavity this size), and explain why such extremely high harmonic numbers are typical for optical (rather than acoustic or string) standing waves.

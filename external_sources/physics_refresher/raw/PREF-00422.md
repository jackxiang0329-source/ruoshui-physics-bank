# PREF-00422 · challenge

Two long parallel wires carry currents of 6 A and 10 A in the same direction, separated by 0.08 m. Find the magnetic force per unit length between them, and state whether the wires attract or repel (same-direction currents in parallel wires attract — you may state this as a known result, or reason through the right-hand rule applied to both wires in sequence).

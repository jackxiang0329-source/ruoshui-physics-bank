# PREF-00572 · challenge

An oscillator's amplitude is measured at 0.30 m at $t=0$ and 0.15 m at $t=6$ s. Assuming exponential decay $A(t)=A_0e^{-t/\tau}$, use the ratio of these two amplitudes and the relationship $A(t)/A_0 = e^{-t/\tau}$ to estimate $\tau$ (hint: you will need to reason about what fraction $1/2$ corresponds to in terms of $e^{-t/\tau}$, or simply state $\tau$ implicitly via the half-life-like relationship without solving for a precise logarithm, since natural logarithms are beyond this course's assumed math background — describe your reasoning qualitatively if an exact numeric answer requires tools beyond basic algebra).

# PREF-00075 · challenge

Two methods are proposed to measure the same steel rod's length: Method A uses a ruler with 1 mm resolution and gives $(150 \pm 1)$ mm; Method B uses a laser measurement tool with much higher resolution but a known, uncorrected systematic offset of $+3$ mm, giving $(153 \pm 0.1)$ mm displayed. Determine which method, if either, gives a result closer to the rod's true length of 150.2 mm, and explain what should be done to make Method B actually superior to Method A.

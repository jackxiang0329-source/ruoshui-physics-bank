# PREF-00421 · challenge

A charged particle with charge $+3.2\times10^{-19}$ C and mass $6.64\times10^{-27}$ kg (comparable to a helium ion) enters a mass spectrometer's magnetic field region of 0.25 T at a speed of $4\times10^5$ m/s, perpendicular to the field. Find the radius of its circular path, and find the time for it to complete one full circle (hint: use the circle's circumference and the particle's speed, or equivalently its period from Lesson 2.5's circular motion tools).

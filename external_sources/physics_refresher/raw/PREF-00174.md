# PREF-00174 · challenge

Two objects, of mass $2$ kg and $8$ kg, are pushed by the same net force of $16$ N, starting from rest. Find the time each takes to reach a velocity of $10$ m/s, and explain, using Newton's Second Law, why the ratio of these times equals the ratio of the masses.

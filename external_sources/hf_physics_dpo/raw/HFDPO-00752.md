# HFDPO-00752

A deep-sea mining robot equipped with flexible titanium alloy grippers must extract nodules from the ocean floor at 4,000 meters depth. The gripper arm is a hollow cylindrical beam (outer radius 5 cm, inner radius 4.8 cm) made of a material with Young’s modulus 110 GPa and a yield strength of 800 MPa. As the robot maneuvers, the arm experiences a torsional load of 120 N·m and a bending moment of 1.8 kN·m. Determine whether the gripper arm will undergo permanent deformation under this combined loading, considering both maximum shear stress from torsion and maximum normal stress from bending, and assess if a safety factor of 2.5 is sufficient.

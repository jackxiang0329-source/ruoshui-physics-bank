# HFDPO-13248

A solar-powered microgrid on a remote island uses a resonant inductor-capacitor (LC) circuit to stabilize voltage fluctuations caused by intermittent sunlight. The system’s natural frequency is initially tuned to 60 Hz, but seasonal cloud cover alters the effective capacitance due to temperature-dependent dielectric properties. If the capacitance increases by 12% over a cloudy week, what phase shift must be introduced via a variable inductor to restore resonant synchronization without altering the frequency? Assume the original inductance is 2.5 mH.

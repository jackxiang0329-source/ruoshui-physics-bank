# HFDPO-15986

In a next-generation quantum memory device, nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond are used to store quantum information via electron spin states. At 10 K, the system exhibits two nearly degenerate spin states (mₛ = ±1/2) with an energy splitting of Δ = 1.04 meV due to crystal field effects, while the ground state (mₛ = 0) lies 2.3 meV below. Given that the NV centers are coupled to a phonon bath with a density of states resembling a Debye model (θ_D = 1200 K), determine the fraction of spins in the mₛ = +1/2 state at thermal equilibrium, and explain how the phonon spectrum influences the population imbalance.

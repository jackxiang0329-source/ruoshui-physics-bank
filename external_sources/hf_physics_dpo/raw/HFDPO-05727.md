# HFDPO-05727

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarimetric imaging sensor to monitor oceanic algal blooms. The sensor detects light scattered at 90° from the sun’s direction, where the polarization state is maximally sensitive to phytoplankton concentration. However, the signal is contaminated by atmospheric aerosols, which introduce partially polarized light with an unknown degree of polarization. The sensor’s analyzer is set to transmit only light polarized at 45° to the incident plane. If the total detected intensity is 320 W/m², and the non-polarized (unpolarized) component contributes 120 W/m², what is the intensity of the fully polarized component, and what is the angle of its electric field oscillation relative to the analyzer?

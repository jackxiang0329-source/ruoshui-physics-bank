# HFDPO-00448

A quantum dot solar cell operates under concentrated sunlight (400 W/m², 550 nm peak wavelength), but its efficiency drops sharply above 25°C due to phonon-assisted carrier recombination. The dot’s energy levels are quantized with a spacing of 0.8 eV. Given that the thermal energy at 25°C is kBT ≈ 0.025 eV, and assuming a Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution for exciton population, calculate the fraction of excited states occupied at 25°C and 60°C. Evaluate whether the observed efficiency loss can be explained solely by thermal excitation, or if other factors such as carrier escape or defect trapping must be considered.

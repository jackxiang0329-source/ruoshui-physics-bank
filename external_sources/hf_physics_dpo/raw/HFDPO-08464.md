# HFDPO-08464

A deep-sea geothermal vent on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge emits superheated water at 400°C, but the surrounding ocean is near 2°C. Engineers propose using a closed-loop organic Rankine cycle (ORC) system with R-134a as working fluid, placed just meters from the vent. The system must extract maximum sustainable power while avoiding thermal shock to the heat exchanger. Given that the condenser is submerged in cold seawater and the maximum allowable temperature difference across the heat exchanger walls is 15°C due to material limits, what is the optimal maximum temperature for the vaporizing stage, and what is the resulting theoretical maximum efficiency of the cycle under these constraints?

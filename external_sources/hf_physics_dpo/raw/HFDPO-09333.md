# HFDPO-09333

A biomedical engineer is designing a non-invasive glucose sensor using near-infrared light (λ = 1550 nm) to measure blood glucose levels through skin tissue. The device relies on detecting subtle changes in the interference pattern of light reflected from different tissue layers. If the epidermis layer has a refractive index of 1.40 and a thickness of 70 μm, and the dermis has a refractive index of 1.35, what is the minimum thickness of a lipid layer (n = 1.45) that could be introduced due to inflammation, causing a phase shift of exactly π radians in the reflected wave relative to the original, thereby disrupting the interference pattern?

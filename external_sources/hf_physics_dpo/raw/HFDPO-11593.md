# HFDPO-11593

During a deep-sea drilling operation in the Gulf of Mexico, a 1.2 km long riser pipe (diameter 0.3 m) connects the ocean floor (1,200 m depth) to a surface drilling rig. The pipe is filled with drilling mud (density 1,650 kg/m³), while seawater (density 1,025 kg/m³) surrounds it. At the surface, the top of the riser is sealed, creating a pressure differential. If the pressure at the top of the riser is maintained at 1 atm (101,325 Pa), what is the net upward force on a 5 m segment of the riser near the surface due to hydrostatic imbalance, and how does this affect the structural stability of the riser?

# HFDPO-13632

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar array charges a battery bank through a DC-DC buck converter, but during polar night, the system relies on a hydrogen fuel cell that supplies 24 V at 50 A. However, the station’s critical sensors require 12 V and must draw 20 A continuously. The existing circuit uses a simple resistor divider to step down voltage, but the resistor dissipates 240 W—nearly double the fuel cell’s power output. Design a switching regulator topology that efficiently steps down the voltage while minimizing power loss, and determine the required duty cycle and inductance for a 50 kHz switching frequency, ensuring the output ripple remains below 1.2 V peak-to-peak.

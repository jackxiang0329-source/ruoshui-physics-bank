# HFDPO-04585

A solar thermal power plant uses molten salt (specific heat 1400 J/kg·K) stored in an insulated tank at 560°C. Due to a sudden cloud cover, the plant must divert to backup gas turbines, but the salt reservoir loses heat to the environment at a rate that causes its temperature to drop 1.2°C per hour. If the plant needs to maintain at least 500°C to operate efficiently, how long can it safely delay switching to backup before efficiency drops below acceptable levels? Assume the salt mass is 180,000 kg and heat loss is primarily via convection and radiation.

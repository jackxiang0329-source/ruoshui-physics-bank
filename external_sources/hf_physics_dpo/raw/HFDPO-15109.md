# HFDPO-15109

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar panel array to charge its battery bank during the short summer days. The system uses 12 V batteries and must deliver 150 W of power continuously to sensors and transmitters. The wiring from the solar array to the battery bank is 50 meters long, using copper wire with a cross-sectional area of 4 mm². Due to a recent storm, one of the connections has corroded, increasing its resistance by 0.5 Ω. What is the total power lost in the wiring, and what is the minimum current the solar array must supply to maintain 150 W at the load, accounting for the faulty connection?

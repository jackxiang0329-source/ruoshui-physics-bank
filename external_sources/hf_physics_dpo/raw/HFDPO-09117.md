# HFDPO-09117

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers, using resonant inductive coupling between coils embedded in the skin and the device. The implanted coil has 8 μH inductance and 0.3 Ω resistance, and is loaded with a 500 Ω resistor representing the pacemaker’s internal circuitry. To maximize power transfer efficiency, the system must be tuned such that the source sees a purely resistive impedance. What capacitance (in nF) must be added in parallel with the external coil, and what is the resulting efficiency of power transfer?

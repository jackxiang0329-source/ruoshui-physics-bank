# HFDPO-06089

During a winter power outage, a family in a remote cabin relies on a small propane heater rated at 1.2 kW to maintain a comfortable indoor temperature. The cabin has walls with a total area of 40 m² and an average thermal resistance of 0.5 K·m²/W. If the outdoor temperature is –10°C and the indoor temperature is initially 5°C, how long will it take for the heater to raise the indoor temperature to 18°C, assuming no heat loss through the floor or roof and the air inside has a heat capacity of 150 kJ/°C?

# HFDPO-11583

A biomedical engineer is designing a micro-actuator for a new drug-delivery device that uses tiny oscillating cantilevers to push medication through nanopores. The cantilever, made of silicon nitride, has a mass of 2.5 μg and a spring constant of 0.8 N/m. It must oscillate at 15 kHz to match the resonance frequency of the target tissue. However, the actual oscillation is damped due to fluid resistance, and the amplitude decays to half its initial value in 25 cycles. What is the damping coefficient, and does the system meet the required precision if the phase shift at resonance must be less than 30°?

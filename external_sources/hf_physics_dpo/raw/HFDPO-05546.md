# HFDPO-05546

A wind farm in northern Scotland uses direct-drive generators with massive permanent magnets arranged in a ring around a rotating shaft. During a storm, engineers notice unexpected voltage spikes in the power output. Each magnet has a magnetic moment of 12 A·m² and is spaced 15 cm apart along a 2.4 m diameter circle. The rotor spins at 18 RPM.

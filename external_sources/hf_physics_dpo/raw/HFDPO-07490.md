# HFDPO-07490

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat = 1500 J/kg·K) stored at 560°C to generate steam for turbines. During a 4-hour night, the plant must maintain 60 MW of output using only stored heat. Assume 90% thermal efficiency in heat-to-electric conversion.

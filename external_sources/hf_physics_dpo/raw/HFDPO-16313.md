# HFDPO-16313

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a solar panel array connected to a battery bank through a DC regulator to power sensors and a data transmitter. The solar panels generate 24 V at peak output, but the transmitter requires exactly 12 V and draws 2.5 A continuously. The system includes two identical 10 Ω resistors in parallel with the transmitter as a load, and the regulator is modeled as a variable resistor to maintain voltage stability. If the battery voltage drops to 10.5 V during a prolonged storm, what must the regulator’s resistance be to ensure the transmitter still receives 12 V across its terminals?

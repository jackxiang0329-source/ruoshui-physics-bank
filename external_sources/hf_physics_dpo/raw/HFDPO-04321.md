# HFDPO-04321

A deep-space probe’s thermal management system uses a network of thermoelectric modules (Peltier devices) to maintain payload temperature during a 3-year mission. Each module, rated at 15 W cooling power at 12 V, requires precise current control—excess current degrades efficiency and risks thermal runaway. The probe’s power bus operates at 28 V DC, and a switched-mode regulator provides variable output. However, wiring resistance between the bus and the modules introduces a 0.8 Ω drop per 10 meters in the 20-meter harness. With four identical modules connected in parallel, what is the minimum regulated output voltage needed to deliver exactly 15 W to each module, assuming constant module resistance?

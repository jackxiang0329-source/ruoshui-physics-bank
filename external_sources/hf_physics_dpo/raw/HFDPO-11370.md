# HFDPO-11370

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber notices that water boils at just 70°C in their aluminum kettle at 4,500 meters above sea level. They need to sterilize medical supplies by boiling them for 10 minutes. If the supplies require a minimum of 100°C to be safe, how much additional pressure must be applied to the kettle to raise the boiling point to 100°C, assuming the pressure is increased uniformly? The enthalpy of vaporization of water is 40.7 kJ/mol, and the ideal gas constant is 8.314 J/(mol·K).

# HFDPO-07591

A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new touch screen that uses capacitive sensing. Engineers notice the screen responds poorly in cold weather, especially when users wear thick gloves. They estimate the human finger’s capacitance to the screen is about 10 pF when touching directly, but a typical winter glove reduces this to only 1 pF. If the screen’s sensor circuit detects changes in capacitance to determine touch, how much smaller is the change in capacitance when a gloved finger touches the screen compared to a bare finger?

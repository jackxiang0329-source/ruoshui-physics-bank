# HFDPO-12486

A fusion reactor prototype uses a solenoid made of high-temperature superconducting tape (YBCO) to generate a 15 T magnetic field. The winding carries 800 A and is designed to operate at 77 K, just above the boiling point of liquid nitrogen. However, during a test, a localized quench is detected—part of the winding suddenly loses superconductivity. What is the minimum energy released as heat in that region if the inductance of the affected section is 3.2 mH? Assume the current drops rapidly to zero over 10 ms due to resistive heating.

# HFDPO-07861

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical drill hole 3.2 km deep to access hot rock formations at 250°C. To extract energy, water is pumped down the hole at 20°C and heated as it flows through fractures, turning into steam that drives turbines. If 1.5 kg of water is pumped down per second and exits as saturated steam at 300°C under 15 bar pressure, how much *net work* does the system perform per second, assuming 75% of the thermal energy transferred to the water is converted to useful work? Ignore kinetic energy changes and heat loss to the surroundings.

# HFDPO-10118

A research team is designing a new type of electromagnetic shielding for a sensitive quantum computing lab. The lab is enclosed within a hollow, conductive cylindrical shield with inner radius 1.5 m and outer radius 1.8 m, made of a material with negligible thickness. During testing, a static charge of +4.2 μC is accidentally placed inside the cavity, 0.3 m from the central axis. The team needs to determine the electric field strength at the outer surface of the shield to ensure no field leakage compromises the experiment.

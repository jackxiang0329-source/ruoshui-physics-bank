# HFDPO-01718

In a high-voltage power transmission system, a superconducting cable is buried 30 meters underground, insulated by a composite dielectric layer (ε₁ = 4.5, thickness = 2 cm) surrounding a central conductor (radius = 1.5 cm), followed by a second layer (ε₂ = 2.3, thickness = 1.5 cm) before the outer sheath. A fault causes a transient voltage surge of 50 kV lasting 200 μs. Calculate the total charge stored in the cable's parasitic capacitance during the surge, and determine the peak current that flows into the system when the surge reaches steady state, assuming the cable behaves as a distributed capacitance with inductance negligible.

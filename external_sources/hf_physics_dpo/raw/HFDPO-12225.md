# HFDPO-12225

A fusion reactor's plasma chamber is enclosed in a cylindrical vacuum vessel with a radius of 1.8 m and height 3.2 m, lined with a dielectric coating (ε_r = 4.5) to suppress surface charges. Engineers need to determine the electric field at r = 1.2 m (outside the plasma core but inside the chamber) to assess dielectric stress. What is E(r = 1.2 m) in N/C?

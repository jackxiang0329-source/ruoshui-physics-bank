# HFDPO-04516

A solar sail spacecraft, 12 meters in diameter and 0.02 mm thick, is deployed in interplanetary space at 1 AU from the Sun. The sail’s material has a thermal conductivity of 0.15 W/m·K and absorbs 85% of incident solar radiation. At equilibrium, the sail’s temperature is measured to be 310 K on the sunward side and 295 K on the shaded side. Using statistical mechanics, estimate the average number of phonons per unit volume in the sail’s material assuming a Debye model with a Debye temperature of 600 K. How does this phonon density relate to the sail’s ability to dissipate heat and maintain structural integrity?

# HFDPO-09042

A wind farm in the Arctic tundra experiences unexpected tilting of turbine towers during seasonal ice accumulation. Each 80-meter steel tower, anchored in permafrost, develops a 1.2° lean after heavy rime ice buildup (density: 900 kg/m³) covers the upper 20 meters, forming a 0.3 m thick shell. The tower’s cross-section is an H-beam with a moment of inertia of 1.4×10⁵ m⁴. Determine the horizontal displacement at the top caused by the center of gravity shift due to the asymmetric ice load, and assess whether the resulting moment exceeds the foundation’s rotational resistance (1.8×10⁸ N·m).

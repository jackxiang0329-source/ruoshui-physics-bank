# HFDPO-13532

Given that the heat diffusion length in the tape's stabilizing copper layer is 2.5 mm in 10 ms, determine the minimum thermal isolation thickness needed in the superinsulation layer to prevent adjacent coils from quenching—accounting for both heat conduction and the temperature dependence of J_c .

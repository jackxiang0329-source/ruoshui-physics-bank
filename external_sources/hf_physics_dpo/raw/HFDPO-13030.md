# HFDPO-13030

A deep-space mining station orbits a small, irregularly shaped asteroid (mass = 8.4 × 10¹⁰ kg, average radius = 1.2 km) at a distance of 3.0 km from its center. Due to the asteroid’s non-spherical mass distribution, the gravitational field varies significantly across the station’s 50 m length. The station’s orientation is fixed relative to the asteroid’s spin axis. Calculate the difference in gravitational acceleration between the station’s closest and farthest points from the asteroid’s center. Assume the asteroid’s mass is distributed in a symmetric but non-uniform shell (density varies as ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 + 0.3 sin²θ)), and treat the station as a rigid rod.

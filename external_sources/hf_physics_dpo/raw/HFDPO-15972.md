# HFDPO-15972

In a fusion energy experiment, a toroidal plasma chamber with major radius 2.5 m and minor radius 0.5 m generates a non-uniform electric field due to asymmetric charge density from ionized deuterium and tritium. At a radial distance of 2.8 m from the torus center, the electric field is measured as 1.2 × 10⁴ N/C, directed radially outward. Assuming the net charge enclosed is localized within the plasma and ignoring external fields, use Gauss’s Law to determine the total charge enclosed by a spherical Gaussian surface of radius 2.8 m. Explain why the symmetry of the system does not permit direct use of standard Gaussian surface assumptions.

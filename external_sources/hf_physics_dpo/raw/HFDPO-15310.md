# HFDPO-15310

A remote weather station in Antarctica uses a small Stirling engine powered by a propane heater to generate electricity for instruments. The engine operates between a hot reservoir at 500 K (from the heater) and a cold reservoir at 250 K (ambient air). During a severe cold snap, the ambient temperature drops to 200 K, but the heater maintains its 500 K output. If the engine's efficiency drops below 15%, the station risks losing critical data. Does it still function reliably, and what is the maximum possible efficiency under these new conditions?

# HFDPO-08982

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a complex, non-uniform plasma confinement field shaped like a toroidal solenoid with a major radius of 2.5 m and a minor cross-sectional radius of 0.3 m. Engineers observe unexpected electric field distortions near the inner wall, threatening stability. The plasma, modeled as a continuous charged fluid with a radial charge density ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−(r−R₀)²/σ²), where ρ₀ = 5 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³, R₀ = 1.2 m, and σ = 0.1 m, is enclosed within a conducting shell with inner radius 1.3 m. Determine the electric field at r = 1.25 m due to the plasma alone, using Gauss’s Law, and assess whether the field exceeds the material breakdown threshold of 3 × 10⁶ V/m.

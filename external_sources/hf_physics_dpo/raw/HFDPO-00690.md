# HFDPO-00690

A deep-space probe near the Lagrange point L2 of a binary asteroid system detects a subtle, periodic dimming of background starlight. The system consists of two asteroids, one with mass 1.2 × 10¹⁸ kg and the other 4.5 × 10¹⁷ kg, orbiting their common center of mass every 14.3 hours. The probe, weighing 230 kg, is currently stationed at L2, 1.2 × 10⁵ m from the center of mass. Calculate the net gravitational acceleration at L2 due to both asteroids and determine whether the observed dimming could be caused by a previously undetected small moonlet, 8 km in diameter with density 2.1 g/cm³, orbiting the system at a semi-major axis of 8.5 × 10⁴ m. If so, what is its orbital period?

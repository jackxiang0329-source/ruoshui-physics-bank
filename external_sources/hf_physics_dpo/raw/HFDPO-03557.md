# HFDPO-03557

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a cleanroom operator notices that tiny silica particles (diameter ~15 μm, density 2.2 g/cm³) are clinging stubbornly to wafers after an ion implantation step. The wafers are held at −200 V relative to ground, and the particles are initially neutral but become charged through triboelectric contact with the wafer surface. If the electrostatic force must overcome a maximum adhesive force of 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ N to dislodge a particle, what is the minimum charge required on a particle to be ejected by the electric field? Assume the field is uniform and approximately 4 × 10⁵ V/m near the wafer surface.

# HFDPO-10010

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical sensor array requires stable 12 V power, but the primary solar panel system (rated at 18 V, 5 A) experiences frequent voltage spikes during sudden cloud cover transitions due to rapid changes in sunlight intensity. Engineers install a flyback diode and a 100 μF capacitor across the load, yet the sensor data shows inconsistent readings during transients. Given that the sensor load draws 2 A fluctuating between 1.5 A and 2.5 A every 100 ms, and the wiring resistance between the panel and load is 0.4 Ω, determine the minimum capacitance needed to limit voltage sag below 10% during the worst-case current surge—assuming the capacitor discharges through the load and the panel output drops to zero momentarily.

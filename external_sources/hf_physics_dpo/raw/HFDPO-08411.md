# HFDPO-08411

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic uses a battery bank charged by photovoltaic panels, but during a prolonged polar night, the system relies solely on stored energy. The station’s control circuitry requires exactly 5.0 V and 20 mA to operate, but the batteries degrade over time, causing their internal resistance to rise unpredictably. At -40°C, one battery’s terminal voltage drops to 4.8 V with an internal resistance of 2.5 Ω. Design a voltage divider using two resistors (one fixed, one variable) to stabilize the output voltage to 5.0 V under load, ensuring the control circuit draws current without dropping below 4.9 V, while minimizing power loss.

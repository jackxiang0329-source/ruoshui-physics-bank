# HFDPO-00361

A deep-space probe transmitting at 8.4 GHz encounters a plasma cloud near Jupiter's magnetosphere, causing signal attenuation and phase distortion. The cloud’s electron density varies spatially as n_e(r) = 10^{12} left(1 + e^{-r/500,text{km}}right) , text{m}^{-3} , where r is radial distance from the cloud’s center. The signal’s initial polarization is linear, but upon exiting the cloud, it exhibits elliptical polarization. Determine the minimum beam width required at entry to ensure the received signal maintains usable coherence for data recovery, given that coherence is lost if the phase variation across the beam exceeds pi/2 radians.

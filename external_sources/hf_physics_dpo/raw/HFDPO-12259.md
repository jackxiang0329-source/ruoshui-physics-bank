# HFDPO-12259

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V DC battery bank (internal resistance 0.15 Ω) powers a data logger and a 150 W solar tracking motor. The motor draws 12 A at full load but draws only 0.8 A when idle. During a test, the logger shows erratic readings when the motor starts. Measurements reveal the battery voltage drops from 12.0 V to 10.3 V during startup. A 50 m copper wire run connects the battery to the motor, but the wiring gauge is missing. Determine the minimum wire gauge (AWG) needed to limit voltage drop to ≤0.5 V during motor startup, assuming the wire resistivity is 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m.

# HFDPO-09087

A lunar outpost’s solar panel array, spanning 12 meters in length and made of a lightweight composite material with uniform density, is mounted on a pivot at one end to track the Sun. During a dust storm, wind forces (modeled as a distributed load of 50 N/m at peak intensity) act perpendicular to the panel, while the panel itself exerts a downward force due to gravity. The panel is held in equilibrium by a single hydraulic strut attached 3 meters from the pivot, angled at 60° above horizontal. Calculate the minimum force the strut must exert to prevent the panel from rotating.

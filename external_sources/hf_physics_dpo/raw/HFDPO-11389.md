# HFDPO-11389

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal region prone to strong gusts. The 12-kg unit is attached to a rigid, 2-meter-long aluminum arm that extends horizontally from a vertical pole. A 5-kg counterweight is mounted 0.8 m from the pole at the opposite end of the arm to prevent tipping. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 200 N at the weather station’s center of mass, 1.8 m from the pole. Determine if the system remains stable, and if not, where the counterweight must be repositioned (from the pole) to restore equilibrium. Assume the pole is fixed at its base and the arm is massless.

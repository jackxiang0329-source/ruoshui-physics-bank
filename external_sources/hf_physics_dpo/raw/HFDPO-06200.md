# HFDPO-06200

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor deforestation in the Amazon must maintain a hovering altitude of 150 m above a remote forest canopy for 8 hours. The drone has a mass of 12 kg and uses a single electric motor with 88% efficiency. During operation, air resistance exerts a drag force proportional to the square of the rotor’s velocity. If the drone must sustain a lift force exactly equal to its weight, what is the minimum total energy input required from the solar panels over 8 hours, assuming the system operates under ideal atmospheric conditions? (Note: Assume no energy is stored; all energy must be supplied in real time.)

# HFDPO-15510

A wind turbine blade in a remote Arctic installation begins showing micro-fractures near the root after 15 years of operation. Assuming the blade is statically modeled as a cantilevered beam fixed at the hub, evaluate whether the maximum internal bending moment at the root exceeds the material’s fatigue-limited moment capacity (2.1 × 10⁶ N·m) under worst-case thermal gradient and loading conditions.

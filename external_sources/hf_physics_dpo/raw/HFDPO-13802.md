# HFDPO-13802

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain village uses a 12V battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During a week of persistent cloud cover, the system's only power source is a 24V battery backup. To prevent damage to the 12V systems, a voltage divider made of two resistors (R₁ and R₂) is used to step down the 24V to a safe 12V for the control electronics. If the control system draws 1.5A when active and R₁ = 20Ω, what should R₂ be so that the output voltage remains 12V under load?

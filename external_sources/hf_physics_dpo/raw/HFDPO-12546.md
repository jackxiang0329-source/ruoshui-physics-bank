# HFDPO-12546

In a remote Antarctic research station, a solar array with 12 identical photovoltaic panels (each rated at 150 W, 24 V open-circuit) is connected in series to charge a 48 V battery bank. However, after a week of continuous sub-zero temperatures and heavy snowfall, the system performs poorly. Measurements show the battery voltage is only 42 V after 6 hours of charging, and a multimeter across one panel shows just 1.8 V under load. The team suspects panel degradation or wiring faults, but no visible damage exists. Using only the station's voltmeter and a known 10 Ω resistor, devise a method to determine whether the fault lies in a single panel’s internal resistance or in a high-resistance connection in the series string.

# HFDPO-00640

In a remote solar-powered research station, a 12 V battery bank is connected to a variable load through a 5 km copper transmission line (resistance 0.003 Ω/m). To monitor energy efficiency, an engineer installs a shunt resistor (0.1 Ω) in series with the load to measure current, but notices a persistent 1.2 V voltage drop across the shunt—despite the load drawing only 8 A on paper. The station's data logger records the battery voltage as 11.8 V and the load voltage as 10.5 V. What is the actual power loss in the transmission lines, and what is the true current flowing through the circuit?

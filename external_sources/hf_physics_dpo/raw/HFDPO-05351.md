# HFDPO-05351

A spacecraft’s thermal control system uses a sealed, rigid chamber filled with argon gas to regulate internal temperature during deep-space transit. The chamber, 1.2 m³ in volume, initially holds gas at 300 K and 1.8 atm. During a solar flare event, the gas is heated to 420 K. Engineers must determine whether the pressure exceeds the chamber’s 2.5 atm safety limit. However, the heating process is non-uniform—some regions absorb more energy due to radiation asymmetry. Calculate the final pressure assuming ideal behavior, then evaluate how non-uniform heating might affect the validity of this assumption.

# HFDPO-15133

During a prolonged solar flare event, a deep-space satellite’s radiation shielding (15 cm of polyethylene) experiences elevated neutron flux from secondary interactions. The neutron energy spectrum follows a Maxwellian distribution with an effective temperature of 40 keV. Calculate the effective neutron-induced damage rate in the satellite’s onboard electronics, accounting for both elastic scattering and (n,α) reactions in boron-10 traces (0.01% abundance) in the shielding material. Assume a total neutron flux of 3.2 × 10⁸ cm⁻²·s⁻¹ and a 30% detection efficiency of the damage monitoring system.

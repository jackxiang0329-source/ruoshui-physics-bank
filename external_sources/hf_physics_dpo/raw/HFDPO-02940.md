# HFDPO-02940

A quantum dot display manufacturer notices that devices using cadmium selenide (CdSe) nanocrystals emit inconsistent green light under varying temperatures. The peak emission wavelength shifts from 530 nm at 25°C to 542 nm at 60°C. Explain this shift using atomic structure principles, and calculate the change in energy level spacing (in eV) due to thermal expansion. Assume the effective mass of electrons and holes remains constant, but the confinement potential changes due to increased dot radius. Use the infinite square well model and data: initial dot radius = 3.2 nm, α = 0.00025 K⁻¹ (coefficient of thermal expansion), and ħ²/(2mₑ) ≈ 3.81 eV·nm².

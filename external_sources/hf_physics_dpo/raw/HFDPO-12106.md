# HFDPO-12106

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The receiver coil inside the pacemaker has 2.0 Ω resistance and 12 μH inductance. The system must deliver 1.5 W of average power to the receiver with a power factor greater than 0.95. What capacitance should be added in series with the receiver coil to achieve optimal power transfer and acceptable power factor?

# HFDPO-05760

A coastal research team deploys a submerged hydrokinetic turbine array in a tidal channel where water flows at 3.2 m/s at the surface, but the velocity decreases to 1.8 m/s near the seabed due to viscous drag. The turbines are designed to extract energy from the flow, but engineers notice unexpected pressure fluctuations that threaten structural integrity. Then, explain why the actual pressure difference may differ from the calculated value and how this affects turbine efficiency.

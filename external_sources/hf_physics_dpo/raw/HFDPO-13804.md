# HFDPO-13804

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable neural interface using flexible microelectrodes embedded in a polymer substrate. The electrodes are arranged in a serpentine pattern with a 2 mm gap between adjacent strips, each 50 μm wide, and spaced 100 μm apart across a 3 cm × 3 cm sensing area. The polymer dielectric has a relative permittivity of 5.2 and thickness of 15 μm. During operation, the device experiences transient voltage swings up to 30 V due to biological noise. The engineer must determine the total capacitance between adjacent electrode strips while accounting for fringing effects and non-uniform field distribution. To prevent signal distortion, the total energy stored during peak voltage must not exceed 2.5 μJ. Is the design safe?

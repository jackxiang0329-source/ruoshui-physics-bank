# HFDPO-08870

A deep-space telescope array on a lunar base uses a primary mirror segmented into 12 identical parabolic facets, each with a focal length of 8.5 m. Due to thermal expansion during a lunar day, one facet shifts 0.3 mm axially and tilts 0.04° from its design orientation. Astronomers detect a smeared image of a distant quasar, with a 1.2 arcsecond FWHM blur. Determine the combined effect of axial shift and tilt on wavefront error, and calculate the required corrective phase delay (in nanoseconds) at the array’s interferometric receiver to restore diffraction-limited resolution. Assume the observing wavelength is 550 nm.

# HFDPO-00275

A rooftop solar panel array in a coastal town is secured with brackets that must withstand strong crosswinds. During a storm, the panel experiences a horizontal wind force of 320 N acting 1.8 m above the base. If the bracket is rigidly fixed to the roof and the panel’s center of mass is 0.9 m above the base, what minimum vertical force must the bracket apply downward at the base to prevent tipping?

# HFDPO-06521

A medical imaging lab uses a resonant circuit in an MRI machine’s RF coil to detect weak signals from hydrogen nuclei. The coil has an inductance of 2.5 μH and a variable capacitance. During a calibration run, engineers notice the signal amplitude drops sharply when the frequency shifts by just 1.2 MHz from the intended 63.8 MHz. They suspect parasitic resistance in the coil is causing excessive damping. What is the effective resistance of the coil if the current drops to 70% of its peak value at this frequency shift?

# HFDPO-03407

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 320°C, which then transfers heat to water in a closed-loop system to generate steam for turbines. During a particularly hot day, the system's heat exchanger becomes partially fouled, reducing its efficiency. Engineers measure that 1.2 MW of thermal power is delivered to the water, but the oil enters at 320°C and exits at 280°C. Assuming the water enters at 70°C and exits as saturated steam at 150 kPa, calculate the minimum possible mass flow rate of water required to achieve this energy transfer, and discuss whether the observed efficiency drop could be due to irreversibilities in the heat exchange process.

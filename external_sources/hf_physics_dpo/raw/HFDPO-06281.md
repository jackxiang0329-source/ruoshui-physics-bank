# HFDPO-06281

A Mars rover navigation team must decide whether to deploy a new low-friction, self-cleaning wheel design for a 300-meter traverse across a regolith field with 15° inclines and sudden dust devils. The wheels have a rolling resistance coefficient of 0.025, but dust accumulation increases effective drag exponentially: F_d = 0.3v² + 0.02v⁴ N, where v is in m/s. The rover’s 12 kg mass is distributed unevenly, causing dynamic shifts in the normal force during motion. Determine the minimum power required to maintain 0.5 m/s average speed across the terrain, accounting for both static friction limits (μ_s = 0.3) and aerodynamic drag from 10 m/s wind gusts.

# HFDPO-12751

During a heatwave, a solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store energy for overnight electricity generation. The salt, initially at 550°C, is stored in an insulated tank. A sudden drop in solar input causes the salt to cool to 450°C over 3 hours. Engineers notice that the stored energy output is lower than expected. Could the actual heat loss be greater than estimated? The salt has a specific heat capacity of 1,400 J/kg·K, and the tank holds 12,000 kg of salt.

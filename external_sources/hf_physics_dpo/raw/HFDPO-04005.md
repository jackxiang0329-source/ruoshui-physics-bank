# HFDPO-04005

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm-diameter silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of −8.0 μC/m² is suspended electrostatically between two parallel electrode plates separated by 3.0 cm. Due to uneven charge distribution during plasma etching, a localized region of the wafer develops a higher charge density of −12.0 μC/m² over a 2.0 cm diameter circular area. Estimate the net electrostatic force (magnitude and direction) this charge imbalance exerts on the rest of the wafer, assuming the wafer’s total charge remains unchanged and the system is in equilibrium.

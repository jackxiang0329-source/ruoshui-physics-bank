# HFDPO-03035

A medical device engineer is designing a next-generation extracorporeal membrane oxygenation (ECMO) circuit for neonatal patients. The system uses a centrifugal pump to circulate blood through a silicone membrane oxygenator. During testing, the team observes unexplained pressure drops and localized turbulence near the inlet manifold, causing microbubble formation. The inlet has a diameter of 12 mm and transitions smoothly into a 6 mm diameter delivery tube. Blood flows at 0.8 m/s in the inlet, with density 1060 kg/m³ and viscosity 3.5 × 10⁻³ Pa·s. The pump maintains a total pressure of 18 kPa at the inlet. Estimate the pressure at the narrow section and determine whether cavitation (vapor bubble formation) is likely, given the blood’s vapor pressure is 1.8 kPa.

# HFDPO-08610

A fusion reactor containment vessel ruptures during a plasma instability event, ejecting 1.2 kg of ionized deuterium plasma at 4.5 × 10⁶ m/s into a vacuum chamber lined with magnetic baffles. The plasma spreads radially, colliding with the walls over 3.8 ms and transferring momentum via electromagnetic impulses. If the total impulse on the vessel wall is measured at 1.8 × 10⁴ N·s, determine whether momentum was conserved in the system—including the vessel, plasma, and magnetic field interactions—given that the vessel was initially at rest and the chamber is isolated.

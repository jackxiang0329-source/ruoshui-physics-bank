# HFDPO-00548

A NASA CubeSat mission to study Jupiter’s moon Io must perform a series of gravity assists using Europa and Ganymede to reach a stable retrograde orbit around Io. The spacecraft arrives at Europa’s orbit with a hyperbolic excess velocity of 4.6 km/s at an approach angle of 120° relative to Europa’s orbital velocity vector. Europa orbits Jupiter at 13.7 km/s. Determine the required orbital inclination of the final Io orbit such that the spacecraft’s post-assist trajectory intersects Io’s orbit at a point where Io’s position is within 200 km of the spacecraft’s path at flyby—accounting for both orbital dynamics and time-of-flight delays due to Jupiter's gravitational time dilation (measured at 2.1×10⁻⁶ s per orbit).

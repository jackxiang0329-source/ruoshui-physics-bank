# HFDPO-00191

A research team is designing a compact MRI-compatible robotic arm for minimally invasive surgery. The arm uses a small permanent magnet with a dipole moment of 0.45 A·m² to align with the uniform 1.5 T magnetic field of the MRI scanner. The magnet is mounted on a frictionless pivot at one end of a 12 cm rigid arm. If the magnet initially points 30° away from the field direction, what is the magnitude of the torque that tends to align it with the field, and how much work is done by this torque when the magnet rotates to align fully with the field?

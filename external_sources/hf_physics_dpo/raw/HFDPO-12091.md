# HFDPO-12091

A lunar mining outpost uses a heat-pipe radiator to reject waste heat from its fusion reactor, but after two years of operation, the thermal conductivity of the wick structure has degraded due to micro-meteorite impacts and sintering. What is the new steady-state temperature difference between the evaporator and condenser, and what are the implications for the reactor’s thermal safety margin?

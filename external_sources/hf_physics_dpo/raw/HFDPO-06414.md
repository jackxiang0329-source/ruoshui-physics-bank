# HFDPO-06414

A marine biologist studying whale communication in the North Atlantic detects a series of low-frequency pulses (18 Hz) from a humpback whale, but the signal degrades significantly when passing through a layer of warm water (25°C) overlain by cold water (5°C) with a sharp thermocline 30 m below the surface. Using the refraction of sound in layered media, determine the critical angle beyond which the sound ray is fully reflected back into the cold layer. Assume adiabatic sound speed variation: c(z) = 1450 + 4z m/s in cold water and c(z) = 1490 + 2z m/s in warm water. Is the whale's call likely to reach a hydrophone 100 m from the source, 40 m beneath the thermocline?

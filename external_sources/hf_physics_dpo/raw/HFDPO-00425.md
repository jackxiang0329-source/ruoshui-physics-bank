# HFDPO-00425

A wind farm in northern Scotland uses large, slow-turning turbines with 120-m diameter blades to generate electricity. During a sudden storm, the wind speed increases from 10 m/s to 25 m/s in 3 seconds, causing the turbine’s rotational speed to accelerate. The generator uses a rotating coil with 200 turns, 1.5 m² area, rotating in a uniform 0.4 T magnetic field. The coil’s resistance is 15 Ω. What is the average induced emf in the coil during the acceleration, and what is the peak power dissipated in the coil right after the wind surge?

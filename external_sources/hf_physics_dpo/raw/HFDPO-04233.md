# HFDPO-04233

A remote desert research station relies on a solar-powered heat engine to generate electricity, using a parabolic dish to focus sunlight onto a thermal receiver at 600°C. The engine rejects heat to the surrounding air at 30°C. What is the minimum rate of solar energy input (in kW) required to sustain this output, and how does the actual efficiency compare to the Carnot limit?

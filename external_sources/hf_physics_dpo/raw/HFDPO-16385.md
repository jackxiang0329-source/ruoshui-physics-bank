# HFDPO-16385

A geophysicist studying glacial rebound in post-Ice Age Scandinavia models the Earth's crust as a viscoelastic layer under a decaying load from melting ice sheets. Given a bulk modulus of 1.2 × 10¹¹ Pa, shear modulus of 3.0 × 10¹⁰ Pa, and a relaxation time of 3000 years, determine whether the observed uplift rate is consistent with standard viscoelastic models assuming a linear stress–strain relationship and exponential relaxation.

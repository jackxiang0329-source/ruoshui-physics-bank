# HFDPO-03558

In a next-generation quantum memory array, 1000 trapped ions are cooled to 100 pK using laser cooling, but thermal fluctuations from residual blackbody radiation cause decoherence. The ions are arranged in a 1D chain with periodic boundary conditions, and each ion can occupy one of two internal states (|0⟩ or |1⟩), forming a quantum spin chain. The interaction energy between neighboring ions is J = 1.5 μeV, and the system is coupled to a thermal bath at T = 100 pK. What is the probability that the system is in a state with 500 |1⟩ and 500 |0⟩ spins, and how does this probability compare to the most probable configuration under the microcanonical ensemble? What does this imply about the coherence lifetime of the quantum memory?

# HFDPO-02531

A wind turbine blade in a coastal wind farm is designed with a curved airfoil shape, but engineers notice that during high winds (15 m/s), turbulence near the blade tips reduces efficiency by 18%. The blade has a chord length of 2.4 m and spans 50 m. Using Bernoulli’s principle, determine the pressure difference between the upper and lower surfaces of the blade near the tip, assuming the air speed above the blade reaches 22 m/s while below it remains at 15 m/s. How does this pressure difference contribute to lift and turbulence?

# HFDPO-05582

A coastal engineering team is evaluating two designs for a breakwater to reduce wave erosion at a vulnerable shoreline. One design uses vertical concrete walls (reflective), while the other uses a sloped gravel structure (dissipative). Waves with a wavelength of 12 meters and a speed of 6 m/s approach the shore at a 30° angle. The reflective wall causes significant wave reflection, creating standing waves that amplify erosion at the base. The team needs to calculate the frequency of the incoming waves and determine the phase difference between incident and reflected waves at the wall to assess the standing wave amplitude. They must also compare how much energy is retained in the reflected wave versus dissipated in the sloped design, assuming 20% energy loss per reflection.

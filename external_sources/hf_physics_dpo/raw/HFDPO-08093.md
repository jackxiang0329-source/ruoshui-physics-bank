# HFDPO-08093

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must navigate a narrow canyon where wind gusts can push it sideways at up to 3.2 m/s. If the drone’s intended flight path is 120 m long, straight and level, and it flies at a constant airspeed of 14 m/s relative to the air, what angle (relative to the canyon’s centerline) must the pilot steer to counteract the wind and land precisely at the target? Assume the wind is perpendicular to the flight path and constant.

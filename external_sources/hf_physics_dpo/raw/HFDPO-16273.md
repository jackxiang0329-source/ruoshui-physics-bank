# HFDPO-16273

A fusion reactor design team must optimize neutron shielding for a tokamak's blanket region, where 14 MeV neutrons from D-T reactions penetrate at a flux of 5 × 10¹⁴ n/cm²/s. The blanket uses a layered structure of lithium-6 and beryllium-9, each with different neutron moderation and absorption cross-sections. The team aims to minimize radiation damage while maximizing tritium breeding. Determine the optimal thickness ratio of Li-6 to Be-9 that maximizes tritium yield per unit shielding mass, considering neutron scattering, capture, and energy degradation.

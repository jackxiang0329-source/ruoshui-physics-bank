# HFDPO-09531

A high-altitude solar-powered drone, designed to patrol the stratosphere for 30 days, relies on a radiative cooling system to manage heat from its onboard electronics. At 20 km altitude, the ambient temperature is –60°C, and the drone’s electronics generate 45 W of heat. The cooling surface (emissivity 0.92) is a 0.8 m² blackened aluminum panel exposed to space. However, the drone must also maintain a stable internal temperature for sensitive sensors. What is the steady-state surface temperature of the cooling panel, and how would the temperature change if the panel were partially shaded by a wing in a 30° solar incidence angle, reducing effective radiation area to 0.5 m² and altering the net heat balance?

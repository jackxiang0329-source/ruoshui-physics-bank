# HFDPO-07742

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day, enabling electricity generation after sunset. The storage system holds 1,200,000 kg of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1,400 J/(kg·K). At peak operation, the salt is heated to 560°C, but ambient conditions cause a steady heat loss of 120 kW over 8 hours while not in use. If the plant needs at least 480°C to operate efficiently, will the stored heat be sufficient to maintain operational temperature overnight?

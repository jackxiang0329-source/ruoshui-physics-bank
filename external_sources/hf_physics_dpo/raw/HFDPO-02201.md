# HFDPO-02201

A Mars rover wheel embedded in a regolith layer composed of fine basaltic dust (density 3200 kg/m³, cohesion 120 Pa) experiences unexpected lateral slippage during ascent on a 25° incline. The wheel (diameter 50 cm, mass 18 kg) applies 350 N of normal force per wheel, yet traction failure occurs at just 4.2 m/s forward speed. Estimate the effective coefficient of rolling resistance and determine whether this is dominated by hysteretic energy loss in the soil or by dynamic fluid drag from dust ejection at high velocity. Use data from the 2023 Ingenuity-2 soil simulation tests showing dust clouds form at Reynolds numbers >1500 around moving wheels.

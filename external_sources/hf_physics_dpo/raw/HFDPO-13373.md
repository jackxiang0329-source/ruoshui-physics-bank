# HFDPO-13373

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain area uses a 12 V battery charged by a photovoltaic panel. The panel outputs 18 V under full sun but only 6 V in partial cloud cover. To protect the battery, a voltage regulator circuit with a series resistor is used to drop excess voltage. If the battery charges at 2 A when the panel is at 18 V, what resistance should the series resistor have to limit current to 2 A during peak sun, and how much power is dissipated in the resistor under these conditions?

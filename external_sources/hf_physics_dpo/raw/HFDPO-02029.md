# HFDPO-02029

A deep-sea mining drone descends at constant speed through 3,200 meters of seawater to extract polymetallic nodules from the ocean floor. The drone's buoyancy system must counteract a net upward force of 18,500 N due to displaced water, while its thrusters provide 22,000 N of downward thrust. During descent, 15% of the energy used by the thrusters is lost to viscous drag and turbulence. If the drone performs 4.2 megajoules of useful work extracting nodules, what is the total energy input from the battery required for the entire 8-hour mission?

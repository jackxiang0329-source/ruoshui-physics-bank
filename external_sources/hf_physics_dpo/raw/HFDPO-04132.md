# HFDPO-04132

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a 12 V battery bank charged by photovoltaic panels during the day. The system must power a 500 W water pump for 4 hours each evening, but the battery’s voltage drops to 10.8 V when nearly depleted. If the pump’s resistance is fixed at 2.5 Ω, and the wiring between the battery and pump has a total resistance of 0.3 Ω, what is the actual power delivered to the pump when the battery is at 10.8 V?

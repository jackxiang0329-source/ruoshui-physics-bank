# HFDPO-13857

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a silicon photodiode to measure sunlight intensity. The diode generates 2.5 mA of photocurrent under full sunlight (1000 W/m²). If the station’s battery needs 100 mA·h of charge per day and sunlight lasts 8 hours, what is the minimum effective area of the photodiode needed to meet this energy demand? Assume 100% efficiency in converting photons to electrons and that the diode’s output is directly proportional to light intensity.

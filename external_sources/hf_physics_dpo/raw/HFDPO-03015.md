# HFDPO-03015

A lunar greenhouse on the Moon's equator uses a closed-loop thermal regulation system with phase-change materials (PCMs) to stabilize temperatures for crop growth. During the 14-Earth-day lunar day, solar irradiance reaches 1360 W/m², but the system must maintain an internal temperature of 25°C while rejecting heat through radiative panels with 0.95 emissivity. The PCM (melting point 28°C, latent heat 220 kJ/kg) is stored in 500 kg of encapsulated material. If the greenhouse gains 120 kW of heat from solar input and internal sources but loses heat only via radiation, how much PCM melts during the day, and what is the final temperature of the system upon sunrise? Assume no heat transfer through structural materials and perfect thermal contact with PCM.

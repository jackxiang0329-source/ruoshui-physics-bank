# HFDPO-05495

A wind turbine blade, 60 meters long and shaped like a thin, tapered cantilever, is mounted on a tower with a 15° pitch angle. During a storm, turbulent wind exerts a non-uniform distributed load along the blade's length, approximated by w(x) = 1200 sinleft(frac{pi x}{60}right) N/m, where x is distance from the root. The blade’s mass per unit length varies as mu(x) = 20 + 0.3x kg/m. Determine the magnitude and location of the maximum bending moment in the blade root section under these conditions, assuming no aerodynamic lift.

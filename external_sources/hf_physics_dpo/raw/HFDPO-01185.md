# HFDPO-01185

A solar thermal power plant in Nevada uses molten salt to store heat from concentrated sunlight, aiming to generate electricity even after sunset. The system stores 5.2 GJ of thermal energy at 560°C in a 75,000 kg tank of sodium nitrate-based salt (specific heat capacity: 1.5 kJ/kg·K). Over 6 hours, the tank loses heat to the environment at an average rate of 1.8 MW due to imperfect insulation. If the plant must maintain a minimum operating temperature of 480°C to drive its turbines efficiently, determine whether the stored energy will be sufficient to sustain operations through the night.

# HFDPO-02422

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a small Stirling engine to generate power from geothermal heat flowing up through permafrost. The engine operates between a heat source at 35°C (from underground) and a cold sink at -10°C (ambient air), absorbing 150 W of heat. Due to ice buildup on the cold side, the heat sink temperature gradually rises. If the engine must maintain at least 8% efficiency to power critical sensors, what is the maximum allowable sink temperature before power generation fails?

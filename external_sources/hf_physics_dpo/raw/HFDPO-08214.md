# HFDPO-08214

A team of researchers is developing a new quantum sensor to detect faint magnetic fields from brain activity. They use a nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center in diamond, which can exist in two spin states: |↑⟩ (spin-up, energy 0 eV) and |↓⟩ (spin-down, energy 0.29 eV). When exposed to a 2.9 GHz microwave field, the system undergoes resonance. The sensor fails if the microwave frequency drifts more than 100 MHz from resonance. What is the maximum allowed deviation in energy (in eV) between the two spin states for reliable operation?

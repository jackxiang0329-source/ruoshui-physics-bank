# HFDPO-02988

In a lunar terraforming research station, a modular power grid integrates solar arrays and regenerative fuel cells across three interconnected habitat domes. During a solar storm, voltage fluctuations cause unpredictable current shifts. Dome A (12 kV, 80 Ω internal resistance) supplies 6.2 A to a shared load, while Dome B (9 kV, 60 Ω) and Dome C (15 kV, 100 Ω) are in parallel with a 25 Ω load between them. Find the current through the shared load and the power dissipated in Dome C’s regulator, using only Kirchhoff’s laws.

# HFDPO-08043

In a remote weather station, a solar-powered microcontroller intermittently resets during sudden cloud cover. The system uses a silicon photodiode (bandgap 1.1 eV) to monitor sunlight intensity, but the output current drops below 1 μA when light levels fall. Engineering logs show the reset occurs when carrier recombination time exceeds the system’s minimum operational delay. Given the diode has a dark current of 50 nA and operates at 25°C, determine the minimum photon flux (photons/m²/s) needed to sustain a photocurrent just above 1 μA, assuming 90% internal quantum efficiency and no reflection losses.

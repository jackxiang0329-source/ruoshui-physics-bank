# HFDPO-01252

A wind turbine farm in coastal Maine experiences sudden gusts during storm season, causing blade rotational speeds to spike. Engineers notice that after a 10-second burst of wind at 25 m/s, the turbine’s angular velocity increases from 15 rad/s to 22 rad/s. The blades have a total moment of inertia of 1.8 × 10⁵ kg·m². If the turbine’s generator must not exceed a maximum torque of 1.5 × 10⁶ N·m, determine whether the system's angular momentum is conserved during the gust, and explain how the turbine’s design must account for energy transfer despite the apparent violation.

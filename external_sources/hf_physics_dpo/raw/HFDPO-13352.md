# HFDPO-13352

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a high-resolution camera uses a custom fish-eye lens to survey a hydrothermal vent field at 2,500 meters depth. Due to extreme pressure, the lens’s refractive index shifts from 1.50 (at surface) to 1.58. The lens has a fixed focal length of 12 cm in air, but the optical path through the water-lens interface alters image focus. The vent emits infrared radiation at 1.55 μm, detected by a silicon photodetector with a circular aperture of 4.0 mm. Determine whether the system can resolve two adjacent sulfide chimneys separated by 0.85 m at the vent site, given that the camera's effective numerical aperture in water must account for the refractive index change and spherical aberration.

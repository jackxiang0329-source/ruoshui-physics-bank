# HFDPO-15650

A solar-powered drone designed to hover at 10,000 meters altitude must maintain stability against strong vertical wind gusts. The drone’s center of mass is offset 0.3 m from its center of lift due to asymmetric battery placement. The lift vector is 120 N, acting at the wing’s aerodynamic center, while a 15 N·m counter-torque is generated by the propeller torque reaction. Determine the required shift in a movable counterweight (mass = 2 kg) along the fuselage to restore static equilibrium under a 20 m/s wind gust creating a 18 N·m overturning moment.

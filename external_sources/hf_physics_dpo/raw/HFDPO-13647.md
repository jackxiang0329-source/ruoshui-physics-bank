# HFDPO-13647

A deep-sea research team deploys a fiber-optic tethered drone equipped with a 1550 nm laser communication system to transmit high-resolution sonar data from 800 m depth. Due to seawater's high absorption and scattering, signal loss exceeds 20 dB/km—nearly 1.6 dB per 100 m—while the drone's laser has a 1.2 mW output and 3 mm beam divergence. The surface receiver has a 25 cm diameter photodetector with 85% quantum efficiency. Calculate the minimum power received at the surface, considering attenuation, beam spreading, and partial collection. Can the system sustain a 10 Mbps data stream if the receiver requires at least 0.1 nW for reliable detection?

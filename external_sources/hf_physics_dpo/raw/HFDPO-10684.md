# HFDPO-10684

A solar-powered weather balloon, carrying a 12 kg scientific payload, ascends through the stratosphere. At 30 km altitude, it reaches equilibrium with a tether line wrapped around a rotating drum on the ground, held by a motor with variable torque. The drum has a 0.6 m radius and is mounted on a cantilevered steel arm 2.5 m long, fixed to a concrete base. The tether makes a 15° angle with the horizontal at the drum, and the arm’s centerline is tilted 10° upward from horizontal due to wind load. The arm’s cross-section is rectangular (8 cm × 15 cm), and the material has a yield stress of 250 MPa. Determine the minimum motor torque required to prevent arm buckling, given that the arm is braced at mid-length by a diagonal strut at 45°.

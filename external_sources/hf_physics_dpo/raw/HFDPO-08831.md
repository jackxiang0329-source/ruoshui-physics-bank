# HFDPO-08831

A wind turbine designed for urban rooftops uses a Venturi-shaped duct to accelerate airflow into the blades. The duct narrows from 2.0 m to 1.2 m in diameter. On a windy day, the wind speed at the entrance is 8.0 m/s, and measurements show the pressure at the narrowest section drops by 150 Pa compared to atmospheric pressure. Is the duct design effectively increasing kinetic energy at the blades, or is this pressure drop likely due to friction and turbulence? Use Bernoulli’s principle to assess the expected pressure drop and evaluate the efficiency of the design.

# HFDPO-03249

In a remote off-grid solar microgrid, a 50 Hz inverter outputs a distorted voltage waveform due to a nonlinear load (a battery charger with rectifier stage). The voltage has a fundamental component of 230 V RMS at 50 Hz and a significant 3rd harmonic (32 V RMS at 150 Hz). The grid’s wiring has a distributed inductance of 2.1 mH/km and capacitance of 120 nF/km. A 3.6 km feeder line connects the inverter to a remote residential load. At the load end, the voltage harmonic distortion causes a sensitive medical device (with a 50 Ω resistive load and 500 mH inductance) to malfunction. Determine whether the 3rd harmonic voltage at the load exceeds 10% of the fundamental RMS voltage, and explain why this might trigger the device failure.

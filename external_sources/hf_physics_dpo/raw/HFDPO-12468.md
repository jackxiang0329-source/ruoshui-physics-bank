# HFDPO-12468

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where charged particles are confined by magnetic fields. During a test, a 0.5 kg plasma blob with initial velocity 300 m/s collides elastically with a stationary 0.2 kg magnetic shield plate, which is part of a movable containment arm. After impact, the shield moves at 250 m/s along the axis of confinement. Calculate the kinetic energy lost to electromagnetic radiation and turbulence, and evaluate whether the energy loss is consistent with energy and momentum conservation under realistic plasma interactions.

# HFDPO-05826

A quantum imaging lab develops a single-photon camera using time-correlated single-photon counting (TCSPC) to capture ultra-weak fluorescence from a cancer biomarker in a patient’s tissue. The team proposes using a pulsed laser (λ = 780 nm, pulse width = 80 ps) with a temporal resolution of 10 ps, but the coherence time of the laser is only 200 ps. Design a method to optimize the detection efficiency while minimizing false positives from background noise and temporal dispersion, without increasing laser power.

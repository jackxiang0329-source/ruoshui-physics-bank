# HFDPO-00345

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal magnetic confinement system with a major radius of 2.5 m and a minor radius of 0.6 m. The plasma current is 15 MA, and the magnetic field on the axis of the torus must be precisely 12 T to maintain stability. However, during testing, the measured field at the center of the plasma (60 cm from the toroidal axis) is only 11.2 T. The design team suspects non-uniform current distribution in the toroidal field coils. Determine the magnitude and direction of the net magnetic dipole moment contribution from the coil's current distribution that could explain this discrepancy.

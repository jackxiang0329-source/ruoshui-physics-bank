# HFDPO-08020

A laser-equipped autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) is tasked with mapping a submerged volcanic vent at 120 meters depth, where light from the surface is severely distorted by thermoclines—layered water strata with varying refractive indices. The AUV emits a narrow beam at 20° from vertical, but due to a sudden 3°C temperature gradient, the beam bends unpredictably upon entering a 10-meter-thick layer with n = 1.332 (vs. 1.330 above). The vehicle’s sensors detect the beam hitting the seafloor 4.7 meters from its projected straight path.

# HFDPO-02954

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable cardiac monitor that uses a flexible capacitive sensor to detect subtle chest movements during breathing and heartbeat. The sensor consists of two parallel conducting layers separated by a 50 µm thick biocompatible polymer dielectric (κ = 3.5). The device must maintain a minimum capacitance of 120 pF while bending around a 5 cm radius. When bent, the effective area decreases by 15%. What initial area (in cm²) must the sensor have before bending to meet the capacitance requirement?

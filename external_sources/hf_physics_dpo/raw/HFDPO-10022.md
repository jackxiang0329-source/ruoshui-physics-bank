# HFDPO-10022

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 10 cm × 10 cm silicon wafer is charged to −3.2 μC after being exposed to an ion beam. A technician notices that the wafer attracts dust particles (avg. mass 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ g) from the cleanroom atmosphere, causing defects in microchips. Assuming the dust particles acquire a charge via induction and are initially 1.5 mm above the wafer surface, what is the minimum charge-to-mass ratio (in C/kg) a dust particle must have to overcome gravity and be pulled toward the wafer? Consider the wafer as a uniformly charged infinite plane and include air resistance as negligible due to low velocity.

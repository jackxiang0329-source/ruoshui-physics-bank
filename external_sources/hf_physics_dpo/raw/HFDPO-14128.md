# HFDPO-14128

A quantum dot solar cell uses a lattice of 125 identical semiconductor nanoparticles, each capable of trapping one electron in a discrete energy level at 0.8 eV above the valence band. At operating temperature (310 K), the system equilibrates with sunlight, creating a non-equilibrium population distribution where the probability of finding an electron in the excited state follows the Fermi-Dirac statistics. You are tasked with designing a feedback control system that maximizes power output by adjusting the bandgap dynamically. Determine the optimal energy separation between the ground and excited state that maximizes the average number of electrons in the excited state under thermal equilibrium, given that the electron chemical potential is fixed at 0.6 eV.

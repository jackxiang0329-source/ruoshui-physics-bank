# HFDPO-01566

In a high-altitude atmospheric research balloon, a 12 cm-radius spherical capacitor is used to measure electric field fluctuations during a thunderstorm’s electric breakdown phase. The capacitor is enclosed in a dielectric shell with non-uniform permittivity: ε(r) = ε₀(1 + 0.1 sin(πr/R)), where r is radial distance and R = 12 cm. As the storm intensifies, the potential difference across the capacitor increases from 0 to 30 kV over 2 seconds. Calculate the total energy stored in the capacitor at peak voltage, accounting for the spatially varying dielectric.

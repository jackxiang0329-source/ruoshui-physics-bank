# HFDPO-15908

A solar-powered drone designed for atmospheric research must conserve energy while navigating turbulent layers in the stratosphere. It uses a combination of photovoltaic panels and a regenerative braking system during descent. If the drone descends 2.5 km from 20 km to 17.5 km altitude at a steady speed, losing 600 kJ of gravitational potential energy, and 40% of that energy is recovered via regenerative braking, how much electrical energy is stored, and what is the net change in mechanical energy over the descent? Assume no air resistance or inefficiencies beyond the 40% recovery.

# HFDPO-03756

During a high-altitude balloon experiment, a cryogenic sensor module (capacitance-sensitive) fails intermittently when transitioning through Earth’s ionosphere. The module’s shielded cable, 12 meters long with a 2 mm inner conductor and 5 mm outer shield, is exposed to variable plasma density (10¹⁰–10¹² m⁻³). At 70 km altitude, plasma conductivity increases sharply, inducing transient charging on the cable’s outer shield. The sensor reads spurious signals only during rapid descent. Determine the effective capacitance per unit length between cable conductor and shield, and explain why the sensor malfunction occurs despite proper insulation.

# HFDPO-05045

A solar-powered drone, designed to hover above a remote mountain research station for weeks at a time, uses a pair of slender, carbon-fiber propeller arms (each 3.2 m long, mass 4.8 kg) mounted on a central hub. The arms are angled at 15° above horizontal to generate lift, with each propeller exerting a thrust force of 120 N at 45° from the vertical. The center of mass of the entire drone system is located 0.4 m above the hub at the center. Determine the minimum tension required in each of four symmetrically placed tether cables (attached 1.0 m above the hub, radial) to maintain static equilibrium during a strong wind gust that applies a 95 N horizontal force at the top of the drone.

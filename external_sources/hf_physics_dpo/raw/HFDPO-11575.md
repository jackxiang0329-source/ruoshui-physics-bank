# HFDPO-11575

In a solar-powered remote weather station, three identical photovoltaic arrays (each with internal resistance 0.8Ω and open-circuit voltage 24V) are connected in parallel to a central battery bank (nominal 24V, negligible internal resistance). However, due to partial shading, one array receives 75% of full sunlight, another 100%, and the third 50%. The system uses a charge controller that adjusts the effective load resistance dynamically to maximize power transfer. If the current from the 50%-sunlight array is measured as 1.2 A, determine the actual current through the 100%-sunlight array and the total power delivered to the battery.

# HFDPO-01855

A quantum imaging lab develops a new single-photon detector array for atmospheric lidar, but encounters unexpected noise spikes during nighttime measurements. The team suspects that residual cross-talk from a nearby T-LC link is contaminating the measurements, but the source is unidentifiable due to low power and non-coherent emission. Determine whether this interference is statistically significant and suggest a real-time mitigation strategy based on spectral filtering and coherence analysis.

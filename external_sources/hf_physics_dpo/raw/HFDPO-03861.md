# HFDPO-03861

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem hosts a colony of tube worms that rely on rhythmic plumes of warm, mineral-rich fluid to sustain their symbiotic bacteria. These plumes oscillate vertically due to coupled fluid-structure interactions, with an effective spring constant of 1.2 × 10³ N/m and a total mass of 0.8 kg. At a depth of 2,400 m, the fluid’s compressibility modifies the restoring force, leading to a nonlinear term proportional to the cube of displacement. If the plume initially displaces 15 cm and the system exhibits damped oscillations with a logarithmic decrement of 0.4, find the time it takes for the amplitude to decay to 10% of its initial value.

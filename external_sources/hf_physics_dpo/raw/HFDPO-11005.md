# HFDPO-11005

In a high-precision quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit operates at 5 GHz, coupled to a transmission line resonator with a characteristic impedance of 50 Ω and a quality factor (Q) of 10,000. What is the maximum instantaneous power delivered to the resonator when the drive is at the worst-case frequency deviation, and how does this compare to the ideal resonant case?

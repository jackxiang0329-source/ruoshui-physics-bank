# HFDPO-03212

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable device to detect early-stage arrhythmias using impedance plethysmography, where subtle changes in blood volume alter the electrical impedance of tissue. The system uses a 50 kHz AC excitation signal at 10 mV peak across a pair of electrodes on the chest. The body's tissue behaves as a complex impedance: 120 Ω resistance in series with a 0.8 μF capacitor (representing cell membrane capacitance). The device must amplify the voltage across the impedance and filter out noise above 100 kHz. What is the magnitude of the current through the tissue at 50 kHz, and how should the filter be configured to maximize signal-to-noise ratio?

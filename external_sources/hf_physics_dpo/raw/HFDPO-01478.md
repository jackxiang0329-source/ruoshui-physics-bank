# HFDPO-01478

A Mars rover deploys a self-replicating microrover with a novel hopping mechanism designed to traverse fractured lava tubes. The microrover launches at 3.2 m/s at a 45° angle relative to the 25° incline of a Martian trench wall (g_mars = 3.71 m/s²). After 1.8 seconds of flight, it must land precisely on a ledge 1.5 m vertically below the launch point but 4.3 m horizontally distant along the slope. Is the trajectory feasible? If not, what launch angle (within ±5° of 45°) achieves a successful landing?

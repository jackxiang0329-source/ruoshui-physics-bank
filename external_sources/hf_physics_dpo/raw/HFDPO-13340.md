# HFDPO-13340

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a high-wind region, using a custom cantilevered arm made of lightweight composite material. The arm, 3.2 m long and weighing 48 kg, is fixed to the wall at a 15° angle above horizontal. A 12 kg solar panel is attached at the free end, and the entire system is subjected to a uniform wind load of 850 Pa acting perpendicularly on the panel's surface (0.8 m × 1.2 m). The mounting bracket must resist both the moment from the wind and the weight of the structure. What is the minimum torque the bracket must provide at the wall to maintain static equilibrium?

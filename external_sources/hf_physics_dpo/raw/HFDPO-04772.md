# HFDPO-04772

A deep-space probe near Europa detects faint, polarized radio emissions from beneath the moon’s icy shell, oscillating at 12.4 MHz with a measured intensity of 3.2 × 10⁻²⁵ W/m². The ice layer is estimated at 15 km thick with a dielectric constant of 3.2 and a conductivity of 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ S/m. Can the signal penetrate the ice and be detected at the surface, and what fraction of the original power is transmitted if the wave enters the ice at normal incidence?

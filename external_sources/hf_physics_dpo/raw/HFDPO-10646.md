# HFDPO-10646

In a cutting-edge neuromorphic computing chip, a neural synapse is simulated using a tunable capacitor array where the dielectric material between two graphene electrodes exhibits non-uniform thickness due to nanoscale fabrication imperfections. The dielectric layer varies linearly from 5 nm at one edge to 15 nm at the opposite edge across a 10 μm gap. A voltage of 0.8 V is applied across the 20 μm × 10 μm capacitor. Calculate the total stored energy and compare it to the expected energy if the dielectric were uniform at the average thickness. Then, determine the effective capacitance and discuss whether this variation enhances or limits the device’s performance in maintaining analog synaptic weights.

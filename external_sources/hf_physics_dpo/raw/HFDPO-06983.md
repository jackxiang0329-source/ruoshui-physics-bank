# HFDPO-06983

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that electrons passing through a double-slit apparatus exhibit interference patterns when unobserved, but the pattern vanishes when a detector measures which slit each electron goes through. Given that the laser photons have a wavelength of 1000 nm and the slits are separated by 200 nm, calculate the minimum photon momentum required to resolve the electron’s path (Δx ≈ 100 nm), and explain why measuring the path collapses the wavefunction.

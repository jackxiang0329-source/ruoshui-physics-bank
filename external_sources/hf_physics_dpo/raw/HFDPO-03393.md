# HFDPO-03393

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable hover during data collection in turbulent stratospheric winds. The drone's hexagonal frame (side length 1.8 m) supports four identical solar panels at its vertices, each weighing 12 kg and extending 0.6 m outward. Due to uneven solar heating, the panels experience a non-uniform thermal expansion that causes their centers of mass to shift by up to 8 cm radially outward. At what wind speed (in m/s) does the drone’s center of mass shift beyond the stability threshold of the support base, risking overturning? Assume the drone’s center of mass is initially 0.4 m above the frame, and the base has a 1.2 m diameter. Use static equilibrium and moment analysis.

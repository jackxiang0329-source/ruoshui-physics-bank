# HFDPO-01712

A deep-space communications array on Mars receives a faint, modulated signal from Earth during a solar storm. The signal, carrying data at 2.4 GHz, experiences significant phase distortion due to plasma turbulence in the ionosphere. The received wave’s electric field is modeled as E(t) = E_0 cos(omega t + phi(t)) , where phi(t) = alpha sin(beta t) , with alpha = 0.1, text{rad} and beta = 10^6, text{rad/s} . Calculate the effective bandwidth of the modulated signal and determine whether it remains within the 200 kHz tolerance of the receiving demodulator.

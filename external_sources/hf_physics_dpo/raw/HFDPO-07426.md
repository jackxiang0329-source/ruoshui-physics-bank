# HFDPO-07426

A wind turbine blade, 60 meters long and weighing 15,000 kg, is mounted on a horizontal shaft at its center. During a storm, gusts exert a net upward lift force of 120 kN, distributed non-uniformly along the blade’s length (peaking at 20 kN/m near the tip). The blade is secured by a single pivot at the hub and a cable anchored 10 meters behind the pivot. If the system is in equilibrium, what is the minimum tension the cable must withstand to prevent the blade from lifting?

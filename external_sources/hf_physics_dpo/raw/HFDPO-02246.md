# HFDPO-02246

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged geological formation shaped like a truncated cone, with a 15-meter tall, 8-meter base diameter, and 4-meter top diameter. The structure is partially embedded in silt, and the ROV must deploy a stabilizing buoyant collar around its lower 6 meters to prevent it from sinking further into the sediment. The surrounding seawater has a density of 1025 kg/m³, and the silt exerts a vertical resistance of 12,000 N per square meter of contact area. What minimum buoyant force must the collar generate to counteract the net downward force on the submerged section, accounting for both hydrostatic pressure gradients and the silt’s resistance?

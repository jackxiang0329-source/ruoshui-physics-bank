# HFDPO-13075

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a swirling plume of sediment suspended in seawater near a hydrothermal vent. Measurements indicate a turbulent dissipation rate of 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ W/kg and a mean salinity gradient of 0.05 psu/m. The ROV’s sensors detect a localized density inversion just above the plume’s core.

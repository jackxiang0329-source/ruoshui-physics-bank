# HFDPO-11988

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a fixed, east-west orientation to maximize morning and afternoon sun exposure. However, glare from the panels is disrupting nearby wildlife observation towers, particularly during sunrise and sunset when sunlight strikes the panels at a shallow angle. Engineers notice the glare intensity peaks when the sun is near the horizon, and they suspect polarization effects from the anti-reflective coatings are amplifying the issue. The panels use a dielectric coating with a refractive index of 1.55. At what angle of incidence (measured from the normal) would the reflected light become completely polarized, and would this correspond to the time of maximum glare?

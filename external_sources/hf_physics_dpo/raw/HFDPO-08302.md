# HFDPO-08302

The vent’s chimney structure grows slowly over decades, driven by mineral precipitation as the fluid cools and expands through porous rock. Despite continuous heat loss to the environment, the internal fluid temperature remains nearly constant. What thermodynamic principle explains this persistent internal temperature, and how does it reconcile with the second law when heat flows irreversibly to the cold ocean?

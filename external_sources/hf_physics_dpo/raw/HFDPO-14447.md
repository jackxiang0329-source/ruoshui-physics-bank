# HFDPO-14447

A quantum imaging lab develops a single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) array to detect faint bioluminescent signals from deep-sea microorganisms. The array operates at 680 nm with a photon detection efficiency (PDE) of 45% and is cooled to 150 K to reduce dark counts. However, when imaging a dense microbial colony under high background fluorescence (10⁶ photons/cm²/s), the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) drops below 2 due to correlated noise from afterpulsing. Given that the afterpulsing probability is 12% per detected photon and the frame rate is 100 Hz, what is the minimum number of photons per pixel required to achieve an SNR ≥ 5 under these conditions?

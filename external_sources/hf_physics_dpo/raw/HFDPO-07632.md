# HFDPO-07632

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V DC battery bank is charged by a photovoltaic array during the day and must power sensors and a telemetry module overnight. The array delivers 1.8 A under peak sun, but the battery has a 15% internal resistance due to degradation. A 24 Ω load (sensors + transmitter) draws current at night. After 8 hours of darkness, the battery voltage drops to 10.4 V. Determine whether the system can maintain function for 12 hours, assuming the battery's open-circuit voltage is initially 12.6 V and capacity is 80 Ah.

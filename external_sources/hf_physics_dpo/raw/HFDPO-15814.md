# HFDPO-15814

During a winter storm, a city’s water pipeline bursts after freezing overnight. The pipe, made of steel, had 1.2 m³ of water at 4°C when temperatures dropped to −5°C. If the water expands by 9.0% upon freezing and the pipe’s internal volume is fixed, what pressure does the ice exert on the pipe walls? Assume ice forms completely and the bulk modulus of ice is 8.0 × 10⁹ Pa.

# HFDPO-15764

A solar-powered drone designed to hover at 25,000 feet uses four slender, carbon-fiber support arms (each 1.8 m long, 3 cm diameter) to stabilize a 7 kg camera pod suspended beneath a central fuselage. The arms are anchored at 90° angles to each other, with the camera pod offset 0.4 m vertically below the center of mass of the fuselage. Due to wind gusts, the system experiences a horizontal force of 12 N at the pod, and the arms must resist bending and shearing. Calculate the minimum required torsional stiffness (in N·m/rad) of each arm’s joint to prevent angular deformation exceeding 0.5° under load, assuming uniform distributed bending stress and neglecting arm mass.

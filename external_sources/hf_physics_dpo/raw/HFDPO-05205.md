# HFDPO-05205

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted on a lightweight aluminum frame secured to the roof structure with four bolts. The array, measuring 3.2 m by 1.8 m, weighs 120 kg and is tilted at 30° to the horizontal. During a storm, wind exerts a uniform pressure of 600 Pa perpendicular to the panel’s surface. Determine the minimum clamping force each bolt must provide to prevent the array from rotating upward at the front edge—assuming the bolts are located at the front corners and the center of mass is at the geometric center.

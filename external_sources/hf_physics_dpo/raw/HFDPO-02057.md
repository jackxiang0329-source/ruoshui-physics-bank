# HFDPO-02057

In a high-voltage DC transmission line, a 500-km copper cable (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, diameter 2.5 cm) carries 1.2 kA. Due to a recent landslide, a 100-meter section is buried in wet clay with resistivity 20 Ω·m, causing localized heating. Can this lead to insulation failure if the cable’s melting point is 1085°C and ambient temperature is 25°C?

# HFDPO-07812

Measurements show a 2.3 × 10⁶ T⁻¹ increase in the electron spin relaxation rate per Tesla of residual magnetic field, and the chip is exposed to a fluctuating 1.6 nT field from nearby power rails. Could hyperfine interactions due to nuclear spins in the silicon substrate (with 3.1% natural abundance of ²⁹Si, I = 1/2) contribute significantly to this decoherence? Estimate the spin-lattice relaxation rate from the nuclear spin bath and compare it to the observed decay.

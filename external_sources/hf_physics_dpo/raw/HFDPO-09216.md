# HFDPO-09216

A fusion reactor prototype uses a spherical plasma containment vessel with a radius of 2.5 m, where deuterium-tritium fuel is compressed via magnetic fields to 10⁹ K and 10²² particles/m³. During a microsecond-long pulse, 5.0×10²² fusion reactions occur, releasing energy in the form of 14 MeV neutrons and 3.5 MeV alpha particles. The neutron flux is uncontained, but 95% of alpha particles are thermalized within the plasma, contributing to heating. Estimate the net change in the plasma's internal energy during the pulse, assuming negligible radiation losses. How does this compare to the initial kinetic energy stored in the plasma before compression?

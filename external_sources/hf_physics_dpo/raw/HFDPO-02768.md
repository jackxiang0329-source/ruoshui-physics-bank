# HFDPO-02768

A fusion reactor’s magnetic confinement chamber uses a rapidly rotating plasma ring (radius 1.2 m) stabilized by external magnetic fields. Due to a control system glitch, the ring’s angular velocity decreases non-uniformly: α(t) = –0.05t rad/s². At t = 0, the ring spins at 2000 rpm. Engineers must determine if a critical rotational kinetic energy threshold (5.0 × 10⁶ J) is maintained for 3 seconds to prevent plasma disruption. Is the threshold breached?

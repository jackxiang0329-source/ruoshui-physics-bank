# HFDPO-15051

During a deep-sea hydrothermal vent survey, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) detects a turbulent plume rising from a fissure at 2,500 meters depth, where seawater density is 1040 kg/m³ and viscosity is 1.3 × 10⁻³ Pa·s. Engineers observe that the plume begins to deflect horizontally after rising 18 m, despite no apparent external flow.

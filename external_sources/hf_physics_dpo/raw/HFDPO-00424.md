# HFDPO-00424

A solar farm in Arizona experiences frequent dust accumulation on its photovoltaic panels, reducing efficiency by 18% over just two weeks. The dust particles, with a typical radius of 5 μm and density of 2.5 g/cm³, become charged via triboelectric contact with the panel surface. If the panels are coated with a hydrophobic material that holds a uniform surface charge of +2.0 nC/m², what is the minimum initial charge a dust particle must carry to remain suspended 1 mm above the surface against gravity? (Consider only electrostatic and gravitational forces.)

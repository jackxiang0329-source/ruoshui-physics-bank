# HFDPO-03448

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a dual-polarization antenna array to receive signals from ground stations, but interference from solar radio bursts—emitted as partially polarized radiation at 1.4 GHz—degrades signal integrity. The bursts exhibit a degree of polarization of 0.65 and a preferred orientation of 30° from vertical, while the desired signal is purely vertically polarized. Engineers must design a polarization filter using a phased array of crossed dipoles with adjustable phase shifts to maximize the signal-to-interference ratio (SIR) at the receiver. What phase shift must be applied across the horizontal and vertical dipole elements to maximize SIR, assuming optimal alignment and ideal components?

# HFDPO-04584

In a fusion reactor design, a plasma ring with 2.5 kg of deuterium-tritium fuel is confined in a toroidal chamber at 150 million K. During a microburst instability, 1.2% of the plasma mass is ejected radially outward at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s. Simultaneously, the remaining plasma contracts, increasing its central pressure. Calculate the net change in the system’s total linear momentum and explain how angular momentum conservation constrains the possible directions of ejection to prevent chamber deformation.

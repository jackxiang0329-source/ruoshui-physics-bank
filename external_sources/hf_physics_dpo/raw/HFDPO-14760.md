# HFDPO-14760

A 75-meter-tall wind turbine blade, modeled as a thin, non-uniform rod with mass density varying as λ(x) = λ₀(1 + 0.02x/L), where x is distance from the hub, L = 75 m, and λ₀ = 45 kg/m, is mounted horizontally on a rigid tower. The blade is supported only at the hub by a frictionless pin. During a sudden gust, the blade experiences a distributed aerodynamic lift force per unit length of f(x) = ½ρC_Lv²(x) = 1200(1 − x/L) N/m, with v(x) proportional to x due to rotational speed. Calculate the magnitude and direction of the reaction force at the hub when the blade is stationary in this loading condition.

# HFDPO-04921

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 1.2 km deep, 8 cm diameter coil of superconducting wire suspended in a borehole filled with molten basalt (μ ≈ 1.05μ₀, σ ≈ 1.5×10⁶ S/m). The temperature gradient induces a slow, upward convective fluid motion, creating time-varying magnetic fields. At 800°C near the base, the resistivity drops, but the thermal expansion alters the coil’s diameter by 0.3% over 10 minutes. Determine the induced EMF in the coil due to both motional and transformer induction, assuming an average radial magnetic field component of 0.8 T increasing at 2.3×10⁻³ T/s.

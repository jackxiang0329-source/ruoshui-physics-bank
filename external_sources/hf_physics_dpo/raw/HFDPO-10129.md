# HFDPO-10129

During operation, electron-hole pairs generated by sunlight undergo rapid recombination, reducing efficiency. Measurements show a 3.2 eV bandgap and a measured electron effective mass of 0.04mₑ. However, experimental data reveals an anomalous photocurrent suppression at low illumination intensities—unexpected given the material’s high absorption coefficient. Explain how quantum confinement and charge carrier localization in the nanocrystal interface, combined with phonon-mediated tunneling (a solid-state effect), could lead to this behavior in a regime where standard Shockley-Queisser models fail.

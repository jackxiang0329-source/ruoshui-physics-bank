# HFDPO-04560

A lunar habitation module on Mars uses a lightweight, folded truss structure made of carbon-fiber struts to support a solar array. During a dust storm, the array is subjected to a sustained wind force of 850 N at a 30° angle above horizontal, acting on a 3.2 m × 2.4 m surface. The truss is pinned at one end to the module and supported by a tether anchored at 45° below horizontal at the other. The system is in static equilibrium, but the tether is near its tensile strength limit (max 1200 N). Determine whether the tether is safe under these conditions, considering the possibility that the wind force may vary due to turbulence.

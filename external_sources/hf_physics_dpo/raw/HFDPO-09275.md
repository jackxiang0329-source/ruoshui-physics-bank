# HFDPO-09275

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits an unexpected drop in efficiency when exposed to prolonged sunlight, despite maintaining stable temperature. The observed emission peak shifts from 620 nm to 595 nm after 12 hours of illumination, indicating a change in the quantum confinement energy.

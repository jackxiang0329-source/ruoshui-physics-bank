# HFDPO-13147

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanostructure to enhance light absorption by coupling incident sunlight into guided modes via diffraction. The array consists of 500 nm diameter circular quantum dots spaced 800 nm apart in a square lattice. During testing under AM1.5G sunlight (550 nm peak wavelength), engineers observe a sharp enhancement in photocurrent at 620 nm. Explain why this enhancement occurs at a wavelength longer than the peak solar irradiance and determine the diffraction order responsible, assuming constructive interference occurs when the grating equation is satisfied.

# HFDPO-14968

A small solar-powered weather station in Antarctica uses a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) containing 50 grams of plutonium-238 to keep instruments operating through the long, dark winter. If the station needs at least 1.2 watts of continuous power to function, will the RTG provide sufficient energy for 5 years?

Engineers need to estimate the reduction in wave energy passing through the breakwater due to viscous drag and flow contraction — but they’re unsure whether to use Bernoulli’s equation, momentum balance, or empirical drag coefficients. How should they analyze the flow through the slots to predict energy loss?

# HFDPO-11599

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal area prone to strong gusts. The 25 kg unit is secured by a bracket fixed to a steel beam using two bolts. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 800 N at the center of the station’s 1.2 m tall vertical tower. The bolts are 0.3 m apart horizontally, and the center of mass of the station is 0.8 m above the bracket. What is the minimum clamping force each bolt must exert to prevent the bracket from rotating or slipping, assuming the coefficient of static friction between the bracket and beam is 0.4?

# HFDPO-09892

A medical research team is developing a new MRI contrast agent that enhances signal detection in brain scans. The agent contains nanoscale magnetic particles suspended in a biocompatible fluid. During testing, they observe that the particle suspension causes a measurable distortion in the uniform magnetic field of the MRI scanner (1.5 T), particularly near the edges of the imaging region. The particles are uniformly distributed but are magnetically anisotropic, meaning their magnetic moments align preferentially along a single axis. Given that the system is static (no time-varying fields), and the magnetic field is measured to deflect by 0.3° at a distance of 2 cm from the boundary of the suspension, determine the effective magnetization vector **M** of the particles, assuming the material behaves like a linear magnetic medium with relative permeability μᵣ = 1.001.

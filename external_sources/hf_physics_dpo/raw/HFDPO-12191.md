# HFDPO-12191

A marine biologist is studying how sound travels through ocean water to monitor whale communication. She places two hydrophones 1,200 meters apart on the seafloor. When a whale sings, the sound arrives at the first hydrophone 0.9 seconds before the second. Given that sound travels at 1,500 m/s in seawater, what can she infer about the whale’s position relative to the line connecting the two hydrophones?

# HFDPO-02009

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a 12 V battery bank and multiple sensors, but during a prolonged sandstorm, the data logger shows erratic readings and the system intermittently shuts down. Engineers discover that a 100 Ω resistor in the voltage regulation circuit has overheated and failed, creating an open connection. The station relies on a 5 V regulated supply derived from the 12 V battery through a voltage divider with a second resistor. Without the 100 Ω resistor, the remaining 470 Ω resistor now directly connects to the battery. What is the actual voltage delivered to the data logger, and why might this explain the system failure?

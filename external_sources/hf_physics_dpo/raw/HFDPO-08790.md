# HFDPO-08790

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood samples using size-based trapping in a narrow channel. What is the viscous drag force on a single cancer cell as it passes through the channel, and how does this compare to the inertial forces? Is the flow regime appropriate for this separation method?

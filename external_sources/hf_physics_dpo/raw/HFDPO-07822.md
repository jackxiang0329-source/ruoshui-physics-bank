# HFDPO-07822

A biomedical engineer is designing a biosensor that uses a 2D grating of sub-micron pillars to detect minute changes in refractive index caused by protein binding. The grating has a lattice spacing of 600 nm, and the device is illuminated with coherent green laser light (λ = 532 nm) at normal incidence. When a target protein binds to the surface, it shifts the effective refractive index, altering the diffraction pattern. The engineer observes that the first-order diffraction peak shifts from 12.5° to 14.3°. What is the change in effective refractive index experienced by the light in the grating region?

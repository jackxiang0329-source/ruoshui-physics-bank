# HFDPO-14372

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses reaction wheels to maintain orientation, but a sudden solar flare causes a torque ripple in one wheel, inducing precession. The wheel has a mass of 12 kg, radius 0.3 m, and spins at 3000 rpm. If the satellite’s moment of inertia about its principal axis is 45 kg·m² and the torque induced by the flare is 0.8 N·m, how long does it take for the satellite’s orientation to deviate by 5° due to this precession?

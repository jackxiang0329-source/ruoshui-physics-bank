# HFDPO-11142

A spacecraft re-entry capsule descends through Earth’s atmosphere, deploying a set of deployable aerodynamic fins to stabilize its rotation. Initially spinning at 8 rad/s about its center of mass, the fins deploy over 0.6 seconds, increasing the moment of inertia from 12 kg·m² to 45 kg·m². During deployment, a frictional torque of 5.2 N·m opposes the spin. What is the final angular speed of the capsule as the fins fully lock, and does the capsule stabilize sufficiently to avoid tumbling upon atmospheric entry?

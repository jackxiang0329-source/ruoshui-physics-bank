# HFDPO-09000

A solar-powered satellite in low Earth orbit uses a cylindrical shield made of conductive material to protect its sensitive electronics from charged particles in the Van Allen belts. The shield has a length of 2.0 m and a radius of 0.5 m. During a solar storm, the shield accumulates a total charge of +1.5 × 10⁻⁹ C. What is the magnitude of the electric field just outside the curved surface of the shield, assuming uniform charge distribution?

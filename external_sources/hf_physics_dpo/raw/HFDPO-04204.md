# HFDPO-04204

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a single 12 V battery bank and must run a data logger that draws 15 mA continuously, plus a temperature sensor that cycles on every 10 minutes for 30 seconds at 100 mA. If the total system efficiency is 85% due to resistance and connectors, what is the minimum voltage that must be maintained at the battery to ensure the data logger receives at least 11.5 V during peak load?

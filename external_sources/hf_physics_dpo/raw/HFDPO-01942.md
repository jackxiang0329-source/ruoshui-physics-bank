# HFDPO-01942

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole filled with conductive brine to extract heat from underground magma chambers. The brine circulates through a coil of superconducting wire buried 2 km below the surface, where it encounters a time-varying magnetic field due to subsurface convective currents in the molten rock. At a depth of 1.8 km, the magnetic flux through a single turn of the coil changes at a rate of 0.35 T·m²/s, and the coil has 1200 turns. However, due to thermal expansion, the coil’s radius increases by 0.05% per hour. What is the induced EMF in the coil after 24 hours, assuming the magnetic field remains constant in magnitude but varies in orientation due to fluid motion?

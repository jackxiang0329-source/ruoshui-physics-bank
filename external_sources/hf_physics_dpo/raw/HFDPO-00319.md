# HFDPO-00319

A drone delivery system uses a lightweight, rigid boom extending horizontally from a grounded support to carry a 4.5 kg package. The boom is secured at its base by a hinge and supported by a diagonal cable making a 35° angle with the horizontal. The cable has a breaking tension of 750 N. If the boom itself weighs 6.0 kg and its center of mass is 1.2 m from the hinge, how far from the hinge can the package be placed before the cable exceeds its safe tension?

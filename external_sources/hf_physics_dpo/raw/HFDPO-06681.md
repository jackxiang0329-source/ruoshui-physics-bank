# HFDPO-06681

During a deep-space mission, a satellite’s primary instrument—a sensitive infrared spectrometer—must be kept at 70 K to avoid thermal noise. The instrument is shielded by a multi-layered radiation shield with 10 identical, parallel layers, each with an emissivity of 0.02 and separated by vacuum gaps. The outermost layer faces the Sun at 5800 K and absorbs 75% of incident solar radiation. What is the minimum required heat dissipation rate (in watts) from the instrument’s internal cooler if the system is in steady-state and radiative equilibrium? Assume the instrument is a perfect blackbody and neglect heat conduction through supports.

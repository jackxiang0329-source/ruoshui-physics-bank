# HFDPO-06816

A quantum dot solar cell uses PbSe nanocrystals with a bandgap of 0.8 eV to harvest infrared light. During a test, the device efficiency drops by 18% after 100 hours of operation under constant 1-sun illumination. Researchers suspect carrier trapping at surface defects, but theoretical models predict negligible trap density. Given that the average charge carrier lifetime in the quantum dot is measured at 2.4 ns and the diffusion length is 50 nm, analyze whether the efficiency loss is due to trap-assisted recombination or thermal degradation of the quantum dot lattice. Assume the trap density is 5×10¹² cm⁻² and the trap capture cross-section is 1×10⁻¹⁵ cm².

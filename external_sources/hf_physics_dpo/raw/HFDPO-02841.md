# HFDPO-02841

A solar farm in Nevada uses modular tracking panels that pivot to follow the sun. Each panel is 3 meters long, weighs 120 kg, and is supported at one end by a frictionless hinge. A motor applies a force at the far end to maintain balance against wind gusts. During a storm, wind exerts a 450 N horizontal force at the panel’s center. What minimum force must the motor apply perpendicular to the panel to keep it stationary at a 30° angle from horizontal?

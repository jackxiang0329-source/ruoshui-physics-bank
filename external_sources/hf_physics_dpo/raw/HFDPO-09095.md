# HFDPO-09095

A drone equipped with a monochromatic laser pointer (λ = 632.8 nm) is hovering 120 m above a calm lake. The laser beam strikes the water at a 35° angle and reflects off the surface, then passes through a thin oil slick (n = 1.45) floating on the water (n = 1.33). As the drone adjusts its angle slightly, the reflected light from the oil slick changes from bright to dark. What is the minimum thickness of the oil slick that causes this destructive interference in the reflected light?

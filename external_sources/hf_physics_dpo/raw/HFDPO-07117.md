# HFDPO-07117

A high-altitude atmospheric research balloon carries a sensitive magnetometer measuring the Earth's magnetic field gradients near the ionosphere (60 km altitude). During a geomagnetic storm, the instrument detects a time-varying magnetic field component ∂B/∂t ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻⁷ T/s over a 500 m² loop area. At the same time, a nearby plasma wave sensor records electron density fluctuations. Using Maxwell’s equations, determine the induced electric field magnitude and direction within the loop, and explain how this electric field might drive current in the ionospheric plasma. What role does displacement current play in maintaining continuity of the magnetic field dynamics during the storm?

# HFDPO-04082

A deep-sea geothermal vent in the Pacific Ocean emits superheated water at 450°C, flowing through a porous rock matrix into cold seawater at 2°C. Engineers aim to harvest energy using a modular heat engine system submerged near the vent, but the system must operate efficiently while avoiding thermal shock to sensitive microorganisms thriving in the boundary zone. Given a maximum heat transfer rate of 1.2 MW from the vent fluid to the working fluid and a finite heat capacity of the ambient ocean (modeled as a thermal reservoir), what is the maximum sustainable power output of the engine if the working fluid undergoes irreversible heat exchange and the system must maintain a minimum temperature difference of 50°C across the heat exchanger to prevent biofouling and thermal stress?

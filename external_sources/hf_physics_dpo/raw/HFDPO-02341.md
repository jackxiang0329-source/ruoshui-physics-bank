# HFDPO-02341

A quantum dot solar cell uses lead sulfide nanoparticles (diameter ~3.2 nm) to absorb sunlight, but efficiency drops sharply above 500°C. Engineers suspect that thermal excitation of carriers into interband states disrupts charge separation. Given the bandgap of PbS is 0.42 eV at room temperature and increases by 0.0012 eV/K due to quantum confinement, estimate the temperature at which the thermal energy (kBT) equals the bandgap. How does this relate to the observed efficiency loss?

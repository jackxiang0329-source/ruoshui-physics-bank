# HFDPO-02545

A lunar mining colony on the far side of the Moon is developing a tethered satellite system to relay data to Earth, using the Moon’s gravity well to stabilize orbits. However, the Moon’s non-uniform gravitational field (modeled by a quadrupole moment) induces slow orbital precession. Given the Moon’s mass (7.34 × 10²² kg), radius (1.737 × 10⁶ m), and gravitational quadrupole coefficient (J₂ ≈ 2.03 × 10⁻⁴), calculate the net secular rate of orbital node regression (in deg/day) due to gravitational anisotropy. How does this compare to the satellite’s operational lifetime (5 years) and what design implications arise?

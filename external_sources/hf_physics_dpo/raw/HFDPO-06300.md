# HFDPO-06300

In a high-vacuum semiconductor manufacturing chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform surface charge due to ion bombardment during plasma etching. The charge density varies radially as σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r/R), where σ₀ = 5.2 μC/m² and R = 7.5 cm. A nearby 2 mm-thick, grounded conductive shield is positioned 1.2 cm away from the wafer’s edge. Calculate the electrostatic force per unit area on the shield near the wafer's edge, considering induced charge polarization and edge effects.

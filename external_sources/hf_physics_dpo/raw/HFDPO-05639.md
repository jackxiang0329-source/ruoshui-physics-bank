# HFDPO-05639

A geothermal drill bit, shaped like a conical helix with a 5 cm radius and 1.2 m length, must penetrate 100 meters of basalt rock at a constant rate of 3 m/min. The rock’s shear strength averages 120 MPa, and frictional resistance generates heat that increases the bit’s temperature by 0.8°C per meter drilled. If the bit’s heat capacity is 1.5 kJ/kg·°C and its mass is 8 kg, what fraction of the total work done goes into overcoming shear resistance versus heating the bit? Assume no energy is lost to the surroundings.

# HFDPO-08440

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal magnetic confinement chamber where deuterium-tritium plasma reaches 150 million Kelvin. During a 10-second pulse, the plasma loses 2.4 × 10⁹ J of thermal energy due to radiation and wall contact, yet the magnetic field strength is adjusted to maintain confinement. If the initial plasma mass is 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ kg with a uniform internal energy density, determine whether the total energy of the system (plasma + magnetic field) is conserved, and explain how the system’s electromagnetic energy and kinetic energy are redistributed during the pulse.

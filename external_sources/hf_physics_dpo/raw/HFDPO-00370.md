# HFDPO-00370

A solar-powered water purification system in a remote village uses a network of three parallel solar arrays connected to a central battery bank through a complex junction with resistive wiring. Array A generates 12 V and supplies 5 A, Array B (identical) supplies 5 A, but Array C—partially shaded—produces only 8 V and draws 3 A from the grid. At the central junction, a 2 Ω resistor connects the combined output to the battery. If the battery charges at 8 A, what is the current through the shaded array (C) and how does Kirchhoff’s Current Law resolve the apparent power imbalance?

# HFDPO-15319

A renewable energy startup is designing a gravity-based energy storage system using massive concrete blocks suspended in a disused mine shaft. Each block (mass = 8,500 kg) is lifted 320 m using surplus solar power during peak generation. When demand spikes, the block is lowered to generate electricity via a generator with 83% efficiency. Engineers must determine whether this system can deliver 1.2 MWh of usable energy per cycle, while also evaluating how much energy is lost to friction and air resistance if the actual output is 1.02 MWh per cycle.

# HFDPO-08211

During a high-altitude expedition, a team discovers a frozen lake partially covered in a thin layer of ice that fluctuates in thickness due to wind-driven waves. They notice that when the wind is calm, the ice grows steadily, but during gusts, thinning occurs rapidly—even though the temperature remains below 0°C. The ice layer initially measures 3.0 cm thick and the surface temperature is −5.0°C. Wind gusts bring air at −3.0°C with a convective heat transfer coefficient of 25 W/m²·K. How much time does it take for a 0.50 cm reduction in ice thickness to occur under the wind gusts, assuming the water beneath is at 0°C and the ice thermal conductivity is 2.2 W/m·K?

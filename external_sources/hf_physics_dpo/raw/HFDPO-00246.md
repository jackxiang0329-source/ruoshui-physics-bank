# HFDPO-00246

A deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent field at 2,500 meters below the surface, where the surrounding basaltic rock exhibits anisotropic elasticity. During operation, the drone's manipulator arm applies a 120 N force at a 30° angle to a fracture plane oriented at 45° relative to the principal stress direction. The rock's Young’s modulus varies from 45 GPa (perpendicular to layering) to 68 GPa (parallel), with a Poisson’s ratio of 0.27. If the drill tip induces a shear stress of 1.8 MPa across the fracture plane, and the rock fails when shear stress exceeds 3.2 MPa, determine whether the application of force will trigger a controlled fracture or cause catastrophic failure.

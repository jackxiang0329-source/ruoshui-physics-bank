# HFDPO-13525

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a 120-meter-long, 1.8-meter-diameter steel pipeline to transport superheated brine (T ≈ 240°C) from a depth of 2.3 km. During operation, the pipeline experiences thermal stress due to rapid cooling after shutdown. The steel has a Young’s modulus of 200 GPa, a thermal expansion coefficient of 12 × 10⁻⁶/°C, and a yield strength of 450 MPa. When the pipeline cools from 240°C to 15°C over 3 hours, what is the maximum axial stress developed, and is buckling likely given the pipeline is restrained at both ends?

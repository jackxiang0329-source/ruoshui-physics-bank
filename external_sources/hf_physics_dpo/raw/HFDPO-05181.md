# HFDPO-05181

A quantum computing lab in Zurich is testing a new superconducting qubit array cooled to 15 mK using a dilution refrigerator. The qubit’s critical current density is 5 × 10⁶ A/m², but during operation, the system detects unexpected energy dissipation in the Josephson junctions. The junctions are made from niobium with a critical magnetic field of 180 mT. The qubit is exposed to a stray magnetic field from a nearby power cable (measured at 90 mT), causing phase slips and decoherence. Determine whether the applied field is within the safe operating range for the fluxoid quantization condition in a loop of diameter 200 μm, and explain why the system might still lose coherence despite being below the critical field.

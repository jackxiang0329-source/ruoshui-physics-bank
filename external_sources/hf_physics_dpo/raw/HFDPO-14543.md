# HFDPO-14543

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses high-pressure steam from underground reservoirs to drive turbines, but due to fluctuating underground heat flow, the steam pressure varies over time. Engineers must design a control system that maintains turbine output within 5% of nominal by adjusting the steam flow rate. The turbine’s output is modeled as P(t) = 100e^{-0.02t} MW for t in [0, 100] minutes, where t is time in minutes. If the plant must deliver exactly 9000 MWh of energy over 100 minutes, is the turbine’s time-varying power sufficient? If not, how much additional energy (in MWh) must be supplied by a battery system during this period?

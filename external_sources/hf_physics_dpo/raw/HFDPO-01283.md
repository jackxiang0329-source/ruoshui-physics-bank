# HFDPO-01283

A planetary probe deploying a high-precision seismometer on Mars uses a motorized arm with a rotating mass to stabilize its orientation during touchdown. What is the minimum angular speed the rotor must maintain—just before impact—to prevent the arm from rotating more than 2° during the 0.15 s collision?

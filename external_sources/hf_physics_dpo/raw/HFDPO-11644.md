# HFDPO-11644

A quantum computing chip uses niobium-based Josephson junctions operating at 4.2 K in a dilution refrigerator. During a stability test, engineers notice unpredictable phase slips in the qubit circuits, causing decoherence. The junctions are designed with a critical current density of 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m², but measurements show persistent current oscillations with a period of 8.5 ns. Could these oscillations stem from flux trapping in the superconducting loop due to incomplete screening of magnetic fields during cooldown? Estimate the magnetic flux quantum trapped in the loop and determine whether the observed oscillations are consistent with flux quantization.

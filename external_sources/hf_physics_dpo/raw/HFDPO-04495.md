# HFDPO-04495

A deep-sea autonomous vehicle uses a coherent blue laser (λ = 450 nm) to probe a hydrothermal vent plume, where temperature gradients create a refractive index profile n(z) = 1.33 + 0.0015 sin(2πz / 20), with z in meters. As the beam propagates vertically through the plume, it experiences continuous phase modulation due to spatially varying n. The vehicle detects a distorted interference pattern on a detector 100 m below the source. What is the cumulative phase shift accumulated by the beam over this distance, and how does this shift affect the coherence and detectability of the signal?

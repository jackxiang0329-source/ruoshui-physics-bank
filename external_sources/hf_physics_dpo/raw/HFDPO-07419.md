# HFDPO-07419

A team of bioengineers is designing a synthetic cellular environment to study protein folding under confined nanoscale conditions. They embed a single protein chain (N = 100 amino acids) in a lipid bilayer with tunable rigidity, where the environment induces a free-energy landscape with two dominant states: folded (native) and misfolded (aggregated). The entropy of the folded state is 15% lower than that of the fully disordered chain, while the energy difference between folded and misfolded states is ΔE = −12 kT. However, molecular crowding increases effective stiffness, reducing the number of accessible conformational states by 40%. What is the equilibrium fraction of proteins in the folded state at T = 310 K, and how does this compare to the expected value in a dilute solution?

# HFDPO-05318

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote desert village uses silicon photovoltaic panels with a bandgap of 1.12 eV. During peak sunlight, the panels receive 1000 W/m² of irradiance, but only 18% of this energy is converted to electrical power. The system operates a 12 V DC pump that draws 5 A. One day, a dust storm reduces sunlight by 30% and the panel temperature rises to 60°C, decreasing the bandgap by 0.02 eV. How much current can the system deliver to the pump now? Assume the open-circuit voltage drops by 0.05 V per 10°C rise above 25°C.

# HFDPO-00315

A 75-meter-tall wind turbine blade made of composite material, weighing 12,000 kg, is being installed on a tower during a storm. The blade is tilted at 45° to the horizontal and secured only by a single bolt at its root, which must resist both shear and moment from wind forces. The wind exerts a non-uniform pressure increasing linearly from 0 at the tip to 1,200 Pa at the root, and the blade’s center of mass lies 30 m from the root. What minimum shear and moment capacity must the bolt have to prevent failure while the blade is hoisted?

# HFDPO-02467

A quantum computing chip operates at 15 mK using a dilution refrigerator, with superconducting qubits connected via nanoscale aluminum traces (diameter 50 nm, resistivity 1.2×10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 15 mK). Given the trace length is 120 μm and the effective mass of electrons in Al is 9.1×10⁻³¹ kg, determine whether resistive heating from the current pulse causes a measurable temperature rise that could decohere the qubit—justifying the necessity of using superconducting materials in this regime.

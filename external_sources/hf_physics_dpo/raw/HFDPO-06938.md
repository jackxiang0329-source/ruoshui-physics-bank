# HFDPO-06938

A biomedical engineer is designing a portable device that uses laser speckle contrast imaging to detect early signs of poor blood flow in diabetic patients' feet. The device emits near-infrared light (λ = 850 nm) through a diffuser to create a speckle pattern on the skin. If the average speckle size observed on a patient’s foot is 0.3 mm, what is the approximate diameter of the laser beam’s focusing spot at the diffuser, assuming the speckles form due to interference over a distance of 15 cm from the diffuser to the skin?

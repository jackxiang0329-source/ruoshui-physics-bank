# HFDPO-15667

A deep-sea mining drone descends vertically through 2,000 meters of ocean, dragging a 300 kg ore cable with variable tension due to drag and buoyancy. The cable’s linear density increases gradually from 0.15 kg/m at the surface to 0.35 kg/m at the bottom due to sediment adhesion. As it sinks, the drone maintains constant speed despite changing forces. What is the total work done by the drone’s motors to lower the cable and itself (mass 120 kg) to the seabed, considering that drag force is proportional to velocity and depth-dependent buoyancy reduces effective weight by 18% at depth?

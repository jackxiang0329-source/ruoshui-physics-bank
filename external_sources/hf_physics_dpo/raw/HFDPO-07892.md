# HFDPO-07892

A high-altitude research drone carrying scientific sensors must descend from 12,000 meters to 3,000 meters in under 45 minutes to avoid storm systems. The air at 12,000 m is -55°C and 20 kPa, while at 3,000 m it’s 5°C and 70 kPa. Assume the air temperature changes linearly with altitude and the drone’s descent is isentropic.

# HFDPO-09638

A satellite constellation in Sun-synchronous orbit must maintain precise ground track repeatability for climate monitoring, but solar radiation pressure causes a gradual drift in orbital inclination. Given a 700 km circular orbit with a 98.6° inclination, a satellite cross-sectional area of 12 m², and a mass of 850 kg, calculate the minimum required Δv impulse (in m/s) needed every 90 days to counteract the cumulative inclination drift induced by solar radiation pressure. Assume a solar flux of 1361 W/m², perfect reflectivity, and a mean solar angle of 45° relative to the orbit plane.

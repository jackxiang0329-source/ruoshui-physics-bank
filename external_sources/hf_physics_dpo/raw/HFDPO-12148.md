# HFDPO-12148

A high-altitude balloon carrying a cryogenic sensor package descends rapidly after a telemetry failure. The package, insulated with a 2.0 cm thick layer of aerogel (thermal conductivity 0.015 W/m·K), maintains a core temperature of 77 K. The surrounding air temperature drops to –60°C during descent. After 45 minutes of descent, the sensor’s internal temperature rises to 95 K. Determine whether the package will exceed the 100 K operational threshold before reaching the ground 1200 m below. Assume the balloon’s descent rate is constant at 5 m/s, and heat transfer occurs only through conduction across the insulation.

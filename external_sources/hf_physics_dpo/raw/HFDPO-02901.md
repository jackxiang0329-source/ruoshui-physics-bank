A GPS satellite orbits Earth at 20,200 km altitude, moving at 3.87 km/s relative to Earth’s surface. Due to special relativity, its onboard atomic clock runs slower than clocks on the ground. If the satellite’s clock gains 7.2 microseconds per day due to general relativity (gravitational time dilation), how much faster (in microseconds per day) must the satellite’s clock be adjusted to account for special relativity alone?

# HFDPO-04174

A quantum computing chip using topological qubits based on Majorana zero modes in a one-dimensional semiconductor-superconductor heterostructure fails to maintain coherence after 1.2 microseconds at 20 mK. The device exhibits anomalous conductance peaks at zero bias, but also shows a sudden suppression of the zero-bias peak when a magnetic field exceeds 250 mT. Analyze whether this behavior arises from the interplay between proximity-induced superconductivity, spin-orbit coupling, and the Zeeman effect, and determine the minimum effective electron mass required to stabilize Majorana modes under these conditions.

# HFDPO-10788

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to extract heat from a basaltic rock formation at 300°C. The borehole is lined with a 10 cm thick steel casing (k = 50 W/m·K) and insulated with a 5 cm layer of silica aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K). Water flows through the inner tube at 200°C, cooling to 160°C over a 2 km depth. The system must maintain a minimum heat transfer rate of 15 MW to sustain grid output. What is the required inner tube diameter if the convective heat transfer coefficient inside the tube is 2500 W/m²·K? Consider transient effects due to variable rock temperature with depth.

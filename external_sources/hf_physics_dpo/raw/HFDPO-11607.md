# HFDPO-11607

If the satellite’s clock is synchronized to GPS atomic clocks (accurate to 10⁻¹⁰ s), what is the required precision in the satellite’s atomic clock to ensure the entanglement correlation is not masked by time-dilation-induced phase drift over a 10-minute observation window?

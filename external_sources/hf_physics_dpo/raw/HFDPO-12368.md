# HFDPO-12368

A research team is developing a passive cooling system for solar-powered microgrids in arid regions, where nighttime temperatures can plunge to 5°C after daytime peaks of 45°C. They plan to store thermal energy in a 200 kg concrete slab (specific heat 880 J/kg·K) during the day, then release it slowly at night to maintain a stable output. However, the slab is insulated with a 15 cm layer of aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K), and heat loss occurs via conduction and radiation. At night, the surrounding air is at 5°C, but convective heat transfer is negligible due to low wind. What is the time required for the slab to cool from 40°C to 30°C, assuming the emissivity is 0.9 and the slab radiates from both sides (area = 4 m²)?

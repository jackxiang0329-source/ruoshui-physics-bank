# HFDPO-05389

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarizing filter array to monitor ocean surface reflectance for climate modeling. The filters are arranged in a rotating quadrant pattern with transmission axes at 0°, 45°, 90°, and 135°. During a calibration pass over a calm ocean, the sensor records intensities of 125, 75, 100, and 80 W/m² respectively. The expected sunlight is unpolarized but glint at the surface introduces partial polarization. What is the degree of polarization of the reflected light, and what is the orientation of the dominant polarization axis?

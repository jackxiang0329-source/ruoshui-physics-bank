# HFDPO-15543

The water enters at 15°C and must reach 300°C to generate steam efficiently. The pump must overcome both gravity and viscous drag, with the total resistance force modeled as F_{text{res}} = alpha v + beta v^2 , where alpha = 0.04 text{N·s/m} and beta = 0.002 text{kg/m} . The pump delivers 12 kW of mechanical power. What is the maximum sustainable flow rate (in L/min) such that the water reaches 300°C by the time it exits the borehole, assuming no heat loss to the surrounding rock?

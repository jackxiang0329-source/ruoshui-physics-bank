# HFDPO-04668

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland is experiencing unexpected lateral stress on its central support column due to asymmetric thermal expansion in the surrounding basaltic rock. The 15-meter-tall column, pinned at the base and fixed at the top to a rigid steel ring, shows measurable shear displacement at mid-height. The rock exerts a non-uniform distributed load, peaking at 85 kN/m at 7.5 m depth and decreasing linearly to 20 kN/m at the surface. Calculate the horizontal reaction force at the base and determine whether the column’s design (yield strength 450 MPa) is at risk of shear failure, given a cross-sectional area of 0.8 m².

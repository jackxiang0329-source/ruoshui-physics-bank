# HFDPO-15571

A solar farm in a remote village uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic panels wired in a mixed series-parallel configuration to power a community center. Each panel produces 18 V and can deliver up to 5 A. During a cloudy day, one panel (Panel E) fails, dropping to 6 V output due to partial shading. The system’s main load draws 30 A total, distributed across three branch circuits. Using Kirchhoff’s laws, determine the current through the branch containing the faulty panel, given that the voltage drop across the other two branches is 14 V.

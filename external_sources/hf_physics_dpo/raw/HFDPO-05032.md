# HFDPO-05032

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a solar-powered circuit with two panels in parallel, each with 12 V open-circuit voltage and internal resistance of 0.8 Ω. The system charges a 24 V battery through a charge controller that draws 1.5 A constant current. One panel is partially shaded by snow, reducing its effective voltage to 8 V while keeping the same internal resistance. What is the current through the unshaded panel? Assume the charge controller maintains 24 V across the battery.

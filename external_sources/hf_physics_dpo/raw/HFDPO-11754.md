# HFDPO-11754

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to detect changes in reef structure. During a deployment, she notices a 30% drop in the intensity of a 350 Hz sound wave after it travels 200 m through a reef zone. She suspects the drop is due to both absorption and scattering from reef rubble. Given that the wave’s amplitude decreases by a factor of 1.4 over the same distance, determine the absorption coefficient in dB/km if scattering contributes 12 dB/km of attenuation.

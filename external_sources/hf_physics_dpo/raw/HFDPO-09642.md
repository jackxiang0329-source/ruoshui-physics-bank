# HFDPO-09642

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden plasma jet breach, ejecting 2.5 kg of superheated deuterium-tritium plasma at 1.2 × 10⁶ m/s tangentially across a 1.8 m radius toroidal wall. Engineers must determine if the resulting angular momentum change will destabilize the magnetic confinement before corrective fields activate. The vessel has a moment of inertia of 4.3 × 10⁴ kg·m². Calculate the final angular velocity of the vessel and assess whether the shift exceeds the stability threshold of 0.015 rad/s.

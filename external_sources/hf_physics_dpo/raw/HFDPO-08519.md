# HFDPO-08519

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a custom fisheye lens system detects faint bioluminescent signals from a newly discovered hydrothermal vent community. The lens, with a 180° field of view, suffers from severe spherical aberration, distorting the shape of glowing organisms. To correct this, an engineer proposes placing a corrective meniscus lens (n = 1.52) with one spherical surface of radius 15 cm and the other flat. If the original lens forms blurred images of objects 20 m away, what should be the radius of curvature of the concave surface of the meniscus lens to perfectly focus the distant light onto the sensor plane, 10 cm behind the lens system? Assume the system operates underwater (n = 1.33).

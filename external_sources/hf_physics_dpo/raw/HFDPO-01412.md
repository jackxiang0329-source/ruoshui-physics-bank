# HFDPO-01412

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. On a clear summer day, 12,000 kg of salt (specific heat capacity 1,500 J/kg·K) is heated from 250°C to 560°C. At night, the salt cools down to 300°C, transferring energy to a steam turbine. However, due to insulation degradation, the system loses 12% of the stored energy as heat to the environment during cooling. What is the net useful work output, assuming the turbine operates with 38% efficiency?

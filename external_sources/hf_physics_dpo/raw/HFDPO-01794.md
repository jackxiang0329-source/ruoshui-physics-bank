# HFDPO-01794

A deep-sea hydrophone array detects a low-frequency seismic signal (f = 12 Hz) propagating through the ocean floor and water column after an underwater landslide on the continental slope. The wave travels through basalt (density 2800 kg/m³, bulk modulus 3.5 × 10¹⁰ Pa) and then enters seawater (bulk modulus 2.3 × 10⁹ Pa, density 1025 kg/m³). The signal is observed 9.6 seconds after the landslide initiation at a receiver 5.2 km away. Calculate the wave speed in the basalt layer, assuming the signal spends 3.4 s traveling through the ocean layer.

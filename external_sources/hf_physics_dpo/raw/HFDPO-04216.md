# HFDPO-04216

A solar thermal power plant uses molten salt (specific heat 1400 J/kg·K, density 1800 kg/m³) stored in an insulated cylindrical tank (diameter 8 m, height 10 m) to generate electricity over 12 hours. The salt is heated to 560°C during the day and must maintain a minimum temperature of 480°C at the end of the night to sustain turbine operation. However, the tank loses heat through convection (h = 12 W/m²·K, ambient 20°C) and radiation (ε = 0.95, σ = 5.67×10⁻⁸ W/m²·K⁴). What is the minimum insulation thickness (k = 0.04 W/m·K) required to meet the temperature constraint?

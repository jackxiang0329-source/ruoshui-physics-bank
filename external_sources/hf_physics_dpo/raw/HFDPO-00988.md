# HFDPO-00988

A cryogenic storage tank for fusion-grade deuterium-tritium fuel is designed to maintain a supercooled liquid state at 18 K, but during a pressure fluctuation from a faulty regulator, localized regions of the liquid drop below the thermodynamic triple point pressure of 1.5 × 10⁻⁴ atm. The tank’s inner wall, coated with a microstructured surface intended to suppress nucleation, suddenly exhibits spontaneous solidification in thin films. Estimate the minimum energy barrier (in eV) for homogeneous nucleation of solid deuterium under these conditions, considering the interfacial energy and entropy of fusion, and explain why the microstructure might have failed despite being engineered to inhibit phase transition.

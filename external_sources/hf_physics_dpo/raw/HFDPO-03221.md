# HFDPO-03221

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km deep into hot rock. To extract heat, a fluid is pumped down the hole at a rate of 150 kg/s, rising through a coiled pipe embedded in the rock. The fluid enters at 50°C and exits at 180°C, with negligible pressure drop. The system must overcome viscous drag and gravitational potential in the return flow. Given the pipe has a 10 cm diameter and the fluid’s average viscosity is 0.015 Pa·s, determine the minimum power input required from the pump to sustain this flow, and evaluate whether the thermal energy recovered justifies the energy cost of pumping.

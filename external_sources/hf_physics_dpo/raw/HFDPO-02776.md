# HFDPO-02776

A solar-powered drone, designed for high-altitude atmospheric research, uses a lightweight carbon-fiber truss structure to support its 2.4 kg payload and 1.8 kg solar array. During a test flight, engineers notice the nose of the drone tilts downward by 3.5° when stationary in calm air. The center of mass of the payload is 0.32 m forward of the truss pivot, while the solar array is offset 0.45 m aft and has a center of mass 0.18 m below the pivot. Assuming the truss applies a single reaction force at the pivot and no moments, what is the required horizontal placement of the drone’s center of mass relative to the pivot to maintain neutral pitch?

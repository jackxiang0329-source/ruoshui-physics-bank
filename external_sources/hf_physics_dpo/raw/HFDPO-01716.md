# HFDPO-01716

A remote weather station in Antarctica relies on a small Stirling engine powered by a propane heater to generate electricity for sensors and communication gear. The engine operates between a hot reservoir at 420 K (from the heater) and a cold reservoir at 250 K (ambient air). The station needs 120 W of continuous electrical power, but the engine’s mechanical output is only 85% efficient at converting heat to work. If the heater supplies heat at a rate of 600 W, what fraction of the heat input is lost to the environment, and could the system sustain operation during a 48-hour storm when solar backup is unavailable?

# HFDPO-14140

During a deep-sea research mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged pipeline rupture at 1,200 meters depth. The leak releases fresh water into the surrounding seawater, forming a visible plume. Engineers must estimate the upward velocity of the plume’s leading edge to predict dispersion and prevent environmental damage. Given the density of seawater (1025 kg/m³), the plume’s fresh water has a density of 1000 kg/m³, and the pressure difference across the interface drives buoyancy-driven flow. What is the initial upward velocity of the plume if the pressure difference is due solely to the hydrostatic head imbalance?

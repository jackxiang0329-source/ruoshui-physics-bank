# HFDPO-02998

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 12 cm diameter silicon wafer charged to −8.0 nC is suspended by a fine insulating fiber. A nearby grounded metal shield, 3.0 mm away, develops a small 5.0 μm-radius defect that collects a localized charge of +120 pC. Calculate the magnitude and direction of the net electrostatic force on the wafer, accounting for both the shield’s uniform field and the point-like perturbation. Assume the wafer is a uniformly charged circular disk and the electric field from the defect dominates near the surface.

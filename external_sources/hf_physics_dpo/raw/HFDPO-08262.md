# HFDPO-08262

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must hover precisely above a remote ridge at 800 m elevation while navigating strong crosswinds. The drone’s mass is 3.2 kg, and its propellers generate a maximum thrust of 38 N. When a 15 m/s wind gust strikes perpendicular to its intended hover direction, the drone begins to drift. To maintain position, it must adjust its thrust vector. What is the minimum angle (from vertical) the drone must tilt its thrust vector to counteract the wind drift, assuming the wind exerts a drag force proportional to velocity squared with a coefficient of 0.45 kg/m?

# HFDPO-09280

During a deep-space mission, a spacecraft's cryogenic fuel tank (volume 12 m³) is cooled to 20 K to maintain liquid hydrogen, but a micro-meteoroid strike causes a localized rupture at 25 K, triggering a rapid, non-equilibrium phase transition. The tank's inner surface has a superhydrophobic coating with a nucleation barrier of 1.8 × 10⁻⁹ J/m². Given that the interfacial energy between liquid and solid H₂ is 5.2 × 10⁻⁴ J/m² and the latent heat of fusion is 3.6 × 10⁴ J/kg, determine whether solidification will occur spontaneously at the rupture site, and if so, estimate the minimum critical radius of the solid nucleus.

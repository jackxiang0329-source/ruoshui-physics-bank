# HFDPO-08091

A coastal engineer is designing a new breakwater to protect a harbor from destructive storm swells. Measurements show that during peak storms, ocean waves have a wavelength of 42 meters and travel at 15 m/s. The breakwater must be constructed so that waves diffract just enough to reduce wave energy inside the harbor, but not so much that the harbor becomes unnavigable. What is the minimum width of the breakwater’s opening to allow significant diffraction of 42-m waves while keeping the central wave peak within a 5° angular spread?

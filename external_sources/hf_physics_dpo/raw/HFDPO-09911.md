# HFDPO-09911

A satellite in low Earth orbit experiences extreme thermal cycling as it transitions from direct sunlight to Earth’s shadow every 90 minutes. Its outer surface, coated with a multi-layer insulation (MLI) blanket, has an effective emissivity of 0.05 and absorbs 90% of incident solar radiation. If the satellite’s average solar flux is 1360 W/m² and the internal heat generation is 450 W (distributed uniformly in a 1.2 m³ volume), what is the maximum steady-state surface temperature of the satellite during sunlight exposure, assuming it radiates exclusively from its outer shell and the internal heat conducts through to the surface with negligible thermal resistance?

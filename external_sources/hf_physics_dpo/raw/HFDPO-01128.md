# HFDPO-01128

A quantum imaging lab develops a single-photon camera for detecting exoplanet atmospheres via starlight transit, but interference from atmospheric turbulence corrupts the quantum state of entangled photon pairs. The system uses a 780 nm pump laser (30 mW) to generate entangled photon pairs via spontaneous parametric down-conversion in a 2 cm BBO crystal. To maintain entanglement fidelity above 0.95 under turbulent conditions, the system must compensate for spatial mode distortion. However, the wavefront sensor detects a 150 nm RMS phase aberration across a 5 mm pupil. Determine the minimum number of adaptive optics correction elements needed in a deformable mirror to resolve the dominant spatial modes and restore entanglement, assuming Zernike mode correction and a 100 kHz update rate.

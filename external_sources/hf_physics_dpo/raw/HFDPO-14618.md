# HFDPO-14618

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (radius 10 cm) operates at 1.5 MHz with 0.8 H inductance and 5 Ω resistance, while the receiver coil (radius 2 cm) has 0.15 H inductance and 2 Ω resistance. The mutual inductance is 12 μH, and the system must deliver 50 mW to the receiver with at least 70% efficiency. The transmitter is driven by a 10 V RMS voltage source. Determine the required capacitance values for both coils to achieve resonance, and evaluate whether the system meets efficiency and power delivery requirements.

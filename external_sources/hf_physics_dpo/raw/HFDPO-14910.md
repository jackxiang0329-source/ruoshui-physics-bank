# HFDPO-14910

A biomedical engineer is designing a photonic sensor to detect early-stage cancer by measuring changes in light scattering from tissue samples. The sensor uses a laser with λ = 633 nm shining through a microfluidic chip with a periodic array of nanoscale pores (d = 500 nm), creating a diffraction pattern on a detector. When healthy tissue is analyzed, the first-order maxima appear at ±18°. After introducing a sample with tumor markers, the pattern shifts, and the first maxima now appear at ±16°. What is the effective refractive index of the altered tissue, assuming the pore spacing remains constant?

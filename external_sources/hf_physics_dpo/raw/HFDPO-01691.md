# HFDPO-01691

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanoparticles to harvest sunlight, but its efficiency drops sharply above 300 K due to phonon-assisted recombination. Each quantum dot contains 1000 electrons confined in a 3D harmonic potential with energy spacing Δε = 0.1 eV. At room temperature, what fraction of electrons occupy the first excited state, and how does this fraction change if the dot is cooled to 77 K? Assume Fermi-Dirac statistics and degenerate ground state with g₀ = 2.

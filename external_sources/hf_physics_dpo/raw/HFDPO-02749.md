# HFDPO-02749

A team of engineers at a fusion reactor prototype site detects a sudden spike in neutron flux from the deuterium-tritium plasma, increasing from 1.2×10¹⁴ n/s to 4.5×10¹⁴ n/s over 30 seconds. The reactor’s neutron shielding uses a 15 cm layer of lithium-6 carbide. Given that lithium-6 has a thermal neutron capture cross-section of 940 barns and the neutron energy spectrum is thermal (avg. energy 0.025 eV), determine whether the increase in flux is due to a plasma instability or a failure in the neutron detector calibration. Assume the lithium-6 density is 2.2 g/cm³ and the system operates at 150 million K.

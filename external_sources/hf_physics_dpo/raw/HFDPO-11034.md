# HFDPO-11034

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for delicate coronary arteries. The stent’s central segment is a helical spring made of a nickel-titanium alloy (NiTi) with a Young’s modulus of 45 GPa, a radius of 0.3 mm, and 12 turns. When implanted, the stent must expand radially by 1.8 mm under a physiological pressure of 120 mmHg. What is the minimum applied axial force needed to achieve this expansion, and how does the stored elastic energy during deployment compare to the energy dissipated in the surrounding tissue? Assume the spring constant is derived from torsional elasticity and neglect plastic deformation.

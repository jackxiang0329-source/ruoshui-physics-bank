# HFDPO-07703

A lunar lander descends near the south pole of the Moon, where a patch of shadowed ice has formed a 25° incline with a coefficient of static friction μ_s = 0.35 and kinetic friction μ_k = 0.28. The lander's retrothrusters provide a variable thrust, but due to a sensor glitch, they only activate at 60% efficiency. At the moment of touchdown, the lander’s velocity is 0.8 m/s down the slope, and the control system attempts to maintain zero acceleration. What thrust magnitude and direction must the thrusters apply to prevent sliding, and how does this change if the ice surface is disturbed, reducing μ_s to 0.20?

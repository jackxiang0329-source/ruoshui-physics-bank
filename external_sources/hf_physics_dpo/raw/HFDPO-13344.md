# HFDPO-13344

A high-voltage power transmission line spans 10 km through a forested valley, suspended between towers 30 m above ground. Due to moisture in the air and nearby trees, the line develops parasitic capacitance to ground, creating a leakage current that reduces efficiency. Engineers estimate the line has a capacitance of 1.2 μF per kilometer. If the line operates at 230 kV RMS at 60 Hz, what is the effective capacitive reactance of the entire line, and how much reactive power (in MVAR) is being drawn by this parasitic capacitance?

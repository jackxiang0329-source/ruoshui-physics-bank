# HFDPO-02199

A spacecraft returning from Mars uses a controlled aerobraking maneuver through the thin upper atmosphere, experiencing variable drag due to changing atmospheric density. At an altitude of 120 km, it enters at 5.8 km/s with a lift-to-drag ratio of 0.4, and its trajectory must be adjusted in real time to avoid either burning up or skipping out of the atmosphere. Given the atmospheric density follows ρ(h) = ρ₀e⁻(h−h₀)/H with ρ₀ = 0.015 kg/m³ at h₀ = 100 km and scale height H = 12 km, determine the minimum safe angle of attack required to maintain a descent rate of 1.2 km/s while safely dissipating kinetic energy over a 300 km horizontal arc.

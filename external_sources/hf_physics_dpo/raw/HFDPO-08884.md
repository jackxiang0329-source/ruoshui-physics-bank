# HFDPO-08884

A biomedical engineer is designing a laser-based early-detection system for skin cancer using two wavelengths—405 nm (violet) and 635 nm (red)—to probe tissue with different absorption profiles. The 405 nm light is strongly absorbed by melanin, while the 635 nm light penetrates deeper but scatters more. In a test, the 635 nm beam enters the skin at a 30° angle, undergoes total internal reflection at the epidermis-dermis boundary (n = 1.40), but the 405 nm beam does not. What is the minimum refractive index of the epidermis required for total internal reflection of 635 nm light but not for 405 nm light, given that the dermis has n = 1.40?

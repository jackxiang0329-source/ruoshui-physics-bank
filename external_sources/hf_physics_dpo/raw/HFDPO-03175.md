# HFDPO-03175

Post-irradiation characterization shows a 23% reduction in short-circuit current, yet photoluminescence spectra indicate minimal defect-related recombination. The measured carrier lifetime decreases from 180 ps to 65 ps under irradiation. Given that displacement damage is modeled by a 10¹² cm⁻² fluence of 1 MeV equivalent neutrons, determine whether the efficiency loss is primarily due to increased non-radiative recombination at interface traps or to a shift in the Fermi level caused by radiation-induced bulk doping. Consider the role of carrier capture by radiation-induced defects with energy levels near mid-gap and analyze the impact on carrier extraction.

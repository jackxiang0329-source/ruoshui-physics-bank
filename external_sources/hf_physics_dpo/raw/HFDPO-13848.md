# HFDPO-13848

A solar-powered remote weather station in a high-altitude desert relies on a network of 12 identical photovoltaic cells arranged in a series-parallel configuration to charge a 24 V battery bank. During a sudden dust storm, the light intensity drops to 30% of nominal, and one of the 12 cells develops a partial short (internal resistance of 0.5 Ω), while the rest remain at 1.0 Ω. The battery bank has an internal resistance of 0.2 Ω, and a 10 Ω load draws power for telemetry. Determine the voltage delivered to the load and explain how the system efficiency drops compared to ideal conditions.

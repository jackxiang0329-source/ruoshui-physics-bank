# HFDPO-01084

A fusion energy research team designs a tokamak reactor with a toroidal plasma chamber surrounded by a complex array of magnetic coils. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the magnitude of the radial electric field at a point r = 2.6 m inside the vacuum gap.

# HFDPO-00621

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain peak is secured by a cable system anchored to bedrock, but recent avalanches have shifted the anchor points. The main support pole, 8 meters tall and weighing 120 kg, is tilted 15° from vertical due to uneven ground. The cable, attached 5 meters up the pole, runs horizontally to a new anchor 6 meters from the base. Given the pole’s center of mass is at its midpoint and the system is in static equilibrium, what is the tension in the cable, and does the anchor experience sufficient friction (μ_s = 0.6) to prevent slipping if the maximum static friction force is based on the normal force from the ground?

# HFDPO-09319

A solar farm in Arizona uses thin-film photovoltaic panels connected in a series-parallel array to power a remote research station. Each panel produces 400 W under peak sunlight, but due to shading from nearby rock formations, only 75% of the panels receive full irradiance. The system uses 12 AWG copper wiring (resistivity = 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m) to connect the panels to the inverter, with a total wire length of 600 m. If the system must deliver 85% of its theoretical power to the load with minimal losses, what minimum cross-sectional area must the wires have?

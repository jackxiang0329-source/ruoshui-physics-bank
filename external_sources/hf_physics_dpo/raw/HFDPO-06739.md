# HFDPO-06739

In a cryogenic quantum computing setup, a 2D array of superconducting qubits is cooled to 15 mK, where thermal fluctuations are minimized but residual phonons from the substrate still induce decoherence. What is the critical phonon density (per unit area) above which thermal excitation of the lowest-energy phonon mode exceeds the ground state population by a factor of 100, potentially destabilizing quantum coherence?

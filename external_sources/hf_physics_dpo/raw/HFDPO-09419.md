# HFDPO-09419

A rotating space habitat on Mars, designed to simulate Earth-like gravity via centrifugal force, has a 200-meter diameter ring with a non-uniform mass distribution: 60% of the mass is concentrated in the outer rim (radius 100 m), and 40% is in a central hub (radius 20 m). At a certain point, a 70 kg astronaut walks from the hub toward the rim, changing the system’s moment of inertia. The rotational kinetic energy decreases by 12% during the walk. What is the new angular speed? Assume no external torques and uniform frictional torque opposing rotation.

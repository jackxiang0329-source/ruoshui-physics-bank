# HFDPO-09619

A quantum computing lab uses a niobium-based superconducting loop to store quantum information. The loop has a critical current density of 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m² and a cross-sectional radius of 0.2 mm. A persistent current of 5.0 μA is established at 4.2 K. If the loop is cooled below its critical temperature in the presence of a 30 μT magnetic field aligned with the loop’s axis, will the flux quantum be trapped? Calculate the magnetic flux through the loop and determine whether the current can remain persistent without dissipation.

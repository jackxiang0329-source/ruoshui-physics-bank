# HFDPO-03017

A coastal city is designing a wave-energy harvester that uses a submerged, horizontal U-tube turbine placed at a depth of 12 meters, where ocean currents flow at 2.8 m/s. The turbine extracts energy by creating a pressure drop across its blades, reducing local flow speed to 1.3 m/s. If the turbine’s inlet and outlet diameters are 0.6 m and 1.4 m respectively, and seawater density is 1025 kg/m³, calculate the net pressure difference across the turbine and determine whether cavitation is likely to occur, given the vapor pressure of seawater at 15°C is 1.7 kPa.

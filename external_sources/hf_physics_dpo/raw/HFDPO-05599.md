# HFDPO-05599

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar array charges a 24 V battery bank through a maximum power point tracker (MPPT) that behaves as a variable resistor. The system must power a climate control unit requiring 1.5 kW at 24 V during peak winter nights when solar input drops to 300 W. However, the MPPT’s efficiency degrades by 15% when the battery voltage sags below 22 V due to internal resistance. If the battery has a 0.3 Ω internal resistance and the load draws constant power, what is the minimum battery voltage that allows stable operation without overloading the MPPT?

# HFDPO-10123

A wind turbine in a remote village spins at 30 rpm when the wind speed is 12 m/s. After a sudden gust increases the wind speed, the turbine’s rotational speed jumps to 45 rpm. Assuming no mechanical losses and that the turbine’s blades are rigid, what is the new wind speed, if angular momentum is conserved during the brief gust?

# HFDPO-14352

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses a toroidal field coil with 1200 turns, carrying 15 kA, to stabilize plasma at 150 million K. During a sudden current drop to 5 kA over 0.2 s, the coil induces a large back-EMF that risks quenching superconducting windings. To mitigate this, engineers install a shunt resistor network in parallel with the coil. What must be the resistance of the shunt network to limit the induced voltage across the coil to 200 V during the current decay, assuming the coil’s self-inductance is 3.5 H?

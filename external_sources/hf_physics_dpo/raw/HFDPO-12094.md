# HFDPO-12094

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial solar panels mounted at a 30° tilt to capture both direct and reflected sunlight. However, glare from the polished aluminum backing of the panels causes interference with nearby drone navigation systems, which rely on polarization-sensitive cameras. The reflected light from the panels is partially polarized, with the electric field vector aligned horizontally. If the panels reflect 45% of incident sunlight with a degree of polarization of 60%, and the drone’s camera has a polarization filter that can be rotated to block 90% of horizontally polarized light, what is the minimum angle the filter must be rotated from vertical to reduce glare intensity to less than 10% of the original reflected intensity?

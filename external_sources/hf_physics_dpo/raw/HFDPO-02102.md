# HFDPO-02102

During a dust storm on Mars, a scientific rover’s solar panel accumulates a non-uniform charge due to triboelectric effects with airborne silica particles. The panel, a 1.2 m × 0.6 m rectangular surface, develops a surface charge density that varies as σ(x) = σ₀(1 + 0.5 sin(πx/L)), where x is measured from one edge, L = 1.2 m, and σ₀ = 3.0 × 10⁻⁷ C/m². A nearby sensor detects an electric field of 120 N/C at a point 0.3 m directly above the center of the panel. What is the actual surface charge distribution responsible, and how does the non-uniformity affect the field?

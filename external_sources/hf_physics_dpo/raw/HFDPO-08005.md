# HFDPO-08005

A biomedical engineer is designing a non-invasive device to detect early-stage tumor growth using dielectric properties of tissue. The system applies a 10 MHz sinusoidal signal across a pair of electrodes placed on the skin, with the tissue modeled as a parallel RLC circuit. Measurements show a resonant frequency shift from 10.0 MHz to 10.2 MHz when tumor-like tissue (higher permittivity) replaces healthy tissue. If the inductance is fixed at 2.5 μH and the original resistance is 100 Ω, what is the change in the capacitive reactance due to the tumor? Assume the resistance doesn’t change significantly.

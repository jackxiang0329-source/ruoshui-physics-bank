# HFDPO-07091

A solar-powered desalination plant in a remote desert region uses a parabolic trough collector to heat seawater in a long, insulated pipe. The pipe, 10 m long and 5 cm in diameter, carries seawater at 15°C at a flow rate of 0.2 L/s. The solar flux absorbed averages 800 W/m² over the collector’s surface, but thermal losses to the ambient air (25°C) occur via convection (h = 12 W/m²·K) and radiation (emissivity 0.9). If the plant must deliver water at 95°C to maintain efficient distillation, what minimum solar flux (in W/m²) must be sustained, assuming no phase change during heating and constant specific heat capacity (c_p = 4180 J/kg·K)?

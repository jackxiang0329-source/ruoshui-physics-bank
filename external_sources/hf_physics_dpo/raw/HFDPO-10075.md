# HFDPO-10075

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarimetric imaging camera to monitor ocean surface pollution, detecting oil spills via polarization signatures. The camera measures the degree of linear polarization (DOLP) of reflected sunlight, which varies due to surface roughness and refractive index differences between water and oil. At a 45° glancing angle, the measured DOLP is 0.32, but the expected value for pure water is 0.45.

# HFDPO-07611

A laser-based quantum imaging system in a biomedical lab detects subtle phase shifts in light passing through a thin, irregularly structured tumor tissue sample. The light, wavelength 633 nm in air, is split into two beams: one passes through the tissue (path length 1.2 mm with refractive index varying between 1.34 and 1.38), the other traverses a reference path in air. The interference pattern on the detector shows a phase difference of 2.7π radians. Given that the tissue’s thickness varies spatially by ±0.05 mm, and the refractive index follows a sinusoidal gradient across the beam width, determine the effective optical path difference (OPD) and predict whether the phase shift is dominated by refractive index variation or path length uncertainty.

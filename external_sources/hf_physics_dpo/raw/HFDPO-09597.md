# HFDPO-09597

A lunar rover's power system uses a regenerative braking circuit with a 24 V lithium-ion battery bank and a variable-load DC motor. During descent on a steep crater slope, the motor acts as a generator, producing 1.8 A of current while maintaining a constant speed. Measurements show the motor's internal resistance is 0.6 Ω, and the external load resistance (including control electronics) is 4.2 Ω. However, thermal sensors detect localized overheating in the control circuitry. What is the actual power dissipated within the control electronics, and why might the thermal issue persist even if the total circuit power is within limits?

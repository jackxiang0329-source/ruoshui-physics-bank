# HFDPO-00610

In a quantum biology experiment, researchers observe that a light-sensitive protein in a bacterium’s photosynthetic complex absorbs photons at 680 nm with 92% efficiency under natural sunlight. However, when the protein is embedded in a nanostructured lipid membrane with quantum confinement features (10 nm thick), absorption drops to 63%. The team suspects wave-like interference within the membrane alters electron transport. Calculate the minimum number of de Broglie wavelengths that fit across the membrane thickness for an electron with kinetic energy 1.8 eV, and explain how this supports the hypothesis that wave-like behavior affects energy transfer efficiency.

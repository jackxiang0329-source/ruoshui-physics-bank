# HFDPO-00632

In a fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma confinement chamber has a non-uniform charge density distribution: ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − r/R₀) for r < R₀, where r is radial distance from the torus axis, R₀ = 0.8 m, and ρ₀ = 5 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³. The chamber’s major radius is 2.5 m, but the small cross-section is circular with radius 0.5 m. A diagnostic sensor measures an electric field magnitude of 2.3 × 10⁴ N/C at a point 1.2 m from the central axis. Using Gauss’s Law, determine whether this measurement is consistent with the modeled charge distribution, and if not, what must be true about the net charge enclosed.

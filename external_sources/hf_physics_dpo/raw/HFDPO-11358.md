# HFDPO-11358

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a solar array with 12 identical photovoltaic modules, each rated at 30 V and 5 A under standard conditions. The system charges a 24 V lead-acid battery bank through a maximum power point tracker (MPPT) buck converter. Over a week, average irradiance drops to 150 W/m² (down from 1000 W/m²), and ambient temperature rises to 10°C, reducing panel efficiency by 12%. The station draws a variable load: 2 A during daylight (6h), 5 A at night (18h). The MPPT operates with 93% efficiency. Determine the minimum battery capacity (in Ah) needed to sustain operation for 3 consecutive overcast days with no charging.

# HFDPO-01332

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where 200 kg of deuterium-tritium fuel is compressed to 100 times atmospheric pressure and heated to 150 million K, sustaining a reaction for 3 seconds. During this time, 0.5% of the mass is converted to energy via E=mc², and the resulting plasma expels 80% of its kinetic energy as directed radiation. The reactor’s magnetic confinement system uses 2.1 MW of power to maintain stability. Evaluate whether the total energy output (radiative + kinetic) exceeds the input energy required to sustain the system, and assess the feasibility of net energy gain under conservation of energy principles.

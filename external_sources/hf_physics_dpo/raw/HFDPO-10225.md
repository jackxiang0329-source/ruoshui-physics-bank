# HFDPO-10225

A fusion reactor's magnetically confined plasma forms a toroidal (donut-shaped) chamber with a major radius of 2.5 m and a minor radius of 0.6 m. During a plasma instability, charged particles leak radially outward, creating a non-uniform charge density described by ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(–(r – R₀)² / σ²), where R₀ = 1.8 m, σ = 0.3 m, and ρ₀ = 8.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ C/m³. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the magnitude of the electric field at r = 2.0 m, just outside the plasma boundary, and explain why a standard cylindrical symmetry assumption fails here despite the toroidal geometry.

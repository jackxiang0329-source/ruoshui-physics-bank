# HFDPO-09554

A wind turbine blade in a remote Arctic installation begins to experience erratic vibrations during high-wind conditions. Engineers notice that the blade’s rotational speed oscillates despite a constant wind input, and suspect that the blade’s variable moment of inertia due to ice accumulation is causing resonance. At a critical wind speed of 25 m/s, the turbine’s rotor, initially at rest, accelerates under a net torque of 1.8 × 10⁴ N·m. The blade’s moment of inertia increases linearly with angle rotated: I(θ) = I₀(1 + 0.02θ), where I₀ = 1.2 × 10⁵ kg·m² and θ is in radians. What is the angular speed after 100 radians of rotation?

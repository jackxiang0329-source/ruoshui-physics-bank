# HFDPO-05349

A fusion reactor’s liquid lithium blanket, designed to breed tritium and absorb neutron heat, experiences a sudden 12% drop in coolant flow due to a valve malfunction. With the blanket initially at 650°C and exposed to 10 MW/m² neutron flux, determine the time it takes for local temperatures to exceed the safe limit of 800°C. Assume the lithium has a density of 534 kg/m³, specific heat of 3.58 kJ/kg·K, and thermal conductivity of 0.29 W/m·K. The blanket’s effective thickness is 15 cm, and natural convection is negligible during transient.

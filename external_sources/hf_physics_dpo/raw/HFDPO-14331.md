# HFDPO-14331

During a high-altitude drone delivery mission over mountainous terrain, a lightweight cargo pod (mass 2.4 kg, cross-sectional area 0.15 m²) is carried by a tethered quadcopter flying at 35 m/s through a narrow valley where the air pressure drops to 78 kPa due to venturi-like wind acceleration.

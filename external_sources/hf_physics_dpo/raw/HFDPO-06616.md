# HFDPO-06616

A seismic-resistant skyscraper in Tokyo uses a massive tuned mass damper (TMD) consisting of a 500-ton concrete block suspended by high-strength steel cables, designed to counteract resonance during earthquakes. During a simulated 2-second-long tremor, the building's base oscillates horizontally at 0.5 Hz with a peak displacement of 1.2 meters. The TMD is initially displaced 0.8 meters from equilibrium and released with zero velocity. The system's damping ratio is 0.03, and the natural frequency of the TMD is tuned to match the building’s fundamental frequency. Determine the maximum relative displacement between the building and the TMD during the first cycle.

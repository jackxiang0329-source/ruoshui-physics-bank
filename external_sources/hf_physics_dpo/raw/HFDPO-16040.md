# HFDPO-16040

A deep-sea research submersible descends into the Mariana Trench, where pressure increases dramatically with depth. At 10,000 meters, the hull experiences extreme hydrostatic stress. The sub's spherical viewport, 0.5 m in diameter, is made of a titanium alloy with a yield strength of 800 MPa. Engineers must determine the maximum allowable pressure difference across the viewport to prevent failure, but the seawater density increases with depth due to compression. Assuming a non-constant density modeled by ρ(h) = ρ₀(1 + αh), where ρ₀ = 1025 kg/m³, α = 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ m⁻¹, and h = 0 at the surface, what is the maximum safe internal pressure (in atm) the sub can maintain relative to the external ocean at 10,000 m?

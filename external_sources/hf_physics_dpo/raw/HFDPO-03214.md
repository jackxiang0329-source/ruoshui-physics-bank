# HFDPO-03214

A solar-powered off-grid cabin in Alaska uses a 24 V battery bank charged by a 300 W photovoltaic array. The array’s output drops to 70% of rated power during winter due to low sun angle and snow cover. A critical heating system (rated 1500 W at 24 V) must operate for 4 hours daily during winter nights. If the system uses a buck converter to regulate voltage and efficiency is 90% across the converter and battery (charge/discharge), what minimum battery capacity (in Ah) is needed to sustain the heater for 4 hours on a single winter day, assuming no sunlight?

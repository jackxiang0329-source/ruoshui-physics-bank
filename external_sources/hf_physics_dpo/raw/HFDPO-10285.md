# HFDPO-10285

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 320°C, which then drives a Rankine cycle turbine. The condenser operates at 80°C, but during a heatwave, ambient temperatures rise to 45°C, forcing the condenser temperature to increase by 12°C. The turbine’s isentropic efficiency drops to 82% due to thermal stress. If the plant’s design efficiency was 34% under standard conditions, what is the new thermal efficiency, and why might the power output drop more than the efficiency suggests?

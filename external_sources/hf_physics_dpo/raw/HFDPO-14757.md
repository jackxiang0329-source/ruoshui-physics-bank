# HFDPO-14757

In a 2023 deep-sea hydrothermal vent expedition, researchers observe a rare microbial community thriving near a chimney emitting supercritical water at 450°C and 300 atm. The microbes form biofilms with proteins whose folding stability is governed by a 2-state model (folded/unfolded), but the entropy change upon unfolding is strongly temperature-dependent due to altered solvent structure. Given that the enthalpy difference ΔH₀ = 50 kJ/mol is constant, and ΔS(T) = 150 J/mol·K + 0.5 J/mol·K² × (T − 300 K), determine the melting temperature Tₘ where the folded and unfolded states are equally probable. How does this Tₘ differ from the prediction assuming constant entropy?

# HFDPO-15716

A biomedical engineer is designing a novel implantable cardiac monitor that uses a microfabricated capacitor array to detect subtle changes in heart tissue capacitance due to edema (fluid buildup). The sensor consists of two parallel plates, each 1 mm² in area, separated by a 5 μm thick dielectric layer with a variable permittivity (ε = ε₀(1 + 0.1ΔC), where ΔC is the change in normalized capacitance). During a clinical trial, the system detects a 15% increase in capacitance when the patient is in a supine position. Calculate the effective permittivity of the dielectric and estimate the amount of interstitial fluid (assumed to have ε ≈ 80ε₀) infiltrating the tissue layer, assuming the fluid replaces the original dielectric uniformly.

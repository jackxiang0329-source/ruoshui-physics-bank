# HFDPO-04978

A photocopier's corona wire charges a drum with -12 μC/m², but after 10 seconds of operation, paper jams due to electrostatic attraction between the charged drum and a nearby ungrounded metal roller. The roller is 2 cm from the drum surface and has a cylindrical shape 15 cm long. Calculate the magnitude of the electrostatic force per unit length on the roller, assuming the drum's charge is uniform and the roller acts like a neutral conductor influenced by induction.

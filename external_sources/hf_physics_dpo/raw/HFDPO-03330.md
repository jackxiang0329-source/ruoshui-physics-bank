# HFDPO-03330

In a quantum cryptography lab, researchers observe that single photons from an attenuated laser source exhibit interference patterns when passed through a Mach-Zehnder interferometer, but only when both paths are unmeasured. When a single-photon detector is placed in one arm to determine which path the photon took, the interference vanishes. However, when the detector is set to a “weak measurement” mode with a 30% chance of detecting the photon without collapsing its state, the interference fringes degrade by 40%. If the photon’s path information is partially accessible, what is the minimum quantum coherence time required for the system to maintain detectable interference, assuming the path difference is 15 cm and the phase noise due to environmental vibrations introduces a dephasing rate of 2.0 × 10⁹ rad/s?

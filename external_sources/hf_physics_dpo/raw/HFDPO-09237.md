# HFDPO-09237

In a high-vacuum semiconductor manufacturing chamber, a 3.2 mm-diameter spherical silicon particle with surface charge density 1.8 × 10⁻⁶ C/m² is suspended between two horizontally aligned, oppositely charged electrodes spaced 12 mm apart. The lower electrode is at −1.5 kV and the upper at +1.5 kV. Due to a sudden power fluctuation, the system's symmetry is disrupted: the upper electrode drops to +1.0 kV while the lower remains at −1.5 kV. Determine the net electrostatic force magnitude and direction on the particle assuming it remains perfectly spherical and uniformly charged.

# HFDPO-01780

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) cooled to 77 K in a liquid nitrogen bath. During startup, a quench occurs when a small section of the coil loses superconductivity due to a localized heating event. The resulting current density in the normal region generates a magnetic field that induces eddy currents in the adjacent superconducting tape, causing a rapid energy dissipation. What is the minimum critical current density required in the surrounding superconducting material to suppress flux penetration and prevent further quench propagation, given the induced electric field magnitude at the interface and the London penetration depth?

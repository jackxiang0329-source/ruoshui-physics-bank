# HFDPO-03156

A solar-powered desalination plant in a remote desert region uses a parabolic trough to concentrate sunlight onto a blackened copper tube (diameter 5 cm, length 3 m) carrying seawater. The tube absorbs 90% of incident solar flux (1000 W/m² at peak sun), but heat loss occurs via convection (h = 25 W/m²·K) and radiation (ε = 0.95). If the seawater enters at 25°C and must exit as vapor at 100°C (with 2257 kJ/kg latent heat), and the tube’s surface is at steady-state, what is the maximum flow rate (kg/s) of seawater the system can process without exceeding the tube’s thermal capacity?

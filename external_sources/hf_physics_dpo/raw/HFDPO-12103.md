# HFDPO-12103

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanocrystals (2.3 nm diameter) embedded in a polymer matrix to harvest sunlight. At room temperature, the electrons in these dots exhibit quantum confinement, shifting their bandgap from the bulk value of 1.4 eV to 2.1 eV. If the solar spectrum peaks at 500 nm and the cell operates under 1000 W/m² illumination, what fraction of incident photons can be absorbed and contribute to electron-hole pair generation? Assume the absorption coefficient at 500 nm is 1.2 × 10⁵ cm⁻¹ and the dot density is 1.8 × 10¹⁸ cm⁻³.

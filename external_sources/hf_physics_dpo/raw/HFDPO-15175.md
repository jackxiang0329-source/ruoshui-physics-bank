# HFDPO-15175

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber discovers that their insulated sleeping bag—rated at -20°C—loses thermal efficiency after being submerged in a snowmelt stream. The bag’s inner lining is exposed to 0°C water for 10 minutes. Assume the climber’s effective surface area is 1.8 m² and specific heat capacity is 3500 J/kg·K for a 70 kg body.

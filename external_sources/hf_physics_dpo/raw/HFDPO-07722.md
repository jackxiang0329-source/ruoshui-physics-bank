# HFDPO-07722

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels mounted at a 30° tilt to maximize sunlight capture. However, glare from reflected sunlight off the panels is causing interference with nearby aircraft navigation systems. The panels are coated with a thin dielectric layer (n = 1.5) designed to reduce reflection, but glare persists at certain angles. Given that sunlight is naturally unpolarized and the sky is clear, determine the angle of incidence at which the reflected light from the panel becomes completely polarized, and explain how this affects the glare problem.

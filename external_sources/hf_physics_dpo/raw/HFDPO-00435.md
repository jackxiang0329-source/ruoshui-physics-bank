# HFDPO-00435

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice that the drill bit—designed to penetrate basaltic rock—slips intermittently under high torque. The bit, a 15 cm long cylindrical rod of mass 0.8 kg and radius 1.2 cm, is driven by a motor delivering 2.5 N·m of torque. However, the bit slips when rotational acceleration exceeds 80 rad/s², despite a coefficient of static friction of 0.6 between bit and rock. At what angular velocity does slipping first occur, assuming the bit begins from rest and the rock exerts a constant resistive torque of 0.3 N·m?

# HFDPO-05466

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a circularly polarized antenna to receive signals from a ground station, but due to ionospheric Faraday rotation, the polarization state of the signal rotates by 45° on the way up. The ground station transmits with right-handed circular polarization (RHCP), but the satellite’s receiver is optimized for left-handed circular polarization (LHCP). If the signal power drops by 3 dB at the receiver when the polarization mismatch exceeds 30°, what is the effective signal attenuation (in dB) when the Faraday rotation is 45°, and how might a real-time polarization compensator adapt to maintain signal integrity?

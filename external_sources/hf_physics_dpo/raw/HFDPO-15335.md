# HFDPO-15335

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor deforestation in the Amazon operates at a constant altitude using lift from its rotors. During a 2-hour mission, it consumes 1.2 kWh of energy from its batteries, but only 65% of that energy is converted into useful mechanical work to overcome air resistance and maintain lift. The drone’s total mass is 3.8 kg and it flies at an average speed of 14 m/s. Calculate the average drag force the rotors must overcome, and evaluate whether the current energy conversion efficiency is sufficient to extend the mission duration by 30% if the drag were reduced by 20% through aerodynamic redesign.

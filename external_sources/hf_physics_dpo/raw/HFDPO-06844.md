# HFDPO-06844

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a 24 V DC system with two parallel-connected lithium-ion battery banks (each 12 V, 100 Ah) and a solar array that delivers 180 W under peak sunlight. The station’s instruments draw 25 A during data transmission, but only 2 A in standby. A voltage regulator maintains stable 5 V for digital circuits. After a 6-hour polar night with no sunlight, the batteries are at 70% charge. One battery bank has developed a faulty cell, increasing its internal resistance to 0.5 Ω, while the other remains at 0.1 Ω. What is the actual power delivered to the regulator during a 5-minute transmission burst, and will the system survive the next 24-hour cycle?

# HFDPO-11462

A solar farm in a high-altitude desert uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a tilted, reflective array to maximize sunlight capture. However, glare from the panels is causing interference with nearby aircraft radar systems. The panels are coated with a polarizing filter that reduces glare by blocking horizontally polarized light. If sunlight strikes the panel at Brewster’s angle (60°), and the polarizer is oriented to block 95% of the reflected glare, what orientation (angle relative to the vertical) should the polarizer be set to, and what percentage of useful solar energy is lost due to polarization filtering?

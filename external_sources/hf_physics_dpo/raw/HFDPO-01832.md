# HFDPO-01832

A wind turbine blade, 60 meters long and shaped like a tapered cantilever beam with a non-uniform cross-section, experiences extreme loading during a storm. The blade’s mass distribution varies linearly from 400 kg/m at the root to 100 kg/m at the tip, and the wind exerts a distributed force of 5000 N/m (due to pressure and drag) normal to the blade's surface, increasing quadratically from the root. Engineers must determine the maximum bending moment at the root to assess structural integrity—without assuming uniform loading or using standard beam tables.

# HFDPO-11474

A lunar soil transport drone, designed to haul regolith across the surface of Mars, operates with four low-pressure, treaded wheels. During a mission test, engineers observe that the drone’s power draw spikes unexpectedly when traversing a 15° incline covered in fine, cohesive dust (μ_s = 0.6, μ_k = 0.4). Simultaneously, the drag coefficient increases due to dust accumulation on the chassis (C_d = 1.2, effective area A = 0.8 m²). If the drone has a mass of 75 kg and moves at a steady 1.2 m/s, what is the total resistive force it must overcome, and how does the dust-induced drag compare to the frictional force at this speed? Assume Martian atmospheric density is 0.02 kg/m³.

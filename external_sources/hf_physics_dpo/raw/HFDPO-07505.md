# HFDPO-07505

During a lunar dust storm on Mars, a rover’s wheel becomes lodged in a 30 cm-deep regolith trench. The soil has a cohesion of 12 kPa and a friction angle of 30°, while the wheel’s contact area is 0.08 m² with a coefficient of static friction μ_s = 0.6. The motor can deliver up to 450 N·m of torque. If the wheel must rotate without slipping to self-extricate, what is the minimum radius required for the wheel to generate sufficient tractive force without exceeding the torque limit—accounting for both soil resistance and rolling drag due to shear deformation at the wheel-soil interface?

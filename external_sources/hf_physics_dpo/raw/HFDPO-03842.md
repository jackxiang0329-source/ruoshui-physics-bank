# HFDPO-03842

A marine biologist is using underwater acoustic tags to track migrating whales in the Pacific Ocean. The tags emit a continuous 15 kHz signal, but due to varying water temperatures, the wave speed changes from 1450 m/s in cooler surface layers to 1520 m/s in deeper, warmer layers. If a tag emits a pulse that travels 2000 m through a layered ocean (1000 m at 1450 m/s, then 1000 m at 1520 m/s), what is the total time delay between emission and reception? How does this compare to the time if the water were uniform at 1485 m/s?

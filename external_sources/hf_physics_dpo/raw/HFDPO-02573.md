# HFDPO-02573

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal magnetic confinement chamber to contain plasma at 150 million Kelvin. During a startup sequence, 2.4 × 10²⁰ deuterium-tritium fusion reactions occur per second, releasing energy primarily as 14 MeV neutrons and 3.5 MeV alpha particles. The neutron flux is partially absorbed by a lithium blanket, generating tritium and transferring kinetic energy to the blanket. If the magnet coils exert a total force of 5.2 × 10⁶ N on the plasma due to the J × B force, and the system’s total momentum must remain conserved, what is the recoil velocity of the entire reactor structure (mass 12,000 kg) after 10 seconds of operation? Assume no external forces act on the reactor during this interval.

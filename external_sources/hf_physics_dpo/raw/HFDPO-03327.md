# HFDPO-03327

A planetary rover on Mars encounters a sandy trench 1.2 m wide and 0.4 m deep. To cross without getting stuck, it must rotate its rear wheels at 25 rad/s while maintaining a steady forward speed of 0.8 m/s. The wheels have a mass of 18 kg, radius 0.3 m, and are solid cylinders. As the rover enters the trench, the rear wheels begin to dig into the sand, creating a torque due to resistance that increases linearly with depth from 0 N·m at the surface to 45 N·m at full submersion. Determine whether the wheels can sustain the required rotational speed without slipping or stalling, using both energy and torque analysis.

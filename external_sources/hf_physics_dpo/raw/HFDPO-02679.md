# HFDPO-02679

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size and deformability. The device uses a narrow channel (width = 50 μm, length = 2 mm) with a constant pressure gradient to drive flow. Healthy red blood cells (diameter ~7 μm, viscosity ~3 cP) are modeled as rigid spheres, while cancer cells (diameter ~15 μm, viscosity ~15 cP) are modeled as viscous droplets. If the pressure drop is 12 kPa, what is the ratio of flow speeds between a single cancer cell and a red blood cell in the center of the channel, assuming both are fully suspended and the flow is laminar?

# HFDPO-04324

A quantum computing chip uses a loop of high-temperature superconducting material (YBCO) with inductance 2.5 nH and resistance approaching zero at 77 K. During operation, a persistent current of 12 μA is induced. When the chip cools from 300 K to 77 K, a magnetic flux quantum (Φ₀ = 2.07×10⁻¹⁵ Wb) is trapped in the loop. Given the loop's critical current density is 3×10⁶ A/m² and circumference is 4 mm, determine whether flux quantization will stabilize the current or if the loop will experience a flux jump due to vortex penetration.

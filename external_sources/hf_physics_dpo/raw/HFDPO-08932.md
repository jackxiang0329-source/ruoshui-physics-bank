# HFDPO-08932

A biopharmaceutical company is developing a novel, needle-free drug delivery system using focused ultrasound to transiently open pores in skin tissue. The system must ensure that maximum strain remains below 12% to avoid irreversible damage, but the material parameters (C₁₀ = 0.12 MPa, C₀₁ = 0.08 MPa) are uncertain due to donor variability. What is the maximum allowable ultrasound pressure amplitude to stay within safe deformation limits, and how does uncertainty in C₁₀ affect the design margin?

# HFDPO-00295

A biomedical engineer is designing a focused ultrasound therapy system for non-invasive tumor ablation. The transducer array emits 2.5 MHz ultrasound waves with a 60 dB intensity at the focal point, but the patient’s skin reflects 45% of the incident energy due to impedance mismatch. To achieve therapeutic heating, the focal intensity must exceed 1500 W/m². Given that the transducer has a diameter of 12 cm, the tissue’s acoustic impedance is 1.6 × 10⁶ Rayl, and skin impedance is 1.4 × 10⁶ Rayl, what is the minimum required source intensity (in W/m²) just outside the transducer to compensate for reflection losses and reach the target at the focus?

# HFDPO-08304

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size-dependent migration in a serpentine channel. The channel, 50 μm wide and 200 μm long, has a sinusoidal cross-section with a peak-to-peak height variation of 10 μm, and blood flows at 0.1 mL/min. The flow is laminar (Re ≈ 0.2), and the effective viscosity of blood is 3.5 mPa·s. How does the non-uniform geometry induce lateral migration of 15 μm diameter cells (density 1060 kg/m³) versus 8 μm platelets (density 1020 kg/m³)? Determine the relative transverse distance traveled by each particle type over one channel cycle, assuming no-slip at walls and steady flow.

# HFDPO-10059

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable cardiac monitor using a low-power, battery-operated circuit to detect subtle changes in skin impedance, which correlate with heart rate variability. The sensor array uses a precision Wheatstone bridge with four 10 kΩ resistors, but one resistor (R₁) is replaced by a variable impedance sensor that varies from 9.8 kΩ (rest) to 10.2 kΩ (during stress). The bridge is powered by a 5 V DC source. The output voltage is amplified by an instrumentation amplifier with a gain of 100. The engineer needs to determine the minimum detectable impedance change and ensure the signal remains within the 0–3.3 V ADC range. What is the maximum allowable offset in the amplifier’s output due to component tolerances if the system must detect a 0.1% change in R₁?

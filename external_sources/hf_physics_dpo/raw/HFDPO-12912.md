# HFDPO-12912

A fusion reactor plasma ring in a tokamak sustains a toroidal current of 15 MA, generating a poloidal magnetic field that confines the plasma. During a sudden disruption, the plasma loses its magnetic confinement in 2.3 ms, and the stored magnetic energy is rapidly converted into thermal energy and kinetic energy of charged particles. If the initial magnetic energy is 4.8 GJ and 78% of this energy is deposited into the reactor’s first wall through radiation and particle flux, calculate the average power delivered to the wall during the disruption. Use conservation of energy and momentum principles to analyze whether the resulting thermal stress could exceed the wall’s yield strength (180 MPa) given a 1.2 cm thick tungsten alloy layer.

# HFDPO-03709

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical weather sensor array operates on a custom 12 V DC power bus powered by solar panels during the day and a bank of lithium-ion batteries at night. The system uses a single 10 Ω shunt resistor to monitor total current draw via voltage drop. One winter, operators notice the sensor readings drift erratically. Upon inspection, the shunt resistor has developed a 5% resistance increase due to thermal cycling, but the data logger assumes the original 10 Ω value. Meanwhile, the battery bank’s internal resistance has risen to 0.8 Ω due to cold-temperature degradation. What is the true current through the system, and how much error does the data logger introduce in its reported power consumption?

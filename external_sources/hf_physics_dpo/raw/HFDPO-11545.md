# HFDPO-11545

A drone-mounted laser interferometer is used to detect minute ground deformations in a volcanic region, measuring phase shifts in a 633 nm He-Ne laser beam reflected from a fixed mirror 2 km away. During a seismic event, the phase shift increases by 1.8π radians over 10 seconds, but atmospheric turbulence causes random phase fluctuations with a standard deviation of 0.3π radians. Determine whether the observed phase change is likely due to actual ground displacement or atmospheric noise, and calculate the minimum detectable ground displacement that could be distinguished from noise at 95% confidence.

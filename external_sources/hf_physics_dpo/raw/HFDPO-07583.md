# HFDPO-07583

A deep-sea research submersible descends through the ocean, encountering a submerged, irregularly shaped hydrothermal vent chimney made of porous basalt. The chimney has a vertical height of 12 m and a variable cross-sectional area that decreases exponentially from 4.5 m² at the base to 0.8 m² at the top. Seawater at 2°C (density 1027 kg/m³) seeps into the porous structure, and pressure differences drive upward fluid flow. Calculate the net upward buoyant force exerted *on the chimney itself* due to the displaced seawater, accounting for the complex geometry and constant external pressure gradient.

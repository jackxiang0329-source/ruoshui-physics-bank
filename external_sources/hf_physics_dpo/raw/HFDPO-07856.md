# HFDPO-07856

A biomedical engineer is designing a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner for a rural clinic with unreliable power. The scanner’s superconducting magnet requires a persistent current of 120 A in a 1.8 m diameter loop to maintain a stable 1.5 T magnetic field. However, the clinic’s backup battery system can only sustain 45 A continuously. To compensate, the engineer considers using a permanent magnet to partially offset the field, reducing the required superconducting current. What magnetic field strength (in T) must the permanent magnet provide, and in which direction, relative to the superconducting loop, to allow the total field to remain 1.5 T while drawing only 45 A from the battery?

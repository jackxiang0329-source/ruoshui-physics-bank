# HFDPO-05192

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to tap into a hot rock formation 3.2 km below the surface. Water is pumped down at 120 L/min through a stainless steel pipe (2.5 cm diameter) to produce steam that drives turbines. Due to friction and the need to maintain pressure, the pump must deliver water at 18 MPa. What is the minimum power input required by the pump, assuming 95% of the pump’s work goes into increasing the water’s internal energy and overcoming viscous drag? Ignore temperature changes in the pipe and assume water is incompressible.

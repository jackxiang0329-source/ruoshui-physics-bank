# HFDPO-05469

A fusion reactor's plasma-facing components in a tokamak are experiencing unexpected localized heating due to eddy currents induced by a slowly decaying vertical magnetic field (ΔB/Δt ≈ 0.15 T/s) during plasma disruption. Estimate the peak eddy current density within the plate and determine whether the gap is sufficient to limit heating to acceptable levels (<1 MW/m³). Assume the magnetic field is uniform and perpendicular to the plate over its extent.

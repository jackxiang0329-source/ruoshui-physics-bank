# HFDPO-06299

A research team designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices discovers unexpected signal interference in a 1.5 MHz frequency band. The system uses a circular coil of radius 5 cm carrying a time-varying current that generates a magnetic field. They need to determine the induced electric field at a point 3 cm from the center of the coil, inside the region where the field is changing. The current increases linearly from 0 to 10 A over 100 μs. What is the magnitude and direction of the induced electric field?

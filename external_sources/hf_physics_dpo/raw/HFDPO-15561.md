# HFDPO-15561

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device, a droplet of blood (μ = 3.5 mPa·s, ρ = 1060 kg/m³) is injected into a 200 μm-wide rectangular channel at 1.2 mL/min. The channel has a 10° bend with a 5 mm radius of curvature. If the droplet’s length exceeds 1.5 mm, it causes flow instability and clogs the system. Using the Hagen-Poiseuille approximation for curved flow and considering inertial effects, determine the maximum safe injection rate to prevent clogging due to viscous shear-induced deformation.

# HFDPO-10338

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice that the onboard atmospheric sampler, designed to collect gas samples at 250 K, is underperforming—measuring significantly lower pressure than expected based on the rover’s surface pressure readings. The sample chamber is sealed with a volume of 1.2 L and contains 0.035 moles of gas. Measurements show the pressure is 32% lower than the theoretical ideal gas pressure calculated from standard Martian atmospheric composition. Could this discrepancy be due to non-ideal behavior from intermolecular forces, and if so, what would be the effective average molecular speed of the gas if it were behaving ideally?

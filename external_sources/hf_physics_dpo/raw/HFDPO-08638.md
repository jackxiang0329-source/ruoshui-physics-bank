# HFDPO-08638

A biomedical engineer is designing a lens-free holographic microscope to detect early-stage cancer cells in blood samples. The system uses coherent laser light (λ = 632.8 nm) shone through a microfluidic channel, and the resulting interference pattern is recorded on a CMOS sensor 20 cm away. The target cells are ~5 μm in diameter and exhibit a refractive index of 1.4, while the surrounding plasma has n = 1.33. To achieve sufficient contrast for detection, the phase shift introduced by a cell must produce a measurable interference fringe shift. What is the minimum phase difference (in radians) that a single cell must introduce across the beam to yield a detectable fringe shift of at least one fringe width?

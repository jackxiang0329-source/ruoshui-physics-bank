# HFDPO-15705

A solar array on a Mars rover must maintain optimal tilt to maximize energy capture, but the Martian wind (average 30 m/s, density 0.02 kg/m³) exerts varying pressure across its 1.8 m × 1.2 m surface. The panel is mounted on a pivot at its base, with a counterweight of 4.5 kg located 0.3 m behind the pivot. The panel’s center of mass is 0.6 m from the pivot, and its weight is 12 N. Wind acts at 0.7 m above the pivot, with pressure varying linearly from 5 Pa at the bottom to 18 Pa at the top due to turbulent flow. What is the minimum tilt angle (from horizontal) that ensures the system remains in static equilibrium without tipping?

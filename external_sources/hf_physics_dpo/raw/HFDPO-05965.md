# HFDPO-05965

A geothermal energy company plans to install fiber-optic sensors in a 500-meter-deep borehole through fractured rock to monitor subsurface temperature gradients. The sensors are encased in a polymer sheath with a Young’s modulus of 3 GPa and a Poisson’s ratio of 0.35. During drilling, the rock exerts a radial stress of 120 MPa. If the sheath has an inner diameter of 8 mm and a wall thickness of 1.2 mm, what is the maximum allowable internal pressure the sheath can withstand without undergoing plastic deformation, assuming the yield strength of the polymer is 45 MPa? How does the lateral strain affect sensor alignment?

# HFDPO-14401

A planetary lander descends through Titan’s dense nitrogen atmosphere, deploying a variable-thrust retro-engine to achieve a controlled soft landing. At 1.2 km altitude, its speed is 80 m/s downward, and it must reduce to 1.0 m/s at 10 m above the surface over 60 seconds. The lander’s mass is 850 kg but decreases at 2.5 kg/s due to fuel burn. Atmospheric drag is velocity-dependent, modeled as F_d = -0.04v^2 . The engine can deliver a thrust from 0 to 3000 N, but must avoid exceeding 1.4 g deceleration to preserve sensitive instruments. What thrust profile (as a function of time) minimizes fuel use while ensuring a safe touchdown?

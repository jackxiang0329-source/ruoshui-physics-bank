# HFDPO-02682

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) descends into the Mariana Trench, encountering a stable underwater "bubble plume" rising from a hydrothermal vent. The plume consists of superheated, low-density fluid (ρ = 850 kg/m³) encased in a thin, impermeable membrane, forming a vertical, cylindrical column 12 m in diameter and 200 m long, suspended in seawater (ρ = 1025 kg/m³). The ROV must calculate the net upward force on the plume to assess structural stability before deployment of instruments nearby. The plume’s top is open to the surrounding water at 10,000 m depth. What is the total buoyant force acting on the plume, and how does it compare to the weight of the plume?

# HFDPO-03447

Using a handheld polarimeter, they measure the degree of polarization at 45° to the vertical and find it reaches 68%—unusually high. Given that sunlight is partially polarized by Rayleigh scattering in the atmosphere and that water scatters light more efficiently at shorter wavelengths, calculate the angle between the sun's direction and the polarization plane of the light reaching the coral. Assume the incident sunlight is unpolarized and the scattering angle in air is 90°, but the effective scattering angle in water due to refraction is 72°.

# HFDPO-05042

A satellite operator is adjusting the orbit of a communications satellite initially in a circular low Earth orbit (altitude 600 km). To avoid collision with space debris, they must raise the orbit to 1,200 km circular altitude using a single Hohmann transfer burn. Given Earth’s mass (5.97 × 10²⁴ kg) and radius (6,371 km), what is the required delta-v (change in velocity) for the first burn at perigee, and how does this compare to the orbital speed at the original altitude?

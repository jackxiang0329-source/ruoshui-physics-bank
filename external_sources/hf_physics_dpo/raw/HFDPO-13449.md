# HFDPO-13449

A coastal desalination plant in Oman uses a solar-powered heat engine to convert seawater into freshwater. The system operates between a hot reservoir heated by concentrated solar fields at 210°C and a cold reservoir at 30°C. If the plant needs to produce 10,000 kg of freshwater daily (requiring 2.5 MJ/kg of energy for phase change and sensible heating), what is the minimum average solar heat input rate (in kW) required to sustain this production?

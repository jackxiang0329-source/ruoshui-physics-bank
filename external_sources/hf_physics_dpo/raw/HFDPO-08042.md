# HFDPO-08042

A quantum imaging lab is developing a lensless microscope using compressed sensing and heralded single photons to image delicate biological samples without damaging them. The system detects entangled photon pairs: one travels to the sample, the other (the "idler") is measured to infer properties of the signal photon. The signal photon's path is randomized via a spatial light modulator (SLM), but due to imperfect phase control, the reconstruction quality degrades. Given that the signal photon experiences a phase distortion modeled by φ(x, y) = 0.5 sin(2πx/λ) + 0.3 sin(2πy/λ), with λ = 600 nm, and the detection window is 10 μm × 10 μm with 256×256 pixels, find the average phase error over the field and determine how it affects the visibility of interference fringes in intensity correlation measurements.

# HFDPO-08441

A geothermal power plant in Iceland detects elevated levels of radon-222 in its underground steam vents, raising concerns about radiation exposure to workers. The plant’s engineers measure a radon activity of 120 Bq/m³ at the surface, and the half-life of radon-222 is 3.82 days. If the steam rises from a depth where radon is produced continuously by decay of uranium-238 in rock, and the average residence time of steam in the reservoir is 1.5 days, what is the equilibrium concentration of radon in the rock-forming fluid, assuming no decay during transport and constant production?

# HFDPO-04871

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates electricity using a rotating magnet inside a stationary coil array. During a sudden storm, the wind speed increases rapidly, causing the rotor to accelerate from 60 rpm to 120 rpm in 3 seconds. If each coil has an area of 0.25 m², 200 turns, and is in a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T, what is the average induced emf in a single coil during the acceleration? Assume the magnetic field remains perpendicular to the coil plane throughout.

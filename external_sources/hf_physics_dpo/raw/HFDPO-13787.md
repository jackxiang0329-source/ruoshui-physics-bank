# HFDPO-13787

During a polar expedition, scientists discover a subsurface ice layer on a remote glacier that exhibits anomalous melting patterns despite sub-zero temperatures. The ice sheet contains trapped pockets of pressurized brine (NaCl solution) at −12°C, yet melting occurs at the ice-brine interface. Measurements show the brine has a density of 1150 kg/m³ and a freezing point depression of 6.5°C. Given that the pressure at the interface is 8.2 MPa and the latent heat of fusion for pure ice is 334 kJ/kg, determine the minimum salt concentration (in wt%) required to sustain this metastable liquid phase under pressure, accounting for both pressure and solute effects on the phase boundary.

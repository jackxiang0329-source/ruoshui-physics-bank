# HFDPO-08662

A quantum imaging lab is developing a single-photon camera for low-light biomedical imaging, but interference patterns from ambient room light (633 nm He-Ne laser leakage) are degrading image fidelity. The camera uses a superconducting nanowire array with a 100 nm pitch, sensitive to photon arrival timing within 10 ps. Assume the system operates at 300 K and the detector’s spatial resolution is limited by diffraction.

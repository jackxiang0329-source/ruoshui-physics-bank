# HFDPO-09782

A deep-space asteroid probe equipped with reaction wheels must stabilize its orientation during a close flyby of a rapidly spinning asteroid (diameter 800 m, rotational period 2.3 hours). To avoid tumbling, the probe uses three orthogonal reaction wheels, each with a mass of 12 kg and radius 0.3 m, initially at rest. After firing, one wheel spins at 4500 rpm to counteract the asteroid's apparent rotational drift. If the probe’s moment of inertia about its center of mass is 420 kg·m² (including the wheels), what is the final angular velocity of the probe’s body, and what is its orientation error after 15 seconds due to imperfect feedback? Assume no external torques.

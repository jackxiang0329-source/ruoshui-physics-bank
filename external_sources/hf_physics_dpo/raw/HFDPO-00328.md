# HFDPO-00328

In a high-vacuum electron beam lithography chamber, a nanoscale silicon tip (radius 50 nm) is used to deposit electrons onto a resist-coated substrate. As the beam intensity increases to 2 nA, the tip becomes charged due to space charge buildup, altering the local electric field and causing beam divergence. Determine the maximum charge density on the tip surface before field emission threshold (E_th = 10⁹ V/m) is exceeded, and evaluate whether the observed beam spread (measured as 3° divergence) is consistent with electrostatic repulsion or purely beam optics.

# HFDPO-07859

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 320°C, which then transfers heat to water to generate steam for a turbine. On a cloudless day, the system absorbs 1.8 MW of solar power, but the oil loses heat to the environment at a rate proportional to the temperature difference, with a heat transfer coefficient of 12 W/m²·K over a surface area of 25 m². The oil flows at 15 kg/s, with a specific heat of 2.1 kJ/kg·K. What is the steady-state temperature of the oil leaving the collectors?

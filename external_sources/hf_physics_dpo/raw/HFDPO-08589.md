# HFDPO-08589

A drone manufacturer is testing a new delivery drone with a 2.4 kg payload suspended 0.8 m below the center of its frame. The drone’s arms are 1.2 m long and rigid, and the payload is attached via a single vertical cable. During flight, wind gusts cause a lateral force of 3.5 N on the payload. To prevent tipping, the drone’s frame must remain in static equilibrium. What minimum tension must the left arm cable maintain to keep the system balanced if the right arm is already experiencing 25 N of tension?

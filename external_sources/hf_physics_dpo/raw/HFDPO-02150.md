# HFDPO-02150

During a dust storm in the desert, a solar panel array at a remote research station begins losing efficiency—measured voltage drops despite clean-looking panels. Engineers discover a fine layer of charged silica dust has accumulated on the surface, creating a uniform surface charge density of +2.5 × 10⁻⁸ C/m². The panels are made of silicon with a dielectric constant of 11.7. What is the electric field just above the panel surface, and how does this field affect nearby uncharged dust particles, potentially reducing their adhesion?

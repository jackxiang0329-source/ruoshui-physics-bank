# HFDPO-14116

A fusion reactor's plasma containment system uses a complex toroidal magnetic field generated by external coils, but engineers detect unexpected helical instabilities in the plasma boundary. The plasma boundary lies at r = R₀ = 2.5 m, and the system has m = 5 helical field lines. Calculate the net magnetic flux through a poloidal cross-section of the torus at z = 0, considering the perturbation’s effect.

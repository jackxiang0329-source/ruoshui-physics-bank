# HFDPO-05320

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that a single electron tunneling through a double-slit barrier fabricated from graphene exhibits interference patterns with a fringe spacing of 1.8 μm at a detector 25 cm away. However, when a quantum dot is placed near one slit to monitor which path the electron takes, the interference vanishes—yet the electron still reaches the detector. Given the electron's kinetic energy is 2.3 eV and the slit separation is 300 nm, evaluate whether the loss of interference is due to wavefunction collapse from measurement or to wavefunction decoherence from quantum entanglement with the quantum dot.

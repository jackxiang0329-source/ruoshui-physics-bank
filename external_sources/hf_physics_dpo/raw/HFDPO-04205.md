# HFDPO-04205

During a deep-space mission, a satellite's solar array degrades over time due to radiation exposure, reducing its effective conductive cross-sectional area by 40% in one critical segment. The array operates at 150 V, and the current path includes a 2.0 m length of copper strip with initial resistance 0.015 Ω. As the array heats unevenly, the resistivity of the damaged section increases to 1.8 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m (from original 1.6 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m). What is the total current through the path if the remaining undamaged portion still has uniform cross-section and resistivity? Assume the voltage is constant across the full array.

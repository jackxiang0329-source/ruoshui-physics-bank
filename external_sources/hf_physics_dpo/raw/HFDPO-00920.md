# HFDPO-00920

A research team at a fusion reactor facility observes unexpected magnetic turbulence in the tokamak’s plasma confinement field, disrupting containment. The toroidal magnetic field, generated by 1200 superconducting coils, is designed to maintain a uniform 4.2 T field at the plasma edge (radius 2.3 m), but measurements show a 6.8% radial deviation in field strength at 1.8 m from the center. Assuming the coils are ideal solenoids with uniform current distribution, calculate the effective current per coil required to produce the nominal field, and determine whether the observed deviation is due to coil misalignment (angular offset) or current imbalance across the coil set. Use the Biot-Savart law and symmetry arguments to evaluate which cause is more consistent with the data.

# HFDPO-07002

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of –2.4 × 10⁻⁸ C/m² is suspended horizontally by electrostatic levitation in a vacuum chamber. A neighboring 10 cm × 10 cm wafer, initially uncharged, is brought within 1.2 mm of the charged wafer along the vertical axis. To prevent unwanted charge transfer due to field-induced electron tunneling, engineers must calculate the minimum work needed to slowly separate the wafers to 3.0 mm apart without inducing breakdown. What is this work, and how does the non-uniform field near the edges influence the result?

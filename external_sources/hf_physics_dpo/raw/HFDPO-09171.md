# HFDPO-09171

In a cutting-edge wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices, a 1.5 MHz sinusoidal AC source powers a primary coil (L₁ = 25 µH, R₁ = 3 Ω) placed 2 cm from a secondary coil (L₂ = 18 µH, R₂ = 2 Ω) in a patient’s chest.

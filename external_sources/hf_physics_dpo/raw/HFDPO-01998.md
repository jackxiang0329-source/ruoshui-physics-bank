# HFDPO-01998

A commercial satellite constellation is experiencing unexpected drift in its Sun-synchronous orbit due to unmodeled atmospheric drag and lunar gravitational perturbations. The constellation’s 120 satellites, each with a cross-sectional area of 8 m² and mass of 1,500 kg, operate at 700 km altitude, but recent tracking shows a 1.2° per day decay in orbital inclination. The lunar tidal forces from a 384,400 km distant Moon are now perturbing the orbit’s nodal precession. Estimate the net change in orbital inclination over 30 days, assuming the drag-induced precession is −0.8°/day and the lunar perturbation contributes +0.6°/day. How should the station-keeping strategy be revised to maintain the required 98° inclination?

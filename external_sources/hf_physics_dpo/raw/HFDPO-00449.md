# HFDPO-00449

In a remote solar farm, a grid of 12 identical photovoltaic panels is wired in a complex parallel-series configuration across three strings, each with 4 panels in series. During a sudden cloud cover event, the output voltage of one panel in the middle string drops to 12 V (from 18 V nominal), while the others remain at 18 V. Measuring the current through each string reveals 3.2 A, 2.8 A, and 5.1 A. Determine the internal resistance of the faulty panel, assuming all good panels have identical internal resistance and the load is shared proportionally across strings.

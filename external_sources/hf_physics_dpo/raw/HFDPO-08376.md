# HFDPO-08376

The stent’s material exhibits nonlinear elasticity with a stress-strain relationship approximated by σ = 200ε + 150ε³ (in MPa), and must withstand 1.2 million expansion cycles without yielding. Given the radial displacement is 9 mm and the wall thickness is 0.15 mm, determine whether the maximum strain exceeds the safe limit of 0.4, and evaluate if the material’s hysteresis energy loss (>50 J/m³ per cycle) would cause thermal damage.

# HFDPO-10162

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland must stabilize a 12-meter-long, 450 kg steel cantilever arm that extends horizontally over a volcanic fissure to support sensors. The arm is anchored at one end with a hinge and supported by a high-strength cable making a 30° angle with the horizontal, attached 8 meters from the hinge. Due to thermal expansion, the arm's center of mass shifts 0.3 m toward the free end under operational heat. What is the minimum tension the cable must withstand to prevent rotation, and how does this change if the cable attachment point is moved 2 meters closer to the hinge?

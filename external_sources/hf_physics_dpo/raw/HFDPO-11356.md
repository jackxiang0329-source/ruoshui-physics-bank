# HFDPO-11356

A satellite constellation in low Earth orbit must reconfigure from a circular 400 km altitude formation to a highly elliptical 300/1200 km orbit to maximize coverage over a polar research station. The mission team has only two short burns available due to limited fuel. If the first burn is applied at perigee of the initial circular orbit and must raise apogee to 1200 km, what is the minimum ∆v required for the second burn at the new apogee to stabilize the final orbit, and how does the timing and orientation of the second burn affect the long-term stability of the constellation under J₂ perturbations?

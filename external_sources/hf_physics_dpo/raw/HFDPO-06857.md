# HFDPO-06857

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a sloped rooftop in a mountainous region, where strong gusts can reach 60 m/s. The station’s 2.4 m × 1.8 m solar panel is secured at an angle of 35° to the horizontal and bolted to a rigid frame fixed to a concrete base. Wind exerts a pressure of 120 Pa on the panel’s surface, with the force acting perpendicular to the panel. The center of pressure is located 1.1 m from the base along the panel’s length. The entire system is anchored at a single point at the base. Determine the minimum coefficient of static friction between the base and the concrete needed to prevent sliding, and evaluate if the base might tip over under wind load—considering the weight of the panel (140 kg) and the frame (60 kg), with their centers of mass located at the geometric center of the panel and frame, respectively. Assume the frame's center of mass is 0.3 m above the base and the panel’s is 0.1 m above the base.

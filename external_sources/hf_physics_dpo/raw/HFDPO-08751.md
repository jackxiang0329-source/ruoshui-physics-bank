# HFDPO-08751

A deep-space probe equipped with a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) using 238Pu is approaching Jupiter. The RTG’s power output must sustain critical systems for 18 years, but radiation damage to the thermocouples has increased the thermal resistance by 15% since launch. If the initial thermal power was 450 W and the decay rate of 238Pu is 87.7 years, what is the final power delivered to the electronics at the end of the mission, accounting for both radioactive decay and degraded thermal efficiency?

# HFDPO-03503

A quantum computing chip uses a 10 nm thick niobium nitride (NbN) superconducting loop with a critical temperature of 16 K. During operation, the loop is exposed to a magnetic field fluctuating between 0.1 mT and 0.3 mT due to nearby control wiring. Engineers observe unexpected phase slips in the Josephson junctions, disrupting qubit coherence. Given that the loop has an inductance of 8 pH and a critical current density of 3 × 10⁶ A/m², determine whether the applied magnetic flux exceeds the flux quantum limit for stable superconducting state in the loop.

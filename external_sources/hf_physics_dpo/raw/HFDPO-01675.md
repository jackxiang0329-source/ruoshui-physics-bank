# HFDPO-01675

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 30° tilt to optimize sun exposure. Each panel weighs 25 kg and is secured by a single bolt at its base, which must resist both downward force and a lateral wind load of 120 N acting horizontally at the panel’s center. The panel’s center of mass is 0.8 m from the bolt. Find the minimum friction force required at the base to prevent tipping, and determine whether a static friction coefficient of 0.6 is sufficient.

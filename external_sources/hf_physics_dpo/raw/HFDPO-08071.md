# HFDPO-08071

Given the ocean’s temperature gradient (15°C at surface, 4°C at depth) and salinity variation (34.5‰ to 36.2‰), determine the effective sound speed gradient and explain how refraction due to wavefront curvature causes the observed phase shift.

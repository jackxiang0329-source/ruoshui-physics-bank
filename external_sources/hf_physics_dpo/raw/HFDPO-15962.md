# HFDPO-15962

During a high-altitude expedition, climbers notice their insulated water bottles develop frost on the outside after being left untouched for hours, even though the air temperature is above freezing. The bottles, initially at 5°C, were filled with 500 mL of water and sealed tightly in a tent at -8°C. After 4 hours, frost appears. Why did freezing occur despite the ambient temperature being above 0°C? Estimate the minimum mass of frost that must have formed, assuming all heat lost came from the water cooling to 0°C and then freezing, and the latent heat of fusion for water is 334 kJ/kg.

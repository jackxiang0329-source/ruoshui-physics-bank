# HFDPO-11833

A quantum imaging lab attempts to reconstruct a faint, spatially incoherent fluorescence pattern from a biological sample using a metasurface-based ghost imaging setup. The metasurface, engineered to impose a stochastic phase mask with a spatial frequency cutoff at 120 μm⁻¹, is illuminated with a coherent laser (λ = 532 nm), while a bucket detector collects the total transmitted intensity. The sample emits at λ = 650 nm with a coherence time of 200 fs. To recover the image, the lab must compute the cross-correlation between the transmitted intensity and the known phase pattern, but faces a 15% calibration error in the phase mask due to fabrication imperfections. What is the minimum number of measurements needed to reconstruct the image with a signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) ≥ 10, assuming shot noise dominates?

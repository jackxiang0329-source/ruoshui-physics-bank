# HFDPO-05829

A research team is designing a low-power neural implant to monitor brain activity in freely moving patients. Is the voltage drop due to battery internal resistance, amplifier nonlinearity, or both? Evaluate the contribution of each factor and suggest a design change to maintain ≥3.0 V under active load.

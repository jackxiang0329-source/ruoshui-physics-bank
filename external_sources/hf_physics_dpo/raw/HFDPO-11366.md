# HFDPO-11366

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stability while deploying a flexible, 12-meter-long sensor array from its underside. The array, with uniform linear density of 0.8 kg/m, sags under gravity but is partially supported by a lightweight, tensioned cable anchored at the drone’s center and fixed at both ends of the array. The drone’s body exerts a vertical force of 150 N downward at the midpoint of the array, and the system is symmetric. Determine the minimum tension required in the cable to prevent the array from drooping more than 1.5 meters at its lowest point, assuming the cable is perfectly flexible and the array is rigidly attached at both ends.

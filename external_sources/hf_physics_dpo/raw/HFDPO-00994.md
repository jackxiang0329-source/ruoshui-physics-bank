# HFDPO-00994

A wildlife biologist uses a polarizing filter on her camera to reduce glare from a freshwater lake while photographing migratory ducks. She notices that when she rotates the filter, the brightness of the water surface changes dramatically, but the ducks remain equally visible. If the reflected light from the water is 90% polarized horizontally and the camera’s sensor detects 120 units of light intensity when the filter is aligned to block horizontal polarization, what is the intensity of the reflected light component that reaches the sensor when the filter is rotated to 30° from the blocking position?

# HFDPO-07149

A team of planetary scientists detects a previously unknown exoplanet in a stable orbit around a red dwarf star, 12 light-years away. Could gravitational lensing by a nearby unseen brown dwarf—located 0.8 AU from the star—account for the discrepancy in transit depth, and if so, what would be the minimum mass required for the lens to produce this effect?

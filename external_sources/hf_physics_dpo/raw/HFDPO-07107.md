# HFDPO-07107

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, sealed titanium cylinder suspected to contain a rare mineral sample. The cylinder is anchored to the seafloor at 3,200 meters depth, where seawater density averages 1028 kg/m³. The cap is held in place by a pressure differential: the interior of the cylinder is sealed at atmospheric pressure (101.3 kPa), while external pressure builds due to hydrostatics. Calculate the minimum force the manipulator must exert to overcome the net inward force on the cap *without* allowing water to breach the seal prematurely. Use exact fluid-statics principles, including the full hydrostatic pressure distribution across the curved cap surface.

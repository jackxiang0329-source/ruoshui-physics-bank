# HFDPO-01801

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer (dielectric constant εᵣ = 11.7) is charged to −300 V relative to ground via a floating gate electrode. A dust particle (diameter 2.5 μm, density 2.3 g/cm³, dielectric constant 4.5) drifts into the gap between the wafer and a grounded guard ring, located 80 μm away. As the particle approaches, an intense electric field induces a dipole moment, causing it to levitate momentarily before being attracted to the wafer. What is the minimum distance from the wafer where the electrostatic force overcomes gravity, and how does the particle’s orientation affect this threshold?

# HFDPO-10265

A deep-sea mining drone, designed to extract polymetallic nodules from the ocean floor at 5,000 meters depth, uses a rigid articulated arm to lift a 120 kg nodule. The arm has two segments (3 m and 2 m) connected by a hinge; the first segment is pinned to the drone at a 30° angle above horizontal. Due to high-pressure fluid forces, a 450 N lateral force acts at the arm’s tip, perpendicular to the second segment. The drone’s thrusters apply forces at its base to maintain equilibrium. Determine whether the joint at the hinge can withstand the maximum torque demand under these conditions, given that the joint’s torque limit is 1,800 N·m. Assume negligible mass of the arm, uniform gravity, and hydrostatic pressure affects buoyancy (nodules are 15% less dense than seawater).

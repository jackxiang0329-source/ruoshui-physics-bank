# HFDPO-09420

A deep-space probe’s radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) uses plutonium-238 decay to maintain power in the cold void beyond Neptune. At launch, the RTG’s thermal efficiency was 5.7%, but after 12 years in space, the thermocouples degrade due to radiation damage and lattice disorder. Engineers observe that the heat output remains constant at 340 W, but the electrical output has dropped to 18 W. Assuming the cold-side temperature is stable at 85 K and the hot-side temperature has decreased due to reduced heat transfer efficiency, what is the new hot-side temperature? Use the Carnot efficiency limit and real thermoelectric performance to estimate the actual thermoelectric efficiency and infer how much irreversibility has increased.

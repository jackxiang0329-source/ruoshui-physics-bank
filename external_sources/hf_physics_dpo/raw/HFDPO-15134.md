# HFDPO-15134

A quantum dot solar cell uses a periodic array of nanoscale pillars (150 nm diameter, 400 nm height) spaced 600 nm apart to enhance light trapping. When sunlight (λ = 600 nm, normal incidence) hits the surface, interference and diffraction patterns significantly affect absorption efficiency. Calculate the smallest angle (from normal) at which the first-order constructive interference occurs due to diffraction from the pillar array, and determine if this peak aligns with the optimal absorption band (peak sensitivity at 600 nm).

# HFDPO-11837

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain ridge uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a 200 W photovoltaic array. The array’s output varies with sunlight intensity, and the system includes a buck converter to regulate voltage to 5 V for microcontrollers. During a sudden storm, cloud cover reduces solar input to 40% of maximum. The station’s power draw fluctuates: 50 mA when idle, 250 mA during data transmission. Design a current-limiting circuit using a single resistor and a Zener diode (5.1 V, 1 W) to protect the 5 V logic board from voltage spikes during brief reconnection after a power outage. What is the minimum acceptable resistance value to prevent overheating, given the converter’s transient response?

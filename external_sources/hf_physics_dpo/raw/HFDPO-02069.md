# HFDPO-02069

A medical research team is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using size-based inertial focusing in a narrow channel. Blood flows through a rectangular microchannel (500 μm wide, 100 μm high) at a flow rate of 10 μL/min. The researchers must predict the maximum velocity at the center of the channel to ensure CTCs (diameter ~15 μm) experience sufficient lift forces for separation, while avoiding shear stress that could damage cells. Assume blood behaves as a Newtonian fluid with μ = 3.5 mPa·s and ρ = 1060 kg/m³. What is the centerline velocity?

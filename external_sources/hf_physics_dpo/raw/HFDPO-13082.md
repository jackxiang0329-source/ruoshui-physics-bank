# HFDPO-13082

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 400°C. The oil flows at 0.5 m/s and has a heat capacity of 2000 J/kg·K and density of 850 kg/m³. Ambient air is at 25°C, with a convective heat transfer coefficient of 15 W/m²·K. The oil's initial temperature is 400°C, but the system must maintain at least 350°C for 15 minutes to prevent thermal stress. Is this feasible? Calculate the temperature of the oil after 15 minutes using energy balance and transient heat transfer modeling.

# HFDPO-00203

A quantum imaging lab attempts to resolve two closely spaced fluorescent nanoparticles (450 nm apart) in a live cell using a super-resolved microscope with a 1.45 NA oil immersion objective and 561 nm laser excitation. The system employs single-molecule localization microscopy (SMLM), but the observed localization precision is limited by photon shot noise and astigmatism-induced blurring. The researchers notice that despite sufficient photon counts per molecule (~15,000 photons), the pair resolution fails when using standard Gaussian fitting due to correlated background noise from autofluorescence.

# HFDPO-01134

A satellite communication system uses dual-polarized antennas operating at 12.5 GHz, but signal degradation occurs during heavy rain. Engineers observe that cross-polarization interference increases by 18 dB when rainfall exceeds 20 mm/h. Given that raindrops act as birefringent scatterers and depolarize signals via multiple scattering, determine the minimum required polarization isolation (in dB) between transmit and receive antennas to maintain a 25 dB signal-to-interference ratio, considering rain-induced depolarization and a 0.85 efficiency in the antenna system.

# HFDPO-04476

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a solar-powered circuit to transmit data hourly. The station's 12 V battery is connected via 200 m of copper wire (diameter 2.0 mm) to a sensor that draws 0.5 A. Due to a recent ice storm, the wire is partially iced over, increasing its effective resistance by 25%. The station’s data transmitter fails to send signals when the voltage at the sensor drops below 11.4 V. Is the system still functional, and if not, what minimum wire diameter (in mm) is required to maintain reliable operation?

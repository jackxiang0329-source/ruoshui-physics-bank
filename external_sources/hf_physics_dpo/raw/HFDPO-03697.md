# HFDPO-03697

A marine biologist is designing a deep-sea sampling pod that must descend to 2,500 m below the ocean surface without collapsing. The pod’s spherical pressure hull has a diameter of 1.8 m and is made of titanium alloy with a yield strength of 900 MPa. At that depth, seawater density is 1,030 kg/m³, and atmospheric pressure at the surface is 101.3 kPa. What is the maximum thickness of the hull wall allowed before the material exceeds its yield strength? Assume the hull is thin-walled and failure occurs due to internal pressure only.

# HFDPO-02399

A planetary scientist studies a rapidly spinning asteroid, 400 meters in diameter, with a non-uniform mass distribution causing its rotation axis to precess at 0.5° per day. If the asteroid’s rotational kinetic energy is decreasing at a rate of 1.2 × 10⁹ J/year due to internal tidal dissipation, what is the magnitude of the torque causing the precession, assuming the spin axis remains fixed in direction over the observation period?

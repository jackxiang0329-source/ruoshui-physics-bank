# HFDPO-07628

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size-induced differences in viscous drag. The device channels blood (μ = 3.5 mPa·s, ρ = 1060 kg/m³) through a 100 μm wide, 500 μm long microchannel at a volumetric flow rate of 0.5 μL/min. Two cell types—small (9 μm diameter, ρ_cell = 1080 kg/m³) and large (15 μm, same density)—are suspended in the fluid. What is the ratio of the drag forces on the large vs. small cells near the channel centerline? Assume laminar flow and use Stokes’ law with correction for finite particle size and boundary effects.

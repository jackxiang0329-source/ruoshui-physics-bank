# HFDPO-00158

A concert hall architect is designing a new performance space with a curved, elliptical ceiling to enhance acoustics. Two singers stand at the foci of the ellipse, each 12 meters apart. A sound wave emitted from one singer reflects off the ceiling and reaches the other singer with a 1.4-second delay. The air temperature is 22°C, and the ceiling’s curvature exactly matches the geometric path of reflection. What is the length of the major axis of the ellipse, assuming sound travels along the shortest reflective path?

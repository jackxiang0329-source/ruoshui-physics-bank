# HFDPO-03862

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain peak uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. The system powers a sensor array (1.5 Ω equivalent resistance), a data logger (3.0 Ω), and a radio transmitter (2.0 Ω), all connected in parallel. Due to a storm, the battery voltage drops to 10.2 V, and the transmitter fails to send signals. Engineers suspect the radio’s internal circuitry draws 0.8 A at nominal voltage. Is the issue due to insufficient current, or could a resistor in the transmitter's power line have opened?

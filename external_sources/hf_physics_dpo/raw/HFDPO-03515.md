# HFDPO-03515

A wind farm in a mountain pass is experiencing uneven blade wear despite identical turbine designs. Data shows that the left blade of each turbine wears 40% faster than the right, even when wind direction is averaged. The turbine blades are 50 m long, and the pass has a 15° incline with a 3 m height difference over 100 m horizontal distance. Wind speed at the lower entry point is 8 m/s, and the turbines are spaced 5 rotor diameters apart. Use Bernoulli’s principle to explain why the wind velocity varies across the rotor plane and quantitatively predict the expected pressure difference across the blades.

# HFDPO-12506

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber uses a self-heating survival pouch that relies on the exothermic crystallization of sodium acetate from a supersaturated solution. The pouch begins at 25°C and must deliver enough heat to raise a 250 g cup of water from 0°C to 40°C within 5 minutes. If the pouch releases 180 J of heat per gram of sodium acetate that crystallizes, and 40% of the heat is lost to the surroundings, how much sodium acetate must the pouch contain to meet the requirement?

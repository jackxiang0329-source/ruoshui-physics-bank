# HFDPO-02720

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device designed for early cancer detection, a viscoelastic blood sample (with apparent viscosity 4.2 × 10⁻³ Pa·s, density 1050 kg/m³) flows through a 250 µm wide, 10 cm long rectangular channel at a volumetric flow rate of 1.2 µL/min. The channel walls are coated with a hydrophobic polymer that induces a slip length of 80 nm. Calculate the pressure drop required to sustain this flow, accounting for both viscous drag and slip effects. How does this compare to the no-slip case?

# HFDPO-08705

In a semiconductor manufacturing facility, an electrostatic chuck holds a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer during plasma etching. The chuck applies a uniform surface charge density of –2.0 μC/m² to securely grip the wafer, which has a dielectric constant of 11.7. During a process shift, the wafer’s surface becomes contaminated with a thin layer of insulating particles, reducing the effective contact area to 65% of the original. What is the new electrostatic force holding the wafer, and how does the reduction in contact area affect the maximum possible clamping force?

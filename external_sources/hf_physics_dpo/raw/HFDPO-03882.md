# HFDPO-03882

A recording engineer is mixing audio for a live opera broadcast in a historic theater with a 2.4-second reverberation time. During rehearsal, she notices that high-frequency vocals (3 kHz) are masked by the delayed sound from the rear wall, causing listener fatigue. The stage is 18 m from the back wall, and the microphone is 5 m from the stage.

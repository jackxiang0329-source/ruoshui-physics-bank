# HFDPO-09244

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2 mm diameter to 4 mm during deployment, while maintaining structural integrity under pulsatile blood pressure (peak ~120 mmHg). The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 70 GPa and a yield strength of 300 MPa. If the stent’s wall thickness is 0.1 mm and it behaves as a thin-walled cylindrical shell, what is the minimum length of the stent required to prevent plastic deformation during expansion, assuming uniform radial expansion and no buckling?

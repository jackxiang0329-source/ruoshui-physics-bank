# HFDPO-16134

A small rural community relies on a solar-powered heat engine to generate electricity for their school, using a parabolic solar collector that heats water to 120°C. The system rejects waste heat to the surrounding air at 25°C. If the engine extracts 2.4 kJ of heat from the hot reservoir each cycle and the community needs at least 500 J of useful work per cycle to power lights and a fan, is the system efficient enough?

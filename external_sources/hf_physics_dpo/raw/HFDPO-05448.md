# HFDPO-05448

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpected efficiency drops under broad-spectrum sunlight, despite theoretical predictions. The device uses a 2D photonic crystal lattice with a periodicity of 450 nm to trap light via guided mode resonances. Measurements show spectral interference patterns in the emitted light, with a distinct dip at 620 nm. However, simulations based on idealized wave optics fail to reproduce the observed dip. You suspect fabrication-induced disorder in the lattice spacing (±10 nm variation) affects constructive interference. Determine whether the dip arises from coherent interference or incoherent scattering, and explain how disorder alters the photonic band structure.

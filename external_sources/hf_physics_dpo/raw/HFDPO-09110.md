# HFDPO-09110

A private space station in a 600 km circular orbit around Earth needs to intercept a drifting debris cloud—currently 15° ahead in the same orbital plane—moving at 10 m/s relative to the station’s original frame. Mission control must choose between two maneuvers: a single Hohmann transfer to match the debris’s orbit (700 km), or a tangential burn to lower the station’s perigee to 500 km and then a second burn at perigee to re-circularize at 700 km. Which method minimizes total Δv required, and what is the optimal timing window (in minutes) for initiating the first burn? Assume circular orbits, negligible atmospheric drag, and use the patched conic approximation.

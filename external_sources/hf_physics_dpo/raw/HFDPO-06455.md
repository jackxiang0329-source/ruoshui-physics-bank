# HFDPO-06455

If the drone has a mass of 24 kg and experiences a constant aerodynamic drag of 32 N during descent, what is the minimum amount of work the solar array must supply to ensure the drone reaches the target altitude with the required kinetic energy? Assume no wind and constant gravitational acceleration.

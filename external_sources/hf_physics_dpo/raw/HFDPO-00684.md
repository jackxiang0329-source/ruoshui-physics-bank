# HFDPO-00684

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a submersible encounters a hydrothermal vent system where superheated water (350°C, 300 atm) surges from the ocean floor. The vent fluid rapidly mixes with cold seawater (2°C, 300 atm), causing sudden phase transitions. Engineers observe unexpected solid deposits forming within the sampling tube, disrupting measurements.

# HFDPO-09284

A research team designs a hybrid geothermal-thermal power plant in Iceland that uses both underground hot rock (550°C) and solar-heated thermal storage (300°C) to power a combined-cycle engine. The plant operates in two stages: first, a high-temperature cycle (Rankine) runs between 550°C and 150°C; second, the waste heat from this cycle is fed into a lower-temperature cycle (Kalina) using ammonia-water mixture, which operates between 150°C and 60°C. If the total heat input from geothermal and solar sources is 120 MW, and the plant achieves 38% overall thermal efficiency, what fraction of the total heat must come from the solar thermal storage to meet this efficiency while minimizing entropy generation across both cycles?

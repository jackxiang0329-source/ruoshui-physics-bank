# HFDPO-02095

A drone delivering medical supplies to a remote mountain village must navigate a narrow, winding canyon with sudden crosswinds. The drone’s autopilot adjusts its velocity to maintain a constant speed of 12 m/s relative to the air, but the wind varies linearly with altitude: at ground level, it’s 5 m/s perpendicular to the canyon’s axis, increasing at 0.8 m/s per 100 m ascent. The canyon slope rises at 15°, and the drone must follow a path that stays 8 m above the terrain. If the drone starts at the canyon mouth (altitude 1000 m) and must reach a landing point 2.4 km along the canyon path, what is the minimum time it can complete the journey?

# HFDPO-05665

A deep-sea submersible descends through the Mariana Trench, where pressure increases dramatically with depth due to the incompressible nature of seawater. The craft's spherical observation dome has a radius of 0.85 m and is made of thick glass with a density of 2500 kg/m³. At a depth of 10,900 m, the external seawater pressure is 1.11 × 10⁸ Pa, and the internal cabin pressure is maintained at 1 atm (1.01 × 10⁵ Pa) for crew safety. The dome is supported by a ring-shaped frame that can withstand a maximum shear force of 2.4 × 10⁷ N. Determine whether the frame is strong enough to prevent the dome from collapsing, considering both hydrostatic pressure forces and the dome’s own weight.

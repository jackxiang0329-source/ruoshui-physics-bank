# HFDPO-02008

A geothermal power plant in Iceland extracts hot water from a 2.5 km deep well at 220°C, which flows into a turbine at 180°C, losing 15% of its thermal energy to the surrounding rock before the turbine. The turbine operates with 30% efficiency, driving a generator that produces 12 MW of electrical power. What is the minimum mass flow rate of the geothermal fluid needed to sustain this output?

# HFDPO-15404

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system to generate electricity from 180°C hot water drawn from underground reservoirs. The working fluid (isobutane) enters the evaporator at 35°C and exits as saturated vapor at 150°C, then expands through a turbine before being cooled and condensed. If the plant operates with a net power output of 12 MW and the heat exchanger effectiveness is 0.85, what is the minimum mass flow rate of geothermal fluid needed to sustain this output? Assume ideal operation in the turbine and condenser, and that the geothermal fluid cools to 100°C after transferring heat.

# HFDPO-03076

A Mars rover’s suspension system uses a hydraulic damper with nonlinear resistance (proportional to velocity squared) to absorb impacts during traverse over rocky terrain. During a critical drive across a 15° sloped crater rim, the rover’s 350 kg chassis begins to oscillate violently after hitting a 20 cm high rock, reaching a maximum displacement of 12 cm from equilibrium. Given the damper’s coefficient is 850 N·s²/m² and the suspension spring stiffness is 42,000 N/m, determine the minimum damping coefficient required to prevent resonance during subsequent traverses at a vibration frequency of 4.5 Hz, assuming the terrain irregularities excite the system at this frequency.

# HFDPO-07043

In a fusion reactor’s divertor region, deuterium ions (mass ~3.34 × 10⁻²⁷ kg) stream at 2.5 × 10⁶ m/s toward a carbon target plate, with a flux density of 8 × 10²¹ ions/m²·s. The plate surface, maintained at 1400 K, experiences localized heating. Estimate the pressure exerted by the ion beam on the plate, and then evaluate how much of this pressure arises from momentum transfer versus thermal pressure from the resulting plasma edge. Assume the ions fully thermalize upon impact and that the plasma edge has an electron density of 1.2 × 10²⁰ m⁻³.

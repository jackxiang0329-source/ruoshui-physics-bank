# HFDPO-02805

A deep-sea mining robot descends into the Pacific Ocean, where the pressure at 3,000 meters depth is 300 atm. The robot carries a sealed cylindrical sample chamber with a 15 cm diameter and 20 cm height. During ascent, a small pressure relief valve fails, allowing seawater to slowly enter the chamber. When the robot surfaces, the chamber is found half-filled with seawater.

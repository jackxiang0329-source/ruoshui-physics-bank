# HFDPO-08158

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 2.0 cm diameter spherical dust particle (density 2.5 g/cm³) becomes charged to -1.5 × 10⁻¹² C. As a charged wafer moves into proximity, the particle levitates stably 5.0 mm above the wafer’s surface, which is held at -80 V relative to ground. Assuming the wafer is a large conducting plane and the particle’s charge is uniformly distributed, determine the electric field strength and direction at the particle’s location due only to the wafer. Then evaluate whether the electrostatic force can indeed balance the gravitational force on the particle given this field.

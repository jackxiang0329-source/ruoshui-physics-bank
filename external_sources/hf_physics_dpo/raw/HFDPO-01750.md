# HFDPO-01750

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The system stores 2.4 × 10⁹ J of thermal energy in 15,000 kg of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1,300 J/(kg·K). If the salt is initially at 300°C and the plant extracts heat until the temperature drops to 200°C, how much work could ideally be extracted by a heat engine operating between the hot salt and the 20°C environment?

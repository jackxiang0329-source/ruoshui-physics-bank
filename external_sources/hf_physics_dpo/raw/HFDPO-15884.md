# HFDPO-15884

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer is suspended by electrostatic forces from a grounded electrode below. The wafer has a uniform surface charge density of –2.5 μC/m², and the control electrode is charged to +100 V relative to ground. The gap between wafer and electrode is 2 mm. Due to irregularities in the wafer’s surface, the electric field is non-uniform. A laser interferometer detects a 1.2 μm vertical displacement of the wafer’s center, suggesting non-uniform force distribution. What minimum charge density must be applied to a third auxiliary electrode (1 cm × 1 cm) located 1 mm above the wafer’s edge to stabilize the wafer’s tilt and restore equilibrium?

# HFDPO-02815

A geologist discovers a buried rectangular metal artifact beneath a glacier in Antarctica, suspected to be a fragment of an ancient magnetic survey instrument. The artifact, measuring 30 cm × 20 cm × 5 cm, generates a magnetic field of 0.025 T at its center, aligned along its longest axis. An unshielded magnetometer placed 10 cm above the center records a field strength of 7.2 × 10⁻³ T, but the measured field direction is tilted 15° from vertical. Could this tilt be due to the artifact’s own magnetic moment, or is the glacier’s iron-rich sediment likely distorting the field? Analyze the expected field from a dipole moment aligned with the artifact and compare it to the observed data.

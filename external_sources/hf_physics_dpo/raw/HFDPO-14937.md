# HFDPO-14937

A solar farm in Nevada uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a grid to capture sunlight, but engineers notice efficiency drops during midday despite peak irradiance. They discover that reflected sunlight from nearby sand dunes, which have a reflectivity (albedo) of 0.45, is causing glare that interferes with solar cells. If the dunes are 1.2 km away and emit sunlight with a power density of 800 W/m² at the panel surface due to reflection, what is the effective intensity of the reflected wave, and could this contribute to a measurable phase shift in the panel’s electrical response? Assume the light reflects at 30° from normal and the panel operates at a wavelength of 600 nm.

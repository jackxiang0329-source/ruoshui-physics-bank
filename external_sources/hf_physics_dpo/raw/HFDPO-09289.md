# HFDPO-09289

A quantum imaging team at a biomedical startup is developing a label-free cancer detection system using spatially incoherent light and second-order correlation measurements (intensity interferometry) to resolve sub-diffraction-limited structures in live tissue. The measured correlation time is tau_c = 1.5,text{ps} . They interpret this as evidence of quantum coherence in a non-thermal, non-classical state. Evaluate whether this observation is physically consistent with known quantum optics principles, and determine if the observed antibunching can genuinely arise from a classical thermal source under such experimental conditions.

# HFDPO-09231

A solar thermal power plant uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a heat transfer fluid (thermocline oil) flowing through a receiver tube. On a clear summer day, the fluid enters at 290°C and exits at 390°C, absorbing 12 MW of thermal power. The tube has a 0.1 m diameter and is 30 m long, with a surface emissivity of 0.92 and an ambient air temperature of 35°C. If the plant is experiencing a 30% drop in energy output due to dust accumulation on the mirrors, what is the new heat transfer rate to the fluid, assuming the fluid flow rate and inlet temperature remain unchanged?

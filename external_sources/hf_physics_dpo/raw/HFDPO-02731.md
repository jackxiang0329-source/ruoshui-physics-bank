# HFDPO-02731

A high-altitude research balloon carrying a cryogenic sensor array descends through the stratosphere after mission completion. The sensor package, insulated with multilayer insulation (MLI) and initially at 10 K, is exposed to a rapidly changing ambient temperature profile—from -60°C at 30 km to +15°C at 10 km—over a 45-minute descent. The package has a total thermal capacitance of 85 J/K and an effective conductive resistance of 0.2 K/W to the environment. The MLI’s emissivity is 0.02, and radiative exchange with the cold sky is significant. However, convective heat transfer increases dramatically as atmospheric density rises. Design a transient thermal model to predict the package’s temperature at 15 km and assess whether the sensor will exceed its 30 K operational limit during descent.

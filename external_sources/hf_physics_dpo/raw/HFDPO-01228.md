# HFDPO-01228

A newly launched satellite in a 700 km circular LEO orbit must perform a critical station-keeping maneuver to avoid collision with a derelict upper-stage booster, now in a slightly elliptical orbit with perigee at 680 km and apogee at 720 km. The closest approach between the two objects is predicted to occur in 3.2 hours, and both objects are moving in the same orbital plane. Should the satellite perform a single Δv impulse at the current position (perigee of its circular orbit) or wait and perform a maneuver at the predicted closest approach point to minimize fuel use while ensuring safe separation?

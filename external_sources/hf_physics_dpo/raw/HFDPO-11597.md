# HFDPO-11597

During a high-altitude balloon experiment, scientists observe that a sealed, flexible polymer pouch containing nitrogen gas expands unexpectedly as it ascends from 2 km to 12 km above Earth. At 2 km, the gas pressure is 79 kPa and temperature is 278 K, but at 12 km, pressure drops to 20 kPa and temperature is 210 K. The pouch’s surface area increases by 35%, and the internal gas volume doubles. Using kinetic theory, explain whether collisions between gas molecules and the pouch wall are responsible for the increased expansion rate, and estimate the ratio of average molecular kinetic energy at 12 km to that at 2 km.

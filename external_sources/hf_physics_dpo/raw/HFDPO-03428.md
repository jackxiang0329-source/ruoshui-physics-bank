# HFDPO-03428

During a solar storm, a geomagnetic pulse induces a sudden current surge in a satellite’s power grid, causing a 0.8 T magnetic field to collapse across a 12 m² superconducting loop in 0.05 seconds. The loop is part of a radiation-shielding system designed to maintain angular momentum stability. If the loop’s moment of inertia is 3.6 kg·m², what is the resulting angular velocity of the satellite’s spin? Assume no external torques and that the induced EMF generates a current that produces a counter-torque via the magnetic field interaction.

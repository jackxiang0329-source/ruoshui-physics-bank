# HFDPO-05131

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a charged microchip (2.0 cm × 2.0 cm, uniformly charged with 5.0 nC) is suspended between two parallel electrodes separated by 1.5 cm. The upper electrode is grounded; the lower is held at +100 V. A stray dust particle (mass 0.3 μg, negligible charge) drifts into the gap. If the particle passes through a 500 μm-wide slit in the upper electrode and enters the field region, what is the minimum initial speed required for it to avoid hitting the lower electrode due to electrostatic attraction? Assume uniform field between plates and neglect gravity.

# HFDPO-02308

During a tropical storm, a coastal research team observes that a reinforced concrete drainage culvert (50 m long, 2 m wide, 1.5 m high) begins to lift during high tidal flow, despite being anchored at both ends. Water flows through the culvert at 4.2 m/s, and measurements show a pressure drop of 8.5 kPa across the structure’s top surface compared to the surrounding static seawater. The culvert has a density of 2400 kg/m³ and is fully submerged in seawater (ρ = 1025 kg/m³). Determine whether the Bernoulli-based hydrodynamic uplift force exceeds the combined weight and anchoring resistance (100 kN), and assess the structural safety margin.

# HFDPO-06128

A solar-powered garden light uses a 6V solar panel and a single LED that requires 2V and draws 20mA to operate efficiently. When the sun sets, a rechargeable 3.7V battery powers the LED through a resistor. If the battery voltage drops to 3.2V after several nights, what resistance must the series resistor have to limit current to 20mA and prevent the LED from burning out?

# HFDPO-02112

The noise spectrum shows a 1/f² divergence at low frequencies, suggesting spin bath correlations. Estimate the effective temperature of the nuclear spin bath and determine whether the observed decoherence is dominant from thermal excitation or quantum fluctuations, given the bath has 1.2 × 10¹⁸ spins with a spin-spin coupling distribution described by a broad log-normal function (σ = 1.4, mean J₀ = 1.3 μeV).

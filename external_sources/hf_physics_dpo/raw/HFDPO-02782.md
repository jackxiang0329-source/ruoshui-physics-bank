# HFDPO-02782

A marine biologist is deploying an underwater acoustic sensor array to monitor whale communication in a 1,200-meter-deep ocean trench. The array uses sound pulses at 15 kHz to detect returning echoes from migrating whales. However, the current signal processing algorithm fails to distinguish between echoes from shallow layers and those from the seafloor. Given that sound travels at 1,480 m/s in seawater and the seafloor reflects sound efficiently, what minimum time delay should the system expect between the emitted pulse and the echo from the seafloor? How might multipath interference affect the data if the whales are 300 m above the bottom?

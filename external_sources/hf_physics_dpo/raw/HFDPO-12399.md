# HFDPO-12399

A Mars rover’s suspension system uses a dual-stage shock absorber with nonlinear damping: the first stage (high stiffness) engages at 0.5 m/s, while the second (lower stiffness) activates at 1.2 m/s. If the rover’s total mass is 280 kg and it begins descent from rest at a height of 10 m, determine whether the shock absorber will fully engage in the second stage before reaching the bottom. Assume the rover’s motion is purely translational and the slope is 15 m long.

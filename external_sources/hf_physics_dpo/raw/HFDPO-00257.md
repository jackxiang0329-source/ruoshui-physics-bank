# HFDPO-00257

A private space company is tasked with deploying a network of 12 small satellites into a tightly packed, non-geostationary elliptical orbit around Mars to monitor seasonal dust storms. The mission team must now calculate the minimum fuel cost (in m/s) to correct the orbit to the intended 10° inclination while preserving the original semi-major axis and eccentricity, using a single impulse at the apocenter. The Martian gravitational parameter is 4.2828 × 10¹³ m³/s². What is the required delta-v?

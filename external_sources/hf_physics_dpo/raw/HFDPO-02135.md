# HFDPO-02135

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a small heat engine to power its instruments, using the temperature difference between the outside air (-15°C) and a buried heat exchanger warmed to 5°C by geothermal gradients. The engine operates in a cycle that absorbs 1200 J of heat from the warmer source per cycle. If the station needs at least 180 J of useful work per cycle to function, is the engine viable, and what is the maximum possible efficiency?

# HFDPO-08880

A city planner is designing a new skatepark ramp system to reduce injuries. A skateboarder starts at rest at the top of a 3-meter-high ramp and rolls down into a flat, frictionless half-pipe. At the bottom, the skateboarder transfers smoothly into a shorter ramp. If the second ramp is only 1.5 meters high, how fast must the skateboarder be moving at the bottom to just reach the top of the second ramp?

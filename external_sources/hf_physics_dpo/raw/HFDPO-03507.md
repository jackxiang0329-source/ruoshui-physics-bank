# HFDPO-03507

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote village powers a community center via a 200 m long transmission line with resistance 0.08 Ω/m and inductance 0.4 mH/m. The turbine outputs 120 V RMS at 50 Hz, but the voltage at the load drops to 105 V RMS due to line losses. The load draws 15 A RMS at a lagging power factor of 0.8. Determine the required capacitance to be installed at the load end to correct the power factor to 0.95 lagging, and calculate the new line current.

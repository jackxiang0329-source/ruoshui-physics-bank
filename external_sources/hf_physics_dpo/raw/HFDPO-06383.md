# HFDPO-06383

A wind turbine blade, 60 meters long and shaped like a tapered airfoil, is mounted on a horizontal axis with a mass of 12,000 kg distributed non-uniformly—density increasing toward the root. At a 30° pitch angle during startup, the blade experiences a net aerodynamic lift force of 85 kN acting 18 m from the hub, directed perpendicular to the blade’s span. The shaft must remain in static equilibrium under this load. What is the required reaction force at the hub (magnitude and direction) to prevent rotation, assuming the blade’s weight acts at its center of mass, located 20 m from the hub?

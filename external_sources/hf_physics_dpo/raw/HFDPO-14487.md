# HFDPO-14487

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits a sharp drop in open-circuit voltage (Voc) under prolonged high-intensity illumination, despite stable material composition. The device uses PbS quantum dots with a 1.5 eV bandgap, embedded in a ZnO matrix. At 1 sun (100 mW/cm²), Voc = 0.82 V; at 10 suns, Voc = 0.61 V. The fill factor remains near 0.75 across intensities. Propose a mechanism explaining the Voc degradation and calculate the dominant recombination pathway responsible, given that the minority carrier lifetime drops from 12 ns (1 sun) to 2.3 ns (10 suns), and the dark saturation current (J₀) increases from 1.2×10⁻⁸ A/cm² to 7.6×10⁻⁸ A/cm².

# HFDPO-07801

A small-scale wind turbine on a remote weather station uses a permanent magnet generator producing 120 V RMS at 60 Hz, but the station’s control electronics operate optimally only when the input voltage is exactly 110 V RMS at 60 Hz. To bridge the mismatch, an engineer adds a series RL circuit between the generator and the load. Given the load draws 2.5 A RMS and has a power factor of 0.8 lagging, what inductance (in mH) must the series inductor have to correct the voltage at the load to 110 V RMS?

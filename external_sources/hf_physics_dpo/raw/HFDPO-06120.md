# HFDPO-06120

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a submersible's navigation system fails due to interference from a buried ferromagnetic wreck emitting a non-uniform magnetic field. The wreck, roughly cylindrical with a radius of 2.5 m and length 15 m, has a magnetization vector of 1.8 × 10⁵ A/m aligned along its axis. At a distance of 10 m from the center of the wreck along its equatorial plane, the submersible's compass detects a field of 50 μT pointing east. What is the effective magnetic dipole moment of the wreck, and how would the field change if the submersible moved 2 m northward?

# HFDPO-00120

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, cylindrical oil reservoir capped by a rigid, impermeable dome made of composite material. The dome has a radius of 15 meters and a height of 8 meters, but it's not perfectly symmetric—its base is slightly tilted, causing uneven hydrostatic loading. The engineers must determine whether the dome will remain stable or experience a net upward force due to buoyancy imbalance, risking catastrophic structural failure. What is the net vertical force acting on the dome, considering both hydrostatic pressure and the submerged shape deviation?

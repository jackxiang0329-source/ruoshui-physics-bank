# HFDPO-06254

During a deep-sea research expedition, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, hollow titanium sphere used in an old oceanographic sensor array. The sphere has an outer diameter of 1.2 m, a wall thickness of 5 cm, and is sealed but contains a small trapped air pocket. When the ROV attempts to retrieve it, the sphere sinks slowly despite being less dense than water. Why?

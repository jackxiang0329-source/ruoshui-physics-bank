# HFDPO-09182

During a Mars sample-return mission, a robotic rover must traverse a 200-meter stretch of fine, electrostatically charged regolith that behaves like a granular fluid with a yield stress of 12 Pa. The rover’s track-tire system exerts a normal force of 1.8 kN per track, and the coefficient of static friction is μ_s = 0.75, but dynamic friction drops to μ_k = 0.52 due to particle rearrangement. If the rover’s motor applies torque to maintain a constant speed of 0.8 m/s, what is the minimum power required to overcome both friction and drag, considering that the drag force follows a quadratic dependence on velocity (drag coefficient C_d = 0.45, projected area A = 0.3 m², Martian atmospheric density ρ = 0.020 kg/m³)?

# HFDPO-01075

A fusion reactor's tokamak design uses superconducting coils to confine plasma at 15 million Kelvin, but a sudden quench in one sector causes a localized magnetic field collapse. The resulting induced currents in the reactor’s vacuum vessel generate a repulsive force exceeding 200 kN. Determine the required thickness of a titanium alloy (σ = 2.5 × 10⁶ S/m, μ₀ = 4π×10⁻⁷ H/m) shield to reduce the induced eddy current density below 10⁶ A/m² within 10 ms, assuming uniform field decay from 5 T to 0 T across the shield.

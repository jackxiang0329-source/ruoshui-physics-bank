# HFDPO-08864

A marine biologist studying coral bleaching notices that sunlight penetrating shallow reefs creates vivid, shifting patterns on the seafloor—bright bands alternating with dark zones—despite clear water. She suspects interference from microscopic calcite crystals in the coral skeleton. Assume the surrounding water has n = 1.33.

# HFDPO-05554

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoscale semiconductor particles to absorb sunlight, but at room temperature, the probability of an electron occupying a higher energy state drops sharply due to thermal fluctuations. Given a 5 nm quantum dot with a discrete energy spacing of 0.3 eV and a bandgap of 1.8 eV, what fraction of electrons in the conduction band are thermally excited above the ground state at 300 K? Assume the density of states follows a 2D-like behavior and the Fermi level is pinned near the conduction band edge.

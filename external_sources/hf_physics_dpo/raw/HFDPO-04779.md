# HFDPO-04779

A quantum computing chip operates at millikelvin temperatures, where stray capacitance between adjacent superconducting qubits (each ~100 nm × 100 nm) and their shared cryogenic readout line can induce unwanted crosstalk, corrupting quantum state readout. The readout line runs parallel to four identical qubits spaced 500 nm apart, with each qubit having a self-capacitance of 0.2 fF and mutual capacitance to the line of 0.8 fF per qubit. If the readout line is driven with a 10 ns rise-time pulse, what is the maximum allowable separation between the line and qubits to ensure the induced voltage spike on adjacent qubits remains below 10 μV, given the line’s impedance is 50 Ω and the pulse amplitude is 1 V?

# HFDPO-01092

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged hydrothermal vent system emitting superheated fluid (350°C) into cold seawater (2°C). The vent discharges at 0.5 m³/s through a 15 cm diameter nozzle. Engineers observe unexpected vortex shedding downstream that disrupts sensor readings. Estimate the Reynolds number at the vent exit and determine whether the flow is in the transitional or fully turbulent regime. Discuss how the density stratification and thermal expansion of the fluid might alter the expected stability of the jet.

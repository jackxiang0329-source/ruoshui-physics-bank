# HFDPO-05161

A marine biologist detects a series of low-frequency pulses from a deep-sea hydrothermal vent, each lasting 1.2 seconds and spaced 3.5 seconds apart. The pulses have a frequency of 27 Hz and travel through seawater at 1530 m/s, but the signal is distorted upon reaching a hydrophone array due to wavefront spreading and multipath interference. The array is positioned 8.4 km from the vent, with sensors at different depths (100 m and 500 m). Calculate the phase difference between the direct and bottom-reflected wave components at the deeper sensor, and explain why the amplitude at 500 m depth is attenuated by 12 dB relative to the shallow sensor, despite the deeper path being longer.

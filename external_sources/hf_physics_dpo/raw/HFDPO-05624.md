# HFDPO-05624

A wind turbine blade in a coastal wind farm operates at a height where average wind speed is 12 m/s, but during storms, gusts reach 30 m/s. The blade’s leading edge has a curved profile, creating a pressure difference that generates lift. How does this compare to the design pressure limit of 1.8 kPa?

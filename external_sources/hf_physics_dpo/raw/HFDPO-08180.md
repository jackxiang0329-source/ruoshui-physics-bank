# HFDPO-08180

A Mars rover’s rear wheel, designed for low rolling resistance, begins slipping unexpectedly during a descent on a 15° sandy slope. The wheel has a radius of 0.3 m, mass 8 kg, and rolls without slipping initially, but as sand compaction decreases under load, the static friction coefficient drops from μ_s = 0.6 to μ_s = 0.4 over 2 seconds. The drag force due to Martian atmospheric turbulence (ρ = 0.02 kg/m³, C_d = 1.2) acts backward, proportional to v². Determine whether the wheel will fully transition to sliding before reaching a terminal speed, given the rover’s initial speed is 1.2 m/s.

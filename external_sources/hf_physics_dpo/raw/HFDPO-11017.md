# HFDPO-11017

In a quantum imaging lab, researchers use a novel polarization-sensitive detector to study entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion. The setup includes a type-II crystal producing correlated photons with orthogonal polarization states. To isolate the desired quantum signal from noise, they must determine the optimal orientation of a rotatable waveplate placed before a polarizing beam splitter to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) for a specific correlation channel. The detector measures intensity as a function of waveplate angle θ, with a known polarization-dependent noise floor of 2.3 nW and signal peak at 18.7 nW. Given that the pump laser is linearly polarized at 45° to the crystal axes and the crystal induces a phase shift of π/2 between the ordinary and extraordinary beams, what waveplate angle θ maximizes the SNR for detecting the HH (horizontal-horizontal) correlation channel?

# HFDPO-02247

A solar-powered weather station on a remote Arctic ridge relies on a tripod-mounted sensor array anchored to permafrost via concrete foundation piles. Each pile is 2.5 m long, 0.3 m in diameter, and embedded 1.8 m into frozen soil with a non-uniform cohesion profile: 50 kPa at the surface, increasing linearly to 120 kPa at 1.8 m depth. Gusts of wind up to 50 m/s (180 km/h) exert a lateral force of 12 kN on the sensor array at 3.2 m above the ground. What minimum pile diameter is required to ensure a factor of safety against lateral failure of 2.5, assuming the pile can only resist shear along its shaft and not bending?

# HFDPO-00224

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels with anti-reflective coatings to maximize energy capture. However, during peak sunlight hours, glare from the panels interferes with nearby air traffic control radar, causing signal distortion. The radar operates at a wavelength of 3 cm and detects signals polarized horizontally. Given that the sun is 50° above the horizon and the panels are tilted at 25° from horizontal, what polarization state emerges from sunlight reflecting off the panel surface, and how can the coating be modified to minimize horizontal polarization in the reflected beam?

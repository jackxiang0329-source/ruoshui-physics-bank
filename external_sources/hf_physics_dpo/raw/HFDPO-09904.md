# HFDPO-09904

The interference peaks at 15.7 GHz and shows standing wave patterns across the 80 cm-long copper waveguide housing the qubits, with nodes spaced 1.2 cm apart. The team suspects a parasitic mode from an unexpected harmonic leakage. How might the structure excite higher-order modes despite operating below the dominant mode cutoff?

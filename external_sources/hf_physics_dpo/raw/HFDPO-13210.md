# HFDPO-13210

A solar-powered rover on Mars must maintain a stable 12 V power supply for its scientific instruments, but the sun's angle causes the photovoltaic array’s output to fluctuate between 14 V and 18 V daily. The rover’s internal circuitry includes a variable load that draws 2 A when active and 0.5 A in standby. Design a passive voltage regulation network using only resistors and a Zener diode (7.5 V, 1 W) to ensure the load never receives more than 12 V, while minimizing power loss. Determine the minimum resistance value needed in series with the Zener diode to prevent burnout under peak input (18 V) and maximum load (2 A).

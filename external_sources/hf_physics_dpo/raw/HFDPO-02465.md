# HFDPO-02465

A research team is designing a compact magnetic levitation module for a next-gen quantum sensor. The device uses a high-temperature superconductor (YBCO) cooled by liquid nitrogen (77 K) to levitate above a permanent magnet track. The levitation height is critical—too low, and mechanical contact causes decoherence; too high, and magnetic forces weaken. At 77 K, the superconductor exhibits a critical current density of 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m². The magnet produces a vertical field gradient of 0.08 T/m at the levitation point. What is the minimum cross-sectional area of the superconductor loop that can stably levitate without flux penetration, assuming Meissner screening and flux pinning balance the weight?

# HFDPO-01163

In a next-generation quantum memory device, ultracold rubidium atoms are trapped in a 3D optical lattice with site spacing of 500 nm, cooled to 150 nK. The system exhibits strong on-site interactions (U = 2.3 μK) and tunneling amplitude J = 0.8 μK. Recent measurements show a sudden drop in coherence between neighboring sites during a quench protocol. Analyze whether the system has entered a Mott insulating phase or remains in a superfluid state under these conditions, and evaluate the critical interaction strength required for quantum phase transition.

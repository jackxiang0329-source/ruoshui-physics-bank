# HFDPO-13785

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole lined with a superconducting coil (R ≈ 0) to harvest energy from Earth’s natural magnetic field fluctuations caused by solar storms. During a geomagnetic storm, the field change rate doubles. What is the maximum instantaneous power delivered to a resistive load (500 Ω) if the system converts induced voltage via superconducting-to-normal transition in the coil?

# HFDPO-03152

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain uses a small antenna to send temperature and wind data every 10 minutes via radio waves. The antenna operates at 433 MHz and must transmit through a dense forest where signal strength drops significantly. If the station’s transmitter emits 100 mW of power and the receiving base station is 2 km away, what is the intensity of the radio wave at the receiver, assuming isotropic emission and no absorption?

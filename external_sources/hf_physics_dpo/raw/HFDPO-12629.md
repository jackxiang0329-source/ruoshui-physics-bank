# HFDPO-12629

In a quantum satellite testbed, a ring-shaped superconducting loop (radius 5.2 cm, critical current density 1.2 × 10⁸ A/m²) is cooled below T_c and initialized with a persistent current. After a 10-minute exposure to a weak, fluctuating magnetic field (ΔB ≈ 0.3 nT, frequency 25 Hz), the measured flux through the ring drifts by 1.8 × 10⁻⁶ Φ₀. Assess whether this drift arises from quantum tunneling (fluxon motion) or thermal excitation through the Josephson junctions, given a junction energy barrier of 12 meV and a bath temperature of 1.8 K.

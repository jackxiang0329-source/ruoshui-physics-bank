# HFDPO-04062

A solar-powered drone designed for long-duration atmospheric research must maintain a stable onboard temperature during a 24-hour mission over the Arctic. The drone's thermal regulation system uses a network of copper resistive heaters, each rated at 50 W, to prevent critical electronics from freezing.

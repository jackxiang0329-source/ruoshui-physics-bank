# HFDPO-08068

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem thrives near a mid-ocean ridge where superheated water (350°C, 30 MPa) emerges from the crust into cold abyssal seawater (2°C). Engineers propose harvesting waste heat from a nearby geothermal plant to power a closed-loop heat engine that pumps seawater into the vent to enhance mineral deposition for sustainable mining. The engine operates between the hot vent fluid and the cold ocean, with a maximum theoretical efficiency of 62%. If the engine produces 1.2 MW of net power while rejecting heat to the ocean at a rate of 1.8 MW, what is the required rate of heat input from the vent fluid, and what does this imply about the sustainability of continuous operation?

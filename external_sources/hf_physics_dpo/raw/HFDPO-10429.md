# HFDPO-10429

A farmer notices her solar-powered irrigation pump stops working during strong thunderstorms. The pump relies on a small generator that spins when water flows through a turbine. She suspects the storm’s changing magnetic fields are inducing unwanted currents in the generator coil. If the magnetic field through the coil changes from 0.02 T to 0.05 T in 0.4 seconds, what is the average induced emf in a 100-turn coil with an area of 0.01 m²?

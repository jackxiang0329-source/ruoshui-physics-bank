# HFDPO-03133

A quantum computing lab in Zurich detects mysterious, frequency-specific electromagnetic noise disrupting qubit coherence during operations at 2.5 GHz. The noise appears only when a superconducting ring current stabilizes in a fluxonium qubit, but vanishes during ramp-up. The lab’s Faraday cage is intact, and nearby electronics are shielded. However, a newly installed cryogenic pump with a rotating magnetic bearing operates at 150 RPM in proximity. Use Maxwell’s equations to determine whether the pump’s rotating dipole moment could generate resonant EM interference, and calculate the required magnetic dipole moment strength to induce the observed noise given the qubit’s sensitivity threshold of 10 nT in a 1 cm³ volume.

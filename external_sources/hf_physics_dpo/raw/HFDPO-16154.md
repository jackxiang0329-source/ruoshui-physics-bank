# HFDPO-16154

A quantum satellite network uses entangled photon pairs for secure communication across Earth’s equator, with one station in Cape Verde and another in São Tomé, 2,600 km apart. The satellites orbit at 600 km altitude, moving at 7.6 km/s relative to Earth. Due to relativistic time dilation and the Sagnac effect, the phase coherence of the entangled photons degrades over time. If the system requires phase stability within 0.1 radians over a 10-second transmission window, what is the maximum relative velocity between the satellites (as measured from Earth) that still permits successful entanglement distribution, assuming only special relativity effects (ignoring gravity)?

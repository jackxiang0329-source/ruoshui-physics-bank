# HFDPO-09826

A wind turbine in a remote mountain valley generates 1.2 MW of power at peak efficiency, but engineers notice voltage fluctuations during sudden gusts. The turbine’s 300-turn rotor coil (diameter 1.8 m) spins within a uniform magnetic field of 0.45 T. When wind speed increases from 12 m/s to 16 m/s in 0.5 seconds, the rotational frequency jumps from 15 Hz to 20 Hz. Calculate the average induced emf during this transition and explain why the voltage spike might damage the grid interface.

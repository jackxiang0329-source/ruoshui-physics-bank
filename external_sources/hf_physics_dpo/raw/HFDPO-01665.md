# HFDPO-01665

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses a laser-based sensor to measure micro-scale surface topography of reef structures. The device emits a 632.8 nm helium-neon laser focused through a circular aperture of 1.2 mm. When scanning a patch of live coral, a diffraction pattern with a central maximum width of 1.8 mm appears on a 30 cm detector screen. The biologist suspects the pattern might be influenced by interference from microscopic surface ridges spaced 50 μm apart. What is the angular width of the central diffraction maximum, and does the observed pattern indicate significant interference from surface features?

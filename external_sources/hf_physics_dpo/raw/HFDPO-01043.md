# HFDPO-01043

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that electrons tunneling through a 1.2 nm-thick silicon oxide barrier in a Josephson junction exhibit interference patterns when the tunneling current is measured over time—yet the same electrons fail to produce such patterns when detected via single-electron transistors with sub-100 fs resolution. Given that the electron’s de Broglie wavelength is 0.34 nm and the measurement uncertainty in position is Δx ≈ 0.5 nm, evaluate whether the loss of interference is due to the destruction of coherence from wavefunction collapse, or if it can be explained by decoherence from environmental coupling.

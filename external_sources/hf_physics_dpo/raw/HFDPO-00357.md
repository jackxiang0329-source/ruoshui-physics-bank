# HFDPO-00357

A solar farm in Arizona uses photovoltaic panels arranged in series-parallel strings to power a remote weather station. Each panel outputs 35 V and 8 A under peak sunlight, but the station’s control system only accepts 120 V DC and requires 480 W. The connecting cables are 50 m long, made of copper with a cross-sectional area of 4 mm². One day, the station reports erratic power readings. You suspect voltage drop in the wiring is causing insufficient power delivery. What is the maximum number of panels in a single string that avoids a voltage drop exceeding 5% of the panel’s rated output?

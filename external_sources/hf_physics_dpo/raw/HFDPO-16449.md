# HFDPO-16449

During a high-altitude drone delivery mission in the Himalayas, a prototype delivery drone encounters sudden turbulence at 5,200 meters, where air density drops to 0.75 kg/m³ and temperature is –15°C. Engineers suspect increased aerodynamic drag due to boundary layer transition on the blades. Is the energy loss consistent with this drag change?

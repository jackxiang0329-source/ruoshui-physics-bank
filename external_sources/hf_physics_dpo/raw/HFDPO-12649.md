# HFDPO-12649

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor forest fires in remote regions must glide silently from 4,500 m altitude to a 2,000 m elevation over a 30 km horizontal distance, using only aerodynamic lift and solar energy stored in a supercapacitor bank.

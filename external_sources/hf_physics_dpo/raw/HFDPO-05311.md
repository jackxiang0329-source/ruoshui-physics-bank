# HFDPO-05311

A solar-powered desalination unit in a remote coastal village uses a porous membrane coated with nanostructured graphene to selectively allow water vapor through while blocking salt ions. During peak sunlight, the system maintains a vapor pressure gradient of 3.1 kPa across the membrane at 32°C. The membrane’s pores are 2.5 nm in diameter, and water molecules are modeled as hard spheres with a diameter of 0.28 nm. Estimate the mean free path of water vapor inside the pores and determine whether Knudsen diffusion or viscous flow dominates.

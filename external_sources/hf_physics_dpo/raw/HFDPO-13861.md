# HFDPO-13861

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a dual-polarization antenna to communicate with ground stations, but signal degradation occurs during solar storms. The incoming cosmic microwave background radiation, polarized at 45° to the antenna’s reference axis, interferes with the satellite’s signal. Given that the satellite’s receiver filters out 60% of the orthogonal polarization component but also attenuates the desired polarization by 10%, determine the effective signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) degradation when the incident power per unit area is 3.2 × 10⁻²⁰ W/m² and the background is unpolarized except for the 45° polarized component. Assume the receiver’s bandwidth is 20 MHz and the system noise temperature is 120 K.

# HFDPO-12309

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a circularly polarized antenna to receive signals from a ground station, but interference from reflected signals off a nearby mountain range causes signal fading. The reflected wave undergoes a phase shift of π/3 upon reflection and has the opposite circular polarization. If the direct signal has an intensity of 2.5 mW/m² and the reflected signal has 0.8 mW/m², what is the net intensity at the receiver when the two waves are combined, assuming the receiver is sensitive to only one polarization state?

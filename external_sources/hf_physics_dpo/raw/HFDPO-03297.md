# HFDPO-03297

A solar microgrid on a remote research station in the Arctic uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic panels connected in a complex mesh with shared load buses and reconfigurable switches. At peak load, one panel outputs 14 A at 19 V under partial shading. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current distribution when the system is reconfigured to balance charging and minimize total energy loss across the interconnections.

# HFDPO-05344

In a quantum cryptography lab, two entangled photons are generated with orthogonal polarization states, but due to imperfect alignment of waveplates in the transmission path, one photon’s polarization is distorted by a 15° phase shift relative to the other. The receiving station measures the correlation between polarization outcomes using a polarization analyzer rotated at 30° to the original basis. What is the expected violation of the CHSH inequality, and how does the phase mismatch affect the security of the quantum key distribution?

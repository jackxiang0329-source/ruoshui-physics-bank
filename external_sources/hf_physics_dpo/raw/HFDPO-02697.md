# HFDPO-02697

A deep-space probe traveling at 0.8c relative to Earth sends a series of 100 MHz radio signals at 1-second intervals (its onboard time). An Earth-based observatory detects these signals, but due to relativistic effects, the signal timing and frequency are distorted. If the probe is 4 light-years away when it emits its first signal, how long after the first signal was sent does Earth receive the 10th signal?

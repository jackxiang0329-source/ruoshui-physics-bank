# HFDPO-08195

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic uses a silicon photodiode to monitor sunlight intensity, but during winter months, the readings fluctuate unpredictably. The diode’s dark current doubles every 10°C rise in temperature, and ambient temperatures vary from –40°C to –10°C. If the diode’s intrinsic carrier concentration at –30°C is 1.5 × 10¹⁰ cm⁻³, what is the ratio of the total current (photocurrent + dark current) at –10°C to that at –40°C, assuming constant light intensity and negligible recombination?

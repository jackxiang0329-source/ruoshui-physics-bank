# HFDPO-09360

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable hover during data collection at 20,000 meters, where air density is 0.08 kg/m³ and wind gusts reach 12 m/s. The drone’s quadcopter frame is symmetric but has unevenly distributed battery packs, causing the center of mass to shift 15 cm from the geometric center. Each of the four identical propellers generates 35 N of thrust at 45° to the vertical due to tilt for stability. Determine the minimum torque each motor must provide to prevent rotational tipping, given that the propellers are 0.8 m from the center and the drone’s total mass is 12 kg. Assume the drone is not translating but may rotate about its center of mass.

# HFDPO-10836

A biomedical engineer is designing a resonant inductive coupling system to wirelessly charge implantable cardiac rhythm monitors. To maximize power transfer efficiency while avoiding harmonic distortion, the engineer must determine the optimal capacitance to add in series with the receiver coil so that the system resonates at 1.2 MHz. However, the internal coil has a self-capacitance of 12 pF due to its tight winding. What value of external series capacitance must be added to achieve resonance at 1.2 MHz, and what is the resulting impedance seen by the transmitter?

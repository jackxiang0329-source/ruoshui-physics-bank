# HFDPO-01536

A small offshore wind farm uses a 50 Hz AC grid to transmit power to a coastal substation 12 km away. The transmission line has a resistance of 0.2 Ω/km and inductance of 1.5 mH/km. Due to poor power factor, the substation's load draws 1.2 MW at 0.7 lagging, causing excessive voltage drop. What capacitor bank (in μF) must be installed at the substation to improve the power factor to 0.95 lagging, and how much reactive power will it supply?

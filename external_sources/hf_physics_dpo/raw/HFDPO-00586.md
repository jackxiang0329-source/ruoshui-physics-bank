# HFDPO-00586

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day, enabling electricity generation after sunset. The system stores 12 GJ of thermal energy in a 50,000 kg tank of sodium nitrate-based salt, initially at 290°C. At night, the salt cools to 200°C while driving a heat engine with a maximum theoretical efficiency of 40%. Engineers must determine whether the stored energy can sustain a 50 MW power output for 6 hours. Is it feasible?

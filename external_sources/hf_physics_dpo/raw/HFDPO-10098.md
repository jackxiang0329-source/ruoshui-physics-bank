# HFDPO-10098

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor atmospheric methane over the Amazon Basin must maintain steady flight at 12 km altitude during a 72-hour mission. The drone’s wings, optimized for low Reynolds number flow, experience unsteady aerodynamic forces due to thermal updrafts and turbulence. At a critical moment, a sudden 4.5 m/s vertical wind gust induces a pitch oscillation, with the center of mass shifting 0.3 m forward relative to the aerodynamic center. Given the drone’s moment of inertia about the pitch axis is 18.7 kg·m², and the resulting pitching moment coefficient is −0.42 (per radian), determine the maximum angular acceleration that could destabilize the control system—before active stabilization corrects it—under these transient conditions.

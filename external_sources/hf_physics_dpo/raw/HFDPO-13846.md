# HFDPO-13846

A solar-powered drone designed for long-duration atmospheric monitoring hovers steadily at 800 m altitude, where wind gusts reach 22 m/s. The drone’s 3.2 m-long solar array, mounted on a lightweight carbon fiber mast, must withstand aerodynamic forces during a sudden gust. If the array has a projected area of 2.1 m² and a drag coefficient of 1.3, and the mast can endure a maximum bending moment of 145 N·m at its base, is the structure safe? Assume the wind acts perpendicularly to the array and the center of pressure is at the midpoint.

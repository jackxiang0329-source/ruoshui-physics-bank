# HFDPO-12982

A solar farm in rural Nevada uses a hybrid grid-tied system with three parallel strings of photovoltaic panels, each feeding into a common inverter. During a sudden cloud cover, the output of string A drops to 65% of its rated current (28 A), while strings B and C remain at full capacity (42 A each). The inverter’s internal diode allows backflow only if the voltage across it exceeds 0.7 V. Given that the combined bus voltage is 520 V and the system’s total load draws 90 A, analyze whether current will reverse through the inverter’s protection diode and, if so, what magnitude it would have.

# HFDPO-06298

A planetary defense mission detects an asteroid, 2.3 km in diameter with density 2.8 g/cm³, on a trajectory that will graze Earth’s orbit at 1.2 AU. To deflect it, a kinetic impactor must alter its velocity by 0.07 mm/s at the closest approach. Given that the impactor (mass 5.2 tons) strikes at 15 km/s relative to the asteroid and the deflection must be complete before the asteroid enters Earth’s gravitational sphere of influence (~1.5 million km), what is the minimum distance from Earth’s orbit at which the impact must occur to succeed? Assume the asteroid’s orbit is nearly circular and ignore atmospheric drag.

# HFDPO-16275

A marine survey vessel deploys a towed underwater sensor array with a 500 m long conductive cable moving at 5 m/s through Earth’s magnetic field (50 μT, vertical component). The cable’s orientation varies dynamically during surveying. At a critical moment, the cable forms a 30° angle with the horizontal plane and the vessel’s velocity vector is 15° west of north, while Earth’s field points vertically downward. Calculate the induced EMF across the cable ends, then analyze how the phase shift between the velocity and magnetic field vectors affects the net EMF in a rotating frame of reference.

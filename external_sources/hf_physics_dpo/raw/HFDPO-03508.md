# HFDPO-03508

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must ascend from 3,000 m to 15,000 m while maintaining a constant speed against a temperature-dependent air density that varies as ρ(h) = ρ₀e^(−h/H), where ρ₀ = 1.2 kg/m³, H = 7,500 m, and h is altitude. The drone’s mass is 12 kg, and its propeller thrust adjusts to counteract drag, which scales with v²ρ(h). Given that the drone’s solar panels provide 180 W of power continuously and efficiency losses reduce usable power to 85%, determine the minimum thrust required at 10,000 m to maintain constant speed, assuming the drone completes the climb in exactly 4 hours.

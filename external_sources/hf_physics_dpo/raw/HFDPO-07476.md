# HFDPO-07476

A satellite in geostationary orbit (35,786 km above Earth) carries a highly precise atomic clock synchronized with ground-based clocks. After one year of operation, mission control notices a time discrepancy of 45 microseconds—clocks on the satellite are ahead. Explain why this happens using special relativity and general relativity, then calculate the contribution of special relativity alone to the time dilation effect, assuming the satellite’s orbital speed is 3.07 km/s.

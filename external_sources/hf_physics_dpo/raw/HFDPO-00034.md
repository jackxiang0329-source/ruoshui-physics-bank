# HFDPO-00034

A fusion reactor’s magnetic confinement system uses a toroidal field generated by 1200 superconducting coils, each carrying 15 kA, arranged in a symmetric ring with a major radius of 6 m and minor radius of 1.2 m. Due to a manufacturing defect, one coil is misaligned by 2° from the ideal azimuthal position. Calculate the net radial force per unit length on the flawed coil due to the magnetic interaction with the rest of the system, assuming the other coils operate perfectly and the field is axisymmetric.

# HFDPO-11922

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert monitors temperature and wind speed but intermittently fails during peak sunlight hours. Engineers discover the issue is not solar panel output but a voltage spike in the DC regulation circuit. The station uses a 24 V solar array wired in series with a 12 V lead-acid battery (internal resistance 0.3 Ω) and a buck-boost converter that maintains a stable 5 V supply to sensors. When sunlight increases, the panel voltage spikes to 32 V, causing the regulator to overheat. The circuit includes a 100 Ω shunt resistor across the battery to monitor charge state. What is the actual voltage drop across this shunt resistor during the spike, and why does it indicate a fault in the regulation logic?

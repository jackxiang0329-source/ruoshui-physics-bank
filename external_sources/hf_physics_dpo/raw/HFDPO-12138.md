# HFDPO-12138

A solar panel on a satellite in low Earth orbit degrades over time due to exposure to high-energy protons from the Van Allen belts. After 2 years, the panel’s efficiency drops by 15%. Assume the panel’s degradation is proportional to the total proton fluence.

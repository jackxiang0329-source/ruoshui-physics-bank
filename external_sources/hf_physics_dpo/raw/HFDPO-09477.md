When sunlight (550 nm wavelength, 10° incidence angle) strikes the array, constructive interference in the diffracted orders boosts efficiency—yet the dominant peak shifts unexpectedly. Analyze the angular dependence of the first-order diffraction peak and determine the effective refractive index of the surrounding dielectric medium causing the shift.

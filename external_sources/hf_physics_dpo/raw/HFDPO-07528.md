# HFDPO-07528

A fusion reactor prototype uses a spherical plasma confinement chamber with a radius of 2.4 m, where deuterium-tritium reactions release 17.6 MeV per fusion event. During a 30-second pulse, the reactor sustains a plasma density of 1.2 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ and a temperature of 150 million K. Engineers observe a 0.8% drop in magnetic confinement energy over the pulse and must determine if the energy loss is consistent with conservation of momentum and energy, accounting for both radiation and neutron recoil.

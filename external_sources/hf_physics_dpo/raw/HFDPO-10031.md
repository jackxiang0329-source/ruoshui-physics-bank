# HFDPO-10031

A biophysics team is developing a synthetic hydrogel scaffold for regenerating damaged heart tissue, designed to mimic the elastic modulus of native myocardium (≈15 kPa). During mechanical testing, they observe that the scaffold exhibits nonlinear elasticity: under small strains (ε < 0.1), it behaves linearly with modulus 12 kPa, but at larger strains (ε > 0.2), the stress-strain curve deviates significantly. Using experimental data from uniaxial tensile tests, they need to determine whether this nonlinearity is due to geometric softening or material-level hyperelasticity. The scaffold is 5 mm thick and subjected to a uniaxial stretch of 1.4 in a custom bioreactor.

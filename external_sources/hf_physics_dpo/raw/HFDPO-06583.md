# HFDPO-06583

A fusion reactor’s plasma confinement system uses a toroidal magnetic field to trap high-energy deuterium-tritium ions, each with 50 keV kinetic energy. During a brief instability, a pulse of 1.2 × 10¹⁹ ions escapes radially, striking a diverter plate with a 20° oblique angle. The plate dissipates 92% of the kinetic energy as heat, but the remaining momentum transfer induces a 0.3 N·s impulse on the structure. Assuming no external forces during the pulse, calculate the change in the center-of-mass velocity of the entire reactor vessel (mass: 850 metric tons) due to momentum conservation.

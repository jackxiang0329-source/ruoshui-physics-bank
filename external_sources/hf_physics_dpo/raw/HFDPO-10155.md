# HFDPO-10155

A wildlife biologist uses a remote camera to monitor a rare bird nest high in a cliffside tree. The camera is mounted 15 meters from the base of the cliff, angled upward at 35° above horizontal. Due to dense foliage, the nest is only visible when sunlight reflects off a small metallic tag on the bird’s leg and into the lens. If the tag is 8 meters above ground and the sun is low in the sky at an angle of 20° above the horizon, what is the minimum horizontal distance the tag must be from the cliff face so that the reflected sunlight reaches the camera lens? Assume the tag acts as a perfect mirror.

# HFDPO-04455

During a sandstorm in the Middle East, a 3.2-meter-tall dust devil forms with a net charge of +150 μC distributed non-uniformly along its vertical axis, peaking at the base. A nearby 12-meter-high wind turbine blade (insulated) develops an induced charge of -45 μC due to the strong electric field. Using the known charge density profile ρ(z) = ρ₀e^(-z/H), where H = 0.8 m and z = 0 at the base, determine the minimum safe distance the turbine must be from the dust devil’s center to avoid corona discharge (threshold field = 3 × 10⁶ V/m).

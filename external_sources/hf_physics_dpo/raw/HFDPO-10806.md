# HFDPO-10806

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote farming village uses a 24 V battery bank charged by photovoltaic panels during the day. The system must power a 120 W submersible pump for 8 hours each night. Due to wiring degradation, the copper cables (50 m long, 2.5 mm² cross-section) have increased resistance. The pump requires exactly 12 V to operate efficiently. If the voltage at the pump drops below 11.5 V, it stalls and fails. What is the maximum current the pump can draw without stalling, and what must be the minimum efficiency of the voltage regulator to maintain proper operation?

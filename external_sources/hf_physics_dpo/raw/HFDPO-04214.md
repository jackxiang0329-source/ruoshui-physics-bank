# HFDPO-04214

A 65 kg climber is suspended from a single rope anchored at two points on a cliff face—3.2 m apart horizontally and 1.8 m vertically offset. The rope forms angles of 40° and 65° with the horizontal on either side of the climber. What is the tension in each segment of the rope, and how does this change if the climber shifts 0.5 m toward the left anchor?

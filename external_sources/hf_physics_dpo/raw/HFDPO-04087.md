# HFDPO-04087

A coastal city is designing a new seawall to protect against storm surges. Engineers discover that during high winds, water accumulates at the base of the wall due to a combination of surge elevation and wave setup, creating a dangerous pressure buildup. The wall is 6 meters high, with a 1.5-meter wide flat base. During a storm, the water level reaches 4.8 meters above the seabed, and wind-driven waves cause a 0.4-meter wave setup. Assuming the wall resists lateral forces from hydrostatic pressure and the flow is steady with negligible viscous effects, what is the total horizontal force (in kN) exerted on the wall’s base by the water?

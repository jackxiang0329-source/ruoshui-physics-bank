# HFDPO-01010

A high-altitude research balloon carrying sensitive atmospheric sensors ascends through the stratosphere, where the air density drops to 1/10th of sea level and the temperature plunges to –60°C. The balloon’s interior is sealed and initially holds 500 m³ of air at 20°C and atmospheric pressure. As the balloon rises, the internal air cools adiabatically and expands. If the internal pressure remains equal to the external pressure due to the balloon’s elasticity, what is the root-mean-square speed of the nitrogen molecules inside after the temperature drops to –60°C?

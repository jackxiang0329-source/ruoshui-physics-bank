# HFDPO-03144

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a slow-moving, dense turbidity current carrying sediment-laden water down a submarine canyon. The ROV’s sensors measure a velocity gradient of 0.05 s⁻¹ at the canyon floor, and the mean flow velocity is 0.8 m/s. Determine whether the local flow at the canyon floor is hydrodynamically stable or prone to instability, and evaluate whether the transition to turbulence is driven primarily by shear or by buoyancy forces.

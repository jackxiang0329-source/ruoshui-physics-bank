# HFDPO-03592

A quantum imaging lab is developing a single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) array for lidar in autonomous vehicles, but interference from ambient sunlight (550 nm) causes false triggering at a rate of 120 kHz. The SPADs are arranged in a 32×32 grid with 1.2 μm pixel pitch, and each pixel has a quantum efficiency of 85% at 550 nm. To mitigate noise, the team proposes using a narrowband interference filter with a 10 nm full-width at half-maximum (FWHM) centered at 550 nm. What is the maximum acceptable transmission bandwidth (in nm) of the filter to reduce false triggers to below 1 kHz, assuming sunlight is uniformly distributed over 100 nm of spectrum and the SPADs are exposed to 10⁴ W/m² of ambient light?

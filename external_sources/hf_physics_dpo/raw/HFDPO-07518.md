# HFDPO-07518

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 10 cm × 10 cm silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform charge density due to electron bombardment during plasma etching—maximum surface charge density reaches 5 nC/m² near the edge, decreasing linearly to zero at the center. The wafer is suspended by four insulating support pins, each 2 mm in diameter and 15 mm long, spaced at the corners. Calculate the net electrostatic force on the wafer and determine whether the stress on any support pin exceeds the fracture limit of 1.2 MPa. Consider both the long-range force from the charge distribution and the local field enhancement at pin edges.

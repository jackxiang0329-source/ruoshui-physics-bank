# HFDPO-06879

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 400°C. During a sudden cloud cover event, the system must rapidly cool the oil to prevent thermal degradation. The 20 m long receiver tube has a 0.1 m inner diameter, contains 350 kg of oil with a specific heat of 2100 J/kg·K, and is insulated with 5 cm of silica aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K). If the ambient temperature drops from 30°C to 15°C over 20 minutes, what is the minimum convective heat transfer coefficient required from a forced-air cooling system to reduce the oil temperature to 300°C? Assume negligible heat loss through the ends and uniform oil temperature.

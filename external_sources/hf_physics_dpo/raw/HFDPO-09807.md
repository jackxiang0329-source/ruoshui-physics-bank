# HFDPO-09807

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanostructure to enhance light absorption via diffraction-coupled resonances. Each unit cell has a 300 nm period with a 120 nm wide slit in a 400 nm thick dielectric film. When illuminated with a broadband solar spectrum (400–800 nm), experimental measurements show a sharp absorption peak at 680 nm. What is the order of diffraction that explains this resonance, and why does the peak vanish when the dielectric thickness is reduced by 50 nm?

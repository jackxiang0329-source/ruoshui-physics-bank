# HFDPO-04627

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2 mm diameter to 4 mm under physiological pressure, while maintaining structural integrity under cyclic loading. The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a nonlinear stress-strain curve approximated by σ = 150ε + 10ε³ (in MPa), and exhibits a residual strain of 2% after unloading. The artery wall exerts a radial force of 0.3 N/cm over the 3 cm length of the stent. Determine the minimum initial wall thickness required to prevent plastic deformation during expansion, and evaluate whether the stent will fail over 10 years of pulsatile stress (120 bpm, 100 mmHg peak pressure), considering fatigue at 90% of the yield strength.

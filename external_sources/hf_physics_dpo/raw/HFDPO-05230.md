# HFDPO-05230

A photographer at a lake notices glare from the water is ruining shots of distant trees. They have a polarizing filter that reduces glare when aligned properly. If the reflected light at the water’s surface is 90% polarized horizontally and the filter blocks 50% of unpolarized light, what percentage of the total incoming light reaches the camera when the filter is rotated to minimize glare?

# HFDPO-12061

A wind farm in a coastal region experiences fluctuating power output due to sudden gusts. Each turbine has a 50-meter diameter rotor with 3 blades, and the generator uses a 1000-turn coil rotating in a 0.4 T magnetic field. If wind speed increases from 12 m/s to 18 m/s in 2 seconds, causing the rotor’s angular speed to rise steadily, what is the average induced emf in the coil during this interval? Assume the coil’s area is fully aligned with the field at all times.

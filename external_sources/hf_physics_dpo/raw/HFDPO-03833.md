# HFDPO-03833

A deep-sea mining drone descends through the Mariana Trench, where the seawater density increases non-linearly with depth due to compression. At 10,000 m, the pressure is 1.1 × 10⁸ Pa, and the local density is 1050 kg/m³. The drone’s internal buoyancy chamber, initially filled with compressible gas at 1.0 atm, is designed to maintain neutral buoyancy at this depth. However, the chamber’s seal fails, allowing seawater to slowly infiltrate. If the chamber’s volume contracts by 30% due to external pressure, what is the new net force on the drone? Assume the drone’s total mass (excluding gas) is 450 kg and its outer volume is 0.75 m³.

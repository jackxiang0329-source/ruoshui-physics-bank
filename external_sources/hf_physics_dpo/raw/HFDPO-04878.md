# HFDPO-04878

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent emits superheated fluid at 350°C through a porous chimney made of sulfide minerals. The chimney has a cylindrical shape, 2 meters tall with a base radius of 15 cm, and its resistivity varies with depth due to changing mineral composition: ρ(z) = ρ₀e^(αz), where ρ₀ = 1.2 Ω·m, α = 0.1 m⁻¹, and z = 0 at the base. Seawater at 2°C surrounds the chimney, creating a radial temperature gradient that drives thermoelectric currents via the Seebeck effect. If the measured voltage difference between the base and top of the chimney is 4.8 V, what is the total current flowing through the chimney?

# HFDPO-13262

A city planning team is evaluating the noise impact of a proposed elevated rail line near a residential neighborhood. The trains emit a continuous 800 Hz tone at a sound power level of 110 dB at 1 meter. The rail line runs 30 meters above ground, and the nearest homes are 50 meters horizontally from the track. A new noise barrier, 4 meters high, is proposed 10 meters from the track on the side facing the homes. Calculate the sound pressure level at a house located 50 meters from the track and 4 meters above ground, considering both direct transmission and diffraction around the barrier. Use the Rayleigh integral approximation for diffraction loss.

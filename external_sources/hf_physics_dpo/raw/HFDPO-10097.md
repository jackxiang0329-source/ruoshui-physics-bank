# HFDPO-10097

A solar-powered Mars rover’s dust shield, made of a conductive polymer film, accumulates static charge during dust storms due to triboelectric charging with airborne silica particles. The shield is a 2.0 m × 1.5 m rectangular plate with a non-uniform charge density σ(x) = σ₀(1 + 0.5 sin(πx/L)), where L = 2.0 m and σ₀ = 3.0 × 10⁻⁹ C/m². A nearby sensor, sensitive to electric fields, must remain below 100 N/C. Calculate the electric field at a point 0.5 m directly above the center of the shield.

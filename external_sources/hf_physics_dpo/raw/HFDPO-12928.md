# HFDPO-12928

A lunar rover on a mission to explore the Shackleton Crater’s rim encounters a sudden sandstorm that deposits a 2.3-meter-long, 150-kg debris beam across its path, straining against the rover’s rear suspension. Engineers estimate the system’s rotational inertia is 310 kg·m² about the pivot.

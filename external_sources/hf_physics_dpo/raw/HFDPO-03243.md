# HFDPO-03243

A coastal engineering team is designing a new breakwater to reduce wave erosion at a vulnerable beach. They measure incoming surface waves with a wavelength of 18 m and a speed of 6.0 m/s. After construction, wave height near the shore drops by 40%, but the team observes a standing wave pattern forming along the breakwater’s edge. Using wave interference principles and the measured wave parameters, determine the minimum distance from the breakwater where a node (point of no motion) would form due to interference between incident and reflected waves.

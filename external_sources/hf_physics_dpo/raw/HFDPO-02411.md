# HFDPO-02411

A research team designing a radiation-shielded data center discovers that stray electric fields from nearby high-voltage power lines are interfering with sensitive superconducting circuits. Using Gauss’s Law, they analyze a spherical protective enclosure made of conductive mesh (radius 3.0 m) surrounding the core electronics.

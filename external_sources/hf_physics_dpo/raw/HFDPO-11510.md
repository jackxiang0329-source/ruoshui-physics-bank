# HFDPO-11510

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses glass panels coated with a thin-film anti-reflective layer to maximize energy capture. On clear afternoons, glare reflections from the panels interfere with nearby wildlife monitoring cameras, creating flickering hotspots. The engineers measure the reflected light’s intensity peak at 32° from the vertical when sunlight (s-polarized at 45° incidence) hits the panel. They need to determine the refractive index of the coating to minimize reflection at this angle.

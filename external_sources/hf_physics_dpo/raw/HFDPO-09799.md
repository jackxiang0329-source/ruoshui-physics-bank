# HFDPO-09799

A small hydroelectric microgrid in a remote village uses a 60 Hz AC generator to power homes and a single-phase induction motor for a water pump. The motor draws 12 A with a lagging power factor of 0.75. To improve efficiency and reduce line losses, the village engineer installs a capacitor bank in parallel with the motor. What capacitance (in μF) is needed to correct the power factor to 0.95 lagging? Assume the voltage is 240 V RMS.

# HFDPO-09269

A wind farm turbine in a coastal region experiences increasing blade fatigue despite operating below rated wind speeds. Engineers discover that dynamic yaw misalignment causes uneven loading, but the rotor’s equilibrium is still maintained statically. Given a 60-meter diameter rotor with blades of uniform mass distribution (15,000 kg each), and a 2° misalignment between the rotor plane and the wind vector, calculate the net horizontal force on the turbine tower’s base due to blade weight imbalance alone. Assume the rotor is fully horizontal, the tower is rigid, and gravity acts vertically.

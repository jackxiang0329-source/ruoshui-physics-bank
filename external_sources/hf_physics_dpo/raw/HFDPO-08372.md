# HFDPO-08372

A solar observatory uses a tunable liquid crystal polarimeter to study the magnetic field structure of the Sun’s corona by analyzing the polarization of sunlight scattered by charged particles. The instrument measures Stokes parameters I, Q, U, V across a 10 nm bandwidth centered at 589 nm, but due to imperfect calibration, a residual phase delay of 0.15π radians exists between the fast and slow axes of the analyzer. At a particular solar location, the measured Stokes vector is (I=100, Q=25, U=−15, V=5) in arbitrary units. What is the true degree of polarization (DoP) and the orientation of the linear polarization, corrected for the instrumental phase error?

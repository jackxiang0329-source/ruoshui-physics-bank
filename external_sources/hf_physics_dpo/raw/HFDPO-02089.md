# HFDPO-02089

A wind turbine in a remote mountain valley generates electricity during strong gusts, but its output fluctuates wildly. The turbine’s 120-turn rotor coil, with area 0.8 m², spins at 15 rad/s in a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T. However, during a sudden wind shift, the field direction tilts 30° relative to the coil’s normal. What is the peak induced EMF before and after the tilt, and why does the turbine’s power output drop significantly despite constant rotational speed?

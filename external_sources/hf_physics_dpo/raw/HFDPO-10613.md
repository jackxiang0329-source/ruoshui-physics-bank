# HFDPO-10613

A solar farm in a remote desert uses flexible, thin-film photovoltaic panels embedded in a conductive mesh to transmit power over 5 km to a central inverter. The mesh uses copper traces with a resistivity of 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, 100 μm thickness, and width of 2.5 mm. During peak sunlight, each panel outputs 300 W at 48 V. However, voltage drop across the transmission network reduces the effective power available. If the total current drawn is 12.5 A, what is the minimum width of the copper trace needed to limit power loss to less than 5% of total output, assuming uniform current distribution?

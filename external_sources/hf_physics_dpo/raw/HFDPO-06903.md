# HFDPO-06903

In a microfluidic organ-on-a-chip device simulating pulmonary capillaries, red blood cells (RBCs) navigate a 50 µm wide channel with a 0.3 mm length, flowing at 1.2 mm/s under a pressure gradient of 800 Pa/m. The plasma has a viscosity of 1.3 mPa·s and the RBCs exhibit non-Newtonian behavior due to aggregation near walls. Design a model to predict the effective viscosity of the RBC-plasma suspension at this flow regime, considering shear-thinning and cell-free layers. How does this effective viscosity alter the pressure drop compared to a Newtonian fluid with the same nominal viscosity?

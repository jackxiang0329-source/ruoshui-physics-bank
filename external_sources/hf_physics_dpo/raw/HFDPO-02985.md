# HFDPO-02985

A deep-sea research team deploys a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) equipped with a wide-angle lens camera to survey a hydrothermal vent field. Due to extreme pressure, the lens casing is made of a material with a refractive index of 1.65, and the camera’s sensor is located 4.2 cm behind the lens’s rear surface. The lens is biconvex, with front and back radii of curvature of 6.0 cm and 3.5 cm, respectively. Light from a bioluminescent organism 80 cm in front of the lens’s front surface enters the ocean (n = 1.34) and then passes through the lens. What is the effective focal length of the lens *in seawater*, and where does the image of the organism form relative to the sensor? (Assume the lens is thin and the air-seawater interface at the front is negligible.)

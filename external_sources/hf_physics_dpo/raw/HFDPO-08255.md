# HFDPO-08255

During a solar eclipse, a team of engineers monitors a remote weather station powered by a hybrid solar-battery system. The station's 12V solar array feeds into a parallel circuit with two batteries: a 12V lead-acid (60 Ah) and a lithium-ion (12V, 40 Ah). When sunlight is blocked, the system switches to dual-battery discharge through a 3Ω load. If the lead-acid battery has an internal resistance of 0.2Ω and the lithium-ion 0.1Ω, what is the current through the load, and which battery delivers more power during the eclipse?

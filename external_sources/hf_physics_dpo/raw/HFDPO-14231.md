# HFDPO-14231

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanoparticles to absorb sunlight, but efficiency drops sharply when the dots are too close together due to electron tunneling between them. If the dots are spaced 2.5 nm apart and the barrier height between them is 1.2 eV, what is the probability that an electron with energy 0.8 eV tunnels through the barrier? Assume the barrier is rectangular and use the WKB approximation.

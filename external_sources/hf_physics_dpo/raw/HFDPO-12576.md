# HFDPO-12576

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V battery bank charges through a solar array via a charge controller that maintains voltage regulation. The system uses two parallel 10 km copper power lines (diameter 4 mm) to deliver current to a remote sensor array. During a cloudy day, the solar input drops to 80 W, but the sensors require 60 W at 12 V. The charge controller’s efficiency is 92%. What is the minimum current the solar array must supply, and what fraction of the solar power is lost as heat in the transmission lines? Assume resistivity of copper = 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m.

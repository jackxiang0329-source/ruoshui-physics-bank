# HFDPO-01887

A fusion reactor prototype uses a ring-shaped superconducting magnet cooled to 4.2 K, maintaining a persistent current of 120 kA to confine plasma. During a sudden power surge, the magnet's quench protection system fails, causing a localized hotspot that raises the temperature to 8.5 K in a 1.3 cm section. The superconductor (Nb₃Sn) has a critical field of 25 T and a critical temperature of 18 K. Calculate the minimum current density that would trigger a full quench in the hotspot, considering the non-uniform magnetic field profile and the kinetic inductance of the coil.

# HFDPO-11012

A coastal wind farm is experiencing inconsistent turbine performance during certain sea breeze events. Measurements show that on days with a 12 km/h offshore wind, the inflow to the turbines is often accelerated by 25% due to a funneling effect through a narrow coastal valley. However, during peak afternoon hours, the turbines stall unexpectedly. Assume incompressible flow and negligible friction.

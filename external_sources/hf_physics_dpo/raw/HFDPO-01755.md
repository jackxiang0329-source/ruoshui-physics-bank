# HFDPO-01755

A solar farm in the desert uses photovoltaic panels mounted on trackers that rotate to follow the sun. Engineers notice that output drops significantly during midday when glare from the sand reflects off the panel surfaces. The panels are coated with a polarizing filter to reduce glare, but the filter is only 75% effective at blocking horizontally polarized light. If sunlight is incident at 59° from the horizontal (the Brewster angle for sand), and unpolarized sunlight delivers 1000 W/m², what is the effective irradiance (in W/m²) reaching the solar cells after the filter?

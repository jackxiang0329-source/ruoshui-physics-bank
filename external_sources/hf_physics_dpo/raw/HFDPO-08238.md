# HFDPO-08238

A solar observatory in the Atacama Desert uses a custom-designed polarimeter to study magnetic fields in the solar corona by analyzing the polarization of sunlight scattered by coronal electrons. The instrument detects a degree of linear polarization of 0.37% at 500 nm, but the signal is corrupted by scattered skylight from the surrounding dust. The ground-based polarization from atmospheric scattering is 0.82% at the same wavelength and is partially polarized with a preferred orientation at 45° to the solar meridian. The instrument's analyzer is aligned at 30°. Determine the effective polarization measurement that would be recorded by the sensor, and evaluate whether this observed signal could be confused with intrinsic coronal polarization.

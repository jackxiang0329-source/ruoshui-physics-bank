# HFDPO-00210

A wind turbine in a coastal region experiences erratic power output despite steady wind speeds. Engineers discover that the blades are designed with a symmetric airfoil, yet under real-world conditions, the lift coefficients vary significantly between the upwind and downwind sides. The blade tips reach 60 m/s relative to the wind, and the local air density is 1.2 kg/m³. At a critical wind speed of 12 m/s, the pressure difference between the upper and lower surfaces of a blade section drops by 18% unexpectedly. What is the minimum angle of attack adjustment needed to restore the intended pressure difference, assuming Bernoulli’s principle governs lift and the velocity ratio across the airfoil is governed by the blade’s geometry?

# HFDPO-15970

Estimate the average kinetic energy transfer per collision and analyze whether the dominant energy transfer mechanism is thermal conduction or direct momentum transfer, given the surface’s electron thermal conductivity (180 W/m·K) and the Debye temperature of tungsten (410 K).

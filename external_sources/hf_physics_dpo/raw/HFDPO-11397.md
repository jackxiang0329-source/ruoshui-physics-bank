# HFDPO-11397

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, biocompatible stent for a patient with atherosclerotic narrowing in a coronary artery. The stent’s wall thickness is 0.12 mm and is modeled as a thin-walled cylindrical shell. Due to the high strain in the vessel wall, the engineer must determine the maximum internal pressure required to achieve deployment while ensuring that the stent remains within its elastic limit, avoiding permanent deformation. The vessel’s compliance adds complexity: the artery expands by 0.2 mm for every 10 kPa of pressure increase. How much pressure is needed to expand the stent fully, and will the vessel’s elasticity affect the required deployment pressure?

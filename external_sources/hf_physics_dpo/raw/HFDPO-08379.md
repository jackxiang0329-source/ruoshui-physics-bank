# HFDPO-08379

A deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent at 2,300 meters below the ocean surface, where pressure is extreme and temperatures exceed 350°C. The drone's internal pressure vessel is sealed and contains a small, rigid reservoir of cooling fluid (density = 940 kg/m³) that must remain at a constant internal pressure to prevent mechanical failure. At the surface, the reservoir is filled with fluid at atmospheric pressure (101.3 kPa). Calculate the pressure difference the vessel must withstand at depth, assuming the fluid’s density remains constant (despite temperature and pressure), and determine the height of a mercury column (ρ = 13,500 kg/m³) that would exert the same pressure difference.

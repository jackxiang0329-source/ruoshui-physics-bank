# HFDPO-10308

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of +8.5 nC/m² is suspended by electrostatic forces between two parallel plates 3.0 mm apart. During a critical deposition step, a 45 μm-diameter dust particle with a charge of -1.2 pC drifts into the gap. If the particle lands on the wafer, it causes a permanent defect. What is the minimum initial distance from the wafer at which the particle must be released (from rest) to avoid contact, assuming no other forces act?

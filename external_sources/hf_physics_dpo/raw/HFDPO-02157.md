# HFDPO-02157

A lunar habitat module, shaped like a truncated cone (base radius 8 m, top radius 5 m, height 6 m), is anchored to the regolith using six tension cables attached at the rim of the top opening. Each cable passes over a pulley on a gantry 10 m above the module’s apex and is anchored to a counterweight on a slope with a coefficient of static friction of 0.45. During a solar storm, dust accumulation increases the effective weight by 12%, and wind gusts exert a horizontal force of 25 kN at the center of mass, which is 3.2 m above the base. Determine the minimum counterweight mass required to prevent tipping, accounting for friction and cable angle variability due to lunar gravity (1.62 m/s²).

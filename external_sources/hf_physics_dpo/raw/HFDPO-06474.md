# HFDPO-06474

A small offshore wind farm uses a combined heat and power (CHP) system to generate electricity and provide thermal energy for a research station. The system operates as a Rankine cycle with a turbine inlet at 400°C and 5 MPa, condensing at 40°C. During a storm, the condenser cooling water flow drops by 30%, causing the condenser pressure to rise. The plant’s efficiency drops from 32% to 26%. Calculate the new condenser pressure and explain why the efficiency decreased more than expected despite only a 30% reduction in cooling flow.

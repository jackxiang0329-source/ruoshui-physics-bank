# HFDPO-16267

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical sensor network relies on a 12 V solar-charged battery system with a 2.0 Ω internal resistance. During a prolonged polar night, the batteries are drained and must be recharged via a 1.5 kW wind turbine. The turbine's output voltage varies nonlinearly with wind speed, modeled as V_t = 0.02v² + 10, where v is wind speed in m/s. The recharging circuit includes a current-limiting resistor (R_L) and a diode to prevent reverse current. If the system must deliver 1.2 A to the batteries without exceeding 13.5 V at the battery terminals, what value of R_L minimizes power loss in the resistor while staying safe?

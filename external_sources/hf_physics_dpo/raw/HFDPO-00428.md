# HFDPO-00428

A deep-space probe equipped with a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) using plutonium-238 begins losing power unexpectedly after 15 years of operation. The initial power output was 250 W, but measurements show it's now only 195 W. Scientists suspect decay heat loss is not the only factor—radiation damage to thermocouples is degrading efficiency. Given that Pu-238 has a half-life of 87.7 years and the RTG’s efficiency was originally 6.5%, what fraction of the current power loss is due to radioactive decay versus radiation-induced material degradation? Assume the degradation rate follows a logarithmic decline: η(t) = η₀ / (1 + kt), where k = 0.01 yr⁻¹.

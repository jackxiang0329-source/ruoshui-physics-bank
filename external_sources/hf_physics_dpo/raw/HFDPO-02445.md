# HFDPO-02445

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a complex toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma, but stray electric fields from charged particle drifts threaten to destabilize the plasma edge. Engineers measure a non-uniform radial electric field near the vessel wall, modeled as E(r) = frac{A}{r} + Br for R_1 leq r leq R_2 , where A = 1.2 times 10^4~text{V·m} , B = 3.0 times 10^2~text{V/m}^2 , R_1 = 0.8~text{m} , R_2 = 1.2~text{m} . They suspect a net charge density is causing this field. Using Gauss’s Law, determine whether the total charge enclosed within a cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius r = 1.0~text{m} and length L = 2.0~text{m} is consistent with experimental data showing Phi_E = 5.3 times 10^5~text{N·m}^2/text{C} .

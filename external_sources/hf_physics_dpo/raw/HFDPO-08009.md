# HFDPO-08009

A fusion energy researcher must design a magnetic confinement system for plasma in a tokamak that sustains 100 million Kelvin temperatures. To compress the plasma to fusion conditions, superconducting coils apply a force that increases from 50 kN to 180 kN over a 2.5 m displacement. The plasma’s internal energy rises by 2.3 MJ due to compression. What fraction of the work done by the magnetic field is converted to plasma thermal energy, and what accounts for the remainder? Assume no heat loss through radiation initially.

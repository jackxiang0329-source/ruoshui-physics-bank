# HFDPO-00117

A city installs a new wireless charging system for electric buses at bus stops. The system uses a time-varying magnetic field generated by a coil under the pavement to induce current in a receiver coil on the bus. If the magnetic flux through the receiver coil changes at a rate of 0.05 Wb/s and the coil has 100 turns, what is the induced electromotive force (EMF) in the bus’s receiver coil?

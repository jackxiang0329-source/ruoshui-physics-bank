# HFDPO-13989

In a dusty desert environment, a solar panel array operating at 750 V accumulates static charge due to wind-blown sand particles (average diameter 120 μm, density 2.6 g/cm³) impacting its surface at 25 m/s. The panel’s surface has a work function of 4.8 eV and is coated with a dielectric layer (κ = 6.5, thickness 0.5 μm). After 15 minutes of operation under dry, low-humidity conditions, the voltage measured between the panel and ground increases by 140 V. Determine the net charge density (in μC/m²) on the panel’s surface and explain whether charge accumulation is driven predominantly by electron transfer or ion adhesion from the sand.

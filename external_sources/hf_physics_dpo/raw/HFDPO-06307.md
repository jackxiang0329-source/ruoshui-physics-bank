# HFDPO-06307

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat = 1600 J/kg·K, density = 1800 kg/m³) stored in a cylindrical tank (diameter = 5 m, height = 10 m) to generate electricity during nighttime. The salt is heated to 560°C during the day and must maintain an average temperature above 500°C for at least 8 hours after sunset. However, the tank loses heat through convection (h = 12 W/m²·K) and radiation (emissivity = 0.92, ambient temp = 20°C). If the tank is insulated with a 15 cm thick layer of aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K), determine whether the salt will stay above 500°C after 8 hours. Assume the tank is perfectly mixed and heat transfer is steady-state at the surface.

# HFDPO-10378

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole (depth 2.5 km) with a conductive fluid column (conductivity σ = 0.1 S/m, density ρ = 1200 kg/m³) that rises due to thermal buoyancy and falls under gravity, flowing through a 50 cm-radius cylindrical cavity aligned with Earth's magnetic field (B₀ = 50 μT, horizontal component). The fluid's velocity varies sinusoidally with time due to thermal oscillations: v(t) = v₀ sin(ωt), where v₀ = 0.3 m/s and ω = 0.1 rad/s. Calculate the time-averaged power dissipated as Joule heating in the fluid due to induced eddy currents. Assume the fluid acts as a single conducting loop with resistance R, and use Faraday’s law to model the EMF.

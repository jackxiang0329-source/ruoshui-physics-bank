# HFDPO-02900

A satellite constellation designed for global high-frequency trading relay must maintain precise timing alignment across four identical orbiters in near-circular, sun-synchronous orbits at 650 km altitude. Due to a recent software glitch, one satellite’s orbit was perturbed by a 2.3 m/s delta-v impulse at apogee, altering its eccentricity. The other three remain on nominal orbits. Calculate the minimum time interval over which the network must reconfigure its synchronization protocol to maintain 100-millisecond timing integrity, assuming the perturbed satellite’s orbit precesses faster than the others due to J₂ effects.

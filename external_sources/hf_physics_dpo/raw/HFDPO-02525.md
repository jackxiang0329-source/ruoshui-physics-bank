# HFDPO-02525

A marine biologist is studying how underwater volcanic activity affects local cetacean communication. During a hydrophone test, a 150 Hz sound pulse from a distant eruption travels through seawater (bulk modulus 2.3 GPa, density 1025 kg/m³) and reaches a pod of dolphins 4.2 km away. The signal is attenuated by 12 dB due to absorption and scattering. If the dolphins rely on detecting signals above 80 dB (re 1 μPa) for navigation, what was the original source intensity (in W/m²) of the pulse at 1 meter from the eruption?

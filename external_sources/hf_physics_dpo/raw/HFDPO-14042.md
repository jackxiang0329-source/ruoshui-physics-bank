# HFDPO-14042

A concert hall designer in Helsinki is optimizing the acoustics for a new performance space intended to host both orchestral music and spoken word. The hall has a volume of 12,000 m³ and a target reverberation time of 1.8 seconds at 500 Hz, but early measurements show the space is too "dead" — reverberation is only 1.4 s. The architect proposes adding acoustic panels with a known absorption coefficient of 0.75 at 500 Hz. However, the panels are expensive and heavy; installing more than 400 m² may risk structural instability. Determine whether the desired reverberation time can be achieved within the structural constraint, and evaluate the trade-offs between panel placement (distributed vs. focused in reflective zones) in terms of sound diffusion and clarity.

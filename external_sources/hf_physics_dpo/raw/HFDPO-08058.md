# HFDPO-08058

A Mars rover navigates a 500-meter-long sandy dune with a variable incline, where the coefficient of kinetic friction varies with slope angle due to granular compaction. At the crest, it deploys a 30° ramp for a sample collection platform and must traverse 15 m down to the base. If the rover's motor provides a constant acceleration of 0.2 m/s² down the ramp while friction increases from 0.15 at 10° to 0.35 at 30°, what is the minimum time to descend without exceeding 1.5 m/s at the bottom, ensuring sample integrity?

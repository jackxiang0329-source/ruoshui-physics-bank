# HFDPO-06027

A quantum dot solar cell uses a 2D array of sub-100 nm semiconductor nanoparticles arranged in a triangular lattice to enhance light absorption. Each dot acts as a diffraction grating element, and the array exhibits strong interference effects due to coherent coupling. When illuminated with 600 nm monochromatic light at normal incidence, the first-order diffraction peak (m = 1) is observed at 15° from the central maximum. However, the measured efficiency drops sharply beyond this angle due to destructive interference from neighboring grating orders. Determine the required lattice spacing and assess whether the array can simultaneously enhance absorption at both 600 nm and 500 nm for improved broadband response.

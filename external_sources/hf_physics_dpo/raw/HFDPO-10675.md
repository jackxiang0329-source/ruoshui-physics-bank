# HFDPO-10675

A private space company is designing a mission to deliver a scientific payload to a retrograde orbit around Mars’ moon Phobos, aiming to study surface outgassing. The launch window opens in 18 months, but a sudden solar storm alters Mars’ magnetic field, increasing the plasma drag on low-altitude trajectories. The team must determine the optimal burn sequence to achieve the target orbit using only two maneuvers: one at the initial transfer point (Mars orbit insertion), and one at apoapsis of the transfer ellipse. What is the minimum total Δv required, and how should the second burn be phased relative to Phobos’ orbital motion to exploit gravitational perturbations?

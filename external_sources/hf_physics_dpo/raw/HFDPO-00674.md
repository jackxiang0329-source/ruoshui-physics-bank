# HFDPO-00674

In a next-generation quantum spintronics device, arrays of 128 nanoscale magnetic islands (each with spin-½ moments) are arranged in a 2D lattice, operating near 10 mK. Due to edge disorder, 16 of the islands are coupled to phonon baths at 30 mK, while the remaining 112 are isolated. The system reaches a non-equilibrium steady state where the average spin polarization fluctuates stochastically. What is the effective temperature of the entire ensemble as measured by the average energy per spin, and why does this differ from the nominal bath temperatures?

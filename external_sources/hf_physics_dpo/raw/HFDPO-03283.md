# HFDPO-03283

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a precision microelectromechanical system (MEMS) accelerometer is failing intermittently due to electrostatic stiction—tiny movable plates stick to fixed electrodes when voltage is applied. The device uses a 500 nm thick, 10 μm diameter circular electrode suspended 100 nm above a grounded substrate. When a 15 V bias is applied, the electrostatic force pulls the plate down until it contacts the substrate. However, simulations show an unexpected force enhancement of 37% compared to the standard parallel-plate model. Investigate the likely cause and calculate the actual force, considering fringing fields and edge effects.

# HFDPO-03033

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device, a biopolymer-laden solution (viscosity 5×10⁻³ Pa·s, density 1050 kg/m³) flows through a narrow, 200 μm diameter circular channel at 1.2 mL/min. Suddenly, a constricting defect—100 μm in diameter and 1 mm long—forms downstream. Assuming laminar flow and no slip, determine whether the pressure drop across the defect exceeds the device’s maximum tolerable ΔP = 15 kPa. If so, what is the minimum radius of a buffer zone (in the form of a tapered transition) needed to keep the pressure drop within limits without increasing pump power?

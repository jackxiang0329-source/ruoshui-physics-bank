# HFDPO-10079

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass spins at 25 rpm when the wind speed reaches 12 m/s, but during a storm, gusts increase to 24 m/s. The turbine’s blades have a moment of inertia of 3.2 × 10⁴ kg·m² and are designed to protect the generator by feathering (rotating blades to reduce drag) when angular speed exceeds 40 rpm. If the torque from wind is proportional to the square of wind speed, at what wind speed will the turbine automatically feather?

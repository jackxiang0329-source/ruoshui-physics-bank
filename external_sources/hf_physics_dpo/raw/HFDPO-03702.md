# HFDPO-03702

A bioengineer is designing a wireless power transfer system to charge implantable medical devices using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil has an inductance of 200 μH and resistance of 5 Ω, while the receiver coil has 180 μH and 4 Ω. They operate at 6.78 MHz, a frequency chosen to comply with industrial standards. Due to material constraints, they must use a single capacitor in series with each coil to tune to resonance. What capacitance should be used in each coil to achieve resonance, and what is the total impedance seen by the driver at resonance?

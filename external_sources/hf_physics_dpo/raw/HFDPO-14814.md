# HFDPO-14814

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical brine well (depth 2.1 km) to extract hot water from underground reservoirs. The brine flows upward through a pipe with a diameter of 0.35 m, emerging at 90°C with a flow rate of 120 kg/s. Due to friction and heat loss, only 70% of the total mechanical energy available from the pressure and elevation drop is converted to electrical energy. If the wellhead pressure is 1.8 MPa and the surface pressure is atmospheric (0.1 MPa), and assuming the brine density is 1020 kg/m³, what is the maximum theoretical efficiency of energy extraction, and why might the actual efficiency be lower than this value?

# HFDPO-09907

During calibration, engineers notice anomalous signal fluctuations in the 10.6 μm thermal infrared band, correlating with solar angle and wind direction—despite the detector being theoretically insensitive to polarization at this wavelength. Given that the instrument's exit window is a 2 mm thick calcium fluoride (CaF₂) plate with a refractive index of 1.43 at 10.6 μm, and the incident radiation is partially polarized (s-component 60%, p-component 40%) at an angle of 45° to the normal, determine the actual polarization state of the beam after transmission and explain the source of the observed signal variation.

# HFDPO-08226

A solar-powered garden light uses a small silicon photodiode to detect dusk and automatically turn on an LED. During daylight, the photodiode generates a current of 0.8 mA under 100 W/m² sunlight. At dusk, with only 10 W/m² ambient light, the current drops sharply. If the circuit activates the LED only when the photodiode current falls below 0.1 mA, how much does the light intensity need to drop for the light to turn on?

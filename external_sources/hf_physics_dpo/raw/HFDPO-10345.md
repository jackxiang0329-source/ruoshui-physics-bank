# HFDPO-10345

A deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent at 2,800 meters below the ocean surface. The vent emits superheated fluid at 350°C, which mixes with cold seawater (4°C) and forms a buoyant plume. The plume has a density of 1,015 kg/m³, while the surrounding seawater averages 1,027 kg/m³. The drone must maintain a stable hover 50 meters above the vent. Calculate the net upward force per unit volume on the plume, and explain how this affects the drone's power requirements for station-keeping.

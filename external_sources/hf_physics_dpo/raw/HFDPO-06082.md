# HFDPO-06082

A Mars rover's front wheel, designed with a micro-textured rubber tread, begins to slip during a sand dune ascent despite optimal torque. The 180 kg rover exerts 320 N of normal force on the front wheel, but data shows the wheel rotates without significant forward translation. Given the sand has a dynamic friction coefficient of 0.42 and the wheel experiences aerodynamic drag at 120 km/h equivalent (in Mars’ thin atmosphere), determine the minimum torque required to initiate motion and explain why the observed slip occurs despite adequate torque input.

# HFDPO-00373

The sub’s hatch is a 0.8 m diameter circular plate, mounted vertically with its top edge 1200 m below the surface. Calculate the net hydrostatic force on the hatch and determine the pressure at its centroid—why does this not equal the pressure at the center of pressure?

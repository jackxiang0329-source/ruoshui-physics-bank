# HFDPO-06471

A solar-powered satellite in low Earth orbit uses a rotating flywheel to stabilize its orientation. During a maneuver, the satellite fires small thrusters, causing the flywheel to spin faster, increasing its angular speed from 500 rpm to 700 rpm. If the flywheel has a moment of inertia of 12 kg·m² and the satellite’s total angular momentum must remain constant, what is the change in rotational speed of the satellite’s body (assuming it behaves as a rigid body with moment of inertia 250 kg·m²)?

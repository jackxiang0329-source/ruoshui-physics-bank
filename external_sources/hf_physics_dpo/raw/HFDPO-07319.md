# HFDPO-07319

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses perovskite-silicon tandem cells with a bandgap of 1.1 eV for the silicon subcell and 1.55 eV for the perovskite top cell. During peak irradiance (1000 W/m²), the top cell absorbs 42% of incident sunlight but suffers from non-radiative recombination due to grain boundary defects—measured by a lifetime of 12 ns. To maintain efficiency, engineers must balance carrier extraction speed against defect density. What is the minimum carrier diffusion length required in the perovskite layer to ensure 90% of photogenerated carriers are collected before recombining, assuming a carrier mobility of 25 cm²/V·s and a built-in electric field of 1.2 × 10⁵ V/m?

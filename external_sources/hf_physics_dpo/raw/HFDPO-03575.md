# HFDPO-03575

A drone racing team is testing a new autonomous navigation system in a dense urban canyon environment where GPS signals are unreliable. The drone must navigate a narrow 120-meter-long alley between buildings, starting from rest and accelerating at 3.2 m/s² until it reaches a maximum speed of 22 m/s, then maintaining that speed before decelerating at 4.0 m/s² to stop exactly at the far end. Due to wind gusts, the actual acceleration varies by ±0.3 m/s² and deceleration by ±0.5 m/s². What is the minimum safe distance the drone must maintain from obstacles during the acceleration phase to ensure it can stop safely under worst-case deceleration, assuming the drone must complete the alley in under 6.5 seconds?

# HFDPO-16221

A biomedical engineer is designing a fiber-optic sensor to detect early-stage cancer by measuring subtle changes in tissue refractive index. The sensor uses a 1.55 μm wavelength laser launched into a silica fiber (n = 1.44) with a core diameter of 8 μm. When placed near tumor tissue (refractive index ≈ 1.38), the evanescent field extends 200 nm beyond the fiber surface. To maximize sensitivity, the engineer must determine how much light couples into the tissue if the angle of incidence at the fiber-tissue interface is 75°. What fraction of the total power is transferred to the tissue?

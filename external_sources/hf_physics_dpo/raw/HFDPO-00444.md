# HFDPO-00444

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 12 cm diameter silicon wafer initially carries a uniform surface charge density of −3.2 μC/m². As a plasma etching process begins, energetic ions from a reactive gas discharge (50 eV kinetic energy) bombard the wafer surface, depositing positive charge at a rate of 4.8 × 10¹³ ions/cm²/s. Simultaneously, electrons from the plasma are attracted to the wafer, neutralizing charge at a rate proportional to the electric field at the surface. After 6.5 seconds, the net charge density stabilizes at −1.1 μC/m². What is the electron flux (electrons/cm²/s) at stabilization, assuming the wafer remains perfectly conducting and the plasma sheath adjusts dynamically?

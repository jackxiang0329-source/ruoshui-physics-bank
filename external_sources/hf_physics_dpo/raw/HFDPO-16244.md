# HFDPO-16244

A student designing a touch-sensitive lamp for a school project uses a single metal plate as a sensor, connected to a 5 V battery via a resistor. When their finger approaches (without touching), the capacitance between the plate and ground increases from 2 pF to 8 pF. How does this change affect the voltage across the sensor plate if the circuit is in a steady state before and after the finger's presence?

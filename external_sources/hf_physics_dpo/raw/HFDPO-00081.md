# HFDPO-00081

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable heart monitor that uses capacitive sensors to detect subtle chest movements during respiration. The sensor consists of two parallel plates, each 2 cm × 3 cm, separated by a flexible dielectric (ε_r = 6.0) that deforms when the chest expands. During inhalation, the plate separation decreases from 0.5 mm to 0.3 mm. If the sensor is initially charged to 5 V and the circuit is isolated, what is the new voltage across the plates after inhalation? How much energy is stored in the system before and after, and where does the difference come from?

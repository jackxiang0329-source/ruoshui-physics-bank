# HFDPO-03626

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarizing filter array to reduce glare from sunlight reflecting off ocean surfaces, but during a solar storm, the Sun’s polarized emissions vary unpredictably. The filter system, designed for circular polarization, must now distinguish between the Sun’s coherent polarized radiation and scattered skyglow. Given that the Sun emits unpolarized light at 10⁴ W/m² and 60% of its emission becomes circularly polarized at 550 nm during the storm, while the skyglow is partially polarized (degree of polarization = 0.4) at the same wavelength, what is the optimal angle of a quarter-wave plate relative to the incoming solar beam to maximize signal detection of the polarized solar component? Assume the detector responds only to vertically polarized light.

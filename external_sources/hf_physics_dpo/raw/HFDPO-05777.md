# HFDPO-05777

A fusion reactor's plasma confinement system uses a complex array of superconducting coils to generate a toroidal magnetic field. During a power surge, a sudden current spike in one of the outer coils induces eddy currents in the adjacent cryostat walls, which are made of high-conductivity aluminum. The changing magnetic flux through the wall creates a repulsive force that threatens to destabilize the coil's position. Given the coil's peak current rise rate of 500 kA/s, a loop area of 0.8 m² in the wall, and a mutual inductance of 2.4 × 10⁻⁶ H between coil and wall loop, what is the peak repulsive force if the wall experiences a current of 1200 A? Assume the magnetic field gradient at the wall is 0.3 T/m, and the system’s symmetry ensures axial force alignment.

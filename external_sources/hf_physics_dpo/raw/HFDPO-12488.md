# HFDPO-12488

A team designing a next-gen optical fiber sensor for early earthquake detection in mountainous regions discovers that surface imperfections on the fiber—caused by microfractures from tectonic stress—generate unexpected diffraction patterns when laser light is transmitted. Using a 633 nm He-Ne laser, they observe a central bright spot surrounded by a faint ring pattern on a detector 2.0 m away. The first minimum in intensity occurs at 1.8 mm from the center. Assuming the dominant scattering arises from periodic surface defects spaced 5.0 μm apart with a groove width of 2.0 μm, what is the effective refractive index of the fiber core if the observed diffraction pattern must be consistent with both single-slit and double-slit interference?

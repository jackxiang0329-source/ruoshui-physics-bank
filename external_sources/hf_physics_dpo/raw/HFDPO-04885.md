# HFDPO-04885

During a deep-sea hydrothermal vent expedition, scientists observe a sudden plume of supercritical fluid erupting from a fissure at 300°C and 300 atm. The fluid, initially rich in dissolved CO₂ and H₂O, rapidly expands and cools upon exiting into the surrounding 2°C ocean. If the vent emits 120 kg/s of fluid with a composition of 65% H₂O and 35% CO₂ by mass, what is the minimum cross-sectional area of the vent required to prevent premature phase separation into liquid and gas during expansion, assuming ideal mixing and adiabatic expansion?

# HFDPO-06722

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole lined with superconducting coils to harvest induced currents from natural convective heat flows in the Earth’s crust. The coil array, 1.2 km long with 500 turns per meter, is wound on a cylindrical core with 0.8 m radius. Due to thermal convection currents, the magnetic field at the core’s center varies sinusoidally with time, peaking at 6.5 × 10⁻⁴ T and oscillating at 0.5 Hz. A sudden volcanic tremor disrupts the flow, causing a sharp 30% increase in the rate of change of flux over 0.2 seconds. Calculate the average induced emf during this interval and evaluate whether the plant’s emergency surge protector, rated at 2.0 kV, will activate.

# HFDPO-07204

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid. The geothermal reservoir supplies water at 145°C, but due to declining thermal output, the plant now operates with a 20°C subcooling at the condenser. If the isobutane enters the turbine at 180°C and 5.2 MPa, and exits as a saturated vapor at 1.2 MPa, what is the maximum theoretical efficiency of this cycle, and how does it compare to the actual efficiency if the turbine is 87% efficient and the pump is 82% efficient? Assume the condenser operates at 45°C and use thermodynamic tables for isobutane.

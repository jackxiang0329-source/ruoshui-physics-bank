# HFDPO-12334

A research team is designing a self-deploying solar shade structure for a Mars rover habitat, consisting of a flexible, 30-meter-wide membrane tethered at four corners to rigid pylons. Due to Martian wind gusts (up to 35 m/s), the membrane sags unevenly, creating a non-symmetrical catenary profile. The team needs to determine the minimum tension at the anchor points to prevent membrane rupture, given that the membrane has a mass of 1.8 kg/m and must withstand a wind load modeled as a distributed force peaking at 420 N/m vertically upward at the center.

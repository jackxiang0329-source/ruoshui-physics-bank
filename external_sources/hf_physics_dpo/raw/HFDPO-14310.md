# HFDPO-14310

A wind farm in coastal Oregon faces efficiency losses during sudden wind gusts. Each turbine has a 15 m diameter rotor with 3 blades, rotating at 20 rpm under normal conditions. During a storm, a sudden 5 m/s gust increases the angular speed to 28 rpm over 3 seconds. The generator uses a coil with 120 turns and area 0.8 m², aligned perpendicular to a uniform 0.4 T magnetic field. Calculate the average induced emf in the coil during the gust, and explain why the power output might not scale linearly with rotational speed.

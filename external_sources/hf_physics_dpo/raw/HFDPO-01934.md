# HFDPO-01934

A fusion reactor’s cryostat maintains its superconducting magnets at 4.2 K using a two-stage dilution refrigerator. During a power fluctuation, the primary cooling loop fails for 120 seconds, causing the bath temperature to rise to 6.8 K. After restoration, the system re-cools. The dilution refrigerator’s heat load during the transient is dominated by parasitic conduction through support structures (1.5 mW/K), and the mixing chamber contains 2.1 kg of ³He–⁴He mixture with a heat capacity of 0.92 J/(kg·K). Determine the minimum entropy change of the universe during the entire event, assuming the heat sink is at 300 K.

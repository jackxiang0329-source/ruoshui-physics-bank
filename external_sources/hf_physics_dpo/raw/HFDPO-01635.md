# HFDPO-01635

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 400°C, which then transfers heat to water in a thermal storage tank. One morning, the system operates with 1200 kg of oil at 380°C being poured into a 5000 kg concrete storage tank initially at 20°C. The oil’s specific heat is 1800 J/kg·K, and concrete’s is 880 J/kg·K. What is the final equilibrium temperature of the system? If the tank is insulated but loses heat at 500 W to the environment, how long will it take for the temperature to drop by 10°C after reaching equilibrium?

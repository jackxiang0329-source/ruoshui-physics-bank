# HFDPO-10050

A team of materials scientists is designing a new type of quantum dot solar cell using cadmium selenide (CdSe) nanoparticles. They discover that the efficiency peaks when the quantum dots are precisely 4.2 nm in diameter. However, during synthesis, the size distribution varies, and they need to predict how the energy band gap—critical for light absorption—will change if the particles grow to 5.0 nm.

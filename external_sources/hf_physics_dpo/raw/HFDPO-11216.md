# HFDPO-11216

A 6.0 kg robotic lander descends onto Mars using a retropropulsion system, firing thrusters at 1200 N for 5.0 seconds to reduce its speed from 35 m/s to 5.0 m/s. During this burn, the thruster ejects exhaust at 2800 m/s relative to the lander. The lander’s total mass decreases from 600 kg to 585 kg due to fuel consumption. What is the lander’s final velocity if the exhaust were instead ejected at an angle of 10° from the vertical (still relative to the lander)? Assume no external forces except gravity (3.7 m/s² on Mars), and that the thrust magnitude remains constant.

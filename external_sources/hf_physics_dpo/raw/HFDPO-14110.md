# HFDPO-14110

A fusion reactor design team is testing a compact tokamak configuration where superconducting coils generate a toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma. The inner radius of the torus is 1.2 m, and the outer radius is 2.0 m. The coils are wound as a single loop carrying 50 kA.

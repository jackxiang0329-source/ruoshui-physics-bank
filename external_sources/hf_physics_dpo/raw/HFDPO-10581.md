# HFDPO-10581

A quantum computing lab in Zurich uses superconducting qubits cooled to 15 mK, embedded in a microwave cavity tuned to 5.2 GHz. During calibration, a sudden spike in cavity photon count is observed, coinciding with a rapid magnetic field fluctuation measured by a SQUID magnetometer. The cavity’s dimensions are 2.4 mm × 2.4 mm × 10 mm. Determine whether the observed fluctuation arises from a violation of Maxwell’s equations or from a non-adiabatic coupling between the qubit’s dipole moment and the cavity’s time-varying electric field, given that the qubit’s dipole moment changes at 1.8×10¹² rad/s.

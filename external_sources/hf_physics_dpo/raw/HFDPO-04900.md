# HFDPO-04900

A wind turbine in a remote coastal village generates 2.4 MW of power during peak wind conditions, but the local grid can only handle 1.8 MW. Excess energy is diverted to charge a massive flywheel storage system spinning at 3,000 rpm. When wind drops, the flywheel slows down, releasing stored energy. If the flywheel has a moment of inertia of 5,200 kg·m², what is its angular speed when it has delivered 1.2 MJ of energy to the grid?

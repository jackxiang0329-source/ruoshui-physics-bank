# HFDPO-10370

In a Mars rover's power distribution system, three solar arrays feed into a central junction box with variable resistances due to temperature fluctuations. Array A delivers 12.8 V at 3.2 A, Array B supplies 13.1 V at 2.1 A, and a shared load draws 4.5 A. A fault causes a ground loop between the negative terminals of A and B, increasing internal resistance in Array B to 0.8 Ω. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current through the fault path and the effective voltage at the junction.

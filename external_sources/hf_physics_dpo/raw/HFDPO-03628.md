# HFDPO-03628

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km deep into hot rock, where a closed-loop pipe carries supercritical CO₂ as a heat transfer fluid. The fluid circulates at 1.8 m/s through a 25 cm diameter pipe, generating electrical power via a linear generator housed in a stationary stator. The system operates in a region with a horizontal magnetic field of 48 μT, tilted 65° below horizontal. Estimate the induced EMF per meter of pipe length and evaluate whether the design can achieve a net energy gain given that the generator’s conversion efficiency is 72% and the fluid’s kinetic energy flux is 4.3 MW per meter.

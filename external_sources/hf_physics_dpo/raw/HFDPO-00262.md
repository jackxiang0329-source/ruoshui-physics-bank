# HFDPO-00262

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must conserve energy during a 12-hour mission. It descends slowly from 15,000 m to 10,000 m using only aerodynamic lift and minimal motor use. If its mass is 8.2 kg and it loses 120 J of mechanical energy per minute due to air resistance, what is the minimum average lift force it must generate during descent to maintain a nearly constant speed? Assume negligible changes in kinetic energy and use conservation of mechanical energy with energy dissipation.

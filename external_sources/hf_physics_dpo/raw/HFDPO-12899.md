# HFDPO-12899

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole (diameter 0.25 m, depth 3.2 km) to extract heat from hot granite. The rock has a thermal conductivity of 2.8 W/m·K and volumetric heat capacity of 2.1 MJ/m³·K. After 15 years of continuous operation, the temperature at the borehole wall has dropped by 180°C. Estimate the average heat extraction rate (in MW) if the rock initially was at 420°C and the borehole wall temperature is maintained at 240°C. Assume one-dimensional radial conduction and negligible heat advection.

# HFDPO-04308

A coastal tidal power plant uses a vertical turbine array in a narrow strait where tidal currents reach 3.2 m/s at peak flow. Due to seasonal sedimentation, the channel’s cross-sectional area narrows by 12% over 5 years. Engineers observe a 15% drop in energy output despite consistent tidal forcing. Model the flow using the continuity and Bernoulli equations, assuming incompressible, inviscid flow, and calculate the new average velocity and pressure change across the turbine array. How much of the efficiency loss is attributable to increased velocity versus flow separation caused by the constriction?

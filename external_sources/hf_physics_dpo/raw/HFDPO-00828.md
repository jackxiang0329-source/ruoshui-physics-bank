# HFDPO-00828

In a fusion reactor prototype, a toroidal plasma must be confined by a magnetic field generated by external coils. The plasma, carrying a total current of 1.2 MA, forms a 1.5 m radius circular loop. To achieve stability, the magnetic field at the center must be precisely 12 T, but the existing coil design produces only 10.8 T. Engineers propose adjusting the coil current and repositioning the coil from 2.0 m to 1.8 m from the plasma center. What new current must the coil carry to achieve the target field, and will the change in position offset the needed increase?

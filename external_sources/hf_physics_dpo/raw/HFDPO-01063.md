# HFDPO-01063

A seismic survey team detects a mysterious low-frequency wave (5 Hz) traveling through a layered rock formation beneath a geothermal reservoir. The wave refracts at the boundary between a 200 m-thick shale layer (sound speed 2,800 m/s) and a deeper sandstone layer (sound speed 3,200 m/s). The wave enters the shale at a 35° angle from vertical. Engineers need to predict the exact depth at which the wave will emerge from the shale layer into the sandstone, accounting for both refraction and possible mode conversion at the interface. What is the depth of the shale-sandstone boundary, and what is the new angle of the refracted wave in the sandstone?

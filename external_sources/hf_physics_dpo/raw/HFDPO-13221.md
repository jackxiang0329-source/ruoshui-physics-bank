# HFDPO-13221

A deep-sea mining drone operates near a hydrothermal vent, harvesting metallic sulfides from a fissure where superheated water (350°C) emerges into cold abyssal water (2°C). The drone uses a thermoelectric generator powered by the temperature gradient to sustain operations. If the vent’s heat flux is 1.2 MW over a 0.5 m² interface and the generator’s maximum theoretical efficiency is limited by Carnot efficiency, but practical constraints reduce its actual efficiency to 45% of Carnot, what is the maximum sustainable electrical power output—and how would a 10% decrease in vent temperature due to crustal cooling impact this over time?

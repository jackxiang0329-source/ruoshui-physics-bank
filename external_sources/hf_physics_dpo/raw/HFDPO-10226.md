# HFDPO-10226

A team at a quantum computing lab is developing a fault-tolerant qubit array using a thin film of YBCO (a high-TC superconductor) patterned into a ring-shaped Josephson junction. During calibration, they observe a persistent current of 1.2 μA flowing in the ring without any external voltage—evidence of flux quantization. However, when they cool the system below Tc = 92 K, the current decays exponentially over 4.3 seconds. Given that the ring has an inductance of 3.5 nH and a resistance of 2.1 mΩ in the normal state, determine whether the decay is due to thermal fluctuations or quantum tunneling of vortex states, and explain how the system’s behavior changes if a magnetic field of 8.7 mT is applied perpendicularly.

# HFDPO-05052

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanostructure to enhance light absorption via diffraction and constructive interference. Engineers observe a sharp absorption peak at 620 nm, but the design predicted a peak at 580 nm. Determine whether the discrepancy is due to refractive index variation, grating order misidentification, or an overlooked phase shift from the hole depth, and justify your reasoning using wave interference and diffraction theory.

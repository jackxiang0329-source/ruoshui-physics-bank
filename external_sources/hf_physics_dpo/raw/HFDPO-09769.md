# HFDPO-09769

A research team in Antarctica monitors a geothermal borehole where superheated brine (65°C, 2.3 MPa) is extracted to generate electricity. The turbine operates in a closed Rankine cycle with a condenser pressure of 0.013 MPa. Assess whether the actual cycle efficiency exceeds the theoretical Carnot efficiency for the same temperature reservoirs, and explain any apparent contradiction in physical terms.

# HFDPO-01277

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice the drill’s rotational speed drops unexpectedly after penetrating a layer of compacted regolith. The drill, modeled as a solid cylinder of mass 4.2 kg and radius 0.12 m, spins at 2100 rpm before contact. After engaging the regolith, friction torque varies with depth as τ = –k·d, where d is depth in meters and k = 0.35 N·m/m. If the drill penetrates 0.08 m before stalling, what is the initial angular speed it must have had to overcome this torque and complete the penetration, assuming no other losses?

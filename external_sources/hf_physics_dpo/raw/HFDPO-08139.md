# HFDPO-08139

During a coastal storm, a marine biologist notices that a floating research buoy tethered to the seabed begins to drift erratically, even though wind speeds are moderate. The buoy’s tether is taut, and the water flow around it appears fast near its base, but the surface water seems calm. Assume the fluid is incompressible and ideal, and the water surface is at rest relative to the atmosphere.

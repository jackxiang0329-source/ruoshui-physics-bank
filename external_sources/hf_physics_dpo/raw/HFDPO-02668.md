# HFDPO-02668

A bioengineer is designing a synthetic vascular graft for small-diameter arteries (3 mm inner diameter) that must withstand cyclic blood pressure (120/80 mmHg) over 20 years. The graft’s polymer matrix has a nonlinear stress-strain response described by the Ogden model with material parameters μ₁ = 1.8 MPa, α₁ = 4, and λ₁ = 1.45. The wall thickness is 0.2 mm, and the graft is pressurized to 120 mmHg during systole. Calculate the maximum hoop stress in the wall and determine whether the material will experience irreversible deformation after 10⁸ cycles, considering fatigue-induced stiffening and residual stresses from the manufacturing process.

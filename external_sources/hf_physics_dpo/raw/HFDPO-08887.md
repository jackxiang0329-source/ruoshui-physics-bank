# HFDPO-08887

A solar-powered drone designed for environmental monitoring must maintain a steady altitude while conserving energy. It descends 10 meters in a gentle glide, losing 150 J of gravitational potential energy. If no energy is added by the motors and air resistance is negligible, how much kinetic energy does the drone gain? What happens to the energy if it instead uses a small electric motor to control its descent at constant speed?

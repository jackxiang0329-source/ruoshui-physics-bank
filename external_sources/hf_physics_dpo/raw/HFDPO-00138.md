# HFDPO-00138

During a Mars rover mission, a solar array panel develops a fault: one of its six interconnected photovoltaic cells short-circuits partially, reducing its output to 80% of nominal voltage while drawing anomalous current. The panel’s 5V nominal circuit includes three parallel branches with resistances of 300Ω, 450Ω, and 600Ω, each feeding a regulator. The short-circuited cell is in series with a 200Ω resistor and lies in the branch with the 450Ω resistor. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the real-time current through the 200Ω resistor if the total current from the solar array is measured at 22 mA and the voltage at the load junction is 4.7 V.

# HFDPO-11577

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 12 cm long, uniformly charged cylindrical rod (radius 0.5 mm, charge density +2.4 μC/m) is suspended horizontally. A nearby 3.0 cm diameter spherical electrode, initially uncharged, is brought to a fixed distance of 6.0 cm from the rod’s center. As the rod is slowly rotated 180° about its center, the sphere gains a net charge of 150 pC due to induced polarization and brief contact with the rod. Calculate the work done by the electrostatic field during the rotation, assuming no energy loss to radiation or secondary discharges.

# HFDPO-01960

A biomedical engineer is designing a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner for a rural clinic with unreliable power. The scanner’s superconducting magnet requires a persistent current of 120 A in a loop of wire with a 0.8 m radius to maintain a uniform 1.5 T magnetic field. However, the facility’s backup battery can only supply 400 A·s of total charge before depleting. Can the current be sustained long enough to complete a 5-minute scan if the system uses a persistent current switch that maintains the current without power after initiation?

# HFDPO-08941

A lunar habitat module uses a custom photovoltaic array with three solar cell strings connected in parallel, each with varying internal resistances (0.45 Ω, 0.60 Ω, 0.35 Ω) and open-circuit voltages (42 V, 41 V, 43 V). During a 3-hour eclipse, the system relies on a single lithium-ion battery (120 Ah, 36 V nominal) to maintain power to life support. When the battery is connected through a shared 2 Ω distribution bus, measured currents diverge: one string draws 18 A, a second 15 A, and the third 20 A, while the battery delivers 53 A. Determine whether the system is in steady-state equilibrium, and if not, identify the root cause(s) using Kirchhoff’s laws.

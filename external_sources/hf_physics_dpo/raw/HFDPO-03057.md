# HFDPO-03057

A wind turbine in a coastal wind farm spins at 15 rpm under steady wind conditions, generating 2.5 MW of electrical power. During a sudden gust, the blades experience a torque of 1.8 × 10⁶ N·m for 3.2 seconds before the control system engages. What is the new angular speed of the turbine after the gust, and how much mechanical energy is dissipated as heat due to the emergency brake system if the rotor’s moment of inertia is 4.5 × 10⁶ kg·m²?

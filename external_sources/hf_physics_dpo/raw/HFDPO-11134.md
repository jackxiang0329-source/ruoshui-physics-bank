# HFDPO-11134

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a small photovoltaic panel made of silicon. On a sunny day, the panel receives 1000 W/m² of sunlight and generates 150 W of electrical power. If the panel’s active area is 0.8 m² and the bandgap of silicon is 1.1 eV, what fraction of the incident photons are effectively generating electron-hole pairs? Assume all photons with energy above the bandgap can create one pair.

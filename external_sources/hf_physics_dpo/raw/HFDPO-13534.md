# HFDPO-13534

In a remote Arctic research station, a team relies on a solar-powered heating system to prevent permafrost thawing around their critical infrastructure. The system uses a long, buried copper cable (1.5 km long, 10 mm diameter) to carry 20 A of current from the solar array to a heated foundation grid. Due to subzero temperatures, the cable’s resistivity increases by 12% compared to room-temperature values. The station manager notices inconsistent heating and suspects energy loss. What is the total power dissipated in the cable, and how much would this power decrease if the cable were replaced with a superconducting version (zero resistance) at cryogenic temperatures?

# HFDPO-08628

During a high-altitude winter expedition, a climber discovers that their insulated water bottle, originally filled with 500 mL of liquid water at 0°C, has partially frozen after being left overnight at -15°C. The bottle’s rigid walls prevent volume change. If the final state contains a mixture of ice and water at 0°C, what fraction of the original water mass has turned to ice? Assume the bottle remains sealed and no heat is exchanged with the environment after freezing begins.

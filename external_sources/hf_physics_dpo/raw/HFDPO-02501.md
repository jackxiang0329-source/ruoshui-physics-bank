# HFDPO-02501

A fusion reactor's tokamak design uses a superconducting toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma at 150 million K, but a sudden quench in one of the outer field coils reduces the central magnetic field by 12% over 0.3 seconds. Simultaneously, the plasma develops a localized current filament with 2.7 × 10⁵ A flowing along a 1.8 m segment. Determine the net force on this filament due to the changing field and whether the resulting Lorentz force could disrupt the plasma boundary, assuming the filament is oriented perpendicular to the average magnetic field.

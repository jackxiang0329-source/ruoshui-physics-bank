# HFDPO-09285

A deep-space probe traveling at 0.95c relative to Earth emits a periodic signal every 10 seconds in its rest frame. Due to relativistic time dilation and the Doppler effect, the signal reception rate on Earth varies as the probe’s trajectory changes. If the probe follows a hyperbolic path past a massive star (gravitational lensing ignored), and its closest approach is at a distance where the radial velocity component is 0.8c, what is the observed frequency of the signal during closest approach, and how does it compare to the frequency measured when the probe is moving directly away at 0.95c?

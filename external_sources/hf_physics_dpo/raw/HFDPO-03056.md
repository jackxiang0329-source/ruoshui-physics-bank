# HFDPO-03056

A quantum computing chip operates at 10 mK and requires ultra-low-noise readout via a superconducting resonator tuned to 5.8 GHz. The resonator is coupled to a transmission line with characteristic impedance 50 Ω, but parasitic capacitance from the chip’s packaging introduces a 0.3 pF shunt at the input. When the system is driven with a 1 μW signal, the measured power transmitted drops by 20% at resonance. Determine the effective inductance of the resonator that would explain this signal loss, assuming ideal superconducting materials and negligible resistance.

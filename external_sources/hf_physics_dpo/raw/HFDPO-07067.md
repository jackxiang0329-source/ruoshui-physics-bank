# HFDPO-07067

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood by size using laminar flow in a 100 μm-wide rectangular channel. The device relies on cells migrating laterally due to viscous drag and shear gradients. If the blood plasma has a viscosity of 1.2 mPa·s and flows at 1.5 mm/s, and a 12 μm spherical cancer cell (density 1040 kg/m³) experiences drag in the flow, what is the lateral displacement of the cell after traveling 2 mm along the channel, assuming it reaches terminal migration velocity? The cell’s terminal migration velocity due to the Segré-Silberberg effect is proportional to the square root of the flow Reynolds number.

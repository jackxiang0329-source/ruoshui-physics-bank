# HFDPO-06556

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (NaNO₃/KNO₃) stored at 565°C to generate electricity via a steam turbine. During a sudden cloud cover event, the heat input drops by 40%, but the plant must maintain 20 MW of output for 3 hours to meet grid demand. The storage tank holds 12,000 m³ of salt with a density of 2100 kg/m³ and specific heat capacity of 1300 J/kg·K. If the salt’s temperature must not fall below 450°C to avoid solidification, determine whether the existing thermal mass can sustain the required power output. Assume 90% thermal efficiency in the power conversion loop and no heat loss to the environment during the outage.

# HFDPO-07333

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid, extracting heat from 220°C water reservoirs to generate 15 MW of electricity. The condenser operates at 40°C, and the isobutane enters the turbine at 160°C and 3.5 MPa. Due to scale buildup over time, the heat transfer efficiency to the working fluid has dropped to 78% of the original design value. How much must the turbine inlet temperature be increased to maintain the same power output, assuming constant turbine efficiency and condenser conditions?

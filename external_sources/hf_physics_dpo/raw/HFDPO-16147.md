The researchers need to determine the effective depletion efficiency at the focal spot when the STED intensity is reduced non-uniformly across the beam profile, requiring integration of intensity distribution, nonlinear saturation effects, and the dye’s excited-state dynamics. What is the effective resolution limit under these conditions?

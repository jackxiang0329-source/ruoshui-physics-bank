# HFDPO-06943

A Mars rover’s rear wheel becomes stuck in a patch of fine, cohesive regolith resembling wet clay, with a known shear strength of 12 kPa and a coefficient of static friction of 0.7. The wheel, 30 cm in diameter, applies a torque of 15 N·m, but begins to spin without progressing. Engineers estimate the soil plowing resistance is proportional to the depth of penetration, modeled as F_drag = 400 × d² (in N, where d is in meters). What is the minimum depth of wheel sinkage required for the rover to gain traction without spinning, assuming no slip and that the normal force is redistributed due to sinking?

# HFDPO-13453

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent made of a nitinol alloy meant to expand inside a coronary artery (radius ~4 mm) under body temperature (37°C). The stent must apply a radial force of at least 0.3 N/mm to keep the artery open without causing tissue damage. If the stent’s original (unstressed) diameter is 3.8 mm and its elastic modulus is 45 GPa, what minimum thickness of the nitinol wire is needed to achieve the required force when expanded to working diameter? Assume uniform hoop stress and linear elasticity.

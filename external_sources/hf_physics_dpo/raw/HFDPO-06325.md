# HFDPO-06325

A solar-powered microgrid on a remote island uses a 48 V DC battery bank to supply critical loads during night hours. If the battery is at 85% state-of-charge and has a 200 Ah capacity, how long can the system sustain the communication load without recharging, and what power loss occurs at each stage?

# HFDPO-05930

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent emits superheated fluid at 350°C, creating a conductive plume of saline water with resistivity varying with temperature. Engineers are designing a sensor array to measure current flow within the plume, but must account for non-uniform heating: near the vent, resistivity is 0.012 Ω·m, decreasing by 0.001 Ω·m per meter away due to cooling. If the plume forms a cylindrical region 2 meters in diameter and extends 10 meters from the vent, and a potential difference of 20 V is applied across its length, what is the total current flowing through the plume?

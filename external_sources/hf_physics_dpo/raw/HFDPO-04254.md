# HFDPO-04254

A fusion reactor's divertor plate—made of tungsten—experiences transient heat fluxes of 15 MW/m² during plasma disruptions. The plate is 5 mm thick, initially at 2000 K. Calculate the time required for the surface to reach the melting point (3695 K), considering phase change, thermal diffusion, and the temperature-dependent thermal conductivity of tungsten (k(T) = 120 + 0.04T W/m·K). Assume no heat loss from the backside and that melting begins when surface temperature reaches 3695 K.

# HFDPO-10016

A Mars rover’s wheel is sinking into fine, cohesive regolith during a critical traverse. The soil has a cohesion of 120 Pa and an internal friction angle of 34°, with a density of 1,750 kg/m³. The wheel applies a normal force of 850 N but begins to slip at a torque of 210 N·m. Calculate the effective coefficient of static friction between the wheel and soil, then evaluate whether the observed slip is due to shear failure of the regolith or exceeding the maximum torque sustainable by friction alone.

# HFDPO-04703

A fusion reactor's tokamak design uses a pulsed magnetic field to compress plasma, but recent instability spikes correlate with sudden voltage surges in the toroidal field coils. Given a 3.2 m diameter coil with 120 turns, carrying 20 kA, and a plasma current rising from 0 to 1.8 MA in 0.12 s, calculate the induced EMF in a nearby diagnostic loop (1.5 m diameter, 10 turns) positioned 15 cm from the main coil. The loop’s plane is parallel to the coil’s, and mutual inductance dominates. Evaluate whether eddy currents in the reactor vessel could disrupt plasma confinement.

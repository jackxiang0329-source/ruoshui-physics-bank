# HFDPO-05348

A cryogenic storage tank for liquid hydrogen (boiling point 20.3 K at 1 atm) develops a microfracture, allowing slow leakage into the surrounding vacuum-insulated chamber. The heat of fusion for hydrogen is 1.2 kJ/mol, and the latent heat of vaporization is 0.9 kJ/mol. If the initial mass of hydrogen in the chamber is negligible, determine how long it takes for the accumulated liquid layer to reach a thickness of 0.5 mm, assuming the condensation rate is limited by heat transfer through the growing solid-liquid interface.

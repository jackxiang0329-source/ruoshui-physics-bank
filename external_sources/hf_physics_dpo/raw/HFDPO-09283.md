# HFDPO-09283

A cosmic-ray observatory detects a high-energy muon traveling at 0.998c, created 15 km above the Earth’s surface in the upper atmosphere. The muon’s proper lifetime is 2.2 μs. From the Earth observer’s perspective, the muon reaches the surface before decaying—exactly what’s observed. But from the muon’s frame, the distance to the ground is contracted. Calculate the distance to the ground in the muon’s rest frame and explain why the muon still reaches the surface.

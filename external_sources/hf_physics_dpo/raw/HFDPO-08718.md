# HFDPO-08718

A mountain climber uses a nylon rope rated to stretch no more than 1.5% of its length under maximum load. If the rope is 60 meters long when unstretched, what is the maximum force the climber can exert before the rope exceeds this safe stretch limit? Assume the rope behaves elastically and has a Young’s modulus of 3.0 × 10⁹ Pa.

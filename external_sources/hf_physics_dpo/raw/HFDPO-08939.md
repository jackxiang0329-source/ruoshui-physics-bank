# HFDPO-08939

A wind turbine blade in a high-altitude mountain pass operates under variable wind conditions, where air density drops to 0.9 kg/m³ at 3,000 m elevation. The blade’s leading edge is designed with a curved airfoil that maintains a pressure differential of 120 Pa between its upper and lower surfaces during peak wind speeds of 25 m/s. However, during a sudden storm, wind speed increases to 35 m/s, and local turbulence causes the flow to separate at the trailing edge, reducing lift by 40%. What is the new net force per unit length on the blade, considering both Bernoulli-based pressure differences and the loss of lift due to flow separation?

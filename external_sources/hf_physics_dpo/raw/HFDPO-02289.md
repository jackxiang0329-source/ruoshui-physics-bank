# HFDPO-02289

A solar farm in a remote desert uses a custom-designed photovoltaic array where each panel's output varies due to micro-shading from sand dunes and dust accumulation. The array is wired in a complex hybrid configuration: 12 parallel strings, each with 25 series-connected panels (each 30 V, 8 A nominal), but internal wiring resistance increases by 0.02 Ω per meter due to corrosion over time. The main conductor runs 300 m from the array to the central inverter. If the inverter requires a minimum input voltage of 350 V to operate efficiently and the total power loss must not exceed 8%, what is the maximum allowable resistance in the wiring system, and how should the system be redesigned to meet the efficiency target?

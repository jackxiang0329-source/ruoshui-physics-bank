# HFDPO-03966

A solar-powered drone designed for long-duration atmospheric research hovers at 12,000 m over the Antarctic plateau, where wind gusts can exceed 45 m/s. At a critical moment during a storm, one cable snaps, and the drone begins to rotate about its center of mass. Engineers must determine the minimum tensile strength (in kN) the remaining cable must have to prevent structural failure during the unbalanced torque, assuming the wing’s center of mass is offset 0.6 m from the tether attachment point, and the drone has a total mass of 320 kg.

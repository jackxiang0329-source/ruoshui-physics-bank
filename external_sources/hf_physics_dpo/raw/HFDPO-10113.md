# HFDPO-10113

In a prototype quantum memory device based on nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond, researchers observe that the spin relaxation time (T₂) degrades under periodic microwave driving at 2.87 GHz, dropping from 100 μs (no drive) to 14 μs at 100 nW power. The system is coupled to a fluctuating bath of 10⁶ nuclear spins with a spectral density resembling a Lorentzian. Modeling the bath as a classical Gaussian random field, determine the effective temperature of the bath that would yield this T₂ reduction due to non-adiabatic transitions.

# HFDPO-02511

In a quantum imaging experiment, a single-photon source emits entangled photon pairs at 780 nm via spontaneous parametric down-conversion in a periodically poled potassium titanyl phosphate (PPKTP) crystal. The pump laser operates at 390 nm with a 5 mW average power. A detector array measures the signal photon while the idler photon is guided through a tunable fiber Bragg grating (FBG) to induce a 200 fs pulse delay. If the FBG must resolve a target coherence time of 1.2 ps for quantum ghost imaging, what is the minimum spectral bandwidth (in nm) required for the idler photons to maintain sufficient entanglement fidelity under 60% pump depletion?

# HFDPO-00014

A satellite constellation is deployed in a sun-synchronous orbit at 700 km altitude, but after launch, ground tracking reveals a growing inclination drift due to lunar gravitational perturbations. The initial inclination is 98.6°, and the orbital period is 98.2 minutes. Given that the lunar perturbation causes a secular rate of inclination change of –0.012° per year, determine the minimum number of annual station-keeping maneuvers (each consuming 5.2 m/s Δv) required over a 5-year mission to maintain inclination within ±0.1° of nominal. Assume maneuvers are perfectly impulsive and optimally timed.

# HFDPO-05031

A team of engineers at a fusion energy startup is designing a magnetic confinement chamber for a deuterium-tritium plasma. The reactor must maintain a plasma density of 1.5 × 10²⁰ m⁻³ and a temperature of 150 million K to achieve ignition. During testing, they observe a neutron flux of 2.4 × 10¹⁸ neutrons/m²/s at the reactor wall. Given that the fusion reaction rate is proportional to the product of the reactant densities and the Maxwellian-averaged cross-section, estimate the energy confinement time required for the reactor to reach breakeven.

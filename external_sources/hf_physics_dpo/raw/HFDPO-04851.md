# HFDPO-04851

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2 mm diameter to 4 mm under physiological pressure. The stent is made from a shape-memory alloy with a non-uniform thickness (t(r) = 0.05 + 0.02r mm, r in mm from center). As the stent expands, shear stresses near the inner surface reach critical levels due to material inhomogeneity. Determine the maximum von Mises stress during expansion, assuming the artery applies a constant internal pressure of 120 mmHg and the material follows a nonlinear elastic law σ = E(ε + 0.5ε²), where E = 180 GPa.

# HFDPO-11453

Analyze the power factor, reactive power demand, and harmonic distortion contribution from the pump’s non-sinusoidal current draw (measured to have a 14% THD at 11th harmonic). Determine the minimum capacitance needed in a delta-connected bank to correct the power factor to 0.98 lagging, considering that capacitor losses introduce a 5% increase in effective reactance.

# HFDPO-12846

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (NaNO₃/KNO₃ mix) to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The salt circulates through a receiver at 560°C and is stored in insulated tanks at 540°C. During a winter night, the plant must deliver 12 MW of power continuously for 8 hours using only stored energy. If the ambient temperature is 10°C, what is the *minimum* mass of salt required to sustain this output, assuming the plant operates as a Carnot engine and the salt’s specific heat is 1.5 kJ/kg·K? Consider the practical limitation that the salt cannot cool below 300°C due to solidification.

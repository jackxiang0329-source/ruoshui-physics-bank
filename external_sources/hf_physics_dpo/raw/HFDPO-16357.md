# HFDPO-16357

During a high-altitude expedition, a team discovers a frozen lake encased in a thin layer of transparent ice, but beneath it, the water remains liquid despite temperatures below −10°C. Consider whether the observed liquid water below the ice is possible under these conditions and explain why.

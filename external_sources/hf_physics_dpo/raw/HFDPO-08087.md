# HFDPO-08087

During a seismic survey for underground oil reservoirs, a 20 Hz seismic wave is generated at the surface and travels through layered rock strata with varying densities and elastic moduli. At a depth of 3 km, the wave encounters a sharp boundary between sedimentary rock (density 2200 kg/m³, longitudinal wave speed 2500 m/s) and underlying basalt (density 3000 kg/m³, longitudinal wave speed 4000 m/s). Engineers need to determine the angle of refraction and the proportion of energy transmitted into the basalt to assess reservoir continuity. The initial wave approaches the boundary at 30° from the vertical.

# HFDPO-15006

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert receives inconsistent data readings during afternoon hours. The station’s microcontroller resets intermittently, and engineers notice the issue coincides with peak sunlight. The device uses a silicon photodiode to detect light levels, but the diode’s reverse leakage current increases sharply under high irradiance. If the photodiode has a dark current of 10 nA at 25°C and its current doubles for every 10°C rise, what is the reverse current when the diode heats to 65°C under sunlight?

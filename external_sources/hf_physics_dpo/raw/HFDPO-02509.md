# HFDPO-02509

In a fusion reactor test facility, a tokamak design uses high-temperature superconducting (HTS) tapes (YBCO-based) to generate a 12 T toroidal magnetic field. During a ramp-up phase, a persistent current mode fails unexpectedly, causing a localized quench. The system’s magnetic sensors detect a transient flux leakage of 0.8 µWb over 1.5 ms near the midplane of the toroidal winding. Given that the winding’s effective inductance is 3.2 H and the critical current density of the HTS tape is 2.5 × 10⁶ A/m², determine whether the quench originated from flux creep in the superconductor or from a mechanical instability causing partial current loss. Assume the tape’s cross-section is 0.1 mm × 4 mm and the flux jump threshold for the material is 300 T/m²/s.

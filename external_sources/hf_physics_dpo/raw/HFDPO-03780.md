# HFDPO-03780

A satellite constellation deployed in low Earth orbit (LEO) experiences unexpected drift due to atmospheric drag and solar radiation pressure. At 450 km altitude, the drag coefficient is 2.2, and the effective cross-sectional area is 8 m². Solar radiation pressure adds a perturbing force of 9.1 × 10⁻⁶ N. The mission requires maintaining a precise orbital inclination of 98° to ensure consistent global coverage. After one week, engineers detect a 1.7° deviation in inclination. What is the net torque per unit mass acting on the satellite due to these non-gravitational forces, and how much delta-v is needed to correct the orbit if the thrusters can only fire at a 30° angle to the velocity vector?

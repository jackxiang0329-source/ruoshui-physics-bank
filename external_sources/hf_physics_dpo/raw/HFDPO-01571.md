# HFDPO-01571

A high-altitude wind farm in the Andes uses tethered drones that fly in figure-eight patterns at 800 m above sea level, harvesting kinetic energy from persistent jet stream winds. Each drone has a mass of 320 kg and generates 1.2 MW of power on average over 30 minutes of operation. Due to air resistance and mechanical inefficiencies, only 68% of the drone’s kinetic energy is converted to electrical energy. If the drone starts its cycle with a speed of 15 m/s and must return to a hover state at the end of each cycle, determine the minimum kinetic energy input required per cycle to maintain continuous power generation.

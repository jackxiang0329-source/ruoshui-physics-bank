# HFDPO-13569

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size-induced differences in flow dynamics. The device uses a narrow channel (100 μm wide) branching into two outlets; one bypasses a constriction with a 20 μm diameter throat, while the other remains wide.

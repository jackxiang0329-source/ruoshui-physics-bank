# HFDPO-14297

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe interference patterns from electrons passing through a nanoscale double-slit array, but the visibility drops sharply when a quantum detector is placed near one slit to determine which path the electron took. Using wave-particle duality principles, explain how the act of "measuring" path information destroys coherence, and calculate the minimum uncertainty in momentum imparted by the detector that would account for this loss of interference.

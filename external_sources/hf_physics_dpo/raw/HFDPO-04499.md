# HFDPO-04499

A lunar lander descends onto the Moon’s surface using variable-thrust retro-rockets, aiming for a soft touchdown within a 30-second window. At 120 m altitude, its vertical velocity is 15 m/s downward, and it must reduce velocity to 0.5 m/s at touchdown. The lander’s mass decreases at 2.4 kg/s due to fuel burn, and engine thrust can be adjusted continuously. The Moon’s gravity is 1.62 m/s². What thrust profile (as a function of time) minimizes fuel consumption while ensuring a safe landing?

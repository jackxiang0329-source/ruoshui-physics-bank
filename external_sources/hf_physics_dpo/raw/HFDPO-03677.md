# HFDPO-03677

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research is experiencing unexpected roll instability during hover. The material’s Young’s modulus is 130 GPa and coefficient of thermal expansion is 7×10⁻⁶/°C. Determine whether the resulting internal stress could trigger buckling, and evaluate if the design meets a safety factor of 2.5 against failure.

# HFDPO-14343

A satellite-based Earth observation system uses dual-polarized radar to monitor deforestation in the Amazon. What is the degree of polarization of the received signal, and how would the system’s ability to distinguish between forest and cleared land be affected if the polarization state is not corrected?

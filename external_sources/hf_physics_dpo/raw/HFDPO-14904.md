# HFDPO-14904

A wildlife biologist uses a portable lens system to photograph a rare nocturnal bird perched 12 meters from a forest clearing. The camera’s fixed focal length lens (f = 100 mm) produces a sharp image on the sensor, but the biologist notices that the bird’s wingtips appear slightly blurred in the final image. After checking, the lens aperture is set to f/4.

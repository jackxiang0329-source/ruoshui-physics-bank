# HFDPO-03555

A solar panel array on a remote weather station is mounted on a tilt mechanism that must withstand wind gusts up to 40 m/s during storms. The 120 kg panel is mounted at a 35° angle, with its center of mass 1.8 m from the pivot. A counterweight of 80 kg is placed 1.2 m behind the pivot to maintain balance. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 1,200 N at the panel’s center, 2.5 m above ground. Will the system tip over, and if not, what is the minimum friction force required at the pivot to prevent sliding?

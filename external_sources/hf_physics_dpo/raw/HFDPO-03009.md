# HFDPO-03009

A geophysicist studying tectonic stress buildup in a subduction zone models the overriding crust as a viscoelastic slab 15 km thick, anchored at depth. During a 30-year interseismic period, the slab accumulates strain at a rate of 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ s⁻¹ due to slab pull. Using a Maxwell model with a shear modulus of 30 GPa and viscosity of 1.2 × 10¹⁹ Pa·s, calculate the accumulated shear strain at the surface and determine whether the strain is primarily elastic or viscous in nature.

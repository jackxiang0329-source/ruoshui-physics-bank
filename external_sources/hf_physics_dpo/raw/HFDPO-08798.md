# HFDPO-08798

A wind turbine blade in a remote Arctic installation experiences sudden ice accumulation during a storm, increasing its rotational inertia by 18% and reducing its efficiency due to aerodynamic disruption. The turbine’s control system detects a drop in rotational speed and activates a braking mechanism that applies a constant torque of 2.1 × 10⁵ N·m to safely stop the blade. If the blade was spinning at 15 rpm before braking, how much mechanical work must the brake dissipate, and what fraction of the original kinetic energy is lost to non-conservative forces during the 12-second deceleration? Assume the blade behaves as a rigid rod of mass 4200 kg and length 50 m.

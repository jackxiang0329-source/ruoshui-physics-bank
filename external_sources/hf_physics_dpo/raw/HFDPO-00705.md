# HFDPO-00705

A fusion reactor prototype uses a spherical plasma chamber with a radius of 2.5 m, confined by magnetic fields. During a brief pulse, a current of 15 MA flows in a toroidal loop around the chamber, generating a magnetic field that compresses the plasma. Simultaneously, 80 MJ of thermal energy is released from the fusion process. If the reactor’s structural frame is isolated from external forces and torques, what is the recoil velocity of the entire 500-ton chamber structure, and how does conservation of momentum interact with energy conservation in this transient event?

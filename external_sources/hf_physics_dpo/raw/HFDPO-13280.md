# HFDPO-13280

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a slowly rotating underwater vent emitting a buoyant jet of warm, mineral-rich fluid at 80°C into cold seawater at 4°C. Assume the fluid is incompressible, the jet entrains ambient fluid at a rate proportional to its velocity gradient, and the Coriolis parameter is 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ s⁻¹.

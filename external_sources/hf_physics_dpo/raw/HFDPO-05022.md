# HFDPO-05022

The vent's chimney structure forms a porous, thermally conductive pathway with an effective cross-sectional area of 0.3 m² and thickness of 0.8 m. If the vent operates continuously over a year and the surrounding rock maintains a near-constant temperature, what is the maximum possible rate of entropy generation within the chimney due to irreversibilities? Assume the fluid exits in equilibrium with the ocean and analyze the thermodynamic feasibility of sustained energy conversion.

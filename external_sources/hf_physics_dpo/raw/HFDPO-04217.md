# HFDPO-04217

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels with anti-reflective coatings optimized for direct sunlight. However, during early morning and late afternoon, glare from the panels interferes with nearby drone surveillance used for maintenance. The glare is polarized, and drones equipped with circular polarizing filters reduce interference—but only when the filter is rotated properly. At a given time, the reflected light has a degree of polarization of 0.85, and the angle of incidence is 58°. What orientation of the polarizer should the drone operator use to minimize glare, and how does this relate to Brewster’s angle?

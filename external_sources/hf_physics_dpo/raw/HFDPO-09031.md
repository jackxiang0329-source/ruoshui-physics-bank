# HFDPO-09031

A laser-based underwater communication system uses a 532 nm green laser to transmit data across a 100 m depth in coastal waters with a refractive index of 1.34 and a scattering coefficient of 0.6 m⁻¹. Engineers observe signal degradation not fully explained by absorption alone. Design a model to predict the spatial coherence length of the beam at the receiver, considering both diffraction and scattering-induced phase distortions, and determine the maximum reliable data rate assuming a minimum coherence length requirement of 1 mm.

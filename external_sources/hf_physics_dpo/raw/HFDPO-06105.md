# HFDPO-06105

A deep-space observatory in a highly elliptical orbit around Jupiter must perform a precise orbital maneuver to avoid a debris cloud resulting from a recent moon-impact event. The cloud is confined to a 500 km-wide ring at an altitude of 1.2 million km above Jupiter’s cloudtops, moving in a near-circular, prograde orbit at 42.3 km/s. The observatory currently orbits with a periapsis of 400,000 km and an apoapsis of 2.8 million km. To safely avoid the cloud, it must raise its periapsis by exactly 150,000 km via a single prograde burn at the current periapsis. What is the required Δv magnitude, and is the maneuver efficient enough to be completed within the observatory’s remaining fuel budget (max Δv = 500 m/s)?

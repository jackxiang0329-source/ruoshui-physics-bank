# HFDPO-15373

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses a toroidal plasma with a time-varying magnetic field that induces currents in its surrounding superconducting stabilizer coils. During a sudden power surge, the magnetic flux through a 12-turn stabilizer loop changes from 0.85 Wb to 2.35 Wb over 3.2 ms. The loop has a resistance of 0.017 Ω and self-inductance of 1.4 mH. An engineer must determine whether the induced current will exceed the critical current threshold of 450 A, risking quenching. What is the maximum induced current, and how does self-inductance affect the timing of this current rise?

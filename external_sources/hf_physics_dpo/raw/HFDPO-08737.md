# HFDPO-08737

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses mirrors to concentrate sunlight onto a central tower, heating molten salt to 565°C. The salt stores 12 GJ of thermal energy per m³. During a clear day, the system absorbs 15 MW of solar power but loses 3 MW to the environment via convection and radiation. If the plant needs to maintain a 200 MW output over 8 hours using this stored heat, how much molten salt (in m³) must be heated above 500°C, assuming the heat engine operates at 35% efficiency and no heat is lost during conversion?

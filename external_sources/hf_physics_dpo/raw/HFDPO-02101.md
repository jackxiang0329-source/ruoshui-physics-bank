# HFDPO-02101

A quantum sensor measuring gravitational waves uses a superconducting niobium resonator cooled to 50 mK, where its mechanical oscillations are governed by a nonlinear spring with restoring force F = -kx - alpha x^3 . During a calibration run, the system exhibits period doubling when driven at 1.2 MHz with 2.5 nW of power. If the resonator’s effective mass is 3.4 pg and alpha = 1.8 times 10^{-12} N/m³, determine whether the observed instability arises from parametric resonance or intrinsic nonlinearity, and explain how the amplitude threshold for chaos would shift if the damping coefficient were halved.

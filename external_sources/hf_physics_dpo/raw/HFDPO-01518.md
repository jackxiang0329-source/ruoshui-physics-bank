# HFDPO-01518

During a Mars rover mission, the robotic arm encounters a stuck drill bit embedded in a rock. To free it, engineers decide to apply a controlled rotational torque using the arm’s motor, but the drill’s rotation is impeded by static friction and a non-uniform resistance torque that increases with angular displacement. The resistance torque is modeled as τ_res = 1.2θ + 0.5θ² (in N·m), where θ is in radians. The motor applies a constant torque of 8.0 N·m. What angular displacement is required for the drill to start rotating if the initial angular velocity is zero, and how much work does the motor perform by that point?

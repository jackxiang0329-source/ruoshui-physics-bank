# HFDPO-11981

A satellite-mounted polarimeter detects sunlight reflected off a glacier in Antarctica, measuring a degree of linear polarization of 38% at a 55° angle to the horizontal. The ice surface is covered with a thin layer of meltwater and microplastic debris (refractive index ~1.55). Engineers suspect the debris alters the polarization signature, but standard models fail to match observations. What is the effective refractive index of the ice-debris interface, and how does the phase shift between s- and p-polarized components change across the surface?

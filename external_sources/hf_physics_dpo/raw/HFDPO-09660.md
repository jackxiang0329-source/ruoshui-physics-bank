# HFDPO-09660

A solar-powered drone, designed to hover at 8,000 m altitude for atmospheric research, uses four counter-rotating propellers mounted at the ends of a rigid, triangular carbon-fiber frame. To maintain equilibrium without relying on active control inputs, the frame must be rigidly braced in a way that ensures no net moment about the drone’s center of mass. Given the frame’s geometry (equilateral triangle, 2.4 m side), determine the minimum tensile force required in a diagonal strut connecting two vertices to prevent rotation due to asymmetric wind loading.

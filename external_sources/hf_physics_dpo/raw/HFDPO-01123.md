# HFDPO-01123

A quantum imaging lab develops a single-photon detector array for entanglement-based microscopy, but observes unexpected noise patterns in images of biological cells. The detector captures interference fringes with a visibility of only 68% despite idealized theory predicting 95%. The setup uses a type-II spontaneous parametric down-conversion source emitting photon pairs at 810 nm (signal) and 1550 nm (idler), with a pump laser at 405 nm. The detection system has a temporal jitter of 12 ps and spatial resolution of 3.5 μm. Explain the origin of the reduced visibility and calculate the minimum phase coherence time lost due to spectral-temporal mode mismatch.

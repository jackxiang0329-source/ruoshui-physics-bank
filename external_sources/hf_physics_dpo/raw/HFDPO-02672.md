# HFDPO-02672

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent to support a narrowed artery. The stent must expand from a diameter of 2.0 mm to 5.5 mm when deployed, undergoing elastic deformation without permanent strain. If the wall thickness is 0.15 mm, what is the minimum hoop stress the material must withstand during expansion, and does this exceed the yield strength of 300 MPa?

# HFDPO-04532

A geosynchronous satellite’s attitude control system uses two counter-rotating flywheels to stabilize orientation without external torques. Each flywheel has mass 12 kg, radius 0.3 m, and is initially spinning at 8,000 rpm in opposite directions. Due to a software glitch, one flywheel accelerates to 10,000 rpm while the other slows to 6,000 rpm over 2.5 seconds. Calculate the net torque exerted on the satellite’s body during this reconfiguration and explain whether angular momentum is conserved in the system.

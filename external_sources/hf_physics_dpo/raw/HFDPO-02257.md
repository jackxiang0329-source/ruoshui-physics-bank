# HFDPO-02257

An off-grid solar farm in rural Kenya uses a custom-designed aluminum truss to support a 120 kg solar panel array tilted at 30° to maximize sun exposure. The truss is secured to a concrete foundation via a single bolt at point A and leans against a frictionless wall at point B. The center of mass of the panel is 1.8 m from the bolt, and the truss is 2.5 m long. What minimum horizontal force must the wall exert at point B to prevent rotation, and how does this change if wind applies a 150 N horizontal force at the panel’s top edge?

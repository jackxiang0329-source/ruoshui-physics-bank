# HFDPO-11304

Measured electric field at 0.5 m from its surface reaches 5.0 × 10⁴ N/C, directed radially inward. The drone’s onboard system must prevent arcing to a nearby conductive wing panel 1.8 m away. Given the breakdown field strength of air is 3.0 × 10⁶ V/m, determine whether the sensor pod poses a risk of sparking to the wing during the flight, and evaluate the physical justification for assuming uniform charge distribution on the pod.

# HFDPO-01818

A quantum imaging team at a defense research lab is developing a single-photon lidar system for autonomous navigation in dense fog. The system uses temporally entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in a BBO crystal, with one photon sent to a target and the other used for correlation detection. Due to atmospheric scattering, the return signal is extremely weak—averaging only 0.3 photons per pulse over a 100 ns window. The team observes a 12% false-positive rate in low-visibility conditions despite optimized filtering. What must the minimum coincidence efficiency be to distinguish a true target return from background noise at a 99.9% confidence level, assuming shot-noise limited detection and a background rate of 2.1 × 10⁴ photons per second?

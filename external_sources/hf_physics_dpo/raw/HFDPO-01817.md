# HFDPO-01817

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible coronary stent made of a superelastic nitinol alloy to support a narrowed artery. During implantation, the stent must be compressed to a diameter of 1.2 mm for delivery via catheter, but once deployed, it expands to 3.8 mm and must maintain a radial force of 0.4 N/mm to prevent restenosis. Given that the stent is cylindrical, 15 mm long, with a wall thickness of 0.1 mm and a Young’s modulus of 45 GPa in its austenite phase, determine the maximum strain energy stored during deployment and explain how the material’s phase transformation contributes to the stent’s recovery behavior.

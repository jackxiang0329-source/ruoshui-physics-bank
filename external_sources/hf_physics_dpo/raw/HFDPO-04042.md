# HFDPO-04042

A Mars rover’s drill bit, designed to sample subsurface ice, spins at 2000 rpm while applying a constant torque of 0.8 N·m. As it penetrates a frozen regolith layer, friction increases nonlinearly: τ_friction = kω², where ω is angular speed in rad/s and k = 1.2×10⁻⁴ N·m·s². The drill’s rotational inertia is 0.03 kg·m². How long does it take to reach maximum penetration depth, defined as when angular acceleration drops below 5 rad/s²?

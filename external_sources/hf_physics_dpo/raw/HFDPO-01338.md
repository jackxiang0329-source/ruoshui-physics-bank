# HFDPO-01338

A solar-powered drone designed for atmospheric research must maintain stable altitude during a 2-hour flight over the Arctic. It carries a 1.2 kg battery that provides 3600 J of energy per minute. As the drone ascends slowly at a constant speed, it uses energy to lift its own mass against gravity. If the drone starts at sea level and rises 500 m, how much energy must be conserved in the system to maintain constant speed, and what fraction of the battery’s total energy is used? Assume no air resistance and constant gravitational acceleration.

# HFDPO-16415

During a lunar mining operation, a solar-powered drill relies on a flexible, 150-meter-long copper cable to transmit 800 W of power from a rover to the drill head. The cable’s resistance increases by 0.2% per degree Celsius due to thermal expansion and Joule heating. If the cable starts at 20°C and operates at 1.5 A, what is the maximum allowable voltage drop across the cable to maintain stable drilling efficiency, and how does self-heating affect this limit over time? Assume ambient lunar temperature is −50°C and no active cooling is used.

# HFDPO-00932

In a quantum computing facility, a 1.2 m-long loop of high-temperature superconducting tape (YBCO) is used to form a persistent current loop in a SQUID magnetometer. During a cooldown from 80 K to 77 K (below Tc), no external current is applied—yet a persistent current of 2.1 A is measured. However, the device’s flux noise increases dramatically. Analyze whether the observed current is consistent with flux quantization, and explain the origin of the unexpected noise in terms of vortex dynamics and thermal fluctuations.

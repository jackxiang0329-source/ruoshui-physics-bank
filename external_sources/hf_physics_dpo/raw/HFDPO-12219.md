# HFDPO-12219

A space probe’s attitude control system uses two counter-rotating flywheels, each with mass 12 kg and radius 0.3 m, mounted on a 1.8 m diameter central shaft. During a maneuver, one flywheel spins at 1200 rpm to stabilize orientation, while the other spins in the opposite direction at 1000 rpm. Suddenly, a torque fault causes the slower flywheel to decelerate at 3.5 rad/s². What is the resulting angular acceleration of the probe’s body, assuming it has a moment of inertia of 85 kg·m² about the axis?

# HFDPO-13067

A solar-powered drone used for monitoring Arctic ice melt has a rigid, asymmetric wing structure made of carbon-fiber composites, with a total mass of 28 kg. During a test flight, sensors detect a 2.3° pitch-up moment caused by uneven ice accumulation on the left wingtip, which adds a 1.2 kg mass 1.8 m from the center of mass. The drone’s center of lift is located 0.4 m aft of the center of mass. Determine the minimum adjustment in the position of a 1.0 kg counterweight (mounted on the right wing root) required to restore longitudinal static equilibrium, considering that the center of lift shifts by 0.05 m for every 10° of pitch angle due to aerodynamic effects.

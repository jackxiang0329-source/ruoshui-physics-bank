# HFDPO-14208

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain a stable hover for 12 hours over a remote glacier. The drone's propulsion system uses four identical electric motors, each drawing 450 W when operating at full thrust. During flight, 18% of the electrical energy is lost as heat in the motors and wiring. If the drone’s battery stores 3.2 kWh of energy, and it must use 30% of its power for sensors and avionics, what is the maximum thrust each motor can produce while maintaining hover? Assume the drone’s total mass is 14 kg, and gravitational acceleration is 9.8 m/s².

# HFDPO-12291

A solar-powered water pump in a remote village fails during cloudy days. The pump’s control circuit uses a silicon photodiode to detect sunlight and activate the pump. On a cloudy day, the photodiode generates only 1.2 μA of current. If the photodiode’s quantum efficiency is 70% at visible light (550 nm), how many photons per second must be incident on the diode to produce this current?

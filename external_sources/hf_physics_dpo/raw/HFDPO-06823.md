# HFDPO-06823

A deep-space mining probe orbits a small, irregularly shaped asteroid (mass = 4.2 × 10¹⁵ kg, average radius = 2.3 km) at a distance of 8.0 km from its center. If the probe must maintain a minimum safe altitude of 5.0 km above the surface to avoid collision, and a sudden 3% drop in thrust occurs during a descent phase, will the probe safely avoid impact? Calculate the minimum required thrust correction needed to maintain orbit, considering the gravitational anomaly.

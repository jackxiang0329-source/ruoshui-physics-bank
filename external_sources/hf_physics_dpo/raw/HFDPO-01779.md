# HFDPO-01779

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a high-resolution imaging system detects a bioluminescent jellyfish at a depth of 800 m, but the light from its glow appears distorted and shifted laterally by 1.2 m when projected onto the camera’s sensor. The camera’s lens has a focal length of 15 cm and is positioned 50 cm from the pressure-resistant viewport. The refractive index of seawater is 1.34, and that of the viewport material is 1.52. Determine whether the distortion is primarily due to refraction at the viewport interface, lens aberration, or a combination of both, and justify your conclusion using ray tracing analysis and the lensmaker’s equation.

# HFDPO-11424

A homeowner installs a solar-powered water fountain in their backyard pond. The fountain uses a small pump to push water up a vertical tube, then out through a horizontal nozzle at the top. When the pump is turned on, water shoots out horizontally at 3.0 m/s. The nozzle has a diameter of 1.5 cm, and the water level in the pond is 0.8 m below the nozzle. What is the pressure at the base of the tube (at the pond level) just before the pump, assuming the flow is steady and ideal?

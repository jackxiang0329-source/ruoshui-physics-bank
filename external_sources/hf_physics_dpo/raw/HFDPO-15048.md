# HFDPO-15048

A biomedical engineer is designing a targeted drug delivery system using magnetic nanoparticles. The particles must be guided through blood vessels to a tumor site using a uniform external magnetic field. If the particles have a magnetic moment of 3.2 × 10⁻²³ A·m² and are located in a region where the magnetic field gradient is 1.5 T/m, what is the magnitude of the force acting on a single particle? How would increasing the gradient affect the force, and why might a non-uniform field be essential for effective targeting?

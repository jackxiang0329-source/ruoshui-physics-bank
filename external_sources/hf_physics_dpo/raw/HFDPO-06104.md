# HFDPO-06104

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size and deformability. What is the approximate ratio of the viscous drag force on a cancer cell to the inertial force at the channel's centerline, and how does this ratio affect the cell’s lateral migration?

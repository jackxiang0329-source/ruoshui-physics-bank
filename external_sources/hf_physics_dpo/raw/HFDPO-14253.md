# HFDPO-14253

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice that the drill head—designed to rotate at a constant angular acceleration of 8.5 rad/s²—fails to penetrate a rock layer despite reaching a peak angular speed of 120 rpm. The drill's 0.35 m long shaft has a moment of inertia of 0.18 kg·m² and is driven by a motor applying a torque of 15 N·m. However, friction in the drill’s bearing system is unknown and suspected to be causing energy loss. What is the effective frictional torque acting on the drill shaft, and how much rotational kinetic energy is lost to friction during the 4.0 seconds it takes to reach peak speed?

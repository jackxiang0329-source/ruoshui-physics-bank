# HFDPO-10406

A drone equipped with a polarized solar panel array flies over a desert at midday, where intense sunlight reflects off the sand at a glancing angle. The panels are designed to maximize energy capture by aligning their polarization axes with the dominant polarization of the direct sunlight. However, the reflected glare from the sand causes significant power loss due to the reflected light being linearly polarized at 56° from horizontal. If the panels are initially oriented at 30° to the horizontal, what should the new polarization axis be adjusted to minimize glare effects and maximize net power input, assuming the direct sunlight is unpolarized and the reflected light carries 40% of the total incident intensity?

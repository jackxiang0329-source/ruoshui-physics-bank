# HFDPO-05454

During a deep-space mission, a rover's thermal regulation system relies on a network of micro-scale resistive heaters embedded in its insulation layer. Each heater is a thin-film carbon nanotube strip (resistivity ρ = 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ Ω·m, length L = 3.2 cm, cross-sectional area A = 1.8 × 10⁻⁸ m²) arranged in a parallel array of 12 elements. The power supply outputs a stable 15 V, but due to degradation in the wiring harness, the effective voltage at the heater array drops to 13.2 V. To maintain thermal equilibrium, the total power delivered to the heaters must remain at 4.8 W. What is the minimum resistance that must be added in series with the array to restore the required power, and how does this change the current distribution if one heater fails?

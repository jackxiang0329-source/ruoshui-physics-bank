# HFDPO-04571

A marine biologist is studying how coral polyps feed in coastal waters with weak tidal currents (0.15 m/s). To capture plankton efficiently, the polyps extend tentacles that create small vortices when pulsing. If a polyp's tentacle generates a vortex with circulation Γ = 0.02 m²/s and the surrounding water has a viscosity of 1.0 × 10⁻³ Pa·s, how far from the tentacle does the vortex influence the flow enough to significantly alter local particle trajectories? Assume the vortex behaves as a point vortex and influence is significant when the induced velocity exceeds 10% of the ambient current.

# HFDPO-02143

During a coastal engineering conference, a team proposes a new wave-energy converter that uses a vertical tube submerged in the ocean, open at both ends, to harness energy from oscillating water levels. When waves pass, water surges up and down the tube, creating airflow at the top due to pressure differences. If the tube is 12 m tall and the water level inside fluctuates by ±1.5 m with each wave, what is the maximum speed of air entering the tube at the top during a surge, assuming the air behaves as an incompressible fluid and the water surface moves rapidly?

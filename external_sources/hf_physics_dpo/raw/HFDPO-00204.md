# HFDPO-00204

A quantum computing chip operating at 15 mK inside a dilution refrigerator experiences unexpected thermal crosstalk between its superconducting qubits, causing decoherence. The chip is mounted on a copper post (1 mm diameter, 5 cm long) thermally anchored to the 4 K mixing chamber. A 10 W electrical heater is placed 2 mm from one qubit, and the measured temperature rise in the adjacent qubit is 2.3 mK. Using only the thermal conductivity of copper (400 W/m·K) and assuming one-dimensional heat transfer through the post, estimate the effective thermal contact conductance (W/m²·K) at the interface between the copper post and the chip.

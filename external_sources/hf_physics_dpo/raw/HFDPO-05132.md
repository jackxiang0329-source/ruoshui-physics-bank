# HFDPO-05132

A marine biologist is deploying an underwater microphone array to monitor whale communication in a deep ocean trench. The array must detect low-frequency calls (15 Hz) traveling through water, but ambient noise from distant shipping lanes (200 Hz) is interfering. The water temperature varies from 4°C at depth to 12°C near the surface, creating a sound channel that bends sound waves. If the whales are 10 km away and the signal must be detected above a 50 dB noise floor, what is the minimum source level (in dB re 1 μPa) the whale call must have to be distinguishable, accounting for absorption (3.5 dB/km at 15 Hz) and the bending effect of the sound channel?

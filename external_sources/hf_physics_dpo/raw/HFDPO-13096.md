# HFDPO-13096

A small solar-powered weather station in a remote valley uses a network of three solar panels connected in parallel to charge a 12 V battery. During a cloudy day, Panel A delivers 2.5 A, Panel B delivers 1.8 A, and Panel C delivers 1.2 A into the battery. The station’s control circuit draws 4.3 A from the battery. Using Kirchhoff’s Current Law, determine the current flowing through the charging regulator and explain why the system cannot sustain operation if the regulator draws more than 5.0 A.

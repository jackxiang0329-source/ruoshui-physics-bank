# HFDPO-03241

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter to 4.0 mm during deployment, but must not exceed a maximum elastic strain of 10% to avoid fatigue failure. The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 45 GPa and a nonlinear stress-strain relationship described by σ = E₀ε + kε³, where E₀ = 40 GPa and k = 1.2 GPa. What is the minimum wall thickness required to ensure elastic recovery without exceeding the strain limit, and how does this choice affect the radial force exerted on the arterial wall?

# HFDPO-09310

A deep-space probe uses a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) that produces 120 W of electrical power at 30 V across a variable-load resistor array designed to maintain system autonomy over 15 years. As plutonium-238 decays, the RTG’s internal voltage drops by 0.1% per year. If the load resistors are fixed at 7.5 Ω and the system’s efficiency drops due to contact resistance growing from 0.01 Ω to 0.15 Ω over time, what is the actual power delivered to the load at year 10, and how does this compare to the designed power?

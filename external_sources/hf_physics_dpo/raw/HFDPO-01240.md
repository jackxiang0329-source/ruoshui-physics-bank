# HFDPO-01240

A space probe's solar array on a mission to Jupiter degrades over time due to radiation-induced defects in its silicon photovoltaic cells. Initially, the cells have a minority carrier lifetime of 10 μs and a saturation current density of 1 nA/cm². After 5 years in radiation-rich environments, measurements show the saturation current density increases to 10 nA/cm², while the minority carrier lifetime drops to 1 μs. Evaluate whether the degradation is dominated by trap-assisted recombination or by increased intrinsic carrier concentration, and determine the effective defect density (in cm⁻³) responsible for the lifetime reduction.

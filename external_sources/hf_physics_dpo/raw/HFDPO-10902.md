# HFDPO-10902

A wind turbine blade, 60 meters long and weighing 12,000 kg, is mounted on a horizontal shaft at its center. During maintenance, a technician climbs 40 meters from the center toward the tip to inspect a sensor. The blade is supported by a pivot at the center and a cable attached 15 meters from the pivot, angled at 35° above horizontal. If the cable can withstand a maximum tension of 850 kN, what is the maximum allowable mass of the technician to prevent cable failure, assuming the blade’s center of mass is at the midpoint?

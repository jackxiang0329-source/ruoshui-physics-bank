# HFDPO-10071

A quantum imaging lab is developing a single-photon camera for low-light biological imaging, but they're plagued by spatial noise from spontaneous emission in the superconducting nanowire detector array. The detector's response is modeled as a Poisson process with a mean photon count rate of 2.5 photons per pixel per millisecond, but the signal from a fluorescent protein target is only 1.2 photons per pixel per millisecond. To isolate the true biological signal, they must determine the minimum spatial correlation length of the signal photons required to distinguish it from background noise using second-order intensity correlation (g²(τ)). What correlation length (in μm) enables a g²(0) value of 0.5, indicating sub-Poissonian statistics, assuming the system’s coherence time is 10 ps and the optical bandwidth is 100 nm?

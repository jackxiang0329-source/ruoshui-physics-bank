# HFDPO-01475

A fusion research team is designing a spherical plasma containment vessel with a thin, conductive outer shell to shield external electronics from intense electric fields. During startup, the plasma inside develops a non-uniform charge density modeled as ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − r/R), where r is the radial distance from the center, ρ₀ = 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³, and R = 0.8 m is the vessel radius. Engineers need to determine the electric field at r = 0.6 m, just outside the plasma but inside the shell.

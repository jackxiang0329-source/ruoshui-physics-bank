# HFDPO-08048

A wind turbine blade in a high-altitude research installation (5,000 m elevation) experiences unexpected vibrations during operation. Engineers suspect that localized airflow separation due to pressure differences along the blade’s surface is causing turbulent vortices. The blade’s leading edge has a curvature that reduces local pressure, but at certain wind speeds, the pressure drop exceeds the ambient atmospheric pressure, risking cavitation-like effects in the air itself. Given a local airflow velocity of 40 m/s at the blade’s tip (where flow is fastest), and standard atmospheric conditions at that altitude (pressure ≈ 54 kPa, density ≈ 0.74 kg/m³), what is the minimum pressure that could develop at the blade’s surface according to Bernoulli’s principle?

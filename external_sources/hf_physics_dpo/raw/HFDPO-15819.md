# HFDPO-15819

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for an implantable cardiac pacemaker using a coil embedded in the skin and a matching inductor in the device. The system operates at 13.56 MHz, but due to tissue inhomogeneity, the effective inductance of the implant coil varies from 5.2 μH to 6.0 μH, while its resistance remains stable at 1.8 Ω. The external coil has a fixed inductance of 5.0 μH and resistance of 0.5 Ω. The goal is to maintain maximum power transfer across the entire expected inductance range. What value of capacitance should be placed in series with the external coil, and how does the quality factor Q of the system change across the inductance range? Is the system still efficient under these conditions?

# HFDPO-06316

A deep-sea research submersible uses a custom-designed fiber-optic communication array to transmit high-resolution sonar data to a surface vessel. The array employs a series of graded-index lenses to collimate light from a 532 nm laser into a narrow beam that travels 1,200 meters through seawater with a variable refractive index profile: n(z) = 1.335 - 0.0005 cdot sinleft(frac{pi z}{100}right) , where z is depth in meters. The system must maintain beam convergence within ±0.05° at the receiver. Determine whether the initial beam divergence angle must be less than 0.02° to meet this constraint, assuming ideal lens design and no absorption.

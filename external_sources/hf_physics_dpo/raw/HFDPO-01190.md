# HFDPO-01190

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V photovoltaic array charges a 12 V lead-acid battery bank through a PWM charge controller. During a 10-day cloudy period, the battery voltage drops to 10.8 V. The unit draws 2.1 A at 24 V. A technician observes a 3.6 V voltage drop across the cable. Assess whether the cable size is adequate and determine the actual power delivered to the load. Recalculate the required cable cross-section to limit voltage drop to 2.0 V, considering resistivity and thermal limits (max 60°C).

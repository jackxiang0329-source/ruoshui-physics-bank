# HFDPO-03944

In a high-altitude polar research station, scientists observe that the rate of atmospheric helium leakage through a micro-porous polymer membrane (pore diameter ~2 nm) exceeds predictions from ideal gas laws. The membrane is used to maintain inert gas pressure in a sensitive cryogenic sensor. Given a temperature of 200 K, helium pressure difference of 1.2 atm across the membrane, and a mean free path of 15 nm in the surrounding gas, analyze whether the leakage is dominated by molecular effusion or viscous flow, and determine the effective pore size if effusion dominates.

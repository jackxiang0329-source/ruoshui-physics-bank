# HFDPO-01778

A team at a quantum materials lab is developing a single-photon detector for quantum communication, but they observe unexpected noise in the signal when operating at 1550 nm. To diagnose the issue, they measure the probability of a photon with energy 0.8 eV being absorbed, yet the measured absorption efficiency is only 60% of the expected value. Could tunneling through a defect layer or insufficient penetration depth explain the shortfall?

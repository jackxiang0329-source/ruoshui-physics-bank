# HFDPO-00118

A solar-powered drone, designed to hover at 2 km altitude for environmental monitoring, uses a lightweight carbon-fiber frame with four symmetrically placed propellers. Due to uneven solar heating across its wings during a sudden cloud passage, one side of the drone warms 12°C more than the other, causing a 0.8% expansion in the leading edge of that wing. The drone’s center of mass shifts 15 cm sideways. With a total weight of 6.5 kg and each propeller generating 1.7 N of thrust, determine whether the drone remains in static equilibrium or begins to tip. Account for both the shift in center of mass and the resulting moment imbalance due to asymmetrical thrust angles caused by the wing deformation.

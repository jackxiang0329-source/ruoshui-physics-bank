# HFDPO-06757

During a coastal storm, a roof panel begins to lift off a small shed when wind blows horizontally at 30 m/s just above it. The air inside the shed remains calm, with a pressure equal to standard atmospheric pressure. If the panel's area is 2 m² and its mass is 10 kg, will the pressure difference due to the wind be enough to overcome the weight of the panel and cause it to lift? Assume air density is 1.2 kg/m³.

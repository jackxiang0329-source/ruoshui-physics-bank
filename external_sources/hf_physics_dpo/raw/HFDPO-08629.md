# HFDPO-08629

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter to 4.0 mm during deployment, enduring cyclic stress from pulsatile blood flow. The stent’s material (nitinol alloy) has a Young’s modulus of 70 GPa and a nonlinear stress-strain relationship described by σ = 300ε + 50ε³ (in MPa). During testing, the stent fails after 2.5 million cycles despite passing initial static stress tests. What is the maximum elastic strain energy per unit volume accumulated during each expansion cycle, and why might fatigue failure occur even if the maximum stress remains below the material’s yield strength?

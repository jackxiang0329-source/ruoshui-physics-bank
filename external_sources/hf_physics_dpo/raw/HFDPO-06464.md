# HFDPO-06464

A coastal city is designing a new seawater desalination plant using a Venturi-based system to draw seawater into a high-pressure chamber without pumps. The system uses a horizontal pipe with a 0.6 m diameter inlet that narrows to a 0.2 m diameter throat. If the seawater flows at 1.8 m/s at the inlet and the atmospheric pressure at the throat must not drop below 75 kPa to avoid cavitation, what is the maximum allowable flow speed at the throat? Assume seawater density is 1025 kg/m³ and atmospheric pressure is 101.3 kPa.

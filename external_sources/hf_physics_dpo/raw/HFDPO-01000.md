# HFDPO-01000

A research team at a fusion energy lab is investigating charge accumulation in a toroidal tokamak plasma chamber, where ionized deuterium and tritium form a high-temperature plasma confined by strong magnetic fields. During a diagnostic pulse, sensors detect an unexpected radial electric field of 8.5 × 10⁴ V/m just outside a 1.2 m radius vacuum vessel wall, despite the chamber being nominally neutral. The plasma is symmetric about the toroidal axis but has non-uniform charge density: ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−r² / a²), where a = 0.3 m and ρ₀ is unknown. Determine the total charge enclosed within a cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius 1.5 m coaxial with the torus, and explain how Gauss’s Law resolves the apparent paradox of a non-zero field in a neutral system.

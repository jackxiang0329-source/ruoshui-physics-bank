# HFDPO-04792

A small off-grid cabin in Alaska relies on a wood-burning stove to heat the interior and generate electricity via a thermoelectric generator (TEG) attached to the stovepipe. The TEG has hot-side and cold-side temperatures of 300°C and 25°C, respectively. If the cabin needs 50 W of electrical power and the TEG operates at 15% of the ideal Carnot efficiency, how much heat energy must the stove supply per hour to meet this demand?

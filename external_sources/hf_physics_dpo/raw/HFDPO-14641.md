# HFDPO-14641

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a solar panel array connected to a battery system to power sensors and a satellite uplink. The solar panels (rated at 120 V, 10 A) charge a bank of 48 V lithium-ion batteries through a buck-boost converter with 92% efficiency. Due to a malfunction, one of the 12-Ω shunt resistors used for current monitoring overheats and fails, causing the system to report incorrect current readings. The station logs that the charging current is 7.5 A, but the actual current is higher. Using only the system’s voltage readings and known component values, determine the true charging current and identify the likely cause of the failure.

# HFDPO-04371

A coastal engineer is designing a floating breakwater to reduce wave energy before it reaches a vulnerable shoreline. The structure consists of a series of cylindrical pontoons, each 10 m long and 2 m in diameter, moored vertically in water 15 m deep. Waves approaching the breakwater have a wavelength of 40 m and a height of 3 m in deep water. The engineer needs to estimate the reduction in wave energy after passing through the array of pontoons, considering both wave diffraction and viscous drag on the cylinders. What is the approximate fraction of incident wave energy transmitted through the breakwater?

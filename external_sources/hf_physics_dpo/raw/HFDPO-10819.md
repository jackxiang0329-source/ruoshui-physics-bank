# HFDPO-10819

A solar farm in the desert uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a grid, but performance drops sharply at noon when glare from the sand reduces efficiency. Engineers discover that the reflected sunlight is highly polarized, with the electric field aligned horizontally. To mitigate this, they install a vertical polarizing filter over a section of panels. If the incident sunlight has intensity 1000 W/m² and the reflection from sand produces 40% of that intensity with horizontal polarization, what is the new intensity incident on the panel after the filter? What percentage of the original power is now captured by the panel?

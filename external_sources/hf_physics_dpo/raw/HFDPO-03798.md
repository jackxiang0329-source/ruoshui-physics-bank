# HFDPO-03798

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and weighing 12,000 kg, is mounted on a horizontal shaft at its center. During maintenance, a technician stands 10 m from the pivot point, applying a vertical force to stabilize the blade momentarily. If the blade's center of mass is 25 m from the pivot, and the shaft can withstand a maximum torque of 3.5 × 10⁶ N·m, what is the minimum force the technician must apply to prevent the blade from rotating due to gravity?

A key concern is that RBCs aggregate at low shear rates near the vessel walls, increasing local viscosity by up to 3×. How does this aggregation affect the effective flow rate compared to a Newtonian fluid with no aggregation? Assume the channel has a rectangular cross-section and analyze the change in volumetric flow rate due to non-Newtonian, shear-thinning, and wall-proximal aggregation effects.

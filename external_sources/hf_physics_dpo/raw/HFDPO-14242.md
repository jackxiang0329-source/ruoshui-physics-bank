# HFDPO-14242

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a persistent swirling current near a hydrothermal vent field, where hot, buoyant fluid jets emerge from the seabed at 300°C into cold seawater at 4°C. The ROV’s sensors detect a vortex with a radial velocity profile that follows v_r(r) = frac{A}{r} , where A = 0.8,text{m}^2/text{s} , and the fluid is incompressible. The ROV must navigate through this region without being pulled into the vortex. Determine the minimum safe distance from the center of the vortex where the axial (vertical) velocity component remains negligible compared to the horizontal motion—assuming the flow is steady, axisymmetric, and governed by conservation of mass and momentum.

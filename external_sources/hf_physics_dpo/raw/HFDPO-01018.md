# HFDPO-01018

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote farm uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic panels wired in a complex mixed configuration—some in series, others in parallel—to charge a 24 V battery bank. Engineers measure 2.8 A flowing into the battery, but the total current from all panels sums to 10.5 A. Using Kirchhoff’s laws, determine the current through the faulty panel.

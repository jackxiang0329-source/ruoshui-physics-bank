# HFDPO-03248

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 25° angle to maximize winter sun exposure. During a heavy snowstorm, 450 kg of snow accumulates unevenly, with 70% on the lower half and 30% on the upper half of the 8 m long panel. The mounting system uses two frictionless pivots at the ends. What minimum friction force must be provided at the lower pivot to prevent the panel from rotating under the snow load, and how does this change if ice reduces the coefficient of static friction to 0.2?

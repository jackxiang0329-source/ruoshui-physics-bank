# HFDPO-13468

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpected efficiency drops at 540 nm under direct sunlight, despite theoretical predictions suggesting peak performance at 580 nm. The device uses a photonic crystal nanoscale grating (period = 320 nm, depth = 180 nm) to trap light via guided mode resonance. Measurements show the 540 nm dip correlates with a sharp reduction in in-plane electric field intensity. Investigate whether this is due to destructive interference from higher-order diffraction modes or mode coupling with surface plasmon polaritons at the metal–dielectric interface (Ag/TiO₂, 50 nm thick).

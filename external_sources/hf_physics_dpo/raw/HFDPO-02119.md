# HFDPO-02119

A satellite-borne polarimeter designed to monitor Arctic sea ice albedo detects unexpectedly high polarization in reflected sunlight over newly formed thin ice, but near-zero polarization over thick, multi-year ice. The instrument measures a degree of linear polarization of 0.18 at 532 nm for thin ice, but only 0.02 for thick ice. Assuming the ice surface scatters light via Fresnel reflection and Rayleigh scattering in a layered medium, determine the effective refractive index of the thin ice layer that would produce this measured polarization difference.

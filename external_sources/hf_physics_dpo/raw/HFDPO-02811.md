# HFDPO-02811

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden plasma disruption, ejecting a 2.5 kg plasma jet at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s tangentially from a 12 m radius toroidal chamber. Engineers must deploy a magnetic brake system to quench the jet’s momentum without damaging the vessel.

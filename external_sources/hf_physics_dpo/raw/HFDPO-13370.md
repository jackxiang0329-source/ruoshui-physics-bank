# HFDPO-13370

A small offshore wind farm uses a 60 Hz AC system to transmit power to a floating substation. The transmission line has a total inductance of 1.2 mH and resistance of 0.8 Ω per km, and the line is 12 km long. The substation requires a power factor of 0.95 lagging to avoid penalties from the grid operator. If the load draws 2.5 MW at 12.47 kV (line-to-line), what capacitance (in μF) must be added in parallel at the substation to correct the power factor, and how does this affect the current drawn from the wind turbines?

# HFDPO-08300

A solar-powered weather station in a remote alpine region uses a dual-panel array connected in a complex loop with a battery bank and a microcontroller. Panel A outputs 18 V at 2.4 A under full sun, while Panel B (partially shaded) produces 12 V at 1.8 A. The system’s wiring forms a junction where currents split to charge the battery (rated 12 V, 50 Ah) and power the sensor suite. At a given moment, the microcontroller draws 1.6 A, and the shunt regulator dissipates 0.8 A as heat. Determine the current through the battery and confirm whether the system maintains current balance at the junction using Kirchhoff’s Current Law.

# HFDPO-03116

A solar panel array on a remote mountain research station is mounted on a sloped, icy roof with a 35° incline. The panels weigh 120 kg each and are secured by a single pivot at the lower edge, with a tension cable running from the upper corner to a fixed anchor on the wall above. During an unexpected thaw, the ice layer melts partially, reducing the coefficient of static friction to 0.15. The cable can withstand up to 1,800 N of tension. What is the maximum length the solar panel can be without causing the system to slip or the cable to fail, given the panel’s center of mass is 0.4 m from the pivot?

# HFDPO-03820

In a quantum computing lab, researchers use a double-slit setup with entangled photons to probe decoherence in superconducting qubits. One photon passes through a slit while its partner is measured in a distant detector. When the measurement path is uncertain, an interference pattern emerges on the screen with fringe spacing of 1.2 mm. But when which-path information is recorded, the pattern vanishes. Given that the slits are 0.2 mm apart and the screen is 1.5 m away, calculate the effective wavelength of the photon involved, and explain how the loss of interference correlates with the quantum information content in the entangled partner.

# HFDPO-03127

A malfunctioning solar farm in a remote desert region experiences inconsistent power output across its subarrays. The system uses a mesh network of 12 identical photovoltaic (PV) panels connected via a web of 15 copper interconnects with resistances ranging from 0.02 Ω to 0.08 Ω. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current through the shaded panel and identify which interconnect carries the highest risk of overheating.

# HFDPO-08715

During a solar storm, a satellite in geostationary orbit detects a sudden increase in electric field strength near its outer hull, peaking at 1.2 × 10⁴ N/C radially outward. The satellite's protective shell is a thin, spherical conducting layer with a radius of 2.3 m and a net charge of +4.5 μC. A nearby dust cloud, known to be electrically neutral overall but with asymmetric charge distribution, is suspected. Estimate the net charge enclosed within a Gaussian surface just inside the satellite’s hull (but outside the dust cloud), and explain how the observed field implies a non-uniform charge distribution in the cloud.

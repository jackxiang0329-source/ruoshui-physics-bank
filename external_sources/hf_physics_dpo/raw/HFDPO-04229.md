# HFDPO-04229

During a severe storm, a hurricane-force wind of 60 m/s blows horizontally across the rooftop of a modular hospital building designed with a flat, lightweight composite panel system. The panels are secured with hidden clips rated for 750 N/m² of uplift force. The building’s interior is pressurized at 101.3 kPa, but the external pressure fluctuates with wind speed. If the roof area is 150 m² and the air density is 1.225 kg/m³, will the roof panels withstand the storm without lifting? Assume the flow over the roof is steady and inviscid, and the wind creates a pressure differential consistent with Bernoulli’s principle.

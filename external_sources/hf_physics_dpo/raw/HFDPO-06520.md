# HFDPO-06520

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits anomalous efficiency drops under prolonged exposure to high-intensity sunlight, despite consistent material composition. The dots are 5 nm in diameter, made of CdSe, with electron effective mass 0.15 mₑ and hole effective mass 0.4 mₑ. At 300 K, the observed carrier recombination rate increases by 300% after 4 hours of irradiation. Investigate whether this is due to (1) radiation-induced defect states in the bandgap, or (2) dynamic polaron formation altering the band structure, using both many-body perturbation theory and tight-binding simulations with defect potentials.

# HFDPO-02454

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a superconducting coil (radius 1.2 m, 200 turns) buried 2.5 km underground. Drilling disturbs the local magnetic field, creating a transient flux change of 0.85 T·m² over 18 seconds as the drill passes through a magnetized basalt layer. The coil’s resistance is negligible, but the system’s output must be evaluated for energy efficiency. What is the induced emf, and how much electrical energy is generated during this transient event? Compare the result to the energy needed to maintain cryogenic cooling of the coil (5.2 kJ). Is the net energy gain viable for monitoring the subsurface?

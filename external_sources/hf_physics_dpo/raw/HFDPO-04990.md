# HFDPO-04990

A solar farm in Arizona uses inverters to convert DC from photovoltaic panels into grid-compatible AC. During peak sun, the system delivers 120 kW at 240 Vrms to a local substation, but the power factor drops to 0.75 lagging due to inductive loads in the distribution network. Engineers notice overheating in the inverter’s output filters. To protect the system, they must add capacitors in parallel to correct the power factor to 0.95 lagging. What capacitance (in μF) must be added per phase if the frequency is 60 Hz and the system is three-phase, Y-connected?

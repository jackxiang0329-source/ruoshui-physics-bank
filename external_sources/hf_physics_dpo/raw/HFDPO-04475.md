# HFDPO-04475

During a deep-sea exploration of a hydrothermal vent field, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) equipped with a high-precision flow sensor detects unexpected oscillations in the plume's velocity—peaking at 0.8 m/s every 3.2 seconds—despite constant vent temperature and pressure.

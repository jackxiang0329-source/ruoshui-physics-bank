# HFDPO-03045

During a mission to power a remote weather station in the Arctic, engineers discover that the solar panel array delivers 18 V under peak sunlight, but the station’s battery bank (rated at 12 V) is draining rapidly. A voltage regulator circuit with adjustable resistance is used to stabilize output, but measurements show inconsistent power delivery. The system uses a 10 Ω shunt resistor in series with a variable load (the battery). When the load resistance is set to 20 Ω, the current is 0.6 A. However, when the load is increased to 40 Ω, the current drops to 0.4 A. What is the internal resistance of the solar panel system, and why does the battery still undercharge despite the regulator?

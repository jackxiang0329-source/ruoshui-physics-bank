# HFDPO-06925

In a quantum computing prototype using superconducting qubits, researchers observe unexpected decoherence at temperatures just above 15 mK, despite shielding from external electromagnetic noise. What experimental signature would distinguish between these two mechanisms at nanosecond-scale coherence times?

# HFDPO-08745

A submarine detects a low-frequency acoustic signal from a distant whale migration route, but the signal’s frequency shifts unexpectedly as it passes through a thermal layer in the ocean. Given that the speed of sound in water increases from 1480 m/s above the layer to 1500 m/s below it, and assuming the submarine is stationary, determine the angle at which the sound wave must have entered the thermal layer relative to the vertical.

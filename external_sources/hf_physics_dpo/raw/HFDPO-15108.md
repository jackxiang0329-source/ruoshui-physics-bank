# HFDPO-15108

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice that the drill bit, rotating at 2000 rpm to penetrate basaltic rock, suddenly begins to wobble uncontrollably. The 0.8 kg drill bit has a radius of 5 cm and is mounted on a motor shaft with a moment of inertia of 0.0015 kg·m². A slight imbalance — a 2 g mass offset by 3 mm from the axis — is suspected. What is the maximum torque the motor must deliver to counteract the resulting vibrational forces at peak rotation, and why might this torque exceed the motor’s rated capacity even if the rotational kinetic energy appears manageable?

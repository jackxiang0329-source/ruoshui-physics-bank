# HFDPO-00519

A laser-based quantum imaging system uses a 633 nm He-Ne laser to probe a nanoscale photonic crystal embedded in a biological tissue sample. The crystal exhibits a photonic bandgap that selectively reflects light, but due to fabrication imperfections, the periodicity varies by ±1.5 nm across a 100 μm × 100 μm region. When the laser is scanned across this area, interference fringes in the reflected beam shift in a non-uniform way, indicating local strain and density fluctuations. What is the minimum detectable change in refractive index (Δn) that would cause a measurable phase shift in the interference pattern, assuming the system can resolve phase differences of 0.01π and the effective path length through the crystal is 50 μm?

# HFDPO-04640

The beam, initially planar with diameter 2 mm, propagates 100 m through this region. After exiting, the beam diverges and must be focused onto a 1 mm² detector. What is the minimum focal length of the receiver lens needed to ensure at least 90% of the beam's intensity is captured, assuming the beam’s phase front becomes distorted but the intensity profile remains symmetric?

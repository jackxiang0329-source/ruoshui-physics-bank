# HFDPO-09616

A renewable energy microgrid on a remote island uses a 50 Hz AC generator to power homes and a critical water desalination plant. The desalination unit draws 12 kW at a lagging power factor of 0.75, while residential loads total 8 kW at 0.90 lagging. A technician notices voltage drops during peak demand. To stabilize voltage, they install a 250 μF capacitor bank in parallel with the system. Calculate the new overall power factor and determine if the capacitor bank adequately compensates for reactive power.

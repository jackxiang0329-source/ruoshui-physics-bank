# HFDPO-16228

A drone delivery system uses a lightweight, rigid 2.4-meter carbon fiber arm to suspend a 3.2-kg package during mid-air stabilization. The arm is hinged at one end to the drone frame and supported by a high-tension cable attached at 60° to the horizontal, 1.8 meters from the hinge. If the cable can withstand a maximum tension of 500 N, what is the maximum mass of a second package that can be added at the far end without exceeding this limit? Assume the arm’s mass is negligible and gravity is 9.8 m/s².

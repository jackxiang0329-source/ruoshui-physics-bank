# HFDPO-08637

A fusion research team is designing a tokamak reactor with a toroidal plasma confined by strong magnetic fields. To mitigate electrode charging from ionospheric plasma interactions, they plan to embed a thin, conductive shell with a variable charge density. During a test run, sensors detect an unexpected electric field of 4.2 × 10³ N/C radially outward at a radial distance of 1.8 m from the torus center—outside the shell. The shell has an inner radius of 1.2 m and outer radius of 2.0 m. Given that the total charge on the shell is unknown and the plasma is assumed radially symmetric, determine whether the shell is net positively or negatively charged, and calculate the required total charge to produce the observed field.

# HFDPO-05107

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses a handheld laser scanner to map tiny surface patterns on coral polyps. The device emits coherent green light (λ = 532 nm) and detects interference fringes produced when the beam reflects off micro-ridges on the polyp surface, spaced 1.2 μm apart. When the scanner is tilted to 35° from normal incidence, a bright fringe appears at 18° from the surface normal. What is the order of this interference maximum, and does the fringe pattern suggest changes in the spacing due to environmental stress?

# HFDPO-13683

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Andes operates on a 24 V DC system, using three photovoltaic panels (each 30 W, 18 V open-circuit) wired in series to charge a 24 V lithium-iron-phosphate battery bank. During a prolonged overcast period, the panels deliver only 40% of their rated power. The station’s sensors, radios, and data logger draw 1.8 A continuously, but power management logic disables non-critical components when battery voltage drops below 21 V. If the battery starts at 24 V with 100 Ah capacity, how long can the station remain operational before critical functions shut down, considering a 15% efficiency loss in the MPPT charge controller and internal wiring resistance of 0.3 Ω total?

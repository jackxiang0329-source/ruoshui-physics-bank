# HFDPO-12994

In a failing lunar habitat prototype, a solar-powered electrolysis unit intermittently shuts down during peak energy demand. The power distribution network uses two parallel DC buses (120 V nominal) feeding a 50 Ω load via a shared bus-bar with variable internal resistance due to microcracks. One bus has a measured current of 2.1 A; the other, 1.8 A. The system’s failure occurs when total current exceeds 4 A. Use Kirchhoff’s Laws to determine the critical internal resistance threshold at which the system becomes unstable due to load imbalance, given that each bus supplies identical nominal voltage but differing effective voltages due to resistance.

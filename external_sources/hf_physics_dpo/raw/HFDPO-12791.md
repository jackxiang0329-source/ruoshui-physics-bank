# HFDPO-12791

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanoscale grating (spacing 450 nm) to enhance light absorption in a thin perovskite layer. Measured reflectance shows a sharp dip at 620 nm under normal incidence, indicating destructive interference in reflected light. However, when the incident angle is increased to 30°, the dip shifts to 580 nm. What is the effective refractive index of the perovskite layer, and how does this shift reveal the role of diffraction order in spectral tuning?

# HFDPO-03364

The flask has a total surface area of 0.8 m² and a thermal conductivity equivalent to a 1.5 cm thick layer of aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K). Ambient outside temperature is –40°C. The lab staff suspects the flask is losing heat faster than expected. What is the rate of heat transfer into the flask, and why might the actual temperature rise be faster than predicted by steady-state conduction alone?

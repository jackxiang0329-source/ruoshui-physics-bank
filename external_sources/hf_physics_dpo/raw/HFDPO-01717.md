# HFDPO-01717

A satellite re-entry capsule, shaped like a truncated cone with a mass of 4,200 kg and a radius of 1.8 m at its base, enters Earth's atmosphere at 7.8 km/s with an initial angular velocity of 0.6 rad/s about its symmetry axis. Aerodynamic drag creates a torque that induces precession, causing the capsule to wobble. If the moment of inertia about the symmetry axis is 2,400 kg·m² and about the transverse axis is 3,600 kg·m², at what rate does the capsule’s angular momentum vector precess? How does this affect the capsule’s stability during descent?

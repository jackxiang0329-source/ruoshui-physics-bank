# HFDPO-15399

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass spins at 20 rpm during a storm, but its generator fails to synchronize with the grid due to uneven blade pitch. Each blade, 45 m long and modeled as a thin rod, has a mass of 12,000 kg. A maintenance drone deploys a counterweight to stabilize the rotor by shifting the center of mass 0.8 m toward the hub. By how much does this shift affect the turbine’s rotational inertia, and what is the new angular acceleration if the wind torque remains constant at 1.8 × 10⁶ N·m?

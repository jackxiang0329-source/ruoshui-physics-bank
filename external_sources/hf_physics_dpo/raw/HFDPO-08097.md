# HFDPO-08097

A research team is designing a novel electromagnetic shield for a quantum computing facility to block stray electric fields from nearby industrial equipment. The shield consists of a hollow, cylindrical Faraday cage made of conductive material with a radius of 1.5 m and infinite length, carrying a uniform surface charge density of 3.2 × 10⁻⁹ C/m². An employee accidentally places a small, charged sensor (0.2 nC) inside the cavity, 0.7 m from the central axis. What is the electric field magnitude at a point 1.2 m from the axis, just outside the shield?

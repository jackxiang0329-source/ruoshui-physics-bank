# HFDPO-09553

A team at a quantum computing research lab is testing entangled photon pairs generated in a satellite orbiting Earth at 7,500 m/s. To maintain coherence, the lab must synchronize the timing of measurements between ground stations and the satellite using a shared clock signal. How must the lab adjust the signal transmission delay to ensure the measured correlation between entangled photons is correctly interpreted, given that the satellite orbits at an altitude of 500 km and the round-trip signal delay is measured as 6.72 ms in the Earth frame?

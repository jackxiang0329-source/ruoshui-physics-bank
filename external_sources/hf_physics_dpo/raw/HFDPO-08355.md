# HFDPO-08355

A solar-powered water purifier in a remote village uses sunlight to evaporate contaminated water, then condenses the vapor into clean drinking water. On a sunny day, the system absorbs 3.5 kJ of solar energy per minute. If the latent heat of vaporization of water is 2260 J/g, how much water (in grams) can be purified in 10 minutes? Assume all absorbed energy goes into vaporization.

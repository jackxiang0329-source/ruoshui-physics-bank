# HFDPO-14924

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 5.0 cm × 5.0 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of −8.0 nC/m² is suspended by electrostatic forces above a grounded metal plate. Due to a sudden power fluctuation, the wafer’s edge develops a localized charge defect: a 2.0 mm wide strip along one edge accumulates an additional 1.2 nC of positive charge. Calculate the net electrostatic force on the wafer, including the effect of charge redistribution, and determine whether the wafer will experience a torque that could cause it to rotate upon release.

# HFDPO-02567

A commercial satellite operator must adjust the orbit of a high-efficiency solar-electric propulsion (SEP) satellite from a 720 km circular low Earth orbit to a 1,100 km circular orbit to avoid atmospheric drag while maintaining optimal power generation. The satellite’s ion thruster provides a constant specific impulse of 3,200 s and a thrust of 0.21 N, with a total mass of 480 kg (including 52 kg of xenon propellant). The mission requires minimal Δv and maximum propellant efficiency. What is the optimal two-burn strategy (in terms of timing and Δv allocation), and what is the total mission time to complete the transfer?

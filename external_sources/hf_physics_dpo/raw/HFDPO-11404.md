# HFDPO-11404

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip designed for early cancer detection, a suspension of lipid-coated nanoparticles (diameter 150 nm, density 1050 kg/m³) flows through a 200 μm wide, 50 μm high rectangular channel at 0.3 μL/min. The fluid (viscosity 1.8 mPa·s, density 1000 kg/m³) is driven by a pressure difference of 12 kPa across a 1.5 cm channel length. Calculate the particle's terminal settling velocity relative to the fluid due to gravity, considering both Stokes’ drag and the influence of wall-induced hydrodynamic forces. How does this affect the particle’s lateral migration and potential capture efficiency in the device?

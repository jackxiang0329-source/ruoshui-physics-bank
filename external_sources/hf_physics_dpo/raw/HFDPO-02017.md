# HFDPO-02017

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels with anti-reflective coatings designed to minimize glare from sunlight. However, during peak hours, nearby wildlife cameras detect intense polarized reflections that interfere with tracking sensors. Given that sunlight is partially polarized upon reflection off the panel's surface at a 58° angle of incidence, and the panels are coated with a material having a refractive index of 1.50, what is the degree of polarization of the reflected light, and at what angle should a polarizing filter be oriented to block 90% of the glare?

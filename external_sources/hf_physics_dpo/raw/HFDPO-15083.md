# HFDPO-15083

A biomedical engineer is designing a non-invasive ultrasound therapy device for tumor ablation, targeting a deep-seated cancerous lesion 12 cm beneath the skin. The patient reports discomfort at the treatment site, suggesting unintended heating in surrounding tissue.

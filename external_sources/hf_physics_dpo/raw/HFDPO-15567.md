# HFDPO-15567

A solar-powered desalination plant in a remote coastal village uses a parabolic dish to concentrate sunlight onto a blackened copper absorber plate (50 cm diameter, 2 mm thick), which heats seawater in a thin wick-lined chamber below. If the system is designed to produce 30 kg of fresh water per day, what is the minimum required absorber surface area to maintain steady-state evaporation, and how does this constraint limit the system’s scalability?

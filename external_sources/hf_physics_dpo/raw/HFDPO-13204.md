# HFDPO-13204

A deep-sea submersible drone, designed to survey hydrothermal vents at 3,000 meters depth, experiences unexpected lateral drift during descent due to unbalanced drag forces. The drone’s streamlined titanium hull has a projected frontal area of 0.85 m² and a drag coefficient of 0.12, but minor asymmetry in its thruster placement creates a net side force. At a descent speed of 1.2 m/s, the ocean current near the seafloor exerts a transverse flow of 0.45 m/s. Determine the effective drag coefficient that would account for both viscous drag and the added resistance from the current-induced yaw, and explain how this alters the drone’s power consumption and stability control strategy.

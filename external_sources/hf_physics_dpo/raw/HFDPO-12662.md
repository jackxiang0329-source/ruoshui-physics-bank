# HFDPO-12662

During a lunar construction mission, a robotic excavator digs a trench on the Moon’s surface, ejecting regolith at high speed. The excavator, initially at rest, has a mass of 850 kg including 120 kg of regolith. After digging, 40 kg of regolith is expelled at 18 m/s relative to the excavator. If the excavator’s thrusters are off and no external forces act, what is the final velocity of the excavator, and how much kinetic energy is gained? Describe the energy source and why the system’s total mechanical energy isn’t conserved.

# HFDPO-00335

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day, aiming to generate electricity 12 hours after sunset. The system stores 1.2 GJ of thermal energy in 30 tons of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1.2 kJ/kg·K. As night falls, heat begins to leak from the storage tank due to imperfect insulation. If the salt cools from 550°C to 430°C over 8 hours, what is the average rate of heat loss, and how does this compare to the required power output (30 MW) needed to meet nighttime demand?

# HFDPO-14216

A deep-sea research submersible descends through the Mariana Trench, encountering a sudden pressure shift due to a submerged tectonic fracture releasing trapped methane gas. The sub's viewport is a 0.5-m-diameter circular plate sealed with a pressure-sensitive polymer gasket. At 10,000 m depth, seawater density increases to 1050 kg/m³ due to compression, and the trapped gas forms a stable bubble at 5000 m, exerting 2.5 atm gauge pressure at that depth. If the gasket fails when the net force on the plate exceeds 180,000 N, what is the maximum safe depth for the sub before retraction, assuming the gas bubble’s pressure remains constant and the gasket is only effective in compression?

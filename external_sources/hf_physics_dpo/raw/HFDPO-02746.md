# HFDPO-02746

During a deep-sea survey, a sonar system detects a submerged fracture in a hydrothermal vent structure, emitting a periodic pressure wave at 120 Hz. The wave travels through seawater (density 1025 kg/m³, bulk modulus 2.3 GPa) and refracts at the boundary with a solid basalt layer (P-wave speed 6.2 km/s). If the wave enters the basalt at 15° to the normal, and the observed transmitted angle in basalt is 22°, calculate the effective wavelength in the basalt and explain whether this fracture geometry could amplify seismic energy at the vent site due to wave interference.

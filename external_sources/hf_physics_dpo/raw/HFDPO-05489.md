# HFDPO-05489

A fusion reactor containment system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) arranged in a toroidal coil to generate a 15 T magnetic field. The tapes sustain a critical current density of 1.2 × 10⁹ A/m² at 77 K, but during a sudden cooling ramp, localized thermal gradients cause flux flow in patches. When a 12 mm² cross-sectional section of the tape experiences a 0.3 K temperature rise from 77 K due to a quench event, the local critical current drops to 4.5 × 10⁸ A/m². Calculate the minimum safe current that must be maintained to prevent full quench propagation, assuming flux motion generates resistive heating at 1.8 × 10⁻⁶ W/m³ per A/m² of current density.

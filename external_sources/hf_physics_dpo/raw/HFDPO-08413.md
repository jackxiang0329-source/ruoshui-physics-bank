# HFDPO-08413

In a solar panel manufacturing plant, a technician notices that dust particles are consistently repelling from the surface of newly coated photovoltaic cells during the final inspection stage. The cells are charged to −5.0 μC due to a corona discharge during the coating process. Dust particles, each with a net charge of +2.5 nC, float near the surface but never touch it—instead, they hover at a stable distance of 1.2 mm. Assuming the cell is a large flat conductor and the dust particle is a point charge, determine the required dielectric constant of the air layer between the particle and the cell for this equilibrium to be stable, given the gravitational force on the particle is 1.8×10⁻¹⁰ N.

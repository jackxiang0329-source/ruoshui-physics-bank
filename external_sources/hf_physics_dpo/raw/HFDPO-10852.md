# HFDPO-10852

A fusion reactor design team at a clean energy startup is troubleshooting anomalous neutron flux readings during plasma confinement experiments. The tokamak’s neutron yield per pulse is 1.2 × 10¹⁴ neutrons, but simulations predict 9.5 × 10¹³, suggesting unaccounted fusion reactions. The team suspects tritium breeding via neutron capture in lithium-6 enriched blankets is underperforming due to neutron energy degradation. Given a neutron spectrum with 60% at 14 MeV and 40% at 2 MeV, and a lithium-6 cross-section of 940 barns at 14 MeV dropping to 12 barns at 2 MeV, determine whether neutron energy downscattering in the blanket significantly reduces tritium production efficiency compared to a monoenergetic 14 MeV source.

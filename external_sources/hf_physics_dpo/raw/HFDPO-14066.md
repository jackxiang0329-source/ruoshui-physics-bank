# HFDPO-14066

A tidal energy turbine array is being deployed in a coastal inlet where strong, bidirectional currents (up to 4.2 m/s) flow over a rocky seabed. Each turbine, shaped like a vertical-axis wind turbine, is mounted on a rigid, tripod base anchored to the ocean floor by friction piles. During peak flow, one turbine experiences a 12,500 N hydrodynamic lift force perpendicular to its axis due to asymmetric flow separation. The base has a 2.8 m diameter circular footprint and is designed to resist overturning via embedment depth and soil friction. Determine the minimum required embedment depth to prevent rotation, assuming a soil shear strength of 65 kPa and a base unit weight of 24 kN/m³, while accounting for both uplift and lateral sliding.

# HFDPO-02463

A coastal town installs a new wave-powered energy converter that uses a vertical pipe open at both ends, submerged in the ocean. During high tide, water surges up the pipe due to wave motion, reaching a maximum height of 3.2 m above sea level at the top. The pipe has a narrow constriction at 1.8 m above sea level, where the cross-sectional area is reduced to 30% of the base area. If the system is designed to capture energy from the increased flow speed at the constriction, what is the maximum pressure difference between the base of the pipe and the constriction during peak surge?

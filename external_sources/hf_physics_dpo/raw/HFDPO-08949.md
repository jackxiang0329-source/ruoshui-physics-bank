# HFDPO-08949

In a high-vacuum semiconductor manufacturing chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of -2.4 μC/m² is suspended electrostatically above a grounded electrode. A 50 μm-diameter dust particle carrying -1.8 × 10⁻¹⁴ C of charge drifts horizontally toward the wafer at 3.2 m/s. To prevent contamination, a uniform electric field is applied perpendicular to the wafer surface. What electric field strength (magnitude and direction) must be applied to exactly balance the electrostatic attraction between the particle and the wafer, ensuring the particle hovers without contacting the surface?

# HFDPO-06672

A team developing quantum-dot solar cells notices that under prolonged exposure to high-intensity sunlight, the efficiency drops by 18% over 500 hours—despite no visible degradation. Spectroscopic analysis reveals a shift in the bandgap energy of the CdSe quantum dots, indicating unintended electron transitions from the valence to defect states. Given that the quantum dots have a radius of 2.3 nm and the effective mass of electrons in CdSe is 0.13 me, estimate the energy required to promote an electron from the ground state to the first excited state using the particle-in-a-sphere model. Then evaluate whether this energy aligns with the observed shift in the bandgap and explain what implications this has for defect formation in real-world quantum-dot devices.

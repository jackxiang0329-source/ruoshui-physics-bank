# HFDPO-00686

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using deterministic lateral displacement (DLD) in a channel with 25-μm-wide pillars arranged in a staggered array. Blood flows at 0.8 mL/min through a 100-μm-wide channel, and the viscosity of the plasma is 1.2 mPa·s. The pillars are spaced such that the flow must navigate around them in a zigzag path. At what critical Reynolds number does viscous forces begin to dominate over inertial effects, preventing particle focusing and compromising CTC capture efficiency?

# HFDPO-11451

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain precise stability during prolonged flight over remote Arctic regions. The drone’s central fuselage, 3.2 meters long, is suspended from four titanium struts arranged symmetrically around a central hub, each angled at 35° from vertical. The struts support a 120 kg payload (sensors, batteries, and communication units) distributed unevenly due to thermal expansion in the Arctic winter—75 kg concentrated 0.6 m from the forward end, and 45 kg 2.1 m from the same end. Determine the minimum tensile strength required for each strut to prevent structural failure, assuming the system is in static equilibrium and the center of mass of the fuselage itself is at its geometric center.

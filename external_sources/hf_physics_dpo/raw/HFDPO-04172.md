# HFDPO-04172

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable, level flight while carrying a 4.2 kg sensor payload offset 0.3 m from its centerline. The drone’s rigid, symmetric frame is supported by four carbon-fiber struts arranged in a tetrahedral configuration, each angled at 65° from the vertical. Due to asymmetric wind gusts during ascent, one strut experiences a 12% increase in tension. Determine the minimum required cross-sectional area of the struts (using a yield strength of 800 MPa and a safety factor of 3) to safely support the system without buckling or yielding.

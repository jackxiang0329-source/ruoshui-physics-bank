# HFDPO-03890

A coastal engineer is designing a low-impact tidal energy harvester to generate electricity from the ebb and flow of ocean tides in a narrow inlet. The inlet has a cross-sectional area of 120 m² at its narrowest point, and during peak tidal flow, water speed reaches 3.2 m/s. However, the turbine must operate efficiently without causing excessive backflow or erosion. If the inlet’s width narrows from 80 m to 45 m over a 200 m stretch due to natural rock formations, what is the expected increase in flow speed downstream of the constriction, and how does this affect the turbine’s power output if the flow depth is 4.5 m and the turbine operates at 40% efficiency?

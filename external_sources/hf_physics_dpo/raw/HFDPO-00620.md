# HFDPO-00620

A wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices uses a 10 MHz oscillating magnetic field generated by a circular coil outside the body. The coil has a radius of 15 cm and carries a current that varies as I(t) = I_0 sin(omega t) , with I_0 = 2.0 , text{A} . A smaller receiving coil, 2 cm in radius and 1 cm thick, lies 3 cm below the skin surface. The receiving coil is aligned parallel to the transmitting coil. Determine the maximum induced emf in the receiving coil due to the changing magnetic flux, assuming negligible mutual inductance losses and a skin depth sufficient to allow field penetration.

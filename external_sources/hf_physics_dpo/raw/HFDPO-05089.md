# HFDPO-05089

A team of astrophysicists detects a gamma-ray burst (GRB) from a distant galaxy, redshifted to 1.35 times its original frequency. The burst's intrinsic duration is measured as 0.8 seconds in the source frame. An Earth-based observatory records the burst as lasting 1.2 seconds. Using only the observed time dilation and frequency shift, determine the Lorentz factor of the source's relativistic outflow—then deduce the outflow’s speed as a fraction of *c*. Explain why the frequency shift alone cannot yield the answer, and assess the implications for the burst’s energy beaming model.

# HFDPO-06430

During a winter storm, a city’s water mains begin to freeze in sections where insulation failed. Engineers discover that a 2.5 m long segment of pipe, initially at −5°C, must be heated to 0°C and then fully melt the ice inside without overheating the pipe. The ice has a radius of 0.05 m, and the pipe is made of steel with a specific heat capacity of 450 J/kg·K and a density of 7800 kg/m³. The ice has a latent heat of fusion of 3.34 × 10⁵ J/kg and a density of 917 kg/m³. How much total energy is required to warm the pipe to 0°C and melt all the ice?

# HFDPO-15489

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpected efficiency drops under high solar irradiance. The active layer uses a 200 nm thick GaAs film with a refractive index of 3.6, sandwiched between transparent indium tin oxide electrodes. At 550 nm wavelength, interference patterns from multiple reflections cause destructive interference in the peak absorption region. Design a nanostructured anti-reflection coating (ARC) with two layers (SiO₂ and TiO₂) to maximize light trapping at 550 nm while minimizing reflectance across 500–600 nm. The coating must be deposited on a curved substrate (radius of curvature 5 cm) with surface roughness < 10 nm.

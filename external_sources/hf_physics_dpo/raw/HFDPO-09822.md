# HFDPO-09822

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial photovoltaic panels mounted at a 30° tilt, but power output drops by 18% during peak sunlight when glare from the sand reduces effective irradiance. The sand's surface reflects sunlight with strong linear polarization, and the panels are sensitive to polarization state. Given the reflectance of sand at 550 nm is 0.40 with a polarization degree of 0.65, and the panels have a polarization-dependent efficiency drop of 12% when incident light is p-polarized, what is the optimal angle of linear polarization for the panel’s anti-reflective coating to maximize power generation?

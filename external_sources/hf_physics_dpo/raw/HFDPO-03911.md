# HFDPO-03911

A quantum computing lab is testing a new superconducting qubit that operates at 15 mK in a dilution refrigerator. The qubit’s Josephson junction exhibits a critical current of 3.2 μA and a characteristic voltage of 2.5 μV when biased above the critical current. During a calibration run, the control circuit induces a 100 nA fluctuating current through the junction. The team observes unexpected phase slips causing decoherence. What is the maximum amplitude of the ac current that the junction can sustain without phase slips, assuming the junction’s capacitance is 50 fF and the Josephson relation governs dynamics?

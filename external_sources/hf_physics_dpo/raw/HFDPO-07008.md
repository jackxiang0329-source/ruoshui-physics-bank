# HFDPO-07008

During a power outage, a remote weather station relies on a solar panel charging a 12 V battery through a 50 m copper wire run to the battery bank. The panel outputs 15 A under peak sunlight, but the station’s data logger only functions properly if the voltage at the battery terminal stays above 11.6 V. Given the wire has a cross-sectional area of 4 mm² and resistivity of 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, is the voltage drop too great? What would happen if the same wire were replaced with one of the same length but with 1 mm² area?

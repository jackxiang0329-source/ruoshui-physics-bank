# HFDPO-16202

In a quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit is shielded within a cylindrical cryostat operating at 15 mK. The device exhibits unexpected microwave-induced phase drift, traced to eddy currents in the aluminum casing. Use Maxwell’s equations to model the non-stationary field penetration and analyze the dominant coupling mechanism.

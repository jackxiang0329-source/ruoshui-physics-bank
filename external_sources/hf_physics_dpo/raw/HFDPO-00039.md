# HFDPO-00039

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (NaNO₃/KNO₃ mixture) to store heat at 565°C for 12-hour operation after sunset. The storage tank is cylindrical (diameter 10 m, height 15 m), insulated with 30 cm of silica aerogel (k = 0.014 W/m·K). After a 4-hour night, the salt temperature drops to 540°C. Estimate the actual thermal conductivity of the insulation layer, assuming steady-state conduction and that the salt loses 2.3 GJ of energy over night. The tank is exposed to ambient air at 15°C with a convective heat transfer coefficient of 12 W/m²·K.

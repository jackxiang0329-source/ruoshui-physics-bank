# HFDPO-08193

A 12-meter-long, 800 kg composite bridge beam—used in a remote mountain village—supports a temporary scaffold at its midpoint, where a 450 kg worker and 200 kg of tools are positioned. The beam rests on two pivoting supports: one fixed at the left end, the other at 3 meters from the right end. The beam’s center of mass is 4.8 meters from the left. If the right support begins to slip due to poor friction and the system is on the verge of tipping, what is the minimum coefficient of static friction required at the left support to prevent rotation?

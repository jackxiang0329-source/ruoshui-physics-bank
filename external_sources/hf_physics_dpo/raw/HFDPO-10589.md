# HFDPO-10589

A geophysicist is analyzing magnetic anomalies near a remote volcanic island to detect hidden magma chambers. The island’s crust has a magnetized basalt layer with a uniform remnant magnetization of 1.2 A/m at an inclination of 60°. Using a drone-mounted fluxgate magnetometer flying at 100 m altitude, they measure a peak vertical component anomaly of 80 nT. What is the horizontal distance from the drone’s path to the center of the subsurface magnetized body, assuming it's a horizontal cylinder 2 km long and 500 m in diameter buried 1.5 km below the surface?

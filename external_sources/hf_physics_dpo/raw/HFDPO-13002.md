# HFDPO-13002

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research begins losing altitude during a routine flight over the Arctic. Engineers suspect a malfunction in the reaction wheel system, which maintains orientation by redistributing angular momentum. The drone’s total angular momentum must remain constant in the absence of external torques, but the wheels are spinning unexpectedly fast, causing the body to tilt. If the drone’s body has a moment of inertia of 1.2 kg·m² and the reaction wheel has a moment of inertia of 0.05 kg·m² initially spinning at 2000 rpm clockwise, what must the wheel’s new angular velocity be to stabilize the drone if the body rotates counterclockwise at 1.2 rad/s?

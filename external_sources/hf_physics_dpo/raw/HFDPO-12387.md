# HFDPO-12387

A space probe traveling at 0.8c relative to Earth sends a radio signal every 10 minutes (its own time) toward Earth. Due to relativistic time dilation and the Doppler effect, the signal intervals observed on Earth vary as the probe moves away. If the probe is currently 2.4 light-years from Earth, how many signals has Earth received by the time the probe is 3.2 light-years away, and what is the average time interval between received signals?

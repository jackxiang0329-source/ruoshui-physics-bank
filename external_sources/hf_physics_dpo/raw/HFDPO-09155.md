# HFDPO-09155

In a semiconductor fabrication facility, a batch of 1000 identical silicon wafers (diameter 300 mm, thickness 725 µm) are being transported through a vacuum chamber using electrostatic chucks. Each wafer accumulates a charge of ±1.5 nC due to triboelectric effects during handling. As the wafers pass near a grounded metal shield (width 10 cm, infinite in height), the electric field induces charge redistribution on the shield surface. What is the minimum distance between the wafer and shield to prevent dielectric breakdown (air breakdown field: 3 MV/m) while ensuring no net force exceeds 50 mN? The wafer’s charge is uniformly distributed on one face.

# HFDPO-11240

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden plasma jet impact at 15° above horizontal, ejecting 2.3 kg of ionized plasma at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s. The vessel, initially at rest, is mounted on magnetic bearings with negligible friction. After the impact, the vessel recoils and rotates due to torque from asymmetric momentum transfer. Given the vessel’s moment of inertia is 1.4 × 10⁴ kg·m², determine whether the total linear and angular momentum are conserved across the event, and evaluate if the observed recoil velocity and angular velocity (measured as 0.32 m/s and 0.082 rad/s) are physically plausible under ideal conservation laws.

# HFDPO-07922

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V battery bank is charged by a 200 W solar array during daylight, but during extended cloud cover, a backup 100 W diesel generator sustains a critical data logger (12 V, 1.5 A) and three sensor nodes (each 12 V, 250 mA). The wiring between the generator and devices is 50 m of copper wire (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m), using 10 AWG wire (cross-sectional area 5.26 mm²). The system fails intermittently when the generator operates—voltages at the logger drop below 11 V. Determine the minimum generator voltage required at its output terminals to maintain 11 V at the logger, assuming the generator has negligible internal resistance.

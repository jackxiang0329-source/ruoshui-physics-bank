# HFDPO-12997

During a desert expedition, a team uses a solar-powered water purifier that relies on blackened metal plates to absorb sunlight and evaporate brackish water. The plates have an area of 2.0 m², an emissivity of 0.95, and are initially at 25°C. Sunlight delivers 850 W/m², but 30% is reflected. If the system must evaporate 1.5 kg of water per hour to meet needs, and 40% of the absorbed energy is lost to convection and radiation, is the purifier sufficient? Assume water’s latent heat of vaporization is 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg.

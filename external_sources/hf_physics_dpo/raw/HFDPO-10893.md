# HFDPO-10893

A marine biologist is studying how underwater volcanic eruptions affect local marine life by measuring pressure waves in the ocean. During an eruption, a 4.2-second-long seismic pulse generates a cylindrical pressure wave that propagates radially outward from the vent at 1.5 km/s, with an initial amplitude of 12 kPa at the source. The ocean density is 1025 kg/m³ and the bulk modulus is 2.3 GPa. Due to dispersion and energy spreading, the wave’s amplitude decays as A(r) = frac{A_0}{sqrt{r}} , where r is distance from the vent. At what distance would the wave’s intensity fall below 10⁻⁴ W/m², the threshold for causing stress in fish otoliths?

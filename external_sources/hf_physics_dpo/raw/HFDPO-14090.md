# HFDPO-14090

A quantum dot solar cell operates at 300 K, with electrons confined in a 10 nm diameter spherical potential well. The device is designed to capture sunlight (blackbody radiation at 5800 K) but suffers from thermal excitation of electrons out of the conduction band, reducing efficiency. The density of states near the Fermi level is effectively constant at 2.5 × 10¹⁹ states/eV·cm³, and the band gap is 1.1 eV. Calculate the fraction of electrons thermally excited above the conduction band edge under solar illumination, and assess whether the thermal population exceeds the photogenerated carrier density given a solar flux of 1000 W/m² and a quantum efficiency of 85% at 1.1 eV. Could this thermal leakage limit practical device performance?

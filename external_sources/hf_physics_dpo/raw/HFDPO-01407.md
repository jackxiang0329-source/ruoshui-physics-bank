# HFDPO-01407

A farmer notices her electric fence charger keeps shutting off unexpectedly when a large metal tractor passes nearby. The charger uses a coil that generates a pulse of current to maintain the fence’s voltage. When the tractor moves past the coil at 2 m/s, a brief voltage spike of 4.8 V is induced. If the magnetic field strength is 0.15 T and the coil has 12 turns with a radius of 0.1 m, what is the rate of change of magnetic flux through the coil during the tractor’s passage?

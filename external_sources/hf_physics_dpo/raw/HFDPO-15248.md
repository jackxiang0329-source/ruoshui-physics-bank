# HFDPO-15248

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to extract heat from rock layers 3.2 km below the surface. A heat exchanger fluid is pumped down at 8.5°C and returns at 145°C. If the system delivers 2.4 MW of thermal power and the pump operates at 78% efficiency, what minimum mechanical power must the pump supply, and how much work is done per kilogram of fluid? Assume the fluid’s specific heat is 4.2 kJ/kg·K and ignore pressure changes in the borehole.

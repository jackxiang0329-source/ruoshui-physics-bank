# HFDPO-13195

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent system emits superheated fluid at 350°C, which flows through a narrow, porous basaltic chimney with a complex network of microscopic channels. The fluid’s resistivity varies with temperature and composition, modeled by ρ(T) = ρ₀ exp(α(T − T₀)), where ρ₀ = 1.2 Ω·m at T₀ = 25°C and α = 0.015 K⁻¹. A probe measures a total current of 4.8 A across a 2.0 m long, 0.8 m diameter chimney section, but the measured voltage drop is 2.4 V. However, the internal heating from current flow raises the average temperature significantly. Estimate the true current through the chimney using iterative resistance correction, and evaluate whether the measured voltage reflects ohmic behavior or requires a nonlinear model.

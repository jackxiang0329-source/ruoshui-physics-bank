A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and modeled as a thin rod rotating about one end, spins at 15 rpm in steady wind. During a sudden gust, the turbine's control system fails, causing the blade to decelerate uniformly and stop in 30 seconds. What is the magnitude of the angular acceleration, and how many revolutions does the blade complete during this deceleration?

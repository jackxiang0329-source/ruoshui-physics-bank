# HFDPO-10401

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and weighing 12,000 kg, is mounted at a 15° angle from horizontal on a vertical shaft. The center of mass is 25 m from the pivot. During a maintenance test, a worker applies a horizontal force at the blade tip to prevent rotation. What is the minimum force needed to maintain static equilibrium when the wind exerts a 45,000 N upward lift force at the center of pressure, 40 m from the pivot, and the shaft experiences a 30,000 N·m resisting torque?

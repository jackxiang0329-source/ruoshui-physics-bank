# HFDPO-10169

A deep-sea research submersible descends into the Mariana Trench, where hydrostatic pressure reaches 110 MPa at 11,000 meters. The sub’s spherical viewport has a 30 cm diameter and is made of a high-strength glass with a maximum tensile strength of 120 MPa. The interior is pressurized to 0.1 MPa (1 atm). Calculate the net inward force on the viewport and determine whether the material can withstand the stress without yielding, considering the geometry of a spherical shell and the distribution of pressure across the curved surface.

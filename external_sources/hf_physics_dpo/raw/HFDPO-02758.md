# HFDPO-02758

However, the surgeon reports inconsistent grip strength—sometimes too weak, sometimes too strong—even with the same pressure. The engineer suspects non-uniform deformation due to subtle changes in material properties across the actuator. What is the effective strain in the actuator segment, and how might localized variations in Young’s modulus (e.g., ±15%) affect the predicted elongation for a given pressure?

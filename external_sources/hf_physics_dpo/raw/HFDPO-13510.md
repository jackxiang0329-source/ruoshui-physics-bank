# HFDPO-13510

During a hurricane, a coastal power station's seawater intake pipe—20 cm in diameter and 15 m long—develops a critical vortex instability that risks drawing in debris and damaging pumps. Given the pipe inlet is submerged 6 m below the surface, ambient seawater density is 1025 kg/m³, and viscosity is 1.05 × 10⁻³ Pa·s, determine whether the vortex breakdown is caused by cavitation, inertial instabilities, or a resonance condition. Justify your conclusion using Reynolds number, Froude number, and the Strouhal number from vortex shedding data.

# HFDPO-08785

Over one hour, the instrument records 1,200 individual measurements, each at a different theta , uniformly distributed from 0° to 360°. The total detected signal is 67.5% of the expected maximum for pure linear polarization. What is the degree of polarization, and why does the measured value differ from ideal expectations?

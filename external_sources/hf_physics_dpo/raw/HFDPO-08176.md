# HFDPO-08176

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass spins at 30 rpm during peak gusts. Engineers notice the gearbox overheats after prolonged operation. The turbine’s 60-meter-long blades have a combined moment of inertia of 2.4 × 10⁷ kg·m², and the generator provides a constant opposing torque of 1.8 × 10⁶ N·m. If the wind suddenly drops to a steady 15 rpm, how long will it take for the blades to slow down, and will the gearbox remain safe if it can withstand up to 3000 N·m of torque during deceleration?

# HFDPO-00943

A wind turbine blade assembly on a remote mountain ridge experiences unexpectedly high stress at the root joint during a storm, despite being rated for 50 m/s winds. The 75-meter-long blade (mass 18,000 kg) is mounted at a 5° tilt, with its center of mass 32 m from the hub. At a 35 m/s wind gust, the aerodynamic lift force acts 40% of the way along the blade with a magnitude of 420 kN, perpendicular to the blade's length. What is the moment (torque) about the root joint, and does it exceed the design limit of 1.8 × 10⁷ N·m?

# HFDPO-09893

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical weather sensor array relies on a custom DC power distribution network to operate during 24-hour polar night. The system uses two identical 12 V batteries connected in series to power a 200 Ω sensor resistor via a 30 m copper wire (resistivity = 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) with variable cross-sectional area. Due to a manufacturing defect, the wire has a non-uniform diameter—0.5 mm at one end, tapering linearly to 1.5 mm at the other. Engineers notice the sensor receives only 10.2 V instead of the expected 12 V. What is the minimum wire diameter along the length that ensures the sensor voltage isn't below 11.5 V under full load?

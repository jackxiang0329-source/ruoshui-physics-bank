# HFDPO-11067

In a cleanroom for next-gen semiconductor manufacturing, a 5.0 cm × 5.0 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of +2.5 μC/m² is suspended horizontally by electrostatic forces between two parallel metal plates 10.0 mm apart. The upper plate has a fixed charge density of −3.0 μC/m². If the wafer’s mass is 2.0 g and air resistance is negligible, determine the vertical position (from the bottom plate) where the wafer remains in stable equilibrium. Assume the electric field between the plates is uniform everywhere except near the wafer, which distorts the field locally.

# HFDPO-00532

A small village in the Andes relies on a solar-powered heat engine to generate electricity for lighting and cooking. The system uses a parabolic dish to focus sunlight onto a receiver at 600°C, while the surrounding air stays at 20°C. The engine runs at 40% of the ideal Carnot efficiency. If the system needs to produce 500 W of useful power, how much thermal energy must it absorb per hour from the sun?

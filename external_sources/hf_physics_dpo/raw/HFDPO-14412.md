# HFDPO-14412

During a cold winter storm, a pilot notices frost forming rapidly on the wings of a small aircraft during a 20-minute climb through a cloud layer at -10°C. The wing surface is initially at -5°C, and the cloud consists of supercooled water droplets with a density of 0.4 g/m³. If the wing has a surface area of 12 m² and the latent heat of fusion for water is 334 kJ/kg, estimate how much mass of ice accumulates on the wing, assuming all droplets that contact the surface freeze immediately.

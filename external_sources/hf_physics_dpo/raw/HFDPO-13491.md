# HFDPO-13491

Using X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy, they detect a 0.8 nm oxide layer forming on the niobium surface. Explain how atomic-level electron configuration, surface oxidation states, and orbital hybridization influence the formation of this oxide layer and its impact on Cooper pair stability in the superconducting state.

# HFDPO-08905

A remote weather station in the Arctic uses a heat pump to maintain a stable internal temperature of 20°C during winter nights when the outside temperature drops to -30°C. The station’s solar panels can only generate 1.2 kW of power during the brief daylight hours. Engineers estimate the station loses heat at 150 W per degree of temperature difference. If the heat pump operates with 70% of the Carnot efficiency, what is the maximum heat output it can deliver during the 4-hour daylight period, and can it fully offset the nightly heat loss?

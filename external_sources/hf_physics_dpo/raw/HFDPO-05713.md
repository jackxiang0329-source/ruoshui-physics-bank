# HFDPO-05713

A satellite-based Earth observation system uses dual-polarized microwave radar to monitor deforestation in the Amazon. The radar emits signals at 10 GHz with horizontal polarization, but after scattering off dense canopy and ground, the received signal shows a 35% vertical polarization component. Given that the scatterers are primarily tree trunks (assumed perfectly conducting cylinders) and the ground has a dielectric constant of 12.5, determine the effective average angle of incidence at which the signal interacts with the canopy to produce this observed polarization ratio. Assume the radar is in a near-grazing geometry and use the Fresnel reflection coefficients and a model of random forest canopy orientation.

# HFDPO-15608

A coastal aquaculture farm uses submerged, cylindrical net pens to raise salmon in a region with strong tidal currents averaging 1.8 m/s. The nets are made of flexible mesh with a porosity of 65% and are mounted vertically with a diameter of 15 m and height of 12 m. During peak tides, the flow induces vortex shedding behind the structure, causing fatigue damage to the mooring system. Assume water density is 1025 kg/m³ and kinematic viscosity is 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ m²/s.

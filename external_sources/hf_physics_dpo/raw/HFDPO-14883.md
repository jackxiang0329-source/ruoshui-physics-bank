# HFDPO-14883

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic uses a 24 V battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During a prolonged polar night, the station relies on the battery, drawing 1.8 A average current. After 5 days, the battery voltage drops from 24.0 V to 21.6 V. The station’s monitoring system logs a rise in internal resistance. If the battery’s initial internal resistance was 0.2 Ω, determine the current capacity (Ah) that the battery must have to sustain operations for 7 days under these conditions, assuming the load resistance remains constant and the voltage decay is due solely to increasing internal resistance.

# HFDPO-13854

In a high-resolution satellite imaging system, sunlight reflecting off a desert surface creates intense glare that saturates the polarization-sensitive detectors. The surface is partially covered with a 1.2 m² photonic crystal layer that selectively transmits p-polarized light at 60° incidence. If the incident sunlight has an initial intensity of 1,000 W/m² and is unpolarized, and the crystal transmits 90% of p-polarized light while reflecting the rest, what is the net intensity of transmitted light, and how does the polarization state change after passing through the crystal?

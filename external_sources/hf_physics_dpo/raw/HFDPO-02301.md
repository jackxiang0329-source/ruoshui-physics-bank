# HFDPO-02301

A solar panel manufacturer notices that their new perovskite-based cells degrade rapidly under intense sunlight, losing efficiency in just weeks. Spectroscopy reveals that electron-hole pairs are recombining too quickly at defect sites in the crystal lattice. Given that the band gap is 1.6 eV and the average energy of thermal vibrations at operating temperature (85°C) is 0.04 eV, explain why defects near the valence band edge significantly reduce the cell’s performance, and calculate the minimum defect depth (in eV) below the conduction band that would allow thermal excitation to the conduction band to occur at 85°C.

# HFDPO-01443

A solar farm in Nevada uses a hybrid series-parallel array of 120 identical photovoltaic modules, each with a 30 V open-circuit voltage and internal resistance of 0.5 Ω. During a sudden dust storm, two rows experience partial shading, reducing their output to 18 V and increasing their effective resistance to 1.2 Ω. The system uses a central MPPT controller that maintains 240 V across the main bus. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current flowing through the shaded branch and explain why the unshaded modules might overheat if the controller doesn’t adjust.

# HFDPO-05861

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarimetric radiometer to measure microwave emissions from ocean surfaces, but interference from reflected signals off a nearby solar panel array—tilted at 37° to the horizontal—degrades data quality. The solar panels are coated with a dielectric material (n = 1.5) and partially polarized due to Brewster’s angle reflection. What polarization state (in terms of Stokes parameters) must the radiometer detect to effectively isolate the ocean's true signal, and how should its antenna polarization be aligned to reject the solar panel interference? Assume incident radiation is unpolarized and the observation angle is 45° from the normal to the ocean surface.

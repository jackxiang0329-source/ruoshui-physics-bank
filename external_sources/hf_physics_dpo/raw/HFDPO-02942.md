# HFDPO-02942

A quantum dot solar cell arrays use nanoscale semiconductor particles arranged in a periodic lattice to enhance light absorption through constructive interference. The array is designed to maximize absorption at a target wavelength of 680 nm (red light), with a lattice spacing of 420 nm. However, experimental data shows a significant drop in efficiency at 620 nm (orange light), despite the structure being optimized for 680 nm. Given that the incident light strikes the array at a 30° angle, determine the effective diffraction order responsible for the destructive interference at 620 nm and propose a structural modification to minimize this loss.

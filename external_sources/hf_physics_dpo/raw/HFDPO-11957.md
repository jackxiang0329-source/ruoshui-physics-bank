# HFDPO-11957

A coastal city is evaluating a new storm surge barrier system designed to protect its harbor during hurricanes. The barrier includes a series of vertical vents that open during high water to prevent pressure buildup. During a test, engineers observe that water rises 0.8 m above the normal sea level in a vent tube connected to a horizontal intake pipe (diameter 1.2 m). If the incoming water speed at the intake is 3.5 m/s and the vent tube has a diameter of 0.15 m, what is the height difference between the water surface in the vent and the sea level outside, assuming ideal flow and atmospheric pressure is equal at both ends?

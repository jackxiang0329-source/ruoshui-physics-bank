# HFDPO-06907

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 300°C, which then transfers heat to water in a steam generator. On a sunny day, the oil flows at 1.2 kg/s, entering at 250°C and exiting at 285°C. The steam generator operates with a 90% heat transfer efficiency. If the system must generate 5 MW of power and the steam turbine has a 40% thermodynamic efficiency, what is the minimum mass flow rate of steam required? Assume steam is produced at 500°C and 10 MPa, and the condenser operates at 40°C.

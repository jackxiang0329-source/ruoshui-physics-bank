# HFDPO-04223

A research team is developing a biocompatible neural interface using a flexible graphene mesh implanted in the cortex of a rat model. The mesh contains 10,000 interconnected microelectrodes, each with a variable resistivity due to inhomogeneous graphene deposition. During calibration, they observe that 15% of electrodes exhibit a resistance 3.2× higher than expected, disrupting signal fidelity. Given the power supply provides 5.0 V and a maximum allowable current of 2.0 µA per electrode to prevent tissue damage, what is the minimum acceptable resistance for each electrode, and how does the network’s effective resistance change if the high-resistance electrodes are bypassed via redundant conductive pathways?

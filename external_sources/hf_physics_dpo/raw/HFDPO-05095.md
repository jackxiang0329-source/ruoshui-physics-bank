# HFDPO-05095

A solar-powered streetlight in a remote village uses a 12 V battery and a LED that draws 0.3 A when on. After a week of cloudy weather, the battery is only at 8 V. The LED won’t turn on if the voltage drops below 10 V. What resistance must be added in series to safely drop the voltage to exactly 10 V while maintaining the LED’s operating current?

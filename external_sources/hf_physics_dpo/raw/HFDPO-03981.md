# HFDPO-03981

A biomedical engineer is designing a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner that uses a rapidly oscillating magnetic field to excite hydrogen nuclei in human tissue. The scanner generates a sinusoidal magnetic field with amplitude 1.5 T and frequency 63.8 MHz within a cylindrical bore 60 cm in diameter. To ensure patient safety, the induced electric field in tissue must not exceed 10 V/m. Using Maxwell’s equations, determine whether Faraday’s law alone is sufficient to assess safety, and calculate the maximum allowable rate of change of the magnetic field that keeps the induced electric field below threshold.

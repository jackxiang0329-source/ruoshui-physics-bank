# HFDPO-10149

A lunar research station uses a network of solar panels and rechargeable batteries to power life-support systems. During a 48-hour eclipse cycle, the station relies on a complex mesh of interconnected batteries (each 12 V, internal resistance 0.3 Ω) and resistive loads (including CO₂ scrubbers, heaters, and communication relays). Engineers observe a 1.8 V voltage drop across a critical junction when all systems are active. Given that three battery branches feed into a central node with resistances of 0.8 Ω, 1.2 Ω, and 1.0 Ω respectively, and the total current entering the junction is 15 A, determine the current distribution and identify which battery is under stress due to excessive discharge.

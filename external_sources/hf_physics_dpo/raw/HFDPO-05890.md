# HFDPO-05890

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland extracts hot water from a 2.5 km deep borehole, where the rock temperature is 320°C. The rig pumps 120 kg/s of water from the reservoir to the surface at 180°C, overcoming 3.2 MPa hydrostatic pressure. If the surface water is cooled to 40°C in a closed-loop heat exchanger before being reinjected, and 18% of the energy is lost to heat dissipation during transport, what is the net electrical power output if the conversion efficiency from thermal energy to electricity is 15%?

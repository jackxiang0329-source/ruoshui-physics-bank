# HFDPO-04795

A deep-space solar probe orbits a distant star, shielded from external fields by a conductive, spherical Faraday cage with radius 3.2 m. During a solar flare, charged particles accumulate on the cage's outer surface, creating a non-uniform charge density: σ(θ) = σ₀(1 + 2cosθ), where σ₀ = 1.8 × 10⁻⁹ C/m² and θ is the polar angle. Determine the electric field at a point just inside the cage’s inner surface, and explain why the field configuration still satisfies Gauss’s Law despite the asymmetry.

# HFDPO-13119

A lunar rover on Mars encounters a steep, loose-sand slope (angle = 28°) during a sandstorm. Its front wheels lock while descending at 1.2 m/s, creating a skid. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the tires and sand is 0.35, but the sand’s internal resistance increases nonlinearly with depth, modeled as a resistive force F_res = 0.8v² (in N), where v is velocity in m/s. The rover mass is 220 kg, and its center of mass is 0.7 m high, 1.4 m behind the front axle. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction required at the rear wheels to prevent rear-wheel lift-off during braking?

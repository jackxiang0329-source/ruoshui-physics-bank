# HFDPO-09632

During a winter storm, a city’s emergency team discovers that salt (NaCl) is being spread on roads more slowly than expected—only 50% of the salt is dissolving into the brine layer needed to lower the freezing point of ice. The ambient temperature is −8°C, and the salt is being applied at a rate of 120 kg per 100 m². Given that the eutectic point of NaCl–water is −21.1°C and the solubility limit is 26.4 wt% at 0°C, explain why the salt is not fully dissolving and determine the minimum additional mass of salt (in kg per 100 m²) required to ensure complete dissolution and effective ice melting.

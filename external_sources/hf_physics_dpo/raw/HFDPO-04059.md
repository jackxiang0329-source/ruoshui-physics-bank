# HFDPO-04059

A deep-space communication satellite orbiting a distant exoplanet at 0.6c must relay data to Earth using a laser link. The satellite’s onboard clock is synchronized with Earth’s atomic time standard before launch, but due to relativistic time dilation, signals sent at regular 1-second intervals (as measured on the satellite) arrive at Earth at irregular intervals. If Earth receives 1000 signals over a 1000-second window in its own frame, what is the true duration of the signal transmission period as measured on the satellite? Assume the satellite’s velocity is constant and the distance is large enough that Doppler shift dominates the observed rate.

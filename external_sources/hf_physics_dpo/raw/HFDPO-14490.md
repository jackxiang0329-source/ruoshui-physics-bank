# HFDPO-14490

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a 12 V DC battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During winter, the array produces only 6 W of power on average, yet the station’s sensors and transmitter draw 1.2 A continuously. A researcher proposes replacing the 12 V system with a 24 V system to reduce resistive losses in the 100 m copper wiring, but the existing 12 V sensors must still be powered. How should the power distribution be reconfigured—using a buck converter, a voltage divider, or a separate 24 V to 12 V regulated supply—and what is the minimum efficiency required for the chosen method to ensure the battery is not drained over 24 hours?

# HFDPO-10913

A deep-space communication array on Mars detects a faint, pulsed signal from a drifting alien probe near the asteroid belt—its frequency drifting due to relativistic Doppler shift. The signal’s original carrier wave was 12.5 GHz, but the received pulse has a varying frequency between 12.48 GHz and 12.52 GHz over 5 minutes. The probe is moving at a constant speed relative to Mars, and its signal is polarized linearly, but the receiving antenna is misaligned by 30°. Estimate the probe’s speed and direction relative to Mars, and determine the net signal strength reduction due to both Doppler shift and polarization mismatch.

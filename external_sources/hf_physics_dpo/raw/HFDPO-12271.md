# HFDPO-12271

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses a novel tandem photovoltaic system where two different semiconductor layers (perovskite and silicon) are stacked to capture a broader spectrum of sunlight. During peak irradiance, the perovskite layer generates 12 A at 1.2 V, while the silicon layer produces 10 A at 0.6 V. The layers are connected in series through a shared metallic interconnect with a resistivity of 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m and a cross-sectional area of 2.0 mm². The interconnect spans 5.0 m between the layers. If the system must deliver 90% of the total power to the grid with minimal thermal degradation, what is the optimal thickness of the interconnect to limit resistive losses to under 5% of total output power?

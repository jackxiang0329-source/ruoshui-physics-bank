# HFDPO-01083

During a high-altitude balloon experiment, a spherical capacitor made of ultra-thin conductive polymer film (radius 0.5 m, thickness 50 nm) is used to store energy for a telemetry burst. The balloon ascends rapidly, causing the atmospheric pressure to drop from 101 kPa to 25 kPa and the temperature to fall from 25°C to –40°C. The capacitor is initially charged to 5 kV with a dielectric constant of 3.2 in air (ε₀ = 8.85×10⁻¹² F/m). What is the final voltage across the capacitor after the environmental change, assuming no charge leakage and that the dielectric remains intact?

# HFDPO-01476

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpected efficiency drops under specific sunlight angles. The device uses a 200 nm thick silicon nitride anti-reflective coating (n = 2.0) on a Si substrate (n = 3.5), designed for normal incidence at 550 nm. However, at 30° incidence, measurements show a 15% reduction in transmitted power. Determine whether interference effects in the coating are responsible, and if so, calculate the effective phase shift and coherence length required to explain the observed drop.

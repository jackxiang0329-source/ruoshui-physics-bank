# HFDPO-10421

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 390°C. The oil flows at 1.2 kg/s through a 150 m long tube with a 0.1 m diameter. After transferring heat, the oil exits at 310°C. Engineers notice that the system loses 5% of its input energy to the surroundings due to imperfect insulation. What is the actual power delivered to the working fluid, and how does this affect the required solar concentration ratio if the plant must maintain 10 MW of useful thermal output?

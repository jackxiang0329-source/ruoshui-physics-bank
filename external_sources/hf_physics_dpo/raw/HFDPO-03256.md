# HFDPO-03256

In a lunar mining operation, a photovoltaic array using perovskite-silicon tandem cells is deployed on the Moon’s south pole, where sunlight varies from 12 hours of continuous daylight to 14 days of darkness. Analysis shows no mechanical damage or dust accumulation. What physical mechanism, involving carrier dynamics and interface recombination, could explain this degradation, and how would you modify the cell design to restore efficiency during long lunar nights?

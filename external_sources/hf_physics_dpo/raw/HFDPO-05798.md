# HFDPO-05798

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where deuterium and tritium nuclei collide at high temperatures, producing helium nuclei and neutrons. The reactor’s magnetic confinement system must maintain energy balance as 80% of the fusion energy is carried away by fast neutrons, which escape the plasma and are absorbed by a lithium blanket. If the blanket converts 90% of the neutron energy into thermal energy and the plant must generate 1.5 GW of electrical power with 35% efficiency, what minimum neutron flux (neutrons/m²/s) must the blanket sustain to meet this demand, assuming each fusion reaction releases 17.6 MeV and 1 MeV = 1.602 × 10⁻¹³ J?

# HFDPO-04741

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue manipulation in minimally invasive surgery. The gripper’s tip is made of a hyperelastic silicone polymer with a strain energy function modeled by the Mooney-Rivlin form: W = C_1(I_1 - 3) + C_2(I_2 - 3) , where C_1 = 120 text{ kPa}, C_2 = 60 text{ kPa} . During testing, the tip is subjected to a uniaxial extension of 40% strain, but due to a design flaw, the lateral constraint is partially removed, allowing some lateral relaxation. The surgeon reports inconsistent grip stability. Determine the actual average axial stress in the material under these non-ideal boundary conditions, assuming the material remains incompressible and the response is governed by the second Piola-Kirchhoff stress. How does this change affect the effective stiffness compared to a fully constrained case?

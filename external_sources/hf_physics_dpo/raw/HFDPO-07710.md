# HFDPO-07710

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (10 cm diameter) operates at 13.56 MHz with a resistance of 2 Ω and inductance of 10 μH. The receiver coil (5 cm diameter) inside the body has 1.5 Ω resistance and 3.2 μH inductance. The mutual inductance between coils is 0.8 μH, and the system must deliver 5 mW to the receiver with >70% efficiency. At what phase angle must the transmitter voltage be adjusted relative to current to maximize power transfer, and what is the required source voltage amplitude?

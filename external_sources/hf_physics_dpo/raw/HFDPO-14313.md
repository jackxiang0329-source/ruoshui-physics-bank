# HFDPO-14313

In a next-generation photovoltaic cell designed for space satellites, a heterojunction interface between amorphous silicon (a-Si) and crystalline silicon (c-Si) exhibits unexpected efficiency drops under prolonged exposure to high-energy proton radiation. Measured carrier recombination rates increase by 40% after 100 keV proton irradiation at 10¹² cm⁻² fluence, despite no observable structural damage. Determine whether this degradation is dominated by radiative recombination at defect states within the bandgap or by Auger recombination amplified by charge trapping. Given: initial minority carrier lifetime τ₀ = 1.2 μs, post-irradiation τ = 0.72 μs; carrier concentration n = p = 10¹⁵ cm⁻³; effective density of states N_c = N_v = 10¹⁹ cm⁻³; intrinsic carrier concentration n_i = 10¹⁰ cm⁻³.

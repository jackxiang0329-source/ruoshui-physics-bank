# HFDPO-02457

In a semiconductor fabrication facility, electrostatic charging during photolithography causes pattern misalignment on silicon wafers, reducing yield. A 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform surface charge density of σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r/R), where σ₀ = 5 × 10⁻⁹ C/m² and R = 7.5 cm. The wafer is suspended in a vacuum chamber with a grounded metal shield 2 mm above it. What is the magnitude and direction of the net electrostatic force on the wafer due to its own charge distribution interacting with the induced image charge in the shield?

# HFDPO-11455

A wildlife biologist uses a handheld laser rangefinder to measure the distance to a rare bird perched on a branch 12 meters above the forest floor. The device emits a 532 nm green laser pulse that reflects off the bird’s wing and returns. However, due to a sudden thermal inversion layer, the air’s refractive index varies with height: n(h) = 1.00030 – 1.2×10⁻⁵ h (where h is in meters). The laser beam bends as it travels upward and downward. What is the actual horizontal distance between the biologist and the bird if the rangefinder reads 15.2 meters?

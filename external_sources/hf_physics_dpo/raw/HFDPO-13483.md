# HFDPO-13483

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a network of high-pressure steam pipes made of a titanium alloy with a Young’s modulus of 110 GPa and a yield strength of 900 MPa. During a sudden pressure surge, one pipe segment experiences a strain of 0.0035, causing a visible bulge. Engineers must determine whether the pipe is permanently deformed or if it will rebound elastically when pressure normalizes. The pipe's original length is 12 m, and the bulge increases its length by 42 mm. Is the material still within its elastic limit? If not, what is the irreversible elongation?

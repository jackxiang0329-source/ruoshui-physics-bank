# HFDPO-11081

A solar-powered rover on Mars relies on a custom-designed photovoltaic array made of thousands of interconnected silicon cells, each with a resistivity of 2.3 × 10⁻⁵ Ω·m and dimensions 1.2 cm × 1.2 cm × 0.05 cm. Due to dust accumulation, the effective cross-sectional area of each cell decreases over time. After 90 sols (Martian days), measurements show a 12% drop in total output current under identical sunlight intensity. What is the equivalent thickness of dust layer (assuming uniform, non-conductive coating) that would account for this resistance increase? Assume the original current was 1.8 A, and use a standard solar irradiance of 590 W/m².

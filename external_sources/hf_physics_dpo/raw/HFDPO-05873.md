# HFDPO-05873

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical weather sensor array powered by a 12 V solar-charged battery bank fails during a 72-hour polar night. The array uses a network of three identical thermistors (nonlinear resistors) in a Wheatstone bridge configuration to detect temperature differentials, but one thermistor is exposed to a sudden ice buildup, increasing its resistance by 30% due to frozen moisture. The bridge’s output voltage to a digital readout drops from 5.0 V to 2.1 V. Given that the other three resistors are fixed at 1000 Ω and the power supply is stable, determine the original resistance of each thermistor and explain why the system fails to detect a 3°C temperature change.

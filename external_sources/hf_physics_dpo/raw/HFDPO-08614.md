# HFDPO-08614

In a microfluidic chip designed for rapid COVID-19 antigen testing, a droplet of saliva (μ = 1.2 mPa·s, ρ = 1020 kg/m³) flows through a 50 μm wide, 200 μm long rectangular channel at a volumetric flow rate of 0.5 nL/s. The channel walls exhibit slight surface roughness that induces a non-Newtonian slip effect modeled by a velocity jump at the boundary. Determine the pressure drop required to maintain this flow, accounting for both the viscous shear in the fluid and the slip length effect (assumed constant at 100 nm).

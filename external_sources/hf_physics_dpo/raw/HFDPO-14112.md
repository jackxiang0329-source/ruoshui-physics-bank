# HFDPO-14112

A high-speed maglev train traveling at 520 km/h enters a curved section of track with a variable radius of curvature that decreases from 8.5 km to 3.2 km over a 1.8 km distance. The train’s mass is 420,000 kg, and its lateral acceleration must not exceed 0.4g to ensure passenger comfort and safety. Design a real-time control strategy to adjust the magnetic levitation forces to maintain stability during the curve, considering both inertial and electromagnetic response delays of 120 ms. At what point along the curve does the system first risk exceeding the acceleration limit?

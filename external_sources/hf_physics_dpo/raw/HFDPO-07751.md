# HFDPO-07751

A wind turbine blade in a coastal wind farm is designed with a curved airfoil shape, but engineers notice that during high winds (15 m/s), the blade experiences unexpected vibrations and reduced efficiency. They suspect that the pressure differential due to airflow speed changes may be causing localized flow separation. Given that the air speed above the blade’s curved upper surface reaches 22 m/s and the density of air is 1.2 kg/m³, what is the pressure difference between the upper and lower surfaces of the blade? Assume the air below the blade moves at 15 m/s and the flow is steady and incompressible.

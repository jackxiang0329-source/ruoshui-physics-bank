# HFDPO-04232

A deep-space probe en route to Jupiter uses a gravity assist from Venus, but due to a sensor glitch, the spacecraft’s closest approach distance to Venus’s surface is unknowingly 200 km less than planned. With Venus’s mass at 4.87 × 10²⁴ kg, radius 6,052 km, and the probe’s incoming asymptotic velocity relative to Venus at 11.2 km/s at a hyperbolic excess speed of 7.8 km/s, what is the actual change in the probe’s heliocentric velocity after the flyby, and by how much does the trajectory deviation (in degrees) differ from the nominal path? Assume Venus orbits the Sun at 35 km/s.

# HFDPO-11807

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses superheated steam (600°C, 10 MPa) extracted from a fractured basalt reservoir to drive a turbine. After expansion, the steam enters a condenser at 50°C, where it's cooled by a closed-loop brine system (specific heat 3.5 kJ/kg·K) flowing at 120 kg/s. The brine enters at 25°C and exits at 45°C. The condenser is a shell-and-tube heat exchanger with 500 tubes, each 1.8 m long and 1.2 cm in diameter. During peak load, the plant experiences a 12% drop in brine flow rate due to pump malfunction. Estimate the new condenser exit temperature of the steam assuming no change in steam mass flow and ideal heat transfer. How does this affect the turbine’s efficiency?

# HFDPO-02804

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland detects elevated neutron background levels deep underground near a magma chamber. Engineers suspect a natural nuclear chain reaction occurred 2 million years ago in uranium-rich rock, and now the rock contains 0.7% U-235 (natural abundance). If the current neutron flux is 4.2 × 10⁴ neutrons/cm²/s and the macroscopic absorption cross-section for U-235 is 1000 barns, what is the effective multiplication factor (k_eff) of the rock formation, assuming a homogeneous medium and negligible neutron leakage?

# HFDPO-01704

A Mars rover’s drill bit, designed to penetrate regolith at 120 rpm, suddenly jams due to a buried rock fragment. The motor, rated at 25 W, continues applying torque while the bit spins down—measured to take 4.7 seconds to stop. Given the bit’s moment of inertia is 0.085 kg·m² and the angular deceleration is not uniform due to variable torque from the jam, estimate the total energy dissipated in the jam and the average torque exerted during deceleration.

# HFDPO-04619

During a winter storm, a city's water pipes begin to freeze, causing burst pipes. A technician inspects a 3 m section of copper pipe filled with water at 0°C and notices ice forming on the inner walls. If the pipe has a diameter of 10 cm and the ice layer reaches 1 cm thick, how much heat (in kJ) must be removed from this section of pipe to fully freeze the remaining liquid water? Assume the ice forms uniformly and no heat is lost to the surroundings after freezing begins.

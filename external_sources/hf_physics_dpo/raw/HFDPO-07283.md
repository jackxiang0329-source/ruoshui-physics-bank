# HFDPO-07283

In a next-generation lithium-ion battery manufacturing line, charged dust particles (diameter ~5 μm, density 2.5 g/cm³) are accumulating on freshly coated electrodes during the drying stage, causing micro-short circuits. The drying chamber operates under a uniform electric field of 12 kV/m to control particle motion, but particles still deposit unevenly. Given that the particles acquire a charge of ±1.6×10⁻¹⁸ C due to triboelectric charging with the air stream, determine the minimum electric field strength required to fully suspend the particles against gravity while minimizing field-induced electrode polarization effects.

# HFDPO-02653

A quantum dot solar cell operates at 300 K, with electrons confined in a 2D semiconductor layer. The density of states near the Fermi level follows a power law, g(E) propto sqrt{E - E_F} , and the device is tuned so that the Fermi energy lies 0.1 eV below the conduction band minimum. Due to thermal fluctuations, the electron distribution deviates from the ideal Fermi-Dirac form. What is the dominant contribution to the excess entropy (beyond the standard Sommerfeld expansion) when the system absorbs 1 meV of thermal energy from the lattice?

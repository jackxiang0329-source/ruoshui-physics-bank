# HFDPO-00307

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a precisely shaped, conducting toroidal shell with inner radius 1.2 m and outer radius 1.5 m to shield against intense electric fields from ionized plasma. During startup, the plasma develops a non-uniform charge density that varies radially as ρ(r) = ρ₀·e⁻(r−1.3)/0.1 (in C/m³), where ρ₀ = 2×10⁻⁹ C/m³ and r is in meters. Engineers must determine the total flux through a Gaussian surface just outside the outer wall to verify insulation integrity. What is the net electric flux through this surface, in N·m²/C?

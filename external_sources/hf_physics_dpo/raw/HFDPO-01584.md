# HFDPO-01584

In a quantum dot solar cell, 1000 electrons are confined in a 2D semiconductor lattice with a tunable energy gap of 1.8 eV. At operating temperature (T = 300 K), the system exhibits strong electron-electron interactions that shift the Fermi level. A researcher measures a charge density of 2.5×10¹² cm⁻² and observes a sharp rise in conductivity at a critical voltage. Determine the effective temperature of the electron system when the chemical potential reaches the conduction band edge, assuming degenerate Fermi-Dirac statistics and a parabolic band structure with an effective mass of 0.067mₑ.

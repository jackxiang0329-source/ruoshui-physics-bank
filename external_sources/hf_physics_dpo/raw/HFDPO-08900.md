# HFDPO-08900

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 200 m long, 0.5 m diameter copper coil suspended in a hot, conductive brine reservoir at 250°C. The brine's natural convection creates a time-varying magnetic field of frequency 0.2 Hz and peak amplitude 50 μT due to turbulent, diamagnetically opposed flow. The coil has 1000 turns, resistance 12 Ω, and is connected to a superconducting energy storage system. Estimate the peak induced EMF and the average power dissipated in the coil over one cycle, considering that the magnetic field’s orientation fluctuates randomly within a 30° cone. How might asymmetry in the brine flow affect the net power output despite symmetric coil geometry?

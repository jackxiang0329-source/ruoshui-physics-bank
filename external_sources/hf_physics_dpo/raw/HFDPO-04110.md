# HFDPO-04110

A quantum imaging lab develops a chip-scale entangled photon source using spontaneous parametric down-conversion in a periodically poled lithium niobate waveguide, emitting photon pairs at 780 nm and 1560 nm with a spectral bandwidth of 1.2 nm. To preserve quantum correlations for high-fidelity ghost imaging, the system must maintain a spectral overlap of at least 85% between the two photons. However, temperature fluctuations in the lab cause a 0.8 nm shift in the 780 nm signal. Calculate the required temperature stabilization precision (in °C) to maintain the spectral overlap, assuming a thermo-optic coefficient of 1.1×10⁻⁵ /°C and a dispersion length of 4.3 mm.

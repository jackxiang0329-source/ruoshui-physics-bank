# HFDPO-01167

During a lunar mining operation, a robotic drill extracts regolith from a 15° incline on the Moon’s surface, ejecting material at 8 m/s relative to the drill. The drill, mounted on a frictionless sled, has a mass of 120 kg and initially carries 80 kg of regolith. As mining proceeds, regolith is expelled continuously at 5 kg/s. If the sled must maintain a constant velocity of 0.4 m/s relative to the lunar surface, what thrust must the drill’s propulsion system generate, and how does conservation of momentum constrain the system’s design under variable mass?

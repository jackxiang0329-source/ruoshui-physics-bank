# HFDPO-08659

A solar-powered desalination plant in a remote coastal village uses a parabolic mirror to focus sunlight onto a blackened copper tube (diameter 4 cm, length 2 m) containing seawater. Given the latent heat of vaporization of water is 2.4 MJ/kg and the tube’s emissivity is 0.95, determine the average surface temperature of the tube.

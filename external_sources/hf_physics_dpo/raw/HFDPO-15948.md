# HFDPO-15948

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoscale semiconductor particles to absorb sunlight, but efficiency drops significantly when the dots are too close together due to electron tunneling between them. If two identical quantum dots are separated by 2 nm and each has a potential barrier of 1.8 eV, what is the probability that an electron with energy 1.5 eV tunnels from one dot to the other? Assume a rectangular barrier and use typical effective mass for electrons in the material.

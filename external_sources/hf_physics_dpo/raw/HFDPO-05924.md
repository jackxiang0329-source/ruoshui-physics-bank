# HFDPO-05924

A medical physicist is optimizing a proton therapy treatment for a deep-seated tumor near the spinal cord. The beam must deliver 2 Gy to the tumor while limiting dose to surrounding tissue to <0.5 Gy. Proton beams lose energy gradually until a sharp increase near the end of their range—this Bragg peak occurs at 18 cm depth in tissue. If the beam enters at 15 cm depth and the tumor spans 2 cm (at 18–20 cm), what energy must the protons have to just reach the start of the tumor, and how does the dose distribution change if the beam energy is increased by 10%?

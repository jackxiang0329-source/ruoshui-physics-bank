# HFDPO-08291

A deep-space probe near Jupiter’s moon Europa is attempting to land on a frozen surface with a subsurface ocean. To stabilize its descent, the probe must adjust its altitude using small thrusters that counteract Europa’s gravitational pull. Given Europa's mass (4.8 × 10²² kg), radius (1.56 × 10⁶ m), and the probe’s current altitude (100 km above the surface), what must the probe’s thrust (in newtons) be to maintain a constant 5 m/s descent rate, assuming the probe’s mass is 350 kg and atmospheric drag is negligible?

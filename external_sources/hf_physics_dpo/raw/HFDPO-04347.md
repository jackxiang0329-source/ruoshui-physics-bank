# HFDPO-04347

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a high-temperature superconducting (HTS) toroidal field coil cooled to 77 K with liquid nitrogen. During a sudden power surge, a local fault causes a transient heat pulse of 1.2 MW/m³ across a 0.5 mm-wide region of the superconductor.

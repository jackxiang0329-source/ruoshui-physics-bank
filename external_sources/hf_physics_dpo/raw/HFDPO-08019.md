# HFDPO-08019

A geothermal energy company in Iceland is drilling into a volcanic rift zone where rock deformation is critical to reservoir stability. At 3.2 km depth, a cylindrical borehole encounters a highly fractured basalt layer with a Young’s modulus of 58 GPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.24. During drilling, a sudden pressure drop causes the surrounding rock to expand radially by 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ m/m. The borehole’s diameter is 0.2 m, and the elastic stress state must be maintained to prevent fracturing. Determine the magnitude and direction of the in-situ horizontal stress component that must be present to balance the radial expansion, assuming the rock behaves as a linear elastic continuum with isotropic properties.

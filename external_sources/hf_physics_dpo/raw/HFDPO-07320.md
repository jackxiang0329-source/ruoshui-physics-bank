# HFDPO-07320

A solar panel array in a desert region experiences frequent dust accumulation, reducing efficiency. Engineers discover that the panels develop a negative charge due to triboelectric effects from wind-blown sand, attracting more charged dust particles. If the panels are 2.0 m × 1.5 m and hold a uniform surface charge density of –3.0 × 10⁻⁸ C/m², what is the magnitude of the electric field just above the panel surface, and how much charge would a typical 0.1 mm diameter dust particle (mass 2.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ kg) need to have to remain suspended against gravity? Assume the dust is polarized and experiences an induced dipole force, but estimate the necessary charge assuming a uniform field approximation.

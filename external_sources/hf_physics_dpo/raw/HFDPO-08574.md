# HFDPO-08574

A biomedical engineer is designing a portable optical sensor to detect early-stage cancer by measuring the scattering signature of tissue. The sensor uses a 633 nm laser and measures scattered light at 45° from the incident beam. In healthy tissue, the scattering follows Rayleigh-like behavior, but in tumor regions, increased nuclear density shifts the scattering spectrum. The engineer measures a relative intensity drop of 18% at 45° compared to a reference. What effective particle radius (in nm) does this suggest for the scattering structures in the abnormal tissue, assuming spherical inhomogeneities and Mie scattering with refractive index contrast of 1.40 vs. 1.33?

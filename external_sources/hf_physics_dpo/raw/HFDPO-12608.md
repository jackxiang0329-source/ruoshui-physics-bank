# HFDPO-12608

The team suspects this glare arises from a combination of specular reflection at the ice-air interface and scattering from brine inclusions. Using the Fresnel equations and a model of a two-layer ice layer (air → ice → brine pockets), determine the optimal orientation of the sensor's polarization filter to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio, assuming the incoming sunlight is unpolarized and strikes the ice at 52°.

# HFDPO-07051

A lunar greenhouse on Mars uses a closed-loop thermal regulation system where heat from crew metabolism and solar gain must be dissipated through radiators to maintain a stable internal temperature. The system operates with a net heat input of 8.2 kW, and the radiators, coated with high-emissivity material (ε = 0.94), are exposed to a cold sink of 120 K (surface temperature). The radiators, however, are not perfectly blackbody due to dust accumulation (effective emissivity drops to 0.78), and their surface area is constrained to 15 m². Can the system maintain thermal equilibrium under these conditions? If not, what design modification—increasing area, improving emissivity, or adding a heat pump—would be most effective, and why?

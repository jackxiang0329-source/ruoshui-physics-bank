# HFDPO-09714

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanostructure with 200 nm spacing between identical emitters to enhance light absorption via constructive interference. When illuminated by 600 nm sunlight at normal incidence, measurements show a sharp peak in absorbance at 45°, but no peak at 30°. Explain the discrepancy using the diffraction grating equation, considering both the interference condition and the limited number of emitters (N = 100). Why does the 45° peak appear despite the absence of a 30° peak, and what is the effective order of diffraction?

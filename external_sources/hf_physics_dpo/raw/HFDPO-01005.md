# HFDPO-01005

A quantum optics lab designs a custom photonic crystal sensor to detect trace atmospheric NO₂ using the shift in diffraction patterns of near-infrared light (λ = 780 nm) reflected from a 2D lattice of silicon nanocylinders. The lattice spacing is 850 nm, and the sensor operates at normal incidence. When exposed to 10 ppm NO₂, the effective refractive index of the air within the lattice increases by 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ due to molecular adsorption. The challenge: predict the angular shift in the first-order diffraction peak and determine whether this shift is detectable with a spectrometer having 0.03° resolution.

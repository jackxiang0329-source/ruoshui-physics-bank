# HFDPO-08028

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km into the Earth’s crust, where temperatures exceed 300°C. To extract energy, a working fluid (a mixture of water and ethanol) is pumped downward at 0.8 m/s through a 15 cm diameter pipe. As it heats up, the fluid expands and rises, driving a turbine. The system operates at steady state, but friction in the pipe causes a pressure drop of 1.2 MPa over the depth. What is the net energy available per kilogram of fluid at the turbine inlet, accounting for pump work, heat gain, and friction losses?

# HFDPO-13706

A fusion reactor prototype using high-temperature superconducting (HTS) tape coils in a tokamak configuration experiences sudden quenching during plasma startup, with temperature spikes in the cryostat exceeding 20 K above the critical temperature. The HTS coils (Bi-2212-based, Tc ≈ 90 K, Hc2 ≈ 100 T) are designed for 15 T fields but operate in a 12 T transient field. Evaluate whether the quench originated from flux creep-induced current redistribution or from thermal instability due to non-uniform cooling in the winding pack.

# HFDPO-01560

A planetary scientist studying Titan’s atmosphere discovers a massive, rotating ice volcano with a base diameter of 80 km and a height of 3.5 km, rotating at 0.012 rad/s. Observations show that the volcano’s rotation is gradually slowing due to internal tidal friction, but the moment of inertia is changing as cryovolcanic material ejects and redistributes. Given that angular momentum is conserved during the ejection phase, and the eruption emits 1.2 × 10¹⁵ kg of ice at 2.5 km above the surface with a tangential velocity of 30 m/s relative to the surface, what is the new angular speed after the eruption?

# HFDPO-04155

A solar-powered research station in Antarctica uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic panels, each modeled as a 24 V ideal voltage source in series with a 0.5 Ω internal resistance, connected in a complex mesh of 18 wires forming three interlocked loops. Due to ice accumulation, two panels (Panel A and Panel B) are partially shaded, reducing their output to 12 V and 18 V respectively, while maintaining the same internal resistance. Engineers measure a 3.2 A current flowing through a central return line between loops. Determine the current through each of the 12 panels, and identify which panel is most likely to overheat under this configuration.

# HFDPO-08105

In a next-generation wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices, a 100 kHz AC current is used to induce a voltage in a secondary coil inside a patient’s body. The primary coil has an inductance of 50 µH and resistance of 0.5 Ω, while the secondary coil (in the implant) has 30 µH and 1.2 Ω. The mutual inductance between coils is 12 µH, and the coupling coefficient is 0.4. Design the optimal load impedance to maximize power transfer efficiency while ensuring the induced voltage in the implant does not exceed 3.3 V peak to prevent tissue damage.

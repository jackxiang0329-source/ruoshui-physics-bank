# HFDPO-00918

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for a coronary artery that must expand to a diameter of 3.2 mm under a pressure of 180 mmHg while remaining within safe stress limits. The stent is made from a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 70 GPa and a yield strength of 450 MPa. If the original wall thickness is 0.12 mm, what is the minimum radius the stent can have to avoid plastic deformation during expansion? Assume the stent behaves as a thin-walled cylindrical pressure vessel.

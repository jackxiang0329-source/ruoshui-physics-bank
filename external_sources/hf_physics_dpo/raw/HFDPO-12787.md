# HFDPO-12787

A team of astrophysicists detects a gamma-ray burst from a distant quasar, 12 billion light-years away, with a measured energy peak at 1.5 MeV. However, the intrinsic spectrum suggests the emission should peak at 0.3 MeV in the source’s rest frame. Then, determine the minimum jet speed (as a fraction of *c*) required to explain the energy shift, and verify whether the source’s apparent luminosity is consistent with special relativistic effects.

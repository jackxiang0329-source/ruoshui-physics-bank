# HFDPO-13425

Spectroscopic analysis shows a gradual increase in mid-gap trap states, while dark current remains stable. The device uses a self-assembled Al₂O₃ tunneling layer between the PbS layer and hole transport material. Design a model to determine the dominant recombination mechanism responsible for degradation, and suggest a modification to the interfacial layer that would suppress it without compromising charge extraction efficiency.

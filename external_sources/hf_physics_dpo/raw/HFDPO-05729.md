# HFDPO-05729

In a high-voltage particle accelerator facility, a cylindrical vacuum chamber (radius 0.5 m, length 10 m) is being charged to −1.2 × 10⁶ V relative to ground to create an electric field for beam focusing. However, dust particles (average radius 10 μm, density 2000 kg/m³) in the chamber are causing unwanted charge accumulation on insulating supports, leading to microdischarges that disrupt beam stability. What is the minimum surface charge density on the chamber wall required to prevent dust particles from levitating due to electrostatic force exceeding gravity? Assume the dust is initially neutral, gains charge via field-induced polarization and breakdown, and that the field is uniform and radial.

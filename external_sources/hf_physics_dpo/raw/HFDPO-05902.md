# HFDPO-05902

A deep-space probe detects faint, polarized radio signals from a distant exoplanet’s magnetosphere, but the signal strength fluctuates unpredictably. The signals have a frequency of 14.2 MHz, matching hydrogen’s hyperfine transition, and propagate through an interstellar medium with electron density varying from 0.1 to 10 cm⁻³ over a 1000 km path. The probe’s antenna measures a 6 dB drop in signal amplitude and a 30° rotation in polarization over one orbit. Estimate the maximum possible Faraday rotation angle expected in this plasma, and determine whether the observed polarization shift is consistent with the plasma’s electron density gradient.

# HFDPO-11414

In a Mars rover's power distribution system, three solar arrays—each with variable output due to dust accumulation—feed a central junction box where currents merge before powering the rover’s instruments. Array A outputs 5.2 A at 48 V, Array B delivers 3.8 A at 47 V, and Array C, partially shaded, produces only 2.1 A at 46 V. At the junction, two independent loads (a drill drawing 4.0 A and a communication relay using 3.5 A) draw current. Due to a wiring fault, an unintended return path exists between the junction and ground through a corroded shield, creating a parallel loop with unknown resistance. Given that the measured current in this unintended loop is 0.6 A, determine the voltage at the central junction and the resistance of the fault path.

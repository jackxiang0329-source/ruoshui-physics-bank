# HFDPO-01180

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) experiences unexpected lateral drift while hovering near a hydrothermal vent field. The ROV, shaped like a streamlined capsule, is positioned 3 meters downstream from the vent axis. Given the plume’s turbulent mixing rate and the ROV’s yaw stability limits (max 5° inclination before control failure), determine whether the induced velocity field from the plume will cause the ROV to exceed its stability threshold. Use the momentum flux model for buoyant plumes and account for entrainment effects.

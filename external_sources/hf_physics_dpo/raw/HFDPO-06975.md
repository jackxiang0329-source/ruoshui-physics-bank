# HFDPO-06975

During a deep-space mission, a probe encounters a subsurface ocean on Europa, where water exists in a supercritical state due to high pressure (320 MPa) and temperature (520 K) near hydrothermal vents. Engineers must determine whether the probe’s heat shield can prevent the supercritical fluid from quenching into a metastable ice-VII phase upon contact, which could fracture the shield. The latent heat of transition from supercritical fluid to ice-VII at this pressure is 1.2 × 10⁷ J/kg, and the shield’s thermal response time is 0.8 s. Given a heat flux of 4.5 × 10⁶ W/m² over a 0.02 m² contact area, will the shield remain intact or undergo catastrophic phase transition?

# HFDPO-11901

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must navigate a narrow canyon with shifting winds. Starting at rest at the canyon’s entrance (x = 0), it accelerates at a rate of a(t) = 2t - 0.1t² m/s² for 15 seconds, then coasts under wind drag modeled as a(t) = -0.3v m/s². The canyon is 300 meters long, and the drone must land precisely at the far end. Determine if the drone reaches the destination within 30 seconds total, and if not, what initial velocity it would need at the start to arrive exactly at x = 300 m at t = 30 s.

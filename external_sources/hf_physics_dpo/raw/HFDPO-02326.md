# HFDPO-02326

A cryogenic fusion reactor uses superconducting magnets cooled by liquid helium-4 in a vacuum-insulated dewar. During a sudden power surge, the helium is rapidly heated beyond its critical temperature (5.2 K), triggering a phase transition to gas. The resulting pressure spike threatens to rupture the dewar, which can withstand up to 500 kPa. Given that 1.2 kg of helium initially at 4.2 K undergoes a sudden 15 K temperature rise under constant volume, estimate the final pressure and evaluate whether the dewar is at risk, considering the non-ideal behavior of helium near the phase boundary.

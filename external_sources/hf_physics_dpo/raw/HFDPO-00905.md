# HFDPO-00905

A team of engineers is designing a gravity-assist trajectory for a deep-space probe near Jupiter to reach a distant exoplanet system. The probe approaches Jupiter at a distance of 1.5 × 10^8 m from its center, moving at 12 km/s relative to Jupiter. Jupiter’s mass is 1.9 × 10^27 kg, and its radius is 7.0 × 10^7 m. The probe must exit the encounter with a velocity increase of at least 30% to reach its destination. Calculate the required impact parameter (closest approach distance perpendicular to initial velocity) to achieve this boost, considering energy and angular momentum conservation in a non-ideal, oblate gravitational field (J₂ = 1.47 × 10⁻²).

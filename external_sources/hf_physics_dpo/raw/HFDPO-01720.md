# HFDPO-01720

A deep-space asteroid mining probe deploys a rotating harvester arm to collect regolith from a rapidly spinning asteroid (radius 2.1 km, mass 8.3 × 10¹⁰ kg, period 1.8 hours). The arm, modeled as a thin rod of length 120 m and mass 4500 kg, extends radially from the probe and begins rotating at 0.8 rad/s about the probe’s center. As the arm mines, 2.7 × 10³ kg of regolith accumulates at its tip. The probe’s thrusters are disabled—how does the asteroid’s rotation affect the arm’s angular velocity over time, and what is the final angular velocity when the arm has collected 90% of its payload, assuming no external torque and conservation of angular momentum?

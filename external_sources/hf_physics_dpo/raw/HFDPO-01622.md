# HFDPO-01622

A desert heat shield prototype uses a phase-change material (PCM) embedded in a ceramic matrix to absorb thermal energy during daytime peak temperatures. The PCM has a melting point of 165°C and a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg. If the shield is exposed to 8 hours of direct solar radiation averaging 950 W/m² and must maintain internal temperature below 150°C, what minimum mass of PCM per square meter is required to prevent melting during peak exposure? Assume 70% of incident energy is absorbed and no heat loss to the environment.

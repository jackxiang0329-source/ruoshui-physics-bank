# HFDPO-15132

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a novel III-V heterojunction solar cell composed of GaAs/AlGaAs layers, operating at 300 K under 1366 W/m² solar irradiance. The cell exhibits a quantum efficiency of 85% at 800 nm but drops to 15% at 1000 nm due to incomplete carrier collection in the wider-bandgap AlGaAs absorber. The device generates 0.75 V maximum power point voltage with a fill factor of 0.82. During a deep-space maneuver, the cell’s temperature increases to 350 K. Estimate the new power output, assuming carrier lifetime and diffusion length are temperature-dependent and follow Arrhenius behavior. Evaluate whether the shift in bandgap due to temperature and the reduced minority carrier lifetime at higher T justify the observed drop in efficiency.

# HFDPO-05165

A wildlife biologist uses a small magnet attached to a tracking tag to monitor a migrating bird. The tag’s magnet is strong enough to deflect a compass needle 15° from magnetic north when placed 30 cm away. If the bird flies near a power line carrying 200 A of current, which produces a magnetic field of 1.3 × 10⁻⁴ T at that distance, will the power line’s field interfere with the compass reading more than the tag’s magnet?

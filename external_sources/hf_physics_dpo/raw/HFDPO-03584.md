# HFDPO-03584

A fusion reactor’s magnetic confinement system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) wound into a toroidal coil, operating at 77 K in liquid nitrogen. During a sudden power surge, the system experiences a flux jump instability, causing localized heating and quenching of 12% of the superconducting volume. The coil’s self-inductance is 5.2 H, and the initial current is 15 kA. What is the required cooling power (in watts) to stabilize the system before the temperature exceeds the critical temperature of 92 K?

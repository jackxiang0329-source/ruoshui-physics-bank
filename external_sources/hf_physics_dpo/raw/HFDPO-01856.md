# HFDPO-01856

A solar farm in Arizona uses 100 identical photovoltaic panels, each producing 300 W at peak sunlight. The panels are wired in series-parallel configurations to feed a central inverter, but after installation, engineers notice a 12% power loss in the transmission lines—despite using 4/0 copper cables rated for 300 A. The cables run 200 meters from the array to the inverter, and the system operates at 600 V DC. What is the minimum cross-sectional area of the cables needed to limit resistive losses to 5%, and could the current design be contributing to system inefficiency?

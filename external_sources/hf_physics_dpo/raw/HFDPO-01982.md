# HFDPO-01982

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a sudden, unpredictable surge in a hydrothermal vent field. The vent emits a high-temperature, buoyant jet of fluid (ρ = 850 kg/m³, μ = 0.002 Pa·s) at 450°C into cold seawater (ρ = 1025 kg/m³, μ = 0.0013 Pa·s) at 2°C. The jet exits with a velocity of 3 m/s from a nozzle of diameter 0.15 m. Engineers observe chaotic eddies and unexpected lateral dispersion—critical for avoiding entanglement with sensitive sensors. Estimate the onset of turbulent mixing and determine the characteristic length scale over which the jet transitions from laminar to turbulent behavior.

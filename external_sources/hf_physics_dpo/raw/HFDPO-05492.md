# HFDPO-05492

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device, a droplet of blood (μ = 3.5 mPa·s, ρ = 1060 kg/m³) is injected into a 50 μm wide, 200 μm long rectangular channel lined with hydrophobic nano-pillars. As the droplet moves at 2 mm/s, it develops a thin lubrication film (~1 μm thick) between its interface and the channel wall due to surface slip effects. The device must deliver the droplet to a reaction zone with less than 1% pressure loss from viscous dissipation. What is the maximum allowable flow rate to meet this constraint, assuming Hagen-Poiseuille flow dominates and slip modifies the effective viscosity via the Navier slip condition?

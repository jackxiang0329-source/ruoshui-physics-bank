# HFDPO-13399

A lunar rover’s primary power system uses a regulated 28 V DC bus, but after 12 months of operation, the voltage drops to 25.2 V at the load during peak solar charging, despite the battery pack being rated at 28 V. The system uses a buck-boost converter with 92% efficiency, and the wiring between the solar panel and the converter has a total resistance of 0.15 Ω. Determine whether the voltage drop is primarily due to wiring losses, converter inefficiency, or degraded solar output, and explain how you would isolate the root cause using only voltage and current measurements at three key points: solar panel output, converter input, and bus terminal.

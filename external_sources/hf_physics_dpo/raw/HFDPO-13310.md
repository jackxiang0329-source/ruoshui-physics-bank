A quantum dot solar cell operates at 300 K, with electrons confined in a 2D lattice of 10 nm × 10 nm quantum dots spaced 5 nm apart. Each dot can hold up to two electrons, and the system is doped to maintain an average of 1.8 electrons per dot.

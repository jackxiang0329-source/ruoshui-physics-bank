# HFDPO-06759

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to isolate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using a spiral channel with a 500 μm width and 1 mm height. Assuming blood behaves as a Newtonian fluid with μ = 3.5 mPa·s and ρ = 1060 kg/m³, estimate the radial migration distance of a CTC due to inertial lift forces over one turn of the spiral (radius ≈ 2.5 mm). How does this distance compare to the cell size, and what design feature might optimize separation?

# HFDPO-04642

A research team is designing a cryogenic storage system for quantum computing qubits, maintaining superconducting circuits at 10 mK. The system uses a dilution refrigerator with a 10 W heat load from environmental leakage. The cold stage is connected via a 2 mm diameter, 50 cm long copper rod with a thermal conductivity of 400 W/(m·K). The outer end of the rod is at 4.2 K (liquid helium bath), while the inner end must stay below 10 mK. Is the rod adequate? What is the maximum thermal power that can be sustained through this rod without violating the 10 mK limit?

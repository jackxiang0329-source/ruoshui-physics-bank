# HFDPO-12650

A fusion energy reactor uses a toroidal magnetic confinement chamber with a non-uniform charge distribution in its plasma core, where electron density varies radially as ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − r/R₀) for r ≤ R₀, and zero otherwise. A diagnostic probe measures the electric flux through a cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius r = R₀/2 and length L = 3 m coaxial with the torus. Given ρ₀ = 5 × 10¹⁵ C/m³, R₀ = 1.2 m, and ε₀ = 8.85 × 10⁻¹² C²/(N·m²), determine the net electric flux through the surface and interpret what this reveals about the plasma’s charge configuration.

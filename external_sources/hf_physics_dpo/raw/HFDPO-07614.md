# HFDPO-07614

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem thrives near a mid-ocean ridge where superheated water (350°C, 200 atm) emerges from Earth’s crust into near-freezing seawater (2°C). Engineers propose a closed-cycle heat engine using the seawater as a cold reservoir and the vent fluid as a hot source, with a working fluid that undergoes phase changes. The system must extract 5 MW of power while operating under a 15% Carnot efficiency limit due to material constraints. What is the minimum required mass flow rate of the working fluid through the heat exchangers, assuming the fluid undergoes a reversible isothermal vaporization at 350°C and condensation at 2°C? The latent heat of vaporization of the working fluid is 1200 kJ/kg.

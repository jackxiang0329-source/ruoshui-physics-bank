# HFDPO-02953

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses steam ejected from a 1.2 km-deep borehole to drive turbines. The steam enters the turbine at 300°C and 5 MPa, expanding through a nozzle before striking turbine blades. If the steam’s mass flow rate is 120 kg/s and the turbine extracts 2.8 MW of mechanical power while maintaining a 150°C exit temperature, what is the rate of work done by the steam on the turbine blades, accounting for both enthalpy drop and kinetic energy gain through the nozzle? Assume negligible heat loss and that the steam behaves as an ideal gas with γ = 1.3.

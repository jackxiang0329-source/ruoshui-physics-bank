# HFDPO-12900

A solar farm in the Atacama Desert experiences periodic dust accumulation on its photovoltaic panels, reducing efficiency by up to 40% within days. The dust particles (average mass 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ kg, radius 15 μm) become electrostatically charged through triboelectric contact with dry wind-blown sand. Measurements show the panel surface develops a uniform surface charge density of −1.2 μC/m². What is the minimum initial charge on a dust particle needed to overcome electrostatic repulsion from the panel and still be attracted to it, given the particle’s charge-to-mass ratio is 0.8 C/kg? Assume gravity is the only other force, and ignore air resistance.

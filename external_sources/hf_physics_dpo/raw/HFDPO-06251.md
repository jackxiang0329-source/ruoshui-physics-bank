# HFDPO-06251

A quantum dot solar cell operates at 300 K, with electrons confined in a 2D semiconductor layer. The density of states near the conduction band edge follows a parabolic form, but interactions with phonons introduce a non-uniform scattering rate: Γ(E) = Γ₀(1 + α(E − E₀)²), where Γ₀ = 1.2×10¹² s⁻¹, α = 2.5×10⁻⁴ eV⁻², and E₀ = 0.15 eV. Given a Fermi level at E_F = 0.18 eV, calculate the effective electron density accounting for both energy-dependent scattering and Pauli exclusion, and evaluate whether the system exhibits strong electron localization or delocalized transport.

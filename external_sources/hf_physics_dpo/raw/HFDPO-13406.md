# HFDPO-13406

A planetary explorer rover on Mars encounters a sudden sandstorm that deposits a 15 kg, irregularly shaped rock slab across its path, partially buried with 40% of its volume submerged in fine regolith. To clear the path, the rover must push the slab at a constant velocity through a 2.3 m gap in the sandbank. The coefficient of kinetic friction between the slab and regolith is 0.62, and the rover’s drive wheels can deliver a maximum force of 180 N. If the slab’s density is 2.7 g/cm³ and the gravitational acceleration on Mars is 3.7 m/s², what is the minimum force the rover must apply at a 15° angle above horizontal to move the slab steadily, and is this feasible?

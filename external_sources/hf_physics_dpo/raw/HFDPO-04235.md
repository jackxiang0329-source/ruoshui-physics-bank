# HFDPO-04235

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 1.2 km deep coil of superconducting wire (500 turns) to harvest energy from Earth’s magnetic field fluctuations due to solar storms. During a geomagnetic storm, the local magnetic field changes at a rate of 0.05 μT/s. The coil has a radius of 15 m and is oriented horizontally. What is the average induced emf in the coil during the storm, and how does this compare to the voltage needed to power a small village (~200 kW at 10 kV)?

# HFDPO-03495

A solar farm in the desert uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a tracking system that rotates to follow the sun. Engineers notice that energy output drops sharply during midday when glare from the sand reflects directly into the panels. The sand reflects light polarized mostly horizontally. If the panels are designed to maximize absorption of unpolarized sunlight but are now exposed to 40% horizontally polarized glare, what angle should a polarizing filter (with axis aligned vertically) be placed at to reduce glare by 75% without significantly dimming the direct sunlight? Assume the glare intensity is 40% of the total incident light.

# HFDPO-09439

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertically oriented, rotating magnetic dipole array (radius 1.5 m, 400 turns) to induce current in a stationary coil array below a magma chamber. The dipole rotates at 60 rpm, producing a time-varying magnetic field modeled as B(t) = B_0 cos(omega t) , where B_0 = 0.8,text{T} and omega = 2pi times 1,text{Hz} . The coil array has resistance R = 1.2,Omega and negligible self-inductance. If the average power output must be 2.4 MW to maintain grid stability, what is the required rate of change of magnetic flux through the coil array at peak operation?

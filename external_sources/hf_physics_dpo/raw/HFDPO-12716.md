# HFDPO-12716

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid. The geothermal reservoir supplies water at 180°C, which heats the isobutane in a heat exchanger. The isobutane enters the turbine at 150°C and 3.5 MPa, expands to 0.6 MPa, and exits at 80°C. Due to fouling in the heat exchanger, the effective heat transfer coefficient has dropped by 30% over a 5-year period. What is the new thermal efficiency, and how does this degradation affect the plant’s annual energy output? Assume the condenser operates at 40°C and isobutane properties are known.

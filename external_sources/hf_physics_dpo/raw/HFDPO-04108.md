# HFDPO-04108

A solar-powered drone, designed to monitor forest fires, descends from 2 km altitude at a constant speed during its return leg. Its 1.5 kg battery powers a motor that maintains thrust exactly balancing gravity. As it descends, it loses 120 kJ of gravitational potential energy. If 80% of this energy loss is converted to thermal energy in the motor due to inefficiency, and the remaining 20% is stored as kinetic energy (but the drone maintains constant speed), where does the rest of the energy go, and what is the energy dissipated as heat?

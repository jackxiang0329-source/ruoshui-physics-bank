# HFDPO-01950

In a Mars rover’s solar power system, a silicon photodiode sensor (bandgap 1.12 eV) detects sunlight intensity but exhibits unexpected drift in output current over time under constant illumination. Given that the diode operates at 15°C and experiences low-level background radiation (0.8 mrad/s), propose a mechanism for the degradation and calculate the expected trap-assisted recombination rate (per cm³·s) needed to explain the observed drop, assuming a bulk carrier lifetime decreases from 1.5 μs to 0.8 μs.

# HFDPO-06221

A quantum computing chip based on superconducting qubits operates at 15 mK in a dilution refrigerator, but coherence times degrade unexpectedly during high-frequency microwave probing. The chip’s Josephson junctions are fabricated from a thin-film NbTiN alloy, and data shows a correlation between decoherence and surface defects observed via scanning tunneling microscopy (STM), which reveal localized electronic states at 12 meV below the superconducting gap. How might these sub-gap states, interpreted as Andreev bound states from Majorana-like zero modes, influence qubit relaxation, and what experimental signature would distinguish them from conventional two-level fluctuators in the dielectric?

# HFDPO-14266

A lunar mining outpost on the permanently shadowed South Pole crater uses a radiative heat sink to cool its high-efficiency solar thermal reactor, which operates at 1,200 K. The sink’s surface is coated with a selective emitter (ε = 0.95 at 1,200 K, ε = 0.1 at 100 K), facing deep space (T ≈ 3 K). During a 48-hour polar night, the system must maintain reactor temperature within ±5 K while processing regolith. Design a minimal-mass heat sink using only a 300 mm × 300 mm flat plate with integrated microchannel cooling and account for non-gray radiative exchange and transient conduction. What is the required effective thermal conductance (W/K) to stabilize temperature if the reactor emits 750 W?

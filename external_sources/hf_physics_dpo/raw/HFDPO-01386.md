# HFDPO-01386

A Mars rover's thermal shield uses a porous ceramic material that traps a thin layer of Martian atmosphere (mostly CO₂) during descent. The shield’s pores are 150 nm wide, and the gas molecules inside have an average speed of 320 m/s.

# HFDPO-02455

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must glide from 20,000 m to 15,000 m while maintaining a minimum kinetic energy to stay aloft. The drone has a mass of 12 kg, and during descent, it uses a variable drag coefficient to control speed—changing from 0.1 at the start to 0.4 at the end due to wing adjustments. Air density decreases exponentially with altitude (ρ(h) = ρ₀e^(-h/H), ρ₀ = 1.225 kg/m³, H = 7,500 m). What is the minimum energy the drone’s onboard solar array must replenish during descent to sustain its flight, assuming 20% of the potential energy loss is lost to non-conservative forces?

# HFDPO-02205

A satellite constellation for global broadband is in a near-circular low Earth orbit at 600 km altitude. During a solar storm, the upper atmosphere expands, increasing atmospheric drag at that altitude by 40%. The mission team must decide whether to perform a reboost maneuver now or wait until the next orbital pass. If they wait, the satellite will lose 250 m/s of orbital velocity due to drag before the next opportune burn window. What is the minimum delta-v required to restore the original orbit after the delay, and how does this compare to an immediate reboost?

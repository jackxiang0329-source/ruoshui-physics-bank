# HFDPO-04158

A biomedical engineer is designing a resonant wireless power transfer system to charge implantable cardiac monitors through the skin using a coil pair operating at 13.56 MHz. The patient’s body introduces a 50 nF parasitic capacitance across the receiver coil, and the inductance of the receiver coil is 5 µH. Due to tissue heterogeneity, the effective series resistance varies dynamically between 2 Ω and 8 Ω. The transmitter delivers 5 W of average power. The system must maintain resonance for efficient power transfer while minimizing heating in surrounding tissue. What is the maximum allowable resistance to ensure the quality factor (Q) exceeds 30, and how does this constraint affect the achievable power transfer efficiency?

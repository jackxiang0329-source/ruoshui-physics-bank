# HFDPO-00531

A drone delivering medical supplies must navigate a narrow canyon during a storm. It enters horizontally at 12 m/s at an elevation of 80 m above the canyon floor. A sudden wind gust imparts a constant vertical acceleration of 2.5 m/s² downward (in addition to gravity). The drone’s autopilot can adjust thrust to maintain horizontal speed, but it must avoid crashing into the canyon’s 50 m high cliff wall 70 m ahead. What is the minimum vertical velocity (downward) the drone must have at the entrance to clear the cliff?

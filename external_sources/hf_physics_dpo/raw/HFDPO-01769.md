# HFDPO-01769

During a winter storm, a city’s water supply pipes begin to freeze despite insulation. Engineers discover that a 200 m section of pipe, carrying water at 5°C, experiences a sudden ambient temperature drop to -10°C. The pipe has a 0.15 m diameter and 1.2 cm thick steel wall. If the ice layer forms uniformly at a rate of 0.5 mm per hour, how long until the flow is completely blocked? Assume thermal conductivity of ice is 2.2 W/m·K and water freezes at 0°C.

# HFDPO-13913

A geothermal power plant in Iceland extracts hot brine at 220°C from a 2.5 km deep well, but the surrounding rock cools the fluid to 150°C by the time it reaches the surface. The plant uses a binary cycle with isobutane as the working fluid, which enters the evaporator at 140°C and 5.8 MPa, then expands through a turbine before condensing at 40°C. The condenser uses seawater at 5°C, which exits at 15°C. If the plant generates 18 MW of electrical power and operates at 72% of the Carnot efficiency for its actual temperature limits, what is the minimum mass flow rate of brine required to sustain this output?

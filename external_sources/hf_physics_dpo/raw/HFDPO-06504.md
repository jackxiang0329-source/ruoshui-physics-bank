# HFDPO-06504

A coastal city is designing a new wave-energy converter to harness ocean swells during storms. The device uses a vertical cylindrical chamber open at the bottom, partially submerged, where rising waves push air upward through a turbine. During a storm, waves reach 2.5 m height with a period of 8 seconds. If the chamber has a diameter of 3 m and the water level inside rises at 0.8 m/s during peak surge, what is the minimum turbine efficiency required to generate 150 kW of power, assuming air density is 1.2 kg/m³ and the flow is steady?

# HFDPO-06142

A prototype quantum sensor using a levitated nanodiamond in a cryogenic vacuum chamber exhibits unexpected frequency shifts in its mechanical oscillations when exposed to low-level electromagnetic noise from nearby control electronics. The diamond nanoparticle (mass = 2.1×10⁻¹⁵ kg) is trapped in a harmonic potential with an effective spring constant of 1.8×10⁻⁴ N/m, but its oscillation frequency drifts by up to 12% under certain RF interference. The interference generates a time-periodic perturbation in the trapping potential. Determine the amplitude of the electromagnetic field that would induce such a frequency shift, assuming the perturbation acts as a small periodic force on the system.

# HFDPO-06899

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar panel array charges a 24 V battery bank through a DC-DC converter, but over two weeks of continuous darkness, the battery voltage drops from 24.0 V to 21.8 V. When the station’s main load (a 120 W heater) is turned on, the battery voltage sags to 20.3 V.

# HFDPO-08903

A marine biologist monitors a pod of humpback whales using hydrophones arranged in a 300-meter-wide circular array. The whales produce low-frequency calls at 28 Hz, but the signal arrives at different sensors with varying phases due to ocean temperature gradients causing sound speed variations (from 1480 m/s near the surface to 1520 m/s at depth). When a call is detected, the phase difference between two opposite sensors is measured as 2.1 radians. To determine the whale’s depth, the biologist must calculate the path difference caused by the curved sound ray and infer the depth where the sound ray bends significantly. What is the depth of the whale if the sound ray follows a path that minimizes travel time through the gradient?

# HFDPO-15286

A quantum computing prototype using superconducting qubits fails intermittently during operation at 15 mK. Engineers observe that decoherence rates spike when the device is exposed to ambient cosmic rays—specifically muons—detected at 12 per hour per cm². Assume the qubit volume is 100 μm³ and the electron density in Al is 1.8 × 10²⁸ m⁻³.

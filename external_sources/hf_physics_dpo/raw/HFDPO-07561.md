# HFDPO-07561

A renewable energy startup is testing a prototype gravity-based energy storage system using massive concrete blocks suspended by cables over a 40-meter vertical shaft. During peak energy demand, a block of mass 12,000 kg is lowered at a constant speed by a generator that converts the block’s gravitational potential energy into electricity. The system must deliver 1.8 MJ of usable electrical energy during the descent, but the generator operates at only 75% efficiency due to friction and electrical losses. How much gravitational potential energy must the block initially possess to meet the energy demand?

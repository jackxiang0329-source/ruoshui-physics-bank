# HFDPO-12081

A quantum dot solar cell relies on discrete energy levels in a nanoscale semiconductor to capture sunlight. At room temperature (300 K), the dot has two accessible states: a ground state at 0 eV and an excited state at 1.2 eV. If the incident sunlight has a spectral intensity peaking at 1.8 eV with a broad distribution, what fraction of the dots are in the excited state under solar illumination? Assume the system reaches equilibrium with the radiation field and use the Fermi-Dirac distribution for photon-induced transitions.

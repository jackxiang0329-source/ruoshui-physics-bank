# HFDPO-16097

In a cutting-edge neuroprosthetic device, a thin-film capacitor made of graphene oxide is embedded in a flexible substrate to store charge for precise neural stimulation. The capacitor’s plates are 100 μm wide, 5 mm long, and separated by a 50 nm dielectric layer with a relative permittivity of 12. During operation, the device experiences rapid voltage oscillations up to 100 Hz. Due to mechanical bending during use, the separation distance fluctuates sinusoidally with an amplitude of ±5 nm. Calculate the time-averaged charge stored on the capacitor over one cycle when the peak voltage is 3 V.

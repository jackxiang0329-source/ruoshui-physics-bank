# HFDPO-05814

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter to 4.0 mm during deployment. The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 60 GPa and a yield strength of 400 MPa. If the stent’s wall thickness is 0.1 mm and the artery exerts an outward pressure of 1.2 atm during expansion, will the stent experience plastic deformation? What is the maximum pressure the stent can withstand without permanent deformation?

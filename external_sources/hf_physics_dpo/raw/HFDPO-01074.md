# HFDPO-01074

A quantum dot computer chip uses electrons confined in a nanoscale semiconductor well to store information as quantum states. Engineers observe that the energy levels shift unpredictably when the dot's width is reduced below 5 nm, especially under high-frequency electromagnetic fields. Given that the electron's de Broglie wavelength must fit as a standing wave across the well, what is the minimum width required for the ground state to remain stable at a 10 THz driving frequency, assuming the electron is effectively free within the dot?

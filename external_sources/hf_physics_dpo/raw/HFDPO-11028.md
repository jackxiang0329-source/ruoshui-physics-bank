# HFDPO-11028

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial solar panels mounted at a 30° tilt, facing south. On a clear day, sunlight strikes the panels with an intensity of 1000 W/m², but glare from the sand reduces efficiency. Engineers want to minimize reflected glare using a polarizing filter on the panel's glass surface. If the sand reflects light with a significant p-polarized component due to Brewster’s angle, and the filter's axis is adjustable, what orientation of the polarizer maximizes power absorption while minimizing reflected glare? Assume the glass has a refractive index of 1.52 and the incident angle is 30°.

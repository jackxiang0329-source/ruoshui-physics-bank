# HFDPO-03153

A fusion reactor's plasma containment system uses a rapidly changing toroidal magnetic field to induce currents in the conducting vessel walls, stabilizing the plasma. During a test pulse, the magnetic flux through the 1200 cm² cross-section of the vessel increases at 0.85 T/s. If the vessel walls have a resistivity of 1.8 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m and thickness 6 mm, what is the magnitude and direction of the induced eddy current density 2 mm below the inner surface, and how does this affect local heating and mechanical stress?

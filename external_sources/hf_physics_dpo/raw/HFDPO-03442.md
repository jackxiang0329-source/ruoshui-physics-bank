# HFDPO-03442

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber discovers a frozen lake with a layer of supercooled water beneath a thin ice crust. When a rock is dropped through the ice, the supercooled water rapidly freezes, forming a dense layer of clear ice. If 2.5 kg of water at –3°C freezes completely, releasing 830 kJ of energy, what is the mass of the ice that could have formed from the same energy release if the water had started at 0°C and undergone only freezing?

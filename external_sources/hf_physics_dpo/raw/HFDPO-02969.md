# HFDPO-02969

A research team at a fusion energy startup is testing a new neutron shield made of lithium-6 enriched polyethylene. During a 10-hour experiment, the shield is exposed to a 1.5 MW deuterium-tritium fusion plasma, producing a steady neutron flux of 3.2 × 10¹⁴ neutrons/m²/s. The lithium-6 captures neutrons via the reaction ⁶Li + n → ⁴He + ³H + 4.8 MeV. If the shield has a density of 0.7 g/cm³, 20% lithium-6 enrichment, and a thickness of 15 cm, how much tritium (in grams) is produced over the experiment, and what fraction of the incident neutrons are captured?

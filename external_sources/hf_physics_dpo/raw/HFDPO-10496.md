# HFDPO-10496

A Mars rover’s robotic arm, designed to collect subsurface soil samples, uses a lightweight composite arm (mass 8.4 kg, length 1.3 m) with a scoop at the end (mass 1.2 kg) that must rotate from horizontal to vertical in 0.9 seconds during a sample acquisition maneuver. The arm is powered by a high-torque motor at the base, but due to limited power and thermal constraints, the torque must be minimized while ensuring the arm doesn’t overshoot the target angle. If the arm starts at rest and the motor torque is limited to 12 N·m, what is the minimum time-to-peak torque profile (in seconds) required to achieve the 90° swing without exceeding angular overshoot of 5°? What is the peak angular acceleration?

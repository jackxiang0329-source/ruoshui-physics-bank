# HFDPO-12622

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (10 cm diameter, 100 μH inductance) operates at 13.56 MHz, while the receiver coil (5 cm diameter, 25 μH) is embedded in the device. Due to tissue losses and misalignment, the system’s efficiency drops to 58% when the coils are offset by 2 mm. The patient’s body introduces a parasitic capacitive load of 15 pF in parallel with the receiver’s impedance. What capacitance should be added in series with the receiver coil to restore resonance and maximize power transfer, assuming the original circuit was tuned to 13.56 MHz before tissue effects?

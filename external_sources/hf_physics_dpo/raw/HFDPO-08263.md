# HFDPO-08263

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The external coil (primary) operates at 13.56 MHz with a 50 μH inductance and 2 Ω resistance. The implanted coil (secondary) has 10 μH inductance, 3 Ω resistance, and a variable capacitance to tune resonance. Due to tissue absorption, the coupling coefficient drops to 0.15. The system must deliver 1.5 W to the implant with >70% efficiency. The engineer must determine the optimal capacitance to achieve resonance and evaluate whether the system can meet efficiency and power targets under real-world constraints.

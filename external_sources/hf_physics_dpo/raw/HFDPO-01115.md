# HFDPO-01115

A wind turbine blade is designed with a curved airfoil shape to generate lift, but engineers notice that during high winds (15 m/s), the blade experiences unexpected vibrations. Using Bernoulli’s principle, explain why the pressure on the upper surface of the blade drops, creating lift—and calculate the pressure difference between the upper and lower surfaces if the air speed above is 20 m/s and below is 10 m/s. Assume air density is 1.2 kg/m³.

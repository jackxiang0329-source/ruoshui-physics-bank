# HFDPO-02548

A deep-space probe orbiting a distant exoplanet detects subtle, periodic gravitational anomalies in its telemetry—variations in orbital velocity that suggest a non-uniform mass distribution beneath the planet’s surface. Assuming the planet is a sphere with radius 6,000 km and mass 5.9 × 10²⁴ kg, determine the minimum possible mass and radial distance of a buried dense ore deposit (density 7,000 kg/m³) causing these perturbations, using only orbital perturbation theory and Newtonian gravity.

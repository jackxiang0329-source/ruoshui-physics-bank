# HFDPO-06116

A small wind turbine on a remote weather station is mounted on a pivoting arm that tilts to face the wind. During a storm, the wind exerts a 350 N force perpendicular to the arm at its tip, 2.4 m from the pivot. The arm is held in place by a horizontal cable attached 1.8 m up the support pole. What tension must the cable provide to keep the arm from rotating?

# HFDPO-14284

A quantum imaging lab uses a coherent laser (λ = 532 nm) to probe a nanoscale photonic crystal with a periodic lattice spacing of 310 nm. The crystal exhibits a bandgap that suppresses transmission for certain angles. The crystal has a refractive index of 2.3 and is surrounded by air. Design a model that predicts the smallest incident angle (measured from the normal) yielding destructive interference in the first-order diffraction pattern.

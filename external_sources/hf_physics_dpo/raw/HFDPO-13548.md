# HFDPO-13548

A solar-powered garden light uses a small silicon photodiode to detect dusk. The diode generates a current of 2.5 μA when exposed to sunlight (1000 W/m²). At night, the same diode produces only 0.1 μA due to ambient light. If the light’s control circuit activates when the diode current drops below 0.5 μA, how much must the ambient light intensity decrease (in W/m²) for the light to switch on?

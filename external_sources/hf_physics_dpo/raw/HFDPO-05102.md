# HFDPO-05102

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustics to monitor fish activity near a reef. She detects a sound wave with a frequency of 15 kHz emitted by a fish school, which travels through seawater (speed = 1500 m/s) and reflects off a nearby reef. The echo returns 0.8 seconds later. What is the distance from the fish school to the reef? If the biologist later observes that the frequency of the echo is 14.9 kHz, what does this indicate about the motion of the fish school relative to the reef?

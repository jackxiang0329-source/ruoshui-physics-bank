# HFDPO-12355

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a 2D hexagonal lattice of silicon nanodots (diameter 25 nm, spacing 80 nm) to enhance light trapping. When sunlight (λ = 600 nm) hits the surface at 30° incidence, constructive interference is observed in the first-order diffraction peak in the forward direction. However, the measured output current drops by 32% under identical conditions when the lattice is slightly misaligned (±2°). Explain how the misalignment disrupts the interference condition, and calculate the effective phase shift between adjacent dots that causes this drop.

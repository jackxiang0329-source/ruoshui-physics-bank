# HFDPO-03868

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates 2.4 MW of electrical power during peak gusts, but the gear system suffers mechanical stress when wind speeds vary rapidly. Given that the turbine blades have a combined moment of inertia of 12,000 kg·m² and each gust causes a 10% change in wind speed over 3 seconds, what is the average torque the control system must apply to prevent rotational acceleration and protect the drivetrain? Use conservation of angular momentum to analyze the system’s response to sudden wind changes.

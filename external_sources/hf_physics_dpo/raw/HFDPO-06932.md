# HFDPO-06932

A solar farm in Arizona uses thin-film photovoltaic panels mounted on adjustable poles to track the sun. Each panel has a resistive interconnect layer with non-uniform thickness due to manufacturing imperfections, causing the resistance per unit length to vary as R(x) = R₀(1 + αx), where x is distance along the strip (0 ≤ x ≤ L), R₀ = 0.5 Ω/m, α = 0.02 m⁻¹, and L = 10 m. When the sun is directly overhead, the total current output is I₀ = 2.0 A. If one segment of the strip (from x = 5 m to x = 6 m) is damaged and now has infinite resistance, how does this affect the total current output? Assume the panels are connected in series and the voltage source is ideal.

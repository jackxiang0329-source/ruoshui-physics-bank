# HFDPO-01341

During a solar storm, a power grid in northern Canada experiences unexpected harmonic distortions causing transformer saturation. Engineers detect a 3rd harmonic (180 Hz) current component in a 60 Hz transmission line with a 150 km overhead cable. The line has inductance of 1.2 mH/km and capacitance of 10 nF/km. A 3-phase load draws 450 kW at 0.95 lagging power factor. What is the magnitude and phase angle of the 3rd harmonic current at the load, and why might this cause overheating despite the fundamental current being within limits?

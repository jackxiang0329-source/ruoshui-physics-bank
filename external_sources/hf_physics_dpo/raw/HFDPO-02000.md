# HFDPO-02000

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged volcanic vent releasing superheated hydrothermal fluid at 350°C into cold ocean water (2°C). The vent emits a continuous jet of fluid with a density of 960 kg/m³ through a 0.15 m diameter nozzle, forming a buoyant plume that rises 120 m before stabilizing. Engineers must determine the minimum internal pressure required at the vent to overcome both hydrostatic pressure at 300 m depth and the dynamic resistance of the plume ascent.

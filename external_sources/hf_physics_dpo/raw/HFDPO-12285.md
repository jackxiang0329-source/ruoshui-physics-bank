# HFDPO-12285

If the qubit is tuned to 4.998 GHz to avoid cross-talk, and the system’s input is driven by a 10 mW microwave signal through a 50 Ω line, what is the effective quality factor of the cavity, and how does this affect the qubit’s coherence time?

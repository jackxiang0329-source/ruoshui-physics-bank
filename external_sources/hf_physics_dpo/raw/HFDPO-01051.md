# HFDPO-01051

During a tropical storm, a coastal research team observes a sudden drop in atmospheric pressure above a narrow estuary, causing water to surge inland at an unexpected rate. The team estimates the pressure gradient between the open ocean and the estuary mouth is 420 Pa over a 150 m horizontal distance. Using Bernoulli’s principle and assuming incompressible, inviscid flow along the surface, determine the minimum speed the wind must generate to account for the observed pressure drop—while also considering the influence of wave setup and the vertical displacement of water surface.

# HFDPO-12898

However, experimental data shows a sharp dip in efficiency at 650 nm, despite theoretical predictions indicating peak absorption at 640 nm. What physical mechanism explains the observed mismatch, and how would you modify the lattice design to restore resonance at 650 nm without altering the material properties?

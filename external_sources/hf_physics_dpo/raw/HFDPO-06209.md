# HFDPO-06209

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a nanoscale photonic crystal lattice to enhance light absorption in the 600–700 nm range. However, experimental data shows unexpected dips in efficiency at 650 nm and 680 nm, despite optimal material properties. The lattice is a square periodic structure with a period of 420 nm and a dielectric contrast of 2.8. Using wave optics, determine whether these dips arise from destructive interference due to Bragg scattering or from guided mode resonances trapped in the lattice, and explain how the lattice symmetry and refractive index profile influence the angular sensitivity of the dip.

# HFDPO-01692

In a semiconductor fabrication plant, a 10 cm × 10 cm silicon wafer is suspended by electrostatic force above a grounded electrode during a plasma etching process. The wafer carries a uniform surface charge density of +50 nC/m², while the electrode below is grounded. A tiny conductive probe, 0.5 mm in diameter, is brought near the wafer’s center to measure local electric potential, but its presence disrupts the field. What is the approximate change in potential at the wafer’s center due to the probe’s approach, assuming it is held at 3 V by an external source and initially 2 mm from the wafer surface?

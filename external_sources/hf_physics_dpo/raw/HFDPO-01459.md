# HFDPO-01459

In a quantum imaging experiment, a single photon source emits photons one at a time through a double-slit setup at 100 kHz, with a slit separation of 800 nm and a screen placed 2.5 m away. The interference pattern develops slowly over time, but due to detector inefficiency, only 60% of photons are registered. When the photon emission rate is increased to 1 MHz, the pattern becomes washed out. Analyze whether this loss of interference is due to a breakdown of wave-particle duality or a measurement-induced collapse, and determine the critical exposure time per photon below which interference must vanish due to quantum backaction.

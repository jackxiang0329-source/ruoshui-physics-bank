# HFDPO-14581

A lunar rover’s rear suspension arm, made of a lightweight composite beam, is designed to pivot at a hinge while supporting a 120 kg scientific payload mounted at its tip. The arm is 2.4 m long and inclined at 35° above horizontal. A tension cable runs from the tip of the arm to a fixed point 1.8 m vertically above the hinge, forming a 22° angle with the horizontal. The system must remain in static equilibrium under the rover’s reduced gravity (1.6 m/s²). What minimum cable tension is required to prevent rotation, and how does the hinge force vector change if the payload’s center of mass shifts 0.3 m toward the hinge?

# HFDPO-14053

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a 12 V battery bank is used to run sensors and a radio transmitter. Over time, the system’s efficiency drops, and data transmission fails intermittently. The station uses a 300 Ω load for the radio and a 150 Ω sensor circuit, both connected in parallel to the battery. An engineer measures a 2.4 V drop across the battery’s internal resistance when the radio is active. Given that the battery’s internal resistance is 0.5 Ω and the system wire resistance is 0.3 Ω per wire (one-way), determine whether the observed voltage drop is consistent with the system design and identify the likely source of failure—faulty wiring, aging battery, or overload.

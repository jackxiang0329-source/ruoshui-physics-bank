# HFDPO-16328

A remote Arctic research station uses a network of tensioned steel cables to stabilize a meteorological tower during extreme wind events. The 30-meter-high tower, anchored to a reinforced concrete base, is braced by three symmetrically placed cables anchored at 45° angles to the ground. During a storm, wind exerts a 12,000 N force perpendicular to the tower’s axis at 20 meters above the ground. The cables are rated for 25,000 N maximum tension. Determine whether the system remains in static equilibrium under these conditions, and critically evaluate whether the current cable layout is sufficient for safe operation.

# HFDPO-00185

A high-efficiency solar thermal plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat ≈ 1500 J/kg·K, density ≈ 2000 kg/m³) stored in insulated tanks at 560°C to generate steam for turbines. During a sudden cloud cover event, the plant must maintain output for 4 hours using only stored thermal energy. If the system loses 12 kW of heat through imperfect insulation and the salt tank holds 1800 m³, by how much will the salt temperature drop in those 4 hours? Assume no phase change and uniform temperature throughout the salt.

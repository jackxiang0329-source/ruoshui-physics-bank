# HFDPO-10579

A deep-sea submersible encounters a sudden pressure surge while descending through a dense brine plume beneath an Arctic ice shelf. The plume, formed by freezing seawater, has a density gradient that increases linearly from 1025 kg/m³ at the top to 1050 kg/m³ at 200 m depth. The submersible’s pressure hull has a hemispherical dome with a 1.5 m radius. Determine the net hydrostatic force acting on the dome’s outer surface at 150 m depth, and evaluate whether the structure can withstand the force if its maximum allowable stress is 4.8 × 10⁶ Pa.

# HFDPO-04751

A photovoltaic array on a Mars rover operates at 120 K due to radiative cooling, but its efficiency drops sharply below 180 K. The cells are made of a silicon-germanium alloy with a bandgap of 0.78 eV at 300 K. An engineer suspects the decrease is due to increased carrier recombination at low temperatures, driven by deep-level traps. Given that trap energy levels are 0.45 eV below the conduction band and the effective density of states in the valence band is 1.5 × 10¹⁹ cm⁻³, calculate the temperature at which trap ionization becomes negligible (i.e., where the fraction of ionized traps drops below 5%). Assume the intrinsic carrier concentration follows the law n_i² = N_c N_v exp(–E_g/kT) and use appropriate approximations.

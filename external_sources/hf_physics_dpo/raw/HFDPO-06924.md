# HFDPO-06924

A portable MRI scanner for remote medical use relies on a superconducting solenoid that must maintain a 1.5 T magnetic field while operating at 77 K. The solenoid has 5,000 turns, a length of 0.6 m, and a radius of 0.15 m. During a power fluctuation, the current drops from 200 A to 180 A in 0.5 seconds. A nearby ferromagnetic surgical tool (permeability μ ≈ 800μ₀, volume 1.2×10⁻⁴ m³) is partially inserted into the bore. What is the induced emf in the tool’s eddy current loop, and how does the resulting force affect the tool’s position?

# HFDPO-06786

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unusual spectral response peaks at 620 nm and 645 nm under coherent illumination, despite having a uniform lattice structure. The device’s efficiency drops sharply when exposed to incoherent light, even though the total intensity remains constant.

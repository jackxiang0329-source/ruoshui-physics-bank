# HFDPO-13062

A research team is developing a compact magnetic levitation (maglev) sensor for measuring micro-scale vibrations in a satellite’s structural frame. The sensor uses a small neodymium magnet (mass 5.0 g, magnetic moment 0.80 A·m²) suspended above a conductive copper plate. When the satellite experiences a 0.5 Hz vibration, eddy currents are induced in the plate, creating a repulsive magnetic force that stabilizes the magnet at a height of 2.0 mm. What is the minimum conductivity of the copper plate (in S/m) required to generate sufficient levitation force to counteract gravity? Assume the magnet’s field is uniform over the plate’s surface and ignore edge effects.

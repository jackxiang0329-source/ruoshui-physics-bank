# HFDPO-11010

A lunar mining outpost on the far side of the Moon must deploy a tethered communication satellite in a stable elliptical orbit just above the terminator line, where sunlight and shadow alternate every 14 Earth days. The satellite’s periapsis is 50 km above the lunar surface, and its apoapsis is 150 km. However, the Moon’s gravitational field is highly non-uniform due to mascons (mass concentrations), which create localized gravitational anomalies with a peak deviation of +2.5 × 10⁻⁶ m/s². Design an orbital correction strategy to maintain stability over a 30-day mission, accounting for these anomalies, and determine the minimum delta-v required to counteract cumulative perturbations.

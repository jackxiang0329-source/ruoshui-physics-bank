# HFDPO-09617

A deep-space probe deploying a 12-meter, 800-kg solar array with two rigid, symmetric panels must spin them out from a folded configuration at 0.5 rad/s² to achieve full deployment in 6 seconds. During spin-up, a torque from a malfunctioning actuator introduces a constant 140 N·m perturbation opposing the intended motion. What is the minimum required torque from the primary drive motor to ensure both panels reach full extension without slippage, given that each panel has a moment of inertia of 1,200 kg·m² about the hinge axis and the motor applies torque at the central shaft?

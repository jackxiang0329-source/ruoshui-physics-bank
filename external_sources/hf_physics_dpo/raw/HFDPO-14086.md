# HFDPO-14086

A small solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain region uses a network of three interconnected batteries (12 V, 15 V, and 10 V) to stabilize power during cloud cover. Two charging resistors (R₁ = 20 Ω, R₂ = 30 Ω) connect the batteries to a central control unit. If the current flowing through R₁ is measured at 0.4 A into the control unit, what must be the current through R₂ and the voltage at the junction point between the batteries and resistors?

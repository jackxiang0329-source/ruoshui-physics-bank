# HFDPO-08899

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a dual-polarization antenna to receive signals from a ground station, but engineers notice unexpected signal degradation during certain orbital passes. The ground station transmits a linearly polarized signal at 10 GHz with a known tilt angle of 30° from vertical. However, the satellite’s receiver detects a mix of co-polarized and cross-polarized components, suggesting the received polarization state has rotated. Given that the signal reflects off a high-altitude cloud layer with a known dielectric constant (ε_r = 3.2) and cloud particle orientation anisotropy, determine the effective polarization rotation angle due to reflection and assess whether the observed cross-polarization is due to reflection-induced depolarization or atmospheric birefringence.

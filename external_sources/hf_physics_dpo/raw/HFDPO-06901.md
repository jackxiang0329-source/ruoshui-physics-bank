# HFDPO-06901

A solar-powered desalination plant in the desert uses parabolic trough collectors to heat seawater to 120°C in evacuated tubes, but after 18 months of operation, engineers notice a 27% drop in thermal efficiency. The system relies on phase-change material (PCM) capsules filled with lauric acid (melting point 43°C, latent heat 165 kJ/kg) to store excess heat for nighttime operation. Measurements show the PCM has undergone significant crystallization fatigue. What is the minimum mass fraction of undegraded PCM required to maintain 85% of the original thermal storage capacity, assuming the remaining PCM has 40% reduced latent heat and the system operates with 1.2 kg of PCM per kW of output?

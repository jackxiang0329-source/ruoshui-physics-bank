# HFDPO-02408

A drone delivering medical supplies in a remote forest encounters unexpected wind gusts that cause its payload to swing like a pendulum. The 1.2 kg medical box is suspended by a 0.8 m cable from the drone’s undercarriage. If the box begins swinging with a maximum angle of 10° from vertical, what is the period of its oscillation? How would the period change if the payload were replaced with a 0.6 kg box of the same size?

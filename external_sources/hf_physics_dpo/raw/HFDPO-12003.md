# HFDPO-12003

A fusion reactor’s cryogenic cooling system uses superfluid helium (He-II) to maintain the superconducting magnets at 4.2 K. During a sudden power fluctuation, the system loses cooling capacity for 12 seconds, causing localized heating in a 15 cm³ volume of superfluid. The helium undergoes a phase transition from superfluid to normal fluid with a latent heat of 83 J/kg and a density of 125 kg/m³. What is the minimum temperature increase in that volume if all absorbed energy goes into heating, and how does this affect the system’s ability to remain superconducting?

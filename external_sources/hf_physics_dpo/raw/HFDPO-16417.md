# HFDPO-16417

A recording engineer is setting up a live performance in a historic church with 12-meter high vaulted ceilings. The primary microphone is placed 6 meters from a large stone arch that reflects sound. When a singer hits a sustained note at 85 Hz, the audience near the back wall notices a noticeable drop in volume—almost like a “dead spot.” The engineer suspects destructive interference from the reflected sound wave. Given the speed of sound is 343 m/s, what is the smallest possible distance between the arch and the back wall that would cause this cancellation?

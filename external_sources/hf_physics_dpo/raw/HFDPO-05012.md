# HFDPO-05012

A hydroelectric dam in the Andes regulates water flow through a turbine system to generate 15 MW of power during peak demand. During a storm, the inflow increases to 400 m³/s, but the turbine’s maximum rotational speed is limited by mechanical stress. Engineers must adjust the blade angle to maintain safe operation while maximizing energy extraction. If the water enters the turbine at 25 m/s and exits at 5 m/s horizontally, and the turbine’s efficiency is 88%, what is the maximum safe mass flow rate (in kg/s) the turbine can handle without exceeding a 1200 kN axial force on the shaft?

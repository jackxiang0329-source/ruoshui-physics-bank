# HFDPO-10553

During a lunar sample return mission, a robotic lander descends to the Moon’s surface using a precision thrust system. At 150 m above the surface, the lander’s vertical velocity is 12 m/s downward, and it begins applying a constant upward thrust that produces an acceleration of 0.8 m/s². The terrain ahead includes a hidden 8-meter-deep crater. If the lander must come to a complete stop within 10 meters above the crater’s rim, what is the minimum time it must delay initiating the braking thrust after reaching 150 m altitude to ensure a safe landing?

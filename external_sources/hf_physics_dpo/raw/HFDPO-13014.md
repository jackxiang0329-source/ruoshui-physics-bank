# HFDPO-13014

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a complex array of superconducting coils arranged in a toroidal geometry to confine plasma at 150 million Kelvin. During a power surge, one coil’s current spikes to 12 kA, creating a magnetic field that induces eddy currents in the surrounding copper thermal shielding. If the shielding has a resistivity of 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, thickness of 2 cm, and the magnetic flux changes at a rate of 0.8 T/s across a 1.5 m² cross-section, estimate the instantaneous power dissipated in the shielding due to eddy currents, and evaluate whether this could compromise the structural integrity under thermal stress.

# HFDPO-14759

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 30° incline to optimize sunlight capture. During a winter storm, snow accumulates unevenly—15 cm on the upper edge and only 5 cm on the lower edge—creating a non-uniform load. What minimum cable tension is needed to prevent rotation, assuming snow density is 300 kg/m³ and the panel’s center of mass is at its geometric center?

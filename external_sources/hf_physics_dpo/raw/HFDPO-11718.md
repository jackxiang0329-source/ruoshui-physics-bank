# HFDPO-11718

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a high-resolution lens system is investigating a hydrothermal vent field at 2,500 meters depth. The lens, designed for water with refractive index 1.34, shows a blurring effect when imaging a 2 mm-tall mineral crystal 1.2 m from the lens. Measurements show the image forms 38 cm inside the lens housing, but the crystal is out of focus.

# HFDPO-06512

A deep-sea mining robot descends at constant speed through 3,000 meters of seawater, hauling a 12,000 kg ore pod. The robot’s thrusters exert a force to counteract both gravity and drag, which increases with velocity. Given the drag force is F_d = 0.5 rho C_d A v^2 , with rho = 1025,text{kg/m}^3 , C_d = 1.2 , A = 0.8,text{m}^2 , and constant descent speed v = 0.5,text{m/s} , calculate the total work done *by the thrusters* during descent. Assume the robot’s mass is negligible compared to the pod.

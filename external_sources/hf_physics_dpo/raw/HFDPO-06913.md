# HFDPO-06913

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a collapsed submarine hull at 2,500 meters depth. The breach has created a sealed cavity filled with high-pressure seawater at the local hydrostatic pressure. Engineers must determine whether a robotic arm can safely deploy a pressure-relief valve without causing catastrophic structural failure due to sudden internal pressure imbalance. The cavity is a cylindrical chamber (diameter: 1.2 m, height: 0.8 m), sealed with a 12 cm thick hatch made of titanium alloy (yield strength: 800 MPa). The hatch is secured by 8 bolts, each with a cross-sectional area of 1.5 cm². What is the maximum allowable pressure differential between inside and outside the chamber that the hatch can withstand without yielding or bolt failure?

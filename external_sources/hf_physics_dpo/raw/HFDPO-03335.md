# HFDPO-03335

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable stent for coronary arteries that must withstand cyclic stress from pulsatile blood flow (100 mmHg pressure variation) over 20 years. The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 50 GPa and a yield strength of 400 MPa, but its effective stiffness varies with temperature due to phase transformation. The artery wall tissue (elastin-rich) has a nonlinear stress-strain relationship described by a hyperelastic Mooney-Rivlin model. What is the maximum allowable radial displacement of the stent to prevent permanent deformation or tissue damage, assuming a 15% failure threshold in cyclic fatigue and a 3 mm diameter artery?

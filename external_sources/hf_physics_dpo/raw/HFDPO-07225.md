# HFDPO-07225

A lunar habitat module relies on a redundant power grid with three solar arrays (each 120 W output, 30 V nominal) feeding a central distribution bus via diode-protected lines. During a 4-hour dust storm, one array fails due to debris blocking its cells, reducing its output to 18 W. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current through each branch and the voltage at the bus if the internal resistance of each array is 0.3 Ω and the load draws 1.2 A at 28 V. What happens if the failed array’s diode shorts internally?

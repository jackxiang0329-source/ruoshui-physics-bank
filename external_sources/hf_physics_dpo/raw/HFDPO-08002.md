# HFDPO-08002

A research team is designing a novel electrostatic shield for a quantum computing chip housed in a cryogenic vacuum chamber. The chip generates a non-uniform electric field due to localized charge imbalances at nanoscale Josephson junctions. To prevent field leakage, they plan to embed a custom dielectric shell (permittivity ε = 4ε₀) around the chip, but must ensure no net flux through any closed surface enclosing the shell—despite asymmetric charge distributions. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the required total charge on the inner surface of the shell to guarantee zero net flux through any Gaussian surface surrounding the entire system, assuming the outer surface remains neutral.

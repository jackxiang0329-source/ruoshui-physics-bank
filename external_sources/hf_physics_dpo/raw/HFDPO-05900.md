# HFDPO-05900

A satellite constellation orbiting Earth at 20,000 km altitude must maintain synchronized atomic clocks for global navigation. Due to gravitational time dilation (General Relativity) and relative motion (Special Relativity), each clock runs at a different rate than clocks on the surface. If the satellite’s orbital speed is 3.9 km/s and Earth’s gravitational potential at that height is −3.2×10⁷ J/kg, by how much does the satellite clock gain or lose per day relative to a ground-based clock?

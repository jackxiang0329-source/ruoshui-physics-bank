# HFDPO-06255

A lunar mining colony plans to deploy a tethered satellite system to stabilize its orbit around a small, irregularly shaped asteroid (mass = 8.7 × 10¹⁰ kg, average radius = 2.3 km) to enhance communication coverage. The satellite, 250 kg, is attached by a 4.5 km tether to a base station on the surface. Due to the asteroid’s non-uniform density, the gravitational field varies by up to 12% across its surface. The base station must remain stable while the satellite orbits at 300 m above the surface. What is the maximum allowable angular speed for the system to remain in a dynamically stable configuration without inducing oscillatory torques?

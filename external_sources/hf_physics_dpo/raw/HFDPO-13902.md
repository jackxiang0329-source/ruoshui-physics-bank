# HFDPO-13902

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate blood cells by size using a serpentine channel with a narrowing constriction. At 37°C, whole blood flows at 0.5 mL/min through a 200 μm wide entrance, entering a 50 μm wide constriction 1.2 mm long. The device operates under a constant pressure differential of 12 kPa across the entire channel. If the effective viscosity of blood is 3.5 mPa·s and the density is 1060 kg/m³, at what Reynolds number does inertial separation become significant—causing larger cells to migrate away from the centerline?

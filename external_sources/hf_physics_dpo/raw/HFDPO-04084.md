# HFDPO-04084

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where deuterium and tritium ions stream at 1.2 × 10⁷ m/s, colliding to form helium nuclei and high-energy neutrons. During a 5-second pulse, the total momentum of the plasma remains nearly zero, but the system experiences a net recoil force. Given that 0.15 kg of fuel undergoes fusion, releasing 2.4 × 10¹⁵ J of energy (mostly in neutron kinetic energy), analyze whether conservation of momentum and energy are satisfied across the system, including the reactor structure.

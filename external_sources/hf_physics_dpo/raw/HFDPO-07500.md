# HFDPO-07500

A farmer installing a solar-powered irrigation system notices that when a large electric fence charger is turned on nearby, the flow of water from a well-controlled by a solenoid valve suddenly stops. The valve uses a small electromagnet to open, and the solenoid has 200 turns of wire. The charger creates a changing magnetic field of 0.015 T in 0.02 seconds near the valve’s coil. What average emf is induced in the valve’s coil if the coil area is 5 cm²?

# HFDPO-13008

A fusion reactor’s plasma containment system uses a toroidal magnetic field generated by 12 identical superconducting coils arranged symmetrically around a donut-shaped vacuum chamber. Each coil carries a current of 80 kA and produces a magnetic field with a peak magnitude of 12 T at the center of the torus. However, slight misalignments in the coil positions—each off by up to 2 mm from ideal—cause a non-uniform field that leads to plasma drift. Calculate the maximum radial component of the magnetic field at the plasma edge (radius 1.5 m), assuming a small angular deviation of 0.5° in one coil’s orientation.

# HFDPO-12818

In a high-frequency wireless power transfer system for electric vehicles, a primary coil with inductance 12 μH and resistance 0.8 Ω is driven by a 100 kHz AC source. A secondary coil, 15 cm apart and aligned coaxially, has 10 μH inductance, 0.5 Ω resistance, and mutual inductance of 2.5 μH. Due to misalignment during parking, the coupling coefficient drops to 0.4. Determine the power delivered to a 12 Ω resistive load in the secondary circuit and explain how the phase relationship between voltage and current changes compared to ideal alignment (k = 0.7). Evaluate the impact of this misalignment on system efficiency and propose a method to compensate for the reduced coupling.

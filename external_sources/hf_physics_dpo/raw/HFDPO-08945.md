# HFDPO-08945

During a winter storm, a city’s water pipes froze, causing a burst in a residential building. The plumber discovers that ice formed inside a 10-meter section of horizontal copper pipe with a 2 cm diameter. The pipe was initially filled with water at 4°C. Given the density of water is 1000 kg/m³, the density of ice is 917 kg/m³, and the latent heat of fusion for water is 334 kJ/kg, calculate how much volume the water expanded by when it froze—was this expansion likely responsible for the pipe rupture?

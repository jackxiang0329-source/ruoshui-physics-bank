# HFDPO-15002

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system to charge implantable cardiac pacemakers through the skin using a resonant inductive coupling setup. The transmitter coil (inside a wearable patch) operates at 13.56 MHz with a 10 μH inductance and 2 Ω resistance. The receiver coil (implanted under the skin) has 8 μH inductance and 1.5 Ω resistance. The mutual inductance between coils is 0.8 μH. The system must deliver at least 5 W of real power to the receiver with a power factor above 0.95. Determine the required capacitance for the transmitter and receiver LC circuits to achieve resonance and maximum efficiency, and analyze the phase relationship between current and voltage in the receiver circuit.

# HFDPO-15967

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable operation during extended missions, but after deployment, it experiences intermittent power failure during dusk when solar input drops. Engineers suspect inefficiencies in the power distribution network. Given that the bus draws 3.8 A continuously, and the wiring between the solar arrays and the bus has a total resistance of 0.15 Ω, analyze whether the voltage sag is due to internal losses in the solar arrays, wiring resistance, or inadequate battery support. Determine the minimum internal resistance the solar arrays must have to prevent voltage sag.

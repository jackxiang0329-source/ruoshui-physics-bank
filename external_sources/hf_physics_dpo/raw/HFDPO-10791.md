# HFDPO-10791

Under 1.5 suns illumination, the device shows anomalous photocurrent enhancement at low bias voltages, but efficiency drops sharply above 0.3 V. Analyze whether the observed behavior arises from spin-dependent electron tunneling via localized defect states or from Pauli blocking in the quantum confined electron gas. Assess the role of Rashba splitting and electron-electron interactions in the observed voltage-dependent conductance.

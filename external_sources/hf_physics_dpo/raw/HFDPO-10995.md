# HFDPO-10995

In a semiconductor fabrication facility, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform surface charge density due to ion implantation during a high-voltage process. The charge distribution is modeled as σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r²/R²), where r is radial distance from the center, R = 7.5 cm, and σ₀ = 4.2 × 10⁻⁹ C/m². Engineers observe unintended particle attraction near the wafer edges during post-process cleaning. Estimate the total electrostatic force magnitude on a 5 μm-diameter dust particle with charge 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁴ C, located 1 mm above the wafer’s edge, and explain why the force is stronger than expected from simple point-charge approximations.

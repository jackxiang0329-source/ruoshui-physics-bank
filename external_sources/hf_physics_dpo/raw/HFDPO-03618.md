# HFDPO-03618

A solar-powered drone, designed for high-altitude atmospheric research, must ascend from 3,000 m to 12,000 m over 4 hours while maintaining a constant speed. The drone’s mass is 8.2 kg, and it experiences a drag force proportional to the square of its velocity, with a coefficient of 0.0025 kg/m. The solar array generates 140 W average power, but only 68% of this is usable due to efficiency losses. If the drone must also carry a 2.1 kg scientific payload, determine whether the energy supplied by the solar array is sufficient to overcome both gravitational potential energy increase and aerodynamic work during ascent.

# HFDPO-07028

A biomedical engineer is designing a multi-wavelength endoscope probe for early cancer detection using autofluorescence imaging. The probe uses 405 nm (violet) and 488 nm (blue) lasers to excite tissue, but the collected fluorescence signal is being corrupted by Rayleigh scattering from the 405 nm excitation wavelength. The scattering intensity at 405 nm is 15 times stronger than at 488 nm due to scattering ∝ 1/λ⁴. To improve signal clarity, they consider inserting a spatial filter that blocks scattered light but transmits emitted fluorescence. The filter’s transmission efficiency is 60% at 405 nm but drops to 35% at 488 nm. What is the net signal-to-scattering ratio improvement expected at 488 nm relative to 405 nm after filtering, assuming equal excitation power?

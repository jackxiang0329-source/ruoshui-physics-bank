# HFDPO-11048

A solar-powered calculator on a windowsill stops working when a cloud passes overhead, even though the room is still well-lit. The calculator uses a small silicon photodiode to convert light into electrical current. If the photodiode generates 2.5 mA under direct sunlight (intensity = 1000 W/m²), what happens to the current when the light intensity drops to 200 W/m²?

# HFDPO-13462

A laser printer's optical system uses a rotating polygon mirror to scan a 405 nm laser beam across a photosensitive drum. The mirror has 8 reflective faces and spins at 12,000 rpm. To achieve a resolution of 1200 dpi, the beam must be deflected across a precise 24 mm scan width in exactly 1/12000 second per scan line. Determine the required focal length of the collimating lens such that the beam divergence after reflection keeps the spot size below 10 μm at the drum surface, assuming the lens has a 20 mm diameter and the mirror is 50 mm from the lens.

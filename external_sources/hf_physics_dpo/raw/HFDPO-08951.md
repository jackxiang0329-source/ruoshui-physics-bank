# HFDPO-08951

A solar-powered streetlight in a remote village uses a small battery bank and three parallel LED circuits, each with a different resistance. One evening, the light dims unexpectedly. The technician measures 2.4 A flowing into the junction from the solar controller, and finds that one path carries 0.9 A through a 10 Ω resistor. What must be the current through the second branch if the third carries 0.5 A?

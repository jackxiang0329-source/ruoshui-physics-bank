# HFDPO-02266

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a high-resolution imaging system detects a faint glow from a hydrothermal vent at a depth of 2,800 m. The camera lens has a focal length of 150 mm and is mounted in a pressure-resistant dome with a refractive index of 1.52. Due to the extreme pressure, the dome's curvature slightly deforms, causing spherical aberration that blurs the image. The vent emits light at 480 nm, and the camera’s aperture is f/4.5. Calculate the optimal position of a corrective lens (in air) placed just outside the dome to eliminate spherical aberration, assuming the original dome acts as a thick lens with an effective radius of curvature of 120 mm. The lens must be designed to maintain focus on objects 5 m from the dome’s outer surface, even as pressure causes radial deformation.

# HFDPO-14875

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden micro-explosion in its plasma boundary layer, ejecting a 12 kg plasma slug at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to the toroidal magnetic field. A second, identical slug is expelled in the opposite direction 5.2 ms later. Determine the final net momentum of the vessel and calculate the induced recoil velocity at the moment the second slug is released, using only conservation of linear momentum and energy.

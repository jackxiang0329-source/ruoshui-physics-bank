# HFDPO-00886

A geophysicist is analyzing magnetic anomalies near a volcanic island chain, where magnetometers detect a 0.8% deviation in Earth’s magnetic field over a 15 km² region. Assuming the anomaly is caused by subsurface basaltic rock with a uniform magnetization of 1.2 × 10⁵ A/m, estimate the depth to the top of the magnetic source if the rock forms a horizontal, rectangular prism embedded in the crust. Use the field of a magnetic dipole approximation and Earth’s field strength of 50 μT at the surface.

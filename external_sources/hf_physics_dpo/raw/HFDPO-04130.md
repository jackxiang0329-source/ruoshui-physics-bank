# HFDPO-04130

A quantum computing lab uses superconducting qubits cooled to 15 mK, where microwave photons (frequency ~5 GHz) are transmitted through a narrow waveguide to manipulate quantum states. However, stray electromagnetic radiation from nearby MRI equipment (operating at 7 T, emitting broadband noise up to 300 MHz) is degrading qubit coherence. The waveguide is 12 cm long and terminates in a perfect reflector. If the lab must ensure that no standing wave antinodes form at the qubit location (1 cm from the input end), what minimum attenuation (in dB) must the waveguide’s impedance-matching network provide to suppress the MRI’s 300 MHz interference by at least 99.9%? Assume the waveguide supports only TE₁₀ mode and use realistic propagation constants.

# HFDPO-16172

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a hybrid lattice tower made of carbon-fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) struts and steel cables to support a 45-ton drill head at a 60° tilt. The tower’s base is anchored to bedrock with four foundation bolts, each rated for 120 kN tensile strength. During a high-wind event (15 m/s), wind loads induce a lateral force of 80 kN at the midpoint of the tower. The rig’s center of mass is offset 2.3 m from the base centerline due to the drill head’s position. Determine whether the foundation bolts will fail under combined axial, torsional, and bending stresses from the wind load and gravitational offset.

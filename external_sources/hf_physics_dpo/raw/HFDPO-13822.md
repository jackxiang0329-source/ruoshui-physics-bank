# HFDPO-13822

A marine biologist uses a waterproof camera with a fixed focal length lens to observe coral growth at 3 meters below the ocean surface. The camera's sensor is 2 cm behind the lens, and the biologist notices that the corals appear blurred. The refractive index of seawater is 1.34. What must the lens’s focal length be adjusted to in order to focus sharply on the coral, and what effect does the water’s refractive index have on the effective optical power of the lens?

# HFDPO-02496

A remote weather station in Antarctica uses a solar array and battery bank to power its sensors, but during prolonged polar night, the system relies solely on a 24V lithium-ion battery bank (1200 Ah capacity) feeding a DC-DC buck converter that supplies 12V to critical instruments. If the load draws 1.5 A at 12V, what is the actual voltage delivered to the load, and how long can the system sustain this load before reaching the cutoff voltage of 10V?

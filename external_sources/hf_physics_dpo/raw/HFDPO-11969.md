# HFDPO-11969

In a deep-space cryogenic storage module for a Mars mission, a sealed chamber containing liquid nitrogen (LN₂) at 77 K gradually warms due to leakage of solar radiation, causing intermittent boiling. The chamber has a volume of 0.5 m³, initially filled with 150 kg of LN₂. A sudden power failure causes the thermal insulation to degrade, increasing heat ingress to 4.2 W/m². The chamber’s surface area is 2.3 m², and the boiling point of nitrogen rises nonlinearly with pressure due to superheating. The system must not exceed 85 K to prevent mechanical stress on the walls. At what time does the system cross the danger threshold, assuming the pressure remains below the critical point and the evaporation rate follows the Clausius–Clapeyron relation with a latent heat of vaporization of 199 kJ/kg?

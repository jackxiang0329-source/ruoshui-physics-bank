# HFDPO-00091

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates 500 kW of power during peak wind conditions. Engineers notice that over time, the turbine blades accumulate ice, reducing their efficiency. On a cold, windy day, the blades are coated with 2.5 kg of ice uniformly distributed across one blade (total blade mass: 150 kg). If the wind speed remains constant but the turbine’s rotational speed drops by 15% due to the added mass and drag, how much additional work must the wind do per revolution to maintain the original power output? Assume the blade’s moment of inertia increases proportionally to the mass distribution and no energy is lost to heat.

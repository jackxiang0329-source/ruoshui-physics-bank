# HFDPO-04307

A team of cryogenic engineers is developing a superconducting quantum processor cooled to 10 mK using a dilution refrigerator. During a cooldown cycle, they observe an unexpected, rapid temperature spike in the 3He bath at ~150 mK, disrupting qubit coherence. Could a phase transition in the 3He–4He mixture be responsible?

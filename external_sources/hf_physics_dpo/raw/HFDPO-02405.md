# HFDPO-02405

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable cardiac monitor that uses capacitive sensors to detect subtle changes in chest impedance during each heartbeat. The sensor consists of two parallel plates separated by a 0.2 mm layer of human skin (dielectric constant ≈ 3.5), with a fixed area of 5 cm². During a patient's resting phase, the system measures a capacitance of 28 pF. However, during a contraction, the effective area of the sensor increases by 10% due to muscle movement. What is the new capacitance? How does this change affect the signal-to-noise ratio in the device’s measurement circuit, assuming the signal depends on capacitive change?

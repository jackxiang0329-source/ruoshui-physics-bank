# HFDPO-09154

A quantum imaging lab develops a new single-photon camera using time-correlated single-photon counting (TCSPC) to detect faint fluorescence from neural activity in living tissue. What is the minimum number of photons per pulse needed to achieve a 0.1% false-positive rate in the presence of 10^5 background photons per second? Assume Poisson statistics, Gaussian timing window, and perfect spectral filtering.

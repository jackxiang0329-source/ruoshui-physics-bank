# HFDPO-05735

A satellite orbiting Mars uses a laser communication array with a polarization-diversity receiver to mitigate signal degradation from dust storms, but during a major storm, the signal-to-noise ratio drops despite optimal alignment. The array’s polarizer has a 45° orientation relative to the transmitted beam, which is linearly polarized at 30° to the horizontal. Dust particles, aligned by atmospheric electric fields, induce birefringence with a path difference of 0.12λ between orthogonal modes. Determine the degree of polarization of the received signal and the optimal receiver polarization angle to maximize SNR under these conditions.

# HFDPO-10603

A coastal city is designing a new wave-energy converter that uses a vertical U-tube submerged in the ocean, where rising and falling sea levels drive water flow through a narrow constriction. During a storm, the water surface on one side rises 1.2 m above the calm average, while the other side remains at average level. The tube has a wide section (diameter 0.8 m) and a narrow throat (diameter 0.2 m). If the system must avoid cavitation (vapor bubbles forming due to pressure drop), what is the minimum pressure difference across the throat that must be maintained to prevent flow separation?

# HFDPO-08554

A solar farm in a high-altitude desert uses perovskite-silicon tandem cells with a bandgap of 1.1 eV for the bottom cell and 1.6 eV for the top cell. During peak irradiance (1200 W/m²), the junctions exhibit non-ideal recombination due to interfacial defects, reducing the open-circuit voltage by 80 mV in the top cell. The top cell’s current is measured at 28 mA/cm² under AM1.5G spectrum. Design a recombination passivation strategy that balances interface dipole formation and carrier mobility degradation, given that the defect density is 2×10¹² cm⁻² and the effective mass of electrons in the perovskite is 0.2m₀. What thickness of a 2 nm ultrathin Al₂O₃ layer is needed to achieve a 95% reduction in interface recombination velocity?

# HFDPO-04443

A research team is designing a magnetic levitation system for a next-generation quantum computing chip housed in a dilution refrigerator. The chip must remain stable at 15 mK, suspended above a superconducting Niobium plate. The levitating chip has a mass of 2.3 grams and induces screening currents in the plate that generate a repulsive force. If the magnetic field gradient near the plate’s surface must be at least 0.85 T/mm to maintain stable levitation, what is the minimum supercurrent density required in the Nb plate, and how does this limit the practical thickness of the superconducting layer?

# HFDPO-14340

A deep-space probe, traveling at 0.95c relative to Earth, emits a continuous laser signal at 500 nm (green light) to synchronize with a distant satellite network. Due to relativistic Doppler shifting and time dilation, the signal received on Earth appears redshifted and the emission rate is slowed. If Earth measures the signal’s wavelength as 1,200 nm and the rate of photons per second as 1.2 × 10⁶, what is the probe’s actual emission rate and wavelength in its rest frame? Assume the probe is moving directly away.

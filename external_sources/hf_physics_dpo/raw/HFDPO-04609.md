# HFDPO-04609

A solar panel array on a residential roof is mounted at a 30° tilt to maximize sun exposure. The panel weighs 60 kg and is secured at its center with a single bolt. If the wind exerts a horizontal force of 120 N at the panel’s top edge, what is the minimum friction force required at the base to prevent the panel from rotating? Assume the panel is 2.4 m long and uniform.

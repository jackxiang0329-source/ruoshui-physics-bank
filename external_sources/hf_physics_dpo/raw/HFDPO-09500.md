# HFDPO-09500

An astronomer on Earth detects these signals, but notes they arrive every 16.7 seconds. However, due to the probe’s increasing distance, the light travel time also affects the observed interval. What is the actual time between signal receptions on Earth, accounting for both time dilation and the increasing distance, if the probe is currently 2.4 light-minutes away?

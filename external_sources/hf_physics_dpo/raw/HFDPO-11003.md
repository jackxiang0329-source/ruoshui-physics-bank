# HFDPO-11003

In a next-generation photovoltaic tandem cell, a perovskite top layer (bandgap 1.65 eV) is lattice-matched to a silicon bottom layer (bandgap 1.12 eV) via a thin, ultra-transparent tunneling junction. During operation under 1-sun AM1.5G illumination, the top cell’s open-circuit voltage drops by 120 mV when the incident light spectrum shifts toward shorter wavelengths—despite no change in total irradiance. Explain this non-monotonic behavior and calculate the dominant recombination mechanism (radiative, Auger, SRH) in the perovskite layer if the photogenerated carrier density reaches 3×10¹⁷ cm⁻³ at peak absorption.

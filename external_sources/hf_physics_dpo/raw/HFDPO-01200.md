# HFDPO-01200

A recording engineer is tuning a live theater’s acoustics for a classical performance. The hall’s main stage has a 12-meter-wide balcony overhang that partially blocks sound from reaching the rear seats. Measurements show that at 150 Hz, the sound intensity at the back row drops by 18 dB due to destructive interference. The engineer suspects the issue stems from path-length differences between direct sound and reflections off the overhang. Using the speed of sound at 343 m/s, calculate the minimum vertical distance from the stage to the overhang’s underside that would cause destructive interference at 150 Hz for a listener directly behind the overhang.

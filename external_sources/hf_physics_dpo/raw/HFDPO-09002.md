# HFDPO-09002

A marine biologist studying coral reef health notices that reef-dwelling fish are avoiding a specific acoustic habitat near a newly constructed offshore wind farm. The wind turbines generate broadband noise peaking at 120 dB at 50 Hz, with significant harmonics up to 1 kHz. Sound intensity drops by 15 dB per decade of distance in shallow seawater. The biologist measures a 30 dB sound pressure level at 500 m from the turbine array. Calculate the distance at which the 50 Hz fundamental frequency falls below 60 dB, the threshold believed to disrupt fish communication. Assume spherical spreading and negligible absorption.

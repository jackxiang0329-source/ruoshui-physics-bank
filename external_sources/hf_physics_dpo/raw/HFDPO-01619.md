# HFDPO-01619

A solar farm in Arizona uses inverters to convert DC from photovoltaic panels into 60 Hz AC for the grid. During peak sunlight, one inverter’s output shows a voltage of 240 V (RMS) but a current lagging by 32° due to inductive loads from nearby transformers. The inverter’s internal electronics detect a power factor of 0.85 and a real power output of 22 kW. What is the apparent power, and what inductance (in mH) must be added in parallel to correct the power factor to 0.98?

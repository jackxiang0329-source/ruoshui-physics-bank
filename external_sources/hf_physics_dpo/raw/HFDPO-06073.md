# HFDPO-06073

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km into volcanic rock to extract heat. A 250 kg steel probe with a 0.8 m diameter cylindrical shape is lowered at constant speed using a winch system. The probe experiences viscous drag from hot water and friction from rock contact, modeled by a resistive force F_{text{res}} = -kv^2 , where k = 0.25 , text{kg/m} . If the probe enters the hot zone at 2.0 km depth and the winch applies a constant tension of 12,000 N to maintain constant velocity, what is the total mechanical energy dissipated by the probe during the final 1.2 km descent?

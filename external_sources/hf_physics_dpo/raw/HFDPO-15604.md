# HFDPO-15604

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible neural interface for brain-computer communication, using a polymer strip (Young’s modulus 5 MPa, Poisson’s ratio 0.45) with a rectangular cross-section (0.2 mm × 1.0 mm) bonded to a rigid silicon substrate. During implantation, the strip is stretched 1.5% elastically and then released. Due to viscoelastic relaxation in the polymer and interface shearing, the residual stress induces micro-cracking after repeated cycles. What is the maximum sustainable strain amplitude before fatigue failure, assuming fracture occurs when tensile stress exceeds 42 MPa and interface shear stress exceeds 12 MPa? Include time-dependent recovery and non-uniform strain distribution over the cross-section.

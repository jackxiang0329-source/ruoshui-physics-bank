# HFDPO-03092

A high-efficiency fusion reactor's divertor plate, composed of a carbon-fiber-reinforced carbon (C/C) composite, experiences transient heat fluxes of 20 MW/m² during plasma disruptions. The plate is 5 mm thick with a thermal conductivity of 12 W/m·K and a volumetric heat capacity of 2.5 × 10⁶ J/m³·K. During a 100 ms disruption, the surface temperature spikes to 2400 K. Model the temperature rise at the rear face (insulated) and determine whether localized melting (melting point: 3800 K) is likely. Use both a finite-difference time-domain approach and an analytical solution based on the parabolic heat equation with a step-function heat flux.

# HFDPO-14702

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert region uses a silicon photodiode to measure sunlight intensity. The diode has a bandgap of 1.12 eV and operates at 40°C. During peak sun, the photodiode generates a photocurrent of 4.8 mA when illuminated with 1000 W/m² sunlight. However, during morning hours, the output drops to 1.2 mA under 250 W/m². A technician suspects recombination losses are increasing due to temperature rise. What is the expected photocurrent at 40°C if recombination is negligible and only the photon flux and bandgap matter?

# HFDPO-01538

During a severe winter storm, a remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar panel array to charge its battery bank through a 150-meter copper wire loop. The system operates at 24 V, and the station draws an average current of 3.2 A during peak operations. However, measurements show a 4.5 V drop across the wiring when the system is active. What is the minimum cross-sectional area of the copper wire needed to keep the voltage drop below 2.0 V under the same load, given copper’s resistivity is 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m?

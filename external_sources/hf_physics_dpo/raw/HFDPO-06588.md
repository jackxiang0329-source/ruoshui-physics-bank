# HFDPO-06588

A quantum computing chip in a cryogenic laboratory exhibits sudden decoherence during a sensitive entanglement experiment. The chip uses niobium-based Josephson junctions operating at 4.2 K, but fluctuations in the magnetic field trace to a nearby superconducting solenoid that accidentally switched modes—now partially quenching and generating transient fields. Given that the critical current density of the niobium is 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m², the junction width is 500 nm, and the magnetic flux quantum is Φ₀ = 2.07 × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb, determine the maximum sustainable magnetic flux threading a single junction without driving it into the resistive state during a dynamic field transition. Assume the junction is part of a loop with inductance L = 1.5 × 10⁻¹² H and that the field changes at a rate of 0.2 Φ₀/ns.

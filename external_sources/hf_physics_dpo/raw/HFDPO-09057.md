# HFDPO-09057

In a deep-space satellite mission, a new solar array uses a flexible, graphene-based conductor to transmit power from its panels to the onboard systems. The conductor is 150 meters long, with a rectangular cross-section that tapers uniformly from 2 mm × 10 mm at the power source to 1 mm × 5 mm at the far end. The array must deliver 450 W of power to a critical instrument at 120 V with less than 5% power loss due to resistive heating. Given the resistivity of graphene is 1.0 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m and the current is constant along the wire, what is the minimum current the system must handle without exceeding the power loss threshold?

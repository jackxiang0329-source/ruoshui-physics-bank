# HFDPO-14681

A biomedical engineer is designing a new optical biosensor that detects pathogen binding by monitoring changes in light diffraction patterns from a nanoscale grating array on a glass chip. The grating has 600 lines per millimeter, and the sensor uses laser light of wavelength 633 nm. When a virus binds to the surface, it locally increases the effective refractive index, shifting the diffraction maxima. During a test, the first-order maximum shifts from 12.3° to 13.1°. What is the change in the effective optical path length (in nm) caused by the virus binding?

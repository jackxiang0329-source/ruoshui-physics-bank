# HFDPO-00682

A deep-sea submersible descends at 0.8 m/s through a stratified ocean layer where water density increases linearly from 1020 kg/m³ at the surface to 1050 kg/m³ at 1000 m depth. Its streamlined hull experiences turbulent drag governed by a velocity-dependent coefficient, but frictional resistance from boundary layer adhesion with water is also significant. The sub’s mass is 2500 kg, cross-sectional area 1.2 m², and drag coefficient is 0.15 at 1 m/s. If the descent is to be stable (constant speed) at 0.8 m/s, what must be the effective thrust force provided by its propulsion system at 500 m depth, considering both variable fluid density and non-linear drag?

# HFDPO-07651

A small satellite in a circular low-Earth orbit (altitude 400 km) experiences a brief atmospheric drag force due to residual atmosphere at that altitude. After the drag, it enters a slightly elliptical orbit with a perigee of 380 km. What must be true about the satellite’s speed at the moment the drag acted, compared to its original circular orbit speed?

# HFDPO-11085

A coastal city faces recurring flooding during storm surges, prompting engineers to design a submerged permeable barrier offshore to reduce wave energy before it reaches the shoreline. The barrier, modeled as a porous wall with a thickness of 3 m and porosity of 0.4, is placed 500 m from the shore in water 15 m deep. Waves with a period of 10 s and height of 2.5 m approach from the open ocean. Using potential flow theory with Darcy’s law for porous media, determine whether the barrier can reduce the transmitted wave height by at least 30% at the shore. Assume linear wave theory and negligible wave breaking.

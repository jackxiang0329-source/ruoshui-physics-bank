# HFDPO-06854

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to extract heat from a fractured basalt formation at 4.5 km depth, where the rock temperature is 280°C. Water is pumped at 100 kg/min through a copper pipe (15 mm diameter, 3 mm wall thickness) with a thermal conductivity of 400 W/m·K. The pipe is insulated with a 10 mm layer of silica aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K), but the insulation has a localized defect covering 5% of the surface. At the surface, the water enters at 70°C. What is the minimum required pumping power to ensure the water exits with at least 250°C, considering non-uniform heat gain due to the insulation defect and variable convection coefficients (h_in = 1200 W/m²·K, h_out = 15 W/m²·K)? Assume steady-state, laminar flow and neglect kinetic energy changes.

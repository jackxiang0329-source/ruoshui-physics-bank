# HFDPO-11691

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses a high-temperature superconducting (HTS) tape wound into a solenoid coil to generate a 12 T field at 45 K. The tape has a critical current density of 1.5 × 10⁶ A/m² at 45 K and a width of 12 mm. During a sudden power fluctuation, the current in the coil drops by 8% over 0.5 seconds—just enough to push the local magnetic field near the inner edge above the critical field of 14 T. Determine whether a persistent current mode can be restored without quenching the coil, given the tape's self-field and the geometry of the winding.

# HFDPO-00458

A deep-space probe is orbiting a dense, non-spherical asteroid with a mass of 2.3 × 10¹⁸ kg and a radius that varies between 1.2 km and 1.8 km due to irregular shape. The probe maintains a stable elliptical orbit with a perigee of 1.3 km above the surface. If the asteroid's gravitational field deviates from spherical symmetry by 12% at the equator due to mass concentration, how much must the probe adjust its velocity at apogee to maintain a circular orbit at 2.0 km above the equatorial surface? Assume no atmospheric drag and use only gravitational data from the current trajectory.

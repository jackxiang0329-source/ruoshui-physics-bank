# HFDPO-02314

A research team designs a hybrid geothermal-photovoltaic power unit for remote Arctic villages, using a closed-loop organic Rankine cycle (ORC) with R-134a as working fluid. The system extracts 500 kW of heat from underground hot springs at 120°C, while ambient air temperatures hover near –15°C. The turbine operates with 88% isentropic efficiency, and the condenser rejects heat to an ambient air heat exchanger. However, during winter months, frost buildup reduces condenser effectiveness by 30%. Determine the actual net power output and thermal efficiency of the system under frost conditions, and propose one engineering modification to mitigate the efficiency loss.

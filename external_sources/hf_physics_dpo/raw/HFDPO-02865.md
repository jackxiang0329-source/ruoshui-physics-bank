# HFDPO-02865

A deep-sea submersible's periscope system uses a series of spherical mirrors to transmit images from 800 m below the surface to the crew cabin, where the observer’s eye is 2.4 m above the waterline. Due to thermal gradients in the ocean, the refractive index varies with depth: n(z) = 1.335 − 5.0×10⁻⁵ z, where z is depth in meters. The first mirror, a concave spherical mirror with 0.6 m radius of curvature, is located at 800 m depth. What is the optimal focal length of a secondary compensating lens placed at 790 m depth to correct for image distortion caused by the continuously varying refractive index, assuming the image must remain focused at the observer’s eye?

# HFDPO-11851

A lunar sample return lander descends under powered descent near the South Pole-Aitken Basin, where gravity is 1.62 m/s² and regolith density varies due to subsurface ice deposits. The lander’s thrusters provide variable thrust (5000–12,000 N) with 1% uncertainty in throttle response. At 80 m altitude, a sudden 0.3 m/s lateral wind gust (from a nearby crater wall) induces a 15° angle between thrust vector and vertical. Calculate the required thrust magnitude and direction to maintain a vertical descent with zero horizontal velocity at touchdown, given a mass of 2200 kg and drag coefficient of 0.85 (projected area 3.2 m²).

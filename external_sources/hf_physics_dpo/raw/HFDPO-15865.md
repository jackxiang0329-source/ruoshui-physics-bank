# HFDPO-15865

A solar thermal power plant uses molten salt (specific heat 1600 J/kg·K, density 1900 kg/m³) stored in an insulated cylindrical tank (diameter 8 m, height 12 m). After a cloudy day, the plant must restart by reheating the salt from 300°C to 550°C using electric resistance heaters embedded in the tank walls. The heaters deliver 1.8 MW of power, but due to uneven heat transfer, only 70% of the energy reaches the salt. The tank loses heat to the environment through convection (h = 12 W/m²·K) and radiation (ε = 0.9), with ambient temperature 25°C. How long will it take to safely reheat the salt, and what is the minimum operational safety margin (in °C) that must be maintained between the salt and the tank wall to prevent thermal stress?

# HFDPO-07977

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where deuterium-tritium pellets are compressed via magnetic fields to initiate fusion. During a pulse, 5.0 × 10⁻³ kg of fuel is injected at 1.2 × 10⁴ m/s tangentially into a 2.5 m radius plasma region. Due to magnetic confinement instabilities, 18% of the plasma’s angular momentum is lost to turbulent eddies over 15 ms. If the system’s moment of inertia is initially 3.2 × 10⁻⁴ kg·m² and the plasma must maintain rotational alignment to sustain confinement, calculate the minimum angular impulse that must be applied by external coils to compensate for the lost momentum.

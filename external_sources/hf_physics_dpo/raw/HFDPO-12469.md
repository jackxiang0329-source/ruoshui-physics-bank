# HFDPO-12469

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable optical sensor to detect early-stage skin cancer by analyzing how light scatters through layered skin tissue. The sensor uses a coherent laser (λ = 633 nm) shone at a 30° angle onto a thin, nanostructured polymer film embedded in the device. The film has a periodic grating with spacing d = 1.2 μm. When the light reflects off the grating, the first-order diffraction maximum (m = 1) is observed at an angle θ. The engineer needs to determine whether this peak will fall within the detector’s sensitive range (35° to 45°), considering both interference from the grating and the phase shift due to the angle of incidence.

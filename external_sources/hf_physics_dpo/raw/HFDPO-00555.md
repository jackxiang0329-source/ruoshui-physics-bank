# HFDPO-00555

A wind turbine blade in a coastal wind farm operates in a turbulent boundary layer with a freestream wind speed of 12 m/s. The blade’s chord length is 3.2 m and it spins at 60 rpm. Due to blade twist and variable wind shear, the local Reynolds number varies from 1.8 × 10⁶ near the root to 2.4 × 10⁶ at the tip. Engineers notice a sudden drop in efficiency during peak winds. Analyze whether flow separation is likely at the tip under these conditions, and determine how much of the blade’s performance loss can be attributed to increased drag from turbulence-induced separation versus reduced lift.

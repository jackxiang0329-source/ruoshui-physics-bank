# HFDPO-06963

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research is experiencing unexpected pitch instability during sustained flight. The drone’s rigid carbon-fiber frame supports a 1.2 kg photovoltaic array mounted 0.8 m forward of the center of mass, while three 0.6 kg lithium-ion batteries are distributed asymmetrically: two at the rear (1.5 m behind center of mass), one at the front (0.3 m ahead). The total weight acts through the center of mass, and lift forces act at a point 0.4 m behind the center of mass. Determine the minimum forward shift of the center of mass due to fuel consumption (assumed to be stored in the rear batteries) that would eliminate the pitch moment imbalance, using the changing distribution of mass to re-establish static equilibrium.

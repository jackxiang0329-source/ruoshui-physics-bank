# HFDPO-04521

A deep-sea submersible uses a curved, transparent acrylic viewport with a radius of curvature of 15 cm to observe hydrothermal vents at 2,500 meters depth. Due to the high pressure, the refractive index of the acrylic increases slightly from 1.49 (at surface) to 1.52 at depth. A scientist observes a 30 cm-long vent chimney that appears magnified and distorted. If the chimney is 1.2 m from the viewport’s inner surface and the observer’s eye is 10 cm behind the outer surface (with the viewport thickness 5 cm), how far and how large does the image appear to the observer, and what causes the distortion?

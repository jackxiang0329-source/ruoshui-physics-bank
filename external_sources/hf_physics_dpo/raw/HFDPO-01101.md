# HFDPO-01101

During a high-altitude mountain expedition, climbers notice their insulated water bottles develop frost on the outside after pouring hot tea, even though the ambient temperature is only -10°C. One climber claims the frost formed because the hot tea cooled rapidly and released latent heat of fusion into the air. Is this explanation accurate? Calculate the minimum mass of water vapor that must condense and freeze on the bottle surface to account for a 0.5 g frost layer, assuming the vapor comes from air at 100% relative humidity and the process releases the latent heat of vaporization and fusion.

# HFDPO-10301

A deep-space probe using a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) powered by plutonium-238 begins to show a 12% drop in power output after 18 years of operation in the outer solar system. The original design specified 475 W thermal output at launch. Given the half-life of Pu-238 is 87.7 years and assuming no decay heat from daughter products contributes significantly, what is the actual mass of Pu-238 that must have been onboard at launch to account for this measured power loss and the decay of the isotope’s activity?

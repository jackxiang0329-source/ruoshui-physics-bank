# HFDPO-04986

A prototype fusion reactor containment vessel operates at 150 million K, using magnetic confinement to isolate plasma from its walls. During a sudden power surge, the reactor’s thermal insulation degrades, allowing 2.4 × 10⁹ J of heat to leak into the surrounding superconducting magnets over 3.5 seconds. The magnets are designed to stay below 12 K to maintain zero resistance. Given that the magnets have a heat capacity of 4.2 × 10⁴ J/K, assess whether the magnet system remains within safe operating limits. Use the second law to evaluate whether the energy transfer is feasible under adiabatic constraints.

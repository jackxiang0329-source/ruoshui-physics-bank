# HFDPO-15355

A 25-meter-long solar panel array, mounted at a 15° tilt on a coastal wind farm, must withstand extreme gusts during storm season. The array’s center of mass is 12 m from the base, and the entire structure is anchored to a reinforced concrete foundation via a single pin connection at the base. During a 50 m/s wind gust (pressure ~1200 Pa), the panel experiences a distributed force that increases quadratically from 0 N/m at the base to 3000 N/m at the top. Determine the minimum coefficient of static friction required between the foundation and the ground to prevent sliding, assuming no overturning occurs.

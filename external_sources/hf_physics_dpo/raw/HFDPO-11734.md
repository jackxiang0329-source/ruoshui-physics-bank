# HFDPO-11734

A solar farm in Nevada uses 10,000 photovoltaic panels, each producing 400 W at peak sunlight. The panels are wired in series strings of 20, connected to a central inverter via copper cables running 300 meters. If the system must operate at 98% efficiency to meet grid demands, what is the minimum gauge of copper cable needed to limit power loss in the transmission lines to less than 2% of total output? Assume the inverter operates at 600 V DC and wires are laid underground (ambient temp 45°C).

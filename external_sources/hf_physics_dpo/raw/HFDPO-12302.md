# HFDPO-12302

A quantum optics lab is developing a compact photonic chip for secure quantum communication, using a 128-element array of subwavelength slits (width 300 nm) in a silicon nitride waveguide, spaced 1.2 μm apart. When illuminated by a coherent laser (λ = 633 nm), the far-field intensity pattern shows unexpected side lobes that degrade signal fidelity. The design team suspects a phase error due to fabrication-induced asymmetry in the slit edges. Determine whether the observed intensity modulation is best explained by single-slit diffraction, multi-slit interference, or a combination of both with phase errors.

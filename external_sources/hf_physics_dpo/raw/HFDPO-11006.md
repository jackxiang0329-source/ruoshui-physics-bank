# HFDPO-11006

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a dual-polarization antenna to receive signals from a ground station, but interference from reflected signals off a nearby mountain range causes signal degradation. The direct signal is vertically polarized, while the reflected signal—having bounced off a steep, metallic cliff at a 60° angle of incidence—arrives with mixed polarization. Given the cliff’s refractive index (n = 2.5) and the polarization state of the incoming wave, determine the degree of polarization (DoP) of the reflected wave and the resulting signal-to-interference ratio (SIR) at the satellite receiver, assuming the antenna is perfectly tuned to vertical polarization.

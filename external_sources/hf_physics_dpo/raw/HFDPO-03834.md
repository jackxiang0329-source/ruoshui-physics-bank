# HFDPO-03834

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar panel array with 24 identical photovoltaic cells (each 0.6 V, internal resistance 0.1 Ω) is connected in series to charge a 24 V battery bank. During winter twilight, sunlight intensity drops to 30% of full irradiance, reducing each cell’s open-circuit voltage to 0.45 V and increasing internal resistance to 0.35 Ω. A critical instrument requires a stable 12 V, 50 mA load. A technician connects a buck converter (efficiency 90%) across the battery bank, but the output voltage sags below 12 V under load. Determine the minimum battery bank voltage required to maintain 12 V output, assuming the converter draws current linearly with load and the battery’s internal resistance is 0.2 Ω.

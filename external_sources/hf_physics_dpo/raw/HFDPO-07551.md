# HFDPO-07551

The system’s response is governed by a forced, damped harmonic oscillator equation with nonlinear stiffness due to geometric effects: k_{text{eff}}(x) = k(1 + 0.1x^2) , where x is the relative displacement. At steady state, what is the peak relative displacement between the reactor and the ground, and how does this value compare to the 0.05 m safety threshold?

# HFDPO-15847

During a critical calibration run, the system exhibits unexpected flux noise that corrupts qubit coherence times, despite maintaining perfect vacuum and stable magnetic shielding. What physical mechanism most plausibly explains the observed noise, and how does it challenge assumptions about "ideal" superconducting behavior in quantum circuits?

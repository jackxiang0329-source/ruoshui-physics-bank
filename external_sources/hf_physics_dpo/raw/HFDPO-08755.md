# HFDPO-08755

In a prototype lithium-ion battery manufacturing line, electrostatic discharge (ESD) events are disrupting the deposition of a nano-scale cathode material (LiCoO₂) onto aluminum current collectors. During processing, a 50 nm-thick polymer film on the collector accumulates charge due to triboelectric contact with conveyor rollers, reaching -120 V relative to ground. The cathode powder, with an average particle diameter of 200 nm and charge of -1.6 × 10⁻¹⁸ C per particle, is suspended in an inert gas. At what minimum distance from the charged film will the electrostatic attraction cause a particle to veer off its intended trajectory and contact the surface prematurely? Assume a particle mass of 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁸ kg and uniform electric field approximation.

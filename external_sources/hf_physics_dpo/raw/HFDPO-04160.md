# HFDPO-04160

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote mountain village powers a community center via a 1.5 km overhead transmission line with 0.8 Ω/km resistance and 2.5 mH/km inductance. The turbine outputs 240 V RMS at 60 Hz, but the lights in the center flicker and the HVAC system overheats. Measurements show a lagging power factor of 0.65 and average real power consumption of 18 kW. What is the required capacitance to correct the power factor to 0.95, and how does this affect the voltage at the load?

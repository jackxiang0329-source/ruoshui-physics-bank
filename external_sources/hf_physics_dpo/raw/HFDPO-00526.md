# HFDPO-00526

In a remote solar-powered research station, a 120 V RMS, 60 Hz AC inverter supplies power to a sensitive scientific instrument requiring a nearly pure sinusoidal current. However, the system’s inductive load (a superconducting solenoid coil) causes a lagging power factor of 0.65, leading to excessive reactive power and overheating in the inverter’s output stage. The station’s engineer must design a passive correction network using a parallel capacitor bank to raise the overall power factor to at least 0.95 without altering the load’s real power consumption. Given that the load draws 4.8 kW of real power, determine the required capacitance and calculate the new line current, comparing it to the original.

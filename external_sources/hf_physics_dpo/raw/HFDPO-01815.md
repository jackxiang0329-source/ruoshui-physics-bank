# HFDPO-01815

A private space station in a 400 km circular LEO is targeting a high-priority microgravity experiment module docked at a geostationary orbit (GEO) satellite. The station must deploy a nanosatellite via a slingshot maneuver around the Moon to intercept the GEO module on its next pass—currently 8 hours ahead in orbit. The module orbits at 42,164 km radius with 0.2° inclination, while the station’s current orbit is at 6778 km radius, 28.5° inclination. The Moon’s orbit is nearly circular at 384,400 km with 5.1° inclination. What sequence of Δv burns and lunar flyby timing minimizes total Δv while ensuring the nanosatellite reaches the module's position with <50 m/s relative velocity?

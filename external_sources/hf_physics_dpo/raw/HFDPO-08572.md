# HFDPO-08572

A satellite in geostationary orbit over the equator must perform a station-keeping maneuver to counteract gravitational perturbations from the Moon and Earth’s oblateness. Over 30 days, its inclination drifts by 0.8° due to the Moon’s pull. What average delta-v in m/s is required to correct this drift using a single impulse at the ascending node? Assume the satellite’s initial orbit is circular at 42,164 km radius, and Earth’s J₂ coefficient is 1.0826 × 10⁻³.

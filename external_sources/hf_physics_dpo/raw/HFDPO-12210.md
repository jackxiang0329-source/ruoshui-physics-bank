# HFDPO-12210

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood based on size differences using laminar flow in a serpentine channel. The channel is 50 μm wide, 10 μm high, and 2 mm long, with blood (μ = 3.5 mPa·s, ρ = 1060 kg/m³) flowing at 1.2 mm/s. The CTCs (diameter ~12 μm) experience drag significantly different from red blood cells (~7 μm). What is the ratio of the drag force on a CTC to that on a red blood cell if both are modeled as spheres in Stokes flow?

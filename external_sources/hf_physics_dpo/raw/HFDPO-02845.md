# HFDPO-02845

Using a 15 cm diameter lens with f = 200 cm, determine the minimum angular resolution required to resolve the reflected image, and assess whether the system can distinguish the satellite’s position from background starlight with a 0.1 arcsec angular separation, considering both diffraction limits and geometric aberration due to misalignment.

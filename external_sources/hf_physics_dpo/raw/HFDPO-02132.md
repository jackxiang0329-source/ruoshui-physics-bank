# HFDPO-02132

A fusion reactor in a remote Arctic research station experiences anomalous neutron fluence spikes during operation, despite stable plasma confinement and no detectable tritium leaks. The reactor uses a deuterium-tritium fuel cycle with a 10 MW thermal output. Measurements show a 40% increase in neutron flux at the reactor's inner wall over a 3-week period, but neutron activation analysis of the wall material shows negligible buildup of ⁶Li or ⁷Li—unusual, since ⁶Li(n,α)He-4 is a dominant neutron capture path in lithium-bearing structural materials. What physical mechanism, not involving fuel or plasma instability, could explain the spike in neutron fluence while preserving the absence of lithium activation?

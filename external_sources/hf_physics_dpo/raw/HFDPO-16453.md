# HFDPO-16453

A laser-based holographic display in a medical imaging lab uses a coherent 633 nm He-Ne laser to project a 3D image of a patient’s lung tissue. The reference beam is split and directed at a photosensitive holographic film at a 30° angle relative to the object beam. During calibration, the lab detects a noticeable ghost image due to a stray reflection from the optical table. If the film has a spatial resolution of 2000 lines/mm and the ghost arises from a 2 mm thick surface layer of the film, what is the minimum angle of incidence that would cause the ghost image to be resolved from the primary image using diffraction-limited optics?

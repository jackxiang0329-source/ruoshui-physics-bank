# HFDPO-13351

A deep-sea research submersible descends through the ocean, encountering a submerged, rigid cylindrical structure (diameter 1.2 m, length 4.0 m) resting on the seabed at 800 m depth. If the water temperature drops from 4°C to 1.5°C near the cylinder (affecting water density), what is the change in net upward buoyant force acting on the cylinder? Assume the cylinder is perfectly sealed and the pressure change is negligible for density variation.

# HFDPO-00881

In a high-altitude solar-powered drone, a GaN-on-SiC power transistor operates at 400°C during sustained flight. The device exhibits unexpected current leakage, measured at 12 μA at V<sub>DS</sub> = 30 V and V<sub>GS</sub> = 0 V, despite being in cutoff. The bandgap of GaN is 3.4 eV, and the substrate is semi-insulating SiC with a resistivity of 10⁸ Ω·cm. The device has a 2 μm thick GaN drift layer with a donor density of 1×10¹⁶ cm⁻³. Determine whether the leakage is primarily due to band-to-band tunneling (BTBT) or Shockley-Read-Hall (SRH) recombination via deep traps, assuming a trap energy level at E<sub>t</sub> = E<sub>c</sub> − 0.8 eV and an effective trap cross-section of 1×10⁻¹⁶ cm².

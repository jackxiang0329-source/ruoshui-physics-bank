# HFDPO-05133

A deep-sea mining drone descends to 3,000 meters below the ocean surface, where the water temperature is 4°C and pressure is 300 atm. What is the minimum theoretical increase in entropy of the universe during the 20-minute ascent from 3,000 m to 100 m, assuming the drone’s internal heat capacity is 250 kJ/K and the surface water remains at 10°C?

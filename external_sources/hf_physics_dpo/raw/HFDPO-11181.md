# HFDPO-11181

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a charged microchip (mass 0.15 g, charge +2.4 nC) is suspended between two parallel plates spaced 1.2 cm apart. The top plate is held at +150 V, and the bottom at -150 V. A technician notices the chip slowly drifting downward due to a slight imbalance in the electric field. To stabilize it, they apply a weak horizontal electric field of 800 N/C. What must the vertical component of the electric field be to perfectly balance the chip’s weight? Assume uniform fields and neglect air resistance.

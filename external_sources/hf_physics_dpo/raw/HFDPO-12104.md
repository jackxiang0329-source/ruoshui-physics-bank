# HFDPO-12104

A wind turbine blade in a remote Arctic installation begins developing a slow, persistent tilt during winter months, despite being secured with high-strength bolts rated for 50 kN of shear force. The blade, 60 m long and weighing 45,000 kg, is mounted on a tower with a 12° pitch. During extreme cold, ice accretes asymmetrically—300 kg on the leading edge of the blade’s lower half, creating a 5° cant. Determine the minimum coefficient of static friction required between the blade’s mounting hub and the tower flange to prevent rotational slip under these ice-loaded conditions, assuming the center of mass shifts 1.8 m from the axis due to ice.

# HFDPO-11912

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoscale semiconductor particles to absorb sunlight. One device employs CdSe quantum dots with a diameter of 4.2 nm, engineered so that the energy gap corresponds to the peak of the solar spectrum (λ = 550 nm). When a photon strikes a dot, it excites an electron from the valence to the conduction band. However, the dot’s small size leads to quantum confinement, altering the electron’s wavefunction and effective mass. If the quantum dot’s electron behaves as a particle in a 3D infinite potential well, what is the minimum photon energy required to excite the electron from the ground state to the first excited state? Assume the effective mass of the electron is 0.15 times that of a free electron.

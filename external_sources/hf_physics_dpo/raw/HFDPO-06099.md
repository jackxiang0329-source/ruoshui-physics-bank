# HFDPO-06099

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoscale semiconductor particles to absorb sunlight, but efficiency drops sharply at room temperature due to phonon-assisted electron relaxation. If the energy gap between the excited electron state and the ground state is 1.8 eV, and the average thermal energy at 300 K is 0.025 eV, what fraction of excited electrons are likely to lose energy non-radiatively via lattice vibrations before emitting a photon? Assume Boltzmann statistics govern the phonon population, and consider that non-radiative decay is significantly enhanced when the thermal energy is comparable to the energy gap.

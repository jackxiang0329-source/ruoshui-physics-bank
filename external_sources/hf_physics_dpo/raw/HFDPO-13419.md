# HFDPO-13419

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement chamber uses a toroidal field coil system with 1200 turns carrying 10 kA to stabilize plasma at 15 keV. During a sudden current surge, the field collapses non-uniformly, inducing eddy currents in the surrounding copper shielding. Given the shielding’s resistivity (1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m), permeability (μ₀), and thickness (5 mm), determine the time scale over which the induced currents dissipate 90% of their peak energy, assuming a self-inductance of 0.8 H.

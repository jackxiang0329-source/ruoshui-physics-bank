# HFDPO-13123

During a deep-sea exploration, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, flexible rubber bladder used to stabilize a seafloor sensor array. The bladder is anchored at the bottom, partially inflated with helium at 1.5 atm (absolute), and hangs vertically in seawater at 4°C. The bladder’s top is at 300 m depth, its bottom at 310 m, and its internal pressure is regulated to prevent collapse. A technician suspects the bladder may rupture due to excessive pressure differential. Evaluate whether the internal pressure can sustain the external hydrostatic pressure at the bottom of the bladder, given the density of seawater is 1025 kg/m³ and gravity is 9.81 m/s².

# HFDPO-15123

During a deep-sea drilling operation, a 2.4 m diameter, 800 m long riser pipe connects a floating platform to a subsea wellhead. During a storm, the platform experiences pitch and roll motions with a 12-second period and 5° amplitude. At what critical flow rate does vortex-induced vibration (VIV) onset become likely due to the interaction between the oscillating platform motion and the internal flow instability, and how might the riser’s effective damping change under these conditions?

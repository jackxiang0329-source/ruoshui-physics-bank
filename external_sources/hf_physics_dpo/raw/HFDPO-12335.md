# HFDPO-12335

In a semiconductor fab, a 50 nm-thick silicon wafer (dielectric constant ε = 11.7) is being charged by a corona wire during a critical etching step. The wire deposits a uniform surface charge density of +1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/m². An adjacent 100 nm-thick dielectric layer (ε = 4.5) on the wafer surface becomes electrically polarized, leading to unexpected electrostatic attraction between the wafer and a nearby grounded metal electrode 1.5 μm away. What is the magnitude of the electric field experienced by the electrode due to the combined effects of free charge and polarization charge?

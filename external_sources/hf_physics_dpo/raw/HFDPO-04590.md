# HFDPO-04590

A deep-sea mining probe descends into the Mariana Trench, where hydrostatic pressure reaches 110 MPa at 11 km depth. The probe’s spherical observation dome has a 0.5 m diameter and is sealed with a titanium alloy hatch rated for 500 kN of clamping force. As the probe ascends through the water column, the internal pressure is maintained at 1.2 atm by a rigid, sealed gas bladder. At what depth does the external hydrostatic pressure cause the hatch to fail due to net inward force exceeding its clamping limit? Assume seawater density is 1025 kg/m³ and gravity is 9.8 m/s².

# HFDPO-12207

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 1.2 km deep, 15 cm diameter copper coil (100 turns/meter) to generate electricity from natural temperature gradients. As magma heats the lower end, the temperature difference induces a time-varying magnetic field due to thermoelectric currents in the surrounding rock. If the temperature increases at 0.8 K/s, and the permeability of the rock is 1.05μ₀, what is the average induced emf in a single loop of the coil over one minute, assuming the magnetic flux changes linearly with temperature?

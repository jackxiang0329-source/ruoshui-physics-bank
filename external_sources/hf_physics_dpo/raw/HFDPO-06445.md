# HFDPO-06445

A homeowner notices that their attic vents are sucking in dust and debris during strong winds, even though the vents are closed. They suspect air pressure differences caused by wind speed over the roof are pulling air upward through gaps. If wind blows at 15 m/s just above the roof, and the air inside the attic is still, what is the pressure difference between the outside air above the roof and the air inside the attic? Assume air density is 1.2 kg/m³.

# HFDPO-06927

A biomedical engineer is designing a dynamic stent for coronary arteries that must expand radially by 15% under physiological pressure (120 mmHg) while maintaining structural integrity over 100,000 cardiac cycles. The stent’s scaffold is made of a shape-memory alloy with a Young’s modulus of 120 GPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.35, but its internal microstructure exhibits non-uniform stiffness due to additive manufacturing defects. The engineer models the stent as a thin-walled cylindrical shell with inner radius 3.2 mm, thickness 0.1 mm, and length 12 mm. Determine the minimum yield strength required to prevent plastic deformation during expansion, assuming the hoop stress exceeds the elastic limit if the radial displacement exceeds 0.48 mm.

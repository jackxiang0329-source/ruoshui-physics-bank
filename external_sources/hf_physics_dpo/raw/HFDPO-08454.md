# HFDPO-08454

A coastal community is designing a floating solar farm on a calm lagoon. Each solar panel platform is a rectangular raft measuring 4 m × 6 m and floats at a 15 cm draft (submerged depth). The raft is made of lightweight, waterproof foam with a density of 200 kg/m³. If the total mass of the solar panels and electronics is 1,200 kg, what is the minimum density of the foam needed to keep the raft afloat without sinking below 20 cm draft?

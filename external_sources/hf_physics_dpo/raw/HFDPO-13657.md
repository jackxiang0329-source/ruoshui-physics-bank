# HFDPO-13657

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for an implantable cardiac pacemaker. The external charger uses a 5 cm diameter coil with 100 turns, driven by a 100 kHz AC current. If the external current rises from 0 to 2 A in 20 µs, what is the average induced emf in the implant coil? Assume 60% magnetic flux linkage efficiency due to misalignment and tissue attenuation.

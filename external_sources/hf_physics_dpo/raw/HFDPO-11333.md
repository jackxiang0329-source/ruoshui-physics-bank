# HFDPO-11333

The shield, 1.2 cm thick, has an initial internal temperature of 280 K and is insulated on one side. If the temperature distribution must remain below 220 K at any point to avoid structural stress, can the shield withstand the thermal shock without active heating? Assume one-dimensional conduction, and use the heat equation with time-dependent boundary conditions.

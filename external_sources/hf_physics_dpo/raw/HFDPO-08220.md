# HFDPO-08220

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip for targeted drug delivery using a bifurcating channel system. At a junction, a fluid stream splits into two branches with cross-sectional areas of 100 µm² and 25 µm², respectively. The inlet velocity is 0.8 m/s, and the fluid (blood-mimetic, viscosity 3.5 mPa·s) flows at a Reynolds number of 1,200. Due to the sharp bend in the smaller branch, pressure drops significantly—how must the pressure difference between the inlet and the outlet of the smaller branch be adjusted to maintain laminar flow while ensuring 20% of total flow is directed into this branch?

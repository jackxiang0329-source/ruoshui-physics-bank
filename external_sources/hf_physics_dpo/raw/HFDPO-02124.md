# HFDPO-02124

During a deep-space mission, a malfunctioning solar array on a Mars rover begins overheating, risking failure of the onboard electronics. The array consists of 12 identical photovoltaic cells arranged in a complex mixed-series-parallel configuration, each with a resistance of 3.2 Ω when illuminated, but with a temperature-dependent resistivity that increases by 0.4% per °C above 25°C. The rover’s power system delivers 24 V DC, but a faulty junction causes two parallel branches to carry unequal currents—branch A draws 1.8 A while branch B draws only 1.2 A due to a partial short. Calculate the effective resistance of the malfunctioning array and determine the temperature rise in branch A relative to branch B, assuming all excess power is dissipated as heat.

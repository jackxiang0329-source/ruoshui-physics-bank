# HFDPO-15165

A quantum computing chip uses a network of Josephson junctions made from a high-Tc cuprate superconductor cooled to 77 K in a liquid nitrogen bath. During operation, a persistent current of 1.2 μA flows in a superconducting loop with inductance 8 nH. Suddenly, a magnetic field fluctuation of 0.05 mT from a nearby power line induces a flux quantum (Φ₀ = 2.07 × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb) in the loop. To maintain coherence, the system must suppress any flux motion. What is the minimum critical current density required in the Josephson junctions to prevent flux penetration, assuming a junction width of 100 nm and a critical current I_c = J_c × A?

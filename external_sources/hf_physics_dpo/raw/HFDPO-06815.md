# HFDPO-06815

In a high-voltage fusion reactor, a pulsed plasma discharge is initiated by rapidly charging a 500 μF capacitor bank to 100 kV in 10 ms via a superconducting switch. However, during testing, the system experiences unexplained voltage overshoots, causing premature breakdown in the insulating ceramic barriers. The engineers suspect parasitic inductance in the charging circuit is resonating with the capacitor bank. Given that the measured peak overshoot is 135 kV and the actual inductance is unknown, determine the effective inductance in the charging loop if the resistance is negligible.

# HFDPO-06068

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, irregularly shaped hydrothermal vent chimney composed of porous mineral deposits. The chimney has a total volume of 1.2 m³ and is partially filled with superheated water at 350°C under high pressure. As the ROV’s manipulator arm gently dislodges a 0.3 m³ fragment, the fragment begins to rise toward the surface. However, the fragment is not solid—it contains trapped bubbles of supercritical fluid (density 0.85 g/cm³) occupying 20% of its internal volume. What is the effective instantaneous buoyant acceleration of the fragment just as it detaches, assuming the surrounding seawater is at 4°C with a density of 1025 kg/m³?

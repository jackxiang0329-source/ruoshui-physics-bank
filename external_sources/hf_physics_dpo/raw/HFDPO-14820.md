# HFDPO-14820

During a critical deep-sea submersible mission, the onboard navigation system fails due to an unexpected voltage drop across a 5.2 km coaxial cable connecting the pressure housing to the sensor array. The mission control team must determine if the voltage drop exceeds the 12 V tolerance before rerouting power, but the temperature of the ocean floor is 2°C, altering the cable’s resistivity. Assume copper’s temperature coefficient of resistivity is 3.9 × 10⁻³ /°C. What is the actual voltage drop, and is the system safe to operate?

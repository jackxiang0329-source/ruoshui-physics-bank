# HFDPO-06171

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 400°C, which then transfers heat to water in a heat exchanger to generate steam. During a sudden cloud cover, the oil flow is reduced by 30%, but the heat exchanger must maintain a steady steam output. If the oil enters at 390°C and exits at 350°C under normal conditions, what is the new exit temperature of the oil to still deliver the same rate of heat transfer? Assume constant specific heat and negligible heat loss to surroundings.

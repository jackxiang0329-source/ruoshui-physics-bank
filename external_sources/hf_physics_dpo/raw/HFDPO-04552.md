# HFDPO-04552

A geophysicist deploys a drifting ocean buoy equipped with a 500-meter-long flexible loop of superconducting wire to measure natural electromagnetic anomalies caused by deep-sea tectonic movements. The loop, initially aligned east-west, drifts at 0.8 m/s northward through a region where Earth’s magnetic field has a vertical component of 50 μT and decreases at 0.5 nT/km due to spatial gradients. As the buoy moves, the loop’s orientation shifts unpredictably due to ocean currents. At a given moment, the loop’s area vector makes a 30° angle with the local magnetic field direction. What is the induced EMF in the loop over the next 2 minutes, accounting for both translational motion and field spatial variation?

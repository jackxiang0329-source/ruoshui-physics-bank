# HFDPO-13355

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, charged dust nanoparticles (diameter 2.5 μm, density 2.1 g/cm³) are observed to levitate unpredictably near a grounded aluminum wafer. The chamber operates at 10⁻⁶ atm, and the ambient electric field is nominally zero. However, the dust remains suspended at a height of 1.2 mm above the surface. Estimate the minimum surface charge density (in μC/m²) the wafer must possess to balance the gravitational force via electrostatic repulsion, assuming the dust is a uniformly charged sphere and the field is approximately uniform over the particle’s size.

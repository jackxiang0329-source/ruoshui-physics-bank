At a given instant, the dipole is aligned such that its field at the coil’s center is B = 1.5 times 10^{-3} , text{T} , but the angle between vec{mu} and vec{r} changes sinusoidally. What is the maximum induced current in the coil, and how does the phase relationship between the dipole rotation and induced current affect long-term energy extraction efficiency?

# HFDPO-08917

A solar thermal power plant uses molten salt (specific heat 1400 J/kg·K, density 1900 kg/m³) stored in a cylindrical tank (height 10 m, diameter 8 m) to generate electricity. After a week of operation, the salt cools from 560°C to 320°C. Engineers need to determine the total thermal energy extracted, but must also account for heat loss through the tank walls (thermal conductivity 0.3 W/m·K, thickness 0.2 m) and radiative losses (emissivity 0.8, ambient temp 25°C). What is the net usable energy available, and how does this compare to the ideal energy if no losses occurred?

# HFDPO-10304

A small wind turbine at a remote weather station generates 150 W of electrical power during a steady wind. Over 24 hours, the turbine's battery stores 10.8 kWh of energy. However, the station’s monitoring system shows that the wind speed fluctuated significantly. How much mechanical energy from the wind must have been captured by the turbine during those 24 hours, assuming no energy is lost to heat or friction in the generator?

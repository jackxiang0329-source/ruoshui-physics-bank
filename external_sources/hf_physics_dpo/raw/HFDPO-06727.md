# HFDPO-06727

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a novel heat-extraction system where molten basalt flows through a vertical borehole surrounded by a superconducting coil (zero resistance, 100 mH inductance). As the basalt's temperature fluctuates daily (±15°C), the magnetic permeability of the surrounding rock changes, inducing a current in the coil. If the rock’s permeability varies sinusoidally at 0.05 Hz with a peak change of 8%, and the coil’s flux linkage is initially 2.5 Wb at baseline conditions, what is the RMS voltage generated across the coil? Assume perfect magnetic coupling and negligible self-resistance.

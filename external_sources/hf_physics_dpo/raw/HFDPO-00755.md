# HFDPO-00755

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Andes uses a network of three interconnected PV panels (100W, 150W, 200W) with internal resistances of 0.5Ω, 0.3Ω, and 0.2Ω respectively, feeding into a shared battery bank (12V nominal) through a complex mesh of wires. Use Kirchhoff’s Laws to determine the actual current through the 150W panel and explain why the 100W panel is sourcing current *away* from the battery despite being a solar source.

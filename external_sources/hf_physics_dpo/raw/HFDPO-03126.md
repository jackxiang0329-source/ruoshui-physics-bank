# HFDPO-03126

During a Mars rover mission, engineers must program a descent sequence for a lander to touch down safely on a 15° inclined slope. The lander descends vertically at 4 m/s when it deploys thrusters to cancel vertical velocity and initiate a horizontal glide at 6 m/s relative to the slope. If the slope is 100 m long and the lander must avoid a 20 m wide crater at the midpoint, what is the minimum horizontal acceleration (magnitude) required to clear the crater while maintaining a safe touchdown speed of 2 m/s parallel to the slope?

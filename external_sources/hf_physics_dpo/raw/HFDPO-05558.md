# HFDPO-05558

During a deep-space mission, a cryogenic fuel system in a lunar lander experiences unexpected ice formation in its feed lines despite being insulated to −150°C. The system uses liquid methane (melting point: −182.5°C) stored at 1.2 atm. Engineers note residual water vapor from incomplete outgassing, now condensing and freezing as ice crystals. If the total vapor pressure of residual H₂O in the line is 0.03 atm and the ice sublimates at a rate that equilibrates with the surrounding gas, what is the minimum pressure in the system needed to prevent ice formation, based on the Clausius–Clapeyron equation for sublimation, and how does this relate to the stability of the liquid methane phase?

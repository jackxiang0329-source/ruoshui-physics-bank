# HFDPO-11320

A quantum dot solar cell employs a 2D photonic crystal lattice with a periodicity of 420 nm to enhance light absorption. The lattice is designed to guide specific wavelengths via Bragg diffraction, but measurements show unexpected interference minima at 620 nm and 740 nm—wavelengths not predicted by simple Bragg conditions. Given that the lattice has a triangular symmetry with a refractive index contrast of 2.4 (SiO₂) vs. 1.4 (air), and a layer thickness of 120 nm, what is the effective refractive index of the photonic band structure, and why do these minima appear despite the lattice being designed for 550 nm?

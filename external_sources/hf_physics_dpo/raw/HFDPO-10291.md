# HFDPO-10291

A geophysicist studying glacial rebound in coastal Greenland measures a 1.2 mm/year uplift rate at a GPS station located 80 km from a retreating ice margin. The crust beneath is modeled as a viscoelastic half-space with a shear modulus of 35 GPa and a relaxation time of 1400 years. Using the standard linear solid model, estimate the current deviatoric stress in the crust due to unloading from the retreating ice sheet, and determine how much the stress would change if the ice loss rate doubled over the next century.

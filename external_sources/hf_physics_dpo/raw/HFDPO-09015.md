# HFDPO-09015

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal superconducting magnet to confine plasma, but during a sudden power fluctuation, the current in the primary field coils drops from 1200 A to 400 A in 0.8 seconds. The plasma chamber is surrounded by a secondary copper shield with 24 turns and a total resistance of 3.2 mΩ. What is the peak induced current in the shield, and how does its self-induced magnetic field oppose the change in the primary field? Assume the mutual inductance between the primary coils and the shield is 5.6 mH.

# HFDPO-01444

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research uses a 12 V battery system connected to a network of 10 identical photovoltaic cells wired in parallel, each with a resistance of 0.5 Ω. During a test flight, the drone’s motor draws 3 A of current, but a sudden drop in sunlight reduces the effective output of each cell by 40%. If the wiring resistance between the cells and the motor increases by 15% due to thermal expansion, what is the new voltage available at the motor terminals?

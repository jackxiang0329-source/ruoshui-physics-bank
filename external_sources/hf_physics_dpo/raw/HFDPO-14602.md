# HFDPO-14602

A solar-powered weather station in a remote alpine region relies on a 12 V battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During a cloudy week, the station’s battery level drops to 8 V. The data logger requires 5 V at 200 mA, but the only available voltage regulator is a 5 V, 300 mA linear regulator rated for 7–15 V input. The station has a 100 Ω resistor and two 220 Ω resistors. Can you design a voltage divider using these components to safely power the logger without exceeding the regulator’s input limits, and what’s the actual voltage delivered to the logger?

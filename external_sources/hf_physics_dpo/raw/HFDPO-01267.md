# HFDPO-01267

A quantum dot array in a next-generation photonic chip exhibits coupled nonlinear oscillations due to electron tunneling between adjacent dots, each with a natural frequency of 2.4 THz and a damping coefficient of 0.03 ps⁻¹. When a laser pulse excites one dot, the energy propagates through the chain via synchronized oscillations. However, researchers observe that the system exhibits unexpected phase slipping after 8 ps, leading to signal degradation. Determine the critical coupling strength required to maintain coherent oscillation across 10 dots for 10 ps, assuming sinusoidal coupling and accounting for both damping and phase dispersion.

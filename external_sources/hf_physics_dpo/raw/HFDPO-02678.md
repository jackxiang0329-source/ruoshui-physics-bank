# HFDPO-02678

A fusion reactor’s plasma containment system experiences a sudden asymmetry in magnetic field strength, causing a 5.2 kg plasma blob to accelerate radially outward at 1.3 × 10⁶ m/s² for 4.8 × 10⁻⁷ s before being recaptured. If the surrounding wall absorbs the entire momentum of the blob upon impact, and the reactor’s structural integrity relies on not exceeding 2.1 × 10⁴ N of impulse per cycle, does the system remain within safe limits? Assume the plasma was initially at rest and no external forces act during the brief acceleration phase.

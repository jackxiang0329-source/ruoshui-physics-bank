# HFDPO-12615

In a quantum computing prototype, a superconducting transmon qubit is coupled to a coplanar waveguide resonator with a nonlinear capacitance due to the Josephson junction. When a voltage pulse of 0.5 V is applied for 10 ns, the charge stored is measured via a dispersive readout. Calculate the total energy stored in the capacitance during the pulse, assuming the voltage ramps linearly from 0 to 0.5 V.

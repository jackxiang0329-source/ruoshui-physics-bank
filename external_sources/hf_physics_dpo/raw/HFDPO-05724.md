# HFDPO-05724

The blade’s effective mass is 850 kg and the restoring stiffness from its attachment point is 4.2 × 10⁶ N/m. Engineers must determine whether the oscillation is truly at resonance and calculate the expected energy dissipated per cycle if the damping ratio is 0.03.

# HFDPO-06981

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a fluid-filled tube, heating it to 400°C. The tube has a diameter of 5 cm, is made of stainless steel (k = 16 W/m·K), and is 10 m long. During a clear day, the tube absorbs 3000 W of solar power per meter of length. If the outer surface is exposed to 35°C ambient air with a convection coefficient of 25 W/m²·K, what is the inner surface temperature of the tube if heat loss through the tube wall is balanced by solar input? Assume steady state and negligible radiation.

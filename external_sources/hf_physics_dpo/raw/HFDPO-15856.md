# HFDPO-15856

A wind farm turbine blade, 85 meters long and weighing 22,000 kg, is mounted at a 12° angle from vertical due to structural fatigue in the support bearing. The blade’s center of mass is located 38% from the root. To restore alignment, engineers plan to install a temporary counterweight at the blade’s tip. What mass and placement (relative to the hinge) of the counterweight are required to achieve static equilibrium without inducing additional stress on the already strained bearing? The system must remain in equilibrium under a 25 m/s wind load that applies a 1.8 MN·m moment about the hinge.

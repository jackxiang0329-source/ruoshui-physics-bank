# HFDPO-02704

During a Mars rover mission, a drill bit embedded in a robotic arm begins to wobble violently when rotating at 2000 rpm, threatening to damage the arm’s bearing. The bit, modeled as a solid cylinder of mass 0.8 kg and radius 0.04 m, is mounted on a 0.3 m long, massless arm rotating about a pivot. Engineers suspect the wobble arises from unbalanced torque due to a 5 g mass defect offset by 2 mm from the rotation axis. What is the minimum angular acceleration (in rad/s²) the arm’s motor must apply to stabilize the system by counteracting the torque-induced precession, assuming the system’s angular momentum vector precesses at 1.2 rad/s?

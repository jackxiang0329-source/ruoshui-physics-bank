# HFDPO-05591

A quantum dot solar cell array uses tunable plasmonic nanostructures to enhance light absorption in the 600–700 nm range. Engineers observe that the peak absorption shifts unexpectedly when the surrounding dielectric medium is changed from air (n = 1.0) to a polymer with n = 1.5. Given that the nanostructures form a square lattice with period 320 nm, and the observed resonance peak shifts from 650 nm to 620 nm, determine the effective refractive index of the guided surface plasmon mode and explain the shift using coupled-wave theory and phase-matching conditions.

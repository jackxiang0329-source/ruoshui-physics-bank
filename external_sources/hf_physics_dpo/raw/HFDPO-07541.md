# HFDPO-07541

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform surface charge density due to ion bombardment during plasma etching. Measurements show the charge density follows σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r/R), where r is radial distance from the center, R = 7.5 cm, and σ₀ = 2.3 nC/m². A charged micro-needle probe (charge q = −50 pC) is positioned 3 mm above the wafer’s edge. What is the net electrostatic force on the probe, and in what direction does it act?

# HFDPO-13850

A marine biologist uses a camera with a fixed focal length lens to photograph coral reefs at 10 meters below the ocean surface. Due to refraction at the water-air interface, the camera’s autofocus system misjudges the true distance to a vibrant coral branch, causing the image to blur. If the lens has a focal length of 50 mm and the biologist adjusts focus as if the coral were 10 m away in air, what is the actual distance from the lens to the image plane, and how far is the coral actually from the lens?

# HFDPO-06549

A spacecraft docking with a rotating space station must align its thrusters to counteract the station’s angular momentum without inducing unwanted torque. The station, a cylindrical habitat with mass 2.4 × 10⁶ kg and radius 50 m, spins at 0.8 rpm to simulate gravity. A 10,000 kg docked module, initially stationary, swings into position via a robotic arm and must be attached precisely during the station’s slow deceleration from 0.8 rpm to 0.3 rpm over 2.5 minutes. What is the minimum average torque the station’s reaction wheels must apply to prevent tilting during docking, assuming the module’s moment of inertia is negligible until attached?

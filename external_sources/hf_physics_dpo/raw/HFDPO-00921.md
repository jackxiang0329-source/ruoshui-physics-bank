# HFDPO-00921

A fusion reactor's first-wall material in a tokamak design is bombarded by 14 MeV neutrons at a flux of 5×10¹⁴ n/cm²/s, with a 5% probability of displacement damage per neutron. After 10⁷ seconds of operation, engineers observe a 3.2% increase in electrical resistivity. Calculate the expected number of atomic displacements per atom (dpa) in the tungsten alloy, assuming all damage is due to primary knock-on atoms and accounting for cascade overlap. How would this prediction change if transmutation to helium (from neutron capture) significantly alters the lattice structure?

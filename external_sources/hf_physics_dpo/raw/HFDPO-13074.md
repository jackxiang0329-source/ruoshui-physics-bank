# HFDPO-13074

A solar panel array on a residential roof is secured with bracket mounts that must withstand strong wind gusts. The panel weighs 45 kg and is mounted at a 20° tilt, with its center of mass 0.8 m from the mounting point. During a storm, a horizontal wind force of 320 N pushes perpendicularly against the panel’s front surface. What minimum friction force must the bracket provide to prevent the panel from rotating about its mount?

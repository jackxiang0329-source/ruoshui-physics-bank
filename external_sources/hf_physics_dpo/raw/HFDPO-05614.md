# HFDPO-05614

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a charged microelectromechanical system (MEMS) cantilever—200 μm long, 50 μm wide, and 2 μm thick—oscillates near a grounded silicon substrate. The cantilever carries a net charge of +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁴ C, and due to electrostatic attraction, it experiences pull-in instability at a gap of 1 μm. However, residual ionized gas in the chamber introduces space charge that alters the effective field. What is the minimum uniform ion density (in m⁻³) that would increase the pull-in gap to 3 μm, assuming the dielectric constant is 1 and the system remains in electrostatic equilibrium?

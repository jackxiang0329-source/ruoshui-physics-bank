# HFDPO-00230

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole filled with conductive brine to harness subsurface heat. The brine flows upward at 0.8 m/s through a 500 m-long cylindrical pipe with a radius of 0.25 m, moving through Earth’s magnetic field (magnitude 45 µT, inclined at 65° to the vertical). A Hall-effect sensor array embedded in the pipe wall measures an induced voltage across the pipe’s diameter. What is the maximum induced EMF between opposite points on the pipe’s cross-section, and how does this voltage vary with the brine flow rate and Earth’s field orientation?

# HFDPO-10560

In a lithium-ion battery manufacturing facility, a conveyor belt transports charged cathode particles (average charge: +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁸ C, mass: 2.5 × 10⁻¹⁰ kg) through a static-sensitive zone. Due to friction with the belt, particles accumulate charge and experience electrostatic repulsion, causing uneven distribution and potential short circuits. The belt operates at 0.8 m/s, and the particle spacing is 0.2 mm at entry, but measurements show a 30% increase in spacing after 0.5 meters. Determine the minimum electric field strength (in V/m) required to counteract the repulsive force and maintain uniform particle flow, assuming the particles are point charges and the field is uniform.

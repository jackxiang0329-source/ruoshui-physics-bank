# HFDPO-05317

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic subwavelength grating (pitch 450 nm) on its surface to enhance light trapping via guided mode resonance. Experimental data shows a sharp absorption peak at 632 nm under normal incidence, but the expected resonance condition from thin-film theory predicts a peak near 720 nm. The device operates under concentrated sunlight (500 suns), and efficiency drops by 18% when the grating is rotated 15° off-normal. Determine the effective refractive index of the guided mode and explain the discrepancy between theoretical and observed resonance.

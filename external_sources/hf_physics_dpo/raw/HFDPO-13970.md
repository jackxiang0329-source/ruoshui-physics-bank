# HFDPO-13970

In a semiconductor fabrication facility, a charged microelectromechanical system (MEMS) cantilever—500 μm long, 50 μm wide, and 2 μm thick—oscillates above a grounded silicon substrate. As it approaches within 100 nm of the substrate, electrostatic attraction causes it to snap down, risking permanent adhesion. Using the method of images, calculate the minimum electrostatic force acting on the cantilever just before contact, and explain why this force is significantly greater than a naive Coulomb estimate would suggest.

# HFDPO-01424

However, experimental data shows inconsistent translocation times—some cells pass through in 120 ms, others take 180 ms—despite identical conditions. Using viscous flow principles, explain the observed variability and determine the effective hydrodynamic resistance experienced by the cell, accounting for both wall effects and deformation-induced slip.

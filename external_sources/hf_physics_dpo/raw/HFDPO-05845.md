# HFDPO-05845

A solar-powered ocean buoy, designed to withstand extreme wave conditions, uses a submerged counterweight system to maintain stability. The buoy’s center of mass is 1.2 m above the waterline, and the counterweight (150 kg) is anchored 2.5 m below the buoy’s base. During a storm, wave forces create a horizontal torque of 1,800 N·m at the buoy’s pivot point. What is the minimum distance from the pivot to the counterweight’s vertical line of action to prevent overturning, assuming the buoy’s hull is perfectly rigid and the water exerts no frictional force?

# HFDPO-06024

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a pair of counter-rotating gyroscopes to stabilize its orientation without thrusters. Each gyroscope has a mass of 12 kg, radius 0.3 m, and spins at 8,000 rpm. After 100 days, thermal expansion has increased each gyroscope’s radius by 0.2%. If the satellite needs to maintain angular momentum conservation while reorienting to track a moving target, how much must the spin rate of each gyroscope change to compensate, and what is the new rotational kinetic energy?

# HFDPO-05682

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoparticles of cadmium selenide (CdSe) with a diameter of 2.5 nm to capture sunlight. When illuminated by violet light (λ = 400 nm), electrons are excited from the valence to the conduction band, but only when the photon energy matches the quantum confinement energy gap. Given that the effective mass of an electron in CdSe is 0.15 mₑ, determine whether the photon energy is sufficient to excite an electron across the bandgap, and explain how the wave nature of electrons contributes to the discrete energy levels observed.

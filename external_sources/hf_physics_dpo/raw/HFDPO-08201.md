# HFDPO-08201

During a deep-space mission, a solar-powered rover on Mars encounters unexpected dust storms that reduce sunlight by 70%. The rover’s primary power system relies on a network of 12 identical photovoltaic cells arranged in a series-parallel configuration, each with a resistance of 0.5 Ω and an open-circuit voltage of 0.6 V. To maintain critical systems, the total current delivered to the central processor must be at least 2.4 A. If the internal resistance of the battery bank increases from 0.1 Ω to 0.8 Ω due to cold temperatures and dust buildup, what reconfiguration of the cell array is required to sustain the current demand under degraded conditions?

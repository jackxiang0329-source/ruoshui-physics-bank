# HFDPO-15276

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and shaped like a uniform rod, spins at 15 RPM in a 12 m/s wind. Due to turbulence, the blade experiences a non-uniform aerodynamic torque that varies with angular position as τ(θ) = 3.2 × 10⁶ sin(2θ) N·m, where θ is the blade’s rotation angle. Engineers must adjust the blade’s mass distribution to prevent excessive stress at the hub during startup. What is the minimum moment of inertia needed at the hub (treated as a point mass at the center) to ensure the angular acceleration never exceeds 0.05 rad/s² during the first 10 seconds of rotation? Ignore friction and assume constant wind force.

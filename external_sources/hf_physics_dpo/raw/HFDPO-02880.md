# HFDPO-02880

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a 12 V battery bank to run sensors and a data transmitter. The system draws 2.5 A during data transmission (5 minutes every hour) and 0.1 A during standby. Over 24 hours, a single 100 Ah battery fails to sustain operation due to unexpected voltage drop during peak load. The technician suspects the wiring resistance is too high. Given that the battery’s internal resistance is 0.02 Ω and the transmission circuit uses 10 m of copper wire (diameter 2 mm), what is the minimum wire gauge required to ensure the voltage at the transmitter never drops below 10.5 V during transmission?

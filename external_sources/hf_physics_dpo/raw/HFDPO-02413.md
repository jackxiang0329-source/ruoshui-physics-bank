# HFDPO-02413

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to extract heat from underground rock formations, pumping water at 30°C down a 2.5 km deep pipe. The water exits as steam at 180°C due to heat absorbed from the rock. Given the water's flow rate is 12 kg/s, the pipe has a diameter of 0.15 m, and pressure drops by 1.2 MPa due to friction and turbulence, what is the net useful power output available from the steam, considering both the thermal energy gained and the work done against pressure? Assume the process is adiabatic above ground and neglect kinetic energy changes.

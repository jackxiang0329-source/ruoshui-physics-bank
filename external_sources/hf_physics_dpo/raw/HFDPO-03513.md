# HFDPO-03513

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 390°C, which then transfers heat to water in a heat exchanger to produce steam. On a clear day, the system delivers 50 MW of thermal power, but during a sudden cloud cover event, the input solar flux drops by 60%. The oil reservoir has a mass of 12,000 kg and a specific heat capacity of 1,900 J/kg·K. How long can the system continue to supply full thermal power before the oil temperature drops below 370°C, assuming no additional heating and negligible heat loss to the environment?

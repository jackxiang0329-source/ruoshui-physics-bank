# HFDPO-11423

A quantum dot solar cell uses a 2D photonic crystal lattice with a periodicity of 420 nm to enhance light absorption in the 600–700 nm range. Engineers observe unexpected interference fringes in the reflected light spectrum, peaking at 630 nm with a 15 nm full width at half maximum. The device operates under normal incidence with a refractive index of 2.3 in the lattice and 1.5 in the surrounding medium. Determine the effective refractive index of the first-order guided mode within the photonic bandgap and explain why the fringe position shifts by 12 nm when the ambient temperature increases by 25°C.

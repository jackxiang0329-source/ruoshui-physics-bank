# HFDPO-03267

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain ridge relies on a 12 V battery bank charged by a 200 W solar panel array. During a prolonged overcast period, the station switches to battery power, drawing an average current of 0.8 A while transmitting data every 10 minutes. The battery has a capacity of 50 Ah and an internal resistance of 0.3 Ω. If the system’s power electronics have an efficiency of 85%, how long can the station operate before the battery voltage drops below the minimum operating threshold of 10.5 V?

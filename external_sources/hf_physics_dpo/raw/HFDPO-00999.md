# HFDPO-00999

A biomedical engineer is designing a resonant inductive coupling system to wirelessly power an implantable cardiac pacemaker buried 5 cm beneath the skin. The external coil operates at 13.56 MHz (ISM band), with a 20 μH inductance and 12 Ω resistance. The implanted coil has 15 μH inductance and 8 Ω resistance, with a mutual inductance of 0.8 μH due to alignment constraints. To achieve efficient power transfer, the system must be impedance-matched at resonance. What capacitance should be added to the external circuit to tune it to resonance, and at what efficiency is power transferred if the source delivers 5 W of real power?

# HFDPO-11097

A medical research team is developing a wireless power transfer system to charge implantable cardiac pacemakers through the skin using a time-varying magnetic field. The external coil, placed 2 cm from the implant, carries a sinusoidal current of 5 A at 1 MHz. The implant’s internal coil has 100 turns, a radius of 3 mm, and is oriented at a 30° angle to the external field. The system must deliver at least 1.2 mW of power to the pacemaker. Assuming ideal coupling and negligible resistance, what is the minimum peak magnetic field amplitude needed from the external coil to meet the power requirement?

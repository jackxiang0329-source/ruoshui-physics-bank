# HFDPO-04171

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a technician notices that charged dust particles are consistently accumulating on the surface of a 5.0 cm × 5.0 cm silicon wafer during a plasma etching process. A nearby grounded metal shield, 2.0 mm away and parallel to the wafer, causes the electric field near the wafer to be distorted. What is the magnitude and direction of the net electric field just above the wafer’s surface, and what is the resulting electrostatic force per unit area on the wafer?

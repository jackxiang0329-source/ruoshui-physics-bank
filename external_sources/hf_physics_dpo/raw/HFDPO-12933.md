# HFDPO-12933

During a lunar mining operation, a robotic drill extracts regolith from a crater wall, launching particles at high velocity into a vacuum chamber where they collide elastically with a stationary collector plate angled at 37°. The plate is mounted on a movable sled with negligible friction. If 1.2 kg of material is ejected at 45 m/s, and the system’s center of mass must remain stationary after the collision, what must be the sled’s recoil speed and mass to satisfy both momentum and kinetic energy conservation?

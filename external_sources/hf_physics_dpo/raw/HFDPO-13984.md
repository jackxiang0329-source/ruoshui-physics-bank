# HFDPO-13984

A concert hall designer is troubleshooting persistent "dead spots" near the balcony overhang, where audience members report muffled sound during string quartet performances. Measurements show sound intensity drops by 12 dB at 1.8 kHz precisely at those locations, despite uniform speaker placement. The hall has a curved ceiling with a 22 m radius and the balcony is 9 m above the stage. Using wave interference principles and diffraction effects, determine whether the dead spots result from destructive interference between direct sound and a first-order reflected wave from the ceiling, or from edge diffraction around the balcony’s front lip. Assume sound speed is 343 m/s and the balcony lip acts as a single slit 1.2 m wide.

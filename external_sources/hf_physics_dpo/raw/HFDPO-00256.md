# HFDPO-00256

A Mars rover's wheel is sinking into loose regolith during a sand dune traversal. The coefficient of static friction between the wheel and sand is 0.65, but the sand exhibits non-Newtonian behavior: its effective viscosity increases nonlinearly with shear rate, modeled as η(v) = η₀(1 + 0.1v²), where v is the wheel's tangential speed in m/s and η₀ = 0.8 Pa·s. The wheel has a radius of 0.3 m, a mass of 12 kg, and a torque of 45 N·m is applied. Determine the minimum torque required to prevent the wheel from stalling, considering both static friction and variable drag from the sand.

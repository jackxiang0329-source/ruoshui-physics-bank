# HFDPO-00731

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses a laser-based underwater scanner that emits red light (650 nm) through a diffraction grating to map micro-topographies of symbiotic algae colonies. At 10 meters depth, the first-order diffraction maximum appears at 12.5° from the central axis. However, when the scanner is moved to a turbid region with suspended sediments, the same maximum shifts to 10.2°. Determine the effective refractive index of the water-sediment mixture, assuming the grating spacing remains unchanged and the shift is due to wavelength change in the medium.

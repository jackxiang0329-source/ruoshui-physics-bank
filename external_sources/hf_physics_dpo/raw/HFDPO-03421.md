# HFDPO-03421

A particle accelerator at CERN detects muons created in cosmic ray collisions at an altitude of 15 km above the Earth's surface. These muons travel at 0.998c relative to Earth. Observers on the ground measure their decay time as 2.2 μs in their rest frame, but the muons are detected at the surface. How do we reconcile the fact that most muons survive the journey, and what does this imply about the time dilation and length contraction from both Earth and muon perspectives?

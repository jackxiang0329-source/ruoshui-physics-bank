# HFDPO-06674

A marine biologist is designing a submerged sensor platform to study deep-sea currents near a hydrothermal vent. The platform is a hollow, spherical shell (diameter 1.2 m, thickness 5 cm, steel alloy with density 7850 kg/m³) anchored by two asymmetric cables: one vertical, the other at 30° above horizontal, both connected to the seabed 8 m below. Given seawater density 1025 kg/m³ and gravitational acceleration 9.81 m/s², what is the maximum horizontal offset (from the centerline) of the center of mass for the platform (including internal electronics, mass 80 kg, center 15 cm from geometric center) that ensures rotational stability?

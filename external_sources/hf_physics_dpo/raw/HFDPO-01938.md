# HFDPO-01938

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to heat oil to 320°C, which drives a Rankine cycle turbine. The condenser rejects heat to ambient air at 35°C. During a heatwave, the cooling water supply drops by 40%, reducing the condenser’s heat transfer efficiency. If the turbine must maintain 1.2 MW of electrical output, what is the minimum required temperature of the superheated steam entering the turbine, assuming the pump work is negligible and the cycle operates at 85% of Carnot efficiency?

# HFDPO-06856

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma chamber where deuterium and tritium nuclei collide at high speed, producing helium nuclei and high-energy neutrons. During a 10-second pulse, the total mass-energy of the system decreases by 4.5×10⁻⁷ kg due to nuclear binding energy release. However, the measured momentum of the plasma remains nearly unchanged, while the neutron flux exhibits asymmetric directional emission. How can we reconcile the observed momentum conservation with the asymmetry in neutron emission, given that the reactor is designed to be isolated and symmetric?

# HFDPO-05073

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 25° tilt to maximize winter sun exposure. During a rare snowstorm, 12 kg of snow accumulates uniformly along the 3.2 m length of one panel, creating a distributed load. The panel is secured at its base by a hinge and a cable attached 0.6 m from the top, angled at 30° above horizontal. The panel’s mass is 45 kg, and its center of mass is at its midpoint. What minimum tension must the cable withstand without the panel sliding or tipping?

# HFDPO-07381

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates power during strong gusts, but engineers notice unexpected wear on the gearbox after only 6 months. The turbine blades, each 45 meters long and modeled as thin rods, rotate at 15 RPM when wind speed peaks at 25 m/s. During a sudden 5-second gust that increases wind speed to 35 m/s, the turbine’s control system delays pitch adjustment by 3 seconds. Estimate the angular acceleration of the blades during this delay and determine if the resulting torque exceeded the gearbox’s 1.2 × 10⁶ N·m design limit.

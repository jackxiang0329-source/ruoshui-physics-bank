# HFDPO-07398

A deep-sea mining drone, shaped like a symmetric truss frame with 8 m long arms, hovers 2 km below the ocean surface, stabilizing a 12-ton mineral collector suspended from its center. The drone's thrusters can only exert forces at 45° angles relative to the vertical, and each provides a maximum thrust of 180 kN. Due to turbulent currents, the system experiences a 40 kN horizontal drag force from the seabed plume. What is the minimum number of thrusters needed to maintain static equilibrium, and what thrust magnitudes are required in each to prevent rotation about the vertical axis?

# HFDPO-00333

A high-altitude research balloon carrying sensitive atmospheric sensors ascends through the stratosphere, where temperature drops to −60°C and pressure falls to 15 kPa. The balloon's internal gas (assumed ideal) was initially at 20°C and 101 kPa with a volume of 500 m³. As the balloon expands, its envelope stretches elastically, but the internal pressure must remain nearly equal to the external pressure to prevent rupture. Calculate the final volume and estimate the root-mean-square speed of nitrogen molecules inside when the balloon reaches equilibrium at altitude.

# HFDPO-06768

A quantum dot solar cell prototype uses a semiconductor nanoparticle with a diameter of 2.5 nm to absorb sunlight. The bandgap energy is tunable due to quantum confinement—when the dot size changes, so does the energy needed to excite an electron. If the energy required to excite an electron from the ground state to the first excited state is measured as 1.8 eV, what is the effective mass of the electron in the dot, assuming a particle-in-a-box model? Use the known size and energy to estimate the electron's effective mass.

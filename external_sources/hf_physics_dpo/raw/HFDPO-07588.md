# HFDPO-07588

During a geomagnetic storm, a satellite in low Earth orbit detects a rapidly fluctuating magnetic field of 50 nT at a frequency of 10 Hz. The spacecraft’s onboard sensors measure a small induced electric field of 2 mV/m tangential to its orbit. Using Maxwell’s equations, determine whether this electric field is primarily due to the changing magnetic field (Faraday’s law) or if displacement current (Maxwell-Ampère law) could play a significant role. Assume the satellite is in a vacuum and the field changes uniformly over the spacecraft’s cross-sectional area.

# HFDPO-07040

A deep-space probe, traveling at 0.95c relative to Earth, transmits a 10-second pulse of data using a 2.4 GHz radio signal. Due to relativistic effects, the signal experiences both time dilation and Doppler shift. An Earth-based receiver must decode the signal before a scheduled mission-critical event. If the probe's onboard clock records the transmission as 10 seconds long, what is the observed duration and frequency shift of the received signal, and would a standard satellite receiver (designed for ~10% fractional Doppler) fail to decode it?

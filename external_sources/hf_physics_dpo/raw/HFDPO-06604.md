# HFDPO-06604

A deep-sea submersible uses a custom-built, fisheye lens to map hydrothermal vent plumes 300 meters below the ocean surface. A scientist observes that the image of a plume’s edge, which is actually 30 meters long, appears compressed. What is the apparent length of the plume’s edge in the image, assuming the lens is perfectly aligned and the observer is at the center of curvature?

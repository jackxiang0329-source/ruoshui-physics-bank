# HFDPO-02141

A fusion reactor prototype using deuterium-tritium fuel experiences an unexplained drop in neutron yield during sustained operation. Initial measurements show 1.2 × 10¹⁹ neutrons per second at 100 MW thermal power, but after 12 hours, this drops to 9.5 × 10¹⁸ neutrons/sec despite stable plasma temperature and density. Analyze whether this decline is due to tritium burnup, neutron absorption in the first wall, or changes in confinement efficiency. Use measured neutron energy spectra and activation analysis of the blanket material (Li₂O) to infer the dominant cause.

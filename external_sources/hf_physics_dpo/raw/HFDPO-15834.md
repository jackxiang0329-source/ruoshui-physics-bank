# HFDPO-15834

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a pair of orthogonal polarization-multiplexed antennas to transmit high-bandwidth data through a turbulent ionosphere. During a solar storm, the ionosphere becomes birefringent, introducing a phase delay of 0.4π between the vertical and horizontal polarization components over a 100 km path. A ground receiver detects a signal with an elliptical polarization state, where the major axis is 30° from vertical and the axial ratio is 2:1. What is the original polarization state of the transmitted signal, and how would you reconstruct it using a digitally reconfigurable polarization filter?

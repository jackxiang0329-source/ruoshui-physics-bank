# HFDPO-01891

A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new model with a thinner screen that still supports capacitive touch. The touchscreen uses a grid of tiny capacitors, each with a plate area of 2 mm² and a dielectric (glass) thickness of 0.1 mm. When a finger approaches, the capacitance increases due to the finger's proximity. If the relative permittivity of the glass is 5.0, what is the initial capacitance of one touch sensor unit?

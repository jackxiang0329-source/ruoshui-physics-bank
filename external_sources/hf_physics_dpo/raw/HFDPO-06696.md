# HFDPO-06696

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal region prone to strong gusts. The 15 kg unit is secured by a rigid tripod base with legs spaced 1.2 m apart in a triangular configuration. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 320 N at 1.8 m above the base. To prevent tipping, the legs must generate sufficient normal forces. What is the minimum friction coefficient between the base and the roof needed to prevent sliding, assuming the tripod is symmetric and the center of mass is at the base’s center?

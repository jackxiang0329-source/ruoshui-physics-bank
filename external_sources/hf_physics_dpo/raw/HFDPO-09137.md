# HFDPO-09137

A wind turbine blade in a mountainous region experiences fluctuating wind speeds due to terrain-induced turbulence. At a critical point, the wind velocity increases from 12 m/s to 28 m/s over a 0.5 m length of the blade’s upper surface, while the pressure on the lower surface remains constant at atmospheric pressure (101,325 Pa). The air density is 1.225 kg/m³. What is the net lift force per meter of blade length if the blade’s chord is 0.8 m and the pressure difference drives blade deflection?

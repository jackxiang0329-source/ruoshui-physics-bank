# HFDPO-14505

A lunar lander descends vertically toward a rough, dusty exoplanetary surface at 2.5 m/s, using thrusters to maintain a controlled deceleration. As it nears the surface, a sudden dust storm creates a horizontal wind gust of 6.0 m/s that imparts a lateral force of 420 N on the lander’s 850 kg body, with the thrusters unable to vector sideways. Given that the lander’s vertical thrusters provide 7,200 N of upward force and the local gravity is 1.62 m/s², determine the magnitude and direction of the lander’s resultant acceleration vector just after the gust hits.

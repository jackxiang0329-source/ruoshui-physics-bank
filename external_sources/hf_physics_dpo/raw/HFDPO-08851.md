# HFDPO-08851

A solar-powered satellite in low Earth orbit experiences intermittent signal dropout during eclipse periods, despite having redundant power systems. Telemetry shows that the onboard CMOS image sensor (operating at 3.3 V) begins failing after ~45 seconds of darkness, with pixel charge leakage increasing exponentially. The sensor uses a 150 nm CMOS process with a p-type substrate (Na = 8×10¹⁵ cm⁻³), and the measured dark current is 2.4 nA/μm² under 120 K conditions—far above design specs. Investigate the physical origin of the anomalous dark current increase in the cold, radiation-exposed environment, and determine whether the defect state responsible is located in the bulk or at the Si/SiO₂ interface.

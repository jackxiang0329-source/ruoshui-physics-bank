# HFDPO-12030

A geothermal energy company is drilling into a volcanic region to harness heat from deep underground. The borehole, 4.2 km deep, passes through a rock layer with a Young’s modulus of 32 GPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.25. As the drill bit advances, the surrounding rock experiences radial stress due to in situ tectonic pressure. At a depth of 3.8 km, microseismic sensors detect small fractures forming around the borehole wall. Assuming the borehole is initially stress-free and has a 0.2 m radius, what is the minimum in situ radial stress that could trigger brittle failure in the rock, given that the rock’s tensile strength is 2.1 MPa, and how would the stress state change if the rock were anisotropic with a 15% directional variation in Young’s modulus?

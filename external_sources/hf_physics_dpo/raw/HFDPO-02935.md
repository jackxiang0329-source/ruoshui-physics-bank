# HFDPO-02935

A biomedical engineer designs a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood by size using a T-shaped channel with a constriction. Blood flows at 0.2 m/s in the main channel (width 500 μm), and the constriction reduces the width to 200 μm. The pressure difference across the constriction is measured as 120 Pa. What is the net force exerted on a 15 μm diameter cancer cell (density 1050 kg/m³) as it approaches the narrow section, assuming it remains stationary relative to the fluid stream?

# HFDPO-01304

A Mars habitat prototype uses a closed-loop thermal energy system to maintain 25°C internal temperature during a 68-hour dust storm, when solar input drops to 120 W/m². The habitat's 45 m² heat shield absorbs solar radiation and transfers energy via a regenerative heat exchanger to a 500 kg phase-change material (PCM) with a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg. If the PCM begins at 10°C and the system must sustain thermal equilibrium throughout the storm, what is the minimum mass of PCM required to prevent internal temperature decay? Assume 80% efficiency in energy transfer and 3.5 W/m² of heat leakage through insulation.

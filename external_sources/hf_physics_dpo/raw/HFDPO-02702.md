# HFDPO-02702

A semiconductor manufacturer faces yield loss in nanoscale chip fabrication due to electrostatic adhesion of sub-100 nm dust particles to the wafer surface during vacuum deposition. The wafer, held at -150 V relative to ground, develops a non-uniform charge distribution from triboelectric charging during handling. Due to the wafer’s local curvature and nearby gate electrodes, the electric field near the particle varies spatially. Using the method of image charges and polarization effects, determine the minimum distance the particle must be lifted by a pneumatic purge to avoid re-deposition, assuming the particle’s weight is balanced by electrostatic adhesion.

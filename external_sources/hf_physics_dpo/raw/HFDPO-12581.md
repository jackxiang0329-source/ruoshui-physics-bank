# HFDPO-12581

A lunar habitat module, shaped like a truncated cone (base diameter 8 m, top diameter 6 m, height 4 m), is anchored to the regolith by three symmetrically placed tether anchors at the base. During a dust storm, wind forces of up to 420 N/m² act horizontally on the curved lateral surface. The coefficient of static friction between the module’s base and lunar regolith is 0.42. Determine the minimum tether tension required at each anchor to prevent sliding or tipping, assuming the module’s center of mass is at 1.8 m above the base and its mass is 8,500 kg.

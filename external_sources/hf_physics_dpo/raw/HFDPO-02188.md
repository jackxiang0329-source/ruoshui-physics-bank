# HFDPO-02188

A deep-sea submersible’s navigation camera, mounted at the front of its hull, captures distorted images of a hydrothermal vent plume due to refractive gradients in the surrounding water caused by temperature and salinity differences. The camera’s lens is 0.8 m from the hull’s inner surface, and the plume creates a refractive index gradient described by n(z) = 1.33 + 0.002e^{-z/1.5} , where z is depth below the lens in meters. The vent’s brightest point lies 4.0 m from the lens along the optical axis. Determine whether the image of the vent point forms inside, outside, or coincident with the hull’s outer surface. Use both ray tracing and the paraxial approximation with the ray equation to evaluate consistency.

# HFDPO-13552

A quantum computing chip using high-temperature superconducting qubits (YBCO) operates at 77 K in a dilution refrigerator. During a sudden power surge, a fluxoid quantization anomaly causes a persistent current loop to form in a Josephson junction ring, disrupting qubit coherence. The ring has a critical current density of 2.5 × 10⁶ A/m², a width of 100 nm, and a magnetic flux quantum Φ₀ = 2.07 × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb. The system’s control circuitry must detect and cancel this trapped flux without disturbing adjacent qubits. Estimate the minimum persistent current required to stabilize the loop, and propose a non-invasive method to disrupt it using a transient magnetic field pulse.

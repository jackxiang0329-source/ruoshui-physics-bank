# HFDPO-03224

A biomechanical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable stent for coronary arteries affected by atherosclerosis. The stent must expand radially by 30% during deployment, but the surrounding arterial tissue exhibits nonlinear hyperelastic behavior with a Young’s modulus of 12 MPa at zero strain and stiffening significantly beyond 15% strain. The stent’s material has a nominal yield stress of 450 MPa and a strain-hardening coefficient of 0.2. What is the minimum required wall thickness to prevent plastic deformation during expansion, assuming the stent operates under in vivo pressure of 140 mmHg (18.6 kPa), and how does the nonlinearity of the arterial tissue influence the residual stress state after deployment?

# HFDPO-11815

A solar-powered drone designed for stratospheric monitoring must maintain stability during high-altitude maneuvers. The drone’s central fuselage is a rigid, symmetric beam 4.2 m long, suspended by two flexible carbon-fiber struts angled at 35° to the vertical, each capable of delivering a maximum tension of 1,850 N. The drone carries a 35 kg payload mounted 1.1 m from the left strut and a 22 kg battery pack 1.6 m from the right strut. The center of mass of the empty fuselage is at its midpoint. Determine the maximum allowable mass of the solar panel array (mounted 0.8 m from the left end) that can be added without exceeding the tension limit in either strut, assuming uniform weight distribution and negligible strut mass.

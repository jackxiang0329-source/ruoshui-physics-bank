# HFDPO-16375

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate blood plasma from red blood cells using a narrow channel (200 μm wide) at 37°C. The chip must process 10 μL/min of whole blood, but flow rate is limited by viscosity: plasma has μ = 1.2 cP, while red blood cells increase local viscosity to 4.5 cP near the walls due to the Fahraeus-Lindqvist effect. What is the minimum pressure gradient required to sustain flow without clogging, and how does this change if the channel surface is modified to reduce wall shear stress by 30% via a hydrophilic coating?

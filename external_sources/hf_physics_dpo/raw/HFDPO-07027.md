# HFDPO-07027

A deep-space probe near a neutron star observes that its onboard atomic clock runs 0.7% slower than identical clocks on Earth. The star has a mass of 1.4 solar masses and a radius of 12 km. Assume the probe is at rest relative to the star and that gravitational redshift dominates over kinematic effects.

# HFDPO-04504

In a quantum computing prototype, a single electron is trapped in a 1.2 nm wide quantum dot fabricated from graphene. The system is designed to operate at 15 K, where thermal decoherence is minimized. When a voltage pulse is applied, the electron is observed to tunnel through a 0.5 nm thick potential barrier with a measured transmission probability of 0.18. A researcher claims this behavior cannot be explained by classical particle models alone. Analyze the data using wave-particle duality concepts to determine whether the electron’s wave-like nature is necessary to account for the observed tunneling, and quantify the minimum uncertainty in momentum that supports wave-like behavior.

# HFDPO-01611

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer is being charged during an ion implantation step. Due to uneven charge distribution from the beam, the surface develops a non-uniform electric potential: V(r) = V_0 left(1 - frac{r^2}{R^2}right) , where R = 7.5 cm and V_0 = 180 V. A nearby microelectrode at z = 200 μm above the center experiences a force from this charge distribution. What is the magnitude and direction of the electrostatic force on a 100 fF capacitor connected to the electrode? Assume the electrode is small enough to be treated as a point probe.

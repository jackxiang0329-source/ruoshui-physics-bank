# HFDPO-15681

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged volcanic vent emitting superheated water at 350°C into the surrounding ocean at 2°C. The ROV must position itself 10 meters above the vent to avoid damage, but a floating debris field from past eruptions is disrupting stability. If the debris has a density of 850 kg/m³ and the water density varies with depth due to temperature gradients (average 1030 kg/m³ at the vent depth), how much of the debris’s volume must be enclosed in a buoyant float (ρ_float = 150 kg/m³) to keep it neutrally buoyant at 10 meters above the vent? Assume the float is sealed and incompressible.

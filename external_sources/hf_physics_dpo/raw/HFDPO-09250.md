# HFDPO-09250

The fin experiences both viscous drag and boundary layer separation due to sudden flow acceleration. Then evaluate whether increasing the fin’s surface roughness by 30% (to simulate sediment buildup) would increase or decrease the overall drag, and explain why this matters for mission efficiency and control stability.

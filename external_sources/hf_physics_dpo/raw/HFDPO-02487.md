# HFDPO-02487

A coastal engineering team is designing a wave-energy converter that uses a submerged venturi-shaped structure to harness tidal flow. Water flows into the device at 2.5 m/s through a 4.0 m-wide inlet, then accelerates through a narrow vertical constriction (1.2 m wide) before exiting into a reservoir. The system must maintain a minimum pressure differential of 15 kPa across the constriction to drive a turbine, but the material can only withstand 80 kPa absolute pressure. Given seawater density of 1025 kg/m³ and atmospheric pressure at the top of the system, determine the maximum allowable depth of the constriction to ensure structural integrity while meeting energy requirements.

# HFDPO-13402

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 390°C. On a clear day, the system absorbs 2.5 MW of solar power, but the oil loses heat to the environment at a rate proportional to the temperature difference: dQ/dt = -k(T - T₀), where T₀ = 25°C and k = 1.2 kW/°C. The oil has a heat capacity of 2.1 kJ/kg·°C and flows at 12 kg/s. What is the equilibrium oil temperature, and how long does it take to reach 95% of that temperature after startup?

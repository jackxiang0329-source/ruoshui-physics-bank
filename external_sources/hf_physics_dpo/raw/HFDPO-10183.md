# HFDPO-10183

A marine biologist uses a waterproof camera with a fixed focal length lens to photograph coral reefs at 10 meters depth. The camera's sensor is 5 cm behind the lens, but due to water pressure, the lens shifts 2 mm forward into the housing. Without adjusting the focus, the biologist captures an image of a 30 cm wide coral structure that appears blurred. What is the approximate actual distance to the coral structure if the image is sharp only when the object is 12 meters away in air?

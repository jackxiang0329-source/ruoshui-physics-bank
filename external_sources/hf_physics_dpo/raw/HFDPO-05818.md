# HFDPO-05818

An underground geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a network of inclined boreholes drilled into hot rock formations at 35° from vertical, each lined with a flexible steel casing 12 m long and weighing 480 kg. Due to uneven thermal expansion, the casing exerts non-uniform pressure against the rock wall, modeled as a distributed load increasing linearly from 0 N/m at the top to 2.5 kN/m at the bottom. The casing is supported at the surface by a rigid clamp that resists both vertical force and moment. What is the minimum clamping force required to prevent rotation, and what is the corresponding horizontal force at the clamp?

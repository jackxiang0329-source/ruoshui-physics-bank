# HFDPO-12718

A wind turbine blade (length 60 m, mass 15,000 kg) begins to stall during a storm due to turbulent airflow, causing a sudden 12° misalignment from its optimal pitch angle. If the blade’s center of mass is 35 m from the hub and the aerodynamic torque increases to 8.5 × 10⁶ N·m at stall, what is the initial angular acceleration of the blade? Assume the blade rotates about a frictionless axis and that its moment of inertia about the hub is 1.2 × 10⁷ kg·m².

# HFDPO-14358

A Mars rover prototype uses a deployable solar array arm made of a lightweight composite beam (1.8 m long, mass 4.2 kg) hinged at the rover’s side. During deployment, a motor applies torque at the hinge, but the arm must hold steady at 60° above horizontal against Martian gravity (3.71 m/s²). The center of mass is 0.9 m from the hinge, and wind gusts (up to 30 N horizontal force) may act at the arm’s tip. What minimum torque must the motor supply to maintain static equilibrium during deployment, and is the arm at risk of rotating if the torque is 15% less than required?

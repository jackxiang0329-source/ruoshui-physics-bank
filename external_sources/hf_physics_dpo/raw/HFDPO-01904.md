# HFDPO-01904

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels with anti-reflective coatings optimized for unpolarized sunlight at normal incidence. During peak hours, glare from the panels reflects off nearby sand dunes and into nearby wildlife observation towers, disrupting thermal imaging cameras used to monitor endangered species. The cameras are sensitive to vertically polarized light, and the reflected glare is predominantly horizontally polarized due to reflection at Brewster’s angle over dry sand. Design a polarization filter system using a combination of linear polarizers and wave plates to block ≥90% of the reflected glare while preserving at least 80% of the useful solar irradiance. What is the minimum number of optical elements needed, and what orientation(s) and type(s) are required?

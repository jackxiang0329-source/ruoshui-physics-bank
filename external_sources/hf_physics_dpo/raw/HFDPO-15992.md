# HFDPO-15992

A fusion reactor prototype uses a spherical plasma chamber with a diameter of 4.2 meters, where deuterium and tritium ions are confined by magnetic fields. At peak operation, the plasma reaches 150 million Kelvin and exerts an outward pressure of 1.2 × 10^7 Pa. Engineers must calculate the total force on the inner wall due to plasma pressure, while ensuring the magnetic confinement system maintains momentum conservation by balancing the net momentum flux of ion streams. If the average ion velocity is 3.0 × 10^6 m/s and the ion density is 1.8 × 10^20 m⁻³, what is the required net magnetic force to prevent bulk motion of the plasma, assuming no external forces and that momentum is conserved?

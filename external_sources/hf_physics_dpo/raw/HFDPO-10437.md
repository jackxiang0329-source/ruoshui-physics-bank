# HFDPO-10437

A polarized solar panel array on a Mars rover uses a thin-film polarizing filter to reduce glare from reflective regolith, but the efficiency drops by 18% during dusty storms when scattered light becomes partially polarized. If the incident sunlight is unpolarized with intensity 1300 W/m² and the filter transmits only 45% of vertically polarized light while blocking horizontal polarization, what must the degree of polarization of the scattered light be for the effective irradiance at the panel to decrease by exactly 18%? Assume the scattered light is partially polarized with a fixed polarization angle and uniform intensity distribution.

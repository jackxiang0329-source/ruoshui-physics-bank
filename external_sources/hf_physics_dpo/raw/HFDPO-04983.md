# HFDPO-04983

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a charged microelectromechanical system (MEMS) actuator begins to exhibit unpredictable sticking behavior during operation. The actuator, a 50 μm × 50 μm rectangular plate with a total charge of –2.4 × 10⁻¹² C, is suspended 15 μm above a grounded silicon substrate. The surrounding environment has a dielectric constant of 2.8 due to residual gas adsorption. When the actuator is driven toward the substrate, it experiences an attractive force exceeding the designed restoring force, causing unintended adhesion. Estimate the net electrostatic force between the actuator and substrate at this separation, and determine whether the force is sufficient to overcome a typical spring constant of 0.8 N/m. Assume the actuator’s charge is uniformly distributed and the substrate remains grounded.

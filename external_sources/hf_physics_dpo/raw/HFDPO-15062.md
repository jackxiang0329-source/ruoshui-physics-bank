# HFDPO-15062

A rural community installs a large, hollow metal Faraday cage around their solar power substation to protect sensitive electronics from lightning strikes. The cage is a perfect sphere with a radius of 3 meters and carries a uniform surface charge of +1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C. A technician needs to calculate the electric field strength exactly 2 meters from the center of the cage—inside the hollow region. What is the magnitude of the electric field at that point?

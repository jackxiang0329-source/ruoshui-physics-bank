# HFDPO-10970

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 100 m long copper coil suspended in hot rock at 350°C. Due to thermal expansion, the borehole diameter fluctuates by ±0.5 mm over a 30-minute cycle. This causes the coil’s effective area to change sinusoidally. If the Earth’s magnetic field has a vertical component of 55 μT and the coil’s average radius is 0.75 m, what is the maximum induced EMF in the coil, and what practical design limitation does this place on the sensor network monitoring the plant’s energy output?

# HFDPO-03831

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km into hot rock to extract steam for turbines. Engineers must calculate the minimum work required to pump 120 kg of cold brine (specific heat capacity 4180 J/kg·K) from the surface to the bottom of the borehole, where it’s heated to 280°C. Assume the brine enters the borehole at 10°C and the temperature increases linearly along the depth. Neglect friction and kinetic energy changes. What’s the total work input needed to raise and heat the brine?

# HFDPO-13977

A deep-sea research submersible encounters a sudden pressure anomaly at 3,500 meters depth during a mission to study hydrothermal vents. The outer hull is a composite of titanium and syntactic foam, designed to maintain structural integrity under extreme pressure. However, a small undetected crack in the foam layer begins to fill with seawater, reducing the buoyancy of the hull. The submersible’s neutral buoyancy was calibrated at 3,000 meters, but the downward drift accelerates as the crack grows. What is the minimum crack area (in cm²) that would cause a net downward force greater than 250 N, assuming the foam’s effective density increases from 450 kg/m³ to 1,025 kg/m³ due to water ingress? The hull has a total volume of 18 m³, and the seawater density is 1,025 kg/m³.

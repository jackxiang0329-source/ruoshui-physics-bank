# HFDPO-06614

A private space station operator detects a debris fragment—32 cm in diameter, mass ≈ 180 kg—on a grazing trajectory toward their 420 km altitude, 51.6° inclination orbit. The fragment’s current position is 1.8 km ahead in track, with a relative velocity of 1.2 m/s in the direction of motion. The station’s thrusters can produce 250 N of thrust for 4.8 s per burn. Determine the minimum number of burns required, and the optimal timing, for a single-impulse maneuver to achieve a 75 m perigee altitude change that avoids collision without exceeding 0.3 m/s Δv budget. Assume no atmospheric drag or third-body effects.

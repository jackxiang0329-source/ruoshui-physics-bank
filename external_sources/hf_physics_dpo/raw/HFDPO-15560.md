# HFDPO-15560

A Mars rover's rear wheel is stuck in regolith with a known shear strength of 3.2 kPa. The wheel has a radius of 0.3 m, a contact patch area of 0.015 m², and a coefficient of static friction μ_s = 0.45. Meanwhile, the rover’s motor applies a torque of 18 N·m, but the wheel begins to spin rather than roll. Assuming the wheel’s mass is 12 kg and rotational inertia is 0.6 kg·m², determine the minimum coefficient of rolling resistance required to prevent further slippage *and* explain why the wheel's angular acceleration exceeds what would be expected from pure rolling dynamics under this load.

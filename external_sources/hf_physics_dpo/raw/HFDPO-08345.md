# HFDPO-08345

A drone-mounted sensor array hangs vertically beneath a high-altitude research blimp cruising at 800 m above a glacier. The array’s 4.2 kg frame is suspended by two non-identical, flexible tethers—one made of Kevlar (2.1 mm diameter, E = 70 GPa), the other of Dyneema (1.8 mm diameter, E = 37 GPa)—each anchored at different points on the blimp’s underside. Due to wind shear, the system tilts such that the Kevlar tether makes a 25° angle with vertical, while the Dyneema tether forms a 38° angle. The blimp remains in static equilibrium despite constant atmospheric turbulence. Determine the tension in each tether and the horizontal displacement of the sensor’s center of mass from the blimp’s suspension point.

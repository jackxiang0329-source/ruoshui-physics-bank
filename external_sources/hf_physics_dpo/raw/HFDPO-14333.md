# HFDPO-14333

A portable medical MRI scanner uses a superconducting solenoid to generate a 1.5 T magnetic field, but due to a power fluctuation, the current begins to decay exponentially with a time constant of 2.4 seconds. The solenoid has 12,000 turns, a radius of 0.15 m, and self-inductance of 8.2 H. During the decay, a nearby 100-turn, 0.05 m radius coil (also superconducting) is placed coaxially, 0.2 m from the solenoid’s center. Calculate the total charge that flows through the secondary coil during the decay, and explain how this affects patient safety protocols.

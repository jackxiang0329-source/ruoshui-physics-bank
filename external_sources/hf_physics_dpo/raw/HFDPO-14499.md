# HFDPO-14499

A fusion reactor’s plasma confinement system uses a toroidal magnetic field, but engineers notice unexplained electric field spikes near the inner wall of the vacuum chamber. The chamber has a minor radius of 0.8 m and a major radius of 3.2 m. Measurements show the plasma has a non-uniform charge density ρ(r,θ) = ρ₀ exp(–r²/2σ²) cos(θ), where r is the radial distance from the torus center, θ is the poloidal angle, ρ₀ = 1.2×10⁻⁷ C/m³, and σ = 0.4 m. Determine the net electric flux through a spherical Gaussian surface of radius 1.5 m centered at the torus axis.

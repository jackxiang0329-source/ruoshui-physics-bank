# HFDPO-01771

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device, a droplet of aqueous solution (viscosity 1.0 mPa·s, surface tension 72 mN/m) is injected into a T-junction channel (width 50 μm) where it must be split asymmetrically by a continuous oil phase (viscosity 5 mPa·s, ρ = 850 kg/m³) flowing at 0.8 mL/min. The droplet’s injection speed is 0.2 mL/min, but due to unexpected coalescence downstream, the split ratio deviates from the designed 3:1. Devise a method to predict and correct the split asymmetry using capillary number analysis and inertial effects in the transition regime (Re ~ 0.1–1), considering both viscous and interfacial forces.

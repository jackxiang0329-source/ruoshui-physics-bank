# HFDPO-13422

A satellite equipped with a dual-polarization microwave antenna transmits signals to Earth through a turbulent ionosphere that randomly rotates the polarization of incoming waves. The ground receiver detects a mixture of horizontal and vertical components, but due to Faraday rotation, the ratio of these components shifts unpredictably over time. At a certain moment, the received signal has an intensity ratio of 4:1 (horizontal to vertical), and the total power is 120 mW. What is the actual power in each polarization plane *before* ionospheric distortion, assuming the original signal was linearly polarized at 45° to the horizontal?

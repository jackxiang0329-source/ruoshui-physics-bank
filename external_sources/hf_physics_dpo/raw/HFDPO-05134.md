# HFDPO-05134

A quantum imaging lab develops a novel ghost imaging setup using entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in a beta-barium borate (BBO) crystal. The system uses a single-pixel detector and a spatial light modulator (SLM) to reconstruct images of microscale biological specimens. However, researchers notice that image contrast degrades significantly when the pump laser intensity exceeds 1.2 W, despite maintaining phase-matching conditions. They suspect nonlinear saturation in the crystal or correlation loss due to temporal jitter. Investigate whether the degradation stems from reduced entanglement fidelity or photon loss due to intensity-dependent nonlinear effects, and determine the optimal pump power for maximum signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) in the reconstructed image.

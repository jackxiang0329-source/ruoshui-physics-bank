# HFDPO-03079

In a quantum computing prototype, electron interferometers are used to maintain coherence across qubit states. During calibration, researchers observe a shift in interference fringe spacing when the electron beam energy is tuned from 15 eV to 25 eV, yet the physical path difference remains unchanged. The measured fringe shift corresponds to a phase difference change of 0.8π radians. Explain whether this phase shift is better interpreted as a wave-like interference effect or a particle-like momentum change, and quantify the required precision of the electron's momentum control to suppress decoherence below 1% over a 10 μs coherence time.

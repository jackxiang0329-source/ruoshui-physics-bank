# HFDPO-03930

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber discovers that their portable water purifier—designed to freeze-purify contaminated meltwater—fails to produce ice crystals at -5°C, despite the ambient temperature being well below freezing. The device relies on nucleation from a cold metal plate, but the water remains supercooled. The purifier uses 200 mL of water with a density of 1 g/mL, and the latent heat of fusion for ice is 334 J/g. What explains the failure, and how much energy must be removed to initiate freezing, assuming the water is uniformly supercooled?

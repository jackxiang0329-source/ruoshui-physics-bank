# HFDPO-10376

During a deep-space mission, a prototype cryogenic fuel storage tank (volume 2.5 m³) containing liquid hydrogen (H₂) at 20 K develops a micro-leak, allowing ambient solar radiation (50 W/m²) to slowly heat the inner surface. The tank’s multi-layer insulation has degraded, with effective thermal resistance of 0.8 K·m²/W. If the tank’s initial state is 95% full of liquid H₂ at its triple point (Tₜ = 13.8 K, Pₜ = 7.5 kPa), what is the minimum time before the first vapor bubble forms at the tank’s bottom due to localized superheating? Assume the tank is spherical and the leak is a 0.1 mm² aperture with no heat transfer to the environment beyond the insulation.

# HFDPO-01058

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (radius 15 cm, 120 turns) operates at 13.56 MHz with a 5 W input, while the receiver coil (radius 3 mm, 20 turns) is embedded in a titanium casing with 2 mm thick insulating layers. The system experiences 68% power transfer efficiency but suffers from heating in the patient’s tissue near the receiver due to eddy currents. What is the optimal coil separation to maximize efficiency while keeping induced current in the surrounding tissue below 0.5 mA, assuming tissue conductivity is 0.6 S/m and relative permeability is 1.0?

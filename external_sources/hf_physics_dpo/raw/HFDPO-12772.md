# HFDPO-12772

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer (dielectric constant κ = 11.7) is charged to −3.2 μC due to ion bombardment during plasma etching. A nearby 20 cm long, grounded metal probe with a 2 mm radius approaches the wafer’s edge at a distance of 5 mm. The probe’s position is controlled by a piezoelectric actuator, but slight misalignment causes the electric field near the probe tip to locally exceed 3 MV/m—risking dielectric breakdown of residual gas. What is the minimum safe distance to prevent arcing, and how does the wafer’s finite extent affect the field enhancement compared to an infinite plane?

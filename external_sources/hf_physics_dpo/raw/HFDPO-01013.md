# HFDPO-01013

A quantum imaging lab is developing a lensless microscope using compressed sensing and coherent illumination at 633 nm. The sensor array has 1024 × 1024 pixels, each 3.45 μm wide, and captures speckle patterns from a disordered scatterer. To reconstruct a 50 μm × 50 μm sample with 1 μm resolution, the system must sample at least 8× the Nyquist rate in the Fourier domain. What is the minimum number of independent speckle patterns needed for successful reconstruction, and how does the coherence length of the laser affect the required number?

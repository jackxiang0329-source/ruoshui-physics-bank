# HFDPO-06341

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar-powered DC system with a 24 V battery bank and three parallel solar arrays, each with internal resistance of 0.5 Ω and open-circuit voltage of 30 V. During a prolonged storm, the station’s load (a data logger and thermal heater) draws 12 A at 24 V. The system includes a charge controller that maintains voltage regulation but fails to regulate current during low-light conditions. A technician discovers that one of the solar arrays is shorted—its output resistance drops to 0.1 Ω—while the others remain functional. What is the total current delivered by the battery bank to the load during this malfunction? How does this affect the system’s sustainability?

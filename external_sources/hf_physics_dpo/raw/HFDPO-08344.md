# HFDPO-08344

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a silicon photovoltaic panel with a bandgap of 1.1 eV. During peak sunlight, the panel generates 300 W of power under standard test conditions. However, over time, the system’s efficiency drops by 15% due to microcracks in the semiconductor material caused by thermal cycling. If the village relies on this system to operate a 12 V pump drawing 10 A, how many additional hours of full sunlight per day are needed to maintain the same daily water output, assuming the original system provided 6 hours of effective power?

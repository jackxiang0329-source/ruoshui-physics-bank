# HFDPO-08580

A desert solar farm uses rows of photovoltaic panels connected in series to power a remote weather station. Each panel produces 24 V and 8 A under peak sunlight, but the wiring between panels has a resistance of 0.5 Ω per meter. If the total wire length from the last panel to the station is 120 meters and the station requires 200 W of stable power, what is the minimum number of panels needed in series to ensure the station receives at least 90% of the intended power, assuming all panels are identical and sunlight is consistent?

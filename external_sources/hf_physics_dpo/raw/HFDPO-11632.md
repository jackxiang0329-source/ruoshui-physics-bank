# HFDPO-11632

During a desert sandstorm, a satellite dish at a remote weather station begins accumulating static charge, causing signal interference and occasional arcing to nearby metal supports. If the dish is grounded through a 10⁸ Ω resistor, but the surrounding air has a breakdown field of 3×10⁶ V/m, determine the maximum sustainable charge buildup before corona discharge occurs, and explain how the dish’s curvature affects the local electric field distribution.

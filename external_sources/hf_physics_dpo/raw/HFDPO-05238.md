# HFDPO-05238

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop tilts at 30° to maximize winter sunlight, but strong winds (up to 40 mph) threaten to lift it during storms. The panel weighs 120 kg and is secured by two brackets at its base, each rated for 600 N of upward force. If the wind exerts a horizontal force of 350 N at the panel’s center of mass, 1.8 m above the base, will the brackets safely hold the panel?

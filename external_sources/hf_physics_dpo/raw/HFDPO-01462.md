# HFDPO-01462

A lunar lander descends vertically near the south pole of the Moon, where surface gravity is 1.62 m/s². At 150 m altitude, its descent engine fires with a variable thrust that decreases linearly from 3200 N to 1800 N over 5 seconds, while the lander's mass decreases due to fuel burn at 12 kg/s. The lander’s initial velocity is 11 m/s downward. Determine the precise velocity and altitude at the end of the 5-second burn, accounting for both changing thrust and mass.

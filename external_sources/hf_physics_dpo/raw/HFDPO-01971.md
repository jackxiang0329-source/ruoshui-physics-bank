# HFDPO-01971

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a periodic nanostructured grating (period 600 nm) to enhance light absorption in the 650–800 nm range. However, experimental data shows a sharp dip in efficiency at 720 nm due to destructive interference in the transmitted light. The grating is etched into a transparent high-index substrate (n = 2.1) and coated with a thin metal layer (10 nm thick, complex refractive index ~2.5 – 0.8i). What phase shift must the grating introduce at 720 nm to restore constructive interference in absorption, given that the incident light is normal and the quantum dots are located 50 nm above the grating?

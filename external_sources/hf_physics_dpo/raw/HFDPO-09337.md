# HFDPO-09337

A private space station orbits Earth at 400 km altitude, but recent atmospheric drag has reduced its perigee to 380 km. Mission control must decide: should they apply a single impulse at apogee to raise the perigee back to 400 km, or use two small burns—first at perigee to raise apogee, then at new apogee to circularize the orbit? The station’s engine can deliver 150 m/s Δv per burn. Which strategy minimizes Δv cost while ensuring a stable 400 km circular orbit?

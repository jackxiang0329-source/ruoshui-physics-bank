# HFDPO-08102

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must navigate a narrow canyon where wind gusts reach 18 m/s perpendicular to its flight path. The drone has a mass of 3.2 kg and a cross-sectional area of 0.45 m². If the wind creates a drag force proportional to the square of the relative wind speed with a drag coefficient of 0.6, what minimum thrust force must the drone generate to maintain a steady 12 m/s forward velocity while compensating for lateral drift?

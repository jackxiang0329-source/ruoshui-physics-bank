# HFDPO-03523

A fusion energy reactor uses a toroidal plasma chamber where a 2.4 kg plasma blob, initially at rest, is compressed by magnetic fields to a final radius of 0.3 m from an initial 0.8 m. During compression, 120 MJ of external work is done on the plasma, but 35 MJ is lost to radiation. If the plasma’s moment of inertia decreases by 60% due to radial compression, what is the final rotational kinetic energy, and does the total energy and angular momentum conservation hold?

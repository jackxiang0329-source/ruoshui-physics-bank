# HFDPO-09416

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a ring of high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) cooled to 77 K to generate a 15 T magnetic field, but during ramp-up, a persistent current decay is observed—measured as a 0.3% drop in field strength per minute after quenching. The tape has a critical current density Jc = 1.5 × 10⁶ A/cm² and a penetration depth λ = 120 nm at 77 K. Determine the effective normal-state resistance of the superconducting loop and interpret what this implies about flux creep and current distribution.

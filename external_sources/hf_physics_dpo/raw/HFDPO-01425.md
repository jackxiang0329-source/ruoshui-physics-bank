# HFDPO-01425

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a polarimetric imager to monitor ocean surface winds, but data from a recent pass shows anomalous polarization signatures over a tropical storm. The instrument measures the degree of polarization (DoP) of reflected sunlight at 45° and 135° azimuth angles relative to the sun’s position. At 45°, the DoP is 0.32; at 135°, it drops to 0.11. The expected DoP over smooth water is 0.18, but the storm’s rough surface introduces wave-induced polarization anisotropy. Determine the magnitude and orientation of the dominant surface wave skew that explains the observed polarization imbalance, assuming the light is reflected from a slightly rough surface with an effective tilt angle distribution governed by a Gaussian model.

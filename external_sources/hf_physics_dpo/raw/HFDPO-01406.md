# HFDPO-01406

A quantum dot solar cell prototype uses lead-selenium nanocrystals (diameter ~5 nm) to absorb sunlight, but its efficiency drops sharply above 50°C. Measurements show the bandgap narrows by 0.03 eV per 10 K increase in temperature. Given that the room-temperature bandgap is 1.74 eV, what fraction of incoming photons (λ = 600 nm) can no longer be absorbed at 70°C due to thermal bandgap reduction? Assume the bandgap shift follows a linear trend and photons must have energy ≥ bandgap to excite electrons.

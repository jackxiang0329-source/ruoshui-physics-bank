# HFDPO-08552

A Mars rover’s rear wheel, coated with a self-cleaning hydrophobic layer, begins to slip on fine, electrostatically charged regolith during a dust storm. The wheel exerts 120 N of normal force per contact point, and the regolith has a dynamic friction coefficient of 0.42, but wind drag (drag coefficient Cd = 1.3, frontal area = 0.08 m²) increases exponentially with velocity due to turbulence. At 1.2 m/s, the drag force equals the maximum static friction. What is the minimum speed at which the wheel can maintain forward motion without slipping, considering both friction and non-linear drag?

# HFDPO-08902

A drone manufacturer is designing a new delivery drone with flexible landing legs made of a polymer that must absorb shock during landings on uneven terrain. Each leg is 12 cm long and deforms by 3 cm when the drone, weighing 2.5 kg, lands from a height of 1.5 m. What is the effective spring constant of one leg if the deformation is purely elastic and the leg returns to its original shape?

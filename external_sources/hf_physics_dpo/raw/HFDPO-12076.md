# HFDPO-12076

A deep-space probe en route to Jupiter uses a gravity assist around Venus, but the flyby trajectory deviates unexpectedly due to unmodeled thermal radiation pressure from the probe’s RTG. Given a nominal flyby distance of 15,000 km (from Venus’s center), a hyperbolic excess velocity of 12 km/s, and a measured deflection angle of 1.8° greater than predicted, determine the effective radiation pressure force acting on the probe during the flyby. Assume Venus’s mass is 4.87×10²⁴ kg and the flyby occurs at a relative velocity of 35 km/s.

# HFDPO-07135

A quantum dot solar cell is designed to absorb sunlight more efficiently by exploiting electron wave behavior in nanoscale structures. Why does the device fail to absorb longer wavelengths, and what is the minimum energy (in eV) required to excite an electron from the ground state to the first excited state within the quantum dot?

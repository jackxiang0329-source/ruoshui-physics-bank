# HFDPO-16243

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 300 m long copper coil (1000 turns) wrapped around a steel core to harvest induced currents from naturally occurring geomagnetic field fluctuations. Over a 24-hour period, the vertical component of Earth’s magnetic field varies sinusoidally with a peak amplitude of 50 μT and a period of 12 hours. The coil has a diameter of 0.5 m and is perfectly aligned with the field. At what average power (in watts) could this system deliver if connected to a resistive load of 1.5 Ω? Assume the magnetic field change is uniform across the coil area and neglect resistance of the wire.

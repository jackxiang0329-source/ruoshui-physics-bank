# HFDPO-04031

A spacecraft's thermal shielding layer, designed to protect against micrometeoroid impacts in low Earth orbit, experiences unexpected degradation. Engineers observe that the shield’s internal temperature rises significantly during orbital sunset, despite minimal solar exposure. The shield contains a nano-porous ceramic material with trapped argon gas at 1.2 atm and 300 K. During the eclipse phase, the gas is thermally isolated, but after 90 minutes, the gas temperature increases by 45 K due to residual heat from the outer surface. Calculate the average kinetic energy increase per argon atom and explain how this contributes to the observed material fatigue.

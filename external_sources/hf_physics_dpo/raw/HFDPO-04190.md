# HFDPO-04190

During cooldown, the system reaches 4.5 K, but a persistent current of 1.2 mA starts flowing in a loop despite no external voltage. The loop has a self-inductance of 2.3 nH. What is the magnetic flux trapped in the loop, and why must the system be carefully re-cooled in zero external field to prevent decoherence?

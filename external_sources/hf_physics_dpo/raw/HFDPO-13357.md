# HFDPO-13357

A solar-powered desert outpost relies on a network of copper interconnects to route 120 A from photovoltaic arrays to a central battery bank 800 m away. Engineers discover the voltage drop over the route exceeds specifications, threatening system efficiency. The wires are 4 mm diameter stranded copper (ρ₀ = 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20°C, α = 0.00393/°C). What is the minimum wire diameter needed to keep voltage drop below 4.8 V under maximum load?

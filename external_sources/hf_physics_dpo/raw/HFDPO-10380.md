# HFDPO-10380

During a lunar seismic survey, a probe detects a low-frequency oscillation in a 1.2 km-long lava tube, with surface measurements showing a standing wave pattern exhibiting three antinodes. Engineers suspect the oscillation is driven by tidal forces from Earth, which induce periodic stress changes at a frequency of 0.0008 Hz. If the average density of the tube’s basaltic walls is 2800 kg/m³ and the material’s Young’s modulus is 35 GPa, what is the fundamental frequency of longitudinal waves in the tube, and does this match a harmonic of the tidal forcing?

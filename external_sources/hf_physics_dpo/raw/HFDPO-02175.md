# HFDPO-02175

A deep-sea mining vessel uses a vertical riser pipe to lift mineral slurry from 2,500 meters below the ocean surface. The slurry, with density 1,800 kg/m³ and viscosity 0.08 Pa·s, flows upward at 1.2 m/s. At 1,000 m depth, a sudden 15° bend introduces flow separation, reducing local pressure by 12% and increasing turbulence. Determine the minimum pump pressure required at the seabed to maintain steady flow, accounting for hydrostatics, viscous losses over 2,500 m, and the pressure drop across the bend using the momentum equation.

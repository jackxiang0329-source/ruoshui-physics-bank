# HFDPO-07878

A quantum sensor in a deep-space observatory detects faint gravitational waves from a distant binary neutron star merger. The sensor uses a superconducting niobium pendulum suspended in a vacuum chamber, tuned to resonate at 100 Hz. After weeks of steady baseline data, a persistent 0.1 Hz modulation appears in the output—coinciding with the satellite’s orbital motion. The team suspects parametric resonance due to periodic changes in gravitational acceleration as the satellite orbits Earth. Can the observed modulation be explained by the pendulum’s nonlinear response to this parametric driving?

# HFDPO-02823

A space probe's solar array degrades over time in low Earth orbit due to electron bombardment from trapped radiation belts. At 1 AU, the array generates 120 W under standard illumination, but after 2 years in orbit, output drops to 95 W. Measurements show the degradation is due to the formation of deep-level traps in the SiC (silicon carbide) junction layer, increasing Shockley-Read-Hall recombination. Given the initial minority carrier lifetime is 1.2 μs and the degradation correlates to a trap density of 2×10¹⁶ cm⁻³, estimate the new recombination lifetime and explain how this affects the open-circuit voltage. Assume the intrinsic carrier concentration is 1.5×10¹⁰ cm⁻³, bandgap is 3.2 eV, and the junction is p-n with equal doping (N_D = N_A = 1×10¹⁷ cm⁻³).

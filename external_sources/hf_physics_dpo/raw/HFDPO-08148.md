# HFDPO-08148

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent emits superheated fluid (400°C, 300 atm) into cold seawater (2°C). The vent's chimney grows slowly as minerals precipitate from the cooling fluid. If heat transfer occurs only via conduction through a 10 cm thick layer of solidified mineral deposit (thermal conductivity 1.5 W/m·K), and the deposit’s internal temperature gradient is linear, what is the minimum thickness of the deposit required to maintain a steady-state heat flux of 500 W/m² without melting (melting point 600°C)? Assume the deposit is in thermal equilibrium with the vent fluid on one side and seawater on the other.

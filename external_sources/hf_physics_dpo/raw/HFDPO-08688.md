# HFDPO-08688

A geosynchronous satellite's reaction wheel, initially rotating at 1200 rpm to stabilize its orientation, suddenly experiences a 0.3% torque imbalance due to bearing wear. Over 15 minutes, the wheel's angular speed increases nonlinearly as magnetic drag and thermal expansion alter its moment of inertia. Given that the wheel's moment of inertia changes according to I(t) = I_0(1 + 0.001sin(omega t)) , with I_0 = 0.45,text{kg·m}^2 and omega = 0.01,text{rad/s} , determine the final angular speed of the wheel and explain how this drift affects the satellite’s attitude control.

# HFDPO-11201

The device relies on interference patterns from light scattered by red blood cells and glucose molecules. If the optical path difference between two interfering rays through a 2 mm thick tissue layer (n = 1.35) must be precisely λ/4 to enhance sensitivity, how much should the thickness of a transparent polymer layer (n = 1.45) be adjusted to compensate for variations in tissue density?

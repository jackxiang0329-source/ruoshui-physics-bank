# HFDPO-08943

A biomedical engineer is designing a targeted drug delivery system using magnetic nanoparticles coated with cancer-fighting agents. These particles are injected into a patient’s bloodstream and guided to a tumor using an external magnetic field. The tumor is located 8 cm beneath the skin, and the engineer uses a solenoid with 500 turns packed over a 15 cm length to generate a field strong enough to pull the particles. What minimum current must flow through the solenoid to produce a field of 0.15 T at its center? Assume the solenoid is ideal and the field is uniform over the region of interest.

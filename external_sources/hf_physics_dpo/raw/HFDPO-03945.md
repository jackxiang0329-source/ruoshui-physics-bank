# HFDPO-03945

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar array and a bank of lithium-ion batteries to power sensors and a satellite uplink. The solar panels produce a variable DC output of 24 V under ideal conditions but drop to 18 V during heavy cloud cover. The station’s control circuit uses a microcontroller with a 5 V logic supply, requiring stable voltage regulation despite input fluctuations. The existing voltage regulator (a 5 V linear regulator) is overheating, and the team suspects the power dissipation exceeds its thermal limits. The system operates 24/7 with a total load current of 1.2 A when active. The team is considering replacing the regulator with a switching regulator to reduce power loss. Design a solution to stabilize the 5 V supply while minimizing heat and energy waste, and calculate the expected efficiency improvement.

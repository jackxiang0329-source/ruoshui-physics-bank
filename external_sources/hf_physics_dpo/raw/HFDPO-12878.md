# HFDPO-12878

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted on a sloped roof at 25° to the horizontal, with the panels tilted at 35° for optimal sunlight exposure. During a rare snowstorm, 20 cm of wet snow accumulates uniformly across the 3.5 m × 2.0 m array. The snow has a density of 300 kg/m³. If the panel mountings can withstand a maximum horizontal force of 1,200 N before slipping, will the system remain stable, and what is the minimum coefficient of static friction required at the base of the mount?

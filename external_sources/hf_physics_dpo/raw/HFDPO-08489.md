# HFDPO-08489

A quantum imaging lab uses entangled photon pairs to enhance resolution in low-light biological microscopy. One pair is split: one photon passes through a live cell sample (weakly scattering, 10 nm refractive index variation), while its partner is sent to a detector without interacting.

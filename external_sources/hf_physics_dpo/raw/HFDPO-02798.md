# HFDPO-02798

A quantum dot solar cell prototype uses a semiconductor nanocrystal with a diameter of 4.2 nm to absorb sunlight. The bandgap energy is tunable due to quantum confinement—when the dot diameter shrinks, the energy gap increases. If the measured absorption peak shifts from 630 nm to 580 nm after synthesis, what is the change in bandgap energy? Assume the effective mass of the electron is 0.15 times the free electron mass, and the hole mass is 0.3 times the free electron mass.

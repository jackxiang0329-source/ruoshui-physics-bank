# HFDPO-05918

In a malfunctioning quantum computing chip, three superconducting qubits are coupled via tunable couplers forming a closed loop with shared ground. During calibration, a current of 1.8 μA enters the loop at a Josephson junction, but measurements show only 1.3 μA exiting through a readout line. The loop contains two Josephson junctions with non-linear inductances (L₁ = 0.3 nH, L₂ = 0.5 nH) and a shunting resistor (R = 75 Ω). The system is oscillating at 10 GHz. Find the current distribution around the loop and explain the apparent discrepancy in current values using Kirchhoff’s laws and circuit dynamics.

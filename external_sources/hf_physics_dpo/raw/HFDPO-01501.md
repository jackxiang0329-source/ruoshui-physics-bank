# HFDPO-01501

During a Mars surface mission, a rover’s suspension system experiences unexpected oscillations when traversing rocky terrain. The rear wheel, with a mass of 12 kg and radius of 0.3 m, rolls without slipping but is connected to a torsional spring (k = 250 N·m/rad) and viscous damper (c = 80 N·m·s/rad) that resist angular motion relative to the chassis. At a certain speed, the wheel encounters a sudden 5.0 cm vertical step, causing transient angular acceleration. Determine the maximum angular displacement of the wheel relative to the chassis immediately after impact, assuming the step imparts an instantaneous angular impulse of 0.6 N·m·s due to the impact force.

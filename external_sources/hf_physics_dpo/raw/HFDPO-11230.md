# HFDPO-11230

A satellite constellation in low Earth orbit must perform a precision repositioning maneuver to avoid a growing debris cloud at 650 km altitude. The leading satellite, moving at 7.52 km/s in a circular orbit, needs to adjust its orbit to a 640 km apogee and 630 km perigee ellipse to safely bypass the hazard. However, the onboard thrusters are limited to 300 m/s total Δv, and the satellite must maintain strict alignment with other fleet members post-maneuver. What is the minimum Δv required to achieve this new elliptical orbit, and can the maneuver be executed within the fuel constraint if the burn must be executed tangentially at the current orbit's perigee?

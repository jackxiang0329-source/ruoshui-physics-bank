# HFDPO-06955

A Mars rover’s navigation system relies on gyroscopic stabilization during sandstorm-induced tremors. The rover's main gyroscope, a uniform solid disk of mass 2.4 kg and radius 8.0 cm, spins at 18,000 rpm to maintain orientation. During a sudden lateral jolt, a torque of 0.35 N·m is applied perpendicular to the spin axis for 0.12 s. What is the resulting precession rate of the gyroscope, and how does this affect the rover’s attitude control over the next 30 seconds?

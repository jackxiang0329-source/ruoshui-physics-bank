# HFDPO-04067

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain region uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a 150 W solar panel array. Due to seasonal snow cover, the panel output drops to 40% of rated capacity for three weeks. The station’s electronics draw 2.1 A continuously. If the battery bank is 80% charged at the start of the period, can it sustain operations without damage? Assume the battery’s safe discharge limit is 40% of capacity and the system has 85% efficiency.

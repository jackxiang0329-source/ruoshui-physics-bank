# HFDPO-06703

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 2.0 cm diameter silicon wafer (dielectric constant εᵣ = 11.7) accumulates charge from ionized ambient gas during plasma etching. At a critical point, the electric field at the wafer surface reaches 1.8 × 10⁶ V/m—just below the dielectric breakdown threshold of 2.0 × 10⁶ V/m—but a microscopic silicon nitride coating (εᵣ = 7.5) on the wafer’s edge begins to exhibit spontaneous discharge via field emission. Given that the coating is 50 nm thick and the total charge on the wafer is uniformly distributed, determine whether the electric field at the coating’s inner surface exceeds the macroscopic field emission threshold of 1.5 × 10⁷ V/m, and explain why the discharge occurs despite the wafer’s bulk field being sub-breakdown.

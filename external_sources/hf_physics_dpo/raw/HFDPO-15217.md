# HFDPO-15217

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid. The geothermal fluid enters at 180°C and exits at 90°C, transferring heat to isobutane in a heat exchanger. The isobutane vapor drives a turbine, condenses at 40°C, and is pumped back. The plant produces 25 MW of electrical power with a turbine efficiency of 82% of ideal Carnot. What is the minimum required mass flow rate of geothermal fluid if it has a specific heat capacity of 4.2 kJ/kg·K? Assume the plant operates with a 30% thermal efficiency.

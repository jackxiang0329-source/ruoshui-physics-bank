# HFDPO-10450

However, the particles are so small that electrostatic repulsion between them causes clustering, reducing coating uniformity. What is the minimum separation distance between two such charged particles where the electrostatic repulsion balances the van der Waals attraction (assumed proportional to 1/r⁶, with coefficient 3.0 × 10⁻¹⁰⁰ J·m⁶)?

# HFDPO-00202

A renewable energy microgrid in a remote village uses a 50 Hz AC inverter to sync solar-generated power with a fluctuating load. Due to long transmission lines, the system exhibits significant inductive reactance (12 Ω) and distributed capacitance (0.004 F), causing oscillations in voltage stability. At peak sun, the inverter outputs 240 V RMS at 50 Hz, but the voltage at the load terminal dips to 210 V when the load demands 15 kW with a lagging power factor of 0.75. What is the actual line impedance magnitude, and how should the system be tuned (via capacitor bank) to restore voltage regulation without destabilizing resonance?

# HFDPO-16451

In a semiconductor fabrication cleanroom, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of –2.4 nC/m² is suspended by electrostatic forces between two parallel plate electrodes 1.2 cm apart. A 50 µm diameter silicon nanoparticle, initially neutral, drifts into the gap and acquires a charge of +3.2 × 10⁻¹⁶ C upon contact with the wafer. What is the minimum initial velocity the nanoparticle must have at the edge of the gap to avoid being pulled back toward the wafer? Assume the electric field is uniform and the particle’s mass is 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ kg.

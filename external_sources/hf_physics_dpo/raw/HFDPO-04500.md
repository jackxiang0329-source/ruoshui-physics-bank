# HFDPO-04500

A biomedical engineer is designing a new MRI shielding room to prevent external electric fields from distorting sensitive imaging. The room is a hollow, uncharged metal cube with 3-meter sides. During a test, a small charged probe is placed just outside one face, creating a uniform electric field of 500 N/C perpendicular to that face. What is the total electric flux through the entire surface of the cube?

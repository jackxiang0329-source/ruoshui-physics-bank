# HFDPO-11143

A NASA Mars rover's power system uses a series of solar arrays and lithium-ion batteries, but during a prolonged dust storm, solar input drops to 18 W per array. Each array has a maximum power point of 14 V and 1.28 A under ideal conditions. After 5 days of darkness, the rover's onboard voltage regulator must maintain 12.6 V to power essential systems. If the batteries are 12 V, 150 Ah, and the internal resistance of each cell is 0.03 Ω, what is the effective load resistance that allows the system to sustain 12.6 V across the regulator while minimizing battery degradation?

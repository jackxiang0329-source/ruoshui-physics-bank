# HFDPO-02287

During a lunar dust storm simulation, a 1.2 kg rover arm with a 0.8 m rigid arm is designed to sweep dust from solar panels. The arm rotates about a frictionless joint at 1.5 rad/s but must decelerate to 0.3 rad/s over 0.6 s to avoid damaging the panel. If dust accumulates at 0.18 kg/m along the arm, increasing its mass distribution unevenly, what torque must the motor apply to safely control the deceleration, and how does non-uniform mass affect angular acceleration compared to a uniform arm?

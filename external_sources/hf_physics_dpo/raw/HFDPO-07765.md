# HFDPO-07765

A renewable energy startup is designing a solar panel array mounted on a sloped rooftop in a coastal city prone to strong winds. The panels are secured with a single bolt at the base of each frame, and the entire system must withstand a horizontal wind force of 1200 N acting at 1.8 m above the base. The frame weighs 350 N and its center of gravity is 1.2 m above the base, offset 0.5 m from the mounting point. Determine the minimum torque the bolt must resist to prevent the frame from rotating, and assess whether a bolt rated for 400 N·m is sufficient.

# HFDPO-15242

During a lunar mining operation, a drill bit made of conductive composite material spins at 1200 rpm in a region with a residual magnetic field of 28 μT, oriented 35° from the vertical. The drill’s cylindrical shaft has a radius of 1.7 cm and length 45 cm. Engineers detect an unexpected 3.2 V peak AC voltage in the drill’s sensor array—originally thought to be electronic noise. Explain whether this voltage can be attributed solely to electromagnetic induction in the shaft, and if so, how consistent is this data with the known magnetic field and rotational speed?

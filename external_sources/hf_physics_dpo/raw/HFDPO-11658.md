# HFDPO-11658

A solar panel array on a remote weather station is mounted at a 30° incline to optimize sunlight capture. During a storm, strong lateral winds apply a horizontal force of 1,200 N at the center of mass, 2.5 m above the base. The panel frame is anchored by two bolts at the bottom edge, spaced 1.8 m apart. To prevent tipping, the bolts must resist both vertical forces and moments. If the panel weighs 850 N, what is the minimum coefficient of static friction required between the bolts and the mounting surface to prevent sliding, assuming the bolts carry equal vertical loads and the system is on the verge of tipping?

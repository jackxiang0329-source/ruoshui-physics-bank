# HFDPO-01008

During a winter storm, a city’s water pipes begin freezing in unheated basement areas. One 2-meter long copper pipe, initially at -10°C, fills with water at 0°C. If the pipe is sealed and the water fully freezes, by how much does the internal pressure increase due to ice expansion? Assume the pipe is rigid and the water occupies 100% of the pipe’s volume before freezing.

# HFDPO-12298

A drone manufacturer is developing a vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) aircraft for urban delivery, but field tests show erratic pitch oscillations during ascent when hovering near a tall building. The drone’s four rotors each produce 25 N of thrust, and the system has a total mass of 8 kg. As it hovers 6 m from a 40 m high building, side winds gust up to 8 m/s. Engineers suspect vortex shedding from the building’s edge induces periodic forces on the drone’s airframe. Using fluid dynamics and rotational dynamics, estimate the minimum moment of inertia the drone’s stabilization system must have to suppress pitch oscillations driven by a 3 Hz vortex shedding frequency.

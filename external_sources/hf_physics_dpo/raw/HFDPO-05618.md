# HFDPO-05618

In a high-vacuum particle accelerator, a beam of 1.2 MeV protons travels through a drift tube with a cylindrical insulating liner made of fused silica (dielectric constant εᵣ = 3.8). If the liner has a radius of 5.0 cm and the accumulated charge density is 2.7 × 10⁻⁸ C/m², what is the resulting potential difference between the liner surface and the beam axis? How might this affect beam stability over time?

# HFDPO-03906

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a remote mountain ridge, secured by a single high-strength titanium rod anchored into fractured bedrock. The rod is inclined at 35° from vertical and supports a 120 kg platform holding sensors, communication gear, and a 25 kg solar array. During a windstorm, gusts reach 45 m/s, generating a horizontal force of 1,800 N on the platform. The anchor point is 2.4 m below the platform’s center of mass, and the rod’s length is 3.8 m. Determine the minimum friction coefficient required at the anchor to prevent rotational slip, given that the rock surface is uneven and the contact area is small.

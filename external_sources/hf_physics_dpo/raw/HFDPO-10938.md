# HFDPO-10938

A quantum sensor designed to detect minute magnetic fluctuations in brain activity (magnetoencephalography) uses nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond. The sensor’s coherence time is limited by spin relaxation due to environmental noise, particularly at 20 K. An experimentalist measures a decoherence rate of 1.2 × 10⁵ s⁻¹, and the NV center’s spin states are initialized to |↑⟩ via optical pumping. After applying a π/2 pulse, the system evolves under a resonant microwave field. What is the optimal pulse duration to maximize the visibility of the Rabi oscillation, given that the microwave Rabi frequency is 2.4 × 10⁴ s⁻¹ and the system’s coherence time is dominated by T₂* = 1/(2Γ) where Γ includes both pure dephasing and relaxation?

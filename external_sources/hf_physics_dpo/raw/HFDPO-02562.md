# HFDPO-02562

In a high-voltage particle accelerator facility, a vacuum chamber contains a spherical electrode with a radius of 0.3 m, charged to +120 kV relative to ground. A dust particle (diameter 10 μm, density 2.5 g/cm³) enters the chamber near the electrode's surface, carrying a net charge of −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁴ C due to contact with a low-work-function material. The particle experiences both electrostatic attraction and ion drag from residual gas. Determine the minimum initial velocity the particle must have to avoid being pulled into the electrode, accounting for the nonlinear electric field and a drag force proportional to velocity (c = 5 × 10⁻¹⁰ N·s/m).

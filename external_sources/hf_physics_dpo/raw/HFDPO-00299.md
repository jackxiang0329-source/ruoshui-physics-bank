# HFDPO-00299

A medical imaging team is developing a new MRI scanner that uses a rapidly oscillating magnetic field (10 kHz) to excite hydrogen nuclei in tissue. The scanner’s coil generates a time-varying magnetic field of 1.5 T at its center, but engineers notice unexpected heating in adjacent copper shielding. Given that the shielding is a 1 mm thick cylindrical shell with conductivity σ = 5.8×10⁷ S/m and radius 20 cm, determine the magnitude of the induced electric field just inside the shield and estimate the average power dissipated per square meter due to eddy currents. Assume the field changes sinusoidally with time and the system is symmetric.

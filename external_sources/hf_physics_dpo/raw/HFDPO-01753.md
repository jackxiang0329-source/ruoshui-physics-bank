# HFDPO-01753

A drone carrying a 2.4 kg payload descends vertically through a dense fog layer at a constant speed of 0.8 m/s when a sudden gust of wind imparts a horizontal impulse, causing it to tilt forward by 12° from vertical. If the drag force is proportional to velocity squared with a coefficient of 0.35 N·s²/m², what is the horizontal acceleration the drone must generate to prevent drifting more than 1.5 m during the next 3 seconds?

# HFDPO-00805

A satellite in geostationary orbit (35,786 km above Earth’s surface) carries an ultra-precise atomic clock. Over one week, mission control observes a cumulative time drift of 38 microseconds relative to clocks on the ground. Given that gravitational time dilation slows clocks in weaker gravitational fields and special relativistic effects speed them up due to motion, what must be the combined correction factor applied to the satellite’s clock to maintain synchronization with Earth-bound clocks?

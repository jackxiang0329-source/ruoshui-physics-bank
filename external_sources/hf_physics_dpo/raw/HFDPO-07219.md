# HFDPO-07219

A coastal city is designing a new seawater desalination plant using reverse osmosis membranes. To minimize energy costs, engineers must optimize the flow velocity through a 15 cm diameter pipeline carrying pressurized brine at 40°C. The system operates at 60 bar, and the brine has a density of 1050 kg/m³ and viscosity of 1.2 × 10⁻³ Pa·s. If the goal is to maintain turbulent flow (Re > 4000) for efficient mixing but avoid excessive pressure drop, what is the minimum flow rate (in m³/h) that ensures turbulence without exceeding a 150 kPa pressure loss over a 100 m length?

# HFDPO-11619

A quantum imaging lab develops a single-photon camera using orbital angular momentum (OAM) states to detect faint biomedical signals from living tissues. The system encodes information in helical wavefronts with ℓ = ±15, but due to atmospheric turbulence during in vivo experiments, the measured intensity pattern shifts by 1.8° per meter of propagation. To recover the original OAM state, the team must design a phase mask that compensates for the induced phase distortion across a 20 cm path. However, the mask fabrication is limited to a resolution of 1.2 μm, and the wavelength is λ = 780 nm. What is the minimum number of discrete phase levels required in the spatial light modulator to achieve a phase correction accuracy better than 0.1 rad?

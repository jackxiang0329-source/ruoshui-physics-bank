# HFDPO-13157

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole (2.5 km deep) to extract hot water from underground reservoirs. The water enters the borehole at 220°C and 15 MPa, rising due to buoyancy and pumping pressure. The system must deliver 50 MW of net power to the grid, but the pump consumes 8 MW. If the water exits the borehole at 180°C and 2.5 MPa, and the mass flow rate is 300 kg/s, what fraction of the total energy extracted from the geothermal fluid is lost to inefficiencies in the system (pumping, friction, heat loss), and how does this compare to the theoretical maximum efficiency given the temperature difference?

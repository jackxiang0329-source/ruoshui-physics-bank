# HFDPO-10178

A marine biologist uses a laser-based underwater communication system to transmit data between two autonomous drones 15 meters apart at a depth of 5 meters. The laser has a wavelength of 633 nm in air, and the water has a refractive index of 1.33. Due to turbulence, the water’s surface fluctuates slightly, causing the laser beam to undergo diffraction at the edge of a diving mask worn by a nearby diver. If the diver’s mask has a circular aperture of 1.2 cm in diameter, what is the minimum angular separation between two distant signal sources (e.g., glowing plankton) that the system can resolve at the drone’s receiver?

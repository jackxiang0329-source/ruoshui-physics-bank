# HFDPO-01265

A fusion reactor’s magnetic confinement chamber uses a toroidal shell with radially varying dielectric coating to suppress plasma instabilities. The coating’s permittivity varies as ε(r) = ε₀(1 + α/r), where r is radial distance from the torus center and α = 0.2 m. At a radial position of 0.8 m, an embedded sensor measures a total electric flux of 3.6 × 10⁻⁸ N·m²/C through a spherical Gaussian surface enclosing part of the torus. What is the net charge enclosed by this surface, and how does the non-uniform permittivity affect the interpretation of Gauss’s Law?

# HFDPO-02514

A particle accelerator in Geneva detects muons generated by cosmic ray collisions at 12 km above sea level, moving at 0.998c relative to Earth. Observers on the ground record an average lifetime of 2.5 μs before decay. However, an onboard detector on a satellite tracking the same muons measures a lifetime of 0.25 μs. How can both observations be simultaneously correct? Calculate the actual proper lifetime of the muon and determine the altitude the satellite must be at to observe this dilated time, assuming uniform motion and no gravitational effects.

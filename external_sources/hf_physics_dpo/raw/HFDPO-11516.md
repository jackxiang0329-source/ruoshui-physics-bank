# HFDPO-11516

During a Mars rover mission, a 120 kg scientific instrument package is lowered from a crane arm 5 meters above the surface using a flexible cable with linear mass density 0.4 kg/m. The crane applies a variable force to prevent oscillations, but due to a software glitch, the cable’s length begins to decrease at a rate proportional to the square of the instantaneous tension. If the instrument starts at rest and the crane begins reeling in the cable at 0.3 m/s², determine the time it takes for the instrument to descend 3 meters with minimal jerk, considering both cable mass and dynamic tension feedback.

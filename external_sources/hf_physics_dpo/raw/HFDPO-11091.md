# HFDPO-11091

A small wind turbine in a rural village generates 150 W of electrical power on a windy day. The turbine's blades turn a generator that converts mechanical energy into electricity. If the blades capture 60% of the kinetic energy from the wind passing through them, and the wind speed is 12 m/s, what is the mass of air passing through the turbine blades each second? Assume the air density is 1.2 kg/m³.

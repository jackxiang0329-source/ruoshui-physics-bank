# HFDPO-02736

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses a custom underwater laser scanner that emits coherent green light (λ = 532 nm) to map micro-structures on reef surfaces. During a dive, they notice that the diffraction pattern from a porous coral skeleton shifts dramatically when the water temperature rises by 5°C, causing interference fringes to blur and lose contrast. The coral’s surface has a periodic lattice spacing of 1.2 μm. What is the minimum temperature-induced change in the refractive index of water that could account for this loss of fringe visibility due to the shift in effective wavelength?

# HFDPO-04121

A solar farm in the desert uses polarizing filters on its photovoltaic panels to reduce glare-induced efficiency loss from sunlight reflecting off sand. The incident sunlight is unpolarized, but sand reflection creates mostly horizontally polarized glare. If the panels are designed to accept only vertically polarized light for optimal absorption, and 60% of the reflected light is horizontally polarized, what fraction of the total reflected light (from both reflection and direct incidence) is now usable by the panels, assuming 40% of total incident light is reflected?

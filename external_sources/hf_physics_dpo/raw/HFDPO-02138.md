# HFDPO-02138

A coastal town is designing a floating solar panel array for a lagoon where the water density varies due to salinity gradients. The panels are anchored to the seabed with cables, and the top of each panel must stay 15 cm above the surface to avoid wave submersion. If the average density of the panel assembly (including framing and solar cells) is 850 kg/m³ and the lagoon water has a density of 1025 kg/m³, how much of the panel’s total volume must be hollow (air-filled) to maintain the required 15 cm clearance?

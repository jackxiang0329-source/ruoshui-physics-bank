# HFDPO-03229

A quantum computing lab in Oslo is developing a new superconducting qubit array cooled to 15 mK using a dilution refrigerator. During a calibration run, engineers notice that the qubit’s resonance frequency shifts unpredictably when the magnetic field exceeds 50 μT—well below the critical field of the niobium-based circuit (Hc2 ≈ 200 mT). They suspect flux trapping in the surrounding superconducting shielding. Calculate the maximum magnetic flux quantum (Φ₀) that could be trapped in a single Josephson junction loop (with inductance L = 10 pH) without causing a frequency shift, and explain how the quantization of magnetic flux relates to the observed instability.

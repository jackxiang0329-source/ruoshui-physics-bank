# HFDPO-04774

A lunar rover’s rear wheel, shaped like a hollow cylinder with mass 25 kg and outer radius 0.6 m, begins to slip on loose regolith during a slope descent. As it rolls without slipping initially, the wheel’s center accelerates at 0.8 m/s² down a 15° incline. Suddenly, regolith compaction fails, reducing the coefficient of static friction to 0.25—but kinetic friction of 0.18 remains. Determine the wheel’s angular acceleration *immediately after slipping begins*, and explain why the wheel’s rotational behavior changes despite constant linear acceleration.

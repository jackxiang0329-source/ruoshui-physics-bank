# HFDPO-05172

A solar farm in the desert uses photovoltaic panels arranged in a fixed tilt but rotates daily to track the sun. Engineers notice that output drops by 12% during midday when glare from the sand reflects off the panels' anti-reflective coating. The coating is designed to minimize reflection at 55° incidence (perpendicular to the glass surface), but the reflected light is polarized. What angle should a polarizing filter be mounted at (relative to horizontal) to maximize blocking of this glare and restore 90% of the lost power? Assume the reflected light is completely polarized parallel to the surface.

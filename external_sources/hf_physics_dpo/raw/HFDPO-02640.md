# HFDPO-02640

In a semiconductor fabrication plant, a charged microchip (2.0 cm × 2.0 cm) with a non-uniform surface charge density σ(x) = σ₀(1 + 0.5 sin(πx/L)), where L = 2.0 cm and σ₀ = 10⁻⁸ C/m², is being transported through a vacuum chamber. A grounded, curved electrode (radius of curvature 1.5 mm) is positioned 0.5 mm away from the chip’s surface, forming a localized electric field. Calculate the total electrostatic force on the chip due to the induced image charges, accounting for both the non-uniformity of the charge distribution and the curvature of the electrode.

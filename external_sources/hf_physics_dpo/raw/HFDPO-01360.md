# HFDPO-01360

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote village powers a community center using a 120 V AC generator, but after installation, lights flicker and sensitive electronics overheat. Measurements show the system draws 8 A at a lagging power factor of 0.6. The wiring has a resistance of 0.5 Ω and inductance of 2 mH. Determine the voltage drop across the transmission line and explain why the voltage at the load is insufficient. Then, calculate the value of a capacitor needed to correct the power factor to 0.95, and assess whether this would reduce the flickering.

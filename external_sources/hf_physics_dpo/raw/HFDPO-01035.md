# HFDPO-01035

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) equipped with a high-precision sensor array detects localized turbulence near a hydrothermal vent chimney. The plume rises 12 meters before dispersing, but the ROV’s sensors show unexpected lateral oscillations. Determine the dominant instability mechanism driving this oscillation, and estimate the growth rate of perturbations if the vertical velocity gradient exceeds the critical Rayleigh number for thermal convection. Use the Boussinesq approximation and a linear stability analysis.

# HFDPO-15221

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a complex, toroidal magnetic field to confine ionized plasma, but engineers detect a persistent electric field inside the vacuum chamber despite no net charge being introduced. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the total electric flux through a spherical Gaussian surface just inside the vessel wall at radius R. Can the observed electric field be reconciled with Gauss’s Law if the plasma is neutral?

# HFDPO-00140

A quantum dot solar cell uses a 2D array of subwavelength holes (diameter 120 nm) arranged in a triangular lattice with spacing 400 nm to enhance light trapping. Under AM1.5G sunlight, the cell shows unexpected peaks in efficiency at 650 nm and 780 nm. Given the hole pattern acts as a diffraction grating with non-uniform phase shifts due to near-field coupling, determine whether the observed peaks are due to constructive interference from first-order diffraction or resonance in the quantum dots. Use the grating equation and consider the effective refractive index of the semiconductor (n = 3.5) and angle of incidence (30°).

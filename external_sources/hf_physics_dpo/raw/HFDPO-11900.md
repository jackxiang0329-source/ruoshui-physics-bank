# HFDPO-11900

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal city, where strong gusts can exceed 30 m/s. The station’s sensor array is secured by a single bolt at the base, but during a storm, engineers notice the mounting bracket begins to pivot slightly at the wall attachment. The 12-kg unit is 0.9 m tall, with its center of mass 0.6 m above the base and 0.2 m forward from the wall. The bracket is made of steel and can withstand a maximum shear force of 150 N. What is the minimum horizontal force (perpendicular to the wall) exerted by wind on the sensor array that could cause the bracket to fail in shear? Assume the wind acts at the center of mass and the bracket is rigid.

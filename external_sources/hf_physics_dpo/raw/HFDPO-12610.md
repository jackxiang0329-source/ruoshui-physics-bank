# HFDPO-12610

A wildlife biologist uses a portable infrared camera to monitor nocturnal animals in a dense forest. The camera’s lens has a focal length of 50 mm and a diameter of 25 mm. When observing a fox 100 m away, the lens aperture limits resolution due to diffraction. The biologist notices that distant trees appear blurred in the image. To improve clarity, she considers replacing the lens with one of the same focal length but a larger diameter. What minimum diameter must the new lens have to resolve two points on the fox separated by 15 cm, assuming infrared wavelength of 10 μm?

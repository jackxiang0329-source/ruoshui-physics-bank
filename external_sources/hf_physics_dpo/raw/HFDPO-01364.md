# HFDPO-01364

A wind turbine blade on a remote island wind farm is damaged during a storm, and engineers must assess whether the broken section—measured at 4.2 meters long, 0.6 meters wide, and 0.15 meters thick—can be safely lowered to the ground using a controlled descent system. The blade fragment weighs 95 kg and is initially suspended 12 meters above the ground at a 30° angle from vertical. If the lowering mechanism applies a constant force of 820 N along the direction of motion to prevent free fall, how much mechanical energy is dissipated during the descent due to the applied force? Assume no air resistance and that the motion is along the arc of a circle.

# HFDPO-01809

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a cylindrical coil (radius 0.8 m, 1200 turns) buried 2 km underground to monitor subsurface magnetic anomalies caused by magma movement. Over a 30-second interval, a sudden upward surge in magma creates a time-varying magnetic field approximated by B(t) = B₀·e^(−t/τ), where B₀ = 80 nT and τ = 15 s. Calculate the peak induced EMF in the coil and determine whether the SQUID can detect the signal, considering thermal noise.

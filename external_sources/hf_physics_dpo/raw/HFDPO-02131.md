# HFDPO-02131

A fusion reactor's plasma containment field is disrupted when a sudden ripple in the toroidal magnetic field induces eddy currents in the surrounding liquid lithium blanket, causing localized heating and mechanical stress. The blanket, 0.3 m thick and with resistivity 1.4×10⁻⁶ Ω·m, experiences a magnetic flux change of 2.5 T/s across its cross-section. Estimate the power dissipation per unit volume due to eddy currents and evaluate whether the thermal expansion from this heating could compromise structural integrity—considering the lithium’s thermal expansion coefficient and yield strength.

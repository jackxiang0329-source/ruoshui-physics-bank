# HFDPO-15701

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, biocompatible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a diameter of 2.5 mm to 4.0 mm during deployment, yet return to 2.8 mm when fully relaxed. The stent is made from a shape-memory alloy with a nonlinear stress-strain response described by σ = 300ε + 150ε² (in MPa), where ε is the engineering strain. If the stent’s wall thickness is 0.1 mm and it must withstand cyclic loading of 100,000 cycles without fatigue failure, what is the maximum allowable applied stress during expansion to ensure both safe deformation and long-term integrity?

# HFDPO-16602

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses superheated steam (250°C, 5 MPa) from a deep well to drive a turbine, but engineers notice a sudden drop in output efficiency. The steam enters the turbine at 15 kg/s, expands to 0.3 MPa, and exits at 200°C. However, the turbine’s exhaust is wet—some condensation forms in the blades. The plant uses a closed-loop system with a condenser that cools the steam to saturated liquid at 0.3 MPa. Determine whether the observed efficiency loss stems from irreversibilities in the turbine or from heat loss in the condenser, using only mass, energy, and entropy conservation.

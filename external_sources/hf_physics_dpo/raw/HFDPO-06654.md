# HFDPO-06654

During a winter storm, a city’s water distribution system experiences repeated freezing events. The pipe has a diameter of 2.5 cm and is rigid (no expansion). What is the minimum internal pressure (in MPa) that develops during freezing, and why does this explain the pattern of pipe failures?

# HFDPO-03616

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a 200 W solar array. The system powers a 1.5 kW water pump during the day, but the wiring between the battery and pump is long and thin (100 m, 2.5 mm² copper), causing significant voltage drop. When the pump runs, the voltage at its terminals drops to 10.4 V—below its minimum operating threshold of 11.5 V. What is the minimum wire gauge (in mm²) needed to ensure the pump receives at least 11.5 V under full load, assuming a resistivity of 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m for copper?

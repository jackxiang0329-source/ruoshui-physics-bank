# HFDPO-10529

A quantum sensing lab uses single electrons to detect minute electric field variations in a biological membrane simulating a neuron's synaptic cleft. The electrons are emitted from a nanoscale source with a known momentum uncertainty of ±0.8 × 10⁻²⁴ kg·m/s and travel 15 nm before being detected at a screen. If the detection system measures interference fringes with a spacing of 3.2 nm, but the fringe pattern disappears when a position-monitoring detector is placed near the path—despite no direct interaction—what must be the minimum uncertainty in the electron’s position at the emission point, and how does this relate to the wavefunction’s collapse in the presence of measurement?

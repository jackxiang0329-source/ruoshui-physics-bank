# HFDPO-03345

A deep-sea research submersible descends to a depth of 3,000 m in the Pacific Ocean. The crew notices a small leak in a viewport sealed by a thick, circular glass disk with a radius of 15 cm. The internal pressure is maintained at 1 atm, but the external seawater pressure is much higher. If the maximum pressure difference the seal can withstand without deforming is 4.5 × 10⁶ Pa, is the seal safe? If not, what minimum thickness of a new titanium reinforcement plate (density 4,500 kg/m³) must be added to reduce the net force on the seal to safe levels?

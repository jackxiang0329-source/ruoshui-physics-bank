# HFDPO-05135

A deep-space communications array on Mars receives a faint signal from Earth at 10.2 GHz, but the signal strength fluctuates wildly due to ionospheric scintillation caused by solar wind disturbances. The spacecraft’s antenna has a gain of 48 dBi, and the Earth transmitter uses a 50 kW beacon with 28 dBi gain. Given the average distance is 225 million km, and the signal experiences a 15% power loss due to interstellar plasma dispersion and a 1.8 dB atmospheric attenuation on Mars, what is the minimum detectable signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) at the receiver if the system noise temperature is 75 K and the bandwidth is 100 kHz? Evaluate whether the signal remains usable under these conditions.

# HFDPO-03110

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a charged microchip (0.5 mm × 0.5 mm, uniformly charged with ±50 pC) is suspended between two parallel electrode plates spaced 10 mm apart. The top plate is held at +100 V, the bottom at -100 V. When a dust particle (0.1 mm diameter, dielectric constant 3.2) drifts into the gap, it becomes polarized and disturbs the microchip’s equilibrium. What is the minimum distance from the bottom plate at which the electric field gradient would cause the microchip to experience a net force exceeding the gravitational force? Assume the chip’s charge is fixed and the dust particle's presence alters the local field via image charges.

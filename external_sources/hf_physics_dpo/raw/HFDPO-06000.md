# HFDPO-06000

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube carrying synthetic oil. On a clear summer day, the oil enters the tube at 320 K and exits at 450 K, flowing at 6.0 kg/s. The average solar irradiance on the collector is 950 W/m², and the effective collector area per trough is 120 m². The system loses 10% of incident energy to convection and radiation. What is the required number of identical troughs to meet the plant’s thermal power demand?

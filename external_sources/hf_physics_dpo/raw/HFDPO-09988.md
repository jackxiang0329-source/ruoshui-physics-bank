# HFDPO-09988

A seismic isolation system in a high-rise hospital in Tokyo uses tuned mass dampers (TMDs) to protect sensitive MRI equipment during earthquakes. Each TMD consists of a 15-ton mass suspended by nonlinear springs (stiffness k = 5.2 × 10⁶ N/m at equilibrium) and viscous dampers (damping coefficient c = 2.8 × 10⁵ N·s/m). During a moderate quake, ground motion is modeled as a sinusoidal displacement with amplitude 0.3 m and frequency 0.8 Hz. The MRI’s sensitive components are damaged if its relative displacement exceeds 2 mm. Determine whether the TMD effectively suppresses resonant amplification and calculate the steady-state relative displacement amplitude of the mass.

# HFDPO-08475

In a high-voltage electrostatic precipitator used in coal-fired power plants, a corona discharge forms at a sharp edge of a wire electrode (radius 0.5 mm) maintained at −50 kV relative to a grounded plate 10 cm away. Given the breakdown electric field of air is ~3 MV/m, determine the minimum distance between the wire and the plate required to prevent sparking, assuming the electric field at the wire's surface must not exceed 80% of the breakdown field. The wire is infinitely long and uniformly charged.

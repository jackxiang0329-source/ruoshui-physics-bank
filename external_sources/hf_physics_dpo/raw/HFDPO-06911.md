# HFDPO-06911

A Mars rover’s rear wheel, designed with a 60 cm diameter and 18 cm width, sinks slightly into the regolith during a 15° slope ascent. The soil exhibits a cohesive strength of 1.2 kPa and an internal friction angle of 32°, while the wheel’s rubber compound has a coefficient of static friction of 0.6 with the soil. As the rover’s motor applies torque, the wheel begins to slip. Given that rolling resistance is dominated by hysteresis loss and modeled by an effective rolling resistance coefficient of 0.04, what is the minimum torque required to prevent slip while maintaining a constant speed up the incline? Assume the wheel’s mass is 8.5 kg, and air drag is negligible.

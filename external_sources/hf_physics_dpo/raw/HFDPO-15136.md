# HFDPO-15136

During a deep-space mission, a satellite’s thermal regulator relies on phase-change material (PCM) stored in microcapsules to absorb heat during solar exposure. The PCM transitions from solid to liquid at 320 K, with a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg. The system must manage 50 W of continuous heat input over 2 hours, and the capsules are arranged in a disordered array with 10^6 particles per cm³. What fraction of the PCM must be in the liquid phase after this interval to remain effective, assuming no heat loss?

# HFDPO-05050

A fusion reactor's plasma containment system experiences a sudden magnetic field fluctuation, causing a localized collapse of the toroidal magnetic confinement. At the moment of collapse, a 2.5 kg plasma filament with initial velocity 1.2 × 10⁶ m/s along the toroidal axis fragments into two pieces. Use conservation of momentum and energy to determine the direction and speed of the second fragment, assuming no external forces and negligible magnetic energy loss during the event. What does the result imply about the stability of the plasma boundary?

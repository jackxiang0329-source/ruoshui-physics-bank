# HFDPO-03621

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits anomalous efficiency drops under prolonged blue-light exposure, despite stable ambient conditions. After 100 hours of 1 sun AM1.5G illumination (λ = 450 nm), the open-circuit voltage drops by 120 mV. Analyze whether this degradation is due to carrier trapping at surface states or photo-induced defect generation in the quantum dot interface.

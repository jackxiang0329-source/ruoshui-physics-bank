# HFDPO-11508

A city planner is designing a new subway station beneath a dense urban park, where residents report disturbing low-frequency rumbling (15–30 Hz) during train arrivals, even though the station is 12 meters below ground. The tunnel walls are concrete with a surface density of 2400 kg/m² and a Young’s modulus of 30 GPa. The station’s ceiling is a 40 cm thick reinforced concrete slab. Measurements show that ground vibrations at 20 Hz exceed 0.5 mm/s, yet airborne noise in the park is below 45 dB(A). What is the primary mechanism transferring vibration energy from the tunnel to the surface, and how much energy is transmitted through the slab if the incident wave amplitude is 1.2 mm/s at 20 Hz?

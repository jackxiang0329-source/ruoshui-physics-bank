# HFDPO-11735

A solar concentrator in a remote desert research station uses a parabolic mirror with a focal length of 2.5 m to focus sunlight onto a receiver. Due to dust accumulation, the mirror’s surface is uneven, causing the focal point to shift by 15 cm. The receiver, designed to be at the ideal focus, is now 15 cm off-target. If sunlight arrives at a 30° angle to the mirror’s axis, what is the diameter of the focused spot at the receiver’s current position? Assume the sun’s angular diameter is 0.53°.

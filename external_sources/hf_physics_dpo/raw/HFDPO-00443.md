# HFDPO-00443

A biomedical engineer is designing a micro-actuator for a new implantable drug delivery system. The device uses a tiny cantilever beam, 1.2 mm long and made of a piezoelectric material, that vibrates at 10 kHz to release medication in pulses. The beam’s effective mass is 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ kg, and its stiffness is 0.45 N/m. If the system is driven by a sinusoidal voltage, what amplitude of displacement will produce a peak acceleration of 3000 m/s² in the beam tip, and how does this compare to the natural frequency of the beam?

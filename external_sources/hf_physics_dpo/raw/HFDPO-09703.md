# HFDPO-09703

A quantum dot solar cell uses nanoscale semiconductor particles to absorb sunlight, but researchers notice that efficiency drops sharply when the dots shrink below 3 nm. Given that electron confinement energy scales as 1/L² (where L is the dot diameter), and the bandgap energy increases by 0.2 eV when L decreases from 4 nm to 3 nm, what is the minimum wavelength of light that can still excite an electron from the ground state? Assume the initial bandgap at 4 nm is 1.8 eV.

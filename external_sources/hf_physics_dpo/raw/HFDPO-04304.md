# HFDPO-04304

A lunar rover’s power system uses a hybrid battery array—6 identical lithium-ion cells in series, each with 3.7 V nominal voltage and 1500 mAh capacity—paired with a solar panel that delivers 18 V at 2.5 A under full sunlight. During a critical extravehicular activity, the rover’s sensors draw a variable current: 2.0 A during data transmission (15% of mission time), 0.5 A during idle (85%). The solar panel is partially shaded, providing only 60% of its rated current. If the batteries must maintain 12 V to avoid equipment failure, what is the minimum battery capacity needed to sustain operations for 4 hours without recharging, and how should the voltage regulation be configured?

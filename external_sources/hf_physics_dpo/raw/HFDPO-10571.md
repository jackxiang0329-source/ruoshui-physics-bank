# HFDPO-10571

A fusion reactor’s plasma confinement system uses a rapidly changing toroidal magnetic field to induce currents in the plasma, maintaining stability. During a 10 ms pulse, the magnetic flux through a 0.5 m² cross-sectional loop in the plasma increases from 0.2 Wb to 1.8 Wb. The plasma has a resistivity of 5 × 10⁻⁵ Ω·m and a loop resistance of 2.5 × 10⁻⁶ Ω. Calculate the induced current and determine whether the energy dissipated as heat during the pulse exceeds 1 kJ—justifying your conclusion with two different methods.

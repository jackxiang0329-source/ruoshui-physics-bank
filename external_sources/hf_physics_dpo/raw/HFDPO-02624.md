# HFDPO-02624

A solar farm in Arizona uses 120 identical photovoltaic panels, each with a 400 W peak output and internal resistance of 0.15 Ω. The panels are wired in 10 parallel strings of 12 panels in series. The farm needs to deliver 45 kW of power to the grid via 200 m of copper cable (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, diameter 4 mm). If the transmission voltage is 600 V, what is the total power lost in the cables and internal panel resistances combined?

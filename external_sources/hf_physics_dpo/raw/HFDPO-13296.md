# HFDPO-13296

A research team is designing a cryogenic satellite sensor housing that must maintain a stable internal temperature of 25 K in deep space, where the external environment is 3 K. The housing has a surface area of 1.2 m² and an emissivity of 0.05. A small 10 W heater is used to counteract heat loss, but it’s placed off-center and creates a localized temperature gradient. The housing is made of a composite material with anisotropic thermal conductivity: 15 W/m·K along the primary axis and 0.5 W/m·K perpendicular to it. The sensor, located 0.3 m from the heater, must not exceed 30 K. Evaluate whether the current design ensures safe operation and determine if the heater’s position or power must be adjusted.

# HFDPO-00831

A marine biologist is studying how underwater volcanic tremors affect local dolphin communication. During an eruption, seismic waves travel through the ocean floor at 5.2 km/s and generate surface waves on the water at 1.5 km/s. Dolphins near the site rely on 20 kHz clicks for echolocation, but the waves interfere destructively when the path difference between the seismic and surface wave arrivals is half a wavelength. If a dolphin is 8 km from the eruption epicenter, at what depth must the seafloor be to create destructive interference for the dolphin’s echolocation signal?

# HFDPO-13992

A fusion reactor's plasma confinement system uses a toroidal magnetic field generated by 12 identical superconducting coils arranged symmetrically around a central chamber. Each coil carries a current of 2.5 kA and has a mean radius of 1.8 m. The reactor operates at 15 T field strength at the plasma edge, but during a sudden quench, one coil fails and the field becomes asymmetric. Calculate the net magnetic force on the surviving coils, assuming they remain intact and the current is constant. How might this asymmetry affect plasma stability?

# HFDPO-01323

In a lithium-ion battery manufacturing plant, a conveyor belt transports charged cathode particles (each 15 μm in diameter, mass 2.3 × 10⁻¹² kg, and carrying −4.8 × 10⁻¹⁶ C) through an electrostatic separator. The belt moves at 0.8 m/s, and the particles enter a region with a uniform horizontal electric field of 3.2 × 10⁵ V/m directed to the left. Due to friction and tribocharging, some particles become charged, but their charge varies randomly. To prevent clumping and ensure uniform deposition, the system must deflect particles by exactly 1.5 mm over a 10 cm travel distance within the field. What is the required range of particle charges to achieve this deflection?

# HFDPO-06656

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a 2D periodic lattice of semiconductor nanocrystals with a spacing of 180 nm to enhance light absorption via constructive interference. When sunlight (λ = 550 nm, normal incidence) hits the cell, a distinct diffraction peak appears at 32° from the normal. However, under operational conditions, the lattice spacing expands by 3.5% due to thermal stress. Calculate the new angular position of the first-order constructive interference peak and explain why this shift could degrade energy conversion efficiency.

# HFDPO-01452

A satellite in a 500 km low-Earth orbit is experiencing unexpected drag due to atmospheric residual air, causing its altitude to decay by 2 km per month. If the satellite's mass is 850 kg and it uses thrusters only once per orbit to maintain altitude, what average thrust force (in newtons) must be applied per orbit to counteract the gravitational work lost to drag? Assume a circular orbit and Earth’s radius as 6371 km with mass 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg.

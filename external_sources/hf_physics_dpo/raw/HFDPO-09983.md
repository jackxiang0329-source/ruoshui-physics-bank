# HFDPO-09983

A Mars rover’s drill bit, designed to penetrate icy regolith, spins at 2,000 rpm while being lowered into a crevice at a controlled linear rate. The bit has a moment of inertia of 0.045 kg·m² and experiences a constant torque from friction with the ice of 0.32 N·m. If the drill is lowered at 0.15 m/s and the drill bit is not slipping on the surface, what is the angular acceleration of the bit, and how does this affect the net torque required to maintain the descent rate?

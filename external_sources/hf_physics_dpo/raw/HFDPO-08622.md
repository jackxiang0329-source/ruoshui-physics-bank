# HFDPO-08622

A marine biologist is deploying an underwater sensor array to monitor coral reef health using wireless power transfer via inductive coupling. The system uses a 15 kHz AC source driving a primary coil (10 mH, 2 Ω resistance) submerged near the surface. A secondary coil (5 mH, 1.5 Ω resistance) is attached to a battery-less sensor on the reef floor, 8 meters below. Due to seawater’s conductivity, the system experiences parasitic capacitance of 40 pF per meter of cable and mutual inductance drops to 60% of ideal. The sensor requires 1.2 W at 1.8 V RMS to operate. Is the system efficient enough? What value of series compensation capacitance (to the secondary) maximizes power delivery, and what is the resulting efficiency?

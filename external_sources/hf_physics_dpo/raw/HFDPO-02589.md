# HFDPO-02589

A research team is developing a wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (radius 5 cm) is powered by a 1.5 MHz AC source with 10 A peak current. The receiver coil (radius 2 cm), placed 8 cm away along the axis, must generate a minimum voltage of 3.2 V peak to activate the device circuitry. The coils are made of copper wire with resistivity 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, 10 turns, and 0.5 mm diameter. The system operates in air with μ₀ = 4π × 10⁻⁷ H/m. What is the minimum number of turns the receiver coil must have to meet the voltage requirement, assuming perfect alignment and known mutual inductance?

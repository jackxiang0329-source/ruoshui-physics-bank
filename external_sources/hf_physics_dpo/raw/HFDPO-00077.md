# HFDPO-00077

A laser-based underwater communication system uses a 532 nm wavelength beam to transmit data through a turbulent ocean layer where refractive index fluctuations follow a Kolmogorov spectrum. To maintain signal integrity, the receiver must capture at least 70% of the beam’s initial power within a 2 cm detection aperture. Determine the minimum beam divergence angle (in μrad) required at the source to ensure this condition, considering both diffraction and wavefront distortion due to turbulence.

# HFDPO-01992

A fusion reactor test site uses a 1.5-meter-long niobium-tin (Nb₃Sn) superconducting solenoid to generate a 12 T magnetic field for plasma confinement. During a cooldown from 300 K to 77 K, the system experiences a sudden quench in one segment due to a minor insulation fault. The local current density exceeds the critical value, causing a transient voltage spike. If the stored magnetic energy in the affected 10 cm section is 450 J and the quench propagates at 1.2 cm/s, what is the maximum power dissipated in the quench front? Assume the critical current density drops abruptly at 9 K.

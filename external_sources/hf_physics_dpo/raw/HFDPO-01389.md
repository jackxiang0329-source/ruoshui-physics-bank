# HFDPO-01389

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpectedly low efficiency despite optimal bandgap tuning. Measurements show electron-hole pairs are recombining too quickly, but simulations suggest they should remain separated long enough for collection. Engineers suspect wavefunction overlap due to quantum confinement is causing unintended tunneling through the quantum well barrier. If the barrier height is 1.2 eV, width 2.5 nm, and the electron’s effective mass is 0.067mₑ, calculate the tunneling probability for an electron attempting to escape the dot. Then, critically evaluate whether this explains the observed recombination rate—considering both wave-like tunneling and particle-like localization—and propose a structural modification to suppress unwanted tunneling without compromising light absorption.

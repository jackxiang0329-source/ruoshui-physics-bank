# HFDPO-11393

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt at 565°C to store heat for electricity generation after sunset. The storage tank has a 10,000 m³ volume and is insulated with a 0.3 m thick layer of aerogel (k = 0.013 W/m·K). The ambient temperature drops to 15°C at night. Assume the salt’s density is 2100 kg/m³ and specific heat is 1400 J/kg·K.

# HFDPO-00647

During a heatwave, a city’s emergency cooling center uses a large insulated tank filled with 2000 kg of water at 15°C to cool incoming patients. A patient with a fever (39°C) is placed in a cooling wrap that transfers heat to the water. If the patient’s body has a mass of 70 kg and a specific heat capacity of 3.5 kJ/kg·K, and the system reaches equilibrium at 22°C, how much heat was transferred to the water? Assume no heat loss to the environment.

# HFDPO-05780

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a triangular support frame made of high-strength steel trusses to stabilize a 120-ton drill mast during extraction. The frame is anchored to bedrock via three pin joints at different elevations, with one anchor located 8 m below the surface and two at 4 m depth. During a seismic event, the ground shifts laterally by 0.3 m at the lower anchor point. Calculate the minimum tensile strength (in MPa) required for the diagonal tension members to prevent structural failure, assuming the mast's center of mass shifts 0.2 m horizontally and the frame’s rigidity is compromised by the displacement.

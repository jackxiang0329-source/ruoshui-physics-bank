# HFDPO-08742

A fusion reactor prototype uses a ring of high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) to confine a plasma at 150 million K. The tapes, wound in a 2.3 m diameter toroid, must carry 120 kA without quenching. At 77 K, the critical current density is 1.2 × 10⁶ A/cm², but thermal fluctuations cause localized weak spots. If the tape’s effective cross-section is 0.3 mm × 10 mm and one 5 cm segment suffers a 40% drop in Jc due to microcracks, what is the minimum operating margin (in %) needed to prevent quenching when the entire current is routed through the weakened region?

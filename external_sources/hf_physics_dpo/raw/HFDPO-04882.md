# HFDPO-04882

However, if the temperature rises to 10 K, the fringes vanish. Explain why the interference disappears using wave-particle duality principles. What physical mechanism causes the loss of coherence, and how does this relate to the electron’s de Broglie wavelength and its interaction with lattice vibrations?

# HFDPO-13676

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain region uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a 150 W solar panel array. The station’s sensors, data logger, and radio transmitter draw an average current of 0.8 A during daylight and 0.2 A at night. If the battery capacity is 80 Ah and it’s currently at 50% charge, how many full days of operation (including night) can the system sustain during a prolonged cloud cover period when the solar panels are inactive? Assume the battery is ideal and the loads are purely resistive.

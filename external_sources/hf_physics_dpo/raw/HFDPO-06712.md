# HFDPO-06712

A wind turbine in a coastal farm must be designed to operate efficiently during storm conditions. The blades, each 45 meters long and with a mass of 12,000 kg, rotate at 15 rpm when the wind speed is 20 m/s. If the turbine’s control system cuts out at 25 m/s to prevent damage, what is the maximum kinetic energy the blades can store before the system automatically shuts down? Assume the blades are uniform rods rotating about one end.

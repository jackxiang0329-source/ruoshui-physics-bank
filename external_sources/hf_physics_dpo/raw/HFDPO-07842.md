# HFDPO-07842

A deep-sea autonomous vehicle descends through the ocean at 1.2 m/s, encountering a persistent, swirling current that increases drag nonlinearly with velocity. The vehicle’s streamlined hull experiences a drag force modeled as F_d = 0.5 rho C_d A v^2 + k v^3 , where rho = 1025,text{kg/m}^3 , C_d = 0.08 , A = 0.75,text{m}^2 , and k = 0.002,text{kg/m} . The vehicle’s thrusters can deliver up to 800 N of thrust, but power efficiency drops sharply when operating near maximum thrust. Determine whether the vehicle can stabilize at its target descent speed, and if not, what minimum thruster thrust is required to maintain equilibrium — considering the nonlinear drag and the energy cost of sustained operation.

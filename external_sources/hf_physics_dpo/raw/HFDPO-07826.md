# HFDPO-07826

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must navigate a narrow, winding canyon with sudden crosswinds gusting up to 12 m/s. The drone has a mass of 3.2 kg and maintains a constant horizontal speed of 8 m/s while hovering 25 m above the canyon floor. At one point, a wind gust pushes the drone sideways with a force of 5.6 N for 0.4 seconds. Determine the drone’s lateral displacement from its intended path when it exits the canyon if the canyon is 120 m long and the drone’s control system applies a corrective thrust that counteracts the wind’s impulse after the gust ends.

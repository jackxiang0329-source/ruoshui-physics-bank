# HFDPO-02658

A wind turbine in a remote coastal village generates 1.2 MW of electrical power during peak wind, but the local grid can only absorb 800 kW. The excess energy is stored in a flywheel system with a moment of inertia of 350 kg·m². If the flywheel spins at 2400 rpm when fully charged, how long can it supply the grid with 100 kW while maintaining a minimum rotational speed of 1800 rpm?

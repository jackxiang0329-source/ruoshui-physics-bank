# HFDPO-09476

A marine biologist studying whale feeding behavior observes that humpback whales use a technique called "bubble net feeding," where they swim in a tight circle while releasing bubbles from their blowholes, creating a rising cylindrical curtain of bubbles. This encircles schools of small fish, compressing them into a tight ball. If the bubble curtain has an average diameter of 10 m and rises vertically at 2 m/s, with the water pressure at the top of the curtain reduced by 15 kPa due to the Bernoulli effect from the fast-moving upward flow, what is the average flow speed of the water inside the bubble column? Assume the water is incompressible, and the ambient pressure at the surface is 101.3 kPa.

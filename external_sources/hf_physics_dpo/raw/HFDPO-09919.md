# HFDPO-09919

In a high-altitude satellite communication system, a silicon photodiode detector operating at 850 nm receives weak optical signals amid intense cosmic ray bombardment. The device exhibits an unexpected rise in dark current—increasing from 0.5 nA at 25°C to 12 nA at -40°C—despite being in a cold vacuum. The satellite’s power budget is tight, and noise from dark current limits signal detection. Determine whether the dark current increase is due to trap-assisted tunneling (Shockley-Read-Hall) or generation-recombination in the depletion region, and justify your choice using carrier lifetime and defect density estimates from the diode’s fabrication process.

# HFDPO-02306

A Mars rover's solar array, designed to track the Sun, relies on a precision motor to reorient its panels daily. During a dust storm, the motor malfunctions and must be manually reset using a handheld winch. The 12 kg panel, rotating on frictionless bearings, is initially at rest at 90° from the optimal orientation. To reposition it, an engineer applies a constant 80 N tangential force at the panel’s edge (1.4 m from the axis) for 0.8 s. Afterward, the panel rotates freely, but friction in the bearing dissipates 15 J of energy per full rotation. What is the minimum number of full rotations the panel must perform before it reaches stable equilibrium (within ±2° of optimal)?

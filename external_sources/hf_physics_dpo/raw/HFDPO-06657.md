# HFDPO-06657

A small village relies on a portable radioisotope heater unit (RHU) for winter warmth, using 20 grams of plutonium-238. The device must deliver at least 5 watts of thermal power continuously for 10 years. Given Pu-238’s half-life of 87.7 years and decay energy of 5.593 MeV per atom, will the heater meet the energy demand over its operational lifetime?

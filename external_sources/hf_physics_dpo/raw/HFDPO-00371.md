# HFDPO-00371

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a photonic crystal back reflector with a periodic nanoscale grating (period 450 nm) to trap light in a 200-nm-thick active layer. To maximize absorption, engineers need to tune the grating’s refractive index contrast and angle of incidence such that constructive interference occurs for wavelengths near 700 nm. Given that the incident light is unpolarized and strikes the grating at 30° from the normal, determine the required effective refractive index difference between the grating material and the surrounding dielectric to achieve first-order diffraction peak at the target wavelength.

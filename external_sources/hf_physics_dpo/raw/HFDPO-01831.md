# HFDPO-01831

A solar farm in Arizona uses a hybrid grid-tied system with two parallel PV arrays connected through a shared inverter, but one array (Array A) is partially shaded by a nearby mesa during afternoon hours. Array A produces 3.2 kW at 48 V, while Array B produces 4.5 kW at 48 V. The system’s charge controller uses a diode-based isolation circuit to prevent reverse current, but a technician notices uneven battery charging. Determine the current through the shared DC bus and the power dissipated in the isolation diode if its forward voltage drop is 0.7 V.

# HFDPO-06028

A deep-space satellite network designed to monitor gravitational wave events near a supermassive black hole at the center of a distant galaxy relies on ultra-precise timing. However, during routine calibration, engineers notice that signals from one satellite are consistently delayed by 3.2 microseconds per day compared to others in a symmetric orbital ring—despite identical propulsion and navigation protocols. The system uses GPS-like atomic clocks synchronized at launch, but relativistic effects from the black hole’s gravity well and the satellites’ orbital velocity are suspected.

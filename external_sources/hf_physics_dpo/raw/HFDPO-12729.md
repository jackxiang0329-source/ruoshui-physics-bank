# HFDPO-12729

In a satellite orbiting Mars, a solar cell array using perovskite-silicon tandem junctions experiences unexpected efficiency decay during prolonged exposure to high-energy electron radiation from solar flares. Measurements show a 12% drop in open-circuit voltage after 100 hours of irradiation at 10^12 e/cm². Given that the dominant degradation mechanism involves deep-level trap formation in the silicon bulk due to radiation-induced lattice damage (trap density ≈ 1.5 × 10^14 cm⁻³), determine the minimum minority carrier lifetime required in the silicon base to maintain a photovoltaic efficiency above 25%, assuming the original junction is otherwise ideal and the perovskite top cell contributes 1.25 eV of bandgap energy.

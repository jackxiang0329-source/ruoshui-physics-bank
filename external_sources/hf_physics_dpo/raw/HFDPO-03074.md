# HFDPO-03074

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma at 15 million K, but a sudden disruption in the poloidal field winding causes a current spike in the plasma. The resulting Lorentz force compresses the plasma column, risking wall contact. Given the plasma current is 12 MA, the toroidal field is 8 T, and the minor radius is 1.2 m, calculate the net magnetic pressure acting radially inward on the plasma boundary—then determine whether a 20 T peak field from a new coil would be sufficient to restore equilibrium, accounting for non-axisymmetric field perturbations from misaligned coils.

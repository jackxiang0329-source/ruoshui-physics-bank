# HFDPO-15079

A neuroengineer is designing a deep-brain stimulation (DBS) implant to treat Parkinson’s disease. The device delivers 1.5 mA of current through a thin platinum electrode (resistivity ρ = 1.0 × 10⁻⁷ Ω·m) with a length of 25 mm and a radius of 0.1 mm. The surrounding brain tissue has a resistivity of 1.5 Ω·m over a spherical region of radius 10 mm centered on the electrode tip. To prevent tissue damage, the power dissipated in the tissue must not exceed 1.2 μW. Calculate the minimum required voltage drop across the electrode to ensure safe stimulation, considering both electrode resistance and tissue resistance in series.

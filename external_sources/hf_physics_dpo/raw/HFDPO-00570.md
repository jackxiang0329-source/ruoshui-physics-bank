# HFDPO-00570

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, ancient shipwreck stabilized by a network of sealed, hollow bronze spheres embedded in the seabed. Researchers suspect the spheres are partially collapsed due to immense hydrostatic pressure, but the exact depth of the wreck is unknown. The ROV measures the current internal pressure of one exposed sphere at 1.2 MPa, and the external seawater density is 1025 kg/m³. Assuming atmospheric pressure is negligible at this depth, calculate the depth of the sphere below the surface, and evaluate whether the structural collapse is consistent with the observed pressure if the sphere's yield strength is 150 MPa and its wall thickness is 1.5 cm.

# HFDPO-02685

A quantum imaging team at a research lab is developing a ghost imaging system to detect faint thermal emissions from a distant exoplanet’s atmosphere, using entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC). The signal photon passes through a narrowband filter (Δλ = 0.5 nm, λ₀ = 1550 nm) and is detected by a single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) after traversing a 100 km free-space path with atmospheric turbulence modeled by a Kolmogorov phase screen. The idler photon remains in the lab and is measured with a bucket detector. To suppress background noise from solar radiation, the team must optimize the spectral filtering and temporal correlation window. What spectral width (in nm) should be used to maximize the signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) if the SPAD’s dark count rate is 100 kHz, the source’s spectral coherence length is 1.2 mm, and the system’s temporal resolution is 10 ps?

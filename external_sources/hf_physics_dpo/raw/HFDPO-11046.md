# HFDPO-11046

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to focus sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 400°C. The oil flows at 2.5 kg/s through a 50 m long tube with a 0.1 m diameter. On a clear day, the solar irradiance is 950 W/m², and the receiver is 90% efficient at converting radiation to thermal energy. If the oil enters at 250°C and the system is losing 12 kW to the environment due to convection and radiation, what must be the specific heat capacity of the oil to maintain steady-state operation?

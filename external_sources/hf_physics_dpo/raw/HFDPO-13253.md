# HFDPO-13253

A sound engineer is tuning a new concert hall with a 12-meter-long rectangular balcony that runs parallel to the stage. During testing, a 440 Hz tuning fork is sounded near the stage, and at a certain point 3.2 meters from the front edge of the balcony, the sound intensity drops significantly—almost to silence. The engineer suspects standing wave interference between the direct sound and the sound reflected off the balcony’s edge. What is the angle between the line from the stage to the balcony edge and the line from the balcony edge to the listener’s position, assuming sound reflects off the balcony’s front edge and travels back to the listener? (Assume sound speed is 343 m/s.)

# HFDPO-10072

A planetary rover on Mars encounters a sand dune with a 25° incline and a 3.2 m radius of curvature at its crest. To avoid tipping, the rover’s center of mass must remain within the base footprint during ascent. The front and rear wheels (each 0.6 m in diameter) are independently driven, but torque is limited to 180 N·m per wheel. If the rover’s mass is 220 kg, with 60% of the weight on the drive wheels, and it begins rolling without slipping at 1.4 m/s, what is the maximum deceleration (in m/s²) it can sustain while climbing without losing traction or toppling? Assume the center of mass is 0.8 m above the ground and 0.4 m behind the midpoint of the wheelbase.

# HFDPO-09324

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable flight during a 12-hour mission. It uses two identical lithium-ion batteries (each 3.7 V, 5000 mAh) to power its propulsion and sensors. During the mission, it gains 150 kJ of gravitational potential energy while ascending, then loses 90 kJ during descent. If the drone’s systems consume 120 W of power continuously, and 5% of the battery’s energy is lost to heat in the electronics, what is the minimum energy the solar panels must supply during the flight to ensure no battery depletion?

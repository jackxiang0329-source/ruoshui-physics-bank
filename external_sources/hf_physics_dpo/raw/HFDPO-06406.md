# HFDPO-06406

During a winter storm, a power line in the mountains begins to accumulate ice at a rate of 1.5 mm per hour due to supercooled rain droplets freezing on contact. If the line has a diameter of 2.8 cm and the ice forms a uniform layer that increases the total radius at that rate, what is the minimum power output (in kW) required to keep the line above freezing, assuming the ice layer must be melted at the same rate it accumulates? Use latent heat of fusion for ice (334 kJ/kg), density of ice (917 kg/m³), and neglect heat loss to the environment except through the ice buildup.

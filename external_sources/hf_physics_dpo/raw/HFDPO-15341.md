# HFDPO-15341

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote farm uses a 12 V battery charged by photovoltaic panels. The system runs a 500 W pump through a 100 m copper wire run with a cross-sectional area of 4 mm². Due to heat buildup, the wire temperature rises by 15°C above ambient. What is the actual current delivered to the pump, and what percentage of input power is lost as heat in the wires? Assume resistivity of copper is 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m at 20°C and temperature coefficient of resistance is 0.00393/°C.

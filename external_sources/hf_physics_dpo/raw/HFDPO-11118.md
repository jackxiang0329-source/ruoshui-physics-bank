# HFDPO-11118

A solar farm in Arizona uses adjustable solar panels mounted on a single pivot arm to track the sun throughout the day. Each panel assembly weighs 45 kg and is 2.4 m long, with its center of mass located 0.8 m from the pivot. The arm is supported by a horizontal cable attached 1.6 m above the pivot. Due to wind gusts, the system must remain stable even when the cable tension is reduced by 20%. What is the minimum coefficient of static friction required between the base of the pivot and the concrete footing to prevent slipping when the panel is tilted to 30° above horizontal?

# HFDPO-04737

A biomedical engineer is designing a micro-scale implantable device that uses a piezoelectric cantilever to harvest energy from human arterial pulsations. The cantilever, 2 mm long and with a mass of 15 µg at its tip, vibrates at a natural frequency of 120 Hz due to blood pressure fluctuations. However, the device must operate efficiently only within a 10 Hz bandwidth around resonance. What damping coefficient (in kg/s) must the system have to ensure the amplitude drops to 1/e of its peak value at ±5 Hz from resonance? Assume a spring constant of 0.8 N/m.

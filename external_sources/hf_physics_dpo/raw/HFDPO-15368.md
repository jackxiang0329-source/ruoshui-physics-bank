# HFDPO-15368

A biomedical engineer is designing a resonant inductive coupling system to wirelessly power an implantable cardiac pacemaker. The primary coil (20 cm diameter) is placed on the patient’s chest, and the secondary coil (5 cm diameter) is embedded in the pacemaker, located 15 cm below the skin. At 13.56 MHz, the system must deliver 5 W of power to the implant with 70% efficiency. The skin and tissue introduce a complex impedance with resistive and capacitive components. What must the mutual inductance be to achieve resonance, and how should the capacitance in the secondary circuit be tuned to maximize power transfer while minimizing tissue heating?

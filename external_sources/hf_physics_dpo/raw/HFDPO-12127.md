# HFDPO-12127

A quantum computing lab uses superconducting qubits that operate at 5 GHz, coupled to a high-Q microwave cavity resonating at 5.001 GHz. When a 10 μW microwave pulse is applied, the cavity’s phase response shifts unexpectedly. Find the effective nonlinear inductance at the drive amplitude that causes a 3° phase shift in the output signal.

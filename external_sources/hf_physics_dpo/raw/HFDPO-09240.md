# HFDPO-09240

In a cutting-edge solar thermal power plant, a parabolic concentrator with a 12-meter diameter and 3-meter focal length must focus sunlight onto a receiver located at its focal point. However, during a rare atmospheric inversion layer, temperature gradients cause a refractive index gradient that bends light rays by up to 0.8° across the aperture. If the receiver is misaligned by just 1.5 cm from the geometric focal point, how much of the total incident solar flux is lost due to both optical aberration and misalignment? Assume normal incidence and uniform solar irradiance of 1000 W/m².

# HFDPO-05381

In a cryogenic quantum memory array, 10^6 identical two-level atoms are trapped in a 3D optical lattice at 100 nK. Due to residual thermal excitations, some atoms occupy the excited state, causing decoherence. The energy gap between ground and excited states is 1.5 meV. Calculate the fraction of atoms in the excited state and analyze how this fraction changes if the lattice depth is reduced by 20%, altering the energy gap to 1.2 meV. What does this imply for long-term data retention?

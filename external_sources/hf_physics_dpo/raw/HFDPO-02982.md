# HFDPO-02982

A wind turbine in a remote mountain village generates electricity using a large rotating coil in a magnetic field, but the output voltage fluctuates wildly during storms when wind speeds vary. The turbine’s generator has 500 turns, a radius of 1.2 m, and operates in a uniform 0.4 T magnetic field. When the wind gusts to 25 m/s, the coil spins at 60 rpm. What is the maximum induced emf, and how could you redesign the system to maintain stable voltage despite changing rotation speed?

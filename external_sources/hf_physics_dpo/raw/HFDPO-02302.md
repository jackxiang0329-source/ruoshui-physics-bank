# HFDPO-02302

A quantum dot solar cell uses a nanoscale array of 100 nm diameter spherical dots arranged in a triangular lattice with 200 nm spacing to enhance light absorption. A researcher observes unexpected intensity oscillations in the reflected spectrum at visible wavelengths (450–650 nm), suggesting collective interference between scattered waves. Given that the incident light is normal to the surface and has a wavelength of 530 nm, determine the dominant order of diffraction that produces constructive interference at the first observable peak in reflection. Assume the dielectric constant of the surrounding medium is 2.25.

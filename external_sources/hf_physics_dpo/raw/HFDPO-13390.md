# HFDPO-13390

A lunar mining drone descends vertically onto the Moon’s surface using a retrorocket, adjusting thrust to maintain a constant deceleration of 0.8 m/s² despite the Moon’s gravity (1.6 m/s²). The drone starts at 100 m above the surface with an initial downward velocity of 12 m/s. As it nears the ground, a 50 kg boulder suddenly detaches from the surface and slides up a 15° incline at 2 m/s, experiencing kinetic friction (μ_k = 0.4). At what time relative to the drone’s touchdown does the boulder reach its highest point, and could a minor vertical oscillation in the drone’s descent (±0.5 m) during final approach cause a collision if the boulder’s motion is not accounted for?

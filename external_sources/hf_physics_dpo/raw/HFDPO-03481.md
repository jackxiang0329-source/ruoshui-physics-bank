# HFDPO-03481

A hydroelectric dam in the Andes regulates water flow through a turbine system that must maintain consistent energy output despite variable rainfall. If the water intake increases to 120,000 kg/s while maintaining the same exit speed, what must the new inlet speed be to preserve the same power output?

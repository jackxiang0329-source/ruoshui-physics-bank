# HFDPO-13887

During a heatwave, a solar-powered desalination unit in a remote coastal village begins producing less freshwater than expected. The system uses a 2 m² solar collector to heat seawater in a blackened copper tank, with a latent heat of vaporization of 2.26 × 10⁶ J/kg and an efficiency of 35% in converting solar energy to thermal energy. If the sun delivers 800 W/m² and the tank loses 120 W to convection and radiation, how much water can be evaporated per hour? What happens to the output if the tank’s insulation degrades and losses double?

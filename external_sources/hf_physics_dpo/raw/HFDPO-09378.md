# HFDPO-09378

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries, aiming to balance radial strength with minimal stress on arterial walls. The stent is made of a nickel-titanium alloy (NiTi) with a Young’s modulus of 70 GPa and a yield stress of 450 MPa. When deployed, the stent must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter to 4.0 mm to fit the artery. If the stent wall is 0.1 mm thick and the expansion is uniform, what is the maximum radial stress experienced in the material during deployment? Assume elastic deformation and use the thin-walled pressure vessel model.

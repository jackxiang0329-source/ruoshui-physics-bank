# HFDPO-11368

A newly discovered near-Earth asteroid, 2024 Δ-7, is on a highly elliptical orbit with a perihelion of 0.85 AU and an aphelion of 2.4 AU. Observations show its orbit precesses due to general relativistic effects and solar radiation pressure. At a critical point near aphelion, a 15 kg micro-satellite is deployed from a mothership to perform close-range mapping. The deployment must place the satellite on a stable, repeating orbit that avoids collisions with the asteroid’s path over the next 7 years. Determine the required Δv and burn direction at deployment to achieve a 3:1 resonant orbit (3 revolutions for every 1 revolution of the asteroid) while minimizing fuel use.

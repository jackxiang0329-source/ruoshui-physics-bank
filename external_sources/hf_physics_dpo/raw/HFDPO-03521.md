# HFDPO-03521

A wind turbine in a remote mountain village must generate enough energy daily to power a small clinic, but the blades are fouled with ice during winter storms. The turbine’s rotor has a moment of inertia of 12,000 kg·m² and spins at 45 rpm when unloaded. After the storm, ice adds an extra 800 kg distributed uniformly at a radius of 1.8 m. If the turbine’s generator can only restart when the rotor regains 90% of its original kinetic energy, what minimum work must the motor perform to restart it?

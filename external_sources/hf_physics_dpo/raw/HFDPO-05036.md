# HFDPO-05036

A quantum dot solar cell uses a 2D photonic crystal lattice with periodic dielectric pillars (refractive index 2.5) arranged in a triangular lattice (lattice constant 420 nm) to enhance light absorption in a 50 nm thick indium gallium arsenide layer. At 630 nm incident light, the system exhibits a sharp absorption peak due to guided mode resonance. Given that the first-order diffraction condition aligns with a Bragg angle of 22° in air (n=1), determine whether the observed resonance corresponds to a symmetric (TE) or antisymmetric (TM) mode, and explain how the effective refractive index of the guided mode compares to that in the surrounding dielectric.

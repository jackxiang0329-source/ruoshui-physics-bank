# HFDPO-12343

A research team is designing a compact, high-voltage capacitor array for a deep-space probe’s power system. The array uses nested cylindrical conductors with inner radius 2.0 cm and outer radius 6.0 cm, each 15 cm long. When charged, the inner conductor holds a uniform surface charge density of +3.0 μC/m². To ensure no electric field leaks beyond the outer conductor (for radiation shielding), they must verify the total charge on the outer conductor. What charge must be present on the outer conductor’s inner surface to guarantee zero electric field outside the system?

# HFDPO-06515

Given that the major radius is 8 m, minor radius 2 m, and the total current in the central solenoid is 20 MA, what is the minimum number of turns per meter in the poloidal coils needed to achieve the required field gradient, assuming the field is confined to the plasma edge region?

# HFDPO-13309

A remote mountain research station uses a geothermal heat exchanger buried 30 meters underground, where the rock temperature is a steady 12°C. The station needs to deliver 15 kW of heating during winter, when the surface temperature drops to -10°C. The exchanger is a 50-m long copper pipe (diameter 5 cm) with emissivity 0.8, buried in a regolith layer with thermal conductivity 1.8 W/m·K. Given that heat transfer from the pipe to the surrounding rock occurs via conduction and radiation, and the pipe wall is maintained at 80°C by a heat-carrying fluid, what is the required heat transfer rate per unit length (W/m) to sustain the system’s thermal load?

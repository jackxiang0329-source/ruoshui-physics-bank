# HFDPO-05008

During a lunar mining operation, a robotic drill extracts regolith from a 500 kg asteroid fragment orbiting in a low lunar halo orbit. After drilling, the robot ejects 15 kg of fragmented material at 80 m/s relative to the asteroid, perpendicular to its direction of motion. If the asteroid initially orbited at 1,200 m/s and the system was initially at rest relative to the lunar frame, what is the new magnitude and direction of the asteroid’s velocity after ejection? Assume no external forces act during ejection.

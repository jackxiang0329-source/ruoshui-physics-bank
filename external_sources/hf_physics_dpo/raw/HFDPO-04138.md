# HFDPO-04138

A high-altitude research balloon carrying sensitive climate sensors descends rapidly through the stratosphere, where air density drops to 0.02 kg/m³ and temperature plummets to −60°C. The balloon’s envelope is made of a thin, elastic polymer that can withstand internal pressure differences up to 150 Pa. If the internal gas (mostly nitrogen) is initially at 100 kPa and 25°C before ascent, what is the minimum volume the envelope must have at altitude to avoid rupture due to internal pressure? Assume the gas remains ideal and the descent is adiabatic.

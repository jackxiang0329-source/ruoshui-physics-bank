# HFDPO-03391

A neutrino detector at the IceCube Observatory in Antarctica records a burst of high-energy neutrinos from a distant blazar, 1.2 billion light-years away. The detected neutrinos have a total energy of 1.5 PeV, but due to time dilation, their observed lifetime on Earth is 1.8 seconds—far longer than their rest lifetime of 2.6 × 10⁻⁶ seconds. Calculate the Lorentz factor γ of the neutrinos, and determine the actual time elapsed in their rest frame during the journey. Explain how the energy of the neutrinos would be perceived differently by an observer moving at 0.9c relative to Earth in the direction of the neutrino flight.

# HFDPO-05270

A laser-based quantum communication satellite uses entangled photon pairs transmitted through Earth’s atmosphere, but signal degradation occurs when the beam passes through turbulent air layers. The laser emits a 1550 nm wavelength beam with a 1.2 mm diameter, and atmospheric turbulence causes random phase fronts with a coherence length of just 3.4 cm. Design a wavefront correction system using a spatial light modulator (SLM) to re-establish coherence across a 5 km path through a 1 km thick boundary layer with a Kolmogorov turbulence spectrum. Determine the minimum number of independent phase correction zones needed on the SLM to maintain entanglement fidelity above 90%.

# HFDPO-09207

A laser show designer is programming a holographic display using a 633 nm helium-neon laser. To project a stable interference pattern onto a curved screen 10 meters away, two identical slits are placed 0.2 mm apart. However, when the system is powered on, the fringe spacing fluctuates erratically. After inspection, the designer realizes the beam passes through a thin, uneven layer of heated air above a stage. The air has a refractive index that varies linearly from 1.0003 at the bottom to 1.0007 at the top across a 15 cm height. What is the effective path difference introduced by the heated air layer, and how does it alter the interference pattern compared to ideal conditions?

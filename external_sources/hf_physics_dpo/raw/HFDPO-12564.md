# HFDPO-12564

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar panel array and battery bank to power sensors and a transmitter. If the station must operate for 72 hours on stored energy, what is the minimum initial battery capacity (in Ah) needed, accounting for both the converter inefficiency and increasing resistance?

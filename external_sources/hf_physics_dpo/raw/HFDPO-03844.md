# HFDPO-03844

A wind farm in a coastal region experiences inconsistent energy output despite consistent wind speeds. Engineers discover that turbulent airflow from nearby cliffs alters pressure distribution over turbine blades, reducing lift and causing oscillations. The turbines are mounted on 80 m tall towers, and measurements show air velocity increases from 12 m/s at the base to 20 m/s at blade height due to flow acceleration over the cliff edge. Using Bernoulli’s principle, determine whether the pressure drop across the blade spans exceeds the structural stress threshold of 1.2 kPa, and evaluate whether the design should be modified to prevent fatigue failure. Assume air density is 1.225 kg/m³ and flow is steady, incompressible, and along a streamline.

# HFDPO-07218

A deep-space probe near Europa detects anomalous microwave emissions (2.4 GHz) originating from beneath the moon’s icy crust, suggesting subsurface liquid water. The emissions are polarized elliptically with a 45° tilt and a phase shift of π/4 between components. The probe’s sensors measure a 0.85 attenuation per km through the ice. Design a receiver array configuration that maximizes signal-to-noise ratio when the wave is partially reflected at the ice-water interface (refractive index of ice: 1.31, water: 1.33).

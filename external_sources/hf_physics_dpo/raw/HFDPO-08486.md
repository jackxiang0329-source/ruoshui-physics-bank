# HFDPO-08486

A coastal engineering team is designing a new breakwater to reduce wave energy entering a shallow lagoon used for aquaculture. The lagoon has a depth of 2.5 m and is exposed to storm waves with a wavelength of 28 m and a height of 1.8 m. The breakwater will be constructed perpendicular to the shore and will have a submerged crest at 1.0 m below the surface. Using the shallow water approximation, determine the wave height just inside the lagoon after the wave passes over the breakwater. Assume negligible dissipation and that the wave speed depends only on depth.

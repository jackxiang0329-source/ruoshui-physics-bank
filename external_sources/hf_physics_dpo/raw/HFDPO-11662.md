# HFDPO-11662

A fusion reactor's tokamak design uses a pulsed magnetic field to confine plasma at 15 million Kelvin, but engineers detect unexplained energy loss in the toroidal field coils. Using Maxwell's equations, determine whether the induced electric field in the shielding can be accurately described by the quasistatic approximation, and evaluate how the displacement current term in Ampère-Maxwell’s law affects the total field energy density over one cycle.

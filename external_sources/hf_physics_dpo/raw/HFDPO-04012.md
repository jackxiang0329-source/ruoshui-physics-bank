# HFDPO-04012

A satellite communication array uses dual-polarized antennas to maximize bandwidth in a crowded orbital frequency band. During testing, engineers observe signal degradation when the satellite passes over a dense forest canopy, despite clear line-of-sight in the theoretical model. The received signal shows a 12 dB drop in power for the vertically polarized channel, but only 2 dB for horizontally polarized. The forest consists of densely packed, randomly oriented tree trunks with average diameter 0.2 m and height 25 m, spaced 3 m apart. Calculate the dominant polarization mechanism responsible for the asymmetry and determine the minimum cross-polarization isolation required to maintain reliable communication in similar environments.

# HFDPO-04551

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a dual-polarization antenna array to receive signals from a ground station, but interference from reflected signals off a nearby mountain range causes signal degradation. The direct signal is vertically polarized with intensity 100 mW/m², while the reflected signal—having undergone a phase shift and partial polarization change—arrives at 70 mW/m² with elliptical polarization. The receiving antenna is designed for pure vertical polarization but can be adjusted to any linear orientation. What angle should the antenna be rotated to maximize the signal-to-interference ratio (SIR), assuming the interference vector makes a 30° angle with the vertical axis and has a polarization ellipse with major axis at 15° from vertical?

# HFDPO-11001

During a thunderstorm, a weather balloon equipped with sensors measures the electric field between the cloud base (at 2,000 m altitude) and the ground. When the field reaches 3 × 10⁴ V/m, lightning strikes. Assuming the cloud and ground act like a parallel-plate capacitor with a plate separation of 2,000 m and a capacitance of 1.8 × 10⁻⁷ F, what is the charge stored on the cloud before the strike?

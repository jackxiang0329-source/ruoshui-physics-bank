# HFDPO-12509

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a high-resolution imaging system encounters a plume of warm, saline water rising from a hydrothermal vent at a depth of 2,500 meters. The refractive index of the surrounding cold seawater is 1.340, but within the plume, it varies continuously from 1.335 at the edges to 1.327 at the center due to temperature gradients. The camera’s lens has a focal length of 120 mm in air, but the system operates submerged and uses a flat, transparent viewport with a refractive index of 1.52. Determine whether the image of a distant vent chimney (100 m away in the plume frame) will be in focus on the camera sensor, and analyze how the refractive gradient affects image sharpness and position—considering both the lens refractive correction and the light path distortion within the plume.

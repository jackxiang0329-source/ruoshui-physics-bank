# HFDPO-03761

In a deep-sea hydrothermal vent system, a cylindrical titanium probe (length 1.2 m, radius 5 mm) is used to measure thermal gradients. The probe's resistivity varies with temperature as ρ(T) = ρ₀(1 + α(T − T₀)), where ρ₀ = 1.3 × 10⁻⁷ Ω·m at 20°C, α = 0.0035/°C, and the probe is exposed to a 300°C environment. A constant 2.0 V potential is applied across its ends. Assuming radial heat conduction is negligible and the temperature is uniform along the length due to rapid conduction, find the total current through the probe, taking into account that its resistance increases with temperature due to self-heating.

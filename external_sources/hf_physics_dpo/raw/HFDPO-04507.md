# HFDPO-04507

A marine biologist uses a polarized camera filter to study coral bleaching under sunlight at noon, but notices that the reflection from the ocean surface interferes with image clarity. The camera is set to capture light at a 55° angle relative to the normal. Calculate the degree of polarization of the reflected light from the water surface and determine if the polarizing filter (aligned to block horizontally polarized light) will reduce glare effectively. Assume the refractive index of seawater is 1.34.

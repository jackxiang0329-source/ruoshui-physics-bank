# HFDPO-11573

A deep-space probe is positioned at the Lagrange L1 point between Earth and the Sun, maintaining a stable orbit relative to both. Engineers plan to adjust its trajectory using a low-thrust ion engine to redirect it toward a near-Earth asteroid.

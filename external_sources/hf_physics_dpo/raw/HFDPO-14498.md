# HFDPO-14498

A quantum computing chip using niobium-based Josephson junctions operates at 15 mK in a dilution refrigerator. During a field calibration, engineers detect anomalous phase slips in the qubit response at low external magnetic fields—just 5 µT—despite the device being shielded. The chip's superconducting loop has a critical current density of 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m², a loop inductance of 80 pH, and a magnetic flux quantum Φ₀ = 2.07 × 10⁻¹⁵ Wb. Determine whether these phase slips are consistent with quantum tunneling of magnetic flux quanta through the junctions, or if they indicate a breakdown in the superconducting state due to vortex penetration. Evaluate the physical plausibility of each mechanism given the measured field and material parameters.

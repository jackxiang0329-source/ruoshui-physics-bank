# HFDPO-01668

A neuroprosthetic device uses a microelectrode array implanted in the brain to detect neural signals, but the electrodes degrade over time due to electrochemical reactions at the interface. Each electrode has a parasitic capacitance of 10 pF in series with a 50 kΩ resistance. When a 100 μV neural spike (duration 2 ms) arrives, the voltage across the electrode decays exponentially. The device must detect signals above 20 μV to function. What is the maximum possible distance (in μm) between the electrode and the neuron’s axon if the extracellular medium has a resistivity of 150 Ω·cm and the electrode is circular with radius 10 μm? Assume the interface capacitance scales with electrode surface area and the neuron is modeled as a point charge source.

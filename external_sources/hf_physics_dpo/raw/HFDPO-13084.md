# HFDPO-13084

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a phased array antenna to transmit high-bandwidth data to a ground station located 400 km away. The signal operates at 12 GHz with a transmit power of 15 W, and the array’s effective aperture is 0.8 m². However, atmospheric attenuation and rain fade reduce signal strength by 3 dB during heavy rainfall. The ground station’s receiving antenna has a gain of 45 dBi and a noise temperature of 120 K. What is the minimum signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) required at the receiver for reliable data transmission, and how does the signal strength degrade when the satellite’s array misaligns by 5° from the optimal pointing angle?

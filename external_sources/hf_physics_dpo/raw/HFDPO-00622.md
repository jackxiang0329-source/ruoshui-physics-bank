# HFDPO-00622

A deep-space satellite uses a pair of counter-rotating gyroscopes to maintain attitude stability without thrusters. Each gyroscope has a mass of 12 kg, a radius of 0.3 m, and spins at 8000 rpm about its axis. During a minor trajectory correction, one gyroscope fails and begins to precess uncontrollably due to a torque from residual magnetic fields. Determine the rate at which the satellite’s orientation changes (in deg/s) if the gyroscope’s spin axis tilts at 15° to the satellite’s body, and the gyroscope’s angular momentum vector precesses at 0.2 rad/s. Assume the satellite’s moment of inertia about its center of mass is 350 kg·m² and is symmetric.

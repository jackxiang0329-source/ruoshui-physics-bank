# HFDPO-01374

A wind turbine in a coastal wind farm spins at 15 rpm during a storm, generating 1.2 MW of power. Suddenly, the gearbox fails, leaving the blades freely rotating with no electrical load. If the turbine’s rotor has a moment of inertia of 3.5 × 10⁶ kg·m² and experiences a constant torque of 2.1 × 10⁵ N·m due to aerodynamic drag, how long will it take for the blades to slow down to half their initial angular speed?

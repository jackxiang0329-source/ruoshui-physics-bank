# HFDPO-02025

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal magnetic confinement chamber to sustain a plasma at 150 million Kelvin. During a 10-second pulse, the plasma emits 1.2 × 10¹⁴ J of radiant energy and expels 4.5 kg of deuterium-tritium fuel at an average exhaust velocity of 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s. If the reactor’s total mass (including magnetic coils and structural components) is 280 metric tons, determine the magnitude and direction of the recoil velocity of the entire reactor vessel after the pulse, assuming no external forces act during the discharge.

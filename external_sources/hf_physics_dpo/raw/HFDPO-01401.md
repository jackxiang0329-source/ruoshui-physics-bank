# HFDPO-01401

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a superconducting loop (radius 1.2 m, resistance = 0) suspended in molten rock at 1100°C. As the loop is slowly lowered at 0.3 m/s through a non-uniform magnetic field generated by tectonic currents (approximated as B(z) = B₀(1 - e^(-z/50)) T, where z is depth in meters and B₀ = 0.02 T), engineers observe fluctuating induced voltages. At what depth does the rate of change of magnetic flux reach its maximum, and what is the peak induced EMF if the loop is perfectly aligned with the field direction?

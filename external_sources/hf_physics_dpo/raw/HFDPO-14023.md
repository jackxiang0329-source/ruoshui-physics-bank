# HFDPO-14023

In a cryogenic fuel storage system for next-generation space launch vehicles, liquid hydrogen (LH₂) is stored at 20 K in a spherical tank with a 3.2 m radius. During a brief power outage, the vacuum insulation fails, allowing heat influx at 1.8 W/m² from the ambient 300 K environment. The tank’s outer surface absorbs radiant heat but has negligible thermal mass. After 4.5 hours, the system shows signs of boiling instability.

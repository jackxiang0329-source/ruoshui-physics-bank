# HFDPO-02251

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for coronary arteries that must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter delivery tube to a 6.0 mm working diameter. The polymer used has a Young’s modulus of 1.3 GPa and a Poisson’s ratio of 0.45. If the inner surface of the stent experiences a tensile stress of 80 MPa during expansion, what is the radial strain at the inner wall, and how does this affect the stent’s overall wall thickness? Assume uniform material properties and small deformations.

# HFDPO-13571

A solar-powered environmental sensor node in a remote forest monitoring network uses a 12 V battery charged by a solar panel. The panel outputs 18 V under full sun but can drop to 9 V during partial cloud cover. The sensor draws 25 mA when active and 5 mA when sleeping. A voltage regulator maintains 5 V to the sensor, but its efficiency drops from 90% at 12 V input to 70% at 9 V. If clouds reduce panel voltage for 8 hours daily, what is the minimum battery capacity (in Ah) needed to sustain operation through a full 24-hour cycle with no recharge?

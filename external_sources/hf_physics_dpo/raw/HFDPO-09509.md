# HFDPO-09509

In a high-altitude research station, scientists observe that a newly synthesized perovskite material used in solar cells fails unpredictably during cold nights, despite being rated for -60°C operation. The thermal conductivity of the material is 1.2 W/m·K, with a latent heat of transformation of 28 kJ/mol. What physical mechanism explains the hysteresis in phase transition temperature, and how does the cooling rate affect the observed phase stability?

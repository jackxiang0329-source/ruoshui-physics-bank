# HFDPO-12063

During a Mars rover mission, engineers notice that the drill bit, designed to sample subsurface regolith, begins to wobble violently after reaching 1200 RPM—despite being perfectly balanced on Earth. The drill’s 25 cm long, 300 g shaft has a moment of inertia of 0.0012 kg·m² about its center, and the bit’s mass is concentrated 18 cm from the axis. When torque is applied at 0.35 N·m, it accelerates angularly but exhibits resonance-like oscillations. Given that the flexural stiffness of the shaft is 450 N·m²/rad, determine the angular speed at which the system enters dynamic instability, and explain why this might not have been predicted by static balance alone.

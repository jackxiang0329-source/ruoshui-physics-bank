# HFDPO-03213

A Mars rover's solar array is degrading under intense UV radiation and dust accumulation, reducing its efficiency. The photovoltaic cells are silicon-based with a bandgap of 1.12 eV, but the spectral mismatch between Mars' sunlight (AM0, 1366 W/m²) and the cell’s response is causing a 22% drop in power output. The team proposes a tandem cell architecture using a perovskite top layer (1.55 eV bandgap) over a silicon bottom cell. Estimate the maximum theoretical efficiency gain and determine the optimal bandgap for the top layer if the AM0 spectrum is known.

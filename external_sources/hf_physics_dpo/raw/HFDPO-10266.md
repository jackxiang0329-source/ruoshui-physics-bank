# HFDPO-10266

During a high-altitude drone delivery mission in the Himalayas, a prototype cargo drone must traverse a 2.4 km stretch of narrow valley where sudden wind gusts (up to 28 m/s) from the slopes cause turbulent airflow. The drone’s streamlined body has a drag coefficient of 0.18, a cross-sectional area of 0.32 m², and a total mass of 18 kg. At 4,500 m elevation, air density is 0.73 kg/m³. To conserve battery, the drone maintains a steady 12 m/s forward speed. The landing gear, made of rubberized composite, experiences static and kinetic friction coefficients of 0.65 and 0.45 respectively with the icy runway. Calculate the minimum horizontal force the motors must sustain to maintain constant speed, considering both aerodynamic drag and ground friction during final descent.

# HFDPO-07402

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic panels wired in a hybrid configuration—some in series, others in parallel—to charge a 24 V battery bank. During a cloudy day, one panel fails and shows zero output, but the system remains operational. Engineers notice the current through the battery is only 85% of its expected value. Using Kirchhoff’s laws, determine which panel failed and how the circuit was originally wired, given that the total current from the array is 15 A under ideal conditions and each panel produces 0.5 A when functioning.

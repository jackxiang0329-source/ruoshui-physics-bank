A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new model with a touch-sensitive screen that uses capacitive sensing. During testing, engineers notice the screen misfires when a user wears thick winter gloves. The screen detects changes in capacitance between the finger and the surface. If the gloves reduce the effective capacitance by 60% due to increased distance and insulating material, what minimum capacitance must the screen system maintain to still register a touch, given that the baseline capacitance without gloves is 12 pF?

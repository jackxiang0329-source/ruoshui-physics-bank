# HFDPO-15283

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain region uses a 12 V battery bank and three identical photovoltaic panels, each with a 15 V open-circuit voltage and 2 A short-circuit current. The panels are connected in parallel to charge the battery, but during a week of overcast skies, the battery voltage drops to 10.2 V. The station’s control circuitry requires 5 V at 0.4 A to operate, regulated by a voltage-divider network using two resistors. If one resistor in the divider has failed (measuring infinite resistance), how can you determine the value of the functional resistor by measuring only the voltage across it—assuming you have a multimeter but no spare parts?

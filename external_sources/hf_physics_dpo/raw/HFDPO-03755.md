# HFDPO-03755

A commercial satellite constellation in low Earth orbit must perform a rapid repositioning maneuver after detecting an unexpected solar flare that threatens ground-based power grids. The lead satellite, initially in a circular orbit at 650 km altitude, needs to transfer to a new orbit with a 30° inclination change while minimizing propellant use. The maneuver must be completed within 90 minutes due to operational constraints.

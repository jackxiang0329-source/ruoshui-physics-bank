# HFDPO-10848

The engineers must determine how much additional cooling capacity is needed to maintain superfluidity, considering that superfluid helium has a critical heat flux of 1.2 kW/m² before transition, and the coil surface area is 5.3 m². The system’s current passive cooling can dissipate 1.8 kW, but the transition reduces thermal conductivity by 90%.

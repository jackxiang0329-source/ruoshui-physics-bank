# HFDPO-01661

A deep-sea submersible drone, designed to study hydrothermal vents at 2,500 meters depth, encounters unexpected lateral drift during a precision maneuver. The drone’s titanium hull (surface area 1.3 m², drag coefficient 0.09) moves horizontally at 0.8 m/s through seawater (ρ = 1,025 kg/m³), but the thrusters are only rated for 35 N of thrust. If the system’s static friction between the drone’s rubberized landing feet and the basalt seabed is μ_s = 0.65, but dynamic friction is only μ_k = 0.40, can the drone maintain position against a current with a 12° angle relative to its axis? Assume the drone’s weight on land is 1,200 N and gravity at depth is 9.78 m/s². Evaluate whether the thrusters alone can resist motion, considering both drag and friction dynamics.

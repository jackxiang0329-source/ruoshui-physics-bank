# HFDPO-08236

A research team is developing a portable water purification system for remote villages using solar-powered electrolysis. The system uses a 12 V solar array connected to a DC voltage regulator that maintains a stable 24 V output to drive two identical electrolysis cells in series, each with an internal resistance of 1.5 Ω and requiring 2 A to operate efficiently. However, during testing, the current drops to only 1.2 A. The team suspects the regulator is failing, but the wiring and connections are verified as sound. Determine whether the issue lies in the regulator’s ability to maintain voltage, or if excessive power loss in the system’s wiring (assumed to be 0.8 Ω total resistance) could explain the current drop. Evaluate the actual power delivered to the electrolysis cells and compare it to the required power.

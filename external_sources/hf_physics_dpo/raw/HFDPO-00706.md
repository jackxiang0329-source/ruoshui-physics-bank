# HFDPO-00706

A high-altitude drone, designed to monitor atmospheric ionization during solar storms, unexpectedly begins drifting off course. Explain why the magnetometer drifts and calculate the maximum induced EMF across the frame, assuming the storm induces a time-varying B-field gradient of 80 nT/s perpendicular to the loop plane.

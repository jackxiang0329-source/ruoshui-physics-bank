# HFDPO-03538

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical heat-exchange borehole (1,200 m deep) to extract thermal energy from subsurface rock. A 2,500 kg steel probe, coated with thermal insulation, is lowered at a constant speed to the bottom, then slowly raised while transferring heat to a working fluid. Given that the probe experiences a variable gravitational field (g ≈ 9.81 – 1.2×10⁻⁶z m/s², z in meters), and drag force scales with velocity as F_d = 0.08v (N), determine the total work done *by the lifting mechanism* to raise the probe from the bottom to the surface, assuming a constant lifting speed of 0.3 m/s.

# HFDPO-01398

During a storm, a coastal town experiences rising water levels, and residents report that their rooftop solar panels are lifting off during high winds. The panels are mounted flat on a 12 m² roof with 1.5 m high parapets. Wind gusts reach 50 m/s parallel to the roof surface. Using Bernoulli’s principle, estimate the net upward force on a panel if the air pressure beneath it remains atmospheric, and determine whether the panel could detach if the panel weighs 180 N and is secured only by 8 bolts with a shear strength of 25 N each. Assume air density is 1.2 kg/m³.

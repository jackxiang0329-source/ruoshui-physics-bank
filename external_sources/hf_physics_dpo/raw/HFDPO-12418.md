# HFDPO-12418

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a complex array of toroidal magnetic coils to confine plasma at 10⁸ K. Engineers notice unexpected electric field drifts in the insulating ceramic shell surrounding the vacuum chamber, which threatens the reactor’s stability. The shell has a radial thickness of 15 cm and contains a non-uniform volume charge density ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−r²/a²), where r is radial distance from the center, ρ₀ = 3.2 × 10⁻⁵ C/m³, and a = 0.8 m. The inner radius of the shell is 2.0 m. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the magnitude of the electric field at r = 2.2 m.

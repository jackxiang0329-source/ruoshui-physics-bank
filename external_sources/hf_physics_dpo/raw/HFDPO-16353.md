# HFDPO-16353

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer (initially uncharged) is suspended by electrostatic force between two parallel metal plates separated by 2.0 mm. During a critical deposition step, a uniform electric field of 3.5 × 10⁴ V/m is applied vertically. However, due to a defect in the dielectric coating, a localized surface charge density of +1.2 μC/m² spontaneously appears on the wafer’s lower surface, causing it to tilt by 5.3° from horizontal. Estimate the minimum charge redistribution (in nC) needed on the upper plate to restore levitation equilibrium, assuming the wafer’s mass is 120 g and the system operates in a vacuum.

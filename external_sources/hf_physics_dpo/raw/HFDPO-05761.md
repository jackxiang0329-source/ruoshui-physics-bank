# HFDPO-05761

During a heatwave, a solar-powered water pump in a remote village begins failing despite adequate sunlight. The pump’s DC motor overheats, shutting down after 15 minutes of operation. Engineers discover the motor’s heat sink is made of aluminum (specific heat 900 J/kg·K), weighs 0.8 kg, and is initially at 35°C. The motor generates 120 W of waste heat, but the heat sink only absorbs 70% of it due to thermal resistance in the interface. Ambient air is 40°C, and natural convection provides a heat transfer coefficient of 10 W/m²·K over the 0.12 m² surface area. What is the maximum temperature the motor reaches before thermal shutdown?

# HFDPO-07786

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a spherical silicon wafer (radius 15 cm) is suspended electrostatically between two parallel plate electrodes. The wafer carries a non-uniform surface charge density σ(θ) = σ₀(1 + 2cosθ), where θ is measured from the upward vertical. The upper electrode is held at +1.2 kV and the lower at –1.2 kV, with a gap of 10 cm. Determine whether the net electrostatic force on the wafer is upward, downward, or zero, and explain how the charge distribution influences this force despite the symmetric electrode setup.

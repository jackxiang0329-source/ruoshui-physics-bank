# HFDPO-00271

During a routine inspection of a geothermal power plant in Iceland, engineers notice a sudden drop in output from a 150 MW binary-cycle unit. The brine flow is 250 kg/s, and the isopentane enters at 45°C and exits at 140°C. The system operates under constant pressure, and heat loss to the environment is negligible. What is the most likely explanation—deposition of mineral scale on the heat exchanger surface, or a reduction in brine flow? Use thermodynamic and heat transfer principles to analyze both scenarios quantitatively, assuming a baseline heat transfer coefficient of 2,200 W/m²K and a surface area of 120 m².

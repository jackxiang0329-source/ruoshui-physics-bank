# HFDPO-05300

In a fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma chamber is surrounded by a series of precisely shaped magnetic coils that generate a non-uniform electric field to confine charged particles. Engineers observe that the net flux through a spherical Gaussian surface placed near the plasma boundary is zero, yet the electric field is not zero anywhere on the surface. How can this be reconciled with Gauss’s Law, and what does this imply about the charge distribution within the reactor’s volume?

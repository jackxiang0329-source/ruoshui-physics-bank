# HFDPO-01171

A deep-space telescope array on Mars detects faint light from a distant exoplanet’s atmosphere, but the image appears blurred due to atmospheric turbulence and a misaligned secondary mirror. The primary mirror (diameter 1.5 m, focal length 4.8 m) produces a diffraction-limited spot size of 0.15 arcseconds under ideal conditions. However, the observed spot is 1.8 arcseconds wide. The telescope’s adaptive optics system corrects for atmospheric distortion but cannot compensate for mechanical misalignment. Estimate the angular displacement of the secondary mirror from its optimal position, and determine whether the error is due to tilt or lateral offset. What is the minimum detectable change in the exoplanet’s atmospheric transmission spectrum given this blurring?

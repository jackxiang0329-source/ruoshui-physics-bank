# HFDPO-06408

A geothermal energy company is drilling into a deep, fractured rock formation where thermal cycling from injection and extraction causes stress buildup in the surrounding rock. The rock behaves as a linear elastic material with Young’s modulus 45 GPa and Poisson’s ratio 0.25. A 500 m long borehole experiences a 120°C temperature change over 24 hours, inducing radial stress. The rock's thermal expansion coefficient is 3×10⁻⁶ /°C. What is the maximum radial stress at the borehole wall, and how does it compare to the rock’s uniaxial compressive strength (120 MPa)?

# HFDPO-11917

A quantum dot solar cell prototype uses a hexagonal array of subwavelength silver nanodisks (diameter 120 nm, height 40 nm) embedded in a SiO₂ matrix to enhance light absorption via plasmonic resonance. At 620 nm incident light (p-polarized), the system shows anomalously low transmission, but measurements reveal interference fringes in the far-field pattern with a spacing of 1.8° between maxima. What is the effective refractive index of the near-field plasmonic mode coupling, and how does it relate to the dispersion of surface plasmon polaritons in this structured medium?

# HFDPO-11432

A renewable energy startup is testing a new gravity-based energy storage system using massive concrete blocks suspended in a disused mine shaft. Each block, weighing 12,000 kg, is lowered from rest at a constant acceleration of 0.3 m/s² over a 300 m vertical drop, while driving a generator via a pulley system with 88% efficiency. The system must deliver 1.1 × 10⁷ J of electrical energy per cycle. What is the maximum possible energy input to the system per cycle, and could the system be sustainable if 15% of the mechanical energy is lost to friction in the pulleys and bearings?

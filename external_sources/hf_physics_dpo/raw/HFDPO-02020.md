# HFDPO-02020

During a deep-space mission, a Mars rover's solar array degrades due to micrometeoroid impacts, reducing its effective conductivity by 30% across one critical circuit loop. The original loop, designed for 15 A at 24 V, now experiences uneven resistance distribution: Section A (3 m of carbon-fiber mesh) has 1.2 Ω, Section B (1.5 m of damaged silicon ribbon) has 2.8 Ω, and Section C (intact copper busbar) has 0.4 Ω. Engineers must reroute power through a redundant parallel path with a single variable resistor to maintain safe operation (≤18 A total). What resistance value must be chosen for the backup path to ensure current balance and prevent overheating (maximum 200 W dissipation), while minimizing power loss?

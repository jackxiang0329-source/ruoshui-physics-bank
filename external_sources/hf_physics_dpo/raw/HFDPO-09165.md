# HFDPO-09165

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 315°C, which then drives a steam turbine. The turbine exhausts to a condenser maintained at 50°C. If the plant’s heat input rate is 120 MW, what is the maximum possible thermal efficiency under the new conditions, and how much more net power could be generated compared to the original 50°C case?

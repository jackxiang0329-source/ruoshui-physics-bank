# HFDPO-02350

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a fluid-filled pipe, heating it to 400°C to generate steam. The pipe is 10 meters long, 5 cm in diameter, and made of stainless steel with a thermal conductivity of 15 W/m·K. During a sunny day, the pipe receives 800 W/m² of solar radiation, but only 90% is absorbed by the pipe’s surface. If the surrounding air is at 25°C and heat loss occurs via convection (h = 10 W/m²·K) and radiation (emissivity ε = 0.9), what is the steady-state temperature of the pipe’s outer surface?

# HFDPO-11617

A Mars rover's rear wheel, designed with a hollow cylindrical shell (mass 12 kg, outer radius 0.45 m, inner radius 0.40 m), encounters a 15 cm-high rock during a traverse. As it climbs over, the wheel pivots about the rock’s edge without slipping. If the wheel was initially rolling at 2.8 m/s with no slipping, what is the minimum coefficient of static friction needed between the wheel and the rock to prevent skidding during the climb? Assume the wheel's center of mass follows a circular arc of radius 0.45 m about the pivot point.

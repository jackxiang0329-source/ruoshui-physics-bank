# HFDPO-13628

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 20° angle to maximize sunlight capture. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 450 N on the panel’s center. The panel weighs 120 N and is fixed at its base with a hinge. To prevent tipping, the installer adds a counterweight at the top edge. What minimum counterweight is needed to maintain static equilibrium if the panel’s center of mass is 1.5 m from the hinge and the counterweight is placed 0.8 m above the hinge?

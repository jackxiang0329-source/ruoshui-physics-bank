# HFDPO-02608

A deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent field at 3,000 meters depth, where it must deploy a flexible titanium alloy tether (Young’s modulus 110 GPa, yield strength 900 MPa) to secure a sample collector. The tether, initially 20 m long and 2 cm in diameter, experiences a dynamic load from ocean currents estimated at 1.8 kN. Due to non-uniform pressure and temperature gradients, the tether’s cross-section tapers from 2.0 cm at the top to 1.2 cm at the bottom. Calculate the total elongation of the tether under load, and determine whether the material remains within its elastic limit.

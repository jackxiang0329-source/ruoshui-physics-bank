# HFDPO-03494

A geothermal energy plant drills two deep boreholes (10 km apart horizontally) into a hot basalt layer, aiming to circulate water through a buried copper pipeline loop to generate electricity. The loop forms a closed circuit with a total resistance of 4.2 Ω, including 3.8 Ω from the pipe and 0.4 Ω from connectors. During operation, the system generates 120 kW of electrical power via thermoelectric conversion at the surface. However, measurements show that 5% of the input power is lost as joule heating in the loop wiring. What is the actual current flowing through the loop, and what is the temperature rise of the copper pipe if it has a mass of 200 kg and specific heat of 385 J/kg·K over 1 hour of operation?

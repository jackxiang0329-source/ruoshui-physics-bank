# HFDPO-16450

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable cardiac sensor patch made of a soft, biocompatible polymer with a Young’s modulus of 1.2 MPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.45. The patch must conform to the irregular, dynamically contracting surface of a patient’s left ventricle, which undergoes a local strain of up to 0.18 during systole. To prevent delamination due to shear stress at the interface, the engineer models the patch as a thin elastic film bonded to a deformable substrate. What is the maximum allowable shear modulus of the adhesive layer if the interfacial shear stress must not exceed 12 kPa under peak cardiac strain, assuming the patch undergoes uniform stretching and the adhesive layer is 5 μm thick?

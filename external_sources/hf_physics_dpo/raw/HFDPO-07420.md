# HFDPO-07420

A deep-sea submersible’s optical sensor array detects faint bioluminescent signals from a rare deep-sea organism at a depth of 1,200 m. The sensor uses a compound lens system with a 30 cm diameter lens, but the signal appears blurred when the sub descends below 800 m. The refractive index of seawater varies with depth due to salinity gradients: n(h) = 1.335 + 0.0002 cdot h (where h is depth in meters). If the lens is designed for ideal conditions at the surface ( n = 1.335 ), determine how much the effective focal length shifts at 1,200 m and explain why the image becomes misfocused—then propose a corrective optical element design using a graded-index (GRIN) lens segment to maintain focus.

# HFDPO-13302

A satellite constellation designed to monitor Earth’s polar ice caps orbits at an altitude of 600 km, but engineers notice a persistent drift in its orbital plane over months. The satellite’s inclination is slowly changing due to gravitational perturbations from the Moon and Earth’s equatorial bulge. Assume a nearly circular orbit with period 96 minutes.

# HFDPO-02495

A farmer notices her solar-powered irrigation pump stops working during strong thunderstorms. The pump relies on a small generator connected to a rotating fan powered by wind. She suspects electromagnetic induction in the generator’s coil is disrupted by nearby lightning-induced magnetic fields. What must happen to the magnetic field through the generator’s coil to induce a current and power the pump?

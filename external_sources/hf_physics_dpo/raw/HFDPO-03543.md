# HFDPO-03543

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a charged nano-scale probe (radius 50 nm, charge -1.6 × 10⁻¹⁸ C) approaches a silicon wafer coated with a 20 nm thick dielectric layer (εᵣ = 10).

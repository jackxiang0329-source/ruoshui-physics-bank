# HFDPO-09801

In a quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit is coupled to a readout resonator via a shared capacitive link. The resonator operates at 5.2 GHz but exhibits unexpected frequency shifts when the qubit is in its excited state—measured shifts of up to 120 MHz. The resonator’s inductance is 1.2 nH, and the capacitance is 1.8 pF. A student models the system as a nonlinear LC circuit where the qubit’s state alters the effective capacitance. What is the required change in effective capacitance to explain the observed shift, and how does this shift relate to the qubit’s transition energy?

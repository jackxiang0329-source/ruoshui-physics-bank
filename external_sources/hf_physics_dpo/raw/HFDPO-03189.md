# HFDPO-03189

A concert hall designer is optimizing acoustics for a symphony orchestra performing Beethoven’s Ninth, where the low-frequency resonance from the double bass section (20–60 Hz) causes noticeable rumbling in the upper balcony. The balcony’s wooden floor (density 500 kg/m³, thickness 0.04 m) vibrates at 38 Hz when struck, creating a standing wave mode that interferes destructively with the 38 Hz harmonic of the basses. The hall’s length is 60 m, and the balcony is 15 m from the stage. What structural modification—adding mass, changing floor thickness, or introducing damping material—would most effectively suppress this resonance without affecting higher-frequency sound quality, and why?

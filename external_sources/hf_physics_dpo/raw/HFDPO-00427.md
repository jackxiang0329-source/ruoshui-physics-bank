# HFDPO-00427

A quantum imaging lab attempts to enhance the resolution of a single-photon camera used in deep-tissue biomedical imaging by exploiting entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC). The system uses a 5 mm type-II BBO crystal pumped by a 405 nm laser at 100 mW. Due to tissue scattering, only 1 in 3000 signal photons reaches the detector after a 2 mm thick tissue sample. If the camera’s pixel size is 10 μm and the system’s effective quantum efficiency is 15%, what is the minimum number of photon pairs that must be generated per second to achieve a reliable detection rate of 5 signal photons per pixel per second, considering detector dark counts (2 Hz) and background noise (4 Hz)?

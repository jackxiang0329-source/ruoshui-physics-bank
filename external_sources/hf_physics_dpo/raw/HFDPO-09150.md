# HFDPO-09150

A wireless charging pad for a smartphone uses a coil that generates a time-varying magnetic field to induce current in the phone’s receiver coil. If the magnetic field strength increases linearly from 0 to 0.5 T over 10 milliseconds, what is the magnitude of the induced electric field at a radius of 2 cm from the center of the coil? Assume the field is uniform over the area enclosed by the path.

# HFDPO-16344

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain precise orientation while hovering near the edge of a mountain ridge. The drone’s center of mass is offset 0.3 m forward due to asymmetric battery placement, and it is attached via a rigid, massless boom to a pivot point on the ridge. Determine the minimum frictional torque at the pivot required to prevent rotation during maximum gusts.

# HFDPO-06121

A coastal city is designing a new stormwater drainage system to prevent flooding during intense rainstorms. Engineers plan to install a series of vertical vent pipes at the top of retention basins to release built-up pressure when water levels rise rapidly. One pipe, 1.2 m tall with a 0.15 m diameter, is connected to a horizontal pipe of 0.3 m diameter. During a storm, water flows through the horizontal pipe at 2.8 m/s. If the system must prevent excessive backpressure (greater than 15 kPa) in the horizontal pipe, what is the minimum height of the vent pipe needed to ensure safe operation? Assume ideal flow and atmospheric pressure at the vent opening.

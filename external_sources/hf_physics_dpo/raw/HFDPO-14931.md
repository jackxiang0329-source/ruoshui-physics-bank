# HFDPO-14931

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a superconducting loop at its base to harvest energy from Earth’s magnetic field gradients. The loop, 1.2 m in diameter, resides in a region where the vertical magnetic field increases from 45 μT at the top of the borehole to 62 μT at the bottom over a depth of 800 m. As thermal convection slowly stirs the surrounding rock, it induces a changing magnetic flux through the loop. If the loop's resistance is effectively zero (due to superconductivity) and the induced current creates a magnetic dipole that opposes the change, what is the peak current generated during a 30-minute period when the field gradient changes linearly?

# HFDPO-07122

A solar farm in Nevada experiences frequent dust buildup on its photovoltaic panels, reducing efficiency by up to 15%. Engineers discover that the panels, when exposed to dry, dusty winds, develop a static charge due to triboelectric effects. If each panel (2 m × 1 m) accumulates a uniform surface charge density of +3 × 10⁻⁸ C/m², and a dust particle (mass 5 × 10⁻¹² kg, charge −2 × 10⁻¹⁴ C) approaches from 1 cm away, what is the minimum initial speed required for the particle to reach the panel’s surface despite electrostatic attraction? Assume the field is uniform near the surface.

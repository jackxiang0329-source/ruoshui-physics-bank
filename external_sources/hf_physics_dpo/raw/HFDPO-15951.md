# HFDPO-15951

A research team is developing a quantum sensor for detecting ultra-weak magnetic fields in a neuroscience lab. The sensor uses a high-temperature superconducting loop (YBCO, Tc ≈ 92 K) cooled by liquid nitrogen (77 K). During calibration, they observe that the loop loses sensitivity when a nearby MRI machine is activated nearby, even though the sensor is shielded. They suspect flux trapping in the superconducting loop due to a transient magnetic field. If the loop has a diameter of 2.5 cm, and a magnetic field of 1.5 mT briefly penetrates during the MRI pulse, what is the maximum current that can persist in the loop due to trapped flux, and will it interfere with the sensor’s operation?

# HFDPO-03657

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 50 nm-thick silicon wafer (dielectric constant εᵣ = 11.7) is suspended above a grounded metal plate by a 10 μm air gap. During an etch step, the wafer accumulates a non-uniform charge density due to ion bombardment—peaking at 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/m² at the center. A probe measures a local electric field of 1.8 × 10⁶ V/m just above the wafer’s surface. What is the actual charge distribution required to produce this field, considering image charges and field screening by the dielectric?

# HFDPO-13252

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must glide from 20,000 m to 15,000 m without engine power, using only thermal updrafts for energy recovery. The drone has a mass of 8.2 kg and a glide ratio of 18.5:1 (horizontal distance per vertical drop). At cruising altitude, the air density is 0.19 kg/m³ and the wing's drag coefficient is 0.045.

# HFDPO-16474

A wildlife biologist studying nocturnal bird migration uses a laser-based tracking system to monitor flock orientation. The system emits a 633 nm red laser through a diffraction grating with 500 lines/mm. At a distance of 1.2 km, the first-order beams form spots separated by 1.1 m on a sensor array. The biologist notices that the pattern shifts subtly when birds fly through the beam, causing interference. What is the effective width of the bird's wingspan if it partially blocks the central maximum, causing the first minimum of the single-slit pattern to coincide with the first-order diffraction peak?

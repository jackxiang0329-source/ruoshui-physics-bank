# HFDPO-10430

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses polarizing filters on its solar arrays to reduce glare from reflected sunlight off ocean surfaces, improving power efficiency. At a certain angle of incidence, the reflected light is 98% linearly polarized horizontally. The satellite’s photovoltaic cells are sensitive to polarization, and the current efficiency drops by 15% when the incoming light is not aligned with their optimal polarization axis. Design a polarization rotation system using a half-wave plate to maximize power capture. Given that the wave plate must be mounted with minimal mass and thermal drift, determine the required orientation angle of the wave plate’s fast axis relative to the horizontal to align the reflected light’s polarization with the cells’ preferred orientation.

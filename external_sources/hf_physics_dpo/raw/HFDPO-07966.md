# HFDPO-07966

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must glide from 18,000 m to 12,000 m while conserving energy for data transmission. The drone's mass is 42 kg, and it experiences a drag force proportional to the square of its speed: F_d = 0.015 v^2 . At 18,000 m, it enters a controlled glide with an initial speed of 35 m/s. As it descends, solar panels generate a variable power input of P(t) = 120 - 0.003t watts (t in seconds), which contributes to maintaining kinetic energy. Determine the drone’s final speed at 12,000 m, assuming no engine thrust and constant atmospheric density.

# HFDPO-01316

In a fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma chamber is enclosed by a superconducting magnetic coil with a radial thickness of 1.2 m. The plasma generates a non-uniform charge density ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−r²/2σ²), where ρ₀ = 5×10⁻⁶ C/m³ and σ = 0.8 m. Engineers need to determine the electric flux through a spherical Gaussian surface of radius 1.5 m centered at the torus’s midpoint, but the plasma’s asymmetry complicates direct integration. How does the net electric flux depend on the total charge enclosed, and what implications does this have for plasma confinement stability?

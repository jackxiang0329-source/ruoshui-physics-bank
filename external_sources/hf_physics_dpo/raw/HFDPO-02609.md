# HFDPO-02609

A fusion reactor designer is testing a new tokamak magnet system using high-temperature superconducting (HTS) tapes made of YBCO. The coils must carry 12 kA of current at 25 K to generate a 12 T magnetic field. However, during a cooldown test, a localized warm spot of 45 K forms in one segment, causing a quench. The system’s cryocooler can remove 300 W of heat from the coil. Is the quench self-sustaining, or can the cryocooler prevent a full thermal runaway? Assume the tape’s critical current drops to 30% of its value at 45 K, and the local resistance generates heat at a rate of 1.8 W/cm.

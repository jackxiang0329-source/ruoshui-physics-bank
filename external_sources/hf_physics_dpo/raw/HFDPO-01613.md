# HFDPO-01613

A research team is developing a flexible, wearable sensor array for monitoring muscle activity in athletes, using a network of thin, conductive polymer strips woven into fabric. Each strip has a variable resistivity that changes with mechanical strain—resistance increases by 15% per 1 cm stretch. During a sprint test, one sensor strip stretches from 10 cm to 12.5 cm under load, while another remains unstretched. The strips are connected in parallel across a 3.0 V constant-voltage source. Given the initial resistance of each strip is 200 Ω and the power supply can deliver up to 120 mW, determine whether the system exceeds safe power limits when both strips are active.

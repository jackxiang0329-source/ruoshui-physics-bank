# HFDPO-02092

A solar panel mounted on a rooftop in a windy coastal town tilts slightly out of alignment after a storm. The panel weighs 120 N and is secured at one end by a hinge, while the other end rests on a roller support. If the wind exerts a horizontal force of 35 N at the center of the panel, which is 2.4 m long and angled at 30° from horizontal, what vertical force must the roller support apply to keep the panel in static equilibrium?

# HFDPO-11493

In a quantum computing prototype, a superconducting qubit is coupled to a readout cavity via a capacitive transmon circuit. The qubit's energy levels depend on the effective Josephson junction capacitance and the shunting capacitance of the circuit. During calibration, engineers observe a 12% shift in the resonant frequency of the cavity—a key diagnostic signal—after minor fabrication variations. Given that the original shunting capacitance was 85 fF and the Josephson junction's critical charge is 3.2 × 10⁻¹⁹ C, determine how much the Josephson junction capacitance must have changed to explain the frequency shift, assuming the cavity inductance remains constant.

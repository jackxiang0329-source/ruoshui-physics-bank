# HFDPO-03437

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole to extract hot water from a 2.5 km deep rock formation with a temperature of 280°C. The fluid flows at 1.2 kg/s and cools to 160°C before returning, but the pipe’s thermal insulation has degraded over time, leading to heat losses. Engineers estimate heat loss through the pipe wall to be 380 W/m. If the pipe has a 12 cm outer diameter and the ambient ground temperature is 4°C, what is the required thermal conductivity of the remaining insulation to maintain the outlet temperature within 5°C of the target? Assume steady 1D radial conduction and a convective heat transfer coefficient of 80 W/m²K between the fluid and pipe wall.

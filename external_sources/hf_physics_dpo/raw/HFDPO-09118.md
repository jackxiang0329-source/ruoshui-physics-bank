# HFDPO-09118

A quantum imaging lab uses a spatial light modulator (SLM) to generate a custom structured light field for super-resolution microscopy, but the resulting interference pattern shows unexpected dark fringes at the center of the image. The SLM applies a phase profile of φ(r) = k₀r² + α·cos(2πf₀r), where k₀ = 1.5×10⁴ rad/m, f₀ = 5000 cycles/m, and α = π/3. The imaging system uses a coherent laser source at λ = 532 nm and a lens with f = 100 mm. Why does the central intensity drop significantly despite the expected constructive interference at r = 0?

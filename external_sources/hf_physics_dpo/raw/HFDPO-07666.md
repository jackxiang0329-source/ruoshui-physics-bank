# HFDPO-07666

In a remote desert research station, a solar-powered water purifier relies on a 24 V DC system with a complex network of resistive heaters and sensors. Two parallel branches are essential: one powers a 120 W heater (R₁ = 4.8 Ω), and the other drives a 60 W sensor array (R₂ = 9.6 Ω). A current-limiting resistor (R₃) of 0.5 Ω is added in series with the main supply line to prevent surge damage. When the station’s battery voltage dips to 21.6 V due to aging, the purifier fails to reach boiling temperature. Determine the minimum battery voltage required to maintain 10 A total current through the system, and explain why simply increasing R₃ would not solve the problem.

# HFDPO-08734

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a small heat engine powered by a buried geothermal pipe warmed by Earth’s internal heat (120°C), while the outside air averages –30°C. The engine must produce 500 W of continuous electrical power to run sensors and a satellite uplink. However, due to ice buildup, the engine’s cold reservoir temperature is actually –10°C, not –30°C. Calculate the actual thermal efficiency and compare it to the theoretical maximum if the cold reservoir were truly at –30°C. What does this efficiency gap reveal about real-world heat engine design under extreme conditions?

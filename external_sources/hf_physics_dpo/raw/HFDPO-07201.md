# HFDPO-07201

A marine biologist is studying how underwater volcanic activity affects whale communication. During an eruption, a 120 Hz sound wave travels through seawater (where wave speed is 1500 m/s) and reaches a hydrophone array 3.6 km away. The biologist notices a 2.4-second delay between the arrival of the sound and the subsequent seismic wave detected by a nearby seismometer. Assuming the seismic wave travels through rock at 5000 m/s, what is the actual distance from the eruption to the hydrophone, and how does this discrepancy inform us about oceanic crust thickness?

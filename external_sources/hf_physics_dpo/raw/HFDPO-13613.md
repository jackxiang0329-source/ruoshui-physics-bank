# HFDPO-13613

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem hosts a colony of tubeworms anchored to a rigid, vertically oriented chimney structure. The chimney, 12 meters tall and made of brittle sulfide mineral, vibrates due to turbulent fluid flow at frequencies near 0.8 Hz. Engineers detect microfractures at the base and fear resonance could trigger catastrophic collapse. The chimney’s fundamental vibrational mode resembles a standing wave with a node at the base and antinode at the top. If the elastic modulus of the sulfide material is 4.5 GPa and its density is 4200 kg/m³, what is the minimum thickness of the chimney needed to avoid resonance with the fluid excitation, assuming the wave speed in the material governs the natural frequency?

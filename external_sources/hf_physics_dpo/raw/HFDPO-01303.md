# HFDPO-01303

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass operates at variable wind speeds, with blades of length 45 m and mass 12,000 kg distributed uniformly along their length. During a sudden gust, the wind exerts a torque that increases from 0 to 6.2 × 10⁶ N·m over 12 seconds, modeled as τ(t) = 5.17 × 10⁵ t N·m. The turbine’s generator applies a constant opposing torque of 2.1 × 10⁶ N·m once it starts rotating. What is the angular velocity of the blades just after the gust ends, and how much rotational kinetic energy is lost to the generator?

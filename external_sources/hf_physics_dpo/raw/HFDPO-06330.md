# HFDPO-06330

A deep-space probe equipped with a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) using Pu-238 begins its journey to Neptune. After 12 years, engineers detect a 7.3% drop in power output compared to its initial value. The RTG was designed with 3.2 kg of Pu-238, which decays via alpha emission to U-234 with a half-life of 87.7 years. However, recent telemetry shows unexpected heat leakage from the shielding, and the actual power output now lags behind theoretical predictions based on pure decay. Propose a plausible explanation for the discrepancy and calculate the expected power output accounting for both radioactive decay and a 2.1% annual heat loss due to imperfect thermal insulation.

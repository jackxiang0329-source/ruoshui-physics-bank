# HFDPO-10302

A fusion reactor prototype uses a ring-shaped superconducting magnet cooled to 4.2 K to confine plasma at 150 million K. The magnet's critical current density is 1.2 × 10¹⁰ A/m² at 4.2 K, but degrades to 3.0 × 10⁹ A/m² at 6.0 K. If a quench begins and the temperature rises to 5.5 K over 30 seconds due to a partial insulation failure, what is the minimum radius of the NbTi coil windings needed to sustain the required 15 kA current without exceeding the degraded critical current density?

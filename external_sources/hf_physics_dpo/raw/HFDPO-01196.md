# HFDPO-01196

A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new waterproof phone that uses a capacitive touch screen. During testing, engineers notice that the screen becomes unresponsive when the user’s hand is wet. The screen’s sensing circuit relies on a capacitance change of 5 pF to detect a touch. If the wet finger has a surface area of 2 cm² and introduces an effective dielectric (water) with κ = 80, how thick (in mm) must the protective glass layer be to prevent false touches from moisture, assuming the air gap capacitance must stay below 0.1 pF?

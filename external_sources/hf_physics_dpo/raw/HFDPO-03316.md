# HFDPO-03316

A biomedical engineer is designing a magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) gradient coil system that must rapidly switch magnetic fields to spatially encode signals. The coil’s behavior is modeled as an RLC circuit with resistance 1.2 Ω, inductance 85 μH, and capacitance 120 nF. The system is driven by a 2.5 MHz sinusoidal voltage source with amplitude 15 V. To prevent tissue heating, the RMS current must not exceed 3.0 A. Determine whether the system operates safely at resonance, and if not, calculate the frequency shift required to reduce the current to the safe threshold.

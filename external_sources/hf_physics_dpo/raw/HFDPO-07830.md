# HFDPO-07830

In a quantum computing lab, a team observes unexpected fluxoid quantization in a 3D-printed niobium superconducting loop used in a flux qubit. The loop has a diameter of 400 μm and a critical temperature of 9.2 K. During cooldown, a persistent current of 1.3 μA is detected. However, the measured magnetic flux through the loop is 2.5 times the flux quantum (Φ₀ = 2.07×10⁻¹⁵ Wb), not an integer multiple. The team suspects trapped vortices from incomplete Meissner screening during the ramp. What is the most likely number of trapped vortices, and how does this affect the qubit’s coherence time?

# HFDPO-11961

A quantum dot array in a next-generation photonic chip exhibits coupled oscillations due to phonon-mediated electron interactions. Each dot behaves as a nonlinear oscillator with anharmonic potential, and when excited by a laser pulse (50 fs duration, 800 nm wavelength), the system enters a synchronized state only if the inter-dot coupling exceeds a critical threshold. Given a measured oscillation frequency of 1.2 THz at low excitation and 1.4 THz at high excitation due to nonlinear stiffening, determine the effective anharmonicity coefficient and assess the required coupling strength to achieve phase synchronization across 16 dots arranged in a ring.

# HFDPO-05506

A wind farm developer must determine whether retrofitting older turbines with blade-tip vortex dampers—designed to reduce aerodynamic noise and fatigue—will improve net energy output. Each damper reduces blade tip speed by 12% due to increased drag, but also reduces turbulence-induced vibrations by 40%. The original turbine produced 2.1 MW at 12 m/s wind speed, with 43% efficiency. With dampers, efficiency drops to 39% due to drag, but operational lifespan increases from 15 to 22 years due to reduced stress. Calculate the total energy yield over 20 years for both configurations, assuming wind speed distribution follows a Weibull model with shape parameter k = 2.1 and scale parameter c = 10.5 m/s. Which design yields more usable energy?

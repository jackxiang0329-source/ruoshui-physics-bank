# HFDPO-09230

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable hover at 20,000 meters, where air density is only 0.08 kg/m³. Its rigid, asymmetrical carbon-fiber frame supports two rotors of unequal size (diameter 1.2 m and 0.8 m), positioned 1.5 m apart along the longitudinal axis. Due to a manufacturing defect, the smaller rotor generates 12% less thrust than intended at the same power input. Determine the required shift in the center of mass (horizontal offset from the centerline) to prevent torque-induced tilt, assuming the drone’s total mass is 8.5 kg and gravitational acceleration is 9.75 m/s².

# HFDPO-00422

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar-powered energy system uses a 48 V DC battery bank to run sensitive climate sensors, but during a prolonged polar night, the system relies on a backup fuel cell stack rated at 45 V output. The sensors require 12 V at 2.5 A, but the station’s only available DC-DC converter has a non-linear efficiency curve: it’s 92% efficient at 10 A output, but drops to 78% at 1.5 A due to internal leakage currents. Design a circuit using two identical 10 Ω resistors and the converter to deliver exactly 12 V to the sensors while minimizing power loss in the system. What must the load resistance be, and how should the resistors be connected?

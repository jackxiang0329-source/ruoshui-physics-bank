# HFDPO-03038

A wind farm in a coastal region experiences inconsistent energy output despite consistent wind speeds. Engineers discover that turbulence from nearby cliffs alters airflow over turbine blades, reducing efficiency. The turbines are designed for laminar flow, but at a critical height, the air velocity increases by 30% due to a constriction effect. If the ambient pressure at turbine height is 101.3 kPa and air density is 1.225 kg/m³, what is the pressure drop across the blade section where velocity increases from 12 m/s to 15.6 m/s? How might this pressure variation impact blade stress and power generation?

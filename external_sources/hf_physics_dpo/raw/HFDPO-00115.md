# HFDPO-00115

A laser printer's resolution is limited by diffraction as the beam passes through a tiny aperture to form tiny dots on a photosensitive drum. If the printer uses a 532 nm green laser and the aperture diameter is 0.1 mm, what is the minimum distance between two dots that can still be resolved on the drum? Assume the laser beam is diffraction-limited and the image is formed at a distance of 20 cm from the aperture.

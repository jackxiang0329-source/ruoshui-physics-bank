# HFDPO-02618

A deep-space asteroid mining probe uses a rotating drill bit to extract regolith from a rapidly spinning near-Earth asteroid (diameter 200 m, rotation period 4.5 hours). To prevent the drill from destabilizing the probe’s orientation, engineers must calculate the minimum angular velocity of the drill’s counter-rotating stabilizer ring (mass 120 kg, radius 1.8 m) such that the probe’s total angular momentum remains zero relative to the inertial frame. The drill bit has a moment of inertia of 3.2 kg·m² and spins at 50 rad/s clockwise. What must the stabilizer ring’s angular speed be—magnitude and direction?

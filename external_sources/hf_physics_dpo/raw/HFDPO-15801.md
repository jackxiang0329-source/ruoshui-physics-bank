# HFDPO-15801

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole filled with conductive brine to extract heat, but engineers notice unexpected eddy currents in the steel casing, causing localized heating and reduced efficiency. The casing, 200 m long and 0.15 m in radius, has a resistivity of 1.7 × 10⁻⁷ Ω·m and is exposed to a time-varying horizontal magnetic field from nearby tectonic motion, modeled as B(t) = B₀ sin(ωt), where B₀ = 0.2 T and ω = 0.05 rad/s. Calculate the total power dissipated in the casing due to induced eddy currents, assuming the field is uniform across the casing cross-section and neglecting skin depth effects. Use Maxwell’s equations and Faraday’s law in integral form.

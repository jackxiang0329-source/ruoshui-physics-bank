# HFDPO-02109

A desert explorer finds a frozen lake at dawn, its surface covered in a thin layer of frost. As the sun rises, the frost disappears rapidly—yet the temperature remains below 0°C. The lake is 6.0 cm deep, and the frost layer has a mass of 2.5 kg spread over 10 m². Using only a thermometer and a stopwatch, she estimates the latent heat of sublimation is 2.8 × 10⁶ J/kg. How much energy per second (in W/m²) must the sun supply to explain the observed sublimation rate?

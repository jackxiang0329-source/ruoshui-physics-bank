# HFDPO-05461

A satellite in a circular low-Earth orbit at 300 km altitude experiences a slight atmospheric drag, causing its orbital radius to decrease at a rate of 1.2 km/year. If the satellite’s initial orbital speed is 7.7 km/s, what is the rate of change of its orbital energy, and how does this compare to the mechanical power dissipated by drag? Ignore solar radiation pressure and assume Earth’s mass is 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg and radius 6371 km.

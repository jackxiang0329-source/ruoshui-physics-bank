# HFDPO-14624

During a deep-sea research expedition, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, rigid cylindrical structure used for ocean thermal energy conversion (OTEC). The cylinder, 12 m long and 2.5 m in diameter, is anchored vertically with its top 80 m below the surface. The fluid above it is seawater (density 1025 kg/m³), while the fluid inside the cylinder is a lighter, stable brine layer (density 1040 kg/m³). The cylinder is sealed at both ends, but the internal brine is in equilibrium with the external seawater through a porous valve at the midpoint. When the ROV attempts to retrieve a sensor from the cylinder’s upper end, the valve becomes blocked. Calculate the net force on the top cap due to fluid pressure imbalance and explain whether the cap is likely to fail under this condition.

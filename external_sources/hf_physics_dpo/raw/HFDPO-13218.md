# HFDPO-13218

A deep-sea research submersible descends to a hydrothermal vent field at 2,500 meters below the surface, where it must stabilize while deploying a sensitive sensor array on a flexible, 12-meter tether. The tether has a linear mass density of 0.8 kg/m and is attached to the sub at a 15° angle from vertical. Due to ocean current shear, the lower 4 meters of the tether experience a horizontal drag force of 22 N/m. The sub's thrusters can apply forces only along its longitudinal axis. Determine the minimum thruster force needed to maintain static equilibrium and the tension at the connection point with the sub.

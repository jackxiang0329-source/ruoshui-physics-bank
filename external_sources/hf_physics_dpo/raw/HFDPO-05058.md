# HFDPO-05058

A deep-space communications array on Mars receives a faint, polarized signal from an orbiting probe using a 1.5 GHz microwave link. The signal experiences phase shifts and amplitude fading due to ionospheric scintillation from a solar storm, with observed intensity fluctuations of 12% over 20 seconds. The array’s receive antenna has a 3 dB beamwidth of 1.2°, and the signal’s polarization is elliptical, with a major axis tilted 30° from vertical. Estimate the minimum required signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) at the receiver to maintain reliable data transmission, considering both multipath interference and polarization mismatch, and determine how much power must be transmitted if the receiver sensitivity is −110 dBm and the path loss is 200 dB.

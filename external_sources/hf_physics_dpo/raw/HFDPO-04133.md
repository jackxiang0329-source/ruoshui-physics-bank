# HFDPO-04133

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole (depth 2.5 km) to extract heat from magma chambers, with a superconducting loop of 1.2 km² cross-sectional area suspended in the borehole's magnetic field. Due to slow thermal expansion of the surrounding rock, the loop's position drifts downward at 0.8 mm/min. The ambient magnetic field (0.5 mT) is weak but stable and aligned vertically. The plant’s control system detects induced currents in the loop and must shut down if the EMF exceeds 50 V to prevent superconducting quenching. Should the system trigger a shutdown under these conditions?

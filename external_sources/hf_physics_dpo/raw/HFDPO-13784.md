# HFDPO-13784

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent for a coronary artery that undergoes cyclic dilation due to pulsatile blood flow. The stent is made of a nitinol alloy with a Young’s modulus of 70 GPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.3. The artery’s inner radius varies from 3.5 mm to 4.2 mm during a cardiac cycle. To prevent vessel damage, the stent must exert a radial stress no greater than 0.15 MPa across its entire expansion range. Using the theory of thick-walled cylinders under internal pressure, determine the minimum wall thickness required for the stent to remain within safe stress limits during full expansion.

# HFDPO-09424

In a quantum dot array used for low-temperature quantum computing, 128 identical dots are coupled to a shared phonon bath at 20 mK. Each dot can occupy one of three states: ground (0), excited (1), or entangled (2), with energies 0, 1.2 meV, and 2.5 meV respectively. Due to strong inter-dot coupling, the system exhibits non-Poissonian energy fluctuations. Experimental data shows a measured average energy per dot of 0.8 meV, yet the naive Boltzmann model predicts 0.5 meV. What is the effective temperature of the system, and how does the entangled state population affect the entropy compared to a canonical ensemble?

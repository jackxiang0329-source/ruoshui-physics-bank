# HFDPO-01721

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating a synthetic oil to 400°C. During a sudden cloud cover event, the system must rapidly shut down to prevent thermal stress. If the receiver tube (3 m long, 5 cm diameter, 2 mm thick) is made of stainless steel (k = 16 W/m·K, α = 4.2×10⁻⁶ m²/s) and cools from 400°C to 150°C in 15 minutes due to forced air convection (h = 25 W/m²·K), determine whether the thermal gradient across the tube wall could induce yielding (yield strength = 250 MPa) due to thermal stress. Assume uniform initial temperature and negligible radiation.

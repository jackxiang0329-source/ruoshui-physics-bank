# HFDPO-12047

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses a novel modular array design where 12 identical photovoltaic panels are wired in a complex mesh of parallel and series branches, interconnected via flexible copper busbars. During peak sunlight, one panel develops a microcrack, reducing its output to 75% of nominal (45 W instead of 60 W) and increasing its internal resistance to 0.8 Ω. The system's monitoring software detects a 12% drop in total power output. Using Kirchhoff’s laws, determine how the current distribution across the mesh changed and calculate the new current through the faulty panel. Assume all good panels remain at 60 W, 0.2 Ω internal resistance, and the load draws a constant 15 A total.

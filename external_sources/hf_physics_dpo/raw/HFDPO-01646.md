# HFDPO-01646

A driver notices their car’s windshield suddenly causes glare from oncoming headlights at night, making it hard to see. They recall that polarized sunglasses reduce such glare, but wonder if the windshield itself is polarizing light. They test it by rotating a pair of polarized sunglasses in front of the windshield while looking at reflected light from the road ahead. At what angle between the sunglasses’ transmission axis and the reflected light’s polarization direction will the glare be minimized?

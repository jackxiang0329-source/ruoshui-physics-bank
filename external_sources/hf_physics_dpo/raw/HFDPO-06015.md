# HFDPO-06015

A fusion reactor prototype uses a toroidal plasma confined by a complex magnetic field generated by 12 superconducting coils arranged symmetrically around the chamber. Each coil carries 15 kA and is modeled as a circular loop of radius 2.0 m. During a diagnostic test, sensors detect a radial magnetic field of 0.85 T at the center of the plasma (1.2 m from the toroidal axis), but the expected field from ideal symmetry is 1.0 T. Engineers suspect a misalignment in one coil, shifting its center by 0.15 m off-axis. Estimate the deviation in the net magnetic field at the center due to this misalignment, using Biot-Savart law and symmetry arguments.

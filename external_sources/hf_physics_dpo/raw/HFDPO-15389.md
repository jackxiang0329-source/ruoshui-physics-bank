# HFDPO-15389

The drone’s total mass is 18.4 kg, and each propeller generates 42.3 N of thrust. Determine whether the tilt is caused by unbalanced moments due to aerodynamic drag or a design flaw in the frame’s torsional stiffness. Evaluate the moment equilibrium conditions and assess the plausibility of each cause using actual data from wind tunnel simulations showing a drag coefficient of 0.32 at the current altitude.

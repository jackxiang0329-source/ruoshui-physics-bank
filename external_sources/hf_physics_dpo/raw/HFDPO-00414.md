# HFDPO-00414

A mountain village relies on a seasonal ice reservoir to supply drinking water during dry months. The reservoir, insulated with a 0.3 m layer of snow and compacted ice, holds 50,000 kg of ice at 0°C. During a 10-day warm spell, the average ambient temperature rises to 5°C, and solar radiation delivers 300 W/m² to the ice surface (20% is reflected). The reservoir has a surface area of 100 m². How much ice remains at the end of the 10 days? Assume heat transfer occurs only by conduction through snow and convection from air, and ignore latent heat loss from sublimation.

# HFDPO-11692

A laser-based interferometric sensor in a fusion reactor diagnostic system detects phase shifts in a 632.8 nm He-Ne laser beam passing through a turbulent plasma column with fluctuating electron density. The beam traverses a 1.2 m path, and a sudden 0.42π phase shift is observed over 150 μs. Given that the refractive index of the plasma is modified by electron density via n = 1 - frac{omega_p^2}{2omega^2} , where omega_p = sqrt{frac{n_e e^2}{epsilon_0 m_e}} , determine the root-mean-square fluctuation in electron density Delta n_e that caused this phase shift. Assume the beam is sensitive only to line-of-sight density variations.

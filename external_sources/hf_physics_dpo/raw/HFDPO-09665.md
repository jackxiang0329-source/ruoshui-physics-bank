# HFDPO-09665

A biomedical engineer is designing a portable device to detect early-stage skin cancer using fluorescence imaging. Healthy skin emits weak blue fluorescence (λ = 450 nm) when illuminated with UV light (λ = 365 nm), but cancerous tissue shows enhanced fluorescence at 520 nm. To isolate the target signal, the device uses a narrowband filter with a central wavelength of 520 nm and a bandwidth of 10 nm. If the UV light source emits 20 mW of power, what minimum transmission efficiency must the filter have to ensure at least 1 μW of 520 nm light reaches the detector, assuming all 365 nm light is converted uniformly to 520 nm in the cancerous region?

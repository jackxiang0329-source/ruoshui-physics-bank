# HFDPO-11504

An offshore wind farm uses 50 Hz AC transmission lines to deliver power to a floating substation, but engineers notice severe voltage distortion and reactive power oscillations during storms. The substation’s transformer has a leakage inductance of 1.2 mH and resistance of 0.3 Ω, and is connected to a 10 kV (RMS) grid through a 150 m cable with distributed inductance of 1.8 μH/m and resistance of 0.02 Ω/m. During a surge, the grid current waveform exhibits a 15% third harmonic component. Evaluate whether the system’s resonant frequency aligns with the harmonic frequency, and determine the impact on power quality—assuming the cable acts as a distributed L-R line.

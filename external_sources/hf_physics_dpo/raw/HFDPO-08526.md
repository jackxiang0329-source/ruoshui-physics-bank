# HFDPO-08526

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses a network of 120 identical photovoltaic panels, each with an internal resistance of 0.3 Ω and an open-circuit voltage of 36 V. During peak sun, each panel delivers 28 A. The panels are wired in a hybrid configuration: 10 parallel strings of 12 panels in series. A technician notices the system’s output drops by 42% under high load. Find the resistance of the load that causes this drop and explain why the voltage sags significantly despite the panels’ high current capacity.

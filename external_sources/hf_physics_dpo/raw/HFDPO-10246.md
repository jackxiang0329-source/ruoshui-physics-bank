# HFDPO-10246

If the energy confinement time τ_E is measured at 2.1 s, but the reactor’s magnetic field fluctuates, causing a 12% reduction in effective confinement, is the system achieving ignition? Evaluate whether the reactor can sustain fusion under these conditions.

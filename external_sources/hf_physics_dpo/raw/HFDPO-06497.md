# HFDPO-06497

In a quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit is shielded within a cylindrical enclosure made of high-permeability magnetic alloy, but stray electric fields from nearby control electronics cause decoherence. The enclosure has inner radius 5 cm, outer radius 10 cm, and is uniformly charged with a volume charge density that varies radially as ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − r/R₀), where R₀ = 10 cm and ρ₀ = 2.0 × 10⁻⁹ C/m³. The qubit sits at r = 3 cm. Determine the magnitude of the electric field at the qubit’s location due to the shielding layers alone, using Gauss’s Law.

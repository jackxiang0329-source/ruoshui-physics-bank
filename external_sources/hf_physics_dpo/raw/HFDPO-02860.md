# HFDPO-02860

As the tilt alters the magnetic flux through the coils, a transient current is induced that must be suppressed by active feedback systems before it destabilizes the levitation. Determine the maximum induced EMF in a single coil during the tilt, assuming it occurs over 0.08 seconds.

# HFDPO-02971

A protein folds into a stable structure in water at 310 K, where the folded state has 1000 available microstates and the unfolded state has 5000. The energy difference between the folded and unfolded states is ΔE = –25 kJ/mol. What is the ratio of the probability of finding the protein folded versus unfolded, and what does this imply about the dominant contribution—entropy or energy—to folding stability?

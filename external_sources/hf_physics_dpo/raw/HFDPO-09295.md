# HFDPO-09295

In a remote solar-powered weather station, a network of three interconnected battery banks (A, B, C) with internal resistances of 0.15 Ω, 0.20 Ω, and 0.10 Ω respectively, supplies power to a control module and a data transmitter. Determine the actual current supplied by each battery when a fault causes a 0.5 V drop in battery B’s effective voltage due to aging.

# HFDPO-14691

In a high-altitude atmospheric research balloon, a novel sensor array uses parallel-plate capacitors to measure ionospheric electron density fluctuations. The sensor operates at a fixed voltage of 12 V. At 25 km, the charge on the capacitor is measured as 6.8 nC. What is the effective capacitance at that altitude, and how does the dielectric compression affect the system’s sensitivity to electron density changes?

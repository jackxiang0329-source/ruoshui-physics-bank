# HFDPO-12646

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent system on the Mid-Atlantic Ridge releases superheated water at 400°C into the surrounding ocean at 2°C. Engineers propose harvesting energy from this thermal gradient using a closed-loop organic Rankine cycle with isobutane as the working fluid. Assume the cycle operates between 400°C and 2°C with 85% isentropic efficiency for the turbine and 90% for the pump.

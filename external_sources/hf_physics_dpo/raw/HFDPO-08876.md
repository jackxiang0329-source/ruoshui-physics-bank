# HFDPO-08876

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 1.2 km long, 5 cm diameter copper coil to harvest heat-induced eddy currents from the surrounding hot rock (resistivity ~1.5 Ω·m, relative permeability ~1.1). As magma pulses beneath the site cause localized temperature gradients, the rock’s conductivity changes over time, inducing a measurable EMF in the coil. Given that the magnetic field component due to thermal currents varies sinusoidally at 0.5 Hz with peak amplitude 8 × 10⁻⁶ T, and considering only the axial component of the field through the coil, what is the peak induced EMF in the coil? Assume the coil has 400 turns and neglect self-inductance.

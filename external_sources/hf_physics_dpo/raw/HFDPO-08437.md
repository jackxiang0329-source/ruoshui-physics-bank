# HFDPO-08437

A remote Arctic research station relies on a buried copper cable (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, diameter 4 mm) to transmit 12 kW of power from a diesel generator 2.5 km away. The system operates at 480 V, but ambient soil temperature averages -15°C, causing the cable’s resistance to fluctuate. If the cable’s resistance increases 20% in winter due to thermal contraction and increased material stiffness, what is the minimum power the generator must supply to maintain 12 kW at the station? Assume the cable’s temperature coefficient of resistivity is 3.9×10⁻³ /°C and the cable’s initial temperature is 0°C.

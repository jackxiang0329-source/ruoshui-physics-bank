# HFDPO-12670

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe unexpected decoherence in a superconducting qubit fabricated from a thin film of NbTi alloy. At 15 mK, the qubit’s relaxation time drops from 10 μs to 2 μs when a 100 G transverse magnetic field is applied—despite the material being in the BCS superconducting state. The researchers suspect vortex pinning at grain boundaries is disrupting Cooper pair coherence. Using measured values: vortex core energy ~4.8 eV, inter-vortex spacing ~50 nm, and electron mean free path ~30 nm, determine whether vortex proliferation or phase-fluctuation-induced decoherence dominates under these conditions.

# HFDPO-10471

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden magnetic field failure, causing a 120 kg plasma cloud (initially at 150 million K and confined in a toroidal chamber) to expand radially outward into a 10 m radius buffer region at near-zero pressure. Estimate the final temperature of the plasma after expansion, assuming conservation of energy and momentum, and assess whether the expansion-induced shockwave could damage surrounding superconducting coils located 2.5 m from the chamber wall.

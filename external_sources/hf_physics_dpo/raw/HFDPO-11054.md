# HFDPO-11054

A deep-sea submersible’s manipulator arm, designed to retrieve a damaged sensor array from a hydrothermal vent field, must extend 3.2 m horizontally while maintaining stability against turbulent flows. The arm is a rigid, 280 kg aluminum cantilever, pinned at the base but not anchored, and experiences a drag force of 1,950 N at its tip due to high-velocity vent effluent. The arm’s center of mass is 1.4 m from the pivot. If the arm’s base can only resist a maximum horizontal reaction force of 1,400 N before slipping, what is the minimum allowable coefficient of static friction between the submersible’s hull and the surrounding sediment to prevent failure during operation?

# HFDPO-02178

A concert hall designer must optimize sound clarity for a new 2,500-seat venue using only reflective panels and absorptive materials. The main stage emits a broad-spectrum sound wave (100 Hz to 12 kHz), but early reflections from the side walls cause destructive interference at 600 Hz, creating a "dead zone" in the 3rd balcony. Given the distance from stage to balcony edge is 32 m, and the speed of sound is 343 m/s, determine the minimum thickness of a curved acoustic panel (density 800 kg/m³, speed of sound in material 1,600 m/s) needed to act as a quarter-wave absorber specifically at 600 Hz when mounted flush with the wall.

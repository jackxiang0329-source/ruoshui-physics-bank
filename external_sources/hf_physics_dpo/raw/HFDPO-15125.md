# HFDPO-15125

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research experiences unexpected voltage drops during dusk, despite fully charged batteries. The onboard system uses a silicon p-n junction photodiode array (bandgap 1.1 eV) to monitor sunlight intensity. At 18:00 local time, ambient temperature drops from 25°C to 5°C, and light intensity decreases to 10% of peak. The photodiode’s reverse saturation current doubles for every 10°C rise. Calculate the change in open-circuit voltage of a single diode junction under these conditions, assuming ideal diode behavior and constant photogeneration. How does this affect the system’s power management logic?

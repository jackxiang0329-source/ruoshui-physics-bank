# HFDPO-07071

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using inertial focusing in a serpentine channel. The channel has a rectangular cross-section (50 μm × 100 μm), and blood flows at a Reynolds number of 0.3, dominated by viscous forces. The device must maintain a stable cell focusing position despite small fluctuations in flow rate (±5%). How does the pressure drop across a 2 cm long channel segment change if the flow rate increases by 5%, and what is the effect on the lateral migration force on a 12 μm diameter CTC modeled as a neutrally buoyant sphere?

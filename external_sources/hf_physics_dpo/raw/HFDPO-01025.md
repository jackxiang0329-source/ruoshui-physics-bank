# HFDPO-01025

A student is designing a small magnetic latch for a solar-powered rover meant to operate on Mars. The latch must hold a panel closed under Martian gravity (3.7 m/s²) using only a permanent magnet. If the panel weighs 0.8 kg and the coefficient of static friction between the magnet and metal housing is 0.4, what minimum magnetic force is needed to keep the panel from opening when the rover tilts?

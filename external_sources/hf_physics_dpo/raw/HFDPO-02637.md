# HFDPO-02637

A deep-space probe, traveling at 0.95c relative to Earth, transmits a 10-minute burst of data (in its rest frame) to a ground station. Due to relativistic time dilation and the probe's distance, the signal arrives compressed in time. The ground station receives the signal over only 3.2 minutes. Simultaneously, a cosmic ray muon, created at 50 km altitude, reaches the surface in 2.0 μs (Earth frame). Explain the apparent contradiction in the probe’s signal duration and the muon’s survival despite both involving relativistic motion. What is the proper time interval for the muon’s decay in its rest frame?

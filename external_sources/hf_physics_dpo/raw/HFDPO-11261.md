# HFDPO-11261

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 10 cm diameter silicon wafer (σ = −2.5 × 10⁻⁶ C/m²) is suspended electrostatically between two parallel plates (separation: 5 mm) charged to ±150 V. A 2 μm-diameter dust particle (ρ = 2500 kg/m³, q = +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) drifts into the gap at 3 cm/s from the negatively charged plate. At what distance from the negative plate will the particle reverse direction before reaching the wafer?

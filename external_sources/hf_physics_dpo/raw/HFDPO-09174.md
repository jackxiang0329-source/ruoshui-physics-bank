# HFDPO-09174

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 12 cm-long, cylindrical silicon wafer (diameter 300 mm) is suspended by electrostatic forces between two parallel, conductive plates maintained at ±150 V. The wafer, initially uncharged, develops a non-uniform surface charge due to ion implantation during processing, with a surface charge density modeled as σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r²/R²), where r is radial distance from the center and R = 15 cm. Calculate the net electrostatic force on the wafer and determine whether this force tends to stabilize or destabilize its position.

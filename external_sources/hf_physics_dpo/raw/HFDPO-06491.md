# HFDPO-06491

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm-diameter spherical silicon wafer (dielectric constant εᵣ = 11.7) accumulates a charge density of 8.5 × 10⁻⁸ C/m² due to ion bombardment during plasma etching. A nearby 3.0 cm-diameter grounded electrode is positioned 2.0 mm from the wafer’s surface. To prevent electrostatic discharge (ESD) that could damage micro-circuitry, what is the minimum capacitance required between the wafer and the electrode system to limit the potential rise on the wafer to 50 V before discharge occurs? Assume the system acts as a parallel-plate capacitor with fringing effects neglected.

# HFDPO-03161

A cryogenic storage tank for liquid helium-4 in a quantum computing lab develops a small leak, allowing atmospheric air to slowly seep in. The tank's volume is 50 L, initially filled with 40 L of liquid He-4 at 1.8 K and 1 atm. After 2 hours, the system reaches a new dynamic equilibrium where the pressure stabilizes at 1.05 atm, and the temperature is maintained at 1.8 K. Assuming air behaves as a mixture of 78% N₂ and 22% O₂, and the vapor pressure of He-4 over liquid is 1.1 atm at 1.8 K, what fraction of the final volume is occupied by liquid helium, and how much of the tank’s internal volume is now gas-phase air?

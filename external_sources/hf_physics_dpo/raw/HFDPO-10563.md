# HFDPO-10563

During a dust storm on Mars, a 1.2-meter-tall robotic drill becomes electrically charged due to triboelectric effects with airborne silicate particles. The drill’s cylindrical body (radius 0.15 m, height 1.2 m) acquires a non-uniform surface charge density that varies with height as σ(h) = σ₀(1 + 0.5 sin(πh/H)), where h is height from the base, H = 1.2 m, and σ₀ = 5 × 10⁻⁹ C/m². Determine the total electric potential at a point 0.3 m radially outward from the drill’s central axis, located at mid-height (h = 0.6 m). Assume the drill is isolated and the surrounding atmosphere is a vacuum.

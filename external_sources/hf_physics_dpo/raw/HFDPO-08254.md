# HFDPO-08254

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a custom 12-meter titanium alloy boom to suspend a 45-ton drilling head at a 35° angle from vertical. The boom is pinned at the base and supported by a hydraulic actuator angled at 60° to the horizontal, attached 3 meters from the pivot. During a sudden pressure surge, the actuator fails mid-operation. Engineers must instantly reconfigure the system using two temporary steel cables anchored to the ground 8 meters from the base, at 45° and 75° to the horizontal, to prevent the boom from toppling before repairs. Determine the minimum tensile strength required in each cable to maintain equilibrium.

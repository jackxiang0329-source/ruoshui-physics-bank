# HFDPO-02182

A marine biologist is using a fiber-optic probe to study bioluminescent plankton at 15 meters depth. The probe’s core has a refractive index of 1.60, and the surrounding cladding has a refractive index of 1.45. The biologist notices that light from the plankton is not reaching the detector when the probe is tilted beyond 30° from vertical. What is the maximum angle of incidence at the core-cladding interface for total internal reflection to occur, and why does the signal drop when the probe exceeds this angle?

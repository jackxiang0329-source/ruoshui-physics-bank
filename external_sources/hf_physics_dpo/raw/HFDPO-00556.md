# HFDPO-00556

A high-altitude research balloon carrying a cryogenic sensor payload ascends through the stratosphere, where the atmospheric pressure drops to 20 kPa and the temperature falls to 220 K. The sensor is sealed in a spherical titanium vessel with a 0.3 m radius, initially filled with argon gas at 100 kPa and 273 K. As the balloon rises, the vessel's rigid walls prevent volume change, but the gas inside is thermally isolated. Calculate the final pressure inside the vessel after ascent, and determine whether the pressure change alone could induce a structural stress exceeding the yield strength of titanium (880 MPa).

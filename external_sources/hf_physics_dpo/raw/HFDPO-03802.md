# HFDPO-03802

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable flight over a remote mountain range. The drone’s total weight is 32 kg, and its center of mass is initially aligned with the frame’s geometric center. What is the minimum distance from the centerline where the mass must be placed to achieve rotational equilibrium, assuming the frame’s moment of inertia is negligible and gravity is 9.8 m/s²?

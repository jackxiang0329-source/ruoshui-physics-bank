# HFDPO-01946

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels mounted at a fixed 30° tilt to maximize annual energy capture. However, glare from the panels during midday causes visual discomfort and potential safety hazards for nearby vehicles. Engineers estimate that unpolarized sunlight reflects off the glass surface at a 58° angle, and the glare is most intense when the reflected light is horizontally polarized. What angle should the polarizing filters in the protective visors of maintenance drones be oriented to minimize this glare?

# HFDPO-10218

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) equipped with a robotic arm retrieves a 120-kg cylindrical titanium sample from a hydrothermal vent field, where water pressure exceeds 30 MPa and temperature reaches 400°C. As the ROV ascends, the sample expands slightly due to decompression, causing a 0.8% increase in volume. The ROV’s power system must maintain stability during ascent despite the changing buoyant force. If the ROV’s thrusters counteract forces in real time, what is the net impulse delivered by the thrusters during the 60-second ascent, assuming the sample’s material properties and seawater density (1025 kg/m³) are constant, and the ROV starts and ends at rest?

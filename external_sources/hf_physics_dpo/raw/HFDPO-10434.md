# HFDPO-10434

In a cryogenic storage facility for quantum computing qubits, a superconducting niobium film (thickness 50 nm) is cooled below 9.2 K to enter the superconducting phase. During cooldown, a localized thermal fluctuation causes a 10 nm × 10 nm region to briefly exceed the critical temperature due to a microdefect in the substrate. Analyze how the resulting phase boundary propagates and whether vortex nucleation will occur at the edge of the superconducting domain, given that the magnetic field at the boundary remains at 1.2 mT and the upper critical field is 14.5 mT.

# HFDPO-01214

A marine biologist is deploying an autonomous underwater vehicle (AUV) to monitor coral reef health using sonar, but notices erratic signal reflections near a submerged volcanic ridge. The AUV emits a 12.5 kHz pulse, and the returning signal from the ridge shows a Doppler shift corresponding to a perceived frequency of 12.48 kHz due to the AUV’s forward motion. The ridge is 800 m away, and water temperature varies linearly from 15°C at the surface to 5°C at 400 m depth, where the ridge lies. The AUV is traveling at 3.2 m/s at a depth of 400 m. What is the actual speed of sound at the ridge depth, and how does it affect the calculated distance if the biologist assumes a constant sound speed of 1500 m/s?

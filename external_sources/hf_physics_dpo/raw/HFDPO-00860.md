# HFDPO-00860

A rural off-grid solar installation uses a 24V battery bank to power essential loads. During a week of low sunlight, the system’s charge controller fails, and the battery discharges slowly through a hidden fault—unintentional leakage through a degraded insulation layer in a 120 m copper wire run between a solar panel and the battery. Measurements show a steady 0.8 A current flowing from the battery even when no loads are connected. What is the resistance of the fault path? Assuming the fault is due to insulation breakdown across the wire’s outer sheath, estimate the resistivity of the moisture-contaminated path if it spans a 1 mm gap across a 2 cm diameter wire.

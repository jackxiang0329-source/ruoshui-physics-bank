# HFDPO-15861

A solar-powered drone, designed for high-altitude atmospheric monitoring, must ascend from 2,000 m to 15,000 m over 4 hours while maintaining a constant horizontal speed of 30 m/s. Its mass is 12 kg, and it experiences air resistance proportional to the square of its speed, with a drag coefficient of 0.25 kg/m. The solar panels generate 320 W of power during the ascent, but efficiency drops by 15% due to atmospheric scattering. How much mechanical energy is stored in the drone’s battery at the peak if it started with 10 kJ of stored energy?

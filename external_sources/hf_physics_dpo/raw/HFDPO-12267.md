# HFDPO-12267

A high-efficiency solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat ~1.5 kJ/kg·K) stored in a cylindrical insulated tank (diameter 10 m, height 12 m) to generate electricity during nighttime. At peak solar input, salt is heated to 560°C from 300°C over 6 hours. However, during a 10-hour night, the plant must maintain minimum output temperature of 520°C. Given the tank’s surface emittance is 0.92, ambient temperature averages 15°C, and convective heat transfer coefficient is 12 W/m²·K, determine whether the salt will remain above 520°C by morning. Assume negligible temperature gradients in the salt and that heat loss dominates only through the tank’s curved and top surfaces.

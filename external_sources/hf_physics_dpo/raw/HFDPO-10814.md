# HFDPO-10814

During a solar storm, a satellite in geostationary orbit accumulates negative charge at a rate of 5.0 × 10⁻⁹ C/s due to electron flux from the magnetosphere. The satellite’s surface is coated with a dielectric layer (εᵣ = 4.5) 1.2 mm thick, and its effective surface area is 8.5 m². To prevent arcing to a nearby uncharged metallic antenna 25 cm away, the potential difference must stay below 1.2 kV. Determine the minimum resistivity of the dielectric coating required to maintain safe voltage levels, assuming uniform charge distribution and negligible charge leakage elsewhere.

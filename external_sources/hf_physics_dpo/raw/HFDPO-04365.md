# HFDPO-04365

During a deep-sea mining operation, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) must navigate a plume of fine sediment suspended in seawater, rising from a disturbed seafloor vent at 1200 m depth. The plume forms a conical jet with a 15° half-angle, carrying 40 kg/s of sediment (density 2650 kg/m³) in water (density 1025 kg/m³, viscosity 1.2 cP). The ROV must stay 10 m above the vent to avoid engulfment. Using only measurable parameters—average velocity at 10 m height and plume width—how can one estimate the entrainment coefficient and assess whether the plume’s momentum flux is sufficient to maintain vertical lift against gravity and viscous drag?

# HFDPO-00485

During a solar storm, a geomagnetic disturbance induces currents in a 500 km long undersea power cable spanning the North Atlantic, with a resistivity of 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m and a cross-sectional area of 1.2 × 10⁻² m². Determine the magnitude and direction of the induced electric field along the cable and assess whether the resulting current could cause thermal degradation, given a maximum safe current density of 12 A/m².

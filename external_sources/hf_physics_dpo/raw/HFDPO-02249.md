# HFDPO-02249

A deep-sea mining drone descends at a constant speed through a stratified ocean layer, where water density increases nonlinearly with depth due to pressure and temperature gradients. The drone’s thrusters provide a variable upward force to counteract buoyancy and drag, both of which change with depth. At 2,000 m, the drone experiences 12,500 N of drag and 18,000 N of buoyancy; at 3,000 m, drag rises to 16,200 N and buoyancy to 21,500 N. The drone’s mass is 1,200 kg and it maintains a steady descent. What must be the magnitude and direction of the net force from the thrusters at 2,500 m, given that drag and buoyancy vary linearly with depth in this region?

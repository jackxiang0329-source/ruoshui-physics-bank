# HFDPO-02590

A planetary probe descends through Titan’s dense atmosphere using a spinning, rigid rotor-wing system for stabilization and controlled descent. The rotor, with mass 12 kg and radius 0.8 m, spins at 450 rpm about its central axis. As atmospheric drag increases, the rotor experiences a torque of 5.2 N·m opposing its spin. If the probe needs to maintain angular momentum within ±0.5% of its initial value for inertial navigation accuracy over a 90-second descent, what minimum initial angular speed (in rad/s) must the rotor have to compensate for drag-induced angular deceleration?

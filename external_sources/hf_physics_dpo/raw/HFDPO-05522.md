# HFDPO-05522

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat from concentrated sunlight, maintaining a reservoir at 560°C. During a prolonged cloud cover event, the system must rely solely on stored energy to generate electricity for 8 hours. The plant's heat engine operates with a cold reservoir at 120°C and has a maximum theoretical efficiency of 48%. If the stored salt has a heat capacity of 1.5 kJ/kg·K and a mass of 12,000 kg, and the system loses heat to the environment at a rate proportional to the temperature difference (k = 0.015 kW/K), what is the maximum amount of work the plant can deliver during the outage? Assume the salt cools uniformly and the engine operates continuously at its maximum efficiency.

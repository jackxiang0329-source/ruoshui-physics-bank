# HFDPO-00908

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that qubits made from superconducting circuits lose coherence in under 10 microseconds when exposed to low-frequency electromagnetic noise from nearby cryogenic pumps. The noise spectrum peaks at 1.2 GHz, matching the energy splitting of a Josephson junction qubit. Assume the qubit operates at 15 mK and the phonon density of states follows a Debye model with a Debye temperature of 400 K.

# HFDPO-00086

A wind farm in coastal Oregon experiences fluctuating power output due to turbulent gusts. Each turbine has a 120-meter blade span and converts 40% of kinetic energy from wind into electrical energy. During a sudden storm, wind speed increases from 10 m/s to 25 m/s over 10 seconds. If the air density is 1.2 kg/m³ and the turbine’s rotor area is fully exposed, by what factor does the power output change? Assume the turbine’s control system maintains rotor speed constant—what mechanical torque must be absorbed by the gearbox to prevent overspeed?

# HFDPO-01449

The channel is lined with a hydrogel layer (10 μm thick) that exhibits non-Newtonian shear-thinning behavior, with power-law index n = 0.7 and consistency index K = 0.02 Pa·sⁿ. Estimate the pressure gradient required to sustain this flow, and evaluate whether the hydrogel layer's deformation under shear could alter the effective channel geometry and affect droplet focusing.

# HFDPO-09753

During a deep-space mission to Europa, a probe detects anomalous thermal gradients beneath the icy crust. Scientists observe that a 10 m³ pocket of liquid water, initially at 0°C and under 40 MPa pressure, is encased in ice at −10°C. The surrounding ice is slowly warming due to tidal heating. What is the minimum rate of temperature increase (in °C/day) needed to initiate a phase transition from ice to water throughout the entire pocket before it re-freezes, given the thermal conductivity of ice is 2.2 W/m·K and the latent heat of fusion is 334 kJ/kg? Assume the ice shell is 1 km thick, and the system is in quasi-static equilibrium.

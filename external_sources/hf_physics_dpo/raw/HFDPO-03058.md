# HFDPO-03058

A satellite communication array uses a dual-polarization antenna system to double data capacity, but on a stormy day, signals degrade unexpectedly. The system transmits circularly polarized signals at 12 GHz, with right-hand circular polarization (RHCP) for downlink and left-hand (LHCP) for uplink. However, after passing through a dense rain layer with randomly oriented ice crystals, the received signal shows significant cross-polarization interference—only 68% of the original RHCP power is received, and 22% of the power appears in the unwanted LHCP channel. Estimate the average degree of ellipticity (in degrees) of the wave's polarization state after passing through the rain, assuming the ice crystals impart random phase shifts between orthogonal components.

# HFDPO-01369

A satellite-based quantum communication link uses entangled photon pairs generated by spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) to transmit secure keys across 1,200 km of atmosphere. The source operates at 810 nm pump wavelength, and the signal photons are detected at 1550 nm. Due to atmospheric turbulence, the beam divergence increases by 1.8 μrad/km. What is the minimum aperture diameter needed at the receiver to maintain 85% coupling efficiency into a single-mode fiber, assuming the fiber’s mode field diameter is 10.4 μm and the beam waist at the source is 50 μm?

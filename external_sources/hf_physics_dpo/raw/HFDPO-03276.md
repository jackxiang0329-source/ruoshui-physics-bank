# HFDPO-03276

A solar-powered satellite in geostationary orbit uses a network of 12 identical photovoltaic arrays arranged in a modular circuit to distribute power to onboard systems. Due to micrometeoroid impacts, two arrays (Array A and Array B) have degraded to 70% efficiency, while the remaining 10 operate at full capacity. The system is designed with a hybrid mesh topology where current splits across parallel branches before converging into a central power bus with variable load demands. At peak illumination, the total current generated is measured at 44.8 A, but power readings indicate a 12% deficit in delivered energy. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current through each of the six independent loops in the network, assuming all junctions and resistances are known except for the unknown internal resistance of the degraded arrays, which must be inferred.

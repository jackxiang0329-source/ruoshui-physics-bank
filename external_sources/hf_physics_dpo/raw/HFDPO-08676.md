# HFDPO-08676

During a mountain rescue operation, a 75 kg climber is suspended on a rope anchored to a rock face, with the rope running over a rough, icy ledge (coefficient of kinetic friction μ_k = 0.35). The rope makes a 40° angle with the horizontal on the climber’s side and a 25° angle on the other side. To prevent the rope from slipping, the rescuer must apply a minimum force. What is the minimum force the rescuer must exert to keep the system in equilibrium, and how does this change if the rope's tension increases due to dynamic movement?

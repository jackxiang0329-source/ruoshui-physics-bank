# HFDPO-09610

During a high-altitude expedition, a climber notices their insulated water bottle’s lid is frost-covered despite the water inside being liquid. The air temperature is −15°C, and the bottle’s outer surface is at −10°C. If the bottle’s seal fails, allowing air to enter, how much heat must flow from the water to the surrounding air for 5.0 g of water to freeze on the inner lid surface? Assume the air inside the bottle initially has a dew point at −10°C and the system remains at thermal equilibrium during freezing.

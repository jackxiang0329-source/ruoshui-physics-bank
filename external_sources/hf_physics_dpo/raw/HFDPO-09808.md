# HFDPO-09808

A fusion reactor's plasma-facing wall is lined with a complex multilayered dielectric shield designed to minimize charge accumulation during high-energy ion bombardment. Engineers detect persistent electric fields within the shield despite its macroscopic neutrality. Given a 15 cm thick spherical shell of radially graded permittivity ε(r) = ε₀(1 + 0.04r²), where r is in meters from the center, and a uniform ion flux depositing +2.4 × 10¹⁴ C/m³ of bound charge uniformly throughout the volume, determine the electric field at r = 7.5 cm and explain why Gauss’s Law cannot be applied using constant ε₀.

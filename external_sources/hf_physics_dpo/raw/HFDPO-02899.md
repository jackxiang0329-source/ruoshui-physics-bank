# HFDPO-02899

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that a superconducting qubit fabricated from a thin film of niobium (Nb) exhibits unexpected decoherence at temperatures just above 4 K. Measurements indicate the radiation induces electron-hole pairs in the Nb film, yet the observed decoherence rate exceeds predictions from standard BCS theory. Estimate the minimum energy threshold (in meV) for radiation-induced quasiparticle generation that would significantly disrupt the qubit’s quantum state, given that the superconducting gap Δ ≈ 1.5 meV and the film thickness is 50 nm. Assume the radiation couples through the electromagnetic field, and use realistic values for the electron effective mass and density of states.

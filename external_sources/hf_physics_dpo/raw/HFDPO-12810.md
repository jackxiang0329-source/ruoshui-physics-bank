# HFDPO-12810

A quantum communication satellite in low Earth orbit must transmit entangled photons to ground stations through atmospheric turbulence, but signal fidelity degrades due to random phase distortions. The system uses a spatial light modulator to pre-compensate for wavefront errors measured by a beacon laser. Given a 600 km path through a turbulent layer with a Fried parameter r_0 = 15 cm at 1550 nm, and a 20 cm aperture receiver, determine the minimum number of phase correction modes required to restore the fidelity of the Bell-state measurement to >90%, assuming each mode corrects a single Fourier component of the phase screen.

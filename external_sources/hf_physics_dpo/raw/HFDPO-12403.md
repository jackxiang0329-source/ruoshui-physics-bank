# HFDPO-12403

In a cryogenic quantum computing array, 128 superconducting qubits are coupled to a shared bath of phonons at 15 mK. Each qubit can occupy two states: |0⟩ (ground) or |1⟩ (excited), with an energy splitting of 5.2 GHz. The system exhibits correlated decoherence due to non-Markovian phonon scattering. If the total phonon population in the bath is measured at 2.7 × 10⁶ phonons, determine the effective temperature of the bath *as perceived by the qubits* when their collective energy is 6.8 × 10⁻¹⁸ J, and explain why this differs from the bath’s physical temperature.

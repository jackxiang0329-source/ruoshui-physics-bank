# HFDPO-02156

A deep-space telescope array on a lunar base uses a Fresnel zone plate as its primary collector, designed to focus infrared radiation from a distant exoplanet’s atmosphere. The plate has 100 concentric zones with outer radius 5.0 m and operates at a wavelength of 12 μm. Due to manufacturing tolerances, the phase shift across each zone is off by ±0.05 radians, causing a blurred focus. Determine the minimum angular resolution the system can achieve when the beam is reconstructed, and explain how the phase error alters the effective focal length.

# HFDPO-06212

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a charged microelectromechanical system (MEMS) cantilever—40 μm long, 5 μm wide, and 1 μm thick—experiences unexpected stiction to a silicon substrate due to electrostatic forces. The cantilever carries a uniform surface charge density of +2.5 nC/m², while the substrate has a fixed negative charge density of –1.2 nC/m². The dielectric constant of the insulating oxide layer between them is 3.9. Calculate the net electrostatic force per unit width acting on the cantilever when its tip is 100 nm above the substrate, and explain why this force could cause irreversible sticking despite mechanical stiffness.

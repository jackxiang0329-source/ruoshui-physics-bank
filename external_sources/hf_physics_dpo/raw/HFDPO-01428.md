# HFDPO-01428

Engineers suspect air drag and track-induced rolling resistance are destabilizing the system, but their simulation assumes constant drag coefficients. Given that the train’s mass is 120,000 kg, the coefficient of rolling resistance increases from 0.0012 to 0.0021 when lateral forces exceed 15 kN, and air density varies with altitude (950 g/m³ at 1.5 km), can the train maintain stability without active stabilization systems?

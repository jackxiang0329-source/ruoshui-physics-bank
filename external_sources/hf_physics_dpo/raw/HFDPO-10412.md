# HFDPO-10412

A solar farm in Nevada uses flat-plate collectors to heat water for a nearby desalination plant. Each collector has a surface area of 2.5 m² and operates at an average temperature of 75°C under peak sunlight (1000 W/m²). The system loses heat to the environment via convection and radiation. The ambient temperature is 30°C, and the emissivity of the collector surface is 0.92. If the collector must supply 12,000 W to the water loop, what is the minimum required convective heat transfer coefficient to maintain steady-state operation?

# HFDPO-06360

During a deep-sea geological survey, a research vessel detects anomalous seismic wave patterns from a previously unmapped underwater fault line. The P-waves travel at 6.5 km/s through the oceanic crust, while S-waves move at 3.8 km/s. Seismometers placed 120 km apart on the seafloor record a 14.6-second delay between the arrival of P- and S-waves. However, the data shows a frequency shift in the waveforms—higher frequencies arriving first, suggesting dispersion. Determine the thickness and composition of the layered sediment layer above the crust that could explain both the arrival times and the frequency dispersion, assuming the sediment layer has a density of 2.1 g/cm³ and a shear modulus of 0.3 GPa.

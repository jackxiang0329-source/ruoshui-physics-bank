# HFDPO-04088

A fusion reactor’s tokamak chamber uses a toroidal magnetic field to confine superheated plasma at 150 million K. What is the instantaneous rate of change of magnetic flux through the plasma cross-section due to this field decay, and how might this affect the induced electric field that drives plasma current?

# HFDPO-06760

A quantum dot solar cell designed for space missions uses lead-sulfide nanocrystals embedded in a perovskite matrix. However, at orbital temperatures (275 K), electron tunneling through the insulating matrix causes rapid charge recombination. Assume electron mass is 0.1 me and the matrix is uniform.

# HFDPO-04628

A satellite in geostationary orbit (35,786 km above Earth's equator) must perform a station-keeping maneuver to counteract gravitational perturbations from the Moon and Sun. To maintain its orbital slot within ±0.1° longitude, it uses thrusters to adjust its velocity. Given that the satellite's thrust efficiency is 450 s specific impulse, and it needs a Δv of 1.8 m/s over 1 year, what is the minimum propellant mass fraction required for this maneuver? Assume the satellite’s dry mass is 1,200 kg.

# HFDPO-09051

A high-power laser fusion reactor in development uses a spherical lithium-6 target (radius 2.5 mm, density 534 kg/m³) to breed tritium via neutron capture. During a plasma implosion, the target is heated non-uniformly: the surface rapidly reaches 12,000 K due to ion heating, while the core remains at 800 K. The thermal diffusivity of lithium-6 is 5.2 × 10⁻⁵ m²/s, and its volumetric heat capacity is 2.9 × 10⁶ J/(m³·K). Estimate the time it takes for the core temperature to rise by 100 K, and comment on whether this heating rate threatens tritium retention before fusion ignition.

# HFDPO-00017

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote village powers a single-phase AC inverter that feeds a local community center. The inverter outputs 240 V RMS at 60 Hz, but the load consists of a mix of resistive heaters (1.2 kW), LED lighting (80 W, power factor 0.95), and a 10 HP induction motor (efficiency 88%, power factor 0.82 lagging). A technician notices voltage sags during motor startup. Calculate the total complex power drawn from the inverter at full load, and determine the required capacitance (in μF) to correct the power factor to 0.95 lagging at full load.

# HFDPO-10306

A team of materials scientists is designing a new type of thermally responsive polymer for use in adaptive aerospace coatings. At 300 K, 40% of the chains are extended. What is the entropy change per chain when the temperature increases to 450 K, assuming the energy states are non-degenerate and the system is in thermal equilibrium?

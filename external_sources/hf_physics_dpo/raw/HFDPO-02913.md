# HFDPO-02913

A deep-sea research team deploys a pressure sensor at 1,200 meters below the ocean surface. The sensor is housed in a spherical titanium capsule with a diameter of 0.3 meters. To prevent crushing, the capsule must maintain internal pressure equal to the external pressure at that depth. What is the total force exerted by seawater on the outer surface of the capsule? Assume seawater density is 1025 kg/m³ and atmospheric pressure is negligible compared to hydrostatic pressure.

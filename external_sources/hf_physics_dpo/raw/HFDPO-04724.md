# HFDPO-04724

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent explorer drone, shaped like a streamlined cylinder (diameter 0.6 m, length 2.4 m), descends at a constant speed of 0.8 m/s through seawater at 4°C. The drone's thrusters provide a thrust force of 320 N, but drag and buoyancy must be balanced by friction and form resistance. Given that the seawater has a density of 1025 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity of 1.57×10⁻³ Pa·s, determine the effective coefficient of drag (Cd) assuming turbulent flow and that skin friction contributes 40% of total drag.

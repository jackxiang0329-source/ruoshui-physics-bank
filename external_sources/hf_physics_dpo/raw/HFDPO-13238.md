# HFDPO-13238

A desert solar thermal plant uses parabolic mirrors to focus sunlight onto a central receiver atop a 100-meter tower, heating molten salt to 565°C. The heated salt flows into an insulated storage tank, where it is used to generate steam for a turbine. During a 12-hour sunny day, the plant stores 120 GJ of thermal energy. However, due to imperfect insulation, 15% of this energy is lost to the environment during storage. If the turbine converts 40% of the available thermal energy into electrical work, how much net electrical work is delivered over the day, and what fraction of the original solar input is actually converted to usable electrical energy?

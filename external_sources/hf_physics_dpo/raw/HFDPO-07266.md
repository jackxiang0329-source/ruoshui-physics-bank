# HFDPO-07266

A nanoscale protein motor in a living cell converts chemical energy from ATP into mechanical work, moving along a microtubule track. At 310 K, the motor operates with a 60% efficiency, and each ATP hydrolysis releases 50 kJ/mol of energy. The motor’s stepping process involves overcoming a free-energy barrier of 20 kJ/mol. What is the probability that the motor takes a forward step in a given time interval, assuming the transition rate follows Arrhenius behavior and the attempt frequency is 10^10 s⁻¹?

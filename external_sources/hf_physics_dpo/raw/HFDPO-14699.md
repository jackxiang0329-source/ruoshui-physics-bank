# HFDPO-14699

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer (initially uncharged) is suspended by electrostatic levitation using four parallel field plates spaced 2 mm above and below the wafer. During processing, the wafer accumulates a non-uniform charge density due to ion bombardment: σ(x,y) = σ₀(1 + 0.3cos(2πx/L)) where L = 15 cm and σ₀ = 5.0 nC/m². What is the net force on the wafer, and is levitation stable? Assume the field plates are grounded and the wafer’s thickness is negligible.

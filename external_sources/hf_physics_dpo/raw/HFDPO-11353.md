# HFDPO-11353

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a receiver tube filled with synthetic oil. On a clear day, the system absorbs 800 W/m² of solar flux, and the oil flows at 2.5 kg/s through a 10 m long tube with a diameter of 0.08 m. The oil enters at 290°C and must exit at 350°C to maintain turbine efficiency. Given the oil's specific heat capacity is 2100 J/kg·K and its density is 850 kg/m³, determine whether the current flow rate is sufficient to achieve the required temperature rise. If not, calculate the minimum flow rate needed.

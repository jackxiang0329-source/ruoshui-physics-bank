# HFDPO-16309

A wind turbine with three 55-meter-long blades is spinning at 15 rpm in a steady 12 m/s wind. Due to a sudden gust, the wind speed increases to 18 m/s over 3 seconds. If the turbine’s rotational inertia is 8.2 × 10⁷ kg·m² and the blades are designed to maintain constant angular velocity via a generator torque controller, what average torque must the generator apply during the gust to prevent overspeeding?

# HFDPO-09912

A quantum imaging lab is developing a single-photon camera for detecting faint biological fluorescence in live cells, but background noise from scattered laser light (532 nm) is saturating the detector. The system uses a 1 mm diameter pinhole to spatially filter light, but diffraction limits resolution and throughput. Design a hybrid spatial-spectral filter using a dielectric multilayer coating on the pinhole that transmits only photons with wavelengths >535 nm (to block the laser) while preserving the spatial resolution. What is the optimal number of bilayers and their thicknesses to achieve >90% transmission at 540 nm and >95% suppression at 532 nm, given a refractive index contrast of 1.6 (high) and 1.3 (low)?

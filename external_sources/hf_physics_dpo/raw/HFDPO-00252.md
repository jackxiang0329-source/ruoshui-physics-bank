# HFDPO-00252

The blades have a total moment of inertia of 1.8 × 10⁶ kg·m². During the gust, the turbine’s generator disengages, so no electrical power is extracted. If the turbine must be safely stopped within 45 seconds after the gust ends to prevent mechanical stress, what constant torque (magnitude and direction) must the braking system apply?

# HFDPO-11335

A marine biologist uses underwater acoustic tags to track migrating humpback whales in the Pacific Ocean. One tag emits a sound pulse at 150 Hz, but due to ocean temperature gradients, the sound waves refract as they travel 5 km to a hydrophone array. The water temperature drops from 18°C at the surface to 5°C at depth, altering the wave speed from 1450 m/s to 1420 m/s. If the sound path bends such that the angle of incidence at the boundary is 40°, what is the angle of refraction? How does this affect the timing of signal reception compared to a straight-path assumption?

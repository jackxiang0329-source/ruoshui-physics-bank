# HFDPO-02206

A coastal city is evaluating a new floating solar farm designed to reduce land use and mitigate heat island effects. The array consists of 120 rectangular solar panels, each 6 m long and 3 m wide, floating on a dense, uniform polymer mat that keeps them level. During high tides, water flows past the edges at an average speed of 1.8 m/s. Engineers are concerned about vortex shedding from the panel edges causing structural fatigue over time. Determine whether the flow is likely to induce significant periodic forces on the panels, and estimate the dominant vortex shedding frequency. Assume water density is 1025 kg/m³ and dynamic viscosity is 1.05 × 10⁻³ Pa·s.

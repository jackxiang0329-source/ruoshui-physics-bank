# HFDPO-08268

In a deep-sea research submersible, a laser rangefinder uses a single convex lens with 15 cm focal length to measure distances to undersea hydrothermal vents. During a dive, the system detects a sudden image shift in the sensor array—apparent target position differs by 4.2 cm from expected. Given that the laser beam enters the lens at a 6° angle off-axis and the lens diameter is 8 cm, determine whether this shift is due to spherical aberration or beam misalignment, and calculate the precise deviation angle of the ray at the lens exit if spherical aberration is the dominant factor.

# HFDPO-07270

A solar thermal power plant in Nevada uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The system stores 1.2 GJ of energy in a 25,000 kg tank of salt initially at 500°C. At night, the salt cools down to 350°C while transferring energy to a steam turbine. Assuming the specific heat capacity of the salt is 1200 J/(kg·K), and the turbine operates at 35% efficiency, how much electrical energy can be extracted? What is the entropy change of the salt during this process?

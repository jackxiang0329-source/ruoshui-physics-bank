# HFDPO-04580

A deep-sea research submersible uses a laser rangefinder to map underwater geological formations, but encounters distorted readings when scanning a steep, sediment-laden slope. The laser beam enters the water at a 35° angle from air, refracts at the surface, and travels 12 meters before striking the slope. Due to varying salinity gradients, the refractive index increases linearly from 1.33 at the surface to 1.35 at 12 m depth. If the beam strikes the slope at 50° relative to the normal, what was the true angle of the slope relative to horizontal?

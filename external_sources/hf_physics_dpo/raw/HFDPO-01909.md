# HFDPO-01909

A lunar rover’s power system uses a network of solar cells connected in series-parallel arrays to charge a 24 V lithium-ion battery bank. During a 14-day lunar night, the rover relies on stored energy, but a fault in the charge controller causes one branch of the array—rated at 12 V and 5 A—suddenly to draw reverse current from the battery. If the battery discharges at 4 A during sleep mode and the faulty branch draws 2 A in reverse, what is the actual power dissipated in the faulted branch, and how does this affect the battery’s terminal voltage over time?

# HFDPO-15888

A solar panel array on a residential roof is mounted with a single pivot at the base, angled to maximize winter sun exposure. During a heavy snowstorm, 120 kg of snow accumulates uniformly along the 3.2 m length of the panel, which is tilted at 40° from the horizontal. The panel’s center of mass is located 1.6 m from the pivot. What is the minimum tension required in a cable anchored 1.0 m above the pivot and running horizontally to the panel’s edge to prevent the panel from rotating downward?

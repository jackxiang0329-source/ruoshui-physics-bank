# HFDPO-08243

A wind turbine blade in a coastal wind farm is experiencing uneven wear on its leading edge, despite regular maintenance. Engineers notice that the pressure distribution along the blade’s surface varies significantly at different wind speeds. The blade has a chord length of 2.4 m and is designed with a NACA 0012 airfoil profile. At a wind speed of 15 m/s, measurements show a pressure of 12 kPa at the top surface near the tip, but only 10 kPa at the bottom. Given that the air density is 1.225 kg/m³, determine the local lift coefficient and explain how an imbalance in dynamic pressure due to Bernoulli’s principle could contribute to accelerated erosion at high angles of attack.

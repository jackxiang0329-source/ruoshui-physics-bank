# HFDPO-07619

A quantum dot solar cell designed for space satellites uses CdSe nanoparticles embedded in a TiO₂ matrix, with an effective bandgap of 1.7 eV. During a deep-space mission, engineers notice a 12% drop in efficiency after 18 months, despite stable illumination. Spectroscopy reveals increasing trap states near the nanoparticle surfaces. Determine the minimum surface recombination velocity (in cm/s) required to explain the efficiency loss, assuming carrier diffusion length in TiO₂ is 80 nm and the average carrier lifetime drops from 150 ps to 65 ps due to surface traps.

# HFDPO-00930

In a high-voltage particle accelerator, a spherical vacuum chamber with radius 1.2 m is maintained at +150 kV relative to ground. A small, conducting dust particle (radius 50 μm, density 2.3 g/cm³) becomes electrostatically trapped near the chamber wall due to a non-uniform electric field. The particle must be removed without disrupting the vacuum or causing arcing. What is the minimum electric field gradient (in V/m²) required at the chamber wall to overcome the Coulomb force and initiate controlled particle ejection? Assume the particle carries a charge of 8.0 × 10⁻¹⁵ C and that electrostatic forces dominate over gravity.

# HFDPO-01582

A quantum dot array in a nanoscale optoelectronic device exhibits coupled oscillations due to electron tunneling between adjacent dots, each dot acting as a potential well. At room temperature, thermal fluctuations cause the system to lose coherence, but coherent oscillations are restored when cooled to 4.2 K. If the oscillation frequency is 1.8 THz and the initial charge imbalance is 0.3 e, determine the time at which the charge oscillation reaches 5% of its initial amplitude, and explain how the system’s damping ratio and quantum coherence affect this decay.

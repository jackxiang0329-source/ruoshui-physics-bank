# HFDPO-01106

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The system stores 12 GJ of energy in a 30,000 kg tank of molten nitrate salt initially at 560°C. At night, the salt cools to 380°C, transferring heat to a steam turbine with 38% thermal efficiency. How much electrical energy is actually generated, and why does the actual output fall short of the ideal Carnot limit despite the high-temperature difference?

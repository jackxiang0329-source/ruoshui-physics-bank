# HFDPO-00245

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and modeled as a thin rod, spins at 15 rpm in a steady wind. During a sudden gust, the angular acceleration increases to 0.3 rad/s² for 4 seconds before stabilizing. If the blade’s mass is 12,000 kg and the hub exerts a torque to resist blade twisting, calculate the total torque required from the hub during the gust to prevent structural stress. Assume the blade's moment of inertia is about its root.

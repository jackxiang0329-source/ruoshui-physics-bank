# HFDPO-01499

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) wound into a solenoid coil, operating at 77 K. During a sudden quench event, a localized region of the tape loses superconductivity, generating a resistive hotspot with 50 mΩ resistance. The stored magnetic energy in the 200-turn coil (inductance 3.2 H) must be dissipated safely. What is the minimum quench protection current threshold needed to trigger a controlled dump within 25 ms before the hotspot exceeds 400 K?

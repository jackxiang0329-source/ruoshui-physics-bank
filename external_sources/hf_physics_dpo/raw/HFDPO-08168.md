# HFDPO-08168

A team of engineers is designing a satellite-based GPS system for a moon colony. The satellite orbits at 3,000 km above the lunar surface, moving at 1.5 km/s relative to the moon’s surface. Due to its high speed and weaker gravity, clocks on the satellite tick slightly differently than clocks on the ground. If the satellite’s clock gains 15 nanoseconds per day compared to a clock on the lunar surface, what is the total relativistic time difference after one lunar day (27.3 Earth days)?

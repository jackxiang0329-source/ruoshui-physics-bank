# HFDPO-07608

In a fusion reactor prototype, a plasma of deuterium and tritium ions is confined in a toroidal chamber. During a 10-ms pulse, the total kinetic energy of the ions drops by 2.3 MJ, but the magnetic field strength remains constant. Simultaneously, 1.8 MJ of radiation is detected. If the system is isolated and no mass escapes, what must be the change in internal energy, and how does this reflect the interplay between conservation of energy, momentum, and particle number in a non-ideal plasma environment?

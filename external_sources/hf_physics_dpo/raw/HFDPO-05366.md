# HFDPO-05366

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal area prone to high winds. The station’s 1.2 m tall pole is anchored to a concrete slab via a flange bolted at its base. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 450 N at the top of the pole, 1.2 m above the ground. The concrete slab has a base area of 0.6 m × 0.6 m and a weight of 200 kg. The coefficient of static friction between the slab and the roof is 0.65. Determine whether the slab will pivot or slide under the wind load, and what minimum bolt torque (in N·m) is required to prevent rotation if the bolts are located 0.15 m from the pole’s centerline.

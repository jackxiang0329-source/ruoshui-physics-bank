# HFDPO-01448

A fusion-energy reactor’s magnetic confinement chamber experiences a sudden plasma jet eruption, ejecting 1.2 kg of deuterium-tritium plasma at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s perpendicular to the main magnetic axis. The reactor’s inertial damping system, designed to absorb recoil via momentum conservation, must respond within 2.5 ms. If the reactor’s reaction mass is 350,000 kg, what impulse must the damping system deliver to prevent structural displacement beyond 0.4 mm? Assume the system behaves as a rigid body with negligible external forces during the event.

# HFDPO-10220

A solar-powered drone used for monitoring wildfires must ascend from a valley floor to a ridge 800 meters above, carrying a 4.5 kg camera system. The drone’s battery stores 250,000 J of energy. If 20% of the energy is lost to air resistance and motor inefficiency, how much gravitational potential energy does the drone gain during the climb?

# HFDPO-02359

A quantum computing startup is developing a fluxonium qubit array using high-temperature superconducting (HTS) YBCO rings with a critical current density of 10⁷ A/m² and a London penetration depth of 150 nm. During testing, the device exhibits unexpected decoherence when cooled below 77 K in a 0.5 mT magnetic field—despite the field being below the theoretical upper critical field. The team suspects vortex proliferation due to non-uniform current distribution. Evaluate whether vortex penetration is thermodynamically favorable under these conditions, and determine the minimum ring radius required to suppress vortex entry at 77 K.

# HFDPO-14371

A lunar greenhouse module on the Artemis Base uses a transparent aerogel-insulated wall to maintain a stable 25°C internal environment despite 14-day-long lunar nights where external temperatures plunge to -170°C. The wall consists of a 10 cm thick layer of silica aerogel (thermal conductivity 0.015 W/m·K) sandwiched between two 2 cm thick aluminum sheets (k = 205 W/m·K). The internal surface is heated by a radiative panel emitting 350 W/m², but convective heat transfer is negligible in microgravity. Determine the steady-state temperature gradient across the aerogel layer, and explain why the overall thermal resistance is dominated by the aerogel despite aluminum’s high conductivity.

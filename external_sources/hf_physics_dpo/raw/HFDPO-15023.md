# HFDPO-15023

A Martian dust storm generates a sustained 25 m/s horizontal wind over a 500 m² solar panel array mounted on a rigid, frictionless pivot at its center. Each panel has a mass of 120 kg and is 10 m long, extending radially from the pivot. Dust particles (average diameter 10 μm, density 2500 kg/m³) embed into the panel surface at a rate of 0.3 g/m²·s, increasing the panel’s mass and altering its center of mass. Determine whether the cumulative torque from wind pressure and mass redistribution will cause the array to rotate past 15° from vertical before the dust accumulation halts further movement.

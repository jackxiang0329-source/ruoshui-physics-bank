# HFDPO-13360

A deep-sea mining vehicle descends to 3,000 meters below the ocean surface to collect polymetallic nodules. The vehicle’s internal pressure hull is designed to withstand the external hydrostatic pressure, but a small, 2 mm diameter crack appears in a weld seam. Engineers must determine whether the crack will allow a dangerous inward flow of seawater or remain sealed by surface tension forces. The seawater has a density of 1,025 kg/m³, the surface tension between seawater and the steel hull is 0.073 N/m, and the contact angle is 105°. Is the crack likely to let water in, or will capillary forces hold it shut?

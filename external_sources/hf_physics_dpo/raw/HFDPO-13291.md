# HFDPO-13291

A fusion reactor’s tokamak design uses a rapidly pulsed toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma, but sudden quenches in the superconducting coils cause dangerous flux changes. During a 10 ms power loss, the magnetic flux through a 0.5 m² diagnostic loop near the coil drops from 8.0 Wb to 1.2 Wb. The loop has a resistance of 0.03 Ω and is used to monitor inductive disturbances. What is the peak induced current magnitude in the loop, and why might this reading underpredict the actual current if the loop is not perfectly coupled to the changing flux?

# HFDPO-07423

A Mars rover’s rear wheel, designed with a knobby tread for traction, begins to slip during a 15° incline ascent through a field of fine, cohesive regolith. The wheel, 0.6 m in diameter, rotates at 5 rad/s while the rover moves at 0.8 m/s, indicating dynamic slip. The regolith exerts a non-Newtonian drag force proportional to velocity squared and a Coulomb-like friction force with μ_s = 0.65 and μ_k = 0.45. Determine the total resistance force opposing motion and identify the dominant resistive mechanism—rolling drag or kinetic friction—under these conditions.

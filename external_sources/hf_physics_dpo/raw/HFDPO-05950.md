# HFDPO-05950

A coastal city is designing a new seawater desalination plant where brine (high-salinity wastewater) must be discharged efficiently through a submerged outfall pipe to prevent harmful local concentration buildup. The pipe has a diameter of 0.5 m and discharges brine at 3 m/s into the ocean. The density of the incoming seawater is 1025 kg/m³, and the brine is 1040 kg/m³. The outfall is 20 m below the ocean surface. What is the minimum discharge velocity needed to ensure the brine plume remains submerged and does not rise toward the surface due to buoyancy forces?

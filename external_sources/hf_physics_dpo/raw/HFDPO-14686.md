# HFDPO-14686

A biomechanical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable stent for coronary arteries, where the vessel wall undergoes cyclic dilation (10% strain) during each heartbeat. The stent is made from a nitinol alloy with a Young's modulus of 60 GPa and must maintain structural integrity under repeated stress without yielding. If the stent’s inner radius expands from 3.5 mm to 3.85 mm during systole, what is the maximum hoop stress experienced in the stent wall (thickness: 0.2 mm), and does it exceed the alloy’s yield strength (250 MPa)? Assume thin-walled cylinder approximation and linear elasticity.

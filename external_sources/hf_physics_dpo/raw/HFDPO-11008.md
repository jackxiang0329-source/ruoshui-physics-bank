# HFDPO-11008

A cargo drone delivering medical supplies across a remote valley must navigate a sudden sandstorm. The drone, with a total mass of 8.4 kg, travels horizontally at 12 m/s through air with a drag coefficient of 0.85 and frontal area of 0.62 m². The sand particles in the storm increase the effective air density to 1.6 kg/m³. If the drone’s propellers can generate a maximum thrust of 15 N, determine whether it can maintain constant speed during the storm, and calculate the minimum coefficient of static friction needed between the drone’s landing skids and the sandy terrain to prevent slipping when braking from 12 m/s over 9 meters.

# HFDPO-08399

A solar-powered rover on Mars must recharge its batteries during the day while sustaining critical systems through the 12-hour night. The rover’s primary battery bank (12 V, 80 Ah) is charged via a 200 W photovoltaic array with a non-ideal MPPT regulator that only delivers 85% of maximum power under variable dust conditions. At night, two subsystems draw current: a thermal heater (75 W) and a communication module (30 W), both regulated to maintain constant power draw. If dust accumulation reduces the array’s efficiency by 15% over a 6-hour charging window, can the battery supply enough energy to maintain both systems throughout the night without over-discharging below 20% capacity?

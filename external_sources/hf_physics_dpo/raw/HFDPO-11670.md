# HFDPO-11670

A solar concentrator array in a desert research station uses parabolic mirrors to focus sunlight onto a central receiver 15 meters high. Due to seasonal tilt changes, the sun’s angle relative to the ground shifts from 25° to 50° between winter and summer. Engineers notice the focused beam drifts off-target. What mirror tilt angle (relative to the horizontal) must be set to keep the reflected beam centered at the receiver when the sun is at 40° above the horizon? Assume the mirrors are ideal parabolic reflectors and sunlight is a parallel beam.

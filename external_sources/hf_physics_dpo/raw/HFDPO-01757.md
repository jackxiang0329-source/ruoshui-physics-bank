# HFDPO-01757

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates power during high-wind events, but engineers notice it occasionally stalls unexpectedly during gusty conditions. The turbine has three blades, each 45 m long and weighing 12,000 kg, rotating about a central hub with a moment of inertia of 8.6 × 10⁷ kg·m². During a sudden 25 m/s gust, the blades experience a torque of 3.2 × 10⁶ N·m due to aerodynamic imbalance. If the turbine was initially spinning at 15 rpm, how long does it take to come to a near stop (defined as <1 rpm) if a braking system applies a constant opposing torque of 2.1 × 10⁶ N·m?

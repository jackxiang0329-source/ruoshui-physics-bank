# HFDPO-15064

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses superconducting coils to stabilize a plasma at 150 million Kelvin. Engineers estimate the blob loses 80% of its kinetic energy through radiation and resistive heating over 0.3 seconds. What is the average force exerted on the divertor wall during impact, and how does this compare to the structural yield strength of tungsten (1.5 GPa)?

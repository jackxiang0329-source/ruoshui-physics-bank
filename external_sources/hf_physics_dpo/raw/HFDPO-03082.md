# HFDPO-03082

A deep-sea research submersible encounters a sudden pressure surge during descent near a hydrothermal vent field, causing a brittle titanium alloy hatch to develop a microfracture. The hatch is circular, 0.8 m in diameter, and holds a sealed internal chamber at atmospheric pressure. The external seawater pressure at the current depth is 1.2 × 10⁷ Pa, while the internal pressure remains at 1.01 × 10⁵ Pa. The hatch is held shut by a spring-loaded clamp with a stiffness of 3.5 × 10⁴ N/m, initially compressed by 5 cm. Determine the minimum depth at which the net outward force on the hatch exceeds the maximum clamping force, risking catastrophic failure. Assume seawater density is 1030 kg/m³ and gravity is 9.81 m/s².

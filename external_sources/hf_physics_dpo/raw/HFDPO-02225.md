# HFDPO-02225

A fusion reactor containment vessel experiences a sudden plasma jet breach, ejecting 12 kg of superheated deuterium-tritium plasma at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s tangentially along a 3.2 m radius toroidal path. Engineers must calculate the resulting angular momentum of the vessel-plasma system to predict structural stress on the magnetic confinement rings. The vessel has a moment of inertia of 9.5 × 10⁴ kg·m² and was initially at rest. What is the final angular speed of the vessel after the ejection, and does the system’s total angular momentum remain conserved under these dynamic conditions?

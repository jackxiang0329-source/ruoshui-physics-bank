# HFDPO-06365

A research team designing a next-generation electrostatic shield for a lunar habitat discovers that the shielding efficiency drops unexpectedly when charged dust particles from the moon’s surface accumulate on the outer surface of the conductive shell. What is the total charge enclosed within a Gaussian surface of radius 1.5 m, and how does this affect the electric field at that radius?

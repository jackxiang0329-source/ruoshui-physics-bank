# HFDPO-10700

A research team is designing a new type of electromagnetic shield for sensitive quantum computing hardware placed inside a cylindrical chamber with a radius of 0.8 m and height of 2.5 m. The chamber is lined with a conductive material that must redistribute charge to maintain zero electric field inside the device. During testing, they measure a total enclosed charge of +5.0 μC on the inner surface of the shield. What is the magnitude and direction of the electric field just outside the shield’s outer surface, and how does this relate to the charge distribution on the shield?

# HFDPO-10476

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid, extracting heat from 180°C underground water to drive a turbine. The condenser operates at 40°C, and the plant produces 25 MW of electrical power with a thermal efficiency of 11.2%. However, during winter, the geothermal fluid temperature drops to 150°C, and the condenser must still maintain the same cold reservoir temperature. By what percentage does the maximum theoretical efficiency decrease, and what must be done to maintain the same power output if the turbine efficiency remains unchanged?

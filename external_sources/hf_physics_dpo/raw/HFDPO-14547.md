# HFDPO-14547

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day, aiming to generate electricity at night. The salt, initially at 350°C, is stored in an insulated tank and used to boil water in a steam turbine. If 10,000 kg of salt (specific heat capacity 1300 J/kg·K) cools from 500°C to 350°C over 6 hours, and the system operates with 40% efficiency, how much electrical energy is delivered to the grid? What happens to the rest of the energy?

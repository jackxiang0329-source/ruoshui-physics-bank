# HFDPO-04115

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain area uses a small grid of three identical solar panels wired in parallel to charge a battery. During a clear day, each panel outputs 18 V and can supply up to 3 A. The station’s control circuit draws 5 A total. If a faulty wire causes one panel to deliver only 1 A, what is the current through the second and third panels, and how does Kirchhoff’s Junction Rule help explain the redistribution?

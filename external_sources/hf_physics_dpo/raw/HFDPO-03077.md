# HFDPO-03077

In a cutting-edge quantum imaging lab, researchers use a near-infrared laser (λ = 850 nm) to probe a nanostructured photonic crystal designed for single-photon detection. The crystal has a periodic lattice with a grating spacing of 300 nm and is coated with a subwavelength aluminum layer that introduces phase shifts during reflection. An interference pattern emerges on a CCD sensor 2.4 cm behind the crystal, but instead of sharp fringes, the intensity distribution shows a central peak surrounded by broad, fading rings. The first minimum occurs at a 7.3° angle from the central axis. Determine the effective refractive index of the aluminum coating, assuming a single reflection from the grating and neglecting absorption.

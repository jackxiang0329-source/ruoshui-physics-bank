# HFDPO-05275

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert region uses a 12 V battery charged by a photovoltaic array. During a week of cloudy days, the battery drops to 9 V. To prevent damage, the system automatically shuts down when voltage falls below 10 V. The station’s sensors and processor draw 50 mA when active. A technician finds that a faulty resistor in the voltage monitor circuit is causing the shutdown to trigger too early. What resistance value would the faulty resistor have if it's creating a voltage divider with a 10 kΩ reference resistor, and the measured voltage across the reference drops to 1.0 V when the battery is at 9 V?

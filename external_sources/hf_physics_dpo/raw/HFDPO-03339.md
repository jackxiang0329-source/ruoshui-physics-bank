# HFDPO-03339

A marine biologist is deploying a sensor package to measure deep-sea pressure at 2,000 meters below the ocean surface. The package is enclosed in a rigid, sealed housing with a small, flexible diaphragm that deflects in response to pressure changes. If the diaphragm is made of a material that can withstand a maximum pressure difference of 250 kPa before deforming permanently, will it survive the descent? Assume seawater has a density of 1,025 kg/m³ and atmospheric pressure at the surface is 101 kPa.

# HFDPO-12746

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent made from a shape-memory alloy with a Young’s modulus of 80 GPa and a yield strength of 450 MPa. The stent must expand from a 2.5 mm diameter delivery catheter to a 6.0 mm diameter artery while minimizing residual stress to avoid vessel damage. The material exhibits nonlinear elastic behavior described by a hyperelastic strain-energy function. What is the minimum wall thickness required to ensure the hoop stress during expansion does not exceed 300 MPa, and how does the choice of material thickness affect the long-term fatigue life under cyclic pulsatile flow?

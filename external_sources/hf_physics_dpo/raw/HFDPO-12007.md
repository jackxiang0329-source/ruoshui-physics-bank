# HFDPO-12007

A solar-powered weather station on a remote mountain ridge uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During a week of cloudy weather, the system draws an average of 1.8 A from the battery. After five days, the battery voltage drops to 10.2 V, indicating significant depletion. The battery’s internal resistance increases from 0.15 Ω to 0.40 Ω over time due to aging. If the station’s load behaves as a constant resistance, what was the original load resistance, and how much energy (in Wh) was extracted during those five days?

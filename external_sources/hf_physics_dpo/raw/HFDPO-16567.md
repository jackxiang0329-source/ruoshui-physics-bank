# HFDPO-16567

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable cardiac monitor that uses capacitive sensing to detect subtle changes in chest impedance during heartbeats. The sensor consists of two parallel rectangular electrodes (8 cm × 3 cm) separated by a flexible dielectric layer (εᵣ = 4.5, thickness = 0.2 mm) embedded in a polymer patch worn on the chest. During a heartbeat, the distance between the electrodes changes by up to 5 μm due to chest expansion. Calculate the maximum change in capacitance and determine whether this ΔC is detectable by current integrated circuit (IC) technology capable of measuring ΔC as small as 0.1 fF. Assume the electrodes remain perfectly aligned and fully overlapping throughout the motion.

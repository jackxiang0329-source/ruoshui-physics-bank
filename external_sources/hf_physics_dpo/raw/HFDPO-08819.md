# HFDPO-08819

A wildlife photographer in a dense rainforest uses a polarizing filter on their lens to reduce glare from sunlight reflecting off a shimmering butterfly wing. The wing appears to shift from bright to dark as they rotate the filter. If the reflected light is 75% polarized, at what angle between the filter’s axis and the plane of polarization must the photographer set the filter to reduce the brightness by half?

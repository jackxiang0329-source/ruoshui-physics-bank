# HFDPO-12248

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, irregularly shaped hydrothermal vent chimney composed of porous mineral deposits. The chimney is partially buried in sediment and emits superheated fluid at 350°C under a pressure of 20 MPa. The ROV’s manipulator arm attempts to retrieve a sample from the vent’s upper rim, but the arm stalls due to an unexpected upward force. Given that the surrounding seawater is at 2°C and 10 MPa, and the vent’s internal fluid is less dense than seawater due to thermal expansion, calculate the net buoyant force acting on the 1.2 m³ volume of displaced seawater and determine whether the upward force exceeds the arm’s lifting capacity of 15 kN. Assume the chimney is stable and the internal fluid forms a continuous column.

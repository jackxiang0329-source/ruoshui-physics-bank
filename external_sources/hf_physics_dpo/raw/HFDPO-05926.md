# HFDPO-05926

A small-scale wind turbine on a remote farm powers a water-pumping system that uses a single-phase AC motor with a rated power of 1.5 kW and a power factor of 0.6 lagging. The motor draws current at 240 V RMS, but the farm’s distribution line has a significant inductive reactance due to long wiring. When the turbine operates, the voltage at the motor terminals drops to 220 V RMS. To prevent system inefficiency and frequent tripping, the farmer installs a capacitor bank in parallel with the motor. What capacitance (in μF) must be added to correct the power factor to 0.95 lagging at full load, assuming a line frequency of 60 Hz?

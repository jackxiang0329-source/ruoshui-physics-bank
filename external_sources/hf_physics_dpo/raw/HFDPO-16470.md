# HFDPO-16470

A geothermal drill rig in Iceland must stabilize a 12-meter-tall, non-uniform steel tower (mass 8,500 kg, center of mass 6.2 m above base) during high winds. The tower is anchored by three cables attached at 9 m height, symmetrically spaced at 120° intervals around the base. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 42 kN at 8 m height, creating a torque that threatens to overturn the tower. Determine the minimum tension each cable must withstand to prevent rotation about the base, assuming cables are perfectly inextensible and the tower’s base is frictionless but can resist horizontal force.

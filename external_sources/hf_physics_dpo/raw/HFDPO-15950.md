# HFDPO-15950

A quantum imaging lab uses a squeezed light source at 1550 nm to enhance the sensitivity of a gravitational-wave interferometer's photodetector. The system operates with a beam waist of 100 μm and a total power of 10 mW. However, after integration, the interferometer shows unexpected phase noise rising at high frequencies. The team suspects that optical path length fluctuations from thermal lensing in the beam splitter are introducing coherence degradation. Calculate the minimum detectable phase shift due to thermal drift, given that the beam's intensity profile has a Gaussian envelope and the material has a thermo-optic coefficient of 1.5 × 10⁻⁵ K⁻¹ and a thermal conductivity of 1.3 W/m·K. Use both the beam propagation method and a perturbation model to compare results.

# HFDPO-09161

A spacecraft's heat shield during atmospheric reentry experiences intense heating due to friction with air molecules. At peak descent, the shield's surface reaches 1800 K, and the surrounding air has a molecular mass of 29 u with an average speed of 520 m/s. If the heat shield must dissipate 1.2 × 10⁶ J per square meter of surface area over 30 seconds, estimate the number density of air molecules near the shield using kinetic theory principles. Assume ideal gas behavior and that all kinetic energy transfer to the shield is due to molecular collisions.

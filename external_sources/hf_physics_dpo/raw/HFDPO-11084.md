# HFDPO-11084

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar array with 12 identical photovoltaic panels (each 20% efficient, 250 W max output at STC) powers a critical climate monitoring system via a 400 m DC transmission line made of copper wire (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m). What input voltage must the buck converter receive, and how much power is lost in the transmission line?

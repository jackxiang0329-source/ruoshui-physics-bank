# HFDPO-02881

A deep-sea submersible’s optical sensor array detects faint bioluminescent signals from a newly discovered hydrothermal vent ecosystem, but the lenses are fogging due to pressure-induced temperature shifts. The system uses a compound lens with a 50 mm focal length and a 25 mm diameter, designed for clear imaging at 1000 m depth. During descent, the lens temperature drops from 15°C to 5°C, and the refractive index of the lens material (silicon nitride) changes according to n(T) = 1.96 - 0.0004(T - 15) , where T is in °C. The object distance is fixed at 200 mm. Determine how much the image position shifts due to thermal contraction and refractive index change, and whether the sensor must adjust focus to maintain sharp imaging.

# HFDPO-05860

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial photovoltaic panels angled at 30° to maximize sunlight capture, but reflective glare from the panel surfaces severely disrupts nearby wildlife observation towers. Engineers must design a polarization-filtering mesh that reduces glare without blocking more than 15% of the incident solar radiation. Given that sunlight is partially polarized at 30° to the horizontal due to atmospheric scattering, and the panels reflect light predominantly polarized parallel to the surface (s-polarized), what minimum extinction ratio must the mesh achieve to reduce glare by 90% while preserving sufficient energy input?

# HFDPO-02724

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system with isobutane as the working fluid. The geothermal reservoir provides 120 kg/s of water at 180°C, which cools to 90°C before returning underground. The isobutane enters the evaporator at 50°C and 3.5 MPa, and exits as saturated vapor. The turbine produces 7.2 MW of power, and the condenser operates at 40°C. If the actual cycle efficiency is 83% of the theoretical maximum, what is the mass flow rate of isobutane needed to sustain this output?

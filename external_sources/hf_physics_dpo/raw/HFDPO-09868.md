# HFDPO-09868

In a lithium-ion battery manufacturing facility, a conveyor belt carries charged cathode plates (each with a uniform surface charge density of +2.5 µC/m²) through a narrow, grounded metal tunnel. Due to static buildup, plates occasionally stick to the tunnel walls, causing jams. The tunnel has a rectangular cross-section (0.6 m wide, 0.4 m high) and terminates in a Faraday cage. If the dielectric constant of the air in the tunnel is 1.0006 and the electric field near the wall exceeds 3.0 × 10⁶ V/m, arcing may occur. What is the minimum distance from the plate to the tunnel wall required to prevent arcing, assuming the plate is much larger than the tunnel and the field is primarily due to the plate’s charge alone?

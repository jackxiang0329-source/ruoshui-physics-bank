# HFDPO-09046

A quantum sensor in a biomedical lab detects faint magnetic signals from neural activity, but noise from nearby lab equipment scrambles the data. The sensor’s core is a nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center in diamond, with a spin-1 system that splits under an external magnetic field. At 1.5 Tesla, the energy difference between spin states is 4.3 GHz. To improve signal resolution, the team must design a pulse sequence that coherently isolates the spin state while suppressing thermal and environmental decoherence. How would you construct a dynamic decoupling sequence using π-pulses to extend coherence time by a factor of 5, assuming the dominant noise is from 1/f magnetic fluctuations with a power spectral density of S(ω) ∝ 1/ω?

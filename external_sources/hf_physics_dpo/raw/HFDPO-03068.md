# HFDPO-03068

A geothermal power plant in Iceland extracts 15 MW of heat from a subterranean reservoir at 220°C, using a binary cycle with isobutane as the working fluid. The plant operates with a condenser at 40°C and a turbine efficiency of 88%. During a seasonal cold snap, the ambient temperature drops to 5°C, increasing the condenser’s heat rejection efficiency. However, the turbine inlet temperature drops to 200°C due to reduced reservoir flow. Evaluate whether the plant’s net power output increases or decreases under these conditions, and explain why.

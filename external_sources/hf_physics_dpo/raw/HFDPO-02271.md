# HFDPO-02271

A wind farm in coastal Maine is evaluating whether to install taller turbines (150 m) to capture stronger winds at higher altitudes, but the increased tower weight and transportation costs raise concerns. The taller turbine’s rotor assembly weighs 80,000 kg and must be lifted from ground level to its 150 m mount point using a crane. The crane uses 250,000 J of energy from its diesel engine during the lift, but only 85% of that energy is converted to useful work due to mechanical inefficiencies. Calculate the minimum additional vertical distance the rotor must be lifted (beyond 150 m) to justify the extra energy cost, assuming the rotor is lifted at constant speed and gravitational potential energy is the main energy consideration.

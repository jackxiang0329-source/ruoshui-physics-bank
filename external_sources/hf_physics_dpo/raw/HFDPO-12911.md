# HFDPO-12911

A fusion reactor prototype uses a ring of high-temperature superconducting tape (YBCO) cooled to 77 K to confine a 15 T magnetic field. During a rapid shutdown, the field collapses non-uniformly, inducing eddy currents that heat a 2 cm² section of the tape to 90 K. Given the critical current density *J_c* = 1.2 × 10⁶ A/m² at 77 K, and a 30% drop in *J_c* per 10 K rise, assess whether the tape will quench (lose superconductivity) in this region.

# HFDPO-03703

A fusion reactor prototype in Iceland uses a deuterium-tritium plasma confined in a magnetic tokamak, generating 50 MW of thermal power. The reactor operates at a 30% efficiency for converting fusion energy to electricity. Due to unexpected neutron leakage, the shielding around the vacuum vessel is heating up at a rate of 1.2 kW per square meter. If the shielding material (tungsten-carbide composite) has a thermal conductivity of 120 W/m·K and a specific heat capacity of 320 J/kg·K, how long will it take for a 15 cm thick section of shielding to increase in temperature by 45°C? Assume steady-state heat conduction and no heat loss to surroundings.

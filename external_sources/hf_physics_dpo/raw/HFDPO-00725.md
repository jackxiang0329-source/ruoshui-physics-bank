# HFDPO-00725

A satellite-borne laser communication array uses a custom-designed refractive lens to focus a 1550 nm beam onto a ground receiver 1,200 km away. The lens is made of fused silica with a non-uniform refractive index profile, designed to minimize spherical aberration. However, thermal gradients in orbit cause localized index variations, distorting the focal spot. Given a measured beam divergence of 0.8 μrad at the emitter and a 2.5 mm spot diameter at the receiver, determine the effective focal length of the lens system and explain how the thermal-induced index gradient could alter the Rayleigh range, assuming the lens operates at a numerical aperture of 0.18.

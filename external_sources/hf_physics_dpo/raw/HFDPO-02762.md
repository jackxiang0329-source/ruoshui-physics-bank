# HFDPO-02762

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue manipulation in minimally invasive surgery. The gripper’s finger is a hollow, cylindrical silicone elastomer (E = 2 MPa, ν = 0.49) with inner radius 2 mm, outer radius 4 mm, and length 50 mm. When pressurized internally to 15 kPa, it must extend by at least 3 mm to reach deep tissue. However, due to high Poisson’s ratio, the finger expands radially, increasing friction with surrounding tissue and potentially causing damage. What internal pressure is required to achieve a 3 mm axial extension while minimizing radial expansion?

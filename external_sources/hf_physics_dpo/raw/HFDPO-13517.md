# HFDPO-13517

A geothermal power plant in Iceland extracts steam at 280°C from a subterranean reservoir, but due to seasonal variations, the condenser temperature fluctuates between 30°C and 50°C. The plant operates a binary cycle using isobutane as the working fluid, with turbine inlet conditions at 180°C and 4.2 MPa. Over a 24-hour period, the turbine efficiency degrades by 15% due to fouling in the heat exchangers. What is the maximum theoretical net work output per kilogram of isobutane over this period, and how does the fluctuating condenser temperature affect the overall annual energy yield compared to a fixed 40°C condenser, assuming 85% of the time the system runs at peak efficiency?

# HFDPO-02582

A fusion reactor’s plasma containment system uses a toroidal magnetic field generated by external coils to confine ionized hydrogen at 150 million Kelvin. During a sudden power fluctuation, the magnetic field drops by 30% over 0.5 seconds. Engineers notice the resulting electric field induces eddy currents in the reactor’s superconducting shield, risking thermal instability. What is the magnitude of the induced electric field at the inner wall of the torus (radius 2.5 m), assuming cylindrical symmetry and uniform field decay?

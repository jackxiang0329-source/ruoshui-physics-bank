# HFDPO-02792

A cryogenic storage tank for liquid helium-4 in a quantum computing facility experiences unexpected pressure spikes during cooldown from 4.5 K to 2.17 K—the lambda point. The tank’s volume is fixed at 15 L, and the initial state is saturated liquid helium at 4.5 K. As temperature drops, a small fraction of the liquid undergoes a phase transition to the superfluid state at 2.17 K. The system is monitored via pressure and temperature sensors, but the pressure surge occurs just below the lambda point. Estimate the pressure increase due to the discontinuous change in density and the accompanying entropy release during the phase transition. Assume ideal behavior for the vapor phase and use the Clausius-Clapeyron equation in an integrated form across the transition.

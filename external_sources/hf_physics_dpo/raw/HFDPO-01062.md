# HFDPO-01062

A stealth drone uses a metasurface coating that manipulates reflected light via subwavelength grating structures to minimize radar cross-section. The coating is designed to suppress reflection at 15.5 GHz (microwave regime) by engineering destructive interference across a 60° incidence angle, but engineers notice a sudden resonance peak in the reflected intensity at 15.7 GHz. Given the grating period is 2.1 mm and the effective refractive index of the coating material is 2.4, determine the minimum thickness of the dielectric layer that could produce this unexpected resonance, assuming the grating acts as a periodic waveguide with effective phase shift due to total internal reflection.

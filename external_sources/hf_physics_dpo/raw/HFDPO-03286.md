# HFDPO-03286

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using a spiral channel with a 100 μm radius of curvature. Blood flows at 1.2 mm/s through the channel, and the density of blood plasma is 1025 kg/m³. At a bend, a pressure sensor detects a drop of 18 Pa relative to the outer wall. Using Bernoulli’s principle, estimate the velocity difference between the inner and outer walls of the bend. Then, explain how this pressure gradient could be exploited to trap CTCs without external forces.

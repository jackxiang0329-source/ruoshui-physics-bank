# HFDPO-02337

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue handling in minimally invasive surgery. The gripper uses a hyperelastic silicone membrane (E = 1.2 MPa, ν = 0.49) bonded to a rigid frame. During operation, a pressure difference of 25 kPa is applied across the membrane, causing it to deflect by 8 mm at its center. The membrane is circular with a 30 mm diameter and clamped at the edges. The surgeon reports inconsistent grip force—sometimes too weak, sometimes too strong—despite stable pressure. What is the effective stiffness of the membrane under this loading, and how might non-linear elasticity and geometric nonlinearity explain the observed variability?

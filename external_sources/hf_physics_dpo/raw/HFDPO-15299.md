# HFDPO-15299

A lunar mining robot on the far side of the Moon must sustain a steady internal temperature of 25°C while operating during a 14-day night, during which it relies solely on a 2.4 kWh lithium-ion battery and passive radiative cooling to a 20 K background (space). The robot’s 45 kg chassis has a thermal conductivity of 12 W/m·K and an effective emissivity of 0.92 over its 1.8 m² surface. Internal electronics generate 180 W of waste heat, but the robot must avoid overheating. What is the minimum thickness of a multilayer insulation (MLI) blanket (with effective thermal conductivity of 0.015 W/m·K) required to maintain thermal stability across the 14-day night without active heating?

# HFDPO-11749

A hydroelectric dam in the Andes regulates water flow through a turbine system to stabilize regional power supply. During a peak demand surge, engineers divert 120 m³ of water per second from a reservoir 80 m above the turbine. The turbine converts 85% of the water’s gravitational potential energy into electrical energy, but a portion is lost to friction and turbulence in the penstock. If the generator produces 88 MW of power, what fraction of the theoretical maximum power is being lost to non-conservative forces?

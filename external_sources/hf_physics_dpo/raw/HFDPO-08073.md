# HFDPO-08073

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size using laminar flow in a narrow channel. The device uses a 500 μm wide, 100 μm high, and 10 mm long rectangular channel. Blood flows at 1.2 mm/s, with viscosity 3.5 mPa·s and density 1060 kg/m³. The cancer cells (diameter ~12 μm, density ~1040 kg/m³) are expected to migrate toward the channel walls due to inertial lift forces in the viscous flow. Estimate the ratio of the viscous drag force on a cancer cell to the inertial lift force at the channel centerline, assuming the effective velocity gradient is 200 s⁻¹. Is this ratio sufficient to allow separation within the channel?

# HFDPO-09519

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable neural interface for recording brain activity using microelectrodes embedded in a polymer substrate. The device uses interdigitated micro-capacitors (IDCs) to detect subtle changes in local dielectric properties caused by neural ion fluxes. Each IDC has 20 finger pairs, each 100 μm long, 10 μm wide, separated by 5 μm in a 300 μm × 300 μm area. The dielectric is a biocompatible hydrogel with a tunable permittivity (ε_r = 2 to 8) depending on ion concentration. During a neural burst, the local ε_r increases from 4.5 to 6.3 over 5 ms. What is the rate of change of stored energy in the capacitor during this transient? Assume the voltage across the device remains constant at 100 mV due to active feedback.

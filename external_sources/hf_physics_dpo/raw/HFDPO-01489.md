A cosmic-ray muon detector at a high-altitude research station records muons traveling at 0.998c, arriving from the upper atmosphere. The muons’ average lifetime in their rest frame is 2.2 μs, yet the detector observes them surviving the 15 km descent. How can this be explained without invoking experimental error, and what is the observed lifetime of the muon in the Earth’s frame?

# HFDPO-05284

A quantum optics lab is developing a photonic crystal sensor to detect trace amounts of airborne pathogens by measuring shifts in the diffraction pattern of laser light scattered through a nanostructured silica film. The film has a periodic lattice spacing of 420 nm, and the laser wavelength is tunable between 500 nm and 650 nm. When illuminated at 580 nm, the first-order diffraction peak (m = 1) is observed at 18.5° in air. However, when the sensor is exposed to a target pathogen, the peak shifts to 19.2°. The lab suspects the pathogen alters the effective refractive index of the film. Determine the change in effective refractive index caused by the pathogen.

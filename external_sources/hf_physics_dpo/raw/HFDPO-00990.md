# HFDPO-00990

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent system emits superheated fluid at 350°C through a narrow, porous rock conduit 2 meters long with a cross-sectional area of 0.001 m². The fluid’s resistivity varies with temperature as ρ(T) = ρ₀(1 + α(T − T₀)), where ρ₀ = 1.2 × 10⁻³ Ω·m at T₀ = 25°C and α = 0.0025 K⁻¹. If the fluid flows with a voltage drop of 45 V across the conduit, what is the total current? Assume steady-state thermal equilibrium and that the temperature profile along the conduit is linear.

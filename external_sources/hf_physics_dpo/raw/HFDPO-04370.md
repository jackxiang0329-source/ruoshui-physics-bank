# HFDPO-04370

In a quantum computing lab, researchers attempt to maintain coherence in a superconducting qubit by isolating it from environmental noise. The qubit is designed as a nanoscale Josephson junction, where a Cooper pair tunneling through a thin insulating barrier exhibits wave-like interference. At 10 mK, the thermal de Broglie wavelength of the Cooper pair (effective mass ~2mₑ) is 2.5 μm. To maintain quantum superposition, the qubit’s phase uncertainty must remain below 0.1 radians. However, stray electromagnetic fields from nearby circuit traces introduce a potential fluctuation ΔV = 0.5 μV over a 100 nm region. Determine whether this fluctuation disrupts the qubit’s coherence by comparing the phase shift induced by the potential to the coherence threshold.

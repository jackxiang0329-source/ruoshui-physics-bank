# HFDPO-06156

A quantum imaging lab develops a novel ghost imaging system using entangled photon pairs generated via spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in a periodically poled potassium titanyl phosphate (PPKTP) crystal. The system uses a spatial light modulator (SLM) to encode information onto one beam (signal), while a bucket detector measures total intensity on the other (idler). The SLM displays a 512×512 pixel pattern with 50% duty cycle, but the reconstructed image shows significant speckle noise and degraded resolution. The crystal temperature is stabilized at 35°C, and the pump laser (405 nm, 50 mW) is focused to a 100 µm waist. The team suspects phase mismatch due to thermal drift in the crystal’s poling period. Determine whether the observed image degradation stems primarily from phase mismatch or beam divergence effects, and propose a diagnostic protocol to isolate the dominant error source in this entanglement-based imaging setup.

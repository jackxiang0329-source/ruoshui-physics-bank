# HFDPO-01325

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanoparticles (1.2 nm diameter) embedded in a perovskite matrix. Under sunlight, electron-hole pairs are generated, but recombination losses exceed 40% due to surface defects. Measured photoluminescence decay shows a bi-exponential profile: τ₁ = 12 ps (fast component, 60% intensity), τ₂ = 450 ps (slow component, 40% intensity). What fraction of excited carriers are trapped at surface states, assuming the fast decay is due to non-radiative recombination via defect states (E_d ≈ 0.3 eV above the valence band), while the slow decay corresponds to radiative recombination in the band gap? Use Fermi-Dirac statistics and density of states in quantum confinement.

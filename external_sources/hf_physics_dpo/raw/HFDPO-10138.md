# HFDPO-10138

A team at a quantum computing startup is debugging a superconducting qubit that loses coherence after just 12 microseconds. They suspect atomic-scale defects in the aluminum film used for the Josephson junctions. The defect density is measured at 8 × 10¹⁰ cm⁻², and each defect traps a single electron in a localized state within the superconducting gap. Given that the Fermi energy of aluminum is 11.7 eV and the superconducting gap is 0.35 meV, estimate the shift in the critical current due to these trapped electrons, and evaluate whether such a shift could explain the observed decoherence rate.

# HFDPO-08883

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal region prone to high winds. The station’s frame, weighing 120 N, is secured to a rigid concrete base via two bolts at points A and B, located 0.8 m apart horizontally. During a storm, a steady wind exerts a 350 N force perpendicular to the panel, 1.5 m above the base. The base has a friction coefficient of 0.6 with the roof. Determine the minimum clamping force required at each bolt to prevent tipping, assuming no lateral sliding and equal load sharing between the bolts.

# HFDPO-08540

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable hover during data collection. The drone’s frame is a lightweight carbon-fiber truss with asymmetrical solar panel placement: one 2.4 m² panel (mass 8 kg) mounted 1.5 m forward of the center of mass, and a 1.2 m² panel (mass 4 kg) 1.2 m aft. Wind gusts exert a 120 N horizontal force at the front edge, 2.0 m above center of mass. Determine the minimum reaction force at the rear support leg (located 1.0 m aft of center) required to prevent tipping, and evaluate whether the drone’s current design is stable under worst-case wind conditions.

# HFDPO-05009

A research team is developing a wireless power transfer system for underwater drones using resonant inductive coupling. Due to oceanic currents, the drone’s orientation drifts 15° from alignment with the field. What is the peak induced EMF in the receiver coil when it passes through the region where the field strength changes at 2.4 T/s?

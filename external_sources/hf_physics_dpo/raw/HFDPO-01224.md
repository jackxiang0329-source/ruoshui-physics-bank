# HFDPO-01224

A solar-powered drone is designed to hover at 2 km altitude during a multi-day atmospheric survey, using only photovoltaic cells on its wings. The drone's total mass is 7.2 kg, and its energy-efficient propellers require 85 W of continuous power to maintain lift against gravity and aerodynamic drag. If the solar array generates 110 W/m² under full sun but operates at 22% efficiency due to thermal losses and angle misalignment, and the drone begins with a fully charged battery storing 1.8 MJ, what is the maximum time it can remain airborne before relying on stored energy? Assume sunlight intensity is constant and the drone must remain aloft for 24 hours—how much additional energy storage (in MJ) is needed?

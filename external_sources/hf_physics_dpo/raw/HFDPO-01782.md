# HFDPO-01782

A biomedical engineer is designing a photonic crystal sensor to detect early-stage cancer biomarkers by measuring minute changes in light wavelength reflected from a nanostructured surface. The sensor uses a periodic array of nano-pillars with a spacing of 450 nm, illuminated with white light. When a biomarker binds to the surface, it increases the local refractive index by 0.03, shifting the first-order diffraction peak from 630 nm to 642 nm. What is the new effective wavelength within the grating, and how does this shift confirm the presence of the biomarker?

# HFDPO-08400

A deep-sea research submersible descends through the Mariana Trench, encountering a rare, stable layer of brine pool surrounded by less dense seawater. At a depth of 10,900 meters, the brine (density 1,150 kg/m³) forms a 5-meter-thick horizontal layer beneath a 20-meter layer of seawater (density 1,025 kg/m³). The submersible’s pressure hull is designed to withstand 120 MPa internal pressure, but its external sensors are mounted on a 1.2-meter-long titanium arm extending below the hull. As the sub dives, the sensors enter the brine layer. What is the effective pressure difference across the hull’s external sensor housing (neglecting dynamic effects), and does this pose a risk of mechanical failure given the hull’s pressure rating?

# HFDPO-04697

A modern semiconductor manufacturing facility uses electrostatic precipitators to remove submicron dust particles from high-purity argon gas streams. During a critical fabrication run, engineers notice erratic particle deposition on silicon wafers, suspected to be caused by uncontrolled charge accumulation on insulating quartz nozzles in the gas delivery system. The nozzles are cylindrical, 5 cm long with a 2 mm radius, and hold a uniform volume charge density of 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³. The surrounding gas has a dielectric constant of 1.0006. Calculate the electric potential difference between the nozzle surface and its axis, and determine whether this potential could cause breakdown in the argon stream (dielectric strength ~30 kV/cm).

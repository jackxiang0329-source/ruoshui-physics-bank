# HFDPO-05471

A seismic survey team detects a mysterious low-frequency wave (f = 0.5 Hz) propagating through a layered subsurface structure beneath a geothermal field. The wave travels at 2.8 km/s in a 150 m thick sandstone layer (density 2200 kg/m³) overlying a 100 m thick shale layer (density 2600 kg/m³). The sandstone has a known shear modulus of 5 GPa. The team observes constructive interference in the reflected wave train at certain depths. What is the minimum thickness of an overlying sediment layer (assumed to be water-saturated with bulk modulus 2.5 GPa and density 1050 kg/m³) that would cause destructive interference of the wave reflected from the sandstone-shale interface relative to the wave reflected from the top of the sandstone?

# HFDPO-02856

A laser satellite communication array uses a 500 nm wavelength beam to transmit data across 300 km of atmosphere. As the beam passes through a turbulent layer near the tropopause, it diffracts around a 2 km-wide mountain peak. Assuming the beam's initial diameter is 10 cm and the mountain acts as a single slit, at what minimum distance *beyond* the peak does the first diffraction minimum occur, and how does this affect signal strength for a receiver on the opposite slope?

# HFDPO-01212

A fusion reactor containment chamber uses nested concentric cylindrical plasma shells, each with non-uniform charge density varying radially as ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−r²/σ²). The innermost shell (r = 0 to 1.2 m) has ρ₀ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁹ C/m³ and σ = 0.8 m. Engineers need to determine the electric field at r = 1.5 m, but cannot use direct integration due to computational delays. Design a Gaussian surface and use Gauss’s Law to calculate E(r = 1.5 m), accounting for the full charge distribution inside.

# HFDPO-09210

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses photovoltaic panels wired in series strings, each feeding a central inverter. If the original wire gauge was 10 AWG (5.26 mm²), and resistivity of aluminum is 2.65 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, calculate the temperature rise at the splice assuming 80% of the power lost is dissipated as heat and the splice has a thermal mass of 2.5 g with a specific heat of 900 J/(kg·K). Is the rise safe for the materials?

# HFDPO-09598

During a dust storm in the Mojave Desert, a 2.0 m × 1.5 m solar panel on a remote weather station becomes uniformly charged to +120 μC due to triboelectric effects with sand particles. What is the magnitude and direction of the electric field at the probe’s location, and how much work must be done to bring a +5.0 nC test charge from infinity to the probe’s position?

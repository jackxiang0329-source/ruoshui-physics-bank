# HFDPO-04213

Engineers need to estimate the induced magnetic field at the panel’s surface to ensure electromagnetic interference with nearby control systems remains below safety thresholds. What is the peak magnitude of the magnetic field generated, and how does this relate to the displacement current in Maxwell’s equations?

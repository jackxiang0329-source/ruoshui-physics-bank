# HFDPO-09496

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 12 cm-long silicon nanowire with uniform linear charge density λ = 5.0 × 10⁻¹² C/m is suspended horizontally between two electrodes. Due to a sudden power fluctuation, the surrounding dielectric suspension medium (ε_r = 3.8) develops a localized conductivity anomaly, creating a non-uniform electric field that causes the wire to experience a net force. If the field varies as E(x) = E₀(1 + 0.15 sin(2πx/L)) where x is position along the wire and E₀ = 400 V/m, calculate the net electrostatic force on the wire and determine whether it will shift significantly from equilibrium.

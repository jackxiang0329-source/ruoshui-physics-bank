# HFDPO-05164

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to monitor whale communication in a protected sea canyon. At 15°C, sound travels at 1,500 m/s in water, but the temperature drops to 8°C near the canyon floor. If a whale’s call at 120 Hz is detected at 4,200 m from the surface source and arrives 2.8 seconds after it was emitted, what is the actual depth of the canyon floor where the signal was recorded? Assume sound speed in water follows v ≈ 1400 + 4.5T (m/s), where T is in °C.

# HFDPO-08036

During a high-altitude drone delivery mission in the Himalayas, a solar-powered UAV must glide silently through rarefied air to avoid disturbing endangered snow leopards. At 6,000 m altitude, the aircraft encounters a constant headwind of 12 m/s while descending at a 5° glide angle. The drone’s wingspan is 3.2 m, total mass is 4.5 kg, and its drag coefficient (Cd) varies with Reynolds number: Cd = 0.03 + 0.002/√Re. The air density is 0.43 kg/m³. If the drone’s lift-to-drag ratio must exceed 18 to maintain safe glide, what is the minimum speed it must maintain to avoid stalling, and will it achieve this ratio at 20 m/s true airspeed?

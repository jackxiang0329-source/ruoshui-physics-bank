# HFDPO-04418

A solar-powered weather station in a remote mountain region uses a 12 V DC battery bank charged by a photovoltaic array. During a week of cloudy weather, the station’s sensors and communication module consume 60 mA continuously. The battery bank consists of two identical 12 V lead-acid batteries connected in parallel. One battery is known to have a 15% higher internal resistance than the other.

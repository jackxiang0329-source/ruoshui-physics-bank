# HFDPO-11867

A wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices uses a 1.5 MHz resonant coil pair buried under tissue. The transmitter coil has resistance 2.4 Ω and inductance 12 μH, while the receiver coil has 3.1 Ω resistance and 9.8 μH inductance. Due to skin effect and eddy currents, the effective frequency-dependent resistance of the receiver increases by 40% at 1.5 MHz. The system must deliver 85 mW to a 50 Ω load with 80% efficiency. Determine the required input voltage to the transmitter and the phase angle between voltage and current at the transmitter.

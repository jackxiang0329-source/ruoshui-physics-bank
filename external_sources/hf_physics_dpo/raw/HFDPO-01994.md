# HFDPO-01994

A fusion reactor’s plasma confinement system uses a toroidal magnetic field generated by 12 identical superconducting coils arranged symmetrically around a central chamber. Each coil carries 50 kA and produces a field of 1.5 T at the center of the torus. However, during a sudden power fluctuation, one coil fails, reducing its current to 10 kA. What is the new magnitude and direction of the net magnetic field at the center, and how does this asymmetry threaten plasma stability?

# HFDPO-13871

A marine biologist uses a fiber-optic sensor to monitor coral health by measuring slight changes in light intensity transmitted through a 2.5-meter-long optical fiber submerged in seawater. The system detects interference fringes from reflected light in the fiber, which shift when the coral’s microstructure alters due to bleaching. At 532 nm wavelength, the system registers a fringe shift of 18.5 fringes over 12 minutes. Assuming the fiber’s refractive index changes uniformly due to thermal expansion, what is the average rate of refractive index change (in /s), and how might this relate to the thermal sensitivity of the sensor?

# HFDPO-07446

A quantum dot solar cell array uses a photonic crystal nanostructure to enhance light trapping, but efficiency drops unexpectedly under oblique sunlight. The array’s periodic grating (period = 600 nm) is designed to resonantly couple 650 nm light normal to the surface, yet at 45° incidence, the reflected signal shows a 30% reduction in peak intensity. Estimate the effective refractive index change required in the grating layer to maintain resonance at 45°, and discuss how structural imperfections could cause phase mismatches even if nominal design conditions are met.

# HFDPO-09397

During a desert heatwave, a solar-powered desalination unit in a remote village begins underperforming. If the water is flowing at 0.004 kg/s and needs to reach 85°C to enable efficient distillation, what is the minimum solar intensity required to sustain this process, assuming the absorber reaches steady-state temperature?

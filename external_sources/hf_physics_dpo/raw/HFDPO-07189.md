# HFDPO-07189

A satellite in low Earth orbit uses a pair of counter-rotating reaction wheels to stabilize its orientation. During a maneuver, one wheel malfunctions and suddenly locks, transferring angular momentum to the satellite body. The satellite, initially spinning at 0.1 rad/s about its minor axis, has a moment of inertia of 120 kg·m². The locked wheel has a moment of inertia of 3.5 kg·m² and was spinning at 800 rpm in the opposite direction. Determine the new angular velocity of the satellite about its minor axis immediately after the lock.

# HFDPO-01053

In a next-generation neural interface chip, a 50 nm thick dielectric layer (ε_r = 4.5) separates two graphene electrodes arranged as a parallel-plate capacitor, each with a 100 μm × 100 μm footprint. During neural signal recording, the device must maintain a capacitance within a strict 1.2–1.4 pF range despite temperature fluctuations from 25°C to 60°C, as capacitance changes affect signal sensitivity and noise. Given that the dielectric’s relative permittivity varies with temperature as ε_r(T) = 4.5 + 0.02(T − 25), and graphene’s area expands by 0.1% per 10°C rise, determine if the capacitance remains within the required range. If not, what design modification could stabilize it?

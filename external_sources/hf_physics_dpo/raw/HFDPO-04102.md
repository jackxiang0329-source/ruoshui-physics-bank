# HFDPO-04102

A remote environmental monitoring station uses a solar-powered circuit to transmit data every 15 minutes. The system’s battery delivers 12 V and must supply 200 mA for 10 seconds per transmission. Due to aging, the wiring between the battery and sensor has increased resistance from 0.5 Ω to 2.0 Ω. If the station must remain operational for at least 6 months on a single full charge, will the voltage drop across the wires reduce the power available to the sensor below the minimum 2.4 W needed?

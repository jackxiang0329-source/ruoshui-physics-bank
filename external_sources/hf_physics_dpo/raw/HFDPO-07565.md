# HFDPO-07565

A fusion reactor’s lithium blanket must withstand intense neutron flux while maintaining electrostatic shielding to prevent plasma instabilities. The blanket contains a spherical shell of dielectric material (ε = 4ε₀) with inner radius 1.2 m and outer radius 1.5 m, uniformly charged with total charge Q = +3.6 × 10⁻⁶ C. A small, conductive sensor ring (radius 0.1 m) is embedded at the center, isolated from the shell. What is the electric field magnitude at the sensor’s position, and how does the dielectric polarization affect the net flux through a Gaussian surface just inside the inner radius of the shell?

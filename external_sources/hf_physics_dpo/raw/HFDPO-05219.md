# HFDPO-05219

A deep-sea submersible equipped with a narrow-angle laser rangefinder detects a faint reflection from a submerged hydrothermal vent plume at a depth of 2,300 meters. The laser beam enters the ocean at a 12° angle from the vertical, but due to the sharp temperature gradient in the water column, the ray bends progressively—refracting through a stratified medium with a continuously varying refractive index n(z) = 1.338 – 0.00012z, where z is depth in meters. The beam reaches the vent at a point 15 meters horizontally displaced from the entry point. What is the actual horizontal distance to the vent (assuming straight-line path in a uniform medium), and why is the measured distance misleading?

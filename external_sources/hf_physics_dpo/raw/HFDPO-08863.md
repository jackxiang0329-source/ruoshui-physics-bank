# HFDPO-08863

A lunar lander descending at 5 m/s activates its thrusters to achieve a soft touchdown on a rugged crater floor. The thrust force varies as F(t) = F₀e^(-kt), where F₀ = 8000 N and k = 0.1 s⁻¹.

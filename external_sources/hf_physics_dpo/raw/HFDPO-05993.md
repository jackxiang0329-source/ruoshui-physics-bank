# HFDPO-05993

A wind turbine blade in a high-altitude wind farm operates at 300 m altitude where air density is 1.00 kg/m³. During a storm, engineers notice sudden vibrations in the blade tips. They suspect the turbulence is due to localized pressure drops caused by airflow over the blade's curved surface. Given that the wind speed at the blade tip is 50 m/s and the air accelerates to 75 m/s over the top surface (due to the blade’s shape), what is the pressure difference between the top and bottom surfaces of the blade at that point?

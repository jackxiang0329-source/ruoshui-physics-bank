# HFDPO-05167

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain a steady hover at 20,000 meters during a 4-hour mission. The drone’s lift system operates with 75% efficiency, and its energy comes from photovoltaic cells with 22% sunlight conversion efficiency. On the mission day, solar irradiance is 1000 W/m² but reduced by atmospheric scattering and cloud cover to an effective 600 W/m². The drone’s total mass is 8.5 kg. What is the minimum frontal area of the solar array required to sustain hover without depleting its onboard batteries? Assume no wind and that all energy used is for lifting the drone against gravity.

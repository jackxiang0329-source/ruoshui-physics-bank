# HFDPO-11503

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertically oriented, 1.2 km long cylindrical borehole to circulate fluid through fractured rock. The local magnetic field varies sinusoidally with time due to solar wind interactions—measured as B(t) = (30 μT) sin(2π × 10⁻⁵ t), with t in seconds. The borehole contains a 100 m long conductive metal casing (resistivity 1.7 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, diameter 0.15 m). Determine the induced current density in the casing wall at t = 250 s, considering the changing flux through the casing’s cross-sectional area and the self-inductance of the loop formed by the casing and return path. Assume the casing forms a closed conducting loop with negligible resistance compared to induced EMF.

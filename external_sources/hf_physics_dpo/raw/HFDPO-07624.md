# HFDPO-07624

A biomedical engineer is designing a handheld optical sensor to detect early-stage skin cancer by measuring the scattering and absorption of near-infrared light (λ = 850 nm) through epidermal tissue. The sensor uses a single-mode fiber to deliver light into the skin and a detector 2 mm away to capture transmitted intensity. Given that the reduced scattering coefficient (μ′_s) is 12 cm⁻¹ and the absorption coefficient (μ_a) is 0.8 cm⁻¹, what fraction of the incident light intensity reaches the detector? Assume the tissue is homogeneous and the light propagation follows the diffusion approximation.

# HFDPO-00891

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to monitor the frequency and intensity of waves generated by passing ships near a protected reef. At a depth of 15 m, a ship’s propeller produces a sound wave with a frequency of 450 Hz that travels at 1,480 m/s in seawater. The reef’s surface reflects 30% of the wave’s energy, and the biologist detects a reflected wave with an amplitude reduced by 40% compared to the incident wave. What is the wavelength of the incident wave, and how much energy is lost to absorption and scattering in the water column?

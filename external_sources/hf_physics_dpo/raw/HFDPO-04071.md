# HFDPO-04071

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole lined with superconducting coils to harvest induced currents from natural convection-driven fluid flows in the subsurface. The fluid, with resistivity 0.3 Ω·m and density 950 kg/m³, flows upward at 0.8 m/s through a 1.2 m diameter channel, generating a time-varying magnetic field due to its motion in Earth’s ambient 50 μT, vertically aligned field. The coil system has 150 turns and radius 0.6 m, aligned horizontally. At a given instant, the fluid velocity decreases linearly from 0.8 m/s to 0.2 m/s over 10 seconds. Calculate the average emf induced in the coil during this interval.

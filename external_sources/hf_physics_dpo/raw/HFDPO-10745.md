# HFDPO-10745

In a high-voltage fusion reactor, a pulsed current of 50 kA must be delivered in 20 μs to compress a plasma via magnetic confinement. To achieve this, a bank of solid-state capacitors is charged to 12 kV and discharged through a superconducting coil with 0.8 μH inductance. However, oscillations in the discharge circuit can destabilize plasma confinement. What is the minimum capacitance required to limit the peak current ripple to 10% of the average discharge current, while ensuring the voltage across the capacitors doesn’t drop below 9 kV during the pulse?

# HFDPO-01798

A Mars rover navigating the Valles Marineris canyon encounters a 12° incline covered in fine, cohesionless regolith with a dynamic friction coefficient of 0.32 and a drag coefficient of 0.48 (based on wind tunnel tests). During a dust storm, wind speed reaches 35 m/s, increasing effective drag on the rover’s body by 40%.

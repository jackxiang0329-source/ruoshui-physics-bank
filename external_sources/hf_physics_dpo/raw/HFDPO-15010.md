# HFDPO-15010

A wind turbine in a remote mountain valley generates 2.5 MW of power during peak winds, but local engineers notice voltage fluctuations when transmission lines connect to the grid. The turbine’s 300-turn rotor coil (radius 1.2 m) spins at 180 rpm in a uniform magnetic field of 0.4 T. During a sudden wind gust, the rotational speed increases to 210 rpm over 4 seconds. What is the average induced EMF in the coil during this acceleration, and how does this transient EMF affect grid synchronization?

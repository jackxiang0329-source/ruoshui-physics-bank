# HFDPO-16157

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The system stores 1.2 GJ of thermal energy in 15,000 kg of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1,400 J/(kg·K), initially at 250°C. At night, the salt cools to 180°C while transferring heat to a steam turbine. If the turbine operates at 38% thermodynamic efficiency, how much electrical energy can be extracted? Assume no heat loss to the environment and that the turbine operates on a Carnot cycle between the salt temperature and ambient air at 20°C.

# HFDPO-13242

A Mars rover's wheel, designed for loose regolith, begins to lose traction during a slope descent, causing a "tumble" motion where the wheel alternates between rolling and slipping. As the slope angle increases from 12° to 20°, the wheel’s angular acceleration shifts unpredictably. Estimate the critical slope angle at which rolling transitions to slip-dominated motion, considering both static friction limits and aerodynamic drag. Is the transition smooth or abrupt, and why?

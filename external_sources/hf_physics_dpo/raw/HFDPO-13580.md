# HFDPO-13580

A research team aboard an Arctic research vessel detects anomalous microwave signals (frequency = 9.4 GHz) from a previously unknown subsurface structure beneath 120 meters of permafrost. The signal strength drops sharply at depths beyond 150 m. Given the permafrost's complex dielectric properties—relative permittivity ε_r = 3.2 at 0°C, decreasing to 2.1 at -15°C—and a thermal gradient of 0.5°C/m, determine the depth at which the electromagnetic wave's amplitude is reduced to 10% of its surface value. Assume the wave propagates vertically and the permafrost has a small but non-negligible conductivity of 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ S/m.

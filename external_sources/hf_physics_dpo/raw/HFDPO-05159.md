# HFDPO-05159

In a prototype neural interface for next-gen brain-computer implants, nanoscale electrodes are coated with a dielectric layer to prevent tissue damage from leakage currents. The system operates in a saline-rich environment (ε_r ≈ 80), and each electrode has a capacitance of 4.5 pF at 100 Hz. However, during in-vivo testing, the effective capacitance drops to 2.8 pF due to ion migration at the electrode-dielectric interface. What is the effective thickness of the degraded dielectric layer if the original was 15 nm, and how does this change affect the energy storage capacity at a fixed voltage?

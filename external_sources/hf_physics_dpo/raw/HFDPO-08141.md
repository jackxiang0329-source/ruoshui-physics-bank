# HFDPO-08141

A drone designed for precision delivery in mountainous terrain must navigate a narrow canyon where wind gusts vary nonlinearly with height due to terrain-induced turbulence. At 100 m altitude, the horizontal wind speed is 12 m/s, increasing to 24 m/s at 150 m due to a jet-like effect. The drone, mass 4.5 kg, enters the canyon at 130 m with a velocity of 8 m/s horizontal and 0 m/s vertical, but must maintain a stable hover at 140 m above a moving obstacle. Assuming the wind force is proportional to the square of the relative wind speed and the drone’s thrust can adjust instantaneously, what thrust magnitude and direction are needed at the moment it reaches 140 m, and how does the thrust direction change as it ascends?

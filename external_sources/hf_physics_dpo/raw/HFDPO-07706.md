# HFDPO-07706

A solar farm in Nevada uses ground-mounted solar panels tilted at 30° to optimize winter sunlight. Each panel is 2.5 m long, 1.2 m wide, and weighs 45 kg. Strong desert winds (up to 60 km/h) create a distributed pressure of 350 Pa across the panel’s surface. The panel is mounted with a single pivot at its base and secured by a cable anchored 1.5 m behind the pivot. What minimum tension must the cable withstand to prevent the panel from lifting during peak wind?

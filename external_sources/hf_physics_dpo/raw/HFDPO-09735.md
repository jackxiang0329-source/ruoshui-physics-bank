# HFDPO-09735

A Mars rover's rear wheel gets stuck in fine, electrostatically charged regolith during a dust storm. The wheel exerts 1,200 N of normal force, and the regolith’s effective coefficient of static friction rises to 0.83 due to particle adhesion, but the dynamic friction drops to 0.41 once motion begins. However, as the wheel spins, it generates a drag force from displaced dust particles, modeled as F_d = 0.02 v^2 (in N, with v in m/s). What minimum torque must the motor apply to initiate motion, and what is the wheel’s terminal speed if the motor applies a constant 80 N·m torque?

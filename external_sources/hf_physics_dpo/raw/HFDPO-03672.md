# HFDPO-03672

During a deep-space communication test, a spacecraft orbiting Mars transmits a 10.5 GHz signal toward Earth using a phased array antenna. Due to plasma disturbances in the ionosphere, the received signal experiences anomalous phase shifts and amplitude oscillations, with a modulation depth of 18% at Earth’s 2.4 GHz receiving frequency. The signal’s polarization rotates by 47° over a 30-minute window. What is the minimum electron density gradient in the ionosphere that could explain both the Faraday rotation and the amplitude modulation, assuming the signal traverses 150 km of ionospheric plasma along a slant path?

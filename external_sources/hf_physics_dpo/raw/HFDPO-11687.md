# HFDPO-11687

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain precise power delivery to its sensors and communication systems while navigating unpredictable cloud cover. The drone’s power system uses a 24 V lithium-ion battery bank connected in parallel with a photovoltaic array (rated at 30 V open-circuit, 12 A short-circuit), both feeding a 12 V regulated power bus via a DC-DC buck converter. The buck converter has a 90% efficiency and is only rated for 50 W input. Calculate the minimum required battery capacity (in Ah) to sustain full operation for 3 hours during continuous cloud cover, assuming the array produces no power.

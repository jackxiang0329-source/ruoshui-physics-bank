# HFDPO-04739

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a thin, uniformly charged silicon wafer (radius 15 cm, surface charge density +2.4 μC/m²) is suspended electrostatically between two parallel plates (separation 1.2 cm). A stray dust particle (mass 3.5 ng, charge +120 e) approaches from below. If the particle begins 0.8 cm below the wafer with zero initial velocity, at what height above the wafer does it momentarily stop before falling back? Assume the wafer’s field dominates locally and the system is in equilibrium when no external forces act.

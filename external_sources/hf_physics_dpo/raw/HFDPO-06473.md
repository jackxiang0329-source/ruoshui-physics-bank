# HFDPO-06473

During a cold snap, a city’s water distribution system experiences freezing risks. A 200 m long steel pipeline buried 1.5 m underground carries water at 4°C. If the soil temperature drops to -5°C and the thermal conductivity of soil is 1.8 W/m·K, how long will it take for ice to begin forming at the pipe’s inner surface? Assume the water is well-mixed and the pipe wall is thin. Consider heat transfer from water to soil via conduction and phase change.

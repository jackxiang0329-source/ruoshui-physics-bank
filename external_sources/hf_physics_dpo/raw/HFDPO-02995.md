# HFDPO-02995

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 400°C, which then transfers thermal energy to a molten salt storage system. During a 6-hour night, the plant must sustain output using only stored heat. The salt tank (80,000 kg, specific heat 1,400 J/kg·K) cools from 560°C to 320°C, but the system loses 12% of the stored energy via conduction and radiation to the environment. If the plant’s thermal-to-electrical efficiency drops from 38% to 32% as temperature decreases, by what percentage does the **effective energy available for electricity generation** decrease compared to an ideal, lossless scenario?

# HFDPO-16480

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using size-based filtration in a narrow channel (50 μm wide). Given that blood has an effective viscosity of 3.5 mPa·s and the channel length is 10 mm, what is the pressure drop required to sustain this flow, and how does a 10% increase in viscosity due to concentrated platelets affect the flow rate if the same pressure drop is applied?

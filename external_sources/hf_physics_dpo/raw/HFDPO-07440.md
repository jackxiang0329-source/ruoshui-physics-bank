# HFDPO-07440

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, wearable heart monitor that uses a capacitive sensor to detect subtle chest movements during respiration. The sensor consists of two parallel plates, each 1.5 cm by 3.0 cm, separated by a 0.1 mm thick flexible dielectric film (κ = 4.5). When the patient inhales, the distance between the plates increases by 10% due to chest expansion.

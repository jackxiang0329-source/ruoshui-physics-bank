# HFDPO-08318

In a cutting-edge neuromorphic computing chip, a neural synapse is emulated using a variable capacitor made of graphene layers separated by a 2-nm-thick hafnium oxide dielectric. During operation, the dielectric thickness dynamically changes due to ion drift, altering capacitance. If the initial capacitance is 50 fF and the dielectric thickness increases by 0.5 nm over 100 ms under an applied voltage of 1.2 V, what is the instantaneous rate of energy stored in the capacitor during this transition, assuming the voltage remains constant and the area is 100 μm²?

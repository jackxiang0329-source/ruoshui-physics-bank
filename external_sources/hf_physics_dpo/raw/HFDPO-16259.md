# HFDPO-16259

In a high-vacuum semiconductor manufacturing chamber, a 15 cm diameter silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of -2.4 μC/m² is being transported between two grounded metal rails. As it moves, triboelectric charging from the transport belt induces an additional surface charge of +1.8 μC/m² on the wafer’s edge. The wafer is suspended by electrostatic forces from a set of three parallel, charged electrodes spaced 8 mm above the wafer’s surface, each 5 cm wide and 20 cm long. What is the minimum voltage required on each electrode to maintain stable levitation without mechanical contact, assuming the system operates at room temperature and negligible air resistance?

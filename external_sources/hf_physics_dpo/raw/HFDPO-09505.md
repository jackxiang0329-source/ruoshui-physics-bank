# HFDPO-09505

A geologist discovers a strange anomaly in a remote region: a 30-meter-long iron-rich rock formation aligns perfectly with Earth’s magnetic field, but its internal magnetization is 15% stronger than expected from crustal remnant magnetism. If the local Earth’s magnetic field is 50 μT at 65° inclination, what is the net magnetic dipole moment of the rock, and how might the rock have acquired such strong, oriented magnetization?

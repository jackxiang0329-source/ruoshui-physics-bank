# HFDPO-00289

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 12 cm × 12 cm silicon wafer is being charged during plasma processing, accumulating a non-uniform surface charge density σ(r) = σ₀(1 − r²/R²), where r is radial distance from center, R = 6 cm, and σ₀ = 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ C/m². Engineers observe that charged dust particles (mass 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁵ kg, charge −1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C) are levitating unpredictably above the wafer’s edge.

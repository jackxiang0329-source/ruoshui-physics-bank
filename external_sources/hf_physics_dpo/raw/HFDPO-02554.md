# HFDPO-02554

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote village powers a water pump via an AC generator, but the pump's motor draws a lagging current, causing the voltage at the pump to drop below the required 220 V RMS. The generator outputs 240 V RMS at 60 Hz, and the transmission line has a resistance of 2.0 Ω and inductance of 15 mH. The pump motor has a rated power of 3.0 kW at 0.70 power factor. What capacitance, placed in parallel at the pump’s input, will restore the voltage to 220 V RMS across the motor?

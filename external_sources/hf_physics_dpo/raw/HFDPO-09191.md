# HFDPO-09191

A 120-meter-tall wind turbine in a coastal region experiences severe torsional oscillations during high-wind events, threatening blade integrity. The turbine’s rotor shaft behaves as a torsional pendulum with a rotational spring constant of 3.2 × 10⁶ N·m/rad and a moment of inertia of 4.5 × 10⁴ kg·m². Wind gusts apply a sinusoidal torque of amplitude 1.8 × 10⁵ N·m at a frequency of 0.8 Hz, while damping is modeled as viscous with a damping coefficient of 1.1 × 10⁴ N·m·s/rad. Determine whether the system is likely to experience dangerous resonance, and if so, what modification to the shaft’s stiffness would eliminate the peak amplitude.

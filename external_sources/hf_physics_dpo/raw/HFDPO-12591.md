# HFDPO-12591

A wind turbine in a remote alpine valley experiences sudden gusts exceeding 30 m/s during storm season. The blades, each 60 m long and with a mass of 12,000 kg, are designed to pitch to reduce rotational inertia when wind exceeds 25 m/s. However, during a storm, the pitch system fails, and the blades spin freely. If the turbine’s generator locks at 100 rpm due to grid overload, what is the minimum energy dissipated by the braking system to prevent blade structural failure, given that the blades can withstand a maximum kinetic energy of 2.1 × 10⁹ J?

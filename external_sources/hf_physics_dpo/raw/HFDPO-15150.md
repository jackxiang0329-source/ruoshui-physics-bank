# HFDPO-15150

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to focus sunlight onto a central receiver tube, heating synthetic oil to 390°C. During a sudden cloud cover event, the system loses solar input, but the oil continues to transfer heat to a storage tank at 280°C. The receiver tube has a diameter of 8 cm, a length of 15 m, and is insulated with a 5 cm thick layer of silica aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K). If the ambient temperature drops to 15°C overnight, how long will it take for the oil temperature to fall to 300°C? Assume the oil has a specific heat of 1.8 kJ/kg·K and a mass flow rate of 0.5 kg/s, and neglect internal convection resistance.

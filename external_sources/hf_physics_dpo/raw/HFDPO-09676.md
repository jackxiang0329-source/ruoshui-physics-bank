# HFDPO-09676

A fusion reactor's magnetic confinement system uses a toroidal superconducting coil array to stabilize plasma at 150 million K. During a pulse, a sudden quench in one coil generates a transient axial magnetic field of 2.4 T over 5 ms, inducing eddy currents in the adjacent stainless steel vacuum vessel (resistivity ρ = 7.2 × 10⁻⁷ Ω·m, thickness 5 cm). The vessel’s effective cross-sectional area for current flow is 2.8 m². Calculate the peak electromagnetic force per unit length on the vessel wall due to the interaction between the induced current and the changing magnetic field, and discuss how this force could compromise structural integrity over repeated pulses.

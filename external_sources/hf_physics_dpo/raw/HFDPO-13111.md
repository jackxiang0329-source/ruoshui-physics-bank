# HFDPO-13111

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The implant contains a 20 μH inductor in series with a 50 pF capacitor, while the external transmitter uses a 50 μH inductor. The system operates at a frequency where the impedance of the implant’s LC circuit reaches minimum (resonant condition). However, due to tissue absorption, the effective resistance in the implant’s circuit increases to 8 Ω. If the transmitter delivers 1.6 W of average power at 2.5 MHz, what is the peak current through the implant’s inductor, and how does the phase angle between voltage and current change compared to an ideal resonant case?

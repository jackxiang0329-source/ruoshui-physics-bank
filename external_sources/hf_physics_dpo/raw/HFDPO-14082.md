# HFDPO-14082

A bioengineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate exosomes (30–150 nm diameter) from blood plasma using a serpentine channel with 100 µm width and 10 µm height. The chip operates at 37°C with a flow rate of 1 µL/min. Due to the small scale, viscous forces dominate, and flow is fully developed laminar. How does the velocity profile in the channel differ from that in a macroscopic pipe, and what is the maximum shear stress experienced by exosomes near the walls? Assume plasma has a viscosity of 1.2 cP and density of 1030 kg/m³.

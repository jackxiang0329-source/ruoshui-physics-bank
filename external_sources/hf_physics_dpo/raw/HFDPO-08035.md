# HFDPO-08035

A marine biologist studying coral reef health notices that underwater acoustic signals from a newly installed tidal energy turbine disrupt the communication patterns of reef fish. The device emits a continuous 500 Hz sound wave, and measurements show the wave travels through seawater at 1,500 m/s. When the wave reaches the reef, it partially reflects off a 30 m deep coral ledge and partially transmits into the shallower water above. The biologist wants to determine the phase shift between the incident and reflected waves at the reef surface, assuming total internal reflection occurs at the interface. What is the phase change upon reflection, and how does this affect the interference pattern detected by underwater hydrophones?

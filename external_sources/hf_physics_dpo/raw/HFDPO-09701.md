# HFDPO-09701

A coastal city is designing a new seawater desalination plant that intakes water from a tidal inlet where the average flow speed varies from 0.3 m/s at low tide to 1.8 m/s at high tide. Engineers must ensure the intake pipes don’t induce cavitation, which occurs when local pressure drops below water’s vapor pressure (2.3 kPa at 25°C). Given that the inlet has a 2 m diameter pipe and the ambient pressure is 101.3 kPa, what is the maximum depth at which the intake can be placed without risk of cavitation during peak flow? Assume steady, incompressible flow and neglect minor losses.

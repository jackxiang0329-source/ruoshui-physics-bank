# HFDPO-08915

A prototype quantum sensor based on nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond is being tested for detecting minute magnetic anomalies in geological surveys. The sensor operates at cryogenic temperatures (~4 K) to suppress thermal noise, yet unexpected signal drift occurs over time, degrading sensitivity. Measurements show a 2.3% shift in the NV center’s spin resonance frequency over 12 hours under constant magnetic field conditions.

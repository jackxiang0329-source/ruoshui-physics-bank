# HFDPO-11039

A drone performing precision aerial surveying in a coastal forest suddenly encounters a sudden wind gust of 12 m/s perpendicular to its forward motion at an altitude of 60 m. The drone’s mass is 3.2 kg, its cross-sectional area is 0.45 m², and its drag coefficient is 0.8. If the drone's control system can apply a corrective thrust of up to 20 N at any angle, determine whether it can maintain its intended flight path (within 2° of original heading) over the next 4 seconds, given that wind-induced drag is proportional to the square of the relative wind speed. Assume the wind is constant and the drone's aerodynamic center is aligned with its center of mass.

# HFDPO-09865

A solar-powered drone operating at high altitude relies on a custom DC power distribution network using three parallel solar arrays, each with internal resistance of 0.5 Ω and an open-circuit voltage of 36 V. The drone’s payload system draws 15 A at 24 V, but during a critical descent, the arrays are partially shaded, reducing their effective voltage to 28 V each. To maintain stable voltage to the payload, the pilot activates a supercapacitor bank (12 F, already charged to 28 V) in parallel with the arrays. What is the minimum capacitance required to sustain the payload voltage above 23 V for 12 seconds during the shaded period?

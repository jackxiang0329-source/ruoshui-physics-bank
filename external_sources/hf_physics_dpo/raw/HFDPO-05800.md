# HFDPO-05800

A small biomedical device uses a coil to generate a changing magnetic field to stimulate nerve cells in a patient’s arm. The coil has 100 turns and a radius of 2 cm. If the current in the coil increases from 0 to 3 A in 50 milliseconds, what is the magnitude of the induced electric field at a point 4 cm from the center of the coil, assuming the field is uniform over the coil’s area?

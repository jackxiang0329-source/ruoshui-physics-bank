# HFDPO-03402

During a desert heatwave, a solar-powered refrigeration unit in a remote medical outpost fails to keep vaccines cool. The unit uses a heat engine driven by a 150°C solar collector, rejecting heat to ambient air at 45°C. The refrigeration cycle requires a minimum 15°C temperature difference between the cold reservoir and the ambient air to operate efficiently. Given that the heat engine’s actual efficiency is 50% of the Carnot efficiency and the refrigeration cycle’s coefficient of performance is 3.0, can the system maintain the required cold reservoir temperature?

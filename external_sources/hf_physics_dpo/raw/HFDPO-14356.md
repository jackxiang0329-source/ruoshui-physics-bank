# HFDPO-14356

A coastal tidal energy turbine array in the Pentland Firth operates under extreme hydrodynamic forces, with each turbine blade spanning 18 m and rotating about a central shaft. During a storm surge, the system experiences a sudden 35° tilt due to asymmetric seabed sediment displacement, introducing a 420 kN lateral force at the blade’s tip. The shaft must remain in static equilibrium despite this off-axis loading. Calculate the required reaction torque at the base to prevent rotation, assuming the blade's center of mass is 7.2 m from the shaft and it weighs 110 kN.

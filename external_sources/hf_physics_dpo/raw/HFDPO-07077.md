# HFDPO-07077

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform charge density due to ion beam exposure—peaking at 8.5 nC/cm² at the center and dropping linearly to zero at the edges. Determine whether this force exceeds the gravitational pull by more than 100×, and critically evaluate if the assumption of a uniform field is justified in this context.

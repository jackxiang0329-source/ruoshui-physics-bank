# HFDPO-14314

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a flexible composite tendon to suspend a 12,000 kg sensor package at the end of a 300 m vertical borehole. Due to thermal gradients, the tendon’s linear density varies with depth as λ(z) = 8.5 + 0.012z kg/m (where z is depth from the surface). The top of the tendon is anchored at the rig’s surface and fixed in place. What is the maximum tension in the tendon, and what is the vertical displacement at the anchor point due to the tendon’s own weight and non-uniform stretching? Assume the tendon has a Young’s modulus of 3.2 GPa and a cross-sectional area of 0.025 m².

# HFDPO-03816

The graft must withstand cyclic pulsatile pressure (0–120 mmHg) over 100,000 cycles without failure. The mean radius is 5 mm and wall thickness is 0.2 mm. Determine the maximum strain the material can sustain to stay within safe fatigue limits, assuming fatigue occurs at 1% strain amplitude under cyclic loading.

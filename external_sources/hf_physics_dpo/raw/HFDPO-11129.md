# HFDPO-11129

In a cutting-edge wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices, a 100 kHz AC source drives a primary coil (L₁ = 50 μH, R₁ = 0.5 Ω) near a secondary coil (L₂ = 45 μH, R₂ = 0.3 Ω) with mutual inductance M = 12 μH. What is the minimum value of series capacitance needed on the primary side to maintain resonance at the operating frequency?

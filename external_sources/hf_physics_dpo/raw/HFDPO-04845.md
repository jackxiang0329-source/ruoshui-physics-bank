# HFDPO-04845

A high-efficiency fusion reactor’s divertor tiles, made of tungsten alloy with a thermal conductivity of 120 W/m·K and emissivity 0.35, are exposed to a sustained plasma heat flux of 15 MW/m². After 2 hours of operation, thermal stress fractures emerge at the tile base.

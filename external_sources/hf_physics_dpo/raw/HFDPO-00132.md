# HFDPO-00132

During a deep-space mission, a spacecraft’s thermal shield is exposed to a stream of interstellar hydrogen atoms at 12,000 K, moving at velocities near 1.2 × 10⁵ m/s. The shield’s surface is maintained at 300 K, and its material has a molecular binding energy of 4.8 eV per atom. Estimate the rate of energy deposition per square meter due to inelastic collisions, considering both momentum transfer and internal excitation, and determine whether the shield will sustain structural damage over 10 hours of exposure.

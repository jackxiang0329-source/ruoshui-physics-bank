# HFDPO-12381

In a bioengineered microfluidic device designed to separate circulating tumor cells from blood, a 50 μm diameter spherical cell flows through a 200 μm wide channel lined with anti-adhesion polymer brushes. The blood plasma has a viscosity of 1.2 cP, and the cell moves at 0.8 mm/s relative to the wall. Due to the polymer layer’s softness, the effective slip length at the wall is 15 μm. What is the hydrodynamic drag force on the cell, and how does this slip reduce the force compared to a no-slip wall?

# HFDPO-03484

In a high-altitude research drone designed to sample atmospheric aerosols at 12 km, engineers notice erratic altitude fluctuations despite stable thrust. The drone’s propulsion system uses a ducted fan with a 0.8 m diameter inlet and a 0.4 m diameter nozzle. At cruise speed of 35 m/s, air enters the inlet at 10% of static pressure at that altitude (20 kPa). If the fan imparts 1200 W of power to the air, what is the actual thrust efficiency? Is the pressure recovery at the nozzle sufficient to prevent flow separation, and how would you redesign the duct geometry to maximize thrust while maintaining laminar flow?

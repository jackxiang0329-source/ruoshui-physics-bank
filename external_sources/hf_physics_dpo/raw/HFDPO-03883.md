# HFDPO-03883

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses adjustable solar panels mounted on a single pivot axis to track the sun, but engineers notice a structural issue: during high winds (up to 60 mph), the mounting arms experience uneven stress, risking fatigue failure. Each panel (1.8 m × 3.2 m) weighs 35 kg and is mounted 0.4 m from the pivot, while the support arm (12 m long, 0.15 m wide, 0.1 m deep) is made of aluminum alloy with a density of 2700 kg/m³. The center of mass of the arm is offset 0.5 m from the pivot due to cable routing. Calculate the required torque at the pivot to maintain static equilibrium when the panels are fully extended and the wind exerts a uniform pressure of 450 Pa (acting perpendicular to the panel surface) at a 30° angle to the vertical.

# HFDPO-08233

A team at a quantum materials lab observes anomalous charge oscillations in a two-dimensional electron gas confined within a twisted bilayer graphene heterostructure at a twist angle of 1.05°, with a measured resistance plateau at 1.5 kΩ under a 10 T magnetic field. The data shows clear evidence of fractional quantum Hall-like states, but the expected Landau level filling factor ν = 1/3 is not observed. The group suspects that emergent particle-like excitations—fractionalized anyons—may be mediating the observed conductivity, but no direct signature of their statistics has been measured. How can they design an interferometric experiment using interferometers fabricated with Josephson junctions to distinguish whether the quasiparticles are Abelian or non-Abelian anyons?

# HFDPO-13472

A marine biologist studying coral reef health notices that sound from a passing cargo ship (120 dB at 10 m) is disrupting the feeding behavior of a sensitive reef fish species. The fish are most affected when sound intensity exceeds 90 dB at their habitat, located 500 m from the ship’s path. Given that sound intensity decreases with the square of distance and the ocean layering causes wavefront spreading that increases the effective attenuation to inverse-cube law, what is the minimum safe distance (in meters) the ship must maintain to avoid harming the reef ecosystem?

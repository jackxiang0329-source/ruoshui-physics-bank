# HFDPO-13381

A wind turbine blade, 80 meters long and shaped like a thin, rigid airfoil with a non-uniform mass distribution (density increasing linearly from 15 kg/m at the tip to 45 kg/m at the hub), is mounted on a frictionless pivot at the hub. Strong gusts exert a distributed lift force perpendicular to the blade, peaking at 12 kN/m at the midpoint and dropping to zero at both ends due to vortex shedding. What is the minimum tension in the guy wire (attached at 60 m from the hub, 30° below horizontal) required to keep the blade in static equilibrium at a 15° angle above horizontal during high-wind operation?

# HFDPO-07457

A fusion reactor's plasma confinement system uses a toroidal magnetic field generated by external coils, but localized eddy currents in the conducting vessel wall are destabilizing the plasma boundary. At t = 0, the current in the primary coil ramps up linearly from 0 to 1.2 MA over 300 ms, inducing a time-varying magnetic flux through the wall. The vessel has a resistivity of 1.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, thickness 10 cm, and radial extent 0.5 m. Using Maxwell’s equations, determine whether the induced electric field in the wall exceeds the critical threshold for plasma turbulence (E_c = 3×10⁴ V/m) during the ramp, and assess the role of displacement current in the total curl of the electric field.

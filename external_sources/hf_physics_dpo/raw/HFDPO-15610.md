# HFDPO-15610

A satellite in geostationary orbit above Mars must maintain a precise altitude to stay fixed over a specific point on the equator. However, due to Mars’ slight oblateness and the gravitational pull from its moon Phobos, the satellite’s orbit gradually drifts. If the satellite is currently 18,800 km above the Martian surface (measured from the center), and Phobos exerts a gravitational acceleration of 0.0003 m/s² at that distance, at what rate must the satellite adjust its thrusters to counteract Phobos’ pull and remain stable?

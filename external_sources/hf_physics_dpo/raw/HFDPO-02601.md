# HFDPO-02601

A quantum computing lab in Zurich uses superconducting qubits cooled to 15 mK, where fluctuating magnetic fields from nearby MRI machines induce noise that collapses quantum states. The lab’s shielded room has a 3 mm thick aluminum wall (σ = 3.5 × 10⁷ S/m) surrounding a 1 m × 1 m × 1 m cubic enclosure. A nearby MRI generates a time-varying magnetic field of 50 μT at 1.5 MHz outside the room. Calculate the magnetic field amplitude inside the shielded volume, assuming perfect conductivity is not achieved and skin depth effects dominate. How does the shielding effectiveness depend on frequency?

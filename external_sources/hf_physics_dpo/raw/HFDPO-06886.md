# HFDPO-06886

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical shaft 2.4 km deep to tap into hot rock at 320°C, extracting heat via circulating water. The system must pump 120 kg/s of cold water (15°C) from the surface down to the hot zone, where it absorbs energy and returns as steam at 200°C. During one 30-minute cycle, the plant produces 24 MWh of electrical energy. Calculate the minimum work input required by the pumps to sustain this cycle, and evaluate whether the pump work is a significant fraction of the total energy output.

# HFDPO-11145

A deep-space telescope orbiting Jupiter captures faint light from a distant exoplanet, but the image is blurred due to a misaligned secondary mirror. The primary mirror has a 3.5 m diameter and f = 12 m, while the secondary mirror is a 0.6 m diameter concave element positioned 11.2 m from the primary’s vertex. The spacecraft’s control algorithm assumes the secondary is perfectly aligned, but a sensor glitch caused a 0.8 mm lateral offset and 1.5° tilt. Calculate the radius of the blurred image spot on the detector, 0.5 m behind the focal plane, considering both misalignment and spherical aberration from the primary’s f/3.4 design.

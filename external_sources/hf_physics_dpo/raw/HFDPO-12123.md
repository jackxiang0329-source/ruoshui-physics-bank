# HFDPO-12123

A research team is developing a wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices using a rotating magnetic field generated by a surface coil. The coil, with 120 turns and radius 8 cm, rotates at 300 rpm in a uniform 1.5 T magnetic field. The implanted receiver, a circular loop of radius 2 mm with 50 turns, lies perpendicular to the field axis at a distance of 10 cm from the center of the transmitter. However, the receiver’s output voltage is below design specs. Calculate the peak induced emf in the receiver, considering both the time-varying flux due to the rotating coil and the spatial gradient of the magnetic field over the receiver’s area.

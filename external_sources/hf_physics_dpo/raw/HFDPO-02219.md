# HFDPO-02219

A lunar sample return capsule, descending at 25 m/s through the thin Martian atmosphere, deploys a supersonic parachute at an altitude of 8 km. The parachute’s drag coefficient is 1.35, and its effective area is 120 m², but atmospheric density varies exponentially with altitude, modeled as ρ(h) = 0.015e^(-h/8000) kg/m³ (h in meters). The capsule has a mass of 480 kg and experiences a constant thrust from retrorockets beginning at 3 km altitude to limit peak deceleration to 4g. Determine the minimum thrust required from the retrorockets to maintain this limit during the descent, assuming the parachute remains fully effective throughout.

# HFDPO-06917

In a high-altitude weather balloon experiment, a sensitive atmospheric sensor operates on a 12 V power supply but requires stable voltage during rapid pressure changes. The system uses a 50 μF capacitor in parallel with the sensor to smooth voltage drops. As the balloon ascends, the ambient pressure drops from 100 kPa to 25 kPa in 30 seconds, causing the sensor’s capacitance to increase by 20% due to dielectric polarization in its packaging. If the sensor draws a constant current of 1.5 mA during measurement, what is the minimum initial charge the capacitor must have to prevent voltage from dropping below 10 V during ascent?

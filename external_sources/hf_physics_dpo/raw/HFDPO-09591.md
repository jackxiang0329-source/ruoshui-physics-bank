# HFDPO-09591

A deep-sea mining corporation plans to extract polymetallic nodules from the abyssal plain at 5,000 meters depth, where the water is near-freezing (2°C) and under 500 atm pressure. The nodules contain hydrated iron and manganese oxides that undergo phase transitions during extraction. If the material is brought to the surface rapidly, internal stresses from phase shifts (due to dropping pressure and rising temperature) could shatter the nodules, reducing recovery efficiency. Given that the phase transformation from hydrated oxide to anhydrous form has a molar enthalpy of transition ΔH_trans = 42 kJ/mol and a critical rate of pressure drop for stability is 10 atm/min, determine the minimum safe ascent speed (m/min) to prevent irreversible phase collapse, treating the material as a heterogeneous system with finite heat and mass transfer rates.

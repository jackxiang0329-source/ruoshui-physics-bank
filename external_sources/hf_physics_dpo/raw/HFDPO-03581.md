# HFDPO-03581

During a desert heatwave, a solar-powered water purifier in a remote village begins malfunctioning—condensation forms unevenly on the inner surface of its curved glass cover, causing water droplets to slide off before collecting, reducing output by 40%. The inside surface temperature of the cover is measured at 42°C, but the system is losing efficiency due to localized hot spots. Estimate the net radiative heat loss per unit area from the cover's inner surface and explain how this contributes to non-uniform condensation.

# HFDPO-07406

A drone-mounted laser rangefinder is used to measure the depth of a glacial crevasse in Antarctica. The laser beam, emitted at a 15° angle below the horizontal, reflects off the ice wall at the bottom and returns to the sensor. Due to atmospheric refraction, the beam bends at the air-ice interface, shifting the apparent reflection point by 0.8 m horizontally. If the actual depth of the crevasse is 32 m and the refractive index of ice is 1.31, what is the angle of refraction inside the ice?

# HFDPO-03130

A fusion reactor prototype uses a spherical plasma containment vessel with a radius of 2.4 m, where deuterium-tritium fuel is compressed to 10⁸ K and 500 atm. During a sudden magnetic field instability, 12% of the plasma mass (initially 0.035 kg) escapes radially through a 25 cm diameter port at 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s, causing a recoil impulse on the vessel. Meanwhile, the remaining plasma undergoes asymmetric thermal expansion, emitting 1.2 × 10¹⁰ J of radiation in 1.5 ms. Calculate the final recoil velocity of the reactor structure (mass: 8.2 × 10⁴ kg) and determine whether conservation of momentum and energy can both be simultaneously satisfied under the observed radiation profile.

# HFDPO-02664

During a windy day, a rooftop AC unit starts lifting slightly off its mounting frame. The unit weighs 120 kg and sits on a flat roof where air flows at 25 m/s over its top surface. The bottom remains at atmospheric pressure. If the air pressure underneath the unit is unchanged and the density of air is 1.2 kg/m³, what must be the pressure difference between the top and bottom to lift it?

# HFDPO-07325

In a novel quantum dot solar cell design, 1000 identical semiconductor nanocrystals are thermally coupled to a phonon bath at 300 K, each capable of trapping one exciton. At open-circuit conditions, the system reaches equilibrium with an average exciton occupation of 0.4 per dot. What is the effective chemical potential of the exciton reservoir, and how does its value reflect the balance between entropy maximization and energy cost of excitation? Assume the exciton states are non-degenerate and neglect Coulomb interactions.

# HFDPO-00659

The chimney is 10 m tall with an average cross-sectional area of 0.5 m², and the temperature gradient follows a nonlinear profile due to rapid cooling. If a natural electric current of 4.5 A is observed along its length due to thermoelectric effects, estimate the voltage difference across the chimney and explain why a constant-resistivity model would fail here.

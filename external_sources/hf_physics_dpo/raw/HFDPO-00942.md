# HFDPO-00942

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, irregularly shaped oil reservoir breach at 1,200 meters depth. The reservoir contains crude oil (ρ = 820 kg/m³) trapped beneath a layer of seawater (ρ = 1025 kg/m³). The breach forms a 0.8 m diameter circular portal sloping at 30° from horizontal, with its center 1,200 m below the surface. The oil is under hydrostatic equilibrium but not yet flowing. The ROV’s manipulator must seal the breach without triggering a sudden release. What is the minimum force the seal must exert perpendicular to the portal’s surface to prevent oil from escaping, considering only static pressure forces?

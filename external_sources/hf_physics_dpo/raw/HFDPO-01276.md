# HFDPO-01276

A space probe’s solar array on a mission to Jupiter experiences unexpected voltage drops during prolonged exposure to low-intensity sunlight and radiation. The array uses silicon photovoltaic cells with a bandgap of 1.12 eV, but measurements show a 14% decline in open-circuit voltage after 18 months in orbit. The radiation environment generates trap states in the bulk silicon, with a density of 2.5 × 10¹⁴ cm⁻³ at depths of 100 nm from the surface. How does the presence of these radiation-induced traps affect minority carrier lifetime and recombination dynamics in the depletion region, and what is the net impact on the photovoltage under low-light conditions (photon flux: 3.2 × 10¹⁴ m⁻²s⁻¹)?

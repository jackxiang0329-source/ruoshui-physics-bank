# HFDPO-10078

A city is testing a new bicycle-sharing program with solar-powered docking stations. Each station uses a flywheel to store energy when bikes are returned, braking the flywheel and converting kinetic energy into stored rotational energy. If a 15 kg bike is returned at 3 m/s and the flywheel starts at rest, what minimum angular speed must the 2 kg flywheel (radius 0.2 m) reach to conserve energy? Assume no friction or losses.

# HFDPO-11601

In a remote Arctic research station, a critical sensor array powered by a 12 V solar battery bank must operate continuously during the polar night, relying on a supercapacitor bank storing 2.4 kWh of energy. The station’s control system fails if voltage drops below 9.0 V. The sensors draw 0.8 A at 12 V but increase current to 1.4 A when voltage sags below 10 V. During a 72-hour blackout, the supercapacitor discharges through a variable load. Using only a single 100 μF capacitor in series with a 1.2 kΩ resistor as a voltage stabilizer (to slow discharge), what is the minimum capacitance needed to maintain operation?

# HFDPO-06806

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km into the Earth’s crust to extract heat via circulating coolant. The borehole passes through a region with a slowly varying magnetic field due to ionospheric currents, measured at 45 μT and increasing at 1.8 nT/s. A superconducting loop (diameter 80 cm) is lowered into the borehole for subsurface monitoring. When the loop is fully submerged, the induced EMF decays over time due to changing field alignment and eddy currents in surrounding rock. Calculate the maximum instantaneous induced EMF in the loop during the first 12 seconds of descent, assuming the loop’s orientation changes from perpendicular to parallel to the magnetic field at a rate of 3°/s.

# HFDPO-07698

A fusion reactor’s magnetic confinement system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) cooled to 77 K, but engineers observe sudden quenches during plasma startup. The tapes, arranged in a solenoidal coil with 120 turns, carry 2000 A, yet local hotspots appear at joints where two tape segments are spliced with a silver-soldered electrical bridge. The solder has a normal-state resistivity of 1.6×10⁻⁸ Ω·m and a cross-sectional area of 2.5 mm², but its critical current density (J_c) drops to 60% of the tape’s when heated above 80 K. Determine the minimum temperature rise at the joint that would trigger an instability, assuming the joint’s thermal conductance to the bath is 8 W/K and the power dissipation exceeds the cooling capacity.

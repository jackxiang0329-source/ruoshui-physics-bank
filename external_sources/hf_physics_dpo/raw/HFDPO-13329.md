# HFDPO-13329

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 200 m long copper coil wound around a non-magnetic core, suspended 1.5 km underground. The Earth’s magnetic field (48 μT, tilted 70° from vertical) passes through the coil.

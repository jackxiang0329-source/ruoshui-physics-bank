# HFDPO-05745

A quantum optics lab is developing a high-resolution spectroscopy setup using a custom-designed diffraction grating to analyze emission lines from a distant exoplanet’s atmosphere. The grating, etched with 15,000 lines per centimeter, is illuminated by a laser with a wavelength of 532 nm. However, due to manufacturing imperfections, the grating exhibits a slight non-uniformity in line spacing—specifically, the spacing varies sinusoidally along the grating with an amplitude of ±0.001 nm over a length of 1 cm. The lab needs to determine whether this variation will cause detectable broadening or splitting of spectral lines at the first-order diffraction peak.

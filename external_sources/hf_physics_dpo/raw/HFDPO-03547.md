# HFDPO-03547

A photovoltaic array in a remote desert installation experiences unexpected voltage drops during peak sunlight, despite no visible defects. Spectral analysis shows the sunlight contains 12% more UV radiation than standard AM1.5, and the silicon cells have a 0.3 eV bandgap with a 150 nm depletion width. The charge carrier lifetime under irradiation is measured at 80 ns, but the fill factor drops from 82% to 67% under UV-rich conditions. Evaluate whether bandgap narrowing due to carrier degeneracy or Auger recombination is the dominant loss mechanism, and determine the minimum doping concentration needed to suppress the voltage drop by reducing the effective bandgap shift to less than 15 meV.

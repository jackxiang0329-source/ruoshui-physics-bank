# HFDPO-09067

A wind turbine blade at a coastal wind farm is designed with a curved airfoil shape. During a storm, gusts reach 30 m/s, and engineers notice unexpected vibrations at the blade tips. They suspect pressure differences due to airflow speed variations across the blade surface are causing flutter.

# HFDPO-03043

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a small Stirling engine powered by a propane heater to generate electricity for its instruments. The heater maintains the hot reservoir at 500 K, while the cold reservoir (ambient air) averages 250 K. The engine outputs 120 W of useful power but suffers from poor heat transfer due to ice buildup on the radiator fins. If the station’s battery requires 150 W of continuous power and the engine’s actual efficiency is 35% of the Carnot limit, what is the minimum heat input rate (in W) the heater must sustain to meet the power demand?

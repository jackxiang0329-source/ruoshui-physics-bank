# HFDPO-00336

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (10 cm diameter, 50 μH inductance) operates at 13.56 MHz, while the receiver coil (8 cm diameter, 30 μH) is embedded in a pacemaker. Both coils have resistances of 2 Ω and 1.5 Ω, respectively, and the mutual inductance between them is 2.1 μH. The system must deliver at least 5 mW to the receiver with 70% efficiency under a 25 V peak AC input. Determine whether the system meets the efficiency target, and explain how altering the coupling coefficient or frequency affects performance when the pacemaker shifts slightly within the body.

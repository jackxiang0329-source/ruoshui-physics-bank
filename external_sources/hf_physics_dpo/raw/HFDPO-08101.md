# HFDPO-08101

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a wafer coated with a 500 nm thick dielectric layer (ε_r = 4.5) accumulates a surface charge density of −2.5 μC/m² during plasma processing. When lifted by an electrostatic chuck using a 150 V DC bias, the chuck induces a compensating positive charge on the wafer’s underside. However, due to a design flaw in the chuck’s electrode spacing, the electric field near the wafer’s edge exceeds 1.2 MV/m, causing localized arcing and microcracks. Determine the minimum safe electrode spacing to prevent breakdown, assuming air breakdown occurs at 3.0 MV/m, and evaluate whether the current design is viable under these conditions.

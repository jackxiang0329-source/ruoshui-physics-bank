# HFDPO-10185

In a high-efficiency photovoltaic farm, inverters convert DC from solar panels to grid-compatible AC, but harmonic distortion degrades power quality. A 400 V, 50 Hz three-phase inverter feeds a 120 kW load with a non-linear current draw (THD = 18%) due to rectifier-based equipment. A single-phase line-to-neutral voltage measurement shows a fundamental current of 150 A RMS at 0.95 lagging power factor, but phase current harmonics are detected at 11th and 13th orders, each at 22 A RMS. Evaluate whether the system exceeds IEEE 519-2022 harmonic limits for voltage distortion at the point of common coupling (PCC), and determine if a passive LC filter tuned to 11th harmonic is sufficient to meet compliance.

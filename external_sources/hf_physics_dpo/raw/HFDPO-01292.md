# HFDPO-01292

A satellite network designed to monitor Earth's gravitational anomalies uses precise atomic clocks synchronized to within nanoseconds. During a test orbit, engineers notice a consistent 45 microsecond daily drift in the satellite's clock relative to ground-based clocks. Given the satellite orbits at 20,200 km above Earth’s surface, determine whether this drift is primarily due to special relativistic time dilation, general relativistic gravitational redshift, or a combination of both.

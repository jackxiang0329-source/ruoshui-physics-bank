# HFDPO-05061

A fusion reactor's plasma confinement system experiences a sudden magnetic field collapse, causing a 2.3-meter-long plasma filament to violently recoil backward at 15 km/s into a surrounding metal wall. The filament has a mass of 1.8 × 10⁻⁴ kg and carries a net linear momentum of 2.7 kg·m/s. Engineers must estimate the impulse delivered to the wall to assess structural integrity, but the exact duration of the impact is unknown. Using only conservation of momentum and energy, and assuming the wall is fixed and perfectly rigid, determine the minimum possible average force exerted on the wall during the impact, given that the system’s total kinetic energy before and after the collision differs by at most 15%.

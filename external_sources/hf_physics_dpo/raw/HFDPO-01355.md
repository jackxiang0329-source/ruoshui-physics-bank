# HFDPO-01355

A fusion reactor's plasma containment field relies on a series of superconducting coils arranged in a helical configuration around a toroidal chamber. At peak operation, the plasma generates an internal magnetic field of 12 T tangential to the toroidal axis, but a 5% ripple in the radial component is detected—causing localized heating in the first-wall structure. Given that the coils produce a uniform axial field of 8 T and are spaced with a pitch of 0.8 m, what must be the minimum number of turns per meter in each coil to suppress radial field fluctuations below 0.1 T at the plasma edge, assuming ideal current distribution and negligible shield thickness?

# HFDPO-04243

A seafaring research vessel detects a sudden, low-frequency pulse (4 Hz) from a submerged volcanic vent 12 km away, traveling through seawater at 1500 m/s. The pulse arrives 8 seconds after the initial seismic tremor recorded by land-based sensors. If the seismic wave traveled through the oceanic crust at 6.5 km/s and the seismic wave’s arrival time was measured at t = 0, what is the depth of the water directly above the vent, assuming the seismic wave propagated in a straight line through the crust to the point of vent initiation?

# HFDPO-03518

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue handling in minimally invasive surgery. The gripper’s finger-like actuators are made of a hyperelastic polymer with a nonlinear stress–strain response described by the Mooney-Rivlin model. During testing, a 5 mm diameter actuator is inflated to a pressure of 12 kPa, resulting in a 30% radial expansion. However, the actuator exhibits unexpected buckling at a strain threshold of 0.42, despite being within design limits. Using the material’s measured constants (C₁₀ = 0.8 MPa, C₀₁ = 0.4 MPa), determine whether the buckling is due to material instability or geometric nonlinearities. Propose a design modification to prevent failure without changing the material.

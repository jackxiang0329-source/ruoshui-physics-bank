# HFDPO-00197

A NASA engineer is designing a deep-space probe’s thermal regulation system using a thin, flexible, carbon-nanotube-embedded polymer sheet that acts as both a heater and temperature sensor. The sheet has a non-uniform resistivity: ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 + kr), where r is radial distance from the center (in meters), ρ₀ = 5×10⁻⁶ Ω·m, k = 2 m⁻¹, and the sheet is 0.5 m in radius. When a 12 V potential is applied across the sheet’s inner and outer edges (like a circular annulus), the system must maintain a temperature rise of exactly 20°C without exceeding 50 W total power to conserve battery. What is the required outer radius to meet these constraints? (Assume negligible heat loss and uniform current density in radial direction.)

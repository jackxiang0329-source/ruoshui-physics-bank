# HFDPO-12535

A fusion reactor prototype uses a 12-meter-long, high-temperature superconducting (HTS) solenoid wound from YBCO tape to confine plasma at 15 kA peak current. During startup, the system unexpectedly quenches when a localized magnetic field disturbance reaches 0.8 T near the coil’s center. The HTS material has a critical magnetic field H_c = 1.2 , text{T} at 77 K and a critical current density J_c = 10^6 , text{A/cm}^2 . Determine whether the quench was caused by exceeding the critical field or the critical current density, and explain how the spatial non-uniformity of J_c and field screening in type-II superconductors could have contributed to localized thermal runaway.

# HFDPO-05594

A biomedical engineer is designing a targeted cancer therapy using ultrasound waves to heat tumor cells while minimizing damage to surrounding healthy tissue. The tumor, located 5 cm beneath the skin, has a density of 1.08 g/cm³ and is surrounded by tissue with a density of 1.02 g/cm³. The ultrasound transducer operates at 1.5 MHz, and the goal is to achieve a 10°C temperature rise within the tumor in 30 seconds via constructive interference of reflected waves. What is the minimum intensity (in W/m²) required at the skin surface to achieve this heating, assuming 80% of the energy is absorbed in the tumor and 20% is lost to scattering? Consider impedance mismatches, wave reflection, and thermal diffusion.

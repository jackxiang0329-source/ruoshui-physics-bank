# HFDPO-14461

A quantum dot array in a next-generation solar cell operates at 300 K, with 10,000 electrons confined in a 2D lattice of 100 nm × 100 nm quantum dots, each with a single discrete energy level at −0.5 eV. Due to Coulomb blockade, only one electron can occupy each dot. The system is weakly coupled to a phonon bath that induces occasional electron tunneling. After 100 ns of light illumination (photon flux: 10¹⁷ cm⁻²·s⁻¹, 1.8 eV photons), the measured average occupation per dot is 0.62. Estimate the effective phonon-assisted tunneling rate and determine whether the system is in local thermal equilibrium with the bath.

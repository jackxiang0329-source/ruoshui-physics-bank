# HFDPO-00024

A coastal engineer is designing a new seawall to protect a low-lying town from storm surges. During peak tides, water reaches a height of 3.2 m above sea level, with a horizontal velocity of 2.5 m/s as it approaches the wall. The wall is sloped at 60° to the horizontal, and the engineer must determine the maximum pressure exerted on the wall during a surge event. Assume steady, incompressible flow and neglect viscous effects. What is the peak pressure at the point where the water impacts the wall?

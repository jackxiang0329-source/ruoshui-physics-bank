# HFDPO-06625

A deep-space communications array on Mars receives a signal from a probe orbiting Jupiter, 600 million km away. The probe’s clock ticks at 95% of Earth time due to relativistic velocity, and its signal is Doppler-shifted from a nominal 10.2 GHz carrier frequency. The ground station, using a cryogenically cooled receiver with phase-locked loops, records a signal with a measured frequency of 10.52 GHz. What is the component of the probe’s velocity along the line of sight, and is the probe approaching or receding? Justify your method using both relativistic Doppler shift and Lorentz transformation of the four-frequency.

# HFDPO-07337

In a quantum computing prototype, qubits based on nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond exhibit decoherence due to unintended spin interactions with nearby carbon-13 isotopes. The device operates at 15 K, and measurements show a spin relaxation time of 2.3 ms. Given that the probability of a carbon-13 nucleus (I = 1/2, natural abundance 1.1%) being within 5 Å of a nitrogen-vacancy defect is 0.034, analyze how the hyperfine coupling to these isotopes contributes to the observed decoherence, and determine the effective local magnetic field fluctuation caused by carbon-13 spins.

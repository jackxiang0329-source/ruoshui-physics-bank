# HFDPO-07366

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue manipulation in minimally invasive surgery. The gripper’s finger-like actuator is made of a hyperelastic silicone composite with a nonlinear stress-strain relationship modeled by the Mooney-Rivlin equation. During testing, the actuator deforms under a 0.3 N force, stretching 40% from its original length, but the surgeon reports inconsistent grip strength. The engineer suspects non-uniform strain distribution due to varying cross-sectional thickness along the actuator’s length. Determine the maximum stress developed in the thinnest region given a position-dependent thickness profile t(x) = t_0 (1 + 0.2 sin(pi x/L)) , where L = 8,text{cm} and t_0 = 1,text{mm} , assuming constant axial strain and incompressibility.

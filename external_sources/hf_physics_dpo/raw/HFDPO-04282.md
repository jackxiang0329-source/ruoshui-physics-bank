# HFDPO-04282

A semiconductor manufacturing plant uses electrostatic chucking to hold silicon wafers during plasma etching. One wafer, initially uncharged, is placed on a chuck that applies a uniform electric field of 1.2 × 10⁶ V/m downward. The wafer (15 cm × 15 cm, 0.5 mm thick) has a dielectric constant of 11.7 and is perfectly insulating. After contact, the chuck’s surface develops an induced charge density of 1.8 × 10⁻⁷ C/m². What is the total electrostatic force holding the wafer, and is it sufficient to prevent movement if the wafer experiences a 1.5 N gas drag force?

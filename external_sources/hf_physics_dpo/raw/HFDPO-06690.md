# HFDPO-06690

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor wildfire spread in remote forests must optimize its energy use during a 12-hour surveillance mission. Given a 32% efficiency in converting solar energy to mechanical work and a 25% drop in battery efficiency due to thermal stress at altitude, determine the minimum solar energy (in kJ) the drone’s panels must collect to complete the mission, assuming no wind and minimal air resistance.

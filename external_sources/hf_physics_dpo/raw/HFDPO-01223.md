# HFDPO-01223

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable ECG monitor that uses a flexible capacitor array to detect subtle changes in skin capacitance caused by heart-induced electrical activity. Each sensor unit consists of two parallel plates separated by a 10 μm thick dielectric layer with κ = 8.5. During operation, the effective overlap area varies due to skin movement, but the device must maintain a minimum capacitance of 20 pF to ensure signal stability. If the sensor’s plate area can stretch from 4 mm² to 8 mm², what is the minimum dielectric thickness required to prevent capacitance drop below 20 pF when the area shrinks to 4 mm²?

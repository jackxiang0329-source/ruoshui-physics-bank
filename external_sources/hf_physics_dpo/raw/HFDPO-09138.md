# HFDPO-09138

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a 12 V battery to run a water pump through a 50-meter copper cable. The pump requires 3.0 A at 12 V, but the voltage at the pump drops to only 10.8 V due to resistive losses. What is the minimum wire gauge (AWG) needed to keep voltage drop below 5% while minimizing material cost, given that copper has resistivity of 1.68 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m and AWG 10 wire has a diameter of 2.59 mm, AWG 12 is 2.05 mm?

# HFDPO-05220

A deep-space satellite designed to study cosmic microwave background radiation uses a precision gyrostabilized platform to maintain orientation. A minor manufacturing asymmetry creates a small offset between the center of mass and the geometric center—0.002 m—and a moment of inertia about the symmetry axis that differs slightly from the principal axis by 0.001 kg·m². Determine the angular frequency of the precession and explain how this small imbalance leads to measurable drift over time, using both Euler’s equations and energy-based arguments.

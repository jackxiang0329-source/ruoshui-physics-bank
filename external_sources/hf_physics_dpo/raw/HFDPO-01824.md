# HFDPO-01824

In a cryogenic quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit is shielded within a spherical niobium casing with a 5 cm inner radius and 5.1 cm outer radius. The qubit emits a transient electric field due to charge fluctuations, but the lab’s sensitive magnetometers detect unexpected field leakage. Engineers suspect the shielding isn't perfect and suspect charge accumulation on the inner surface. Given that the measured electric field 10 cm from the center is 2.4 × 10⁴ N/C radially outward, and the outer surface is grounded, what is the net charge on the inner surface of the shield?

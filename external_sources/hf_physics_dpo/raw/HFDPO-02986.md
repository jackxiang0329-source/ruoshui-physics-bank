# HFDPO-02986

A small satellite in low Earth orbit is experiencing unexpected drag, causing its altitude to decrease by 200 meters per day. Engineers need to calculate the change in gravitational force on the satellite over one week to assess whether adjustments to its orbit are needed. If the satellite starts at 400 km above Earth’s surface, what is the percentage change in gravitational force after 7 days? Assume Earth’s radius is 6371 km and mass is 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg.

# HFDPO-04531

A laser-based quantum imaging system used in underground tunnel mapping experiences unexplained signal dropouts at specific depths. The system uses coherent 633 nm light, transmitted through a 2.4 cm diameter fiber optic bundle embedded in a flexible polymer sheath. The tunnel wall has a micro-structured surface with grating-like features (period ≈ 12 μm). Propose a physical explanation for the observed behavior and determine the refractive index difference between the polymer sheath and surrounding geologic medium that would cause this phenomenon.

# HFDPO-14818

A marine biologist studying migratory whales detects unusual disruptions in their navigation near a newly installed offshore wind farm. The turbines generate strong magnetic fields due to their large electric motors. One turbine produces a magnetic field of 1.2 mT at a distance of 100 m. If whales are known to detect Earth’s magnetic field (~25 μT) for orientation, at what minimum distance from the turbine would the turbine’s field exceed 10% of Earth’s field? Assume the field decreases with distance as 1/r³.

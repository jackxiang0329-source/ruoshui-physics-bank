# HFDPO-08320

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a cylindrical plasma chamber with a radius of 1.2 m and height of 4.5 m, lined with a dielectric coating that can withstand electric fields up to 3.2 × 10⁶ V/m. During startup, a non-uniform charge distribution is generated: ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 − r²/R²), where r is radial distance from the axis and R = 1.2 m. The maximum field at the dielectric boundary must not exceed safety limits. What is the maximum allowable value of ρ₀ to prevent breakdown?

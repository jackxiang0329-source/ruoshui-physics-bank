# HFDPO-15864

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole lined with superconducting coils to harvest induced currents from natural geomagnetic fluctuations. The borehole, 2.5 km deep, has a 1.2 m diameter and is embedded in a region where the horizontal Earth’s magnetic field varies sinusoidally with a peak amplitude of 55 μT and a frequency of 1.7 × 10⁻³ Hz. If the coil system has 300 turns and is perfectly aligned with the field’s direction of change, estimate the RMS voltage induced in the coil system over a 24-hour period. How does this compare to the energy required to maintain the superconducting state (1.8 MW)?

# HFDPO-13064

A drone equipped with a custom electromagnetic payload delivery system must release a 2.4 kg sensor package mid-flight at 120 m altitude while navigating a 7 m/s crosswind. Assume air density is 1.225 kg/m³ and gravity is 9.81 m/s².

# HFDPO-06732

A wind turbine in a coastal region generates electricity using a rotating coil within a uniform magnetic field. During a storm, the turbine’s rotational speed increases unexpectedly to 180 rpm due to strong gusts, causing the peak induced EMF in the coil to rise above the safe threshold of 300 V. The coil has 250 turns, area 0.8 m², and rotates in a 0.4 T magnetic field. By what factor must the magnetic field strength be reduced to keep the peak EMF at 300 V?

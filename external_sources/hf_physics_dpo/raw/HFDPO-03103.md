# HFDPO-03103

A relativistic plasma jet from a distant quasar is observed to emit synchrotron radiation with a peak frequency of 10¹⁸ Hz. Astronomers detect this radiation at Earth with a redshifted peak at 10¹⁶ Hz. The jet is directed nearly toward us, yet its apparent transverse motion shows an apparent superluminal expansion of 5c in angular position over 10 years. Determine the true speed of the jet relative to the speed of light and explain the discrepancy between the observed transverse velocity and the constraint of special relativity.

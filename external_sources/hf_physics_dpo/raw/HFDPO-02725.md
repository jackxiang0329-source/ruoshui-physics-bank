# HFDPO-02725

A quantum dot display manufacturer detects faint rainbow-colored fringes in high-resolution images projected through a thin-film filter coated with a periodic array of nanoscale pillars (height 250 nm, diameter 80 nm, spacing 120 nm). The filter is designed to pass only 530 nm green light, but scattered light at 450 nm and 650 nm appears as interference artifacts. Determine whether these artifacts arise from single-slit diffraction or multi-slit interference, and calculate the angular separation between the first-order maxima of the 450 nm and 650 nm wavelengths at normal incidence.

# HFDPO-08510

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size-driven differences in drag. The device uses a narrow channel (100 μm wide) filled with plasma (μ = 1.2 mPa·s, ρ = 1025 kg/m³). A 15 μm spherical cancer cell, denser than plasma (ρ_cell = 1050 kg/m³), flows through the channel at a velocity of 0.8 mm/s. What is the drag force on the cell, and does viscous resistance dominate over inertial effects?

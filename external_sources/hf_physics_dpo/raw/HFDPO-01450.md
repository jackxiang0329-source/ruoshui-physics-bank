# HFDPO-01450

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates power during strong gusts, but engineers notice it occasionally spins too fast during sudden wind surges, risking mechanical failure. The turbine blades (each 50 m long, modeled as uniform rods) have a total moment of inertia of 2.1 × 10⁷ kg·m². During a 10-minute gust, the wind applies a constant torque of 1.8 × 10⁶ N·m. If the turbine starts from rest and the maximum safe angular velocity is 1.2 rad/s, at what point during the gust does the system exceed safe limits?

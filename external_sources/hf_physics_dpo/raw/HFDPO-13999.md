# HFDPO-13999

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanocrystals to absorb sunlight, but efficiency drops sharply when the dots are too close together due to electron tunneling between them. If the dots are separated by 2.0 nm and the barrier height between them is 1.8 eV, what is the probability that an electron with energy 1.5 eV tunnels through the barrier? Assume the barrier is rectangular and the electron mass is the effective mass of a conduction electron in GaAs (0.067mₑ).

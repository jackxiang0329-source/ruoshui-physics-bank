# HFDPO-09594

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day, enabling electricity generation at night. The system stores 5.2 GJ of thermal energy in 120,000 kg of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1,400 J/kg·K. At peak storage, the salt reaches 560°C. Overnight, the salt cools to 420°C, transferring heat to a steam turbine with 38% efficiency. If the plant must maintain a minimum of 4.0 GJ in storage for safety and stability, what is the maximum amount of work that can be extracted, and how does this relate to the second law’s limitation on usable energy?

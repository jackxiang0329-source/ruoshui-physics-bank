# HFDPO-06102

A quantum imaging lab aims to detect faint entangled photon pairs from a spontaneous parametric down-conversion source used in atmospheric lidar, but noise from ambient light at 810 nm (100 W/m²) overwhelms the signal. The system uses a spectrally narrowband filter (FWHM = 2 nm, centered at 800 nm) and a single-photon avalanche diode (SPAD) with 15% detection efficiency and 5 ns dead time. The entangled pairs are generated at 400 nm (photon flux: 10⁹ pairs/s), but the nonlinear crystal introduces spectral broadening (σ = 5 nm). What minimum signal-to-noise ratio (SNR) can be achieved after filtering, and how would increasing the crystal thickness by 2× alter the SNR given phase-matching constraints?

# HFDPO-06362

A research team at a fusion energy lab is designing a compact tokamak reactor using high-temperature superconducting (HTS) coils to confine plasma. Using the Biot-Savart law, determine whether the field at the center exceeds the safe threshold, and propose a design modification to reduce the peak field without sacrificing confinement strength.

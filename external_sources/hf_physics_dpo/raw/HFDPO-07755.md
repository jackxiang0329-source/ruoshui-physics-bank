# HFDPO-07755

A research team studying ancient ice cores from Antarctica discovers a layer with unexpectedly high concentrations of methane trapped in clathrate hydrates. The ice core samples were kept at -20°C during transport, but upon warming to -15°C in the lab, they observe rapid decomposition of the hydrates, releasing gas. Given that the phase boundary for methane clathrate stability at 1 atm is -17°C, and the hydrate decomposes according to CH₄·5.75H₂O(s) ⇌ CH₄(g) + 5.75H₂O(l), determine whether the observed decomposition is due to temperature-induced phase change or another factor, and calculate the minimum pressure required to maintain stability at -15°C.

# HFDPO-01253

A wind turbine in a remote coastal village must maintain stable power output despite rapidly changing wind speeds. The rotor blades have a total moment of inertia of 4500 kg·m² and spin at 22 rpm when the wind is steady. During a sudden gust, the wind torque increases to 11,000 N·m for 3.0 seconds. Engineers need to calculate the new rotational speed after the gust, assuming no mechanical losses. The turbine’s control system uses flywheel inertia to stabilize output—what must the flywheel’s additional inertia be to limit the speed increase to no more than 25%?

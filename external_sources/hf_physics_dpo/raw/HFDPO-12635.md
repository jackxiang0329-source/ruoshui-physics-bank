# HFDPO-12635

A quantum dot solar cell uses a nanoscale semiconductor structure where electron transport depends on tunneling through a thin insulating barrier. The device operates under variable illumination, and the current-voltage (I-V) curve shows non-linear behavior due to temperature-dependent resistance and quantum confinement effects. If the solar cell is exposed to sunlight raising its temperature to 330 K, and the applied voltage remains constant at 0.5 V, what is the new current? Assume the system reaches thermal equilibrium and quantum efficiency is constant.

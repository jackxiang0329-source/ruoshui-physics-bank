# HFDPO-09858

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem is monitored by a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) equipped with a flexible, sealed sampling tube that extends 2.5 meters vertically from the vent’s rim into the surrounding ocean. When the ROV opens the tube’s valve, the high-pressure vent fluid begins to rise, displacing seawater upward. What is the maximum height the vent fluid can rise inside the tube before hydrostatic equilibrium is reestablished? Assume the vent fluid density is 0.85 g/cm³ and the seawater density is 1.03 g/cm³. Ignore temperature gradients and fluid viscosity.

# HFDPO-06511

A high-performance lithium-sulfur battery in a deep-space probe experiences thermal runaway during a critical maneuver. The probe’s thermal management system uses a phase-change material (PCM) with a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg and a melting point of 85°C, distributed in a 2.3 kg enclosure. During a 12-hour solar eclipse, internal heat generation from the battery’s self-discharge is 4.2 W/kg. The enclosure has a thermal conductivity of 0.45 W/m·K and a surface area of 1.8 m², exposed to space at 3 K with an emissivity of 0.85. If the PCM begins melting after 4.1 hours, what was the initial temperature of the PCM when the eclipse started?

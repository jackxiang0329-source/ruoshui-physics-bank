# HFDPO-16211

The rock exerts a variable resistive force per unit area proportional to the square of the depth: F_{text{res}} = k h^2 , where k = 2.0 times 10^4 , text{N/m}^4 . The bit’s mass is 120 kg, and gravity is 9.8 m/s². What is the total work done by the drilling rig’s motor over the first 30 m of descent, accounting for both gravitational potential energy changes and energy dissipated against resistance?

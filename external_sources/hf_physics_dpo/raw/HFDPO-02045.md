# HFDPO-02045

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate rare circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from whole blood using size-based filtration. The device uses a narrow channel (width = 50 μm) with a viscous shear gradient to enhance migration of CTCs toward the walls while leaving smaller red blood cells suspended in the center. Given that blood has an effective viscosity of 4.5 mPa·s, the flow rate is 1.2 μL/min, and the channel is 2 cm long, determine the pressure drop required to maintain this flow and assess whether the flow remains laminar. If not, what redesign is needed to restore laminar conditions?

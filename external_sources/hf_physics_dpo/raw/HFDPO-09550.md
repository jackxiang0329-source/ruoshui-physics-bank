# HFDPO-09550

A deep-sea research drone, shaped like a cylinder with mass 220 kg and radius 0.8 m, descends vertically while rotating about its central axis to stabilize orientation. At a depth of 1,200 m, its angular velocity is 1.5 rad/s, but due to a malfunction, its motor applies a constant torque of 32 N·m in the opposite direction of spin. The drone’s moment of inertia is modified by retractable ballast fins that increase it by 18% when extended. If the fins are fully retracted (I = 0.5mr²), how long will it take for the drone to reverse its rotation completely? Assume fluid drag produces a resistive torque of 4.2 N·m proportional to angular speed.

# HFDPO-01120

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (radius 10 cm) operates at 1.5 MHz with a current amplitude of 2.5 A, while the receiver coil (radius 2 mm) is embedded in the pacemaker near the heart. Due to tissue attenuation and misalignment, the mutual inductance varies between 50 nH and 120 nH. The receiver circuit includes a 100 pF capacitor in parallel with a 3 kΩ resistor to form a tuned LC tank. What is the maximum average power delivered to the pacemaker when the system is optimally tuned, and how does detuning due to coil misalignment affect efficiency?

# HFDPO-12774

A small village in the Andes uses a solar cooker that traps heat from the sun to boil water for drinking. On a clear day, the cooker receives 800 W of solar energy per square meter and has an effective collecting area of 0.7 m². If 70% of the incoming solar energy is converted into useful heat for boiling water, how much thermal energy does the cooker deliver to the water in 15 minutes?

# HFDPO-06413

During a deep-space probe mission, a radioactive isotope (Pu-238) powers a thermoelectric generator, producing 120 W of heat at the hot junction. What is the minimum radiator surface area required if the radiator emits as a gray body (ε = 0.95) and must reject excess heat to avoid core overheating?

# HFDPO-16307

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) with a mass of 1,200 kg deploys a 50 kg sensor module using a spring-loaded mechanism that launches it horizontally at 2.5 m/s relative to the ROV. The ROV, initially at rest relative to the ocean current, begins to drift. If the current exerts a drag force of 320 N opposing motion, and the module must reach a fixed buoy 15 m away in 8 seconds, does it arrive on time? Assume the system is isolated during launch.

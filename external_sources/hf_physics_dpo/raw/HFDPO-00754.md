# HFDPO-00754

A deep-sea mining drone detects a faint, recurring pressure wave at 17 Hz emanating from a hydrothermal vent field. The wave propagates through seawater at 1,500 m/s, but the drone’s onboard sensors only sample at 50 Hz. When the drone moves at 2.5 m/s directly toward the source, the measured frequency shifts to 17.3 Hz. What is the true amplitude of the pressure wave at the vent, assuming the wave is spherical and attenuates at 1/r², given that the drone recorded a peak pressure of 0.4 Pa at a distance of 300 m?

# HFDPO-13034

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent system emits superheated water at 350°C into near-freezing ocean water at 2°C. Engineers propose harvesting energy from the thermal gradient using a closed-loop Rankine cycle with a working fluid that can withstand high pressures and has a critical temperature of 400°C. The system must generate 5 MW of net power while operating within a 700-meter pressure differential. How can the turbine inlet pressure and condenser pressure be optimized to maximize efficiency, and what limits the practical output despite theoretical Carnot efficiency being 65%?

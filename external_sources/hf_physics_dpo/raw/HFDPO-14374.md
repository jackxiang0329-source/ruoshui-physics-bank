# HFDPO-14374

Given that the electric field threshold for air breakdown is 3.0 × 10⁶ V/m, calculate the minimum number of particles needed on the shield to create a local field exceeding this threshold, assuming the particles are uniformly distributed but the shield’s shape causes field enhancement.

# HFDPO-08535

A solar-powered desalination unit in a remote village uses a blackened surface to absorb sunlight, heating water from 25°C to 95°C. The system relies on evaporation to produce fresh water, and the energy required to vaporize 1 kg of water at 100°C is 2260 kJ. If the surface absorbs 1200 W/m² of solar energy and only 30% of it is used effectively for heating, how many square meters of surface area are needed to produce 10 liters of fresh water per hour? Assume all heat goes into vaporization and neglect thermal losses.

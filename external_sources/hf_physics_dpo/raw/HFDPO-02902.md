# HFDPO-02902

A deep-space probe uses a radioisotope thermoelectric generator (RTG) powered by 50 grams of plutonium-238, which decays via alpha emission with a half-life of 87.7 years. The probe's power demand is 120 watts initially, but efficiency drops over time due to decay and material degradation. After 20 years, mission control detects a 15% power shortfall. What is the effective power output of the RTG at that time, and how much additional heat must be generated by alternative means to maintain operations? Assume all decay energy is converted to heat, and no energy is lost during conversion.

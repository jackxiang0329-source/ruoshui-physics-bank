# HFDPO-07042

A futuristic deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent at 2,500 meters, where seawater at 2°C enters a heat exchanger and is heated to 350°C by geothermal energy before being expelled. The system must maintain a minimum 300 K temperature difference across the exchanger to prevent thermal shock to sensitive sensors. Given the drone’s power budget caps at 15 kW and the heat capacity of water is 4.18 kJ/kg·K, determine the maximum sustainable mass flow rate of seawater (in kg/s) while ensuring the Second Law is not violated.

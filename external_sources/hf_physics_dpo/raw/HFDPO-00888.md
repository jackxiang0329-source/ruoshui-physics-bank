# HFDPO-00888

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac pacemakers using inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (10 cm diameter, 50 turns) operates at 13.56 MHz with a peak current of 2 A. The receiver coil (2 cm diameter, 10 turns) is embedded in a titanium-encased pacemaker, 3 mm from the skin surface. The system must deliver 5 mW to the pacemaker under varying patient movement, but parasitic capacitance in the receiver circuit causes oscillations that disrupt timing. Determine the resonant frequency shift and the required external capacitance to re-tune the receiver to match the transmitter, considering both mutual inductance and skin effect at 13.56 MHz in human tissue (σ = 0.3 S/m, μᵣ = 1).

# HFDPO-01777

A quantum dot solar cell researcher observes that her device's efficiency drops sharply when sunlight shifts from direct noon exposure to diffuse morning light, despite identical total photon flux. She suspects the issue lies in the quantum confinement effects altering electron transition probabilities. Given a quantum dot with a diameter of 4.2 nm (effectively a 3D infinite square well), calculate the energy difference between the first excited state and the ground state for electrons confined within it. Then, analyze whether this energy gap aligns with the spectral distribution of diffuse versus direct sunlight (peak ~550 nm vs. 600 nm).

# HFDPO-03378

A solar-powered drone, designed to hover over a remote mountainous region for environmental monitoring, uses four identical carbon-fiber arms arranged symmetrically to support a lightweight, asymmetrical solar array that tilts to maximize sun exposure. The array has a total mass of 5.2 kg and its center of mass is offset 12 cm from the central axis due to uneven panel distribution. Each arm is 1.8 m long, rigid, and anchored to the drone’s central hub at a 15° downward angle from horizontal. If the drone's control system must maintain equilibrium while the array rotates 30° from its vertical orientation, what is the minimum tensile strength (in N) required for each arm to prevent structural failure? Assume the arms are massless, and all forces act in a single vertical plane.

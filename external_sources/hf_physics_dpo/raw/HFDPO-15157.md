# HFDPO-15157

A wind farm in coastal Maine must optimize turbine blade design to maximize energy capture while minimizing structural stress during sudden storm gusts. Engineers observe that the angular momentum is conserved during the initial gust due to negligible external torque. What is the new rotational speed immediately after the gust, and how does this affect the blade’s kinetic energy?

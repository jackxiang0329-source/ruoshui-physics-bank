# HFDPO-09107

In a quantum computing prototype using topological qubits based on Majorana zero modes in a semiconducting nanowire coupled to an s-wave superconductor, researchers observe an unexpected splitting of the zero-energy state at a magnetic field of 2.1 T. The nanowire is 500 nm long, with a spin-orbit coupling strength λ_SO = 1.8 meV·nm and a superconducting gap Δ = 0.35 meV. Assuming the Zeeman energy exceeds the superconducting gap, determine the minimum required effective magnetic field component along the wire axis to fully separate the Majorana modes and stabilize a topologically protected qubit.

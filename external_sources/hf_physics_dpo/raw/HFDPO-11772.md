# HFDPO-11772

A drone delivering emergency medical supplies must navigate a narrow canyon during a wildfire, where sudden wind gusts (up to 12 m/s) disrupt its trajectory. The drone’s controller adjusts its position using real-time GPS, but due to a software delay of 0.4 s, it reacts after the wind has already altered motion. Starting at rest 80 m from the canyon wall, it accelerates at 2.5 m/s² to avoid collision. What is the minimum safe distance from the wall the drone can pass if wind gusts cause a constant lateral offset of 3.0 m/s² for 1.2 s?

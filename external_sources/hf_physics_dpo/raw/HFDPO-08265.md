# HFDPO-08265

A deep-sea research submersible descends into the Mariana Trench, where the pressure at 10,000 meters depth is 108 MPa. The sub’s circular viewing port has a radius of 0.25 m and is sealed with a thick acrylic panel. During a routine inspection, engineers notice a small crack near the edge of the port, allowing seawater to seep in slowly. To estimate the force pushing outward on the cracked area, they must model the pressure distribution across the panel, accounting for both hydrostatic pressure and the structural flexing caused by differential stress. What is the net outward force acting on the cracked region if the internal pressure remains at 0.1 MPa due to pressurized air inside?

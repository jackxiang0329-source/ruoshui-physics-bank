# HFDPO-06003

A solar-powered drone used for monitoring wildfires hovers at a 15° tilt due to uneven wind pressure on its asymmetric solar panel array. The drone’s center of mass is 0.4 m behind the vertical line through its pivot point, and the total weight is 12 kg. The lift force from the rotors acts at a point 0.15 m forward of the pivot. What is the minimum lift force required to maintain static equilibrium, and what tension must be present in the rear stabilizer cable (attached 0.3 m behind the pivot) to prevent rotation? Assume the cable is taut and the system is in equilibrium.

# HFDPO-04382

Each strut exerts a 250 kN force at a 30° angle from vertical, directed inward and upward. During a micrometeoroid impact, one strut fails. To prevent catastrophic tilting, engineers must assess whether the remaining two struts can maintain static equilibrium. Determine if the habitat remains in static equilibrium under the new loading, considering only gravitational and strut forces.

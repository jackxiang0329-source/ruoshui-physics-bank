# HFDPO-14841

In a next-generation neural interface, a flexible graphene electrode array is implanted near the cortex, where it measures tiny electrical signals from neurons. The electrode’s capacitance varies with the thickness of the surrounding cerebrospinal fluid (CSF), which fluctuates due to brain pulsations. If the fluid layer compresses to 120 μm during a pulse, and the electrode area is 1.2 mm² with a dielectric constant of 78 (water-like), what is the new capacitance? Assume the interface remains uniform and no charge is stored.

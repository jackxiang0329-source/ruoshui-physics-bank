During a forest fire, a drone equipped with a capacitive moisture sensor detects subtle changes in soil dielectric properties to identify potential fire hazards. The sensor uses a parallel-plate capacitor embedded in the soil, with plates 10 cm² in area and 2 mm apart. When dry, the soil has a dielectric constant of 3.0. After a light rain, the moisture increases the dielectric constant to 4.8. What percentage change in capacitance does the sensor detect, and how might this affect early warning systems?

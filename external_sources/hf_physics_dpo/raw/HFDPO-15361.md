# HFDPO-15361

A seismic isolation system in a high-rise hospital in Tokyo uses giant nonlinear pendulums (mass 80,000 kg) with tuned damping to protect sensitive medical equipment during earthquakes. During a simulated 1.8 Hz ground oscillation, the system’s pendulum exhibits a 15 cm amplitude and phase lag of 40°. The pendulum’s restoring torque follows a cubic function: τ = –kθ – αθ³ (k = 2.4×10⁶ N·m/rad, α = 1.2×10⁴ N·m/rad³). What is the effective angular frequency of the pendulum’s motion, and why does this system prevent resonance damage despite the earthquake frequency being close to the pendulum’s linear natural frequency?

# HFDPO-00465

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable ECG sensor that uses a flexible capacitor array to detect subtle skin impedance changes caused by heartbeats. The sensor’s capacitor plates, made of conductive hydrogel, are 2 cm wide and spaced 0.1 mm apart, but the hydrogel’s dielectric constant varies with moisture—ranging from 40 (dry) to 60 (sweat-dampened). If the circuit must maintain a minimum capacitance of 5 nF to ensure signal fidelity, what is the minimum length of the capacitor plates needed when sweat increases the dielectric constant and reduces the effective spacing due to compression?

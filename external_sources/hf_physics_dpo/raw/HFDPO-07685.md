# HFDPO-07685

A deep-sea mining drone hovers just above a hydrothermal vent field, suspending a 500 kg mineral collector via a flexible tether anchored to its underside. The tether has a linear density of 0.8 kg/m and extends 120 m vertically in seawater with a current flowing at 1.6 m/s parallel to the seabed. The collector experiences a buoyant force of 4200 N and a drag force of 280 N due to the current. To maintain static equilibrium, the drone must apply a variable tension along the tether. What is the minimum tension required at the drone’s anchor point, and how does the tether’s sag profile affect the force distribution?

# HFDPO-03527

A quantum computing chip operates at 15 mK in a dilution refrigerator, but recent measurements show unexplained heat spikes during quantum gate operations. The chip’s superconducting qubits are housed in a niobium enclosure with a thermal conductivity of 1.2 W/(m·K), 2 mm thick, and 1 cm² surface area. Each gate operation releases 80 pJ of energy into the enclosure. If the system’s heat leaks from the 4K stage are negligible, how long does the temperature rise of the enclosure exceed 20 mK during a 10 ns gate pulse, and why does this matter for quantum coherence?

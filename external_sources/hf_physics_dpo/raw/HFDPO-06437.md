# HFDPO-06437

A private space station orbits Earth at 600 km altitude, maintaining a Sun-synchronous orbit with a local solar time of 10:30 AM. To avoid a debris cloud from a defunct satellite (300 km altitude, 15° inclination), the station must perform a maneuver to shift its orbit. However, thrusters are limited—only 50 m/s of Δv available. Can it safely avoid the debris by adjusting its orbital plane and raising perigee to 650 km without exceeding Δv limits? Assume both orbits are circular and equatorial except for the debris's 15° inclination.

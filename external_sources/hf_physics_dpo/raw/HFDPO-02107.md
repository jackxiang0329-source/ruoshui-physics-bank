# HFDPO-02107

A lunar habitat module is being deployed on the Moon’s surface using a rigid, three-legged landing strut system. Each strut is 3.2 m long, made of aerospace-grade aluminum alloy (E = 70 GPa, yield strength 350 MPa), and must support a 12,000 kg module. The struts are attached at 120° angles around the module’s center, but uneven regolith compaction causes one strut to settle 2.5 cm more than the others. Determine whether the struts exceed their yield strength under this asymmetry, and assess whether the system remains in static equilibrium. Assume rigid joints and uniform mass distribution.

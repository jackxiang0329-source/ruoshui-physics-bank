# HFDPO-00232

Due to a sudden power fluctuation, the magnetic field strength drops by 12% over 0.3 seconds, causing the plasma to expand radially. Engineers observe that the average particle velocity increases by 8% during the disturbance. Assuming the plasma behaves as an ideal gas and no external energy is added, what can be inferred about the conservation of energy and angular momentum during this transient event?

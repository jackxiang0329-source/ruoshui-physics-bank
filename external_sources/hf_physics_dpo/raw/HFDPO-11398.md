# HFDPO-11398

A lunar rover prototype on a simulated Martian surface uses a gyroscopically stabilized suspension system to maintain balance over irregular terrain. During testing, engineers observe that when the rover rotates at 12 rad/s while traversing a 30° incline, the gyroscope’s precession causes a lateral drift of 4.2 cm over 5 seconds. Given that the gyroscope’s moment of inertia is 0.85 kg·m² and its spin angular velocity is 1500 rad/s, determine the net torque acting on the system due to terrain-induced misalignment.

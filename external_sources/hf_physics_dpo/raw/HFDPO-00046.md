# HFDPO-00046

In a fusion reactor experimental chamber, a sudden plasma disruption generates a rapidly expanding toroidal current sheet with a radial current density J_r(r,t) = J_0 frac{r}{R} e^{-t/tau} for r < R , where R = 0.5,text{m} , J_0 = 10^7,text{A/m}^2 , and tau = 10^{-4},text{s} . The magnetic field is initially confined to B_theta(r,0) = B_0 left(1 - frac{r^2}{R^2}right) , with B_0 = 1.2,text{T} . Using Maxwell’s equations, determine the electric field evolution in the poloidal plane and predict the onset time of a secondary magnetic reconnection event due to induced E-fields exceeding a critical threshold E_{text{crit}} = 5 times 10^6,text{V/m} .

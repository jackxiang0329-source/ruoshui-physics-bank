# HFDPO-06617

A marine biologist is studying how underwater volcanic activity affects whale communication in the Pacific Ocean. She detects a 50 Hz sound wave generated by a deep-sea eruption that travels 1,200 km to a pod of humpback whales. The water temperature varies from 4°C at depth to 15°C at the surface, and the wave refracts as it moves through layers with different sound speeds. What is the arrival time difference between the wave traveling along a straight path versus one that refracts optimally due to temperature gradients? Assume sound speed in water is 1,450 m/s at 15°C and increases by 4.5 m/s per °C.

# HFDPO-01670

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses concentrated photovoltaic panels that rely on polarizing filters to reduce glare-induced efficiency losses from reflected sunlight. The panels are mounted at a 45° tilt, and sunlight strikes the surface at a Brewster angle of 55°. A recent efficiency drop is observed only on clear days when the sun is directly overhead. Engineers suspect glare from the desert sand is causing polarization misalignment in the filters. Given that the sand reflects light with a polarization ratio of 0.85 (unpolarized to polarized), determine the optimal orientation of the polarizing filter to maximize transmitted light, and explain why the efficiency loss occurs despite the filter’s intended design.

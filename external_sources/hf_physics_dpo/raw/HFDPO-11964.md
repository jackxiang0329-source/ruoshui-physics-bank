# HFDPO-11964

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and modeled as a thin rod with mass 15,000 kg, is rotating at 12 rpm when a sudden gust causes the generator to disconnect, leaving the blade free to spin under aerodynamic drag. The drag torque is non-uniform, given by τ(ω) = –kω², where ω is angular speed in rad/s and k = 0.05 N·m·s². How long does it take for the blade to slow from 12 rpm to 3 rpm, and what is its angular displacement during this time?

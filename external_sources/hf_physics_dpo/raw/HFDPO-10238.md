# HFDPO-10238

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole lined with superconducting coils to generate electricity from natural magnetic field fluctuations caused by solar wind interacting with the Earth's ionosphere. However, the superconducting material has a critical current density limit of 1.2 × 10¹⁰ A/m². What is the minimum radius of the coil cross-section needed to avoid quenching, assuming the induced EMF drives current through the coil?

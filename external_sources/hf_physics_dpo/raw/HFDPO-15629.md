# HFDPO-15629

A wind farm turbine in a remote mountain pass experiences intermittent blade failure during high-wind events. Engineers discover that the tower’s base mounting bolts are shearing off despite being rated for 50 kN of shear force. The base is anchored by six bolts arranged in a circle with a 1.2 m radius, and the system is designed to resist overturning. Determine the shear force on each bolt and explain why failure occurs—considering both direct loading and moment-induced forces.

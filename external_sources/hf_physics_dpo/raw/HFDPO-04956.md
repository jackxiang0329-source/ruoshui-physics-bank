# HFDPO-04956

In a fusion reactor's divertor region, deuterium ions from the plasma (at 15 keV average kinetic energy) strike a tungsten surface at a flux of 3 × 10²⁰ ions/m²/s. The surface is cooled by liquid lithium flowing at 0.8 m/s with a thermal conductivity of 0.23 W/m·K. How does the ion impact energy distribution influence the steady-state surface temperature, and what is the minimum cooling rate required to prevent tungsten melting (melting point: 3695 K), assuming 60% of ion energy is converted to localized heating?

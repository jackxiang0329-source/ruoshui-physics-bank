# HFDPO-05583

In a cryogenic quantum memory array, 10^6 nitrogen-vacancy (NV) centers in diamond are used to store qubits at 15 mK. Each NV center has two spin states (|↑⟩ and |↓⟩) with an energy splitting of 2.88 GHz due to the Zeeman effect in a 3.5 T magnetic field. The system is coupled to a phonon bath at finite temperature, leading to spin relaxation times on the order of milliseconds. Calculate the equilibrium probability distribution of spin states across the ensemble and the expected number of spins in |↓⟩ after 100 μs of coherent drive with a Rabi frequency of 1.2 MHz, considering both thermal equilibrium and non-equilibrium dynamics.

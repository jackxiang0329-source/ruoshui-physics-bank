# HFDPO-09260

A quantum imaging lab develops a heralded single-photon source using spontaneous parametric down-conversion (SPDC) in a periodically poled MgO:LiNbO₃ crystal, 30 mm long, driven by a 405 nm pump laser. The emitted photon pairs (signal and idler) are designed to be entangled in polarization and momentum. However, experimental data shows a 25% drop in coincidence counts when the pump beam is focused to a 10 μm waist, compared to a 50 μm waist. Why? And how can the system efficiency be optimized without altering the crystal length or pump wavelength?

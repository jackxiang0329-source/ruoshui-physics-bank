# HFDPO-13213

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a coastal region prone to strong crosswinds. The station's 1.2 m × 0.8 m solar panel is secured at an angle of 30° to the horizontal and fixed to a rigid, lightweight aluminum frame. During a storm, winds reach 25 m/s (approximately 90 km/h), creating a pressure of 350 Pa on the panel’s surface. The panel is attached via a single bolt at its base, which must withstand both shear and moment forces. What is the minimum required shear strength of the bolt to prevent failure?

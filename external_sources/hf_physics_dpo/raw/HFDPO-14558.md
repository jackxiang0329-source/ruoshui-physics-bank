# HFDPO-14558

A fusion reactor plasma ring in a tokamak sustains a toroidal current of 15 MA with a magnetic confinement pressure of 20 MPa. During a sudden disruption, the plasma loses confinement, and the magnetic field collapses in 1.2 ms.

# HFDPO-11784

During a deep-sea mining operation, a remotely operated vehicle detects anomalous seismic signals near a hydrothermal vent field. The signals consist of a 25 Hz transverse wave traveling through the oceanic crust at 3.2 km/s, but the data shows a phase shift of π/3 radians between two sensors spaced 4.8 km apart. Engineers suspect interference from a previously undetected subsurface fracture zone altering wave propagation. Determine the effective thickness of the fracture zone if it causes a localized reduction in wave speed to 2.6 km/s, assuming the wave front enters the zone at a 15° angle relative to the normal.

# HFDPO-15958

A deep-sea research submersible descends into the Mariana Trench, where the water density increases significantly with depth due to compression. At 10,000 m depth, the local density is 1060 kg/m³, and the pressure at the surface is 101 kPa. The sub has a spherical viewing dome with a radius of 0.5 m, oriented horizontally. Due to non-uniform density, the pressure varies with depth as ρ(z) = ρ₀ + kz, where z is depth from the surface and k = 0.015 kg/m⁴. What is the net upward buoyant force acting on the dome, considering the fluid's compressibility and the dome’s orientation?

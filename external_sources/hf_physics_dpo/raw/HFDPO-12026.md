# HFDPO-12026

A high-altitude research balloon carrying a cryogenic sensor payload descends through the stratosphere, where the air density drops to 0.02 kg/m³ and temperature falls to 220 K. The sensor's protective casing is a 0.8 m diameter spherical shell with a 3 mm thick aluminum wall. As the balloon re-enters denser air, the casing experiences sudden heating due to viscous drag. Estimate the average rate of internal energy increase in the casing due to molecular collisions with the outer surface, assuming only translational kinetic energy transfer and that the average molecular speed is 450 m/s. How does this compare to the rate of heat loss via radiation if the casing’s emissivity is 0.15 and surface temperature is 260 K?

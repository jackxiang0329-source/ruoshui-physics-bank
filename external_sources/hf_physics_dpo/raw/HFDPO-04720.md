# HFDPO-04720

In a novel quantum dot solar cell, researchers observe that electron-hole pair recombination rates deviate from classical predictions at high excitation densities. The system contains 10⁵ quantum dots, each capable of holding one exciton, with an average thermal energy of 0.025 eV at 300 K. At peak illumination, the average number of excitons per dot is 0.8. Calculate the entropy change of the exciton population when the system transitions from a low-excitation state (⟨n⟩ = 0.2) to high-excitation (⟨n⟩ = 0.8), assuming Bose-Einstein statistics for excitons. How does this entropy change compare to that expected for a classical Maxwell-Boltzmann system?

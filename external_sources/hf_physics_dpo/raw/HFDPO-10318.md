# HFDPO-10318

A deep-sea hydrothermal vent ecosystem thrives near a volcanic ridge where seawater seeps into fractured crust, gets heated to 350°C at 2.5 km depth, then erupts into cold 2°C seawater. The system operates with 85% of the Carnot efficiency, but the heat exchanger suffers from scaling, reducing heat transfer efficiency by 30%.

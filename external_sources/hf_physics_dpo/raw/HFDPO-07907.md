# HFDPO-07907

A coastal desalination plant uses a closed-loop geothermal heat engine to power its reverse osmosis system, extracting heat from underground hot rock at 180°C. The engine rejects waste heat into the surrounding seawater at 15°C, but fluctuating tides cause the cooling water temperature to vary between 12°C and 18°C. If the plant needs to maintain a minimum net power output of 1.2 MW to sustain operations, what is the minimum required heat input rate (in MW) during peak cooling conditions (18°C), and how much does the required heat input increase if the cooling water drops to 12°C?

# HFDPO-02450

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 25° angle to maximize winter sun exposure. During a rare snowstorm, 120 kg of snow accumulates uniformly across a 2.8 m × 4.5 m panel, creating a distributed load. The mounting brackets at the base can withstand a maximum horizontal reaction force of 1,800 N before slipping. If the coefficient of static friction between the bracket and roof is 0.65, will the array remain stable, or will it slide?

# HFDPO-01550

The condenser rejects heat to a 5°C ambient environment. The turbine operates with 85% isentropic efficiency, and the pump work is 12% of the turbine work output. Due to scaling in the heat exchangers, the actual heat transfer efficiency drops to 72% of ideal. What is the maximum possible thermal efficiency of this system, and how does the actual efficiency compare with the theoretical Carnot limit? Evaluate whether the system is operating within realistic thermodynamic bounds given the losses.

# HFDPO-05236

During a power outage, a hiker uses a 12V LED flashlight powered by two AA batteries in series. The flashlight draws 0.3A when on. After 10 minutes, the light dims noticeably. Assuming the batteries’ internal resistance increases over time due to depletion, what is the total resistance in the circuit when the light dims, if the external resistance of the flashlight remains 40 Ω?

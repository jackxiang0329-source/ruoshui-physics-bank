# HFDPO-09903

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass generates power during high winds, but engineers notice it vibrates excessively when wind speeds exceed 18 m/s. The turbine’s 45-meter-long blade, modeled as a thin rod rotating about one end, has a mass of 12,000 kg. At peak gusts, the blade rotates at 15 RPM. Calculate the torque required to maintain constant angular velocity if the wind exerts a tangential drag force of 8,000 N at the blade tip—assuming no friction in the axle.

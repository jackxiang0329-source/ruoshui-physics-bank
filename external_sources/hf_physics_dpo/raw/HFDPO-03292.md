# HFDPO-03292

A gecko-inspired climbing robot designed for inspecting the curved, wind-exposed surface of a high-voltage transmission tower must maintain grip while ascending at 0.8 m/s under steady wind gusts of 12 m/s. The robot weighs 3.2 kg, and the tower’s surface is inclined at 65°. What is the minimum effective friction coefficient required to prevent slipping during the climb, and how does wind-induced drag influence the necessary normal force?

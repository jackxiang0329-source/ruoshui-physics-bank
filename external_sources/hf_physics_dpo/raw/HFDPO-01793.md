# HFDPO-01793

A drone delivering medical supplies in a mountainous region must navigate a narrow canyon with shifting winds. At t = 0, it enters the canyon at 15 m/s, accelerating at 0.8 m/s² due to a tailwind, but experiences a sudden deceleration of 1.2 m/s² when a crosswind gust hits at t = 4 s. The canyon narrows to 30 m wide at a point 120 m from the entrance. Can the drone maintain lateral stability (within ±5 m of centerline) during its passage if its lateral control system responds with a 0.5 s delay and can correct with up to 3 m/s² lateral acceleration?

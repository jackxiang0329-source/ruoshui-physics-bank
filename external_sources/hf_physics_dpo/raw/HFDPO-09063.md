# HFDPO-09063

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to heat synthetic oil to 400°C, which then transfers heat to a working fluid in a Rankine cycle. Due to inefficiencies and heat loss, the plant operates at only 32% thermal efficiency. Engineers propose integrating phase-change materials (PCMs) in the storage tanks to reduce thermal losses during night operations. If the PCM has a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg and a melting point of 350°C, what mass of PCM per kilogram of oil is required to maintain the working fluid supply temperature above 300°C for 8 hours during a night without sunlight, assuming the oil loses heat at an average rate of 2.5 MW?

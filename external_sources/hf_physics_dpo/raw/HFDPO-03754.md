# HFDPO-03754

A wind farm developer must determine the optimal spacing between two adjacent wind turbines in a row to maximize energy capture without creating destructive turbulence. The upstream turbine operates at 12 m/s wind speed and generates a pressure drop of 45 Pa across its blades.

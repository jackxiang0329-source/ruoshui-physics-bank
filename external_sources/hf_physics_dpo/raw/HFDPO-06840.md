# HFDPO-06840

During a Mars rover mission, a drill mechanism spins a 0.85-meter-long, 2.4-kg cylindrical bit at 1200 rpm to penetrate basalt rock. If the motor delivers constant torque and the bit experiences a resistive torque of 1.7 N·m from rock friction, how much time does it take to reach full speed from rest, and what is the maximum angular acceleration if the motor can supply up to 5.0 N·m? Assume the bit rotates about its center and has uniform density.

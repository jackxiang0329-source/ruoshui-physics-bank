# HFDPO-16530

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass has blades that rotate at 20 rpm when the wind speed reaches 12 m/s. During a storm, gusts increase to 24 m/s, doubling the torque applied to the blades. If the turbine’s rotational inertia is 5.2 × 10⁵ kg·m², how much time will it take for the angular speed to increase from 20 to 30 rpm? Assume constant torque during the gust.

# HFDPO-03867

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole 2.5 km deep to extract heat, with a superconducting coil array suspended at the bottom to harvest energy from naturally occurring geomagnetic field fluctuations. The coil, with 1000 turns and a radius of 1.2 m, experiences a time-varying magnetic flux due to solar-induced geomagnetic storms. Over a 30-minute interval, the vertical component of Earth’s magnetic field changes from 54 μT to 62 μT at a rate that approximates a sinusoidal function with a frequency of 0.001 Hz. The coil is oriented to maximize flux linkage. What is the average induced emf in the coil during this period, and how does this compare to the minimum required emf (0.45 V) to initiate a stable current in the superconducting loop?

# HFDPO-00324

In a fusion reactor's plasma-facing component, high-energy helium ash (He⁺ ions) generated by D-T reactions bombard a tungsten surface at ~30 eV average kinetic energy. Simultaneously, the surface is heated to 2000 K by neutron flux. Estimate the effective molecular speed distribution of the escaping He atoms after surface collision, considering both thermal desorption and the initial kinetic energy from ion impact. How does this distribution affect the rate of helium retention in the tungsten lattice?

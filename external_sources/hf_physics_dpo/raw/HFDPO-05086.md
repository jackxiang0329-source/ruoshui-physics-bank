# HFDPO-05086

In a satellite-based Earth observation system, circularly polarized microwave signals (λ = 3 cm) are transmitted from a low-Earth orbiting platform toward a forest canopy. On the ground, a receiver detects a mixed polarization signal with a measured Stokes parameter vector: (S₀, S₁, S₂, S₃) = (100, 20, 50, 80) mW/m². Determine the degree and type of polarization, and explain how the observed polarization evolution could be used to infer forest biomass density and moisture content.

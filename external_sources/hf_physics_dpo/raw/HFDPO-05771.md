# HFDPO-05771

A fusion reactor’s toroidal magnet system uses high-temperature superconducting tapes (YBCO) cooled to 77 K, but during a sudden power surge, the current density reaches 5 × 10⁶ A/m²—just below the critical limit. However, a localized defect in the tape’s grain boundary causes a weak link, where the critical current drops to 3.2 × 10⁶ A/m². If the magnetic field at the defect exceeds 0.8 T due to self-field enhancement, flux vortices penetrate and trigger a quench. Can the system remain stable? Analyze whether the local current exceeds the critical value under combined transport and self-field conditions, assuming the tape carries 12 kA and the field from the main coil is 15 T.

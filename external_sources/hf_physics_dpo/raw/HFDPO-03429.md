# HFDPO-03429

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a reinforced composite arm (length 18 m, mass 4.2 tons) to deploy sensors into a 2.3 km deep borehole. The arm is pivoted at the base and supported by a hydraulic strut at a 35° angle, with the strut attached 6 m from the pivot. The arm's center of mass is 7.2 m from the pivot, and a 1.8-ton sensor package is suspended 15 m from the pivot. Due to shifting bedrock, the pivot experiences uneven frictional resistance (μ_s = 0.55 on one side, 0.38 on the other). What is the minimum force the hydraulic strut must exert to prevent the arm from rotating clockwise under the combined load, assuming worst-case friction distribution?

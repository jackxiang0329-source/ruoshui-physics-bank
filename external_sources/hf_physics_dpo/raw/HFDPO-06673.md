# HFDPO-06673

A biomedical engineer is designing a lens-free holographic microscope to detect early-stage malaria parasites in blood samples. The system uses a coherent laser (λ = 532 nm) and records interference patterns on a CMOS sensor 15 cm from the sample. What is the effective refractive index difference between the parasite and the surrounding plasma?

# HFDPO-01902

A research team is designing a new type of thermally responsive material for energy-efficient smart windows. The material contains nanoscale switches that toggle between two states—transparent and reflective—based on temperature. At 25°C, the probability of a switch being in the reflective state is 0.1. When heated to 50°C, this probability rises to 0.6. Assuming the switches behave as independent two-level systems with energy difference ΔE between states, what is the estimated ΔE in eV? How would the probability change if the system instead exhibited strong inter-switch interactions?

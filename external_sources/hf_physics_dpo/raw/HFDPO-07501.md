# HFDPO-07501

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert region uses a silicon photodiode to measure sunlight intensity. The diode’s dark current is 1.2 nA at 25°C, but during peak solar exposure, the photocurrent reaches 8.5 μA. Due to temperature fluctuations—ranging from 5°C at night to 60°C during the day—the station’s data log shows inconsistent readings. The team suspects temperature-dependent carrier generation is affecting accuracy. What is the minimum bandgap energy (in eV) the diode must have to ensure that thermal generation (not sunlight) doesn't contribute more than 10% of the total current at 60°C?

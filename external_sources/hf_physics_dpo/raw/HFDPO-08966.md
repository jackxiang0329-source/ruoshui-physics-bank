# HFDPO-08966

In a high-voltage semiconductor fabrication lab, a 12 cm × 12 cm silicon wafer with a uniform surface charge density of −3.2 μC/m² is suspended horizontally by electrostatic forces between two parallel metal plates 5.0 mm apart. The upper plate is held at +150 V relative to the lower plate. If dust particles (diameter ≈ 5 μm, dielectric constant ≈ 4.5) begin to float near the wafer, what is the minimum charge a particle must acquire to be electrostatically repelled from the wafer before it can cause a short circuit? Assume the particle is initially 1.0 mm above the wafer and the electric field is uniform.

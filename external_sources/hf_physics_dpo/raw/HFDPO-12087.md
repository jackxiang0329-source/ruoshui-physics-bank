# HFDPO-12087

A deep-sea research submersible descends through the Mariana Trench, where the seawater density increases non-linearly with depth due to compression. At 10,000 m depth, the pressure is 1.1 × 10⁸ Pa, and the density is 1060 kg/m³. The sub’s spherical observation dome (radius 0.5 m) has a slight, undetected crack that allows seawater to slowly seep in. If the internal pressure is maintained at 1 atm (1.01 × 10⁵ Pa), what is the net inward force on the dome, and how does this compare to the structural yield point of the dome material (2.5 × 10⁶ N)? Assume the density increases with depth as ρ(h) = ρ₀ + kh, where ρ₀ = 1025 kg/m³ and k = 0.035 kg/m⁴.

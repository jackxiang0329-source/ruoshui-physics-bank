# HFDPO-15838

A quantum dot solar cell prototype uses a photonic crystal nanoscale lattice to enhance light absorption in a 100 nm thick indium phosphide layer. The lattice is designed with a periodicity of 350 nm and a refractive index contrast of 2.5, creating a bandgap centered at 600 nm. However, under standard sunlight (AM1.5G spectrum), the device exhibits a 30% drop in photocurrent at 580 nm. Analyze whether this dip arises from a photonic bandgap effect or interference cancellation due to evanescent coupling, and determine the effective mode confinement factor at the defect site.

# HFDPO-01573

A biomedical engineer is designing a laser-based photothermal therapy system to selectively destroy tumor cells in deep tissue. The treatment uses a 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser with a beam diameter of 2 mm focused through a lens with a focal length of 50 mm. The tumor, located 8 cm below the skin surface, has a volume of 0.1 cm³ and absorbs 15% of incident laser power. To achieve therapeutic effect, the temperature must increase by at least 10°C in less than 30 seconds. Given the tissue’s thermal diffusivity (0.14 mm²/s) and specific heat capacity (3.5 J/g·°C), what is the minimum required laser power to ensure effective ablation without damaging surrounding healthy tissue?

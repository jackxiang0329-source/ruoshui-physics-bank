# HFDPO-00399

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system to charge implantable cardiac monitors using inductive coupling. The transmitter coil has a self-inductance of 150 μH and resistance of 2.4 Ω, operating at 1.5 MHz. The receiver coil in the implant has 85 μH inductance and 1.7 Ω resistance. The mutual inductance between them is 22 μH. The system must deliver at least 1.2 W of real power to the implant with a power factor close to unity. What capacitance should be added in series with the transmitter coil to achieve optimal power transfer and minimize reactive power?

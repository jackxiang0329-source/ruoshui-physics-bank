# HFDPO-14892

A lunar habitat module, shaped like a truncated cone (base radius 6 m, top radius 3 m, height 4 m), is anchored to the regolith via three symmetrically placed anchor bolts at the base. During a high-wind event on the Moon (where atmospheric pressure is negligible, but electrostatic dust storms can exert lateral forces), a 1500 N wind load acts horizontally at the centroid of the module’s lateral surface. The anchors are embedded 2 m deep in the regolith, and the coefficient of static friction between anchor and regolith is 0.35. Determine the minimum tensile force each anchor must withstand to prevent rotational failure of the structure, assuming the module’s mass is 8500 kg and its center of mass is located 1.8 m above the base center.

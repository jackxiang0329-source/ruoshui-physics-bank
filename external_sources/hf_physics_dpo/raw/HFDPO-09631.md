# HFDPO-09631

A solar farm in Arizona uses large photovoltaic panels that generate electric fields when exposed to sunlight. On a clear day, the electric field near one panel's surface reaches 1.8 × 10⁴ N/C perpendicular to the surface. If the panel is 2 m wide and 4 m tall, and the electric flux through it is measured as 1.44 × 10⁵ N·m²/C, what is the angle between the electric field and the normal to the panel’s surface?

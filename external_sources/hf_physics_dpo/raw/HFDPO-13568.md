# HFDPO-13568

A renewable energy startup tests a prototype wave energy converter consisting of a submerged buoy tethered to a generator on the ocean floor. The buoy, with a mass of 1,200 kg and volume of 2.0 m³, oscillates vertically in deep water (density 1,025 kg/m³) due to sinusoidal waves with amplitude 1.8 m and period 6.0 s. The tether applies a resistive force proportional to velocity, with a damping coefficient of 450 N·s/m. Calculate the average power extracted by the generator over one cycle, assuming energy is only dissipated through the tether and the system reaches steady-state oscillation.

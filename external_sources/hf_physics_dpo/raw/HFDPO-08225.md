# HFDPO-08225

A team of engineers is designing a low-orbit satellite constellation for global internet coverage, operating at 600 km above Earth’s surface. The satellites must maintain precise formation while minimizing fuel use due to orbital decay from atmospheric drag and gravitational perturbations. One satellite in the formation is slightly off-course, and its position is adjusted using micro-thrusters. However, the gravitational gradient across the formation (due to the inverse-square law and non-uniform field near Earth) causes unintended tidal accelerations. What is the magnitude and direction of the net gravitational tidal force per unit mass acting on a satellite located 100 meters ahead of a reference satellite in the same circular orbit, and how does this affect long-term formation stability?

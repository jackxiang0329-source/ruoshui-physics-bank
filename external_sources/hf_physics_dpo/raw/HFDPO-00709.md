# HFDPO-00709

A quantum computing chip operates at 15 mK inside a dilution refrigerator, using a network of niobium-based superconducting loops to store quantum information. Each loop has an inductance of 2.5 nH and is coupled to a Josephson junction with critical current 1.2 μA. During a calibration pulse, a magnetic field disturbance of 0.05 mT is briefly applied, causing a persistent current to circulate in one loop. What is the minimum flux quantum trapped in this loop, and how does the resulting phase shift in the Josephson junction affect the qubit’s coherence time, given a decoherence rate of 200 Hz?

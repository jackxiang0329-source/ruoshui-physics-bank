# HFDPO-09771

During a high-altitude balloon experiment, a spherical sensor pod (diameter 32 cm, mass 8.4 kg) deploys from a capsule at 35 km altitude. As it descends, atmospheric density drops exponentially, but wind shear increases nonlinearly with height. The pod’s drag coefficient fluctuates due to surface imperfections and rotational instability. Given that terminal velocity must be kept below 60 m/s to prevent sensor damage, and that the pod begins descent at 35 km with an initial speed of 5 m/s, determine the minimum required drag coefficient to ensure safe descent, accounting for both laminar and turbulent flow transitions across the altitude range.

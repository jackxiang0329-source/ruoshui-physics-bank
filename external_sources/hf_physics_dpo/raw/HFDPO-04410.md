# HFDPO-04410

A deep-sea mining drone descends through a stratified ocean layer, where the density increases linearly with depth due to temperature and salinity gradients. At 1,200 m, the drone’s pressure sensor reads 12.3 MPa. The surrounding seawater has a surface density of 1,025 kg/m³ and a density gradient of 0.02 kg/m⁴. The drone’s sealed internal chamber is filled with a compressible gas at 101 kPa and 20°C at the surface. Determine the net buoyant force on the drone at 1,200 m if its volume is 1.8 m³ at the surface and the gas density follows the ideal gas law with constant temperature.

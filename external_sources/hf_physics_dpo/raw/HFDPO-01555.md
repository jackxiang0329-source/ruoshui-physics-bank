# HFDPO-01555

A cryogenic storage facility for quantum computing components uses liquid helium-4 at 1.5 K and 1 atm, but due to a minor insulation breach, localized regions experience transient heating, causing partial evaporation. The system is designed with a superinsulated vacuum chamber, yet a 30 cm² area of the wall experiences a heat influx of 2.4 W due to micro-fractures in the multilayer insulation. The helium is in a closed, rigid container with a total volume of 1.8 m³, initially 40% liquid by volume. Determine the minimum time (in seconds) before the pressure exceeds safe limits (80 kPa) due to phase transition dynamics, assuming the process is adiabatic within the bulk but the heat input drives local vaporization.

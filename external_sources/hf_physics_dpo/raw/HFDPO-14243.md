# HFDPO-14243

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole filled with conductive brine to harness heat from underground magma. Engineers install a cylindrical induction coil (radius 1.5 m, 100 turns) at the surface, oscillating at 50 Hz to induce currents in the brine column below (depth 2.0 km, resistivity 0.3 Ω·m). The magnetic field at the borehole center varies sinusoidally with time as B(t) = B₀ sin(ωt), where B₀ = 0.01 T. Calculate the total power dissipated in the brine column due to induced eddy currents, assuming the field is uniform over the coil’s cross-section and the current flows in closed loops within the brine.

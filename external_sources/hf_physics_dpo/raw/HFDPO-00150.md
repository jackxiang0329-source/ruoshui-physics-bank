# HFDPO-00150

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a 200 m long coil of superconducting wire, suspended in a borehole filled with conductive brine. Due to seasonal thermal expansion, the borehole's diameter changes by 0.5% over 6 months, altering the coil's self-inductance. The plant's monitoring system detects a persistent 12 V emf induced in the coil when the temperature shifts. Determine whether this emf arises from changing inductance or from coupling with a nearby geomagnetic field fluctuation (assumed to vary at 0.1 T/year).

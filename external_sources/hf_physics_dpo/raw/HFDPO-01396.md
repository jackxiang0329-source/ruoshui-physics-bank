# HFDPO-01396

A fusion research team is designing a tokamak reactor with a toroidal plasma chamber where charged particles are confined by magnetic fields. To ensure safety, they must calculate the electric field inside a non-conducting, toroidal shell (inner radius 1.2 m, outer radius 1.5 m) uniformly charged with 3.0 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³. Due to symmetry, they assume the charge distribution is spherically symmetric in local cross-sections. Determine the electric field at a radial distance of 1.35 m from the center.

# HFDPO-01060

A research team at a fusion energy startup is testing a new high-temperature superconducting (HTS) coil designed to generate a 15 T magnetic field for plasma confinement. The coil, made of YBCO tape wound into a solenoid with 120 turns and a 25 cm radius, must carry 500 A without quenching. However, during ramp-up, the system unexpectedly loses superconductivity at 480 A. The team suspects local heating due to flux motion, but the external cooling system maintains the 77 K bath. What physical mechanism could explain the quench despite stable bath temperature, and what must the local critical current density be to sustain 500 A without flux flow?

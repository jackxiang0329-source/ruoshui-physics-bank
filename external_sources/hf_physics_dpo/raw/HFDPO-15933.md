# HFDPO-15933

In a high-voltage fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma chamber is enclosed within a conducting vacuum vessel. The plasma has a non-uniform charge density given by ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(−r²/R²), where r is the radial distance from the torus centerline, R = 0.5 m is the characteristic scale, and ρ₀ = 2.0 × 10⁻⁶ C/m³. Engineers need to determine the electric field magnitude at a radial distance of 0.8 m from the centerline, inside the conducting vessel but outside the plasma region. The vessel is grounded and perfectly conducting, with inner radius 0.6 m and outer radius 1.2 m.

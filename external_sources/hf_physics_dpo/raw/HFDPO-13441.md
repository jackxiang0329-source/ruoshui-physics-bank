# HFDPO-13441

A robotic drone carrying a 2.5 kg payload must navigate a sudden 15° tilt in the wind while maintaining stable hover. The drone’s motors can adjust thrust up to 30% per second. If the wind induces a lateral force of 12 N at the instant of tilt, what is the minimum required thrust adjustment (in N) to prevent the drone from drifting more than 0.3 m in 2 seconds? Assume the drone’s mass is 1.8 kg and initial thrust was perfectly balanced.

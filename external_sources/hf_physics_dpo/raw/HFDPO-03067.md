# HFDPO-03067

A biomedical engineer is designing a portable device to detect early signs of cataracts by analyzing how light scatters through the eye’s lens. The device uses a 635 nm laser pointer and measures scattered light intensity at 90°. In a healthy lens, scattered intensity is 1.2 × 10⁻⁶ W/m² at 1 mm from the beam axis. After testing on a patient, the scattered intensity rises to 8.5 × 10⁻⁶ W/m² at the same location. Assuming Rayleigh scattering dominates and the scattering volume is constant, estimate the effective particle size increase in the lens that could explain the change.

# HFDPO-05184

A biomedical engineer is designing a new endoscopic imaging system that uses a fiber bundle to transmit light through a narrow, curved tube in the human body. Each fiber has a core diameter of 10 μm and a refractive index of 1.46, surrounded by cladding with a refractive index of 1.42. The system must transmit light from a green laser (λ = 532 nm) with minimal signal distortion. What is the maximum angle of incidence at the fiber entrance (measured from the fiber axis) that allows total internal reflection to occur, and how does bending the fiber affect this angle?

# HFDPO-08605

A high-efficiency solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat 1500 J/kg·K, density 2100 kg/m³) stored in a cylindrical tank insulated with 15 cm of silica aerogel (thermal conductivity 0.015 W/m·K). During a 6-hour sunny day, the system absorbs 12 MW of solar energy. At night, the salt must maintain a minimum temperature of 470°C to sustain power generation via a heat engine. After sunset, heat loss occurs through the tank's surface (diameter 10 m, height 8 m). If the salt starts at 520°C, will it stay above 470°C after 8 hours of darkness? Assume natural convection and radiation are negligible; focus on conduction through the insulation.

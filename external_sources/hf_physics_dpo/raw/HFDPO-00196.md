# HFDPO-00196

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial photovoltaic panels mounted at a 30° tilt to maximize sunlight capture. However, glare from reflected sunlight off the white sand reduces efficiency and creates hazardous conditions for nearby wildlife. Engineers model the sand as a dielectric surface with refractive index 1.55, and the incident sunlight as unpolarized with intensity 1000 W/m². At what angle of incidence does the reflected light become completely polarized, and what is the maximum intensity of the polarized glare reaching a bird flying 50 m above the panel?

# HFDPO-01268

A high-altitude balloon carrying a cryogenic sensor payload ascends through the stratosphere, where temperatures drop to –60°C and atmospheric pressure falls to 10 kPa. The sensor’s internal chamber contains 0.2 moles of argon gas initially at 20°C and 100 kPa. As the balloon rises rapidly, the chamber expands adiabatically, and the gas cools to –45°C. Calculate the final volume of the chamber, assuming ideal gas behavior and using kinetic theory to estimate the average speed of argon atoms before and after the ascent. Compare the root-mean-square speed to the speed of sound in argon at both states.

# HFDPO-01297

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat captured during the day, aiming to generate electricity for 8 hours after sunset. The storage system holds 12,000 kg of salt with a specific heat capacity of 1,400 J/kg·K. At peak charge, the salt reaches 560°C, but it must remain above 380°C to maintain turbine efficiency. As night falls, the salt cools by natural conduction and radiation. If the plant loses heat at a rate of 2.4 MW initially, how long will it take for the salt to drop to 380°C, and is this sufficient to meet the 8-hour demand?

# HFDPO-12781

During a seismic retrofit of a historic stone arch bridge, engineers discover a critical section where the central voussoir (arch block) is at risk of tipping due to uneven settlement of the abutments. The 12-meter span arch has a semicircular profile with a 1.5-meter radius; the central voussoir weighs 8,200 N and is symmetrically supported by two inclined reaction forces at its base, each angled at 35° from the vertical. Earthquake-induced lateral ground movement has shifted one abutment downward by 4 cm relative to the other, causing the supporting forces to no longer be symmetric. Determine the minimum coefficient of static friction required at the base to prevent sliding of the central voussoir, assuming the friction is the only resistance to lateral motion.

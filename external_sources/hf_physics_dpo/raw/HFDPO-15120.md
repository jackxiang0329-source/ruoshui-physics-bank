# HFDPO-15120

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a precision microfluidic chip is being designed to separate charged nanoparticles using a non-uniform electric field. The chip contains a rectangular array of 100 parallel, infinitely long conducting plates spaced 50 μm apart, each held at a different potential ranging from –20 V to +20 V in a symmetric ramp. A 200 nm diameter nanoparticle with a net charge of –3e enters the gap between two plates at the midpoint, moving horizontally at 1.2 mm/s. Due to the spatial variation in potential, the particle experiences a force that deflects it from its linear path. What is the vertical displacement of the particle after traveling 1.5 mm horizontally, assuming negligible gravity and air resistance?

# HFDPO-07956

A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new touch-sensitive bezel that uses capacitive sensing to detect finger proximity without contact. The sensor consists of a small parallel-plate capacitor with plates separated by 0.1 mm of air (dielectric constant = 1). The device must detect a human finger, which alters the effective capacitance by changing the local electric field.

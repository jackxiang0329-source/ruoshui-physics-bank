# HFDPO-08457

A deep-sea mining drone, shaped like a slender cylindrical hull (length 3.2 m, diameter 0.6 m), is suspended vertically by a tether at a depth of 2,800 m in the Pacific Ocean. The drone’s center of mass is 0.5 m above its base, and the tether attaches at the top. Due to uneven sediment accumulation, the buoyant force acts 0.3 m off-center from the central axis. Given seawater density of 1,025 kg/m³, drone mass of 1,800 kg, and tether tension limited to 12 kN, determine the maximum allowable offset of the center of buoyancy from the centerline before the tether fails or the drone tips.

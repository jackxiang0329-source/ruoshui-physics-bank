# HFDPO-14380

A small eco-village in rural Nepal relies on a solar-powered microgrid with two parallel battery banks (48 V each) connected via a common distribution bus, but one bank has degraded over time, dropping to 42 V. When a 10 A load (a water pump) is switched on, the system behaves erratically—voltage sags, fuses blow. Use Kirchhoff’s laws to determine the current flowing between the batteries through a diode-protected interconnection, assuming the internal resistance of each battery is 0.3 Ω and the load resistance is 4.8 Ω.

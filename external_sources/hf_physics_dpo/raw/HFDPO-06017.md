# HFDPO-06017

A deep-space probe transmits high-frequency data (12.5 GHz) through a plasma sheath formed during atmospheric reentry. The sheath density increases linearly from 1.2×10¹⁵ m⁻³ at the outer edge to 4.8×10¹⁵ m⁻³ over a 25 cm thickness. Using Maxwell’s equations and plasma dispersion relations, determine the fraction of the original signal amplitude that reaches the receiver after traversing the sheath. Assume the wave propagates perpendicular to the plasma gradient and the signal is initially circularly polarized.

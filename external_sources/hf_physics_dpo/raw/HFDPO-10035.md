# HFDPO-10035

A deep-sea mining drone descends through the ocean to extract polymetallic nodules from the seabed at 3,500 meters depth. The drone’s buoyancy system uses a sealed, flexible bladder filled with oil (density 850 kg/m³) surrounded by seawater (density 1,025 kg/m³). As it sinks, the bladder compresses due to increasing pressure—its volume decreases from 1.2 m³ at the surface to 0.7 m³ at depth. The drone’s total mass is 6,000 kg, and the bladder is designed to maintain neutral buoyancy at depth. What must the initial oil mass in the bladder be to achieve neutral buoyancy at 3,500 m, accounting for compressibility and hydrostatic pressure?

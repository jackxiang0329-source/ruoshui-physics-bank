# HFDPO-03426

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 10 cm × 10 cm silicon wafer (initially neutral) is being charged by a nearby high-voltage electrode (±5 kV) during plasma etching. The wafer is suspended by insulating tethers and slowly rotates to ensure uniform exposure. However, the wafer develops non-uniform surface charge density due to asymmetric ion flux, with peak charge density reaching 1.2 μC/m² near one edge. A nearby charged probe measures an electric field of 3.5 × 10⁴ N/C perpendicular to the wafer surface at a 1 mm distance. Is the measured field consistent with the observed charge density?

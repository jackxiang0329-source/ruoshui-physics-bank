# HFDPO-13845

A coastal engineer is designing a wave-energy converter (WEC) that must operate efficiently in deep water (depth = 120 m) where wave energy is highest. The device is tethered to the seabed and uses a submersible float that moves vertically with surface waves. The float has a diameter of 8 m and is designed to respond to waves with a period of 10 s. However, during testing, the float exhibits excessive vertical oscillations at subharmonic frequencies—1/3 of the wave period—despite being tuned for the primary wave frequency. Explain the underlying fluid-dynamic mechanism causing this nonlinear resonance and estimate the effective damping ratio required to suppress it, assuming the system behaves as a nonlinear oscillator under unsteady potential flow.

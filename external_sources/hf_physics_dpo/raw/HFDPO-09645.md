# HFDPO-09645

A deep-sea submersible descends through 3,000 meters of North Atlantic water, encountering a thermocline where temperature drops sharply, increasing water density by 2.4% and viscosity by 1.8%. The craft’s streamlined hull (diameter 2.1 m, length 12 m) travels at 1.6 m/s. If the drag coefficient increases from 0.08 (in warm, less viscous water) to 0.13 in the cold layer due to boundary layer transition, by what percentage does the power required to maintain speed increase? Assume the craft’s mass is 9,500 kg and the density of seawater is 1025 kg/m³ at the surface.

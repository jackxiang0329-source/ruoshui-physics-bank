# HFDPO-01534

A fusion reactor prototype uses a tokamak design with a central solenoid producing a 15 T magnetic field to confine plasma. The toroidal field coils, arranged in a ring with a major radius of 6 m and minor radius of 1.2 m, must generate a 5 T field at the plasma edge. If each coil carries 100 kA and has 120 turns, what is the minimum number of such coils needed to achieve the required field, and how does field non-uniformity due to finite coil spacing affect plasma stability?

# HFDPO-05953

A research team in Antarctica is developing a new thermal insulation layer for portable shelters using phase-changing materials (PCMs) embedded in a polymer matrix. The PCM has a melting point of 15°C and a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg. During a test in a -20°C environment, the shelter’s interior is kept at 20°C by a small heater. The insulation layer is 5 cm thick, with thermal conductivity of 0.04 W/m·K. After 12 hours, the team observes that only 40% of the PCM has melted. What is the average power output of the heater, assuming no heat loss except through conduction and that the PCM behaves ideally?

# HFDPO-06412

A quantum imaging lab uses a two-photon interferometer to enhance resolution in biological tissue imaging beyond the diffraction limit. The detector array records a visibility drop from 0.98 to 0.74 in the interference pattern. Determine whether the observed decrease in visibility is consistent with the phase shift alone or if additional decoherence effects—such as path length mismatch or photon loss—are present.

# HFDPO-08991

A quantum dot solar cell prototype exhibits unexpected efficiency drops under focused sunlight, despite optimized material absorption. Measurements show interference fringes in the reflected light pattern when illuminated at 532 nm, with fringe spacing of 4.2 μm on a detector 15 cm from the surface. The device layer stack includes a 280 nm indium gallium nitride (InGaN) active layer atop a transparent conductive oxide (TCO) with refractive index 1.8. Determine whether destructive interference at the solar cell’s operating wavelength is contributing to reduced photon absorption, and if so, calculate the effective thickness of the TCO layer that would minimize reflection and maximize absorption.

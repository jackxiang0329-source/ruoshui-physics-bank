# HFDPO-02083

A fusion reactor's tokamak design uses a 2.5 T toroidal magnetic field to confine a plasma at 150 million K. However, a small, localized current in the plasma edge causes a minor magnetic perturbation that destabilizes the confinement, leading to micro-turbulence. Engineers observe that the magnetic field lines are locally stretched into a helical structure with a pitch angle of 12°. What is the minimum axial field component required to suppress this instability, given that the plasma beta (ratio of plasma pressure to magnetic pressure) must remain below 10% for stability?

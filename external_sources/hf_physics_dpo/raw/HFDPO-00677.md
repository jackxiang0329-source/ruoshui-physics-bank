# HFDPO-00677

A deep-sea robotic explorer detects a faint, flickering glow from a hydrothermal vent 120 meters below the ocean surface, but the signal is distorted and intermittently lost. The camera lens, designed for high-resolution imaging in clear water, begins to produce severe spherical aberration when the light passes through a turbulent, thermally stratified layer near the vent. Using a known refractive index gradient n(z) = 1.33 + 0.001z (where z is depth in meters from the surface), determine the optimal focal length of a corrective lens placed 10 meters above the camera to minimize image degradation due to refractive path bending. Assume the light rays originate from a point source at the vent and the corrective lens is thin and spherical.

# HFDPO-01356

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor deforestation in the Amazon must maintain level flight at 12 m/s while carrying a 3.5 kg camera payload. The drone’s propellers must exert a thrust force equal to its total weight to sustain altitude. If the battery delivers 1800 J of energy per minute and 85% of that energy is lost to heat and inefficiencies in the motor and electronics, what is the maximum distance the drone can fly before its battery depletes?

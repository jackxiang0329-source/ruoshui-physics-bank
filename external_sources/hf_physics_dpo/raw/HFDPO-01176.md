# HFDPO-01176

A desert oasis engineer designs a passive water-filtration system using a series of tapered bamboo pipes buried underground to draw up shallow groundwater via capillary action and vapor pressure gradients. The system relies on Bernoulli’s principle to maintain flow through a 1.8 m long, non-uniform pipe with a diameter that tapers from 4.0 cm at the inlet to 1.5 cm at the outlet. If the water level in the aquifer is 2.5 m below the surface and the outlet is 0.3 m above ground, calculate the minimum vapor pressure at the pipe’s narrowest point to prevent cavitation, assuming laminar flow, no viscous losses, and atmospheric pressure at the surface.

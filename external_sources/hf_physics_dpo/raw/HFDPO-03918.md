# HFDPO-03918

A city is testing a new kinetic energy recovery system on buses that brake on steep downhill routes. Each bus (mass 12,000 kg) descends a 6% grade (slope angle ≈ 3.4°) at 40 km/h when brakes are applied. The system captures 65% of the mechanical energy lost during braking and stores it in a flywheel. If the bus slows from 40 km/h to 15 km/h over 300 m of descent, what is the rotational kinetic energy stored in the flywheel?

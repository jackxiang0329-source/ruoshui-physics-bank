# HFDPO-16173

A quantum dot qubit in a superconducting circuit exhibits coherent Rabi oscillations at 15 GHz, but coherence degrades rapidly due to coupling with a fluctuating phonon bath. The effective potential experienced by the qubit is anharmonic, with a frequency-dependent coupling strength given by ξ(x) = ξ₀(1 + αx²), where x is displacement and α ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻⁸ m⁻². To stabilize operation, engineers must engineer a nonlinear feedback circuit that shifts the oscillator’s natural frequency depending on amplitude, effectively creating a tunable effective mass. What amplitude-dependent mass function m_eff(A) would cancel the anharmonicity and restore pure harmonic behavior?

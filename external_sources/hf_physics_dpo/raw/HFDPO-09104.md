# HFDPO-09104

During a winter storm, a weather station in the Canadian Prairies records a sudden drop in temperature from –5°C to –15°C over 90 minutes. The station’s humidity sensor indicates 92% relative humidity. A maintenance technician notices that the protective cover on the sensor is rapidly accumulating frost. What is the minimum mass of water vapor (in grams) that must condense and freeze per square meter of surface to form a 0.3 mm thick frost layer, assuming the frost has a density of 0.2 g/cm³ and the area is 1 m²?

# HFDPO-04891

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a closed-loop system where 120 kg of superheated steam at 400°C enters a turbine, expands to 150°C, and exits at a pressure of 1.8 MPa. Engineers estimate the turbine’s rotational speed has dropped by 12% over a year due to blade wear. If the plant must maintain 3.2 MW of electrical output, what is the minimum mass flow rate of steam needed to compensate for the efficiency loss, assuming no heat loss and adiabatic expansion?

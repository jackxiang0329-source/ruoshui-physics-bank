# HFDPO-12390

The biologist suspects the sound is not seismic but generated by a standing wave system formed between the rift and a steep underwater cliff. Can the observed arrival time and frequency range be explained by the formation of a standing wave in a 12-km-long waveguide (seawater layer)? If so, what harmonic mode(s) are most likely responsible, and is this consistent with the reef’s structural response?

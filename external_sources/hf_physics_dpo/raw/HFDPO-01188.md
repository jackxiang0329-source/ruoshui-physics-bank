# HFDPO-01188

A deep-sea research submersible's titanium pressure hull, designed as a thin-walled spherical shell with 1.2 m radius and 8 mm thickness, experiences a sudden 300-meter descent into the Mariana Trench. As pressure rises from 1 atm to 1100 atm, the hull deforms elastically. Engineers must assess whether the accumulated strain energy exceeds the material’s elastic limit to prevent permanent deformation. Given titanium’s Young’s modulus of 116 GPa and Poisson’s ratio of 0.34, estimate the strain energy stored per unit volume and determine if the yield strength of 880 MPa is exceeded at peak pressure.

# HFDPO-04251

A quantum physicist is designing a nano-scale optical filter for a satellite-based spectroscopy instrument, using a 1D grating with 500 nm-wide slits separated by 700 nm. The filter must block all wavelengths from 400 nm to 500 nm except for a sharp peak at 450 nm, which must be transmitted with 90% efficiency. However, a nearby interference pattern from second-order diffraction at 450 nm causes a destructive interference null at 480 nm—potentially corrupting data. What is the minimum number of slits required to suppress this unwanted null to less than 5% of the peak intensity, assuming coherence length of 1000 nm?

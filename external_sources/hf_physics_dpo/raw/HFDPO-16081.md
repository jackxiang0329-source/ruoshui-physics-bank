# HFDPO-16081

A solar-powered microgrid on a remote island uses a hybrid system with two parallel photovoltaic arrays (Array A: 120 V, 30 A max; Array B: 110 V, 35 A max) connected through a shared DC bus with a 5 Ω shunt resistor to monitor total current. During a sudden cloud cover event, Array A’s output drops to 90 V, while Array B remains at 110 V. A battery bank (100 V nominal, internal resistance 0.5 Ω) is connected in parallel to the bus, but the charge controller fails, allowing reverse current flow. Use Kirchhoff’s laws to determine whether the battery will discharge, and quantify the net current through the shunt resistor, assuming no other loads.

# HFDPO-15475

A marine biologist uses a waterproof digital camera with a fixed focal length lens to photograph a coral reef 2.5 meters below the surface. Due to water’s refractive index (1.33), the reef appears closer than it is. To capture a sharp image, the camera’s sensor must be positioned at the image plane. If the lens has a focal length of 50 mm in air, what adjustment must the biologist make to the lens-to-sensor distance when submerged, assuming the lens is designed for air and the camera’s depth gauge reads object distance in water?

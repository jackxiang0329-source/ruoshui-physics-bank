# HFDPO-15426

In a high-vacuum semiconductor fabrication chamber, a 1.2 mm diameter silicon nitride sphere (ρ = 3200 kg/m³, ε_r = 6.5) levitates between two horizontal parallel plates separated by 5.0 mm. The upper plate is held at +150 V, and the lower at −150 V. The sphere, initially neutral, acquires a net charge via field-induced electron emission from the lower plate.

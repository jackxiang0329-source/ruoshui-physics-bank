# HFDPO-12044

During a deep-sea drilling operation, a drilling fluid (mud) with viscosity 0.8 Pa·s and density 1200 kg/m³ is pumped down a 3000 m vertical pipe (inner diameter 0.1 m) at a flow rate of 0.05 m³/s. At the bottom, the fluid must exert a minimum pressure of 5 MPa to stabilize the borehole. Calculate whether the pressure at the bottom will be sufficient, considering both viscous dissipation and hydrostatic head. The system operates at 80°C, where the fluid’s viscosity drops to 0.4 Pa·s—should the operation proceed, or will cavitation occur at the pump inlet due to pressure drop?

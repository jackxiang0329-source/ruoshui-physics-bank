# HFDPO-13193

A biomedical engineer is designing a soft robotic gripper for delicate tissue manipulation in minimally invasive surgery. What is the radial displacement at the inner surface, and how does this affect the grip force if the tissue has a Young’s modulus of 18 kPa and behaves linearly?

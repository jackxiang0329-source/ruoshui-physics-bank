# HFDPO-02443

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses a novel photovoltaic panel design that incorporates a nanostructured surface with periodic grooves spaced 600 nm apart, intended to enhance light absorption via diffraction. During testing, engineers notice that the panel’s efficiency drops sharply at a specific angle of sunlight incidence, particularly around noon in summer. They suspect this is due to destructive interference of reflected sunlight from the grating structure. Given that the incoming sunlight has an average wavelength of 550 nm, at what incident angle (relative to the surface normal) will destructive interference first occur for reflected light of this wavelength?

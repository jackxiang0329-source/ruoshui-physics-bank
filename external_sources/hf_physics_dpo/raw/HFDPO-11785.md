# HFDPO-11785

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The system stores 500 MWh of energy in 10,000 kg of molten nitrate salt, initially at 560°C. At night, the salt is cooled to 320°C to generate steam. Engineers estimate the system’s efficiency is only 65% of theoretical maximum. What is the actual energy output, and how does this compare to the theoretical maximum based on Carnot efficiency assuming a condenser at 40°C?

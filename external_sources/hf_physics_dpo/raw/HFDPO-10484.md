# HFDPO-10484

A satellite in geosynchronous orbit above Mars must maintain precise timing for communication with rovers on the surface. Due to Mars’ weaker gravity and rotation, general relativistic effects cause the satellite’s clock to drift relative to Earth-based clocks by ~22 microseconds per day.

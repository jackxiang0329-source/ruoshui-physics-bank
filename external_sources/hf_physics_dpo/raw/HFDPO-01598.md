# HFDPO-01598

A biomedical engineer is designing a new MRI-compatible neurostimulation device using transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) coils. The device must deliver a pulsed magnetic field of 1.5 T within 100 μs to safely stimulate neurons, but the coil’s inductance and resistive losses limit the field rise time. The coil has a radius of 12 cm, 20 turns, and is wound with 0.4 mm diameter copper wire. The power supply can deliver up to 500 V. What is the minimum required peak current to achieve the target field rise, and does the coil’s parasitic resistance and time constant allow this within the 100 μs window? Can the system be redesigned to meet the timing requirement without exceeding power constraints?

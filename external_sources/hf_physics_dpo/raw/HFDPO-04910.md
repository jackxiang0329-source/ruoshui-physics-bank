# HFDPO-04910

A drone delivery system must navigate a narrow canyon during a wildfire rescue mission. The drone starts at rest 300 m above a cliff edge and descends at a controlled acceleration of 2.0 m/s² for 5.0 s, then instantly switches to free fall with gravity (9.8 m/s² downward). At the same moment, a firefighter on the canyon floor launches a rescue rope straight upward at 35 m/s from 50 m below the cliff. Determine whether the rope will reach the drone’s lowest point, and if so, at what time and position relative to the canyon floor.

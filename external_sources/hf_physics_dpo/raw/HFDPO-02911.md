# HFDPO-02911

In a next-generation quantum memory array, 128 qubits are arranged in a 2D lattice, each coupled to a superconducting resonator. Due to fabrication imperfections, a fraction of qubits have slightly shifted energy levels, creating a local energy disorder of σ = 1.5 GHz. At operating temperature T = 15 mK, the system exhibits anomalous decoherence: the survival probability of a logical qubit state decays faster than expected from standard spin-bath models. You are tasked with diagnosing whether the system is dominated by many-body localization (MBL) or Anderson localization due to disorder, and determine the critical disorder strength required for localization to emerge.

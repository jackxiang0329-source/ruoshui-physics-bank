# HFDPO-02268

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must ascend from 2 km to 5 km above sea level while conserving energy for data transmission. Its lift system generates variable thrust due to decreasing air density, modeled by a force F(h) = F_0 e^{-h/H} , where F_0 = 800,text{N} , H = 10,text{km} , and h is altitude in km. The drone has a mass of 30 kg and experiences drag proportional to speed squared. If it begins at 2 km with speed 10 m/s and must reach 5 km with at least 5 m/s to maintain stability, what is the minimum energy the solar array must supply, accounting for both work against gravity and drag?

# HFDPO-01505

A cosmic-ray muon, created 15 km above Earth’s surface at nearly the speed of light (0.998c), travels toward the ground. From the muon’s perspective, the distance to Earth is significantly contracted. How far does the muon perceive the distance to be, and why does it survive the journey to the surface despite its short lifetime (2.2 μs in its rest frame)?

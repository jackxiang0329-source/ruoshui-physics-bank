# HFDPO-06296

Assuming radiation damage creates deep-level traps with energy levels at Eₜ = Eᵥ + 0.45 eV, determine the effective trap density (Nₜ) that would account for the observed voltage degradation, and explain how this affects the Shockley-Read-Hall recombination mechanism.

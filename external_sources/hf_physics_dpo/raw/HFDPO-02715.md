# HFDPO-02715

A research team is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable medical devices, using a primary coil outside the body and a secondary coil embedded in a pacemaker. The secondary coil (radius 2 cm, 50 turns) is positioned 5 cm away, coaxial and aligned with the primary. Due to tissue attenuation and magnetic shielding in the device casing, only 60% of the magnetic flux from the primary reaches the secondary. Calculate the peak voltage induced in the secondary coil, assuming the system operates in free space with negligible eddy currents.

# HFDPO-02898

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using deterministic lateral displacement (DLD) in a pillar array. The device operates at 37°C, with blood viscosity μ = 3.5 mPa·s and density ρ = 1060 kg/m³. Pillars are arranged in a periodic lattice with pitch 12 μm and diameter 4 μm, and the flow is driven by a pressure drop of 15 kPa over a 1.5 mm channel length. The goal is to achieve a particle capture efficiency of ≥90% for 12 μm CTCs while minimizing shear stress (to preserve cell viability). What is the maximum allowable flow rate (in μL/min) that maintains laminar flow and ensures particle migration through the array without clogging?

# HFDPO-02957

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for an implantable pacemaker using resonant inductive coupling. The transmitter coil (10 cm diameter, 100 turns) operates at 6.78 MHz with a 100 Ω resistance and 25 μH inductance. The receiver coil (5 cm diameter, 30 turns, 20 Ω resistance) is placed 8 cm from the transmitter. The system must deliver 2.5 W to the receiver with >70% efficiency. Determine the required source voltage and capacitance to achieve resonance, and evaluate whether the system can meet efficiency targets under realistic parasitic coupling from nearby tissue (assume 200 Ω loss impedance in the receiving path).

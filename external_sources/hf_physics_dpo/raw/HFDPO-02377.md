# HFDPO-02377

A quantum dot solar cell under concentrated sunlight (1000 suns) shows a sudden drop in efficiency at high irradiance, despite increased current. The device uses InAs quantum dots embedded in a GaAs matrix with a built-in electric field of 1.2 × 10⁵ V/m. At 1000 suns, carrier recombination rates increase non-linearly, and photoluminescence decay times shorten from 2.4 ns (at 1 sun) to 0.3 ns. Explain the dominant recombination mechanism responsible for efficiency loss under high concentration, and estimate the minimum defect density (in cm⁻³) required to account for the observed drop, given that radiative recombination is negligible and Auger recombination dominates only above 10¹⁹ cm⁻³.

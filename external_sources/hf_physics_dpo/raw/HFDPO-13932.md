# HFDPO-13932

A lunar lander descends toward a rocky expanse on the Moon at 20 m/s, 500 m above the surface, using thrusters to maintain a constant deceleration of 2.5 m/s². Just as it reaches 100 m altitude, a sudden micrometeoroid swarm strikes the left thruster, reducing its thrust by 40%. The lander’s guidance system compensates by increasing the right thruster’s output by 25%, but the asymmetric forces cause the lander to begin rotating. Given the lander’s moment of inertia is 7500 kg·m² and the thruster offset from the center of mass is 1.8 m, determine the angular acceleration at the instant of impact. Assume no other forces act on the lander during the brief thruster imbalance.

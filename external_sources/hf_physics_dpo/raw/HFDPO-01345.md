# HFDPO-01345

A fusion reactor prototype uses a 12-cm-diameter superconducting solenoid cooled to 4.2 K with liquid helium. During a sudden power surge, the magnetic field collapses unevenly, inducing a 1.8 A current in a nearby niobium-titanium vortex core. If the vortex penetrates 1.5 mm into the superconductor’s surface and the critical current density is 1.2 × 10^9 A/m², will the superconducting state be locally destroyed? Assume the current density from the vortex is confined to a toroidal shell 0.5 mm wide.

# HFDPO-08779

In a remote off-grid village, a solar-powered water pump system uses a 24 V DC battery bank and a 120 W pump motor with internal resistance of 1.2 Ω. Due to aging, the connecting copper wires (20 m total length, 2.5 mm² cross-section) have developed corrosion, raising their effective resistance. When the pump runs, the voltage at the motor drops to 20.4 V. What is the new resistance of the corroded wiring, and what percentage of the battery’s power is now lost in the wires?

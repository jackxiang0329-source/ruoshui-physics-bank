# HFDPO-01140

In a deep-space satellite power system, a redundant array of thermocouples converts waste heat from the reactor into electricity. Each thermocouple has a resistance of 0.45 Ω and generates 1.2 mV per degree Kelvin of temperature difference across its junctions. The array consists of 240 thermocouples arranged in 6 parallel branches, each with 40 in series. The load requires exactly 3.0 W at 5.0 V. If the hot junctions are at 800 K and the cold junctions are at 400 K, what is the effective internal resistance of the power system, and how must the load resistance be adjusted to maximize power transfer?

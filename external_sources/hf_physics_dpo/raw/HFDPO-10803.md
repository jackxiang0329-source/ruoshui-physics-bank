# HFDPO-10803

A wind turbine in a remote mountain pass spins at 24 rpm during peak gusts, but a sudden mechanical fault causes one of its three blades to detach mid-rotation. The remaining two blades are identical and symmetrically mounted, but the imbalance generates dangerous vibrations. If the original blade had a mass of 1,200 kg and was 50 m long, rotating about its root, how does the sudden loss of one blade affect the system’s rotational kinetic energy and angular momentum? Assume the rotor’s moment of inertia was initially 1.8 × 10⁷ kg·m² and the angular speed remains nearly constant during the event.

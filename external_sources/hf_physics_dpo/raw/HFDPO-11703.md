# HFDPO-11703

A solar-powered sensor network in a remote wildlife reserve uses a single 12 V battery and three identical photovoltaic panels, each rated at 20 W under full sun. The panels are connected in parallel to charge the battery via a DC-DC buck converter that maintains a 12 V output. When sunlight drops to 40% intensity, the panels deliver only 40% of their rated power. If the sensor nodes draw 1.5 A total during active transmission, and the battery must sustain operation for at least 6 hours during low-light nights, what minimum battery capacity (in Ah) is required, assuming 90% efficiency in the converter and 85% battery discharge efficiency?

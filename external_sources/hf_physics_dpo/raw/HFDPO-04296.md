# HFDPO-04296

A Mars rover’s power system uses a thin, flexible copper wire bundle (diameter 0.2 mm, resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) to transmit 100 W from a solar array to a thermal regulation module 5 meters away. The wire is wrapped around a cylindrical spool with a 2 cm radius, forming 150 turns. Due to thermal stress, the wire’s resistivity increases by 12% at the rover’s operating temperature. If the system must maintain at least 95% of the delivered power at the module, what is the minimum voltage required at the solar array?

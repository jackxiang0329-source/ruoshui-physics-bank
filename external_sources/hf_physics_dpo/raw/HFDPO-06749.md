# HFDPO-06749

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a technician notices that microchips are developing random charge spots after being transported through a vacuum chamber. The chamber walls are grounded, but measurements show a persistent electric field of 1200 V/m near a corner where a titanium alloy shield is mounted. What is the potential difference between the shield’s inner surface and the chamber wall, and why might the microchips still accumulate charge despite the grounding?

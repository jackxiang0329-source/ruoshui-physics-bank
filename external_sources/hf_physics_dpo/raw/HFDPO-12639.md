# HFDPO-12639

A satellite operator needs to adjust the orbit of a geostationary communications satellite after a minor thruster malfunction caused a 150 km deviation in its apogee. The satellite’s current orbit is circular at 35,786 km above Earth’s surface, but now has an elliptical path with perigee at 35,636 km. What minimum Δv (change in velocity) is required at perigee to re-circularize the orbit, and how does this compare to the Δv needed if the correction were made at apogee? Assume Earth’s mass is 5.97 × 10²⁴ kg and radius is 6,371 km.

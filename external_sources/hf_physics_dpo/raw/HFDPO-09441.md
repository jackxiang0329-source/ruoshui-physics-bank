# HFDPO-09441

A quantum dot solar cell uses a thin film of aligned organic dyes to enhance light absorption, but performance drops sharply under glare from a nearby reflective window. The dyes are polarized to absorb only light with electric fields aligned along their molecular axis (θ = 0°), but ambient sunlight is unpolarized. To maximize efficiency, engineers consider placing a quarter-wave plate between the cell and the window. If the incident sunlight has a total intensity of 1000 W/m² and is partially polarized with a polarization degree of 0.60 (60% polarized along a 30° direction), what must the wave plate's fast axis be oriented at to make the light incident on the solar cell fully polarized along the dye axis?

# HFDPO-09871

A quantum imaging team at a research lab uses a single-photon interferometer with a variable double-slit setup to probe decoherence in atmospheric turbulence. The slits are separated by 50 μm, each 10 μm wide, and illuminated by a 633 nm laser. When the interferometer is placed atop a 300-meter-tall tower, the fringe visibility drops to 45% compared to a controlled lab environment. The researchers suspect that the turbulence-induced phase fluctuations are distorting the interference pattern. Determine the minimum coherence length required in the atmosphere for this visibility loss to occur, and evaluate whether this is consistent with Kolmogorov turbulence theory for this altitude and wavelength.

# HFDPO-16252

A fusion reactor's cryogenic confinement system uses a high-temperature superconducting (HTS) solenoid cooled to 40 K, designed to maintain a 15 T magnetic field to stabilize plasma. During a power surge, a small section of the coil experiences a localized temperature rise to 60 K due to a quench event. The HTS material has a critical current density J_c(T) = J_c(0) × (1 - T/T_c)^1.5, where T_c = 90 K and J_c(0) = 10^7 A/m². If the coil carries 1.2 kA and the cross-sectional area is 2 mm², determine whether the affected region sustains superconductivity or undergoes thermal runaway, and explain the physical mechanism that could trigger this transition.

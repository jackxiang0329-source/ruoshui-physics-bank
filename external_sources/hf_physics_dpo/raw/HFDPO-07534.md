# HFDPO-07534

A renewable energy startup is designing a gravity-based energy storage system using massive concrete blocks lifted by electric winches during periods of surplus solar generation. Each block has a mass of 12,000 kg and is raised 85 meters vertically through a frictionless pulley system. When energy demand peaks, the blocks descend, driving generators with 88% efficiency. If the system must deliver 1.5 MWh of usable electrical energy over 4 hours, how much gravitational potential energy must be stored initially, and what mechanical work is dissipated during the descent due to inefficiencies?

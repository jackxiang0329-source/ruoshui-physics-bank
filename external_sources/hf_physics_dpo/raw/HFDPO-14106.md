# HFDPO-14106

A wind farm in a coastal region experiences inconsistent energy output due to turbulent airflow caused by offshore cliffs. Engineers observe that turbines located 300 m downwind of a 150 m tall cliff produce 18% less power than expected, despite identical wind speeds measured at the same height. Assume streamline flow over the cliff and a 5% reduction in effective wind speed due to pressure-induced flow separation.

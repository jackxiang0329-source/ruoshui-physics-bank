# HFDPO-01108

A private space station, orbiting Earth at 600 km altitude with a 28° inclination, must perform a critical debris avoidance maneuver. A piece of orbital debris (2 cm diameter, density 7800 kg/m³) is detected on a highly elliptical trajectory intersecting the station’s orbit at 45° true anomaly. The station’s current velocity is 7.58 km/s, and the debris is moving at 7.42 km/s at the point of closest approach. To avoid collision, the station must execute a 5 m/s burn at a specific phase angle. What is the minimum required burn magnitude and optimal direction if the station uses a single impulsive burn? Assume a spherical Earth with J₂ perturbations and atmospheric drag at 600 km is negligible.

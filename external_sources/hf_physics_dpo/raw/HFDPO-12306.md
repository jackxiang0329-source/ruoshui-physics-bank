# HFDPO-12306

If the strain-induced potential well depth is 120 meV and the dot diameter is 8 nm, estimate the minimum photon energy required to excite a carrier from the ground state into the conduction band, considering both quantization effects and the modified effective mass (0.04m₀). How does this compare to the bandgap of bulk GaAs (1.42 eV), and what implications does this have for the cell's efficiency under low-light conditions?

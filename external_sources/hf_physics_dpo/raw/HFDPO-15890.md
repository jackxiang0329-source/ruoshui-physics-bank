# HFDPO-15890

Estimate the effective temperature at which the system would exhibit a phase transition to a spin-ordered state, given that the critical energy scale for ordering is E_c = 4.5 × 10⁻²¹ J. Use the renormalization group concept to determine the scaling behavior of the partition function near criticality.

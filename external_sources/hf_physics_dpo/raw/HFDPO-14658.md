# HFDPO-14658

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a central receiver tube filled with molten salt (NaNO₃/KNO₃), which must remain above 300°C to maintain efficiency. During a sudden cloud cover event, the solar input drops to 30% of its peak value (500 W/m²), while the tube, 8 m long and 0.1 m diameter, loses heat via convection (h = 15 W/m²·K) and radiation (ε = 0.9). The salt has a density of 1800 kg/m³, specific heat of 1300 J/kg·K, and initial temperature of 320°C. The plant’s control system must determine if the salt temperature will drop below 300°C within 15 minutes. How much thermal energy must be actively stored or supplied to prevent this?

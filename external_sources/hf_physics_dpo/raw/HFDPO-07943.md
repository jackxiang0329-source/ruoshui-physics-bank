# HFDPO-07943

A research team investigating volcanic lightning in ash plumes discovers an unexplained oscillating magnetic signal at 12 Hz, 3 km above a dormant caldera. Using a handheld magnetometer array, they measure a time-varying magnetic field amplitude of 60 nT, increasing at 0.8 nT/s, perpendicular to a large, horizontally oriented ash-rich layer with resistivity 150 Ω·m and thickness 200 m. The team suspects induced currents in the conductive layer are generating the signal. What is the minimum horizontal electric field magnitude required in the layer to account for the observed rate of change of the magnetic field, assuming uniform induction?

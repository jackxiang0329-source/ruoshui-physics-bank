# HFDPO-09395

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) experiences intermittent power loss due to a damaged 150-meter coaxial cable connecting it to the surface ship. At a constant 300 V supply from the surface, the ROV draws 2.0 A. What is the actual voltage delivered to the ROV, and why does power delivery fluctuate even with a stable source?

# HFDPO-04853

In a fusion reactor prototype, a toroidal plasma is confined by a magnetic field generated by 12 identical superconducting coils arranged symmetrically around a circular chamber. Each coil carries 50 kA and produces a field of 1.8 T at the plasma center. During a disruption event, a localized fault causes one coil to lose 40% of its current. What is the resulting net force on the plasma column, and how does this asymmetric field alter the plasma's equilibrium position? Assume the plasma is shaped as a 1 m radius torus and carries 2 MA of current in the poloidal direction.

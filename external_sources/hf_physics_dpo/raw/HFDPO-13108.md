# HFDPO-13108

A small hydroelectric microgrid in a remote village uses a 60 Hz AC generator connected to a 10 km transmission line with inductance 0.3 mH/km and resistance 0.5 Ω/km. The load is a 12 kVA inductive motor with a power factor of 0.7 lagging. During peak usage, voltage at the generator drops to 220 V RMS, but the village demands stable 230 V at the load end. What capacitance must be added in parallel at the load to correct the voltage to 230 V without overcompensating?

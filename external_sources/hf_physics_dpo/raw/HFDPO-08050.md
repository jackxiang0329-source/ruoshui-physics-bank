# HFDPO-08050

A newly launched CubeSat in a 500 km circular LEO orbits Earth, but its on-board thrusters are failing to maintain attitude control due to a suspected torque imbalance from asymmetric solar panel deployment. Mission control observes a 0.8° per orbit drift in the orbital plane’s inclination. Given that the Earth’s J₂ perturbation causes a rate of change of argument of periapsis of 2.0° per day and the orbit’s nodal regression is 0.7° per day, determine whether the observed inclination drift is primarily due to J₂, a residual thruster torque, or a combination of both, and quantify the required impulsive correction to restore the original orbit plane after 30 days if the anomaly persists.

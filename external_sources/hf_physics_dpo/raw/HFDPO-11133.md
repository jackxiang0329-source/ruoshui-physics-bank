# HFDPO-11133

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a 120-meter-wide, vertically oriented superconducting loop buried 800 m below the surface to harvest energy from naturally occurring geomagnetic field fluctuations caused by solar wind interactions. If the loop’s inductance is negligible and the superconducting state ensures zero resistance, calculate the average power that could be extracted over one cycle when the loop is optimally coupled to a rectifier circuit with 92% efficiency. Assume the loop is perfectly aligned with the field’s changing component.

# HFDPO-08684

During a cold winter night, a drone delivering medical supplies to a remote Arctic outpost develops a critical issue: its lithium-ion battery pack is nearing the freezing point of its electrolyte (−20°C), risking irreversible solidification and permanent failure. The battery pack has a mass of 0.8 kg and a specific heat capacity of 800 J/kg·K, but the phase transition from liquid to solid electrolyte begins at −20°C and releases 210 kJ/kg upon freezing. If the battery is initially at −25°C and the ambient temperature remains at −30°C, how long must the heater run continuously to safely raise the battery temperature to −15°C, avoiding solidification?

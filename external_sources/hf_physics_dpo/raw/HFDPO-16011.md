# HFDPO-16011

A wind turbine blade, 60 m long and modeled as a thin rod rotating about one end, spins at 15 rpm during a storm. Suddenly, a gust causes a torque of 3.2 × 10⁶ N·m to act opposite the rotation for 1.2 seconds. If the blade’s moment of inertia is 1.8 × 10⁷ kg·m², what is its new angular speed, and will the turbine survive the stress if exceeding 18 rpm causes mechanical failure?

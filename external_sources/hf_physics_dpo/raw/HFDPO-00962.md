# HFDPO-00962

A quantum dot array in a next-generation solar cell operates at 300 K, with 1200 electrons confined in a 2D lattice of 10000 sites. Due to disorder, the energy levels are distributed log-normally with a mean of 0.8 eV and standard deviation 0.2 eV. Under irradiation, photons with energies ranging from 0.7 eV to 1.2 eV are absorbed, but electron-hole pair recombination is non-radiative and governed by multiphonon emission. Estimate the equilibrium electron occupation number at the highest occupied energy level, and evaluate whether the system is in the high-temperature limit for electron statistics, explaining how disorder affects the validity of the Fermi-Dirac approximation.

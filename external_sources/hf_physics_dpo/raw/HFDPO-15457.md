# HFDPO-15457

A drone designer is selecting a material for the landing legs of a new delivery drone. The legs must compress 2 cm when the drone lands at 3 m/s from a 1.5 m height to protect its electronics. If the drone weighs 1.2 kg, what minimum Young’s modulus must the leg material have if each leg has a cross-sectional area of 0.5 cm² and a length of 8 cm before compression?

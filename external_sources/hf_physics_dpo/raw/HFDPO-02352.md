# HFDPO-02352

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate cancer cells from blood based on size differences, using a Y-shaped channel where red blood cells (approx. 7 μm diameter) and tumor cells (15 μm) flow in a 1:100 volume ratio. The fluid is plasma (μ = 1.2 cP, ρ = 1025 kg/m³). The channel has a 500 μm width and 100 μm height, and the applied pressure drop is 120 Pa over a 2 mm length. What is the ratio of the average flow speeds between the two cell types if the flow is laminar and viscous forces dominate?

# HFDPO-01783

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses hot water from a deep well (200°C) to drive a turbine, but the efficiency drops significantly when the fluid cools too much. Engineers must determine the minimum flow rate of water needed to maintain turbine output while minimizing energy loss due to heat dissipation. The water exits the turbine at 120°C, the turbine operates at 75% efficiency, and the system must generate 4.5 MW of electrical power. Assume 20% of the thermal energy is lost to the environment during transport and that the specific heat of water is 4180 J/kg·K. What is the required mass flow rate to meet the power demand?

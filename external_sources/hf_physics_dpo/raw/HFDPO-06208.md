# HFDPO-06208

During a deep-sea mining operation, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) retrieves a cylindrical sample container filled with sediment from 2,500 m below the surface. The container has a diameter of 0.6 m, height of 1.2 m, and is sealed at the top. As the ROV ascends, the pressure decreases, but the sealed container traps the high-pressure water inside. At depth, the internal pressure equals external pressure. On the surface, the internal pressure remains at ~25 MPa due to the seal. What is the net inward force on the top circular end of the container when it reaches the surface?

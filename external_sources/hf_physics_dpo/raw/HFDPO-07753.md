# HFDPO-07753

In a semiconductor fabrication plant, a charged microchip (mass 0.50 g, charge +12 nC) is suspended between two horizontal parallel plates separated by 4.0 cm. The electric field between the plates is non-uniform due to edge effects, measured as 3.0 × 10⁴ N/C near the center but dropping to 2.4 × 10⁴ N/C at the edges. If the chip drifts 1.5 cm toward one edge, what is the net force on it, and will it remain in equilibrium or accelerate? Assume gravity is the only other force acting.

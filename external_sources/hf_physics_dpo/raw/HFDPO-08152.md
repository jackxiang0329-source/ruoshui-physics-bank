# HFDPO-08152

A marathon runner’s performance is being analyzed after a race on a wet asphalt track. The runner’s shoe sole has a coefficient of kinetic friction of 0.65 with dry asphalt, but this drops to 0.35 when wet. During a sudden downpour, the runner maintains a steady pace of 5.2 m/s, but the increased drag from rainwater accumulating on the track reduces their effective traction. If the runner's mass is 68 kg and air resistance is modeled as proportional to the square of their speed (drag coefficient 0.4 kg/m), at what minimum speed must they slow down to avoid slipping while turning at a radius of 12 m?

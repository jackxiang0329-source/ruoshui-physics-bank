# HFDPO-01204

A satellite in low Earth orbit carries a highly precise atomic clock synchronized with ground-based clocks. After one year, mission control notices the satellite clock has gained 45 microseconds relative to Earth’s clocks. What is the dominant relativistic effect causing this discrepancy, and how can you estimate the magnitude of time dilation due to gravity alone, assuming the satellite orbits at 600 km altitude?

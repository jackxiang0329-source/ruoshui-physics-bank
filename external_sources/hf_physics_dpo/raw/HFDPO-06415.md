# HFDPO-06415

A prototype fusion reactor uses a toroidal plasma confined by magnetic fields, where deuterium and tritium nuclei collide at high speeds to produce helium and a neutron. During a 5-second pulse, 1.2 × 10¹⁹ fusion reactions occur, each releasing 17.6 MeV of energy. However, the reactor’s energy output sensor registers only 92% of the expected energy, and plasma instabilities cause localized energy bursts. Propose a quantitative explanation for the missing energy, considering conservation of energy, momentum, and particle number in the plasma. Is the system truly violating conservation laws, or is the measurement inconsistent with a full accounting of all energy forms?

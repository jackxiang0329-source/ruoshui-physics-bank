# HFDPO-10440

During a lunar excavation mission, a robotic arm with a 3.2 m telescoping boom (mass = 45 kg, center of mass at midpoint) must lift a 78 kg regolith bucket from a crater wall. The arm is pinned at the base and supported by a hydraulic strut angled at 37° from horizontal, attached 1.8 m up the boom. The bucket’s center of mass is 2.6 m from the pivot, and the boom is tilted at 55° above horizontal. The team observes oscillations in the strut—could the system be stable under this configuration? Determine the minimum strut tension required to prevent rotation and assess whether the strut could fail under tensile stress if its yield strength is 120 kN.

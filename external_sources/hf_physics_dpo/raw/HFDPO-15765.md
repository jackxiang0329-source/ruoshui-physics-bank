# HFDPO-15765

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial photovoltaic panels mounted at a 30° tilt, facing south. Reflections from the sandy ground create glare that reduces efficiency by up to 8% due to partially polarized light. Using a polarizing filter with a transmission axis at 45° relative to the surface, engineers aim to block the dominant polarization component of the reflected light. If the ground reflection produces light with a degree of polarization of 0.65, what is the percentage reduction in reflected irradiance reaching the panel’s front surface when the filter is applied? Assume the incident solar irradiance is 1000 W/m² and the reflection is from a non-metallic surface.

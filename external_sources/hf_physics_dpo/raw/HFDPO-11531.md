# HFDPO-11531

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a toroidal magnetic field to confine a plasma of deuterium and tritium at 150 million K. During a brief instability, 0.15% of the plasma’s kinetic energy is abruptly converted into electromagnetic radiation over 2.3 microseconds. If the initial total kinetic energy of the plasma is 4.8 GJ and the radiation carries away momentum, what is the recoil velocity of the 1200-ton vessel? Assume no external forces act during the emission.

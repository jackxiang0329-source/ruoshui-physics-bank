# HFDPO-16201

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanocrystals (2.5 nm diameter) embedded in a polymer matrix to harvest sunlight. The bandgap of the dots shifts due to quantum confinement, enabling absorption of visible light that would otherwise pass through conventional silicon cells. If the effective mass of an electron in the dot is 0.08 times the free electron mass, and the dot’s diameter is 2.5 nm, what is the minimum photon energy (in eV) needed to excite an electron from the ground state to the first excited state?

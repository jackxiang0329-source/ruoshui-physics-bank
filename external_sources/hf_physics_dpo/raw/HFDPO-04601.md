# HFDPO-04601

A private satellite operator must adjust the orbit of a communications satellite currently in a 320 km circular low Earth orbit to avoid a newly cataloged piece of space debris on a 340 km circular path with identical inclination. The debris passes near the satellite’s orbit once every 90 minutes. The operator has only one short propulsion window during a 5-minute pass when the satellite is aligned with the debris’s orbital plane. What is the minimum Δv required to perform a plane change combined with a phasing maneuver, assuming the satellite can only fire its engines at orbital nodes and the maneuver must complete before the debris passes?

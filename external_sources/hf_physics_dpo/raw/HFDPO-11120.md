# HFDPO-11120

A research team is designing a new electrostatic shield for a sensitive quantum sensor housed inside a cylindrical enclosure made of conductive material. The sensor generates a uniform charge density of 5 nC/m³ within a central core of radius 2 cm, while the shield wall is 1 cm thick and grounded. To ensure no electric field penetrates the shield, the team must verify that the flux through an imaginary Gaussian surface just outside the inner core matches the enclosed charge. What is the magnitude of the electric field at a radial distance of 2.5 cm from the central axis, and how does this confirm the effectiveness of the shield?

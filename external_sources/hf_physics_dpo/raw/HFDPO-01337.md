# HFDPO-01337

A coastal city is designing a new seawall to protect against storm surge, but engineers notice that during high tides, water surges through a narrow channel between two breakwaters, creating a dangerous backflow that erodes the shore. The channel is 4 meters wide, 2 meters deep, and 50 meters long, with a sudden contraction downstream. During peak surge, water enters at 2.5 m/s and rises 1.2 meters above the channel floor. What is the pressure difference between the upstream and downstream ends of the channel, and how does the flow rate change if the contraction reduces the cross-sectional area by 30%?

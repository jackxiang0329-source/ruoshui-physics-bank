# HFDPO-14452

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric research must maintain stable hover above a remote glacier. The drone’s frame is asymmetric, with solar arrays mounted at unequal distances from the center of mass, and wind gusts of up to 12 m/s exert turbulent forces. To prevent tipping, engineers must balance torques from both aerodynamic lift and gravitational forces. The center of mass shifts by 0.3 m due to fuel consumption over a 4-hour flight. Determine the minimum reaction force at the front landing gear needed to prevent rotation about the rear wheels, assuming the drone’s total weight is 180 N and the wind force acts 0.8 m above the center of mass, perpendicular to the drone’s longitudinal axis.

# HFDPO-11252

A fusion reactor's first wall is shielded by a 2 cm-thick tungsten layer backed by a liquid lithium coolant loop. During a transient heat pulse, the wall absorbs 1.8 MJ/m² of energy in 10 ms. Given tungsten’s thermal conductivity (173 W/m·K), density (19,300 kg/m³), specific heat (134 J/kg·K), and a minimum safe temperature limit of 1,800 K, determine whether the peak temperature at the rear surface of the tungsten exceeds allowable limits. Assume perfect insulation on the coolant side and an initial uniform temperature of 300 K.

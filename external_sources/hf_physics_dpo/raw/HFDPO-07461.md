# HFDPO-07461

In a microfluidic bioreactor designed to mimic capillary blood flow, red blood cells (RBCs) pass through a 50 μm-wide channel at a mean velocity of 1.2 mm/s. When the flow rate increases, the system exhibits sudden flow instabilities—RBCs form transient clusters that obstruct flow and reduce oxygen delivery efficiency. Estimate the critical shear rate at which viscous stresses overcome the suspension’s yield-like resistance, triggering cluster formation.

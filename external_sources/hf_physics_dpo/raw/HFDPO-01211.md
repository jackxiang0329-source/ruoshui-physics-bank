# HFDPO-01211

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer accumulates a non-uniform surface charge due to triboelectric charging during robotic handling. Measurements show a charge density of 2.5 × 10⁻⁸ C/m² near the edges and 8.0 × 10⁻⁹ C/m² at the center, with a linear gradient between them. A nearby charged microelectrode (−1.2 × 10⁻⁸ C, 50 μm diameter) is positioned 200 μm above the wafer’s edge. Determine the net electrostatic force on the electrode and explain how the spatial variation in charge density affects device yield during lithography.

# HFDPO-02897

A deep-sea submersible’s forward-facing lens system, designed to capture high-resolution images at 1,200 meters depth, develops a tiny air bubble trapped between two glass elements. A distant object emits a narrow beam of light at a 15° angle relative to the optical axis. The system’s primary lens has a focal length of 80 mm in water. What is the effective angular deviation of the beam after passing through the bubble, and how much does this degrade image sharpness compared to a defect-free system?

# HFDPO-00861

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to monitor the impact of a newly installed offshore wind farm. The turbines generate low-frequency sound waves at 15 Hz, which propagate through water (v = 1500 m/s). At a monitoring station 8.5 km from the nearest turbine, the measured intensity is 2.3 × 10⁻⁴ W/m². However, the data shows a 23% fluctuation in intensity over time, even with constant turbine output. Evaluate whether these fluctuations are due to constructive and destructive interference from multiple coherent wave paths caused by water temperature gradients bending sound rays, or if another factor—like measurement noise or non-uniform source emission—is more likely. Use the wave properties of interference and refraction to justify your conclusion.

# HFDPO-14013

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible stent made of nitinol alloy to be deployed in a coronary artery narrowed by 60% due to plaque buildup. The stent must expand radially by 3 mm to restore normal blood flow while ensuring the material stays within its elastic limit. Given the stent’s initial radius is 3.5 mm, its wall thickness is 0.2 mm, and the Young’s modulus of nitinol is 60 GPa, what is the minimum length of the stent needed to ensure that the hoop stress during expansion does not exceed 400 MPa?

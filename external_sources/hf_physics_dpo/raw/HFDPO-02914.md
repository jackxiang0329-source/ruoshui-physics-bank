# HFDPO-02914

A biomedical engineer is designing a wireless power transfer system for implantable cardiac monitors, operating at 13.56 MHz. The system uses a resonant inductive coupling between a transmitter coil (L₁ = 25 µH, R₁ = 0.8 Ω) and a receiver coil (L₂ = 18 µH, R₂ = 1.2 Ω), with mutual inductance M = 6.2 µH. The transmitter is driven by a 12 V RMS AC source with variable frequency. The implant must receive at least 2.5 mW of power at 25 mm separation. Determine the optimal operating frequency and the necessary load resistance on the receiver to maximize power transfer efficiency while staying within regulatory EMF limits (max 50 µT field at 5 cm).

# HFDPO-02956

During a deep-space mission to Titan, engineers notice that the methane-based atmospheric probes are experiencing unexpected thermal gradients despite identical external conditions. The probe’s internal pressure sensors show a 12% deviation from the ideal gas law prediction at 95 K and 1.5 atm. Given that methane molecules exhibit significant intermolecular forces and finite molecular volume at these conditions, evaluate whether the deviation arises primarily from excluded volume effects or from attractive intermolecular potentials. Use the van der Waals equation with known parameters (a = 2.303 bar·L²/mol², b = 0.0431 L/mol) and compare to the ideal gas model and a molecular dynamics simulation of 10⁶ methane molecules at the same conditions.

# HFDPO-15713

In a fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma chamber generates a non-uniform electric field due to asymmetric ion density. Engineers observe unexpected charge accumulation on the inner wall near the minor radius apex. Given the radial charge distribution ρ(r) = ρ₀ exp(–r²/2σ²) for r < R, with ρ₀ = 5×10⁻⁶ C/m³, σ = 0.2 m, and R = 1 m, determine the total electric flux through a spherical Gaussian surface of radius 0.8 m centered at the torus’s central axis. How does this flux compare to that through a surface enclosing the full plasma?

# HFDPO-03433

A solar-powered water pump in a remote village uses a 24V panel connected to a network of three parallel pump circuits, each with a variable resistor to adjust flow. One circuit has a known 4Ω resistor and a 2Ω pump motor; another has two 3Ω resistors in series; the third has a 1Ω resistor and a 5Ω resistor in parallel. If the total current from the panel is 8A and the voltage drop across the main junction is negligible, find the current through the 2Ω motor.

# HFDPO-01056

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole drilled 3.2 km into the Earth’s crust, where hot basalt rock maintains a steady 420°C. A superconducting coil with 1,500 turns and a radius of 0.8 m is suspended in a non-conductive fluid within the borehole. As the fluid circulates, thermal convection induces eddy currents in a nearby copper annular shield (resistivity 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, thickness 2 mm) surrounding the coil. The magnetic field from the coil changes at a rate of 0.04 T/s due to fluctuating superconducting current. What is the average power dissipated in the shield per meter of borehole depth, considering only induced eddy currents?

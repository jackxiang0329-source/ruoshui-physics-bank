# HFDPO-09818

A diagnostic sensor measures the electric field at 1.2 m from the center as 1.4 × 10³ N/C radially inward. What must be the charge distribution on the intermediate shell (radius 1.6 m) to satisfy Gauss’s Law and ensure stable field confinement?

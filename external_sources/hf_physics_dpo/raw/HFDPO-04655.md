# HFDPO-04655

A biomedical engineer is designing a non-invasive brain-monitoring device using distributed capacitive sensors placed around a patient’s scalp. The sensors detect small electric fields generated by neuronal activity, but they’re plagued by interference from residual charge buildup on the device casing. What is the magnitude of the electric field 10 cm from the center of the sphere, and how does this affect sensor readings in the space between the casing and the scalp (located 12 cm from the center)?

# HFDPO-03625

In a remote mountain village, engineers are designing a suspension bridge using recycled steel cables originally from a decommissioned mine hoist. Each cable has a cross-sectional area of 0.002 m² and is 150 m long. During winter, temperature drops from 20°C to –10°C, causing thermal contraction. The bridge deck is anchored rigidly at both ends. If the yield strength of the steel is 400 MPa and the Young’s modulus is 200 GPa, what is the maximum temperature drop that could occur before the cables reach yield stress due to thermal contraction?

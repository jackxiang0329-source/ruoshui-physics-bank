# HFDPO-11536

A lunar rover on the far side of the Moon encounters a sudden, steep 15° incline covered in fine regolith with a coefficient of static friction μ_s = 0.25 and dynamic friction μ_k = 0.18. The rover’s suspension system is designed to maintain wheel contact, but a sensor glitch causes one rear wheel to lose traction during ascent. Assume the wheelbase is 1.8 m and the center of mass is initially 0.6 m above the ground.

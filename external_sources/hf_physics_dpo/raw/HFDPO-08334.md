# HFDPO-08334

A Martian terraforming research station uses a network of solar-powered microgrids to maintain life support systems across three interconnected habitat domes. Each dome has variable power demands due to fluctuating atmospheric conditions—Dome A requires 120 W at 15 V, Dome B 90 W at 15 V, and Dome C 140 W at 15 V—connected via a shared 1.5 Ω distribution line from a central inverter. The inverter outputs 18 V, but voltage drops along the lines create inefficiencies. Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the current through each segment of the loop and the actual voltage delivered to Dome C, which is failing to meet its power requirement.

# HFDPO-05313

The dots, made of CdSe with a tunable bandgap of 1.7 eV, show strong photoluminescence at room temperature but fail to sustain charge separation below 100 mW/m². Engineers suspect carrier–carrier interactions are mediating excess recombination, but the dominant mechanism remains unclear—could it be electron–hole Auger recombination or trapped carriers from surface defect states with energies near the band edges?

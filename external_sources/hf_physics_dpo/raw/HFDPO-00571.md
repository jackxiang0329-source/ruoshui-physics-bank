# HFDPO-00571

A small medical device uses a superconducting magnet cooled by liquid helium to generate a stable 1.5 T magnetic field for MRI imaging. During a routine maintenance check, the cooling system loses 15% of its helium due to a leak. Without intervention, the magnet’s temperature rises above its critical temperature of 7.2 K. The device’s safety protocol shuts down the current to prevent damage. If the original current was 120 A and the magnetic field is proportional to the current, what is the new magnetic field strength once the current is safely quenched?

# HFDPO-15486

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat from concentrated sunlight, aiming to generate electricity even after sunset. The salt is heated to 560°C and stored in an insulated tank. During a cold desert night (ambient temperature 10°C), the salt cools to 480°C over 6 hours. Engineers estimate the tank loses 1.2 MW of heat due to imperfect insulation. Is this energy loss consistent with the Second Law of Thermodynamics when considering entropy generation in the system and surroundings?

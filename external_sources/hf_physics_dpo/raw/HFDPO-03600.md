# HFDPO-03600

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt (specific heat 1400 J/kg·K, density 1800 kg/m³) stored in a cylindrical tank (diameter 10 m, height 15 m) to maintain 500°C for electricity generation during nighttime. After 12 hours of operation, the salt cools to 475°C. The tank is insulated with a 15 cm thick layer of silica aerogel (k = 0.015 W/m·K), but thermal bridging through support struts reduces insulation effectiveness by 30%. Estimate the total energy lost to the environment and assess whether the current insulation is sufficient to meet a 24-hour thermal hold requirement without supplemental heating.

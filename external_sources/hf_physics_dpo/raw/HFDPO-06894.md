# HFDPO-06894

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using a narrow channel with a 50 μm height. Blood flows at 1.2 mm/s, and the device must ensure that CTCs (≈15 μm diameter) experience a significant difference in drag compared to red blood cells (≈7 μm) due to their size difference. Given blood’s viscosity is 3.5 mPa·s and density 1060 kg/m³, estimate the ratio of viscous drag forces on a CTC to that on a red blood cell at steady flow. Assume spherical particles and low Reynolds number.

# HFDPO-04345

A quantum dot solar cell uses self-assembled InAs dots embedded in GaAs, each dot ≈ 5 nm in diameter, to capture sub-bandgap photons. At 300 K, the density of states near the conduction band edge exhibits a van Hove singularity due to 2D confinement. If the system’s Fermi energy lies 0.15 eV below the 2D subband edge and the effective mass of electrons is 0.04mₑ, what is the electron density in the dots assuming a parabolic dispersion? Use this to estimate the photocurrent gain per dot under 100 W/m² of AM1.5G sunlight, assuming a 60% absorption efficiency in the dot layer and a lifetime of 100 ps for photoexcited carriers.

# HFDPO-13001

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a 12-meter-long, non-uniform steel arm to suspend a 5-ton drill head. The arm’s center of mass is located 4.2 m from the pivot, and its weight distribution follows a quadratic profile (w(x) = w₀(1 + 0.1x²), where x is distance from the pivot in meters). The arm is supported by a cable anchored 8 m vertically above the pivot and 10 m horizontally from it. Due to thermal expansion, the pivot experiences a 3° clockwise tilt. Determine the minimum tension required in the cable to prevent rotation, considering both the cable’s initial sag and the altered torque distribution.

# HFDPO-14210

A new-generation quantum imaging sensor uses a 650 nm laser to detect single-photon interference patterns from a biological sample embedded in a microfluidic channel. The channel is flanked by two subwavelength grating structures (period 200 nm) that act as phase-modulating waveguides. Due to thermal drift, the effective path length between the grating and detector varies by ±15 nm per hour. To maintain coherence and enable quantum-enhanced resolution, the system must correct for phase drift in real time. What is the minimum update frequency required to maintain interference visibility above 90% when the coherence time of the laser is 100 ps?

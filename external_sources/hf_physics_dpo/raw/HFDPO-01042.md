# HFDPO-01042

A solar-powered streetlight in a remote village uses a 12 V battery and a single LED that draws 0.25 A. After a week, the light dims noticeably even though the solar panel still charges the battery fully during the day. The technician finds that the wiring between the battery and the LED has developed corrosion, increasing its resistance. If the LED still requires 0.25 A to operate at full brightness, what is the new resistance of the corroded wire if the battery voltage drops to 11.5 V across the LED and wire combined?

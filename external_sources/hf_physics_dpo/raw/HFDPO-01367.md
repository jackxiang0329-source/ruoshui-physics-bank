# HFDPO-01367

A solar-powered drone designed for environmental monitoring takes off from a high-altitude research station in the Andes. Before launch, the drone’s battery stores 12,000 joules of electrical energy. During its 30-minute mission, it converts this energy into kinetic energy for flight and gravitational potential energy as it climbs to 1,200 meters. If the drone has a mass of 3.0 kg and no energy is lost to heat or sound, how fast is it moving when it reaches its peak altitude?

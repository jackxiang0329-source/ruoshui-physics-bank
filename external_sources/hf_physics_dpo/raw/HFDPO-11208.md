# HFDPO-11208

A lunar habitat module, shaped like a truncated cone (base radius 4.2 m, top radius 3.0 m, height 5.5 m), is anchored to the regolith via three symmetrically placed struts at the base. During a solar storm, electromagnetic forces induce a net upward lift of 28 kN across the module’s surface—modeled as a uniform pressure of 0.85 kPa acting on the lateral surface. The struts each have a maximum compressive strength of 120 kN and must remain within a 30° inclination from vertical to prevent buckling. Can the struts safely resist the net force without exceeding stress limits or instability thresholds? Determine the minimum required strut cross-sectional area if the material has a yield strength of 820 MPa and a safety factor of 2.5.

# HFDPO-12068

A solar-powered irrigation system in a remote village uses a 12 V battery bank to run a submersible pump through 50 meters of copper wire. The pump requires 2.4 A at peak operation but the villagers notice water flow drops significantly after sunset when the battery voltage sags to 10.8 V. They suspect voltage drop in the wiring. If the wire gauge is 10 AWG (cross-sectional area ≈ 5.26 mm²), what is the actual power delivered to the pump at 10.8 V, and by what percent is this less than the ideal power at 12 V?

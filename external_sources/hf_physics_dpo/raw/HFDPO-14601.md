# HFDPO-14601

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma, but stray electric fields from asymmetric charge distributions in the plasma edge disrupt stability. Engineers observe a non-uniform electric field pattern along the inner wall, with radial field components measured at 120 kN/C near the inner radius and decaying to 45 kN/C at the outer radius. The vessel’s cross-section is a 1.2 m × 1.2 m square with a central hole of radius 0.8 m. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the net charge per meter confined within a 2 m length of the toroidal chamber, assuming cylindrical symmetry in the radial direction and no external fields.

# HFDPO-10679

A wildlife biologist uses a polarized camera filter to reduce glare while photographing a rare bird nesting on a reflective lake surface. The lake’s surface reflects 80% of incident sunlight at the bird’s viewing angle. If the camera’s polarizing filter is rotated to minimize reflected light, what angle should it be set relative to the vertical, and what percentage of the original sunlight intensity reaches the camera sensor when the filter is optimally aligned? Assume the incident sunlight is unpolarized and the reflection follows Brewster’s law.

# HFDPO-07080

During a power outage, a researcher in a remote Antarctic field station must use a hand-crank generator to run a small heater for a sensitive biological sample stored at 4°C. The sample container has a thermal resistance of 0.25 K/W and is initially at -10°C. The heater provides 80 W of power, but due to poor insulation, heat leaks to the outside air at -30°C at a rate proportional to the temperature difference. How long will it take to warm the sample to 0°C if the system reaches steady-state heating?

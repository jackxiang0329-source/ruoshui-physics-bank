# HFDPO-00312

A marine biologist is using passive acoustic monitoring to track humpback whale song patterns across the Pacific. The whales emit low-frequency pulses (around 20 Hz) that travel through seawater at 1500 m/s. During a recent deployment, two hydrophones spaced 3.5 km apart recorded the same vocalization with a time delay of 2.4 seconds. The biologist suspects the whale was moving relative to the hydrophones. What is the whale’s speed and direction relative to the line connecting the two hydrophones, assuming sound travels in straight lines and the whale’s position was directly between them at the time of emission?

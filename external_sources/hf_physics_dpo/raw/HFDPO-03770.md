# HFDPO-03770

In a deep-space quantum communication experiment, a laser signal is sent from a satellite moving at 0.8c relative to Earth toward a receiver on a planet orbiting Proxima Centauri. The signal is encoded with a 100-nanosecond pulse train at 1 GHz. Due to relativistic time dilation and the Doppler shift, the receiver detects a pulse duration of 140 ns and frequency of 625 MHz. Calculate the Lorentz factor of the satellite, and determine whether the receiver’s clock is synchronized with Earth’s to within 10 ns.

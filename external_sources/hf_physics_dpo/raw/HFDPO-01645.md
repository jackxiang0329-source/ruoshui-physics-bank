# HFDPO-01645

A solar thermal power plant uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a fluid-filled tube, heating it to 400°C. If the tube has a length of 10 m, diameter of 5 cm, and is made of aluminum (k = 205 W/m·K), with a 1 cm thick insulation layer (k = 0.04 W/m·K) surrounding it, and ambient air is at 25°C with a convection coefficient of 15 W/m²·K, what is the minimum solar flux (W/m²) needed to sustain the 400°C temperature at steady state? Assume negligible radiation and uniform temperature in the fluid.

# HFDPO-00769

During a dust storm in the Australian Outback, a solar farm operator notices rapid accumulation of charged sand particles on photovoltaic panels, reducing efficiency by 30% within hours. Each panel has a surface area of 2.4 m² and is grounded through a 1.2 MΩ resistor. If sand grains acquire a charge of +1.6 × 10⁻¹⁴ C each and strike the panel at 500 grains per square meter per second, what is the rate of voltage buildup across the panel relative to ground before charge dissipates? Assume the panel behaves like a parallel-plate capacitor with 1 mm air gap.

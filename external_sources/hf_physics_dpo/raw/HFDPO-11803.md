# HFDPO-11803

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a precision microelectromechanical system (MEMS) switch fails during operation due to electrostatic adhesion—stiction—between a 200 nm thick silicon nitride beam and a substrate, despite being designed to remain separated. The beam has a uniform surface charge density of +5.0 × 10⁻⁸ C/m², while the substrate carries an induced opposite charge. Calculate the minimum gap distance at which the electrostatic attraction becomes strong enough to overcome the beam’s mechanical restoring force (stiffness = 1.2 × 10⁻³ N/m), assuming the beam is 50 μm long and 5 μm wide. Use both Coulomb’s law and energy minimization to determine stability.

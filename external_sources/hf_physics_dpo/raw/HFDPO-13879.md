# HFDPO-13879

During a deep-sea hydrothermal vent survey, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, porous rock formation emitting superheated fluid at 350°C into the surrounding ocean at 2°C. The vent effluent has a density of 920 kg/m³, while seawater is 1025 kg/m³. The ROV's pressure sensor detects a 1.2 atm pressure difference between the vent interior and the ambient ocean at a depth of 2400 m. What is the minimum vertical height the superheated fluid must rise within the porous rock before exiting to prevent it from being completely submerged and quenched by the colder seawater?

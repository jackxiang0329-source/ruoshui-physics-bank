# HFDPO-06051

In a quantum computing experiment, a superconducting transmon qubit is coupled to a readout resonator via a shared capacitor. The qubit’s energy levels shift when exposed to stray electric fields from nearby circuitry. To mitigate this, engineers design a hybrid shielding structure using two concentric grounded copper shells separated by a ceramic dielectric (ε_r = 4.5, thickness = 15 μm). The inner shell is biased at 10 mV relative to ground, and the outer shell is grounded. What effective capacitance per unit area (in fF/μm²) must the shielding system provide to maintain qubit coherence for more than 100 μs, assuming dielectric losses and field leakage are the dominant decoherence mechanisms?

# HFDPO-04036

The boom is pinned at the pole and supported by a cable angled at 35° above horizontal, anchored 2.4 m above the boom’s pivot. The cable’s attachment point is 1.8 m horizontally from the pivot. To prevent structural stress, the horizontal reaction at the pivot must not exceed 180 N. Is the cable configuration safe under this load?

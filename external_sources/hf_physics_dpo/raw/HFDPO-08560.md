# HFDPO-08560

A solar panel array on a remote weather station is accumulating dust during a desert sandstorm. Engineers notice that the panels are generating less power than expected. They suspect the dust is creating a static electric charge that repels further dust buildup, reducing efficiency. If the dust layer can be modeled as a uniform spherical shell of charge with radius 2.0 cm and total charge +5.0 nC, what is the electric field magnitude at a point 1.5 cm from the center of the shell?

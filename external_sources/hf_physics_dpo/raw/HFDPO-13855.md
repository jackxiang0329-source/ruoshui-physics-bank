# HFDPO-13855

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses bifacial photovoltaic panels mounted at a 30° tilt to capture sunlight reflected from the sandy ground. During peak irradiance, the albedo from the desert surface is measured at 0.35, but the actual power output is 12% lower than predicted by radiative models. Field measurements show that the surface reflection is highly polarized, with the reflected light predominantly polarized horizontally (s-polarized). Determine the effective polarization-dependent absorption coefficient of the panel’s rear surface, assuming the panel’s front and rear efficiencies are otherwise identical and the incident solar spectrum is unpolarized.

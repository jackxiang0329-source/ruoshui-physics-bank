# HFDPO-05376

A quantum sensor in a medical imaging device detects a weak magnetic field from a patient’s brain activity, but the signal is drowned out by thermal noise. The sensor uses nitrogen-vacancy centers in diamond, each of which can exist in two spin states: |↑⟩ (aligned with the field) and |↓⟩ (opposed). At room temperature, the energy difference between these states is 2.8 meV. What is the probability that the sensor’s spin is in the |↓⟩ state when exposed to a 1.2 T magnetic field?

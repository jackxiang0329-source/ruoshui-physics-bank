# HFDPO-12020

In a remote solar-powered research station in the Antarctic, a critical sensor array fails during a 36-hour polar night. The station’s backup system uses a network of five identical 12 V lithium-ion batteries connected in a complex looped configuration with three junctions and four resistive loads (including a 15 Ω heater, a 30 Ω data transmitter, and two 10 Ω power distribution nodes). Using Kirchhoff’s Laws, determine the internal resistance of each battery and verify if the system can sustain the load for 72 hours without degradation.

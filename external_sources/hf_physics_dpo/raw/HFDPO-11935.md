# HFDPO-11935

A wind turbine blade, 80 meters long and modeled as a slender, non-uniform cantilever beam, experiences a 30° incline in its rotational plane due to uneven ice accumulation on the leading edge. The ice adds a distributed weight of 150 N/m over the first 20 meters from the root, while the blade’s own mass is 40 kg/m. The hub applies a moment of 1.2 MN·m at the root to counteract the bending. Determine the minimum tensile stress in the root’s reinforcing fibers (cross-sectional area: 0.015 m², elastic modulus: 200 GPa) considering both the bending due to self-weight and ice load.

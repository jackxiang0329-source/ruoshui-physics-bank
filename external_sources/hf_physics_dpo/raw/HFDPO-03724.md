# HFDPO-03724

A solar farm in the Mojave Desert uses dual-axis tracking panels optimized for maximum irradiance, but engineers notice a 12% drop in output during midday—coinciding with peak glare from sand and glass. The panels are coated with anti-reflective layers, yet polarized light from specular reflections off the sand and panel glass interferes with photovoltaic efficiency. Given that the sand surface has a refractive index of 1.54 and the panels operate best under unpolarized sunlight, what polarization state and orientation of a thin-film polarizing filter (placed in front of the panels) would most effectively suppress glare while minimizing energy loss? Assume sunlight is incident at 60° from the horizontal, and the Brewster angle for sand is 57°.

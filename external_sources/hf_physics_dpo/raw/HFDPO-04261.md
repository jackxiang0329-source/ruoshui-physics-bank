# HFDPO-04261

A lunar mining outpost on the Moon’s south pole relies on a passive thermal storage system to maintain critical equipment above 150 K during the 14-Earth-day-long night. The system uses a 200 kg block of regolith-simulant composite with a temperature-dependent specific heat capacity: c(T) = 850 + 0.3T J/kg·K (T in K). During the day, solar flux heats the block to 300 K. Assuming negligible heat loss during the day and a constant thermal conductance of 0.05 W/K to ambient, what is the minimum initial temperature the block must reach at dusk to ensure all equipment remains above 150 K until sunrise?

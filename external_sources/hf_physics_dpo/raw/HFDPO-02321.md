# HFDPO-02321

A fusion reactor's inner wall is lined with a complex, non-uniform dielectric composite designed to withstand extreme plasma pressures. During a test run, sensors detect an unexpected electric field of 1.2 × 10⁶ V/m radially inward at a point 30 cm from the reactor’s central axis—just inside the dielectric layer. The system is symmetric, and the plasma emits a total charge of 4.5 C per meter of cylindrical length. Using Gauss’s Law, determine the net bound charge per meter on the inner surface of the dielectric, given that the dielectric has a relative permittivity of 4.2 and the system is in electrostatic equilibrium.

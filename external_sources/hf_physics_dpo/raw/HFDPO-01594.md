# HFDPO-01594

A lunar terraforming team is deploying a network of buried resistive heating cables beneath the Moon’s regolith to gradually warm subsurface ice deposits for water extraction. The cables, made of a temperature-stable alloy with resistivity ρ = 8.0 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, are laid in a spiral pattern, each 100 m long and 2.0 mm in diameter. If the system operates at 120 V and must deliver 1.5 kW of total heating power across 10 parallel cable loops while minimizing energy loss in connecting wiring (resistance ≤ 0.1 Ω), what is the minimum required voltage at the power source, and how does the temperature rise in the cable affect its resistance and overall efficiency?

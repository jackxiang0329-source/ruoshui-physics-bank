# HFDPO-08875

A satellite in low Earth orbit detects anomalous electric field fluctuations near a dense cluster of space debris. The debris field is modeled as a spherical shell of radius 10 km with non-uniform charge density ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 – r/R), where r is radial distance from the center and R = 10 km. The satellite measures a net electric field of 2.8 × 10⁴ N/C at a distance of 12 km from the center. Determine the total charge on the debris shell, assuming ρ₀ is constant.

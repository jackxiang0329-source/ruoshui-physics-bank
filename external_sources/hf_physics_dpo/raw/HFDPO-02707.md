# HFDPO-02707

Due to thruster degradation, the available Δv is limited to 1.8 m/s, and the maneuver must be completed within 3.5 days. Can the operator achieve the required westward shift using a single tangential burn and a subsequent orbital circularization burn? Justify your answer by comparing the required Δv with the available budget, considering the necessary change in orbital plane inclination and the effects of orbital perturbations.

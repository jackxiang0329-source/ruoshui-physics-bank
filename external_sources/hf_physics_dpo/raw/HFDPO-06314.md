# HFDPO-06314

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic uses a single 12 V photovoltaic panel wired through a 15-meter copper cable to a battery bank. The panel delivers 4.5 A under peak sunlight, but during a cold snap, the system’s voltage drops to 10.8 V at the battery due to resistance in the cable. If the cable’s cross-sectional area is 2.5 mm² and the temperature drops to –20°C, what is the actual resistance of the cable, and how much power is lost as heat in it?

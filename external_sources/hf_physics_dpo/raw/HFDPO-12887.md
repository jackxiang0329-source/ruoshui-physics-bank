# HFDPO-12887

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a robotic submersible encounters unexpected resistance while maneuvering through a hydrothermal vent plume, where warm, mineral-rich fluid jets upward at 0.8 m/s from a 25 cm diameter chimney. To avoid structural stress, engineers must predict the viscous drag force on the submersible’s 0.5 m wide sensor array, assuming laminar flow and no slip at the surface. Estimate the total viscous drag force acting on the array, considering both the shear stress from the base flow and the enhanced viscous dissipation due to stratification.

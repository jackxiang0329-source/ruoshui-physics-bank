# HFDPO-08326

During a high-altitude atmospheric research mission, a balloon-borne sensor pod experiences unexpected lateral drift despite no wind at the measurement altitude. The pod, shaped like a streamlined finned cylinder (diameter 0.3 m, length 1.2 m), is suspended 1.5 m below the balloon by a lightweight tether. At 32,000 m altitude, air density is 0.015 kg/m³ and the pod moves at 25 m/s relative to the air. Engineers suspect a Bernoulli-induced lift force due to pressure differences across the pod’s asymmetrical cross-section. Calculate the magnitude and direction of this lift force assuming the upper surface velocity increases by 12% due to curvature, and determine whether it could account for a 0.8° deviation in trajectory over 10 seconds.

# HFDPO-00934

During a high-altitude research flight, a weather balloon carries a sensor that measures internal pressure and temperature of a sealed chamber filled with nitrogen gas. At 30,000 meters, the chamber’s volume is 1.2 L, temperature is 220 K, and pressure is 18 kPa. The balloon descends rapidly, and upon reaching 10,000 meters, the sensor reports a new pressure of 65 kPa. Assuming the gas behaves ideally and the process is adiabatic, what is the final temperature of the gas, and how does this compare to the initial average kinetic energy per molecule?

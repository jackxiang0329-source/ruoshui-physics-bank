# HFDPO-02835

During a heatwave, a solar-powered desalination plant in coastal Chile struggles to produce freshwater efficiently. The plant uses parabolic troughs to heat saline water to 120°C, generating steam that drives a turbine. However, after sunset, the system cools rapidly, and the turbine stops. Engineers propose storing excess thermal energy in a molten salt reservoir (specific heat = 1.5 kJ/kg·K), initially at 100°C. To maintain night-time operation, they need the salt to remain above 85°C for 6 hours. If 500 kg of salt is used and the plant loses heat at 2.4 kW due to insulation losses, what minimum initial temperature must the salt reach to sustain operation? Assume no phase changes and ideal heat transfer.

# HFDPO-03216

During a deep-space mission, a spacecraft’s thermal regulator uses a phase-change material (PCM) stored in a sealed cylindrical chamber to stabilize cabin temperature. The PCM melts at 45°C with a latent heat of fusion of 180 kJ/kg and has a density of 920 kg/m³ in solid form and 850 kg/m³ in liquid. The chamber is insulated but must accommodate volume expansion during melting. If the PCM initially fills 70% of a 0.8 m long, 0.2 m diameter chamber and the system absorbs 12.5 kJ of excess heat from electronics, what fraction of the PCM melts, and will the chamber rupture if its maximum volume tolerance is ±5%?

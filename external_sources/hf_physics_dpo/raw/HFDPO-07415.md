# HFDPO-07415

A marine biologist is using underwater hydrophones to monitor whale communication in the Pacific Ocean, where sound travels at 1500 m/s. A humpback whale emits a low-frequency pulse (100 Hz) near a cliff face 2.3 km away. The hydrophone detects the original pulse and four distinct echoes arriving at 1.8 s, 2.5 s, 3.1 s, and 3.9 s after emission. What physical mechanisms explain the timing and intensity differences among these echoes, and how can the biologist estimate the cliff’s structural features using this data?

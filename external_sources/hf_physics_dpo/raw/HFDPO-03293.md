# HFDPO-03293

A deep-sea research submersible descends into the Mariana Trench, encountering a vertical stratified fluid layer: seawater (ρ = 1025 kg/m³) above a dense brine layer (ρ = 1120 kg/m³) that forms at the ocean floor due to evaporative concentration. The brine layer is 15 m thick, and the submersible’s pressure hull has a circular hatch with a 0.8 m diameter. As the submersible hovers just above the brine interface, engineers must determine whether the net force on the hatch exceeds the 250,000 N design limit. Assuming hydrostatic equilibrium and neglecting atmospheric pressure, calculate the net force acting on the hatch due to the fluid pressure and evaluate if the hull is at risk.

# HFDPO-07114

If the initial average ion speed is 1.8 × 10⁶ m/s and the process conserves angular momentum, what is the final kinetic energy of the plasma, and how does it compare to the energy needed to vaporize the vessel’s inner wall (2.1 × 10⁷ J)?

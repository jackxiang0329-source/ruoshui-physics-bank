# HFDPO-03087

A geophysicist studying post-glacial rebound in Scandinavia models the Earth’s lithosphere as an elastic plate under a time-varying load from melting ice sheets. The plate, 30 km thick with Young’s modulus 70 GPa and Poisson’s ratio 0.25, is subjected to a 1000 Gt ice load that decays exponentially over 10,000 years. At what depth does the vertical displacement rate peak, and how does this rate compare with GPS-measured uplift rates of ~1 cm/yr? Assume the plate is incompressible and the load is axisymmetric.

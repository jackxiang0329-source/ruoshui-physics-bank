# HFDPO-03323

A city installs a new 500 kg kinetic energy recovery system on a busy intersection to recharge streetlights. When a vehicle brakes, it transfers 45,000 J of kinetic energy to the system. If the system stores energy by spinning a flywheel, what angular speed (in rpm) must the flywheel achieve if its rotational inertia is 5 kg·m²?

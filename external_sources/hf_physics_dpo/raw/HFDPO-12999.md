# HFDPO-12999

A solar thermal power plant uses molten salt (specific heat 1,500 J/kg·K, density 1,900 kg/m³) stored in a cylindrical tank (diameter 10 m, height 15 m) to generate electricity during peak demand. After a sunny day, the salt reaches 550°C. At night, the system must deliver 20 MW of power over 8 hours by cooling the salt to 400°C. What is the minimum rate at which heat must be extracted from the salt to meet this demand, and how much would the actual cooling rate need to exceed this if the system is only 75% efficient?

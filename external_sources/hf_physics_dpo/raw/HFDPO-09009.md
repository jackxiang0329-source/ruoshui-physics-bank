# HFDPO-09009

A remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a solar-powered DC system with two parallel arrays of photovoltaic panels (each rated at 120 W, 18 V) connected to a battery bank (12 V, 200 Ah) via a charge controller. The system powers sensors (total 45 W) and a data logger (30 W), while charging the batteries at 4 A. If the charge controller has 95% efficiency and internal resistance of 0.15 Ω in the control circuit, what is the actual voltage across the battery terminals during charging?

# HFDPO-01023

A city planner is designing a new park near a busy highway and wants to reduce noise pollution for visitors. The highway generates a constant 80 dB sound level at the park boundary. If the park's sound barrier is 10 meters tall and placed 5 meters from the highway, how much would the sound level drop if the barrier blocks the direct path and the sound must now diffract around it? Assume the barrier is a rigid, narrow wall and the frequency of the dominant sound is 1000 Hz.

# HFDPO-02048

In a high-altitude satellite payload, a silicon photodiode array detects faint stellar signals at 850 nm, but unexpected dark current spikes occur during orbital night. Measurements show the dark current increases by 120% when junction temperature rises from 25°C to 85°C. Given: intrinsic carrier concentration in Si at 25°C is 1.5×10¹⁰ cm⁻³, bandgap energy E_g = 1.12 eV, and the diode is p-type doped at 1×10¹⁵ cm⁻³. Estimate the dominant dark current mechanism and determine the effective trap density in the depletion region that would explain the observed temperature dependence.

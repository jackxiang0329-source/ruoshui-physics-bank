# HFDPO-04562

A fusion reactor prototype uses a pulsed magnetic confinement system to compress a deuterium-tritium plasma cloud (initially at rest, 1.2 kg, 0.5 m radius) into a dense spherical core (0.2 m radius) in 100 μs. During compression, the plasma’s internal energy increases by 4.8 × 10¹⁰ J due to adiabatic heating. If the magnetic field does no net work, what is the minimum average magnitude of the magnetic force exerted radially inward on the plasma boundary to ensure energy conservation, assuming all kinetic energy from inward motion contributes to internal energy?

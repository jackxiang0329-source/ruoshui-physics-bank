# HFDPO-07013

In a remote wind farm, a 3-phase 50 Hz turbine generator delivers 1.2 MW to a grid with a lagging power factor of 0.85. Due to long transmission lines, the voltage at the turbine terminal drops by 4% when loaded. Engineers measure a 18° phase shift between line voltage and current at the generator. They suspect that inductive reactance from the transmission line is causing resonance with the turbine’s internal capacitance. Given that the line inductance per phase is 0.3 H, determine whether the system is near resonance and calculate the required capacitance to correct the power factor to 0.98 lagging, while avoiding dangerous harmonic amplification.

# HFDPO-13818

A small-scale wind turbine in a remote village powers a single-phase AC system that feeds both a 120 V, 60 Hz lighting grid and a sensitive medical refrigerator needing stable voltage. The turbine’s output fluctuates due to gusty winds, causing the line voltage to spike to 135 V RMS at peak output. To protect the refrigerator, the engineer adds a series LC filter between the turbine and the load. Given that the refrigerator draws 150 W at a power factor of 0.85 lagging, and the filter must suppress harmonic distortion while maintaining resonance at 60 Hz, what inductance and capacitance values are required to achieve resonance and minimize voltage ripple, assuming the line impedance is 0.5 Ω resistive and 0.3 Ω inductive?

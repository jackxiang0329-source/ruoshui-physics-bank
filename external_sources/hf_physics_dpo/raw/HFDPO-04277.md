# HFDPO-04277

During a critical surgery, a surgeon must deliver a precise dose of a viscous medication through a narrow 25 cm-long catheter (inner radius 0.5 mm) using a syringe. The medication has a viscosity of 0.03 Pa·s and density of 1100 kg/m³. If the required flow rate is 0.5 mL/min and the pressure at the syringe end is 12 kPa, will the catheter be able to deliver the medication at the desired rate, or is the pressure insufficient? Assume laminar flow and that the catheter is horizontal.

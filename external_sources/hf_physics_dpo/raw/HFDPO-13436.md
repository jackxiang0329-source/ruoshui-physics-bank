# HFDPO-13436

During a deep-sea exploration mission, a remotely operated vehicle (ROV) encounters a submerged, irregularly shaped oil reservoir sealed beneath a rigid, porous rock cap at a depth of 2.3 km. The rock cap has a vertical fracture 0.6 m wide and 2.0 m long, partially filled with seawater (density 1025 kg/m³). The fracture is inclined at 30° to the horizontal.

# HFDPO-14204

A deep-sea mining drone, designed to extract polymetallic nodules from the ocean floor at 5,000 meters depth, is tethered by a flexible, high-strength composite cable with a linear weight density of 8.2 N/m and a diameter of 12 cm. The drone (mass = 3,200 kg) sits on a sloped seabed (18° incline), and the cable is anchored to a surface vessel. Due to strong thermohaline currents, the cable experiences a lateral drag force of 1,400 N per meter along its length. Determine the minimum tension at the surface anchor required to prevent the drone from sliding uphill, assuming a coefficient of static friction of 0.65 between the drone and the seabed.

# HFDPO-05235

A marine biologist uses a laser-based underwater imaging system to study deep-sea coral formations at 500 m depth. The system emits a coherent green laser (λ = 532 nm) through a 10 cm diameter aperture. Due to temperature gradients in the water column, the refractive index varies with depth, modeled as n(z) = 1.33 + 1.2 × 10⁻⁴ sin(0.01z), where z is depth in meters. The biologist observes unexpected beam spreading and interference fringes in the return signal. What is the effective beam divergence angle at the coral layer, and why does interference appear despite the beam being collimated near the surface?

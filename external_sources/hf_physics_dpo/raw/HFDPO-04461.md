# HFDPO-04461

A coastal engineer is designing a new wave-energy converter that uses a vertical duct with a narrowing throat to amplify water flow and drive a turbine. The device is submerged 8 meters below the surface, where seawater density is 1025 kg/m³. At the wide inlet (diameter 2.4 m), the water speed is 1.2 m/s. At the throat (diameter 0.8 m), the pressure must remain above 15 kPa to prevent cavitation. Does the design risk cavitation? Use Bernoulli’s principle and continuity to evaluate.

# HFDPO-15582

A research team is developing a compact magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) device for use in remote field hospitals. The device uses a superconducting solenoid cooled to 4.2 K, generating a uniform magnetic field of 1.5 T over a 30 cm diameter imaging region. However, during testing, they observe a 12% field distortion near the solenoid’s ends due to fringe fields. They need to design a passive shimming system using high-permeability iron alloy rings placed at the ends to minimize this distortion. Given that the solenoid has 1200 turns and carries a current of 80 A, determine the required magnetic moment of each shimming ring (located at z = ±15 cm from the center) to counteract the axial field gradient and achieve uniformity within ±0.1% over the central 20 cm.

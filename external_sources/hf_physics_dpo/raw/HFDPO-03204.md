# HFDPO-03204

A solar-powered drone operating in a remote mountain valley relies on a custom-built hybrid power system with three interconnected battery banks (A, B, C) and a dynamic energy-sharing circuit. Use Kirchhoff’s Laws to determine the current through Battery B and assess whether the system is at risk of overloading.

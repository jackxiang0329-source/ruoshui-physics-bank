# HFDPO-04185

A solar-powered drone designed to monitor deforestation in the Amazon must maintain a constant altitude of 150 m while flying at 45 km/h. Its propellers transfer 65% of the electrical energy from solar panels into kinetic energy of air, and the drone’s total mass is 8.2 kg. If the solar array generates 180 W and the drone’s batteries store 1.4 kJ of energy, how long can it hover at full power before the batteries deplete, assuming 20% of energy is lost to heat and electronics?

# HFDPO-08075

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate circulating tumor cells (CTCs) from blood using deterministic lateral displacement (DLD). The chip contains rows of cylindrical pillars spaced 10 μm apart, arranged in a staggered pattern. Blood flows through the channel at a volumetric rate of 5 μL/min, with a viscosity of 3.5 mPa·s and density of 1060 kg/m³. A CTC (diameter ~12 μm) experiences drag in the viscous flow, but its inertia is negligible. The engineer needs to determine whether the CTCs will follow the “fluid streamline” or get deflected into a collection channel due to viscous forces. What is the Reynolds number in the channel, and does the flow remain in the Stokes regime?

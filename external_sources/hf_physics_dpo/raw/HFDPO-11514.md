# HFDPO-11514

A team of researchers is designing a quantum dot array for a next-generation solar cell, where each dot can occupy one of three energy states: ground (0 eV), excited (0.8 eV), or ionized (1.5 eV). What is the average number of ionized dots per unit cell if the ensemble has 1000 dots and the ionization process is governed by a Boltzmann factor with an effective energy barrier of 1.2 eV?

# HFDPO-05524

A 12-meter-long solar panel array is mounted on a rooftop in a hurricane-prone coastal city. The panels, each 3 meters long and weighing 45 kg, are attached to a lightweight aluminum frame fixed to the building's steel structure via bolted clamps. During a storm, wind exerts a non-uniform pressure that increases linearly from 0 kPa at the roof edge to 1.2 kPa at the far end. The clamps can withstand a maximum shear force of 1,500 N each. If the structure must remain in static equilibrium under these conditions, how many clamps are needed at the base to prevent rotational or translational failure? Assume the center of mass of each panel lies at its midpoint, and friction at the interface is negligible.

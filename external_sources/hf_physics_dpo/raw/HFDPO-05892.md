# HFDPO-05892

A drone delivery system uses a horizontal cantilever arm to suspend a 4.5 kg package above a rural road. The arm is supported by a rigid strut angled at 35° from the vertical and pinned at the wall. The arm itself weighs 2.8 kg and is uniformly distributed. What is the minimum required tensile strength of the strut to prevent failure, given that the package is centered 1.2 m from the wall and the strut connects 0.8 m from the wall along the arm?

# HFDPO-01209

In a remote Arctic research station, a solar array charges a bank of 12 V lead-acid batteries through a maximum power point tracking (MPPT) charge controller. The system powers a climate sensor array and data logger. One winter morning, sensors report erratic voltage readings. Upon inspection, the technician finds that while the battery bank reads 12.6 V, the voltage at the sensor terminals drops to 10.1 V under load—a 1.5 V drop over a 30-meter copper wire run. The sensor draws 0.3 A during data transmission. Determine the minimum cross-sectional area of the copper wire (ρ = 1.68×10⁻⁸ Ω·m) needed to limit voltage drop to ≤0.5 V under the same load, assuming the wire runs in a single cable.

# HFDPO-03613

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic device to separate cancer cells from blood based on size-dependent drag in a serpentine channel. The channel is 50 μm wide, 200 μm long, and has a viscous fluid (blood plasma, μ = 1.2 × 10⁻³ Pa·s) flowing at 1.5 mm/s. A 15 μm diameter cancer cell (density 1050 kg/m³) is carried in the flow. If the channel’s curvature causes the fluid velocity to vary spatially, how does the drag force on the cell compare to the gravitational settling force? Does the cell remain suspended, or does it accumulate near the outer wall?

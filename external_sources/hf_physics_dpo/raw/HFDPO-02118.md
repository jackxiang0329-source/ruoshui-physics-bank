# HFDPO-02118

The flue’s length is 15 m, and the flow exhibits turbulent characteristics with Re ≈ 1.2×10⁵. How can the sudden drag increase be explained using conservation laws and non-Newtonian fluid behavior, given that the vent fluid forms a buoyant plume with variable density and shear-thinning properties?

# HFDPO-06228

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer carries a non-uniform surface charge density σ(x) = σ₀(1 + 0.1 sin(2πx/L)), where σ₀ = 2 × 10⁻⁹ C/m² and L = 0.15 m. During lithography, a charged microcantilever (charge q = −1 pC) approaches perpendicularly from 100 μm above the wafer surface. The cantilever is sensitive to electrostatic forces and must not deflect more than 5 nm. Determine whether the net force on the cantilever exceeds the spring constant’s threshold (k = 100 N/m), and justify your conclusion using two different methods: (1) direct integration over the charged surface, and (2) modeling the charge distribution as a Fourier series with dominant harmonic components.

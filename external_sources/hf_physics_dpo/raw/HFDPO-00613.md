# HFDPO-00613

A biomedical engineer is designing a capacitive sensor to detect early-stage lung inflammation by measuring changes in the dielectric properties of lung tissue. The sensor uses two parallel plates (1 cm² area, 2 mm separation) embedded in a flexible patch worn on the chest. During normal breathing, the capacitance reads 4.4 pF. After a patient inhales a known irritant, the measured capacitance increases to 5.6 pF. Assuming the dielectric constant of healthy tissue is 32, what is the effective dielectric constant of inflamed tissue, and how might the change in capacitance be used to estimate the extent of inflammation?

# HFDPO-11651

In a lunar habitat's power grid, three solar arrays (A, B, C) feed into a central junction box with redundant wiring. Array A provides 120 V and 15 A, B offers 118 V and 20 A, and C supplies 122 V and 10 A. Due to a wiring fault, current paths from B and C form a closed loop with unknown resistances. If the junction box measures a net current of 5 A flowing out to the habitat load and all junctions must obey Kirchhoff’s laws, what are the individual currents in the interconnecting branches between B and C, and why might this configuration cause long-term thermal stress?

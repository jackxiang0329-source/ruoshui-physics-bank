# HFDPO-14328

A geothermal drilling team is designing a downhole turbine to harvest energy from hot, high-pressure brine flowing at 300°C from a deep well. The turbine must extract maximum useful work while maintaining structural integrity under 15 MPa pressure. Given the brine’s flow rate is 45 kg/s and the turbine’s efficiency is 72%, what is the maximum power output? Assume the brine exits at 200°C and negligible kinetic energy change, with a specific heat capacity of 4.2 kJ/(kg·K).

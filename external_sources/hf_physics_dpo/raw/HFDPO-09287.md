# HFDPO-09287

A smartphone manufacturer is designing a new touchscreen that must respond to light touches without triggering accidental inputs during pocket use. The screen uses a capacitive sensing layer with a plate area of 10 cm² and a dielectric (glass) thickness of 0.5 mm. During testing, the device registers false touches when the user’s hand is near but not touching the screen. What is the minimum capacitance (in pF) that the sensor must detect to distinguish a real touch from stray capacitance caused by nearby conductive objects, assuming the dielectric constant of the glass is 5.0?

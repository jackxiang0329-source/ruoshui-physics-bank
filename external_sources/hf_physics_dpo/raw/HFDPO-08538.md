# HFDPO-08538

During a lunar mining operation, a 450 kg drill rig on a frictionless, inclined lunar surface (12° slope) begins to slip after a seismic event disturbs the anchoring system. The rig is tethered by a 30 m cable wrapped around a massive, frictionless drum (radius 0.8 m, mass 220 kg) mounted at the top of the incline. As the rig descends, it unwinds the cable, causing the drum to rotate. If the drum’s moment of inertia is 70.4 kg·m², what is the rig’s acceleration down the slope, and how far will it travel in 8.0 seconds before the cable fully unwinds?

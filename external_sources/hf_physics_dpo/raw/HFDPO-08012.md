# HFDPO-08012

A deep-sea mining drone descends to a hydrothermal vent field at 2,500 meters depth, where extreme pressure (250 atm) and temperatures (350°C) affect the titanium alloy arms used to collect polymetallic nodules. The arms, designed with a cylindrical cross-section (diameter 12 cm), experience a 0.8% axial strain under 18 kN of sustained load during mining. However, during testing at surface conditions (1 atm, 20°C), the same arms deflect 0.03 mm under 1.5 kN. Determine the effective Young’s modulus of the titanium alloy *under in situ conditions* and explain why the surface test modulus is insufficient for predicting performance at depth.

# HFDPO-07217

A solar-powered desalination unit in a remote coastal village uses a porous membrane to separate saltwater from freshwater. The membrane’s pores are sized so that water molecules can pass through, but salt ions are blocked. At 30°C, researchers observe that the rate of water vapor flux through the membrane drops unexpectedly by 40% after one week of operation. Given that the vapor pressure of pure water at 30°C is 4.24 kPa and the system maintains equilibrium, what is the effective chemical potential difference driving water transport across the membrane? Assume ideal behavior and that the salt concentration increases due to accumulated brine.

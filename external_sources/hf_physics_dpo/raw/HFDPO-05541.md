# HFDPO-05541

A bioengineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate cancer cells from blood by size using a T-shaped flow channel. The chip uses a viscous fluid (viscosity 3.2 × 10⁻³ Pa·s) at 25°C, flowing at 0.8 mL/min through a 200 μm wide, 50 μm high channel. If large cells (diameter 15 μm) experience a drag force 2.5× greater than small cells (5 μm) due to surface interactions, how much longer must the separation section be to ensure large cells deviate 10 μm from their streamlines compared to small cells? Assume laminar flow and use the Stokes’ drag approximation for particle forces.

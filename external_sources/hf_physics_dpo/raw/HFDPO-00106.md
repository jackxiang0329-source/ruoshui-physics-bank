# HFDPO-00106

A quantum dot solar cell uses semiconductor nanoparticles with a bandgap of 1.8 eV, but efficiency drops sharply under high solar irradiance (1000 W/m²) due to carrier heating. If the average electron energy exceeds the bandgap by 0.2 eV, and the dot’s heat capacity is 2.5 × 10⁻²⁰ J/K, what is the temperature rise of the dot assuming all excess energy is retained? Use the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution to estimate the fraction of electrons above the bandgap after heating.

# HFDPO-08219

A satellite orbiting Earth uses a polarimetric imaging system to monitor oceanic algal blooms. The sensor detects sunlight reflected off the ocean surface, which is partially polarized due to specular reflection. At a 60° angle of incidence, the reflected light has a degree of polarization of 0.65. However, atmospheric scattering and bio-optical properties of the water introduce complex polarization modifications. To accurately quantify surface chlorophyll concentration, the instrument must disentangle the surface reflection’s polarization signature from the background depolarizing effects. Given that the water’s refractive index is 1.34 and the atmosphere contributes a 0.15 depolarization factor, what is the effective degree of polarization measured at the sensor, and how would you design a dual-polarization filter setup to isolate the surface reflection component?

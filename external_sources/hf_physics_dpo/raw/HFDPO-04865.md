# HFDPO-04865

A fusion-powered spacecraft uses a toroidal plasma containment field to sustain a D-T reaction at 150 million K. During a microgravity maneuver, a sudden magnetic field fluctuation causes 0.8% of the plasma mass (initially 0.2 kg) to escape radially at 2.1×10⁶ m/s. The remaining plasma compresses symmetrically inward. Calculate the spacecraft’s recoil velocity, given its total mass including the reactor is 12,000 kg. Assume no external forces and that momentum and energy are conserved in the system.

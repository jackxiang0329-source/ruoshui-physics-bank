# HFDPO-15071

A private space station in a 600 km circular low-Earth orbit must adjust its trajectory to avoid a derelict satellite fragment moving in a slightly elliptical orbit with perigee at 580 km and apogee at 620 km, both sharing the same orbital plane. The station’s engine can deliver a Δv of 15 m/s. Calculate the minimum required impulse maneuver (magnitude and direction) to ensure a closest approach of at least 5 km, considering the fragment’s position is currently 45° ahead in true anomaly. Is a single burn sufficient, and how does the choice of burn timing (prograde, retrograde, normal) affect collision risk over the next orbit?

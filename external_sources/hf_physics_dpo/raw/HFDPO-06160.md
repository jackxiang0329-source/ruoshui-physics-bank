# HFDPO-06160

A biomedical engineer is designing a flexible, implantable neural interface for recording brain activity. The device uses a 2D array of microelectrodes coated with a thin dielectric layer (εᵣ = 8.5, thickness = 50 nm) to prevent tissue corrosion, but leakage current due to parasitic capacitance between adjacent electrodes is degrading signal-to-noise ratio. Each electrode has a 20 μm × 20 μm footprint, spaced 100 μm apart center-to-center. The surrounding cerebrospinal fluid has a relative permittivity of 78. Estimate the total capacitance per electrode pair, including the dielectric, inter-electrode fringing, and fluid capacitance, and determine the minimum electrode spacing required to keep inter-electrode capacitance below 0.1 pF while maintaining spatial resolution.

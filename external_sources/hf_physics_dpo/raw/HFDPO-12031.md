# HFDPO-12031

A fusion reactor containment vessel uses a toroidal magnetic field to confine plasma at 15 million Kelvin. Engineers need to design a diagnostic probe that measures the net charge density within a 10 cm segment of the plasma column, but the probe cannot enter the plasma due to extreme radiation. Using external electric field measurements at radial distances of 1.2 m and 1.8 m from the torus center, they obtain φ₁ = 2.4 × 10⁵ V·m and φ₂ = 1.6 × 10⁵ V·m, respectively. The vessel has a major radius of 1.5 m and a minor radius of 0.3 m. What is the average charge density in the plasma segment, and how does the non-uniformity of the field challenge Gauss’s Law approximations?

# HFDPO-10062

A drone delivering medical supplies in a remote mountain village must navigate a narrow canyon with strong crosswinds gusting up to 12 m/s. The drone has a mass of 2.4 kg and a rotor thrust capacity of 30 N per rotor (four rotors total). To maintain stable hover while compensating for wind, it must adjust its pitch angle. If the wind exerts a horizontal force of 18 N at a 30° angle above the horizontal, what minimum pitch angle (from vertical) must the drone tilt to generate enough vertical thrust to counteract its weight plus the vertical component of wind force?

# HFDPO-07512

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a 15 cm × 15 cm silicon wafer is being cleaned using a plasma etch process. After etching, a uniform surface charge density of −12 nC/m² remains on the wafer due to trapped electrons. A nearby grounded metal shield (1 mm thick, 20 cm × 20 cm) is positioned 3 cm above the wafer, but its edge is not perfectly aligned—its far edge is 1.5 cm farther from the wafer’s far edge than the near edge. What is the net electrostatic force (magnitude and direction) on the wafer due to the induced charges on the shield, and how does edge misalignment alter the force compared to perfect alignment?

# HFDPO-05384

A solar-powered streetlight in a remote village uses a 12 V battery and a single LED bulb rated at 3 W. After a week of cloudy weather, the battery voltage drops to 9 V. The LED’s resistance remains approximately constant. What is the new power output of the LED, and will it still be bright enough to safely illuminate the path if the original brightness required at least 2.5 W?

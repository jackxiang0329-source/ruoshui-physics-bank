# HFDPO-10124

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to monitor reef soundscapes. She suspects the source is a deep-sea hydrothermal vent system undergoing increased activity, but must determine whether the observed signal is a standing wave pattern caused by interference between reflected and direct waves, or a progressive wave from a moving source. How can she differentiate between the two using wave properties and data from two sensor arrays 500 m apart?

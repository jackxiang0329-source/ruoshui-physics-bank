# HFDPO-01432

A desert solar farm uses parabolic troughs to concentrate sunlight onto a heat-transfer fluid, raising its temperature to 400°C. The fluid then powers a Rankine cycle turbine with a condenser operating at 60°C. Due to dust accumulation, the system’s efficiency has dropped unexpectedly. Engineers measure that the heat input rate is now 12 MW, but the turbine output is only 2.8 MW. Is the drop due to irreversibilities in the cycle, or is the heat transfer to the working fluid compromised?

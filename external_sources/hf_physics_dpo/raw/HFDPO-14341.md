# HFDPO-14341

In a cryogenic quantum computing lab, a superconducting qubit is coupled to a phononic bath at 15 mK, where thermal fluctuations are negligible yet quantum zero-point motion dominates. The bath consists of a 1D chain of 1000 harmonic oscillators with a uniform phonon frequency spectrum up to 50 GHz. After a control pulse, the qubit emits a single phonon into the bath. What is the probability that the emitted phonon is detected within a 1 ns window by a nearby resonant transducer, assuming the system is in thermal equilibrium *before* the emission and the coupling is weak?

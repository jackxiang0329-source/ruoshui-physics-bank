# HFDPO-01850

A geothermal power plant in Iceland uses a binary cycle system to generate 15 MW of electricity from a 120°C hot water source, cooling it to 85°C before reinjection. The working fluid (isobutane) enters the evaporator at 35°C and exits as saturated vapor at 80°C. Given the mass flow rate of geothermal water is 300 kg/s, determine the minimum theoretical efficiency required for the cycle to produce this power output—then explain why the actual cycle efficiency is significantly lower, even with optimal design.

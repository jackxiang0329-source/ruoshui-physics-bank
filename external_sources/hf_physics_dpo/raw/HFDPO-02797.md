# HFDPO-02797

A solar thermal power plant in the Mojave Desert uses molten salt to store heat collected during the day for electricity generation at night. The salt is stored in a 10,000 m³ insulated tank at 560°C. At night, 150 MW of power is steadily extracted over 8 hours. If the salt’s specific heat is 1,500 J/kg·K and its density is 2,100 kg/m³, determine the minimum final temperature of the salt to sustain this output without violating the Second Law.

# HFDPO-02105

A fusion reactor prototype uses a loop of high-temperature superconducting tape (YBCO) to maintain a 1.2 T magnetic field for plasma confinement. During a cooldown from 77 K to 4 K, the system unexpectedly loses superconductivity at 65 K, and the current drops abruptly. The tape has a critical current density of 3×10⁶ A/m² at 4 K and 1×10⁶ A/m² at 65 K. The loop’s cross-sectional area is 1.5 mm². What is the maximum allowable current to prevent quenching at 65 K, and why might the system have failed despite being below the nominal critical current?

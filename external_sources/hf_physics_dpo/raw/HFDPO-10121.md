# HFDPO-10121

A cyclist riding through a city park notices her bike’s dynamo-powered headlight dims significantly when she slows down near a traffic light. The dynamo is a small generator attached to the wheel that produces electricity as the wheel spins. If the headlight requires 3.0 W of power to shine brightly and the dynamo produces 0.60 W when the wheel rotates at 120 rpm, what minimum rotational speed (in rpm) is needed to maintain full brightness? Assume power output is proportional to rotational speed.

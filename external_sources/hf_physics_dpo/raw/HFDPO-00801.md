# HFDPO-00801

In a microfluidic lab-on-a-chip device designed for early cancer detection, a blood sample containing 5% by volume of spherical cancer cells (diameter 12 μm, density 1060 kg/m³) flows through a 200 μm wide, 1 mm long rectangular channel at a volumetric flow rate of 5 μL/min. The plasma (viscosity 1.2 mPa·s, density 1025 kg/m³) carries these cells through a sudden contraction that reduces the channel width to 50 μm. Determine the approximate axial velocity of the cells just downstream of the contraction, accounting for both viscous drag and inertial migration effects. Assume the flow remains laminar and the cell Reynolds number is less than 0.1.

# HFDPO-00557

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a single 12 V battery and a network of five identical 10 Ω resistors arranged in a bridge-like configuration to regulate power to sensors. During a dust storm, the wind causes one resistor to degrade, increasing its resistance to 25 Ω. The station’s microcontroller reads a voltage difference of 1.8 V across the bridge’s output terminals. What was the original resistance value before degradation, and how does this change impact the current through the circuit?

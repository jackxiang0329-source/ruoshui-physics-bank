# HFDPO-10112

A team of planetary geologists discovers a dense, irregularly shaped asteroid—roughly 12 km long with a non-uniform mass distribution—orbiting near a gas giant. To model its gravitational influence on a low-orbiting probe, they need to calculate the net gravitational force on a 500-kg probe located 4.3 km above the asteroid’s center of mass, assuming the asteroid’s density varies radially as ρ(r) = ρ₀(1 + 0.2 sin(πr/R)), where R = 6 km and ρ₀ = 5,200 kg/m³. How much does the non-uniform density alter the gravitational force compared to a uniform sphere of the same total mass?

# HFDPO-01061

A solar farm in Nevada uses large, grounded metal plates beneath solar panels to shield electronics from electromagnetic interference. Each plate is a flat square, 2 meters on a side, with a uniform surface charge density of 5 × 10⁻⁹ C/m². A small sensor above the plate measures the electric field directly above its center. What is the magnitude of the electric field at a height of 0.5 m above the center of the plate, assuming the plate is large compared to the distance and the field is due only to the plate’s charge?

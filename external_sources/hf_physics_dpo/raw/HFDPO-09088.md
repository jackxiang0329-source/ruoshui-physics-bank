# HFDPO-09088

A coastal research team deploys a network of hydrophones to monitor underwater volcanic activity near a tectonically active seamount. During a monitoring window, a series of seismic waves generated by a submarine landslide are detected at three sensors spaced 2.4 km apart in a straight line. The first sensor records a 20 Hz P-wave 0.8 s before the third. However, the second sensor detects a 12 Hz shear wave 0.5 s after the first. Given that the P-wave travels at 5.2 km/s in the crust and the S-wave at 3.0 km/s, calculate the distance from the landslide to the first sensor and determine the likely depth of the seamount’s magma chamber based on wave interference patterns observed in the hydrophone data.

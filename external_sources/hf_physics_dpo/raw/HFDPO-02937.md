# HFDPO-02937

A deep-sea submersible uses a custom fisheye lens to capture wide-angle footage of hydrothermal vent ecosystems at 2,500 meters depth. The lens has a refractive index of 1.72 and a radius of curvature of 8.0 mm. Due to pressure-induced stress, the lens distorts images by introducing spherical aberration, causing distant vent structures to appear blurred. Given that the sensor is positioned 12.3 mm behind the lens’s vertex, determine the optimal object distance (relative to the lens) that minimizes blur from spherical aberration by matching the longitudinal spherical aberration to the depth of field of the sensor array. Assume the lens is thin and the system operates under water (n = 1.33).

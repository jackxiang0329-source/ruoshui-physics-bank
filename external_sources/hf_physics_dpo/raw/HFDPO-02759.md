# HFDPO-02759

A solar energy farm in Arizona uses modular mounting frames to hold photovoltaic panels at a 25° tilt. During a rare windstorm, gusts reach 60 mph, creating a uniform horizontal pressure of 85 lb/ft² on the panel surface. Each panel measures 6 ft × 10 ft and weighs 220 lb. The mounting frame is anchored at two points: a base hinge at the bottom and a diagonal strut at the top, which can only resist axial loads. What is the minimum tension the strut must withstand to prevent the panel from rotating about the hinge?

# HFDPO-08218

A Martian terraforming project uses a network of superconducting coils to confine a 10,000-ton plasma cloud—composed of ionized nitrogen and argon—at 150,000 K to initiate atmospheric synthesis. Assuming the plasma behaves as an ideal gas with average molecular mass 28 u, estimate the final root-mean-square speed of the ions. How does this compare to the speed required to escape Mars’ gravity?

# HFDPO-09634

A solar panel array on a remote weather station is mounted on a tilting frame to optimize sunlight capture. The 80 kg panel, 3.0 m long, is supported by a pivot at one end and a hydraulic strut at 2.0 m from the pivot, angled at 35° above horizontal. During a storm, gusts exert a horizontal wind force of 450 N at the panel’s center. What minimum force must the strut exert to prevent rotation? Assume the pivot is frictionless and the panel's weight acts at its center.

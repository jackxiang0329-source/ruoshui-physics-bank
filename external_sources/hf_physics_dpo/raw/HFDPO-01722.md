# HFDPO-01722

During a lunar dust storm on the Moon, a retro-rocket lander descends at 1.2 m/s while ejecting fine regolith at 3.5 m/s relative to the lander. The dust particles, unaffected by air resistance but subject to lunar gravity (1.62 m/s²), form a visible plume. If the lander's descent engine cuts off at 48 m above the surface, at what height (from the surface) do the particles reach their peak altitude relative to the Moon’s surface?

# HFDPO-09811

During a deep-space mission, a cryogenic fuel tank on a Mars lander begins developing unexpected frost patterns on its inner surface despite being insulated. The tank stores liquid methane at 112 K, but ambient conditions near the spacecraft’s heat exchanger fluctuate between 108 K and 115 K due to a failing thermal regulation unit. Observers note that frost forms rapidly during cooldown but sublimes slowly during warm cycles—yet the total mass of frost increases over time. Explain why, using phase-transition thermodynamics, and determine whether the phase diagram of methane supports this cumulative frost growth under these conditions.

# HFDPO-01202

A quantum dot array in a next-generation solar cell operates at 300 K, with 1,000 electrons confined in a 2D lattice of 100 identical, weakly coupled quantum dots. Each dot can hold up to two electrons (spin-1/2) and the system is tuned so that the average occupation per dot is 1.8 electrons. Given that the energy levels within each dot are spaced by 50 meV and the thermal energy is kBT ≈ 25 meV, determine the effective chemical potential μ and the entropy per dot. Assume Fermi-Dirac statistics, treat the dots as independent but with a shared chemical potential, and consider the degeneracy of electronic states due to spin.

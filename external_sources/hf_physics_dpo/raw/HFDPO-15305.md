# HFDPO-15305

A geothermal drilling rig in Iceland uses a pivoting support arm to position a 12,000 kg drill head at a 35° angle above horizontal. The arm is bolted to a steel tower at point A and supported by a hydraulic actuator BC inclined at 18° to the horizontal, with the actuator’s piston capable of exerting up to 850 kN. The arm’s center of mass is 4.2 m from A, and the drill head’s center of mass is 7.8 m from A along the arm. Determine the minimum required actuator force to prevent rotation about A, and assess whether the actuator can safely maintain equilibrium under a 1.5x safety factor constraint.

# HFDPO-07318

A deep-sea mining robot descends to a hydrothermal vent at 2,500 meters below the surface, where seawater temperature reaches 350°C and pressure is 25 MPa. The robot carries a sealed, rigid titanium sample chamber filled with a pressurized gas at 1 atm (101.3 kPa) and 25°C. As the chamber cools to the surrounding water temperature, the internal pressure drops. The chamber’s volume remains constant, but the material has a maximum yield stress of 800 MPa. Will the chamber collapse, and if so, by how much pressure difference will it fail?

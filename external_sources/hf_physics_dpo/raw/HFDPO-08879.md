# HFDPO-08879

A wind farm in northern Scotland uses large turbine blades with embedded superconducting coils to harvest energy from fluctuating magnetic fields induced by Earth’s geomagnetic variations during solar storms. During a moderate geomagnetic storm, the magnetic flux through a single 50 m² coil changes at an average rate of 1.2 Wb/s over a 3-second interval. If the coil has 1200 turns and zero resistance, what is the average induced current if the external circuit has a resistance of 2.5 Ω?

# HFDPO-09845

A fusion reactor prototype uses a doughnut-shaped plasma confined by a toroidal magnetic field generated by 12 superconducting coils, each carrying 15 kA. During a startup sequence, a sudden fault causes a 50 ms current surge to 20 kA in one coil, producing a localized magnetic field that induces eddy currents in the copper alloy wall (resistivity 1.7×10⁻⁸ Ω·m, thickness 5 cm). The reactor's safety system must quench the field within 100 ms to prevent thermal stress. What is the minimum thickness of a superconducting stabilizer layer needed in the wall to limit the maximum induced current density to 10⁶ A/m²?

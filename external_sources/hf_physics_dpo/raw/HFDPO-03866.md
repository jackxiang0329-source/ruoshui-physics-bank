# HFDPO-03866

A solar-powered remote weather station in the Arctic relies on a network of 12 identical photovoltaic cells, each rated at 0.5 V and 2 A under full sunlight, connected in a mixed series-parallel array to power a 12 V, 1.5 A control system. During a prolonged overcast period, only 30% of the expected sunlight reaches the array. The station’s battery bank (12 V, 20 Ah) is near depletion. To prevent shutdown, the engineer must reconfigure the array on-site using only the available cables and switches. What is the minimum number of cells that must be connected in series to deliver at least 10 V and sufficient current to charge the battery at 0.5 A, assuming internal resistance of 0.1 Ω per cell?

# HFDPO-11674

A renewable energy startup designs a gravity-based energy storage system using massive concrete blocks lifted by electric winches during surplus solar production. Each block weighs 5,000 kg and is raised 80 meters in 30 seconds. When demand spikes, the blocks descend, driving generators. If the system operates with 85% efficiency during the lift and 90% during the descent, what is the net energy available for grid use per cycle, and how does this compare to the solar energy used to lift the block?

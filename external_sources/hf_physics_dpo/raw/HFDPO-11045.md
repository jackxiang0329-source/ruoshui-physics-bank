# HFDPO-11045

A deep-space communication array on a Mars-bound probe detects a faint, repeating signal from a distant pulsar, but the signal's frequency drifts unpredictably. The probe is traveling at 0.8c relative to Earth, and the pulsar is moving at 0.6c toward Earth in the same line of sight. What is the actual rest frequency of the pulsar’s emission, and how does relativistic velocity addition explain the observed shift?

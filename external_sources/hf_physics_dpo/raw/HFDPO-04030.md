# HFDPO-04030

A solar-powered weather station in a remote desert uses a 12 V battery bank charged by a 100 W solar panel. During a week of consistent sunlight, the system’s charge controller reduces the effective voltage to 11.5 V due to internal resistance. The station’s sensors and radio transmitter draw a fluctuating current: 200 mA when measuring, 500 mA during transmission. If the panel charges the battery at 6 A under ideal conditions, but the actual charging current is only 5.2 A due to losses, what is the internal resistance of the charge controller, and how many hours can the station operate on a full charge before the battery voltage drops below 10.2 V?

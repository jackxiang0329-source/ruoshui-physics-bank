# HFDPO-01198

A spacecraft in deep space performs a gravity-assist maneuver around Jupiter to gain speed for a mission to the outer solar system. The craft approaches Jupiter at 12 km/s relative to the planet and leaves with a velocity of 18 km/s in a different direction. Given Jupiter’s orbital speed around the Sun is 13 km/s and its mass is 1.9 × 10²⁷ kg, evaluate whether the observed speed increase is consistent with conservation of momentum and energy in the Sun-Jupiter-spacecraft system, considering Jupiter’s slight recoil.

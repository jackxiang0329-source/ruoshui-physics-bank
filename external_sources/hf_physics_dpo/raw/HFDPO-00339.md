# HFDPO-00339

A laser-based atmospheric lidar system on a satellite detects faint backscattered light from aerosol particles at 500 km altitude. The emitted laser (λ = 532 nm) is focused to a 10 cm diameter spot at the top of the atmosphere. Due to turbulence and atmospheric refraction, the wavefront becomes distorted, introducing phase aberrations modeled as a random phase variation φ(r) = (π/2) × exp(–r²/σ²), where r is radial distance from the center (σ = 3 cm). Calculate the fraction of the total optical power focused into the central Airy disk of the point spread function, assuming perfect diffraction-limited performance would yield 80% in the disk. How does the phase distortion affect beam performance compared to ideal focusing?

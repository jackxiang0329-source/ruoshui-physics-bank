# HFDPO-05776

A marine biologist studying coral reef health uses underwater acoustic sensors to detect sound patterns from a distant shipwreck. The sensors detect a sustained 420 Hz tone, but the measured wavelength is 3.4 m in seawater at 25°C. The biologist suspects frequency modulation due to Doppler shifts from ocean currents moving at 1.8 m/s toward the sensors. Given that the speed of sound in seawater is 1533 m/s and the current flows uniformly, determine the source frequency of the shipwreck’s signal and explain why the observed wavelength differs from the theoretical value in still water.

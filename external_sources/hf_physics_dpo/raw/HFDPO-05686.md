# HFDPO-05686

A wind farm in the Arctic tundra experiences frequent blade icing during winter storms, reducing efficiency by up to 40%. Engineers observe that ice accumulates preferentially on the leading edges of turbine blades, even though the ambient temperature is just below freezing. The blades are rotating at 20 rpm, and the incoming wind speed is 12 m/s with a turbulent intensity of 15%. The air density is 1.3 kg/m³, and the blade chord length is 3 m. Explain why the ice forms asymmetrically despite uniform environmental conditions, and determine the critical Reynolds number at which the boundary layer transition from laminar to turbulent may trigger the observed ice pattern.

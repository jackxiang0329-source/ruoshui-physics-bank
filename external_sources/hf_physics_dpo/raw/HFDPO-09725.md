# HFDPO-09725

In a semiconductor fabrication lab, a microchip carrier with a uniform surface charge density of −1.2 × 10⁻⁸ C/m² is being transported through an electrostatically shielded chamber. A neutral silicon wafer (3.0 cm × 3.0 cm) is suspended above it by a thin insulating rod, positioned 1.5 mm directly above the center of the carrier. The wafer has a dielectric constant of 11.7 and is isolated from ground. What is the magnitude and direction of the net electrostatic force on the wafer, and how does polarization affect the result?

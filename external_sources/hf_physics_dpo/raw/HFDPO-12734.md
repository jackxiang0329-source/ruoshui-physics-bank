# HFDPO-12734

A Mars rover traversing a 15° incline of loose basalt regolith encounters a sudden wind gust at 42 m/s, causing its dust-repellent coating to fail and triggering a cascade of 200-μm particles to accumulate on the wheel treads. Simultaneously, aerodynamic drag on the exposed wheel surface increases by 37% due to disrupted airflow. Determine the critical wind speed at which the rover can no longer maintain traction or overcome drag-induced torque.

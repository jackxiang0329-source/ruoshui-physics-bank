# HFDPO-15726

In a Mars rover’s solar-powered communication module, a silicon photodiode (bandgap 1.12 eV) must operate efficiently under low-intensity, red-shifted sunlight (peak wavelength 720 nm, intensity 120 W/m²). Given that the diode’s quantum efficiency is 85% at 720 nm and the junction area is 1.5 mm², determine whether the diode can meet the operational requirement under these conditions. Account for both temperature-dependent bandgap narrowing and carrier generation due to sub-bandgap photons via defect states.

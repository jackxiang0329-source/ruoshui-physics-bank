# HFDPO-08998

A team of engineers is designing a lunar landing module for a mission to the far side of the Moon, where communication delays exceed 40 seconds. The module must stabilize itself using gyroscopic attitude control during descent, but the Moon’s lopsided mass distribution—caused by an ancient impact basin—creates unexpected gravitational anomalies. At a particular descent waypoint, the local gravitational acceleration is measured at 1.618 m/s², yet the calculated value based on uniform density is 1.622 m/s². The anomaly correlates spatially with a 500 km-wide region of higher density, modeled as a spherical mass concentration (mascon) buried 40 km beneath the surface. What is the minimum density of the mascon required to produce this deviation, assuming the surrounding crust has an average density of 3100 kg/m³?

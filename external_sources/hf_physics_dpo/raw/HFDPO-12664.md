# HFDPO-12664

In a quantum computing lab, researchers observe that superconducting qubits made from niobium thin films exhibit unexpected decoherence at temperatures just above 1.5 K—despite the material’s nominal critical temperature being 9.2 K. One hypothesis suggests that a spin-fluctuation-driven pairing mechanism, akin to high-Tc cuprates, is induced by magnetic impurities from residual oxygen vacancies in the film. Calculate the effective coupling strength λ_eff required to sustain a pseudo-gap in the quasiparticle spectrum at 1.5 K, given a measured density of states suppression of 35% at 1 meV. Assume a Fermi energy of 3.8 eV and a weak-coupling regime.

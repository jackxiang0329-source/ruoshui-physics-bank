# HFDPO-12586

A solar panel array on a residential rooftop is mounted at a 25° incline to optimize sun exposure. During a winter storm, heavy snow accumulates unevenly—50 kg on the upper edge and 15 kg near the lower support, both acting vertically. The panel system (mass 80 kg) is pinned at the lower end and rests on a frictionless roller at the upper end. What is the minimum horizontal force applied at the upper edge (perpendicular to the panel) needed to prevent the panel from rotating clockwise due to snow load?

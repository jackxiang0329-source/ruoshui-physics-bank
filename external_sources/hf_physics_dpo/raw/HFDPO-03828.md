# HFDPO-03828

A biomedical engineer is designing a wearable cardioverter defibrillator (WCD) that delivers a brief, high-energy AC pulse to restore normal heart rhythm. The device uses a resonant LC circuit with a 50 μH inductor and a variable capacitor charged to 2.5 kV. The patient’s chest impedance varies from 40 Ω to 120 Ω, and the system must deliver a peak current of at least 20 A at resonance. What capacitance value must be selected to achieve resonance at 50 kHz, and what is the minimum peak voltage across the capacitor during discharge if the circuit’s Q-factor drops to 12 due to tissue resistance?

# HFDPO-07435

A biomedical engineer is designing a microfluidic chip to separate red blood cells from plasma in a portable diagnostic device. The chip uses a Y-shaped channel where fluid splits into two branches; one branch has a narrowed segment (diameter 20 µm), while the other remains wide (diameter 100 µm). Blood flows at 0.1 m/s in the main channel. Assuming incompressible, inviscid flow and negligible height differences, at what pressure difference between the two branches must the system be designed to ensure red blood cells (density 1060 kg/m³) are preferentially guided into the narrow branch due to Bernoulli-induced lift forces?

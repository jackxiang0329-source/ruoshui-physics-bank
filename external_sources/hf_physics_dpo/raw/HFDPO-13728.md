# HFDPO-13728

A geothermal energy plant in Iceland uses a vertical borehole with a magnetic sensor array to monitor subsurface fluid flow in a fractured basalt formation. The sensor array detects a time-varying magnetic flux of Φ_B(t) = (5.0 × 10⁻⁶ T·m²) × sin(2π × 10⁻³ t), where t is in seconds. The plant engineers must determine the optimal coil configuration for a downhole induction meter to maximize the induced EMF while minimizing noise from ambient geomagnetic variations. What is the maximum possible induced EMF in a 500-turn coil with an effective area of 2.0 × 10⁻² m², and how should the coil be oriented relative to the dominant flow direction to achieve this?

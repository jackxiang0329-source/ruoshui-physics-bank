# HFDPO-15025

A prototype magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scanner uses a cylindrical superconducting coil with radius 0.5 m and length 1.2 m to generate a uniform magnetic field of 1.5 T. The coil is powered by a time-varying current that increases from 0 to 1200 A over 0.8 seconds during ramp-up. Calculate the magnitude of the induced electric field at a radial distance of 0.3 m from the coil axis during this ramp-up, assuming the field builds linearly. What is the direction of the induced field relative to the current’s direction?

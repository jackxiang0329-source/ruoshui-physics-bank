# HFDPO-06633

A solar-powered drone designed for high-altitude atmospheric sampling must ascend from 1,000 m to 20,000 m while maintaining minimum kinetic energy to avoid stalling. The drone ascends at a nearly constant speed of 3 m/s due to power-limited thrust. Calculate the minimum additional energy (in MJ) the drone's battery must supply to complete the climb, given that solar power provides only 55% of the required work against gravity and drag.

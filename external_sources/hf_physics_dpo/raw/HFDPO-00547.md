# HFDPO-00547

In a cutting-edge fusion reactor design, a toroidal plasma containment chamber is surrounded by a complex array of magnetic coils that generate a non-uniform electric field to stabilize charged particle trajectories. A diagnostic probe measures a radial electric field strength of 1.2 × 10⁶ N/C just outside a 2.5 m radius section of the inner wall, but the field drops to zero at 2.7 m. Assuming the plasma has a uniform volume charge density and the chamber wall is perfectly conducting, what is the total charge enclosed within the 2.5 m radius boundary, and how does this relate to the net charge on the conducting wall?

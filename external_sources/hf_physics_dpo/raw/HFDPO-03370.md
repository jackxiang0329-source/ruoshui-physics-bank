# HFDPO-03370

A wildlife researcher is designing a non-invasive tracking system for migratory birds using tiny magnetic tags. Each tag emits a weak, pulsed magnetic field of 2.5 μT at 5 cm from the source. To monitor bird movement, a ground-based sensor array must detect this signal while rejecting interference from a nearby 120-V power line running parallel 10 m away, carrying 60 Hz alternating current. What is the minimum distance from the power line at which the sensor can accurately detect the tag’s field if the power line's field must be at least 10 times weaker than the tag’s field at the sensor location?

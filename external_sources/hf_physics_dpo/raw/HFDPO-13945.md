# HFDPO-13945

A satellite in geostationary orbit (35,786 km above Earth’s surface) carries an ultra-precise atomic clock. Engineers notice that over one year, the satellite’s clock gains about 45 microseconds relative to a clock on Earth. To maintain synchronization with ground stations, the satellite’s clock must be adjusted. What is the combined effect of gravitational time dilation and special relativistic time dilation on the satellite's clock, and how should the clock’s frequency be pre-adjusted before launch to compensate?

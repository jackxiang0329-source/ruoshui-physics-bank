# HFDPO-06513

A solar-powered weather station is mounted on a rooftop in a windy coastal town. The base of the station’s pole is secured with four bolts spaced evenly around a circular plate. During a storm, wind exerts a horizontal force of 120 N at the top of the 2.5 m tall pole, 1.8 m above the base. If the pole’s weight is 50 N and acts at its center, what minimum clamping force must each bolt provide to prevent the pole from rotating or tipping over?

# Half-Life and Activity — CP-02257

Large amounts of depleted uranium (<math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <msup> <mrow /> <mn>238</mn> </msup> <mtext>U</mtext> </math>

) are available as a by-product of uranium processing for reactor fuel and weapons. Uranium is very dense and makes good counter weights for aircraft. Suppose you have a 4000-kg block of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <msup> <mrow /> <mn>238</mn> </msup> <mtext>U</mtext> </math>

. (a) Find its activity. (b) How many calories per day are generated by thermalization of the decay energy? (c) Do you think you could detect this as heat? Explain.

# Half-Life and Activity — CP-02258

The *Galileo* space probe was launched on its long journey past several planets in 1989, with an ultimate goal of Jupiter. Its power source is 11.0 kg of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <msup> <mrow /> <mn>238</mn> </msup> <mtext>Pu</mtext> </math>

, a by-product of nuclear weapons plutonium production. Electrical energy is generated thermoelectrically from the heat produced when the 5.59-MeV <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <mtext>α</mtext> </math>

 particles emitted in each decay crash to a halt inside the plutonium and its shielding. The half-life of <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <msup> <mrow /> <mn>238</mn> </msup> <mtext>Pu</mtext> </math>

 is 87.7 years. (a) What was the original activity of the <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <msup> <mrow /> <mn>238</mn> </msup> <mtext>Pu</mtext> </math>

 in becquerel? (b) What power was emitted in kilowatts? (c) What power was emitted 12.0 y after launch? You may neglect any extra energy from daughter nuclides and any losses from escaping <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"> <mtext>γ</mtext> </math>

 rays.

# Calculating Pressure

In [Example 14.5](https://saturnaxis.github.io/IntroPhysics/Chapter14/fluids.html#example-problem-calculating-fluid-speed-through-a-nozzle), we found that the speed of water in a hose increased from $1.96\ {\rm m/s}$ to $25.5\ {\rm m/s}$ going from the hose to the nozzle. Calculate the pressure in the hose, given that the absolute pressure in the nozzle is $1.01\times10^{5}\ {\rm N/m^2}$ (atmospheric, as it must be) and assuming level, frictionless flow.

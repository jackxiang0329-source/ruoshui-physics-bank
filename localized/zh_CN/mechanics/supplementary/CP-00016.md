# CP-00016｜立定跳远的起跳加速与射程

**学科：** 力学　**知识点：** 抛体运动／立定跳远

**审核状态：** 中文初译；待独立双语、物理、答案及版权复核；不可正式组卷。

## 中文译题

立定跳远时，运动员先下蹲，再伸腿蹬地起跳。假设从屈膝姿势到完全伸腿的伸展距离为 $0.600\,\mathrm{m}$，在此阶段产生的加速度大小为重力加速度 $g$ 的 $1.25$ 倍。估算跳远距离，并清楚写出采用的假设。（原题指出，向起跳方向摆臂可进一步增加射程。）

# Projectile Motion — CP-00016

In the standing broad jump, one squats and then pushes off with the legs to see how far one can jump. Suppose the extension of the legs from the crouch position is 0.600 m and the acceleration achieved from this position is 1.25 times the acceleration due to gravity, <math xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1998/Math/MathML"><semantics><mrow><mrow><mi>g</mi></mrow><mrow /></mrow><annotation encoding="StarMath 5.0"> size 12{g} {}</annotation></semantics></math>

. How far can they jump? State your assumptions. (Increased range can be achieved by swinging the arms in the direction of the jump.)

# Turbulent or Laminar Flow

In [Example 14.8](https://saturnaxis.github.io/IntroPhysics/Chapter14/fluids.html#example-problem-air-conditioning-systems), we found the volume flow rate of an air conditioning system to be $Q = 3.84\times10^{-3}\ {\rm m^3/s}$. This calculation assumed laminar flow. (a) Was this a good assumption? (b) At what velocity would the flow become turbulent?

# IP-0052｜空调送风管的流量与房间换气

**学科：** 力学　**知识点：** 黏性流体／体积流量

**审核状态：** 中文初译；待独立双语、物理、答案及版权复核；不可正式组卷。

## 中文译题

一套空调系统拟以表压 $0.054\,\mathrm{Pa}$、温度 $20^\circ\mathrm{C}$ 送风。空气经过一根绝热圆管，管径 $18.00\,\mathrm{cm}$、管长 $20\,\mathrm{m}$，出口通向大气压为 $101.30\,\mathrm{kPa}$ 的房间。房间长 $12\,\mathrm{m}$、宽 $6\,\mathrm{m}$、高 $3\,\mathrm{m}$。（a）假定管内为**层流**，求体积流量；（b）估算更换房间内全部空气所需的时间；（c）若施工者改用直径 $9.00\,\mathrm{cm}$ 的风管以节约成本，求新流量。

## 英文原题（存档摘录）

# Air Conditioning Systems

An air conditioning system is being designed to supply air at a gauge pressure of $0.054\ {\rm Pa}$ at a temperature of $20^\circ{\rm C}$. The air is sent through an insulated, round conduit with a diameter of $18.00\ {\rm cm}$. The conduit is $20\ {\rm m}$ long and is open to a room at atmospheric pressure $101.30\ {\rm kPa}$. The room has a length of $12\ {\rm m}$, a width of $6\ {\rm m}$, and a height of $3\ {\rm m}$. (a) What is the volume flow rate through the pipe, assuming laminar flow? (b) Estimate the length of time to completely replace the air in the room. (c) The builders decide to save money by using a conduit with a diameter of $9.00\ {\rm cm}$. What is the new flow rate?

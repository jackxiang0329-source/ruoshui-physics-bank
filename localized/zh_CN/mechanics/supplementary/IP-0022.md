# IP-0022｜无摩擦倾斜弯道的理想车速

**学科：** 力学　**知识点：** 圆周运动／倾斜弯道

**审核状态：** 中文初译；待独立双语、物理、答案及版权复核；不可正式组卷。

## 中文译题

某些试车场和赛车场（例如 Daytona 国际赛车场）的弯道具有很大的倾斜角。这种倾斜使车辆即使不依赖轮胎摩擦，也能以较高的速率过弯。若弯道半径为 $100\,\mathrm{m}$、路面倾角为 $31^\circ$，且忽略路面摩擦，计算适宜的过弯速率。

## 英文原题（存档摘录）

# Ideal Speed Banked Tight Curve

Curves on some test tracks and race courses, such as [Daytona International Speedway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daytona_International_Speedway), are very steeply banked. This banking allows the curves to be taken at high speed even without tire friction. Calculate the speed at which a $100\ {\rm m}$ radius curve banked at $31^\circ$ should be driven if the road is frictionless.
